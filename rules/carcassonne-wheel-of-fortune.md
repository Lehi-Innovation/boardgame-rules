## Overview

Carcassonne: Wheel of Fortune is a set of 72 Carcassonne land tiles built around a large, four-by-four-tile "Wheel of Fortune" hub piece and a big pink pig, originally released by Hans im Glück in 2009. Nineteen of the land tiles carry a colored Wheel of Fortune icon; whenever one is drawn, the pig advances around six wheel sectors (Fortune, Tax, Famine, Storm, Inquisition, Plague) and the sector it lands on triggers a scoring event for some or all players. The rules are otherwise identical to the basic Carcassonne game — normal tile-drawing, tile-placement, and follower-deployment rules apply, and every land segment (including those on the Wheel tile itself) is scored the same way as in the basic game.

The set can be used either as a stand-alone replacement for the basic Carcassonne game (it includes its own scoreboard and 40 followers across 5 colors) or added into a game of Carcassonne alongside the basic game and other expansions. Players who consider the full 72-tile set "too much of a stand-alone" addition may instead use just the 19 tiles that carry a Wheel of Fortune icon.

## Setup

1. Place the Wheel of Fortune hub tile in the middle of the table. It serves as the starting tile — land tiles must be connected to it at the beginning of the game (and may be connected to it later as well). All land segments on this tile count toward scoring as usual.
2. Place the big pig on the "Fortune" section of the wheel, with its nose pointed to the right.
3. If playing stand-alone, use the set's own scoreboard and the 40 followers (5 colors) instead of a base game's components.

## Turn Structure

The rules are identical to the basic Carcassonne game except as noted below. On each turn:

1. **Draw a tile.** If the drawn land tile bears a colored Wheel of Fortune icon, immediately resolve the Wheel of Fortune (move the pig, perform the sector's event, score crown-space followers — see Actions) *before* placing the tile.
2. **Place the tile**, following normal Carcassonne adjacency rules.
3. **Deploy a follower (optional).** If the player has not deployed a follower onto the land tile they just placed, they may instead place one follower on any free crown space on the Wheel of Fortune. No more than one follower may occupy a single crown space. This is the player's one placement for the turn, not an additional placement.

## Actions

### Resolve a Wheel of Fortune Tile
When a player draws a land tile with a colored Wheel of Fortune icon, it is placed in front of them, and the following occur in order:

1. **Move the pig.** The player moves the big pig forward, always clockwise around the wheel, as many wheel segments as the number shown in the icon on the drawn tile.
2. **Perform the event.** The Wheel of Fortune has six sectors, each representing one event. Only the sector the pig's movement ends on is activated (segments merely passed over trigger nothing). The Fortune event affects only the player whose turn it is; the other five events affect every player:
   - **Fortune** — the active player receives 3 points.
   - **Tax** — every player scores for their knights: for each knight they have, the player earns 1 point for each pennant in that knight's city, plus 1 point for each of their own knights in that city. (Any follower placed in a city — including the Mayor or a Wagon — counts as a knight here.)
   - **Famine** — for each of their farmers, every player receives 1 point for every completed city adjacent to that farm, scored the same way farms are scored at final scoring (only a player with a majority on the farm scores; castles count double per farmer; any pig/pig-herd/castle farm bonuses that would apply at game end also apply here). Barns do not score for Famine, since barns are not followers and therefore not farmers.
   - **Storm** — every player receives 1 point for every follower currently sitting in their personal supply (i.e., not on the board).
   - **Inquisition** — every player receives 2 points for each monk they have in play.
   - **Plague** — every player must return 1 follower from a land tile to their personal supply (followers may not be removed from a crown space). The active player removes a follower first, then the other players follow in clockwise order.
3. **Score crown-space followers** in the activated sector only — followers on other sectors, or on segments the pig merely passed over, are not scored and stay where they are:
   - A follower standing alone on the only crown space in a sector scores 3 points.
   - A follower standing alone on a crown space in a sector that has two spaces scores 6 points.
   - If two followers occupy a two-space sector, each scores 3 points — even if both belong to the same player.
   - After points are allocated, all scored followers are returned to their owners' personal supply (so they can be redeployed later).
4. **Complete the turn as normal.** The player places the drawn land tile and may deploy a follower to it (or to a free Wheel of Fortune crown space) as usual.

## Scoring / Victory Conditions

Ordinary Carcassonne scoring applies throughout: completed cities, roads, cloisters, and farms are scored exactly as in the basic game, and every land segment on the Wheel of Fortune hub tile itself scores normally like any other tile. On top of that, drawing a Wheel-of-Fortune-icon tile triggers the wheel event for whichever sector the pig lands on (see Actions): Fortune, Tax, Famine, Storm, and Inquisition award points, while Plague instead forces every player to return a follower to their supply. Crown-space followers sitting in the activated sector are scored (3 points if alone in a single-space sector, 6 points if alone in a two-space sector, or 3 points each if two followers share a two-space sector) and then returned to their owners. As in the basic game, the player with the most points when the game ends wins.

## Special Rules & Edge Cases

- A follower placed on a Wheel of Fortune crown space is not removed from play the way a normal tile placement is until its sector is scored — once scored, it returns to its owner's personal supply and can be redeployed on a later turn.
- Followers on wheel segments the pig passes over during its move are unaffected that turn; only the sector where the pig's movement ends is scored.
- No more than one follower may ever occupy a single crown space.
- A Plague event removes one follower per player from a land tile only (never from a crown space), starting with the active player and proceeding clockwise.
- When combining the Wheel of Fortune with a large multi-expansion game, rather than using the Wheel of Fortune tile as the starting tile, it may instead be placed to one side (for example, next to the scoreboard) to preserve the integrity of the playing area, since every other visible feature on the board is otherwise semi-realistic; the reverse side of the tile has a more abstract design that can be used for this purpose.
- Dragon expansion interaction: the dragon can fly around the *outside* of the Wheel of Fortune, treating those landscape areas as normal tiles and eating any followers on them there; followers on the Wheel of Fortune itself are unaffected by the dragon.
