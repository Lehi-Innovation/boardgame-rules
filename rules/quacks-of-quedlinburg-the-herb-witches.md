## Overview

This expansion requires [The Quacks of Quedlinburg](../quacks-of-quedlinburg/). Keep its nine-round structure, explosion rules, and scoring, adding three once-per-game witch abilities, overflow bowls, variable-value locoweed, orange 6-chips, recipe sets 5–6, and equipment for a fifth player.

Source: [publisher's English expansion rulebook](https://www.schmidtspiele.de/files/Retail/72dpi_PNG/88232_Quacks_The_Herb_Witches_GB.pdf), PDF pages 1–4. The correct BoardGameGeek expansion ID is **269259**.

## New Components

- 8 ingredient-book tiles, including locoweed recipes and the replacement orange book.
- Ingredient chips; the rulebook lists **153 plus 3 spares**. Use its individual component illustration/your edition's inventory when counting a physical set.
- 20 rubies, 12 witch tiles (4 choices for each of 3 penny colors), 15 witch pennies, and 5 overflow bowls.
- Fifth-player equipment: 1 pot, 1 bag, 1 flask, 2 droplets, 1 scoring marker, 1 rat stone, and 1 zero/50 seal.

## Setup Changes

1. Set up the base game, including a fifth player if desired. A fifth player uses the same starting ingredients and resources as the others.
2. Choose ingredient books. Sets 5 and 6 join the four base-game sets. Replace the orange book with the expansion's version and add the orange 6-chips to the supply.
3. Choose the locoweed recipe to use and put out that book and its chips. Locoweed has no fixed printed value; its current recipe determines movement.
4. Each player takes one penny of each of the **three colors**, plus the matching overflow bowl placed by the pot's spoon.
5. Randomly select **one witch of each color/type** and put those three tiles beside the round indicator. Return the unused witches to the box. All players use the same three selected abilities.

## Rule Changes

### Witch pennies

Spend the matching penny to use a selected witch **during the phase specified on her tile**. Each penny can be spent only once per game. Different players independently use the shared witch abilities; one player's use does not exhaust the tile for anyone else. You may spend multiple differently colored pennies in one round. Each unused penny scores **2 points at game end**.

### All twelve witch abilities

Only three selected abilities are in play. The descriptions identify each by effect rather than artwork.

| Timing | Witch effect and restrictions |
|---|---|
| Preparation, after exploding | Use your **full flask** to return the explosion-causing white chip to your bag. Empty the flask normally; you may continue drawing or stop. This is the exception to the base flask's explosion prohibition. |
| Preparation, before stopping and before explosion | Draw 6 chips. Place any number in any order, resolving normal chip effects; return unused chips. Pause synchronized round-9 draws until this finishes. Normal explosion checks still apply while placing them. |
| Preparation, before explosion | Return the last **two white chips** placed to your bag, or only one of those two. They need not be adjacent or the final two chips overall. Leave all gaps and other chips in place. |
| Evaluation after explosion | Receive both normal points and shopping money. You may also roll the bonus die if your potion reached the farthest scoring space. |
| Shopping | Upgrade the last two chips placed in your pot one level each, **or** upgrade one chip anywhere in your pot one level. Only 1→2 or 2→4; no pumpkins, no newly purchased chips. You may use this even if you exploded and chose points. |
| Shopping | Double your shopping budget. The normal maximum of two differently colored purchases remains. In round 9 this also doubles money available for the usual 5:1 point conversion. |
| Shopping | Obtain one additional identical copy of a chip you just bought for free. Buying two different colors therefore yields three chips, two matching exactly. |
| Shopping | Add **2 money for every ruby you possess**; do not spend those rubies. |
| Points | Score according to the number of nonwhite colors in your pot: **1–4 colors: 3 points; 5: 4; 6: 7; 7: 10; 8: 14**. Locoweed counts as a color. |
| Points | Reveal the chips left in your bag. Score **2 points each** for nonwhite 2s, 4s, and 6s, and for purple and locoweed chips. White chips and other value-1 chips do not score. Return the chips afterward. You may use this even if you exploded and chose shopping. |
| Rubies | If your scoring space shows a ruby, replace its normal ruby reward with as many rubies as that space's printed points. This does not cancel normal point entitlement. If the event offers 2 rubies instead, choose the event or this witch rather than combining them. |
| Round end | For this round's end, each flask refill or droplet advance costs **1 ruby instead of 2**. Repeat as affordable. This discount does not change the final 2-rubies-per-point conversion. |

### Overflow bowls

If a chip reaches or passes pot space **33**, put it on 33. Excess movement is lost. A final-space chip whose action would affect the next drawn chip loses that prospective effect.

You may continue drawing into the overflow bowl. Colored chips in the bowl perform **no ingredient-book actions**, while white chips still add their printed values to the explosion total. At evaluation D, the spoon supplies **15 points and 35 money**; additionally score **half the total value of chips in the overflow bowl, rounded down**, as points. Normal explosion consequences still apply: choosing shopping forfeits the scoring-phase points unless a separate ability grants them.

All players who reached 33 tie for endpoint distance for bonus-die purposes, regardless of overflow quantity; they roll if their pot did not explode. Overflow does not create a higher pot position.

### Pumpkins and locoweed

Orange chips have no additional action. Prices are **3 for value 1** and **22 for value 6**. No effect can upgrade an orange 1 to an orange 6.

Choose one locoweed book:

| Recipe | Cost | Movement/action |
|---|---:|---|
| Rat-based | 8 | Move the number of spaces your rat stone advanced at the start of this round, including event effects, **plus 1**, maximum **4**. Use that established rat movement, not a later changed score gap. |
| Copy | 10 | Copy the value and action of the last **nonwhite** ingredient in your pot. If there is none, move 1 with no action. |

When another effect asks for locoweed's printed value, treat it as **1** where the rules specify this, such as the purple recipe and yellow preview below.

### New recipe sets

The first recipe in each row is set 5 and the second is set 6. Prices for multivalue ingredients are **1 / 2 / 4**. Blue/red/yellow act in preparation; green/purple/black resolve during evaluation B, including exploded pots.

| Color and set | Price | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Blue 5 | 8 / 15 / 19 | If the number of orange chips already in your pot is at least blue's printed value, gain that many points immediately. Orange values do not affect the count. |
| Blue 6 | 5 / 10 / 18 | Examine the preceding 1, 2, or 4 chips according to blue's value; gain 1 ruby for every white 1 among them. Count preceding chips, not empty pot spaces. |
| Red 5 | 6 / 11 / 18 | Move red by its own value or the greatest printed value of a red already in the pot, whichever is higher. |
| Red 6 | 7 / 11 / 17 | After placing red, draw another chip and set it aside. You **must** place this chip this round, before or after stopping, even after explosion. A saved white still counts toward exploding when placed. Place any normally drawn chip before inserting a saved chip. |
| Yellow 5 | 10 / 14 / 20 | After placing yellow, draw a preview chip and advance yellow that chip's value further; preview locoweed counts as 1. Return the preview to the bag. |
| Yellow 6 | 8 / 12 / 18 | Optionally pay exactly **1 ruby** for **3 extra spaces**. Cannot repeat the payment for the same yellow. |
| Green 5 | 5 / 9 / 16 | Each green among your last two chips lets you select a chip currently in the pot, at most that green's value, as a starting chip for the next round. Its action then works normally. Two qualifying greens can save two chips, placed in either order. The qualifying green itself is eligible. |
| Green 6 | 4 / 8 / 14 | Roll the bonus die once for each green among your last two chips. Under the matching “The pot is full” event, roll twice per qualifying green. |
| Purple 5 | 13 | Add the printed points on the **occupied spaces** of all purple chips. Use that total as a separate budget in evaluation B to buy up to 2 differently colored chips. Do not combine it with step-E money. In round 9 it can instead buy points at 5:1. |
| Purple 6 | 16 | Each purple scores the **printed value** of the chip immediately following it. Bonus movement does not increase this reward; locoweed counts as 1. A purple without a following chip gives no such reward. |
| Black 5 | 10 | A black bought in step E or received from an event goes into the bag of the player to your **left**; advance your droplet once. During evaluation B, gain 1 ruby for every black in that neighbor's pot; that pot's owner also gains 1 ruby for each black among their own last two chips. |
| Black 6 | 9 | The player(s) with the farthest black chip advance one droplet once; the player(s) with the second-farthest black receive 1 ruby. One player may receive both bonuses using two chips. Ties share the corresponding bonus. |

## Special Rules & Edge Cases

- Witch effects do not freely change phases. Use them at their printed timing, and keep the usual explosion choice unless an ability explicitly overrides it.
- The return-two-whites witch cannot repair an already exploded pot. The full-flask witch specifically can.
- A saved red-set-6 chip must eventually be placed; red set 2 from the base game instead allows red chips to be held for later rounds. These are different recipes.
- Reaching 33 does not make later white draws safe. Overflow whites can still explode the pot, and colored overflow chips do not activate the book effects described above.
- Chips revealed by Fortune Teller instructions such as “Overpowering ingredient” or “A second chance” are risk-free for explosion purposes. The ingredient placed by “Overpowering ingredient” does not activate its book action.
- Pumpkins cannot be upgraded through books, events, or witches. An orange 6 must be obtained directly as a 6.
- Expansion BGG identity is **269259**, distinct from the base game's **244521**. This expansion's rules add to the base summary rather than replacing it.
