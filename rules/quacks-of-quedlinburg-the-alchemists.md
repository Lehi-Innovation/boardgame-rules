## Overview

This expansion requires [The Quacks of Quedlinburg](../quacks-of-quedlinburg/). Every player selects one patient and uses a separate essence track. After preparing a potion, count ingredient colors, an exact white-chip total of seven, and neighboring explosions to determine the essence. The chosen patient supplies extra rewards or powers for a later round. All usual base-game scoring and explosion consequences remain unless a power explicitly changes them.

The publisher recommends learning it with the base game first. It can later be combined with [The Herb Witches](../quacks-of-quedlinburg-the-herb-witches/), whose player equipment is needed for five-player play. Source: [publisher's English rulebook](https://www.schmidtspiele.de/files/Retail/300dpi_JPG/88319_Alchemisten_GB_web.pdf), PDF pages 1–6. The correct BoardGameGeek expansion ID is **316597**.

## New Components

- 8 patient charts and 8 matching patient selection markers.
- 5 alchemist flask boards, 5 essence markers, and 20 double-sided essence cards: four cards per player, showing eight patient choices across their faces.
- 20 additional Fortune Teller cards.
- 30 locoweed ingredient chips and 2 double-sided locoweed books, providing four recipes.
- 40 replacement white firecracker chips: **25 value 1, 10 value 2, and 5 value 3**. These replace worn white chips; they do not all enter the starting bag.

## Setup Changes

1. Set up the base game. Add new Fortune Teller cards appropriate to the expansions being used. Cards carrying the Alchemists symbol require this expansion; cards carrying the Herb Witches symbol require that expansion. Unmarked cards can also be used without expansions.
2. Select one locoweed recipe. These four books are not tied to particular recipe sets, so any may accompany any base ingredient set. Place its chips in the supply.
3. Each player places an alchemist flask board above their pot, an essence marker on its zero space, and takes the four double-sided essence cards of their color.
4. Put the eight patient markers in a bag and draw **3**. Display those three matching patient charts. Return all selection markers and the five other charts to the box.
5. Each player chooses one of the three displayed patients and places the matching essence-card side on their flask. Several players may choose the **same** patient. Return unused essence cards to the box.
6. Apply any chosen patient's starting-bag changes: **Carrot Nose** adds one orange 1 (10 starting chips); **Forgetfulness** adds one blue 1 and one red 1 (11 starting chips). Other patients keep the base bag.

## Rule Changes

### Round order and essence calculation

Insert an **Essence Phase after everyone finishes potion preparation and before the normal evaluation**. All players perform this phase, including players whose pots exploded.

At its start, reset your essence marker to **0**. It does not accumulate across rounds. Then calculate:

1. **One space for each different nonwhite ingredient color in your pot.** Multiple chips of one color count once. White contributes no color.
2. **One additional space if white-chip values total exactly 7.** Neither 6 nor 8 qualifies, even if another ability changes your safe explosion limit.
3. **One additional space for each immediately neighboring player whose pot exploded.** Left and right can contribute 2 in a game of at least three players. In two players, the same opponent counts **once**, for a maximum of +1.
4. Apply a relevant locoweed essence bonus if that recipe is in play.

For example, red, orange, and blue give 3 spaces; white totaling exactly 7 adds 1; one exploding neighbor adds 1, producing essence level **5**.

Follow the tube from the resulting flask space to the inserted essence card. The card determines whether that level grants a bonus, points, a rat-tail increase, or a stored ability. **These printed patient-card rewards differ; essence level is not automatically the number of chips, points, or money awarded.** You may deliberately choose a lower level before receiving the reward.

- Printed points are awarded immediately.
- A printed rat symbol adds **one rat-tail space at the start of the next round**, in addition to the normal head start.
- An oval patient-bonus glass resolves at the timing stated on that patient's chart.
- Stored essence abilities can be usable even when the tube ends at no bonus glass; the remaining essence level is still available for the patient's specified payments.
- Resolve ordinary evaluation afterward. Your own explosion still determines whether you receive normal points or shopping money.

### The eight patients

#### Nervousness

At essence level 1, receive an extra rat tail for next round. Otherwise, use the **chip count shown in the connected oval** at the beginning of your next preparation. Draw that many chips, return all whites to the bag, and lay the colored chips beside your pot.

Before each placement during preparation, choose either a displayed chip or a fresh blind draw. Placed ingredients resolve normally. You cannot place more chips after your pot explodes. With **red set 2**, red chips drawn from the bag retain that recipe's restriction: place them only after stopping.

#### Ear Worm

Immediately after determining your essence reward, draw the **number of chips shown in its connected oval**, one at a time. Place each by normal movement and resolve its ingredient action before drawing the next. Whites drawn by this patient power **cannot cause an explosion**, even if the white total becomes greater than 7.

Under **red set 2**, drawing a red still consumes one of the allowed draws even if it is not placed in the pot in this round. This power happens before normal evaluation, so the final pot positions affect the evaluation that follows.

#### Carrot Nose

Begin the game with the additional orange 1 described in setup. In the next round's preparation, whenever you draw a pumpkin, you may spend **2 essence spaces** to put it on the next available ruby space instead of using its normal movement value. Reduce your marker by 2 each time you pay. The pumpkin's printed value is irrelevant to that placement. Ordinary points or rat tails pictured on the essence card are separate immediate/next-round rewards.

#### Wing Ears

In the following round's preparation, whenever you draw a white chip, choose normal placement or one of these payments:

- Spend **2 essence** to move it **twice its printed value**: a white 3 moves six spaces, but still contributes only 3 to the white explosion total.
- Spend **3 essence** to return it to the bag.

**Neither option can be used if the white chip just drawn causes your pot to explode.** These powers do not repair an explosion. Any points/rat tail indicated by the essence card are additional rewards.

#### Chicken Eyes

Receive the connected patient-chart bonus **immediately after the Essence Phase**. Follow the tube to the specific pictogram; it grants one listed reward, rather than every reward pictured on the chart. The chart's possible rewards are:

- 1 ruby; 1 orange 1; refill your flask; 1 red 1; or 3 rubies.
- Exchange one value-1 chip in your pot for value 2 of the same color; or for value 4 of the same color, according to the indicated icon.
- Roll the bonus die **twice**, taking each result; or **four times**, taking each result, according to the indicated icon.
- Advance your droplet **2 spaces**.

For exchanges, use a color/value combination supplied by that ingredient; the pumpkin-upgrade prohibition remains in force.

#### Witch's Hump

During the next preparation, whenever you place a chip **on a ruby space**, you may spend **2 essence** for its chart reward. The chip's printed value/type determines the bonus:

| Chip just placed | Reward |
|---|---|
| Value 1 | 1 ruby |
| Value 2, or black hawkmoth | Roll bonus die once |
| Value 3, or purple ghost's breath | 1 yellow 1-chip |
| Value 4, or locoweed | 3 points |

Named black/purple/locoweed categories use their listed special row, rather than being treated as ordinary value-1 chips. Put an awarded ingredient in your bag immediately. A droplet rolled on the die advances immediately; already placed chips remain where they are, even if the droplet reaches an occupied space.

With **red set 2**, red chips placed after stopping cannot activate this essence power. Normal points and rat-tail rewards on the essence card are in addition to the stored power.

#### Forgetfulness

Begin with the additional blue 1 and red 1 described in setup. During the following preparation, return any colored ingredient in your pot to the bag by paying essence equal to its **printed value**. **Neither white nor locoweed may be returned using this power.** Other chips do not shift to close the gap. This essence's printed points are an additional reward.

#### Vampirism

At essence level 1, receive an additional rat tail for the next round. At another level, immediately buy **one extra ingredient** using the **money amount shown in the connected oval on the essence card**. Put it into your bag immediately. This is a separate purchase during the Essence Phase, before the base game's shopping phase; do not pool its budget with ordinary shopping money.

### The four locoweed recipes

Choose one of these books; the prices and movement rules are printed on PDF page 6.

| Price | Movement during preparation | Extra effect |
|---:|---|---|
| **11** | 1 space | During essence calculation, advance **1 extra space per locoweed in the pot**, in addition to the one color-count space for having locoweed present at all. Two locoweeds therefore contribute 3 essence spaces in total. |
| **16** | Number of different **nonwhite colors** in the pot, counting locoweed itself | Count the first locoweed as its new color even before placing it. Count colors, not chip count or printed values. |
| **10** | 1 space | After placing it, optionally return a nonwhite chip from your pot to the bag. Other ingredients remain in their positions. |
| **12** | Sum of the printed values of white chips currently in the pot, **minimum 1** | No extra action. Use the white value total, not the number of white chips. |

Locoweed chip prices are fixed by the selected recipe; these are not value-1/2/4 versions to be upgraded among.

### Final round

In round **9**, calculate essence once more, but replace **every normal essence reward**—patient bonuses, stored powers, rat tails, and pictured points—with **1 victory point per essence space reached**. Then perform ordinary evaluation and the usual final money/ruby conversions from the base game.

## Special Rules & Edge Cases

- Essence can be made even after **your own** explosion. Own explosion does not grant the neighbor-explosion bonus; only the immediate left/right players count.
- In two players, an exploding opponent gives +1, not +2. This remains true even though the opponent occupies both neighboring seats.
- The extra white-chip replacements refresh worn pieces; do not add all 40 to active bags or automatically add another white chip for this expansion. The base game's round-6 white 1 addition still applies.
- You cannot save essence through the next calculation: spending powers operate in the following preparation, then the next Essence Phase resets the marker before producing new essence.
- An exact white total of 7 grants its extra essence even when other rules would allow more whites safely. Ear Worm's risk-free draws and potions with altered explosion limits do not redefine “exactly 7.”
- Points and rat tails on the card accompany a patient's power where indicated. An empty bonus tube does not inherently turn off an essence-spending action.
- Use the printed flask/card tube connections to read reward quantities; they are patient-specific. The essence level is a resource/position, not a universal reward table.
- The rulebook uses “firecrackers” for the base game's white ingredients. They are the same color/mechanic, not a new colored ingredient.
- This expansion alone supplies essence equipment for five, but does not supply the fifth **base-game pot and bag**. Five-player games require the extra player equipment from Herb Witches.
