## Overview

Ezra and Nehemiah is a thematic worker-placement/hand-management game for 1-4 players (with a dedicated solo mode against an automated opponent) set in ancient Jerusalem. Players take on the role of a returning exile leader who rebuilds the Temple, clears rubble and constructs the city Walls and Gates, and teaches the Torah to fellow exiles, while keeping the Altar burning. The game is played over 3 Rounds, each representing a week: 6 days of work followed by a Sabbath day of rest, scoring, and feeding of Workers. The aim is to have the most Victory Points (VP) when the game ends after the third Sabbath.

## Components

- 66 Workers (2 white + 16 in each of 4 player colours)
- 4 Altar Markers
- 40 Coins (worth 1 Silver) and 10 Pouches (worth 5 Silver) — freely exchangeable 5-for-1
- 33 Scroll Tiles (8 Gold, 22 Wood, 3 Stone)
- 40 Bread (worth 1 Food) and 10 Baskets (worth 5 Food) — freely exchangeable 5-for-1
- 1 Shofar (First Player Marker)
- 1 Rubble Bag
- 4 Tents
- 12 Blessing Markers (4 in each of 3 colours: Orange, Purple, Red)
- 2 Prophets (Zechariah, Haggai)
- 4 VP Markers
- 104 Resources (30 Stone, 24 Cinders, 20 Gold, 30 Wood)
- 20 Development Tiles (4 Guards, 4 Labourers, 4 Farmers, 4 Traders, 4 Elders)
- 1 Solo Board (double-sided), 1 Temple Mount, 1 Main Board, 4 Player Boards, 4 Player Aids, 1 Solo Player Aid
- 40 Character Cards (10 in each player colour)
- 48 Wall Cards (12 in each player colour), 6 Gate Cards, 6 Neutral Wall Cards
- 10 Focus Cards and 6 Scheme Cards (Solo mode only)
- 1 Setup Card
- 2 Altar Cards (only 1 used per game)
- 3 Hero Cards (Zerubbabel, Ezra, Nehemiah)

## Setup

**Main Board:**
1. Place the Main Board centrally. Place all 6 Gate Cards alongside it, workers-outline side faceup.
   - **1-2 players:** Turn over the Muster Gate and East Gate and place onto their Main Board spaces; place 2 Silver on each remaining Gate Card. Cover the 6 centre-most top Wall spaces (between/adjacent to the Muster and East Gates, plus 1 to the left) with Neutral Wall Cards.
   - **3 players:** Place 1 Silver on each Gate Card alongside the board. Cover the 3 Wall spaces between the Muster and East Gates with Neutral Wall Cards.
2. Place 10 of each Resource (Gold, Stone, Wood, Cinders) plus all Silver and Food alongside the board to form the Main Supply.
3. Place the remaining 64 Resources in the Rubble Bag; draw randomly to fill each Wall/Gate space with the number of Resources shown by its icons. Leftovers go to the Main Supply; the bag returns to the box.
4. Sort Scroll Tiles by their backs. Randomly place 1 Gold Scroll Tile faceup on each space of the top row, 1 Wood Scroll Tile on each space of both middle rows (9 total), and 1 Stone Scroll Tile on each space of the bottom row. Return the rest to the box.
5. Place Food to the right of the Scroll rows: 4VP row = 3 Food, 3VP row = 2 Food, 2VP row = 1 Food, 1VP row = 0 Food.
6. With fewer than 4 players, place the Temple Mount over the Temple, correct side up for player count.
7. Choose (random or selected) 1 of the 2 Altar Cards; return the other to the box. Place the Prophets on the numbered spaces indicated by the highest red-flag positions on the chosen card.
8. Place the 3 Hero Cards alongside the Main Board.
9. Randomly determine a first player; give them the Shofar.

**Player Boards (each player):**
1. Give each player 1 Player Board, 1 Player Aid, and 1 random Development Tile of each of the 5 types (slotted undeveloped-side up).
2. In their colour: 16 Workers (6 in Supply, rest to the Main Supply); 1 Tent on the top-centre Map space; 1 Altar Marker on the Altar Track's 0 space (stacked in descending player order, Shofar holder's marker on top); 1 VP Marker on the Score Track's 10VP space; 10 Character Cards shuffled facedown, drawing 4 to hand; 12 Wall Cards shuffled facedown.
3. Take the Resources shown on the bottom-left of their Guards Tile onto 2 of its spaces (from the Main Supply). Each player also gains 5 Silver and 3 Food.
4. Give each player 1 of each Blessing Marker, placed on the 0 spaces of their tracks.

**Levite setup:** Place 1 Worker of each player's colour from the Main Supply on the designated space at the bottom of the Levite Track (near the bottom-left of the Main Board). Each player may have up to 6 Levites, placed bottom to top in their own column.

**Gatekeeper setup:** In reverse turn order (starting to the right of the Shofar holder, counter-clockwise), each player places 1 Worker of their colour on a chosen corner Gate (Old Gate, Sheep Gate, Horse Gate, Fountain Gate).
- **1-2 players:** Each player places 2 Gatekeepers, placing their first before either places their second.
- **3 players:** 1 corner Gate remains neutral/empty (a White Worker may optionally go there).

**White Worker setup (1-2 players only):** Place 1 White Worker on each of the 2 outer Scroll Tiles of the bottom row.

**Component limits:** Resources, Silver, and Food are unlimited (use substitutes if they run out). Workers are limited — never more than 16 per colour.

**Hand limit:** After drawing 4 Character Cards during setup, players draw a new card each turn and should hold 4 in hand between turns, unless their Draw Pile is insufficient.

## Turn Structure

The game runs 3 Rounds, each a 7-day week: 6 work days followed by a Sabbath.

**Work days:** Starting with the Shofar holder and proceeding clockwise, each player takes one turn per day until all players have taken 6 turns. On each turn, in order:
1. Play 1 Character Card from hand to 1 of the 3 piles on the Player Board (must happen before the Main Action; each pile holds at most 2 Character Cards — the top card is hidden/covered, but players may look at their own covered cards anytime).
2. Take 1 of the 3 Main Actions.
3. Draw 1 Character Card from the Draw Pile, if possible (in the final Round, players may run out and simply not draw).

Additionally, at any point during the turn, a player may also: take 1 Auxiliary Action, use 1 Trader, and use 1 Elder (each at most once per turn).

**Prepare for Sabbath** (after all players' 6th turn, resolved simultaneously; timing disputes resolve in turn order from the Shofar holder):
- Assign any Workers remaining in Supply as Farmers or Labourers (as many as available); each produces its printed Resources/Food/Silver (Labourers choose 1 of 2 printed options).
- Resolve the Prophets' Judgment (see Special Rules).
- Resolve any other applicable effects, in any order.

**Sabbath** (simultaneous):
- Feed all Workers except Levites and unassigned Main Supply workers: pay 1 Food each. For each Worker that can't be fed, lose 2VP immediately (score can go below 0VP).
- Tuck 1 Character Card (Rounds 1 and 3) or 2 Character Cards (Round 2) from the Player Board (not hand) beneath the Player Aid.
- Score all tucked Character Cards (their Sabbath scoring conditions), including cards tucked in prior Sabbaths.

**After Sabbath** (Rounds 1-2 only; Round 3 goes straight to end-game scoring):
1. Advance the Prophets on the Altar Track to their next-Round positions.
2. Pass the Shofar clockwise to the next starting player.
3. Retrieve all Workers from the Player Board (including Traders, Elders, Farmers, Labourers) back to Supply — never to the Main Supply.
4. The player furthest right on the Altar Track (the Altar Leader; ties broken by lower marker = further ahead) gains 1 Worker from the Main Supply.
5. Shuffle remaining (untucked) Character Cards into a new Draw Pile. After Round 2 specifically, also draw 1 Character Card to hand (players hold only 3 cards at that point).

## Actions

**Character Cards:** Each has 3 Banners (colored icons usable while visible on the Player Board — a printed number is its value, otherwise it's worth 1), a Gate name with a Trade (usable via Auxiliary Action only on the turn the card is played), and a Sabbath scoring condition (activates once tucked).

**Main Actions** (choose exactly 1 per turn; each requires Banners of a single colour, tallied from: visible Character Cards on the Player Board, the top Banner of tucked Character Cards and Hero Cards, Traders/Elders used this turn, and relevant Scroll Tile abilities — unused Banners are simply wasted):

- **Temple and Altar (Red Banners):** May place 1 Levite before or after the action (never during), or place none.
  - *Placing a Levite:* Requires an available Worker; pay the Food cost shown for the next empty row in the player's Levite column, then place the Worker there and gain the printed reward immediately (varies by colour, e.g. Teal/Red = 2 Food for 1 Cinders; Purple = 3 Food for 1 Stone; Yellow = 5 Food for a wild Blessing + 2VP). Max 6 Levites.
  - *Using Levites:* Each Levite may be used once, either to place 1 Resource (in the Temple or on the Altar) or to grant 1 additional Red Banner this turn (using it for a Banner means it can't also place a Resource).
  - *Temple placement:* Requires 1-5 Red Banners (shown down the Temple's side) per Resource; place only Stone, Wood, or Gold (not Cinders) into the left-most empty column of the chosen row. Stone/Wood = 1VP each, Gold = 2VP each, plus the reward printed for that row: 5-Banner row = 1 Worker; 4-Banner row = 1 Tent movement; 3-Banner row = 1 Food; 2-Banner row = 1 Silver; 1-Banner row = no reward. Once full, no more Resources may be placed in the Temple.
  - *Filling a Temple Sector* (3 Sectors total, resolved immediately when the last space in a Sector is filled): (1) the player who placed the final Resource gains 2 Food; (2) the player with the most Levites gains 2VP (ties broken by Altar Track position, further ahead wins); (3) the player with the fewest Levites loses 1VP (ties broken by Altar Track position, further behind loses; ignored in 1-2 player games); (4) every player gains 1 Silver per Levite they have.
  - *Altar placement:* Requires 1 or 3 Red Banners (shown above the Altar) and only Wood or Cinders. Each Wood costs 3 Red Banners and moves 2 spaces on the Altar Track; each Cinders costs 1 Red Banner and moves 1 space. Placed Resources may be returned to the Main Supply immediately or kept for theme. Moving onto/past a space grants all rewards shown there; landing on another player's Altar Marker places yours on top. If already at the Altar Track's end, gain 1 Silver per movement instead of moving. Altar Track position is the game's universal tie-break rule (a marker on top of a stack is considered ahead of markers beneath it).

- **Rubble, Walls, and Gates (Grey Banners):** Choose exactly 1 Wall or Gate space per turn; may both clear rubble from it and build on it in the same turn.
  - *Clearing rubble:* Costs Grey Banners per Resource — Cinders 1, Stone/Wood 2, Gold 3. Clear as much or as little as affordable; cleared Resources go straight to the player's Supply and may be used immediately, even to build the same space. Fully clearing a Wall space's rubble grants a Blessing of the indicated colour (partial clearing does not; clearing a Gate never grants a Blessing).
  - *Building:* Requires the Grey Banners and Resources printed on the space (Grey Banners just spent clearing rubble cannot be reused). All Gates cost 3 Grey Banners, 1 Wood, 2 Stone. Discount: pay 3 Grey Banners or 1 Gold (any number of times) to reduce the Stone/Wood cost by 1 each time.
  - *Building a Wall:* Draw 3 Wall Cards from the Draw Pile; place 1 over the Wall space (resolving its immediate central effect, no Blessing for this), then place 1 of the other 2 on top of the Wall Card Draw Pile and the last on the bottom (draw from what's available if fewer than 3 remain).
  - *Building a Gate:* Move the Gate Card onto the built space, collecting any Silver on it. Place 1 Worker from Supply onto the Gate Card's outlined space (this Worker becomes a permanent Gatekeeper) and gain its printed immediate reward. Requires an available Worker. Gate Card rewards (top of the card) are only gained via connections, not on building.
  - *Connections:* Building a Wall next to an already-built Gate, or a Gate next to an already-built Wall (order doesn't matter), triggers the reward printed across the top of that Gate for all players who own the connecting pieces. Owning both the Wall and the Gate grants the reward once, not twice. A Gate flanked by 2 previously built Walls can trigger the connection effect twice total over the game. Neutral Walls/Gates (present in <4-player setups) cannot be interacted with; with 3 players, building the Muster or East Gate does not connect to the adjacent Neutral Walls.
  - *Wall/Gate VP:* printed in the bottom-left corner of Wall/Gate Cards; scored only at game end.

- **Torah and Tenting Around the City (Blue Banners):** May place 1 Scribe on a Scroll Tile, move the Tent, or do both (either order).
  - *Teaching the Torah:* Place a Worker (becomes a Scribe, permanent) on an accessible Scroll Tile, paying Silver/Gold plus the row's Blue Banner cost (bottom row: 3 Blue Banners + 2 Silver; next row: 5 Blue Banners + 3 Silver; next: 6 Blue Banners + 1 Gold; top row: 7 Blue Banners + 2 Gold). Only bottom-row tiles are initially reachable; a placed Scribe unlocks the 2 tiles diagonally above it (not tiles horizontally adjacent). Each Scroll Tile holds only 1 Scribe. Branching off another player's Scribe costs 1 Silver paid to that Scribe's owner (no cost to branch off your own Scribe; choose which owner to pay if multiple options exist).
  - *Row Bonus:* The first player to place a Scribe in any of the top 3 rows immediately gains all Food placed to the right of that row during setup.
  - *Scoring Scribes:* At game end, each Scribe scores VP per its row's printed value; top-row Scroll Tiles also score their unique printed condition (in addition to the 4VP row value).
  - *White Workers (1-2 players):* Branching off a White Worker costs 1 Silver to the Main Supply (no extra cost to place directly on its tile). Doing so moves that White Worker to the next tile above it, hugging the outside edge; reaching the top row returns it to the box. White Workers do not trigger the Row Bonus.
  - *Tenting:* Move the Tent 1-4 spaces clockwise around the Map, costing Blue Banners (and 1 Cinders/Stone/Wood if moving 3-4 spaces), gaining every reward crossed (Blessings, Silver, Gold, Food, Workers, Altar Track movement, VP). Movement must be done as a single action — banner costs cannot be split across separate movements or combined with different cost tiers to move further. Any number of Tents may share a space.
  - *Special Map icon rule:* Any time a player would gain a Worker or Blessing but cannot, they instead move their Tent 1 space.

**Auxiliary Action** (at most 1 per turn, any time, including before the Character Card is played): Trade or turn over a Development Tile.
- *Trade:* Two types on Character Cards. "Silver-for-Resource" Trades require paying 1 Silver first to the Gate's owner (the player with a built Gatekeeper there); if unbuilt, pay onto the Gate Card instead; if the trading player owns the Gate, skip this payment; if the Gate is neutral, pay the Main Supply. Then take 1 of the 2 printed Trade options (top or bottom), paying any additional Silver to the Main Supply. "Resource-for-Silver" Trades require no Gatekeeper payment and may be used any number of times per turn.
- *Turn over a Development Tile:* Pay the Resources shown top-left to the Main Supply, gain the reward on the green bar, then flip the tile. Each flipped tile is worth 2VP at game end. Workers already placed as Traders/Elders on a tile do not move or retroactively gain new benefits when it flips.
- *Guards Tile resource limit:* Until a player's Guards Tile is flipped, they have a limited storage capacity; any excess Resources at the end of any turn (including other players' turns and the Sabbath) must be returned to the Main Supply (player's choice which). Exceeding the limit mid-turn is fine. Flipping the Guards Tile removes this limit.

**Using a Trader / Elder** (at most 1 of each per turn): Place 1 Worker from Supply onto 1 of 4 Trader spaces or 4 Elder spaces on the Player Board (each space holds only 1 Worker). They remain there until end of Round (unless retrieved by an effect) and return to Supply after each Sabbath. Traders provide Silver/Food/Resources (plus a new effect once flipped); Elders provide extra Banners (plus Cinders/Food/Silver via some tiles).

**Blessings:** Any time a specific Blessing is gained, advance that Blessing Marker 1 space right on the Player Board; a "wild" Blessing lets the player choose which marker to advance. A full set (1 of each of the 3 colours, not necessarily aligned in the same column) grants the column bonus above the left-most Blessing Marker whenever completed. Any time a Blessing would be gained but can't, move the Tent 1 space instead.

## Scoring / Victory Conditions

The game ends after the 3rd Sabbath. End-game scoring:
1. Convert all remaining Gold, Stone, Wood, Cinders, and Food into Silver value (does not need to happen physically): Gold = 3 Silver; Stone/Wood/Food = 2 Silver; Cinders = 1 Silver.
2. Score, on the Score Track: 1VP per 5 Silver; 2VP per flipped Development Tile; 3VP for the Altar Leader (furthest right on the Altar Track); all printed VP on built Walls and Gates; all VP from Scribes (per their row's value); all VP from top-row Scroll Tile unique scoring conditions.
3. Highest total VP wins. Ties are broken by Altar Track position (furthest right wins; a marker on top of a stack counts as ahead of markers beneath it).

There are two VP icon types printed throughout the game: a Silver-coloured icon scores immediately when triggered, and a Gold-coloured icon scores only at game end. Scores may exceed 99VP — continue circling the Score Track while tracking the extra 100s.

**Solo mode victory:** The player wins by scoring higher than the automated opponent; a tie is won by whoever is the Altar Leader.

## Special Rules & Edge Cases

- **Prophets' Judgment** (resolved every Prepare-for-Sabbath step): Each player resolves the Altar Card effect for their Altar Marker's position relative to the Prophets Zechariah and Haggai (behind Zechariah, between the two Prophets, or ahead of Haggai) for the current Round. Effects mirror standard rewards (Food, Resources, VP) with two special icons: a forced Food loss (converting each unpayable Food into a 2VP loss instead) and effects that let a player retrieve 1 Trader/Elder (must be immediately reassigned as a Farmer/Labourer since this happens during Prepare for Sabbath) or flip 1 Development Tile for free (paying no cost but still gaining the benefit). The Prophets move to new Altar Track positions after each Sabbath.
- **Levites are not fed** during Sabbath (thematically, they receive a portion of Temple offerings), unlike all other Worker types on the board.
- **Component shortages:** Resources, Silver, and Food are unlimited — substitute if physical components run out. Only Workers (16 per colour) are a hard limit.
- **Golden Altar Track tie-break rule** applies to all tie situations in the game, including final scoring, not just Altar-specific effects.
- **Neutral Walls/Gates** (set up in games with fewer than 4 players) can never be interacted with by any player; with 3 players specifically, building the Muster Gate or East Gate does not create a wall connection to the adjacent Neutral Walls.

### Solo Mode

Solo mode uses the same rules as multiplayer, with an automated opponent controlled via Focus Cards and Scheme Cards.

- **Setup:** Follow standard setup (give the opponent the Shofar). Give the opponent the Solo Board (start with the 1-star side for a standard difficulty; the other side is a harder variant for later games) and Solo Player Aid, shuffled Focus Cards face down on the Lion space and Scheme Cards face down on the Lamb space, the Worker count shown next to the Focus Cards (rest to Main Supply), 1 Tent, 1 VP Marker on 10VP, 1 Altar Marker on 0 (stacked on top of the human player's, since the opponent holds the Shofar), 12 shuffled Wall Cards, Silver/Gold as shown, 1 of each Blessing Marker, and 1 starting Levite. The human player places their 2 Gatekeepers on any corner Gates; the opponent's remaining 2 corner Gates receive 1 Worker of the opponent's colour each. White Workers are used as normal.
- **Opponent resource conversion rules:** The opponent never collects Stone, Wood, or Cinders (converts each to 1 Gold instead when it would gain any) nor Food (converts each to 1 Silver instead). It also never needs to feed Workers at Sabbath. When the opponent moves its Tent and would gain 2 Food, it gains 2 Silver instead. On the Altar Track, Food gains convert to Silver gains.
- **Silver payments:** The human player pays the opponent's Supply directly (e.g., branching off its Scribes, using its Gates); when the opponent must branch off the human's Scribes, it pays nothing but the human still gains 1 Silver from the Main Supply. When the opponent uses the human's Gates, it pays the human's Supply.
- **Wild Blessings for the opponent:** Move its left-most Blessing Marker; if there's a choice, prioritize Orange over Purple, and Purple over Red.
- **Special icon effects for the opponent:** the "gain Worker/Blessing but can't" rule still moves its Tent 1 space; it also moves its Tent 1 space instead of retrieving an Elder/Trader when an effect calls for that (e.g., placing its 6th Levite).
- **Opponent turn sequence:** (1) Flip 1 Focus Card, covering any previous one — its top shows a target Gate and a directional arrow indicating which Gates/Walls/Scroll Tiles to prioritize. (2) Make 1 Trade (spending as much Silver as possible, following standard Trade rules including Gatekeeper payments) or, if unable, move the Tent 1 space instead. (3) Flip 1 Scheme Card, covering any previous one; each has 3 rows of possible actions. (4) Resolve the top-most action on the Scheme Card whose requirements (shown left of an arrow) are met; if none met, move down the list. There are only 6 unique Scheme Cards, and the opponent cycles through all 6 each Round.
  - Requirements listed are what's *needed*, not necessarily what's *spent* — the action itself dictates the spend.
- **Opponent Temple/Altar actions:** Pays 2 Gold to place a Levite if possible (ignoring the Food cost but still gaining the row reward, receiving Gold instead of Stone/Wood/Cinders; skipped if already at 6 Levites). Its final (6th) Levite gives 3VP and moves the Tent 1 space instead of retrieving a Trader/Elder. For each Levite, in order, it will place 1 Gold in the Temple, spend 1 Silver for 1 Gold, or move 1 space on the Altar Track (resolved one Levite at a time, since one Levite's Gold/Silver gain can fund the next). Placed Gold always fills the left-most incomplete Temple column from the bottom; each Gold placed earns 2VP plus all row rewards (Food conversions apply); filled Sectors resolve immediately using the standard rules.
- **Opponent Rubble/Walls/Gates actions:** Keeps any Gold cleared; returns Stone/Wood/Cinders cleared to the Main Supply for 1 Gold each; gains the Blessing for fully clearing a Wall; only clears a Gate space if also building that Gate. To build, it pays a flat 3 Gold to the Main Supply (ignoring the board's printed cost), using any Gold just gained from clearing; a Gate build still needs an available Worker. It gains connection rewards normally. It always builds Wall spaces with rubble before those without, and always uses the top card of its Wall Draw Pile. Target space is set by the Focus Card's listed Gate; if that Gate is already built (or it's not building a Gate), it moves clockwise or counter-clockwise (per the Focus Card's arrow) to the first legal space.
  - *Edge cases:* If all Gates are built and it's instructed to build a Gate, skip to the next Scheme Card action. If instructed to build a Wall and no space has rubble, it will still build (from scratch) only if it has 3+ Gold to spend.
- **Opponent Torah actions:** Places a Scribe on the highest or lowest possible Scroll Tile as instructed, ignoring printed row costs — instead paying 1-4 Gold equal to the row's VP value minus 1 Gold per fully filled row beneath it (a row counts as filled only when every tile holds a non-White Worker). If it can't afford the target tile, it tries a cheaper eligible option before giving up the action for this turn. It ignores all Scroll Tile icons/immediate VP, but does collect and resolve Hero Cards (which provide no further ongoing benefit to it). If it's first to reach the higher rows, remove the setup Food from that row's right side and give the opponent 1 Silver per Food removed. When multiple eligible tiles exist, the Focus Card's arrow position picks among them (dividing the card into 2, 3, 4, or, ignoring middle option(s), 5-6 target zones). It prefers branching from its own Scribes; will only branch off a White Worker if it can spend 1 Silver (preferred over branching off a human Scribe); when forced to branch off a human Scribe it pays nothing but the human gains 1 Silver from the Main Supply (the human is still paid normally for the opponent branching off their Scribes).
- **Opponent Sabbath:** Resolves Prophets' Judgment using its own Solo Board conditions. In Rounds 1-2, all 10 Focus Cards and 6 Scheme Cards are reshuffled into fresh Draw Piles (Prophets still move, Shofar still passes, and it still gains a Worker if Altar Leader). It scores like a normal player except it never scores Development Tiles (has none) or Scroll Tile printed scoring conditions. Its own Sabbath scoring (varies by Solo Board side A/B): (1) 1 Silver (Side B: 2 Silver) per Worker still in Supply; (2) VP equal to the row value of its highest-row Scroll Tile; (3) 1VP per Gatekeeper/Scribe/Levite of whichever type it has most of (Side B: 1VP for each of all three types); (4) VP equal to its right-most Blessing's value, plus 1VP per golden Altar Track space reached or passed. It does not need to feed Workers.
