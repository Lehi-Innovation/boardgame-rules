## Overview

Deadwood 1876 is a hidden-team bluffing game set during the Black Hills gold
rush. Players use guns from their hand to rob Safes (containing Gold,
Badges, or Showdown Guns) from other players and from shared Heists.
Players sharing an establishment (platform) are secretly on the same team.
After a fixed number of turns, the establishment with the most combined
Gold advances to a Final Showdown, where teammates turn on each other and
fight to the death — the last player alive keeps all the Gold and wins.

## Components

- 3 Wooden Platforms (establishments: Gem Theatre, Saloon, Grand Central Hotel; remove Grand Central Hotel in 2-3 player games)
- 9 Wooden Star Pawns (one per player, represents player location)
- 9 Character Cards (one per player; flavor/identity only, flip to black-and-white side when defeated in the Final Showdown)
- 10 Wooden Dice, one type per gun: Messenger, Winchester, Colt, Deringer, Pepperbox (each gun has its own die with different value distribution — stronger dice win shootouts more often)
- Linen bag (for randomly assigning starting stars)
- 25 Safe cards: 5 Badges, 5 Mystery Safes (silver border, optional), 7 Guns (Showdown Guns), 8 Gold Values
- 50 Deadwood cards, each playable as EITHER a gun (top) or a special item (bottom): 10 Horse, 12 Duster, 13 Stetson, 15 Holster
- 2 Reference cards

## Setup

1. Place the establishment platforms in the center. **2-3 players:** remove the Grand Central Hotel (2 establishments only).
2. Each player takes a character card and its matching star. Place matching stars in the linen bag; starting with the Gem Theatre and moving clockwise, draw stars one at a time and place them into establishments to randomly assign starting positions. **4-player games:** do not place any stars in the Grand Central Hotel at setup (players may still move there later).
3. Shuffle the Deadwood card deck; deal 4 cards to each player (3 cards each in 8-9 player games). Remaining cards form a face-down draw deck.
4. Remove the 5 silver-bordered Mystery Safes from the Safe deck (set aside; only added back in for the optional Mystery Safes variant). Shuffle the remaining Safes. Place 3 in a face-down stack in the middle (2 in 8-9 player games) — these are the Heist stack. Deal 2 Safes face-down to each player. Return any leftover Safes to the box unseen; they are out of the game.
5. Each player privately views their 2 Safes, chooses one, and passes it face-down to the player on their left. Everyone views and shuffles their new pair of Safes, then lays them face-down in a row in front of them.

## Turn Structure

Everyone rolls the black (Messenger) die; highest roller goes first, play proceeds clockwise.

On your turn, play exactly 1 Deadwood card from hand, using either:
- **Its special item** (bottom of card — Horse, Duster, Stetson, or Holster; Holster is the exception, see below), or
- **Its gun** (top of card) to start a **Robbery** or a **Duel**.

After your turn, discard all cards used in it. Everyone who played a card that turn (attacker, defender, and anyone who gave a Holster) draws back up to a full hand of 4 (3 in 8-9 player games). Reshuffle the discard pile into the deck whenever it runs out.

**Heist** — each time the last player in turn order finishes their turn, immediately resolve a Heist: reveal the top Safe from the Heist stack, every player simultaneously plays a gun from hand and rolls its die, and the highest roller takes the Safe (reroll ties) and shuffles it into their row. Played guns are discarded and replaced before play continues.

The game runs for a fixed number of turns: after the Heist stack is exhausted (its final Heist resolved), each player gets exactly one more turn — 4 total turns per player (8 in 2-3 player games, going around the circle twice per Heist cycle; 3 in 8-9 player games).

## Actions

### Robbery
Lay a gun face-up in front of a specific Safe belonging to another player. The defender must play a gun from hand to defend. Both roll their gun's die simultaneously (reroll ties). Higher result wins the Safe — the attacker (if they win) privately views it and shuffles it into their row; if the defender wins, nothing changes and no shuffle occurs.

### Duel
Lay a gun face-up in front of another player's character card; they must defend with a gun from hand. Roll simultaneously (reroll ties). If the attacker wins, swap star positions with the defender — unless the defender was in the attacker's own establishment, in which case the attacker instead sends the defender to any establishment with a vacancy. If the defender wins, nothing happens.

**Establishment capacity:** 2/establishment (2-5 players), 3/establishment (6-8 players), 4/establishment (9 players).

### Special Items (Deadwood cards)
- **Horse:** Move your star to any establishment with a vacancy.
- **Duster:** Discard it, draw 4 cards, then discard back down to a hand of 4 (7→discard 3; in 8-9 player games, 6→discard 3, ending with 3).
- **Stetson:** Privately view any 2 Safe cards (need not belong to the same player, and can be your own); replace face-down in their original position without revealing them to anyone else. Cannot view Safes still in a future Heist or Safes removed from play.
- **Holster:** Played out-of-turn by a non-combatant to assist either side of a Robbery/Duel roll before dice are rolled. The assisted player rolls both their played gun's die and the Holster's die and sums them. Only one Holster may be used per combatant; if offered multiple, the recipient picks one and returns the rest, and cannot refuse to use at least one if offered. Holsters cannot be used in Heists or the Final Showdown. If a roll happens before a Holster is offered, that roll stands (the opponent can still receive one).

## Scoring / Victory Conditions

**Badge Round** (after the last player's final turn): players reveal Badges one at a time, taking an extra turn as each is revealed, in fixed order — Tin, Iron, Copper, Silver, Gold. Skip any badge nobody holds. Owning multiple Badges grants multiple extra turns. The Safe-limit law (see below) no longer applies once the Badge Round starts.

**Final Showdown:** All remaining Safes are revealed. Gold values are summed per establishment; the establishment(s) with the most combined Gold advance to the Final Showdown (ties: the tied establishment with fewer players advances; if still tied, all tied establishments advance together). Non-advancing players are eliminated.

- If only one player advances, **that player wins immediately**.
- If multiple players advance, they fight to the death: each simultaneously plays a card from hand as a gun and rolls its die plus the die for every Showdown Gun they hold (Showdown Guns apply to every roll for the rest of the Final Showdown). Lowest total flips their character card to its black-and-white side (reroll ties). A player who loses again while already flipped is eliminated. Once Deadwood cards stop being replenished (they aren't refilled during the Final Showdown), surviving players pick up whatever hand they had left and keep fighting with it.
- **Last player standing keeps all the Gold and wins the game.**

### The 3 Laws of Deadwood
1. You may only shuffle your Safes immediately after gaining one via Robbery or Heist; otherwise they stay face-down in their existing order (you may still privately view your own Safes anytime).
2. Never show your Safes or hand to anyone — verbal bluffing and deception about their contents is expected and encouraged.
3. **Three-Safe limit:** if you would gain a 4th Safe (Robbery or Heist), you must immediately give any one of your Safes to the player with the fewest Safes (your choice if tied). The recipient may not shuffle it in at that time. This law stops applying once the Badge Round begins.

## Special Rules & Edge Cases

- **Establishments are hidden teams:** players in the same establishment at game end share their Gold total and, if they advance, must still fight each other to the death in the Final Showdown.
- Revealed Badges have no Gold value and cannot be attacked; if a Badge is stolen before its owner uses it, the extra turn goes to the new owner instead.
- Gun die strength ranks Messenger (strongest) > Winchester > Colt > Deringer > Pepperbox (weakest); rulebook gives full head-to-head win-probability odds between each pair.
- **Optional Mystery Safes variant:** shuffle in any number of the 5 silver-bordered Mystery Safes at setup. A starred Mystery Safe (Dagger, Explosives, or Poker Deck) must be revealed and resolved right after the final Badge Round turn, before other Safes are revealed; resolve multiple in alphabetical order.
  - **Bad Sarsaparilla:** owner cannot use their best Showdown Gun in the Final Showdown (no effect if they have none, don't advance, or hold the Dagger).
  - **Cash:** owner auto-advances to the Final Showdown regardless of their establishment's Gold total (their Gold still counts toward their team). In 2-player games, if Cash would bring the Stranger into a 3-way Final Showdown, the Stranger is removed instead.
  - **Dagger (starred):** owner chooses either −1 Gold plus a Pepperbox Showdown Gun, or +1 Gold plus a *negative* Pepperbox Showdown Gun (its die result is subtracted from every Final Showdown roll, including tiebreaks).
  - **Explosives (starred):** owner removes one other player's unrevealed Safe from the game entirely, shown to all.
  - **Poker Deck (starred):** owner plays a gun and rolls its die, then rolls a red die — a result of 0-1 subtracts, 2-3 adds, the first die's value to/from the owner's Gold total.
- **Optional "Peace-Lovin'" team victory:** if nobody advancing owns a Showdown Gun, all advancing players win together instead of fighting.
- **Optional "Fair Share" team victory (6-9 players only):** if every advancing player has at least 1 Gold, they all win together; any advancer at 0 or negative Gold forces a normal fight to the death.
- **Optional Horse Thief rule (not for 2-3 player games):** a white Horse can be played immediately after attacker/defender lay guns in a Robbery to roll a 3rd-party bid for the Safe (highest of all three wins, ties reroll). Horse Thieves can't give/receive Holsters, still follow the 3 Laws, and can't be used during the Badge Round.
- **2-3 player adjustments:** go around the circle twice per Heist cycle (8 total turns per player instead of 4); Stetsons reveal only 1 Safe instead of 2; Holsters can't be played out-of-turn — instead, on your turn you attach one to a Safe or character card to auto-apply on its next attack/Duel, discarding after use (only one Holster per Safe at a time; Safes with an attached Holster can't be shuffled).
- **2-player "Stranger":** a rolled-off Stranger character with 2 Safes and no turns is added to one establishment; it can be attacked/Dueled by the human opponent acting in its defense. If the Final Showdown includes the Stranger, the opponent uses their own hand plus the Stranger's Showdown Guns; if you win, you win outright; if the Stranger alone advances, both human players lose and are "banned from playing for seven days" (a joke penalty, not enforced).

## Player Reference

**Turn:** Play 1 Deadwood card as either its special item OR its gun (Robbery or Duel).
**Gun strength (best→worst):** Messenger > Winchester > Colt > Deringer > Pepperbox.
**Heist:** Triggers each time the last player in turn order finishes a turn — reveal top Safe, all play a gun and roll, highest wins it.
**Game length:** 4 turns/player (8 in 2-3 player games; 3 in 8-9 player games), then the Badge Round, then the Final Showdown.
**Badge reveal order:** Tin → Iron → Copper → Silver → Gold (each grants an extra turn to its owner).
**Win:** Be in the establishment with the most Gold, then be the last one standing in the Final Showdown (or the only one to advance).
**3 Laws:** (1) shuffle Safes only right after gaining one; (2) never reveal Safes/hand; (3) max 3 Safes — give away the extra if you'd exceed it (until the Badge Round).
