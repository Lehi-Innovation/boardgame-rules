## Overview

Collect gem tokens, buy development cards that provide permanent purchase discounts, and attract nobles. Prestige points on purchased cards and acquired nobles determine the winner. Reaching **15 points triggers the final round**; it does not guarantee victory.

This summary follows the English 2024 artwork refresh of the base game. It includes the first-player marker supplied with that printing. [Source rulebook](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-spacecowboys/uploads/2025/10/SCSPL01EN_SPLENDOR_RULES_LIGHT.pdf), PDF pages 1–2.

## Components

- **40 tokens:** 7 each of white diamonds, blue sapphires, green emeralds, red rubies, and black onyx; plus 5 yellow gold tokens.
- **90 development cards:** 40 level I, 30 level II, and 20 level III.
- **10 noble tiles**, 1 first-player marker, and the rule sheet.

Each development card has a gem cost, a permanent bonus of one color, and sometimes prestige points. Nobles show the colors and numbers of card bonuses needed to attract them. Tokens and card bonuses serve different purposes: tokens pay costs, while bonuses reduce them.

## Setup

1. Shuffle each development level separately. Arrange the three decks vertically and reveal **4 cards from each**, forming a 12-card market.
2. Shuffle the nobles and reveal **number of players + 1**. Return all other nobles to the box.
3. Form a public supply of gem and gold tokens, adjusted as follows.
4. Give the youngest player the first-player marker. That player starts; play proceeds clockwise. Players begin without tokens or cards.

| Players | Tokens of each ordinary gem | Gold tokens | Nobles |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 | 4 | 5 | 3 |
| 3 | 5 | 5 | 4 |
| 4 | 7 | 5 | 5 |

See PDF page 2, Setup.

## Turn Structure

Take **exactly one** of the four actions below. Replace any market card removed from its row immediately, if its deck has cards remaining. Check whether you qualify for a noble at the end of your turn and take at most one. Also return excess tokens so that you finish with **at most 10**, counting gold.

All token holdings, purchased cards, bonuses, nobles, and prestige scores remain visible. Reserved cards are held in your hand. The game continues clockwise until the final-round condition is resolved.

## Actions

### Take gems of different colors

Take **3 ordinary gem tokens of different colors**. If the supply cannot provide three different colors, take two or one as available. Gold cannot be taken with this action.

### Take two matching gems

Take **2 ordinary gem tokens of one color**, but only if **at least 4** tokens of that color were in the supply before taking them. This action cannot take gold.

### Reserve a development card

Take either one face-up market card or the top card of a development deck. Keep the reserved card in your hand; a blindly reserved card is not shown to other players. Then take **1 gold token** if available.

- The reservation limit is **3 cards**. At that limit, you cannot reserve another card, even if you intend to buy it later.
- Reserving remains legal when no gold is available.
- Reserved cards cannot be discarded; purchasing them is the only way to remove them from your hand.
- Unpurchased reserved cards cause no end-game penalty.

### Purchase a development card

Buy one face-up market card or one of your reserved cards. First subtract your permanent bonuses of each color from the corresponding cost, to a minimum of zero for each color. Pay the remaining cost with tokens, returning payment to the supply.

Each gold token substitutes for one gem of any color. You may spend gold even when you hold the matching ordinary gem. Place the purchased card face up in your tableau; its bonus remains available for subsequent purchases. Bonuses are never spent. Excess discounts do not generate tokens. A fully discounted card still requires this purchase action.

### Attract a noble

At the end of your turn, compare your **card bonuses**, not your tokens, with the nobles' requirements. If you qualify, acquiring a noble is mandatory and uses no action. If several qualify, choose **one**. Keep its points permanently; you do not spend or surrender the qualifying cards. Additional nobles must wait until later turns.

See PDF page 2, Actions, Bonuses, Noble Tiles, and Piece Pool Limit.

## Scoring / Victory Conditions

Your score is the total prestige on purchased development cards and acquired nobles. When a player has **15 or more** at the end of their turn, finish the round so that every player has taken the same number of turns. The player immediately before the first player takes the last turn.

Highest prestige wins. If tied, the tied player with the **fewest purchased development cards** wins. If still tied, those players share victory. Reserved cards are not purchased cards and do not contribute points or bonuses. See PDF page 2, End of the Game.

## Special Rules & Edge Cases

- You may temporarily exceed 10 tokens during your turn. At its end return any excess tokens of your choice, including tokens just taken; gold counts toward the limit.
- Ordinary gem-taking actions never give gold. Reservation is the normal source of gold.
- The four-token condition for taking a pair is checked **before** removal: a supply of three does not permit taking two.
- A depleted development deck leaves fewer than four cards in its market row. Do not replace from another level.
- Each purchased card supplies one permanent bonus, regardless of how many prestige points it shows. Noble requirements count these bonuses only.
- A noble is acquired automatically when eligible, but never more than one per turn. Eligibility does not disappear when you acquire a different noble because bonuses remain yours.
- Hitting 15 is a round-ending trigger. Later players can overtake the triggering player's score.
