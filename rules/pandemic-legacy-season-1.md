## Overview

Pandemic Legacy: Season 1 is a cooperative game — all players win or lose together — in which the team works to develop cures and prevent outbreaks before 4 diseases contaminate humanity. One disease has shown signs that it may become resistant to any treatment. The game is a Legacy campaign of 12 game months, January through December: succeed in a month and you move on to the next month; fail and you get exactly one more chance at that month (a fresh game with the same rules and objectives) before moving on regardless.

Unlike games that fully reset, some elements carry forward permanently: you will write on the board, put stickers on cards and the board, and sometimes destroy (tear up) cards. Some rules, labeled A through Y, are missing from the rulebook and are gained as stickers during the campaign, permanently changing the rules. Sealed packages (8, numbered) and 5 dossiers hide content that is revealed only when the game instructs you to open them — never open or peek early. At the end of every game (win or lose) the group makes two Game End Upgrades. In all other ways the game resets each play: pieces on the board, cards in hand, outbreaks, etc. all start fresh.

If you have never played Pandemic, the rulebook recommends at least two warm-up games using none of the Legacy rules (skip Game Months, Legacy Deck, Dossiers, Objectives, Funding, Game Calendar, Panic Levels, Scars and Lost Characters, and Game End Upgrades); the object of those warm-ups is simply to cure the four diseases before any game-losing condition triggers.

## Components

- 1 board (world map; divided into six regions, each roughly a continent, which come into play later)
- 5 Character cards
- 61 Player cards (48 City cards, 5 Epidemic cards, 8 Event cards)
- 62 Legacy cards (arranged in a fixed order — never look through or shuffle them)
- 96 Disease cubes (24 in each of 4 colors)
- 4 Cure markers (vial side / eradicated side)
- 48 Infection cards
- 4 Pawns and 4 Reference cards (one per player color)
- 6 Research stations
- 1 Infection rate marker and 1 Outbreaks marker
- 4 Civilian cards
- 1 Reminder token (used occasionally to remind players of something)
- 5 Dossiers and 1 Sticker sheet
- 1 Package box with 8 numbered packages (open only when instructed)
- You will need a pen — not included in the box.

## Setup

1. **Read the mission briefing.** When playing a month for the first time, draw cards off the top of the Legacy Deck one at a time until you reach a card that says STOP on the back (it tells you when to continue drawing — mid-game, at game end, or at the start of the next month). Read the mission briefing and integrate any new cards. Some setup steps are missing at first and will be added later in the campaign. If replaying the same month, your mission briefing and objectives are the same — do not draw or replace Legacy cards.
2. **Set out the board and pieces.** Put all structures nearby (initially you only have research stations). Separate the cubes by color into 4 supply piles. Place a research station in each city with a starting research station sticker — you begin the campaign with one in Atlanta.
3. **Place outbreaks marker, cure markers, and objectives.** Outbreaks marker on the "0" space of the Outbreaks Track; the 4 cure markers vial side up at the bottom of the disease tracking area; all current objectives in the objectives area of the board.
4. **Select Funded Events and deal Player cards.** As a group, decide which Funded Event cards go into the Player Deck — the deck can have as many Funded Event cards as your current Funding Level (4 for your first game). Shuffle these Event cards and the City cards together and deal each player an initial hand, giving cards according to the number of players (per the setup table in the rulebook). In Pandemic Legacy, players keep their hands faceup at all times.
5. **Prepare the Player Deck.** Divide the remaining Player cards into 5 facedown piles, as equal in size as you can. Shuffle 1 Epidemic card into each pile, facedown. Stack the piles to form the Player Deck, placing smaller piles at the bottom.
6. **Select characters and starting location.** Each player selects a Character card and a player color, taking the matching Reference card and pawn. (In game 1, each player creates a character: pick a Character card and give the character a name.) Characters persist from game to game, and you do not have to play the same character every game. Place all pawns together at any one research station — players start their first game in Atlanta. If there are no research stations in play, players start in the ruins of Atlanta. Return unused Character cards and pawns to the box.
7. **Place the infection rate marker and infect 9 cities.** Put the infection rate marker on the left-most "2" space of the Infection Rate Track. Shuffle the Infection cards and flip over 3: put 3 disease cubes of the matching color on each of those cities. Flip 3 more: 2 cubes each. Flip 3 more: 1 cube each. (18 cubes total, always matching each city's color.) Place these 9 cards faceup on the Infection Discard Pile; the rest form the Infection Deck.
8. **Use your game-winning bonus.** Winning a month gives a bonus for the next month played — use it now. (You have no bonus for your first game.)
9. **Begin play.** Take all the pawns and blindly select one: that player goes first, and play proceeds clockwise.

## Turn Structure

Each player turn is divided into 3 steps, done in order; after step 3 the player on the left goes next. Players should freely give each other advice, but the player whose turn it is decides what to do.

### 1. Do 4 Actions
Do up to 4 actions (the Generalist may do up to 5): any combination of the 8 actions below, repeating actions if you like, each time counting as 1 action. Your character's special abilities may change how an action is done. All cards discarded by actions go to the Player Discard Pile.

### 2. Draw 2 Player Cards
Draw the top 2 cards of the Player Deck together. **If fewer than 2 cards remain when you are about to draw, the game ends and your team has lost — do not reshuffle the discard pile to form a new deck.**

Any Epidemic cards drawn are never added to your hand and are not replaced with new draws. Resolve each immediately, in these steps:

1. **Increase** — move the infection rate marker forward 1 space on the Infection Rate Track.
2. **Infect** — draw the **bottom** card of the Infection Deck. Unless that disease color has been eradicated, put 3 cubes of that color on the named city; if the city already has cubes of that color, add only enough to bring it to 3, and then an outbreak of that disease occurs there. Discard the card to the Infection Discard Pile.
3. **Intensify** — reshuffle **only** the Infection Discard Pile and place it on top of the Infection Deck.

Drawing 2 Epidemics at once is rare but possible: do all three steps once, then again. The second epidemic's Infection card is then the only card "reshuffled" and ends up on top of the Infection Deck, so an outbreak will occur in that city during Infect Cities unless an Event card prevents it. Discard resolved Epidemic cards.

**Hand limit:** if you ever have more than 7 cards in hand (after first resolving any Epidemics you drew), discard cards or play Event cards until you are at 7. Only Player cards count toward the hand limit.

### 3. Infect Cities
Flip Infection cards from the top of the Infection Deck, one at a time, equal to the current infection rate (the number printed below the space the infection rate marker is on). For each card: place 1 disease cube matching the city's color on that city, unless that disease has been eradicated. If the city already has 3 cubes of that color, do not place a 4th — an outbreak of that disease occurs there instead. Discard each card to the Infection Discard Pile.

## Actions

| Action | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Drive/Ferry** | Move to a city connected by a white line to the one you are in. A white line that runs off the board wraps around to the other edge (e.g., Sydney and Los Angeles are connected). |
| **Direct Flight** | Discard a City card to move to the city named on it. You cannot use Direct Flight in or out of a city that is rioting, collapsing, or fallen. |
| **Charter Flight** | Discard the City card that matches the city you are in to move to any city. You cannot use Charter Flight into or out of a city that is rioting, collapsing, or fallen. |
| **Shuttle Flight** | Move from a city with a research station to any other city that has a research station. |
| **Build a Structure** | Discard the City card that matches the city you are in to place a structure there. In January, the only structures available are research stations; more come later. Take the structure from the pile next to the board; if all structures of a type have been built, you may instead take one from anywhere on the board and move it to your current city. Each city may contain one of each type of structure. You cannot build a research station in a city that is rioting, collapsing, or fallen. |
| **Treat Disease** | Remove 1 disease cube from the city you are in, returning it to the supply. If that color has been cured, remove **all** cubes of that color from your city. If a city holds cubes of several cured colors, you must still Treat Disease once per cured color. |
| **Share Knowledge** | Either give the City card that matches the city you are in to another player, or take that card from another player. The other player must also be in that city, and both of you must agree. If the player receiving the card now has more than 7 cards, they must immediately discard a card or play an Event card. (On your turn you may take the matching card from another player — and you may take **any** City card from the Researcher — if you are in the same city.) |
| **Discover a Cure** | At **any** research station (its city's color does not need to match), discard 5 City cards of the same color to cure that disease. Cover the disease icon in the disease tracking area with its cure marker. If no cubes of that color are on the board at that moment, the disease is immediately eradicated (flip the marker to its eradicated side). |

**Cured diseases:** the cubes stay on the board and new cubes can still be placed by epidemics and infections — but treating that color is now easier. (If a positive mutation makes curing require no action, a player may discard the needed cards immediately, even outside their turn.)

**Eradication:** if the last cube of a **cured** disease is removed from the board, that disease is eradicated — flip its cure marker to its eradicated side. No new cubes of an eradicated disease are placed when its cities are infected. Eradicating is not needed to win. The first time you eradicate a disease, invent a name for it and write the name on the board in the disease tracking area. Eradicating also unlocks the option to give that disease a Positive Mutation at the end of that game. Removing the last cube of a disease that is *not* cured has no effect.

## Scoring / Victory Conditions

**Win:** You win as soon as you meet the necessary number of objectives for the month — the number is shown on the board for that month, and every objective marked MANDATORY must be among those completed. The game ends immediately, even in the middle of a player's turn or a series of outbreaks. You do not have to declare which optional objectives you are pursuing; some objectives stay in play until completed, others expire at the end of a particular month. January starts with one objective, and its mandatory objective is: discover cures to the 4 diseases.

**Loss conditions (any one ends the game immediately):**
- The outbreaks marker reaches the last space of the Outbreaks Track (the 8th outbreak).
- You are unable to place the number of disease cubes actually needed on the board (possible during an epidemic, an outbreak, or infections). Any further cubes and/or outbreaks then don't happen.
- A player cannot draw 2 Player cards after doing their actions. Never reshuffle the Player Discard Pile to form a new deck.

**Game end and campaign progression (win or lose):**
- The group chooses **two Game End Upgrades** at the end of every game it completes.
- Clear the board of all pieces and discard all City cards — everything except permanent changes resets for the next game. Never put Legacy cards back in the Legacy Deck, even when replaying a month.
- **If you win:** you get the Win Bonus for the next month (described on Legacy Deck cards); start your next game at the beginning of the next month; your Funding Level is cut by 2 (minimum 0).
- **If you lose:** if this was the month's first play, you must try the month again (same mission briefing and objectives); otherwise move on to the next month. Your Funding Level is increased by 2 (maximum 10).
- Record wins/losses, players, and your new Funding Level on the game calendar (back cover of the rulebook).

## Special Rules & Edge Cases

**Legacy framework:**
- The Legacy Deck describes the campaign's 12 months. It is a one-way trip: draw and read cards in order, stopping at STOP cards; never look ahead, shuffle, or return cards. If it gets scattered, have a non-player restore the order using the numbers in each card's corner.
- Dossiers: when a Legacy card shows dossier numbers or letters, open (tear off) the matching dossier doors one at a time, resolving each before opening the next. Some contain one-time stickers; others create cards used in future games.
- Packages: open a numbered package only when a yellow box instructs you to. Before your very first game, place the "Open Package 8" sticker on door 8 of the package box: open Package 8 if you lose 4 games in a row.
- Writing on the board, stickers, and torn-up components are permanent. Missing rules A–Y are stickered into the rulebook as you gain them and permanently change the rules.
- If you discover you played a rule wrong, usually just note it and move on. If the mistake made a whole game far too easy or too hard, the group should reduce or increase funding for the next game accordingly.

**Panic levels:** Whenever an outbreak occurs in a city, its panic level goes up by one — place the level 1 (unstable) sticker the first time, then each next numbered sticker over the previous one. Effects are **cumulative**:

| Level | State | Effect |
|-------|-------|--------|
| 1 | Unstable | No effect. |
| 2–3 | Rioting | Players cannot take Direct Flights or Charter Flights in or out. Research stations there are **destroyed and cannot be rebuilt** (when a city's panic level reaches 2, remove any research station present; cover a starting research station sticker with a destroyed research station sticker). No research station may be built in a rioting, collapsing, or fallen city. |
| 4 | Collapsing | Also: discard 1 card of the city's color to enter this city with the Drive/Ferry action. |
| 5 | Fallen | Also: discard an additional card of the city's color (2 total) to Drive/Ferry into this city. If your character is in a city when it falls, that character is lost. |

- When an outbreak occurs in a city already at panic level 5, place no sticker but resolve the outbreak as usual.
- If the game is ending due to outbreaks, only increase panic levels for the first 8 outbreaks; if chain reactions would outbreak more than 8 cities, the players choose which cities outbreak and raise their panic levels, stopping at the 8th outbreak.
- In later games, cities can become isolated so that their outbreaks do not affect adjacent cities; the isolated city's panic level still rises, but the Outbreaks marker does not advance.

**Outbreaks and chain reactions:** When a city that already has 3 cubes of a color would receive another, an outbreak of that disease occurs: move the outbreaks marker forward 1 space, then place 1 cube of that color on every city connected to it. Any connected city already holding 3 cubes of that color does not get a 4th; instead each suffers a chain reaction outbreak after the current outbreak is done (move the marker forward 1 more space and place cubes the same way, but never add a cube to a city that has already outbreaked while resolving the current Infection card). A city can hold up to 3 cubes of **each** color, so multiple colors can coexist there. Every city that outbreaks has its panic level increased, and every character standing in an outbreaking city receives a scar.

**Scars:** When your character must be scarred (standing in a city that outbreaks — other causes appear in later rules), choose an available scar sticker and add it to your Character card. A character can have more than one of the same scar. Sample scars: Insomniac (hand limit −1), Fear of Small Planes (discard a card to use Shuttle Flight), Intimidated (spend 1 extra action to Treat Disease in a rioting city), Demoralized (discard 1 card every time an Epidemic is revealed).

**Lost characters:** If you must place a scar on a character with no empty scar slot, or your character is in a city when it falls (panic level 5), the character is lost: destroy (rip up) the Character card — it can never be played again. Discard all cards you have, take the Civilian card matching your pawn, and place your pawn in a city with a research station (this may be your current city); if there are no research stations in play, place it in Atlanta. If your character was lost during your turn, continue your turn, keeping any remaining actions.

**Characters (special abilities):**
- **Medic:** Treat Disease removes all cubes of one color, not 1. Once a disease is cured, the Medic automatically removes all cubes of that color from his city just by entering or being there — no action needed, and it also happens during other players' turns. He also prevents cubes (and outbreaks) of cured diseases from being placed in his location.
- **Scientist:** needs only 4 (not 5) City cards of the same color to Discover a Cure.
- **Researcher:** when doing Share Knowledge, may give **any** City card from her hand to a player in her city (it need not match the city); the transfer must be from her hand to the other player's, and can happen on either player's turn.
- **Dispatcher:** as an action, may either move any pawn (if its owner agrees) to any city containing another pawn, or move another player's pawn (if its owner agrees) as if it were his own. He can only move pawns — he may not direct them to do other actions. When moving another pawn as his own, use the Dispatcher's scars and upgrades, and any cards played or discarded (for Direct/Charter Flights or to enter a collapsing or fallen city) come from the Dispatcher's hand; a Charter Flight discard must match the city the pawn is moving **from**.
- **Generalist:** may do up to 5 actions each turn, and has slots for 4 character upgrades (other characters have 2).

**Funding and Event cards:**
- Your Funding Level is 4 at the start of the first January game and moves up and down between games (win: −2, lose: +2; minimum 0, maximum 10). Each setup, the group adds Funded Event cards of its choice to the Player Deck — at most as many as the current Funding Level.
- Playing an Event card is not an action, and the player who plays it decides how it is used. Events may be played at any time **except between drawing and resolving a card** (e.g., you cannot cancel an outbreak after its Infection card is flipped, but you may play events after it resolves and before the next flip). Discard played Events to the Player Discard Pile. Funded Event examples: One Quiet Night (skip the next Infect Cities step — flip no Infection cards), Remote Treatment (remove 2 disease cubes from the board).
- City cards may gain stickers over the campaign (each card at most one). A stickered card is played either as a City card or for its sticker effect — never both. Unfunded Event stickers make a card both a City and an Event card, usable every game regardless of funding.
- Players may always examine the discard piles, and all hands are kept faceup.

**Game End Upgrades** (choose 2 after every completed game, win or lose; the same upgrade may be chosen twice):
- **Character Upgrade:** add an upgrade sticker to a character that was in the just-played game, using one of its upgrade slots (2 per character; Generalist 4). Upgrades may be stuck over existing upgrades — covered upgrades are no longer in play. Examples: Grizzled (no scar from being in an outbreaking city), Flexible (as an action, discard a card to draw a same-color card from the Player Discard Pile), Forecaster (at the start of your turn, look at the top 2 Infection cards), Archive Access (hand limit +1), Local Connections (once per turn, Treat Disease in a city connected to yours), Pilot (Direct Flight without discarding — show the card and return it to your hand).
- **Unfunded Event:** add an event sticker to any City card that has no sticker.
- **Starting Research Station:** pick a city that currently contains a research station and mark it with a starting research station sticker (place it near the city, never in a panic level sticker box). Every future setup places a free research station in each stickered city.
- **Positive Mutation:** only for a disease eradicated during the just-played game. Mutations must be added in order (you can't add level 4 before levels 1, 2, and 3). Examples from the sticker sheet: a level 3 mutation lets you Discover a Cure for that disease with one fewer card; a level 4 mutation makes Treat remove all cubes of that color from your city. A disease with the "Efficient to Sequence" mutation is cured as soon as one player discards the necessary cards — no action required, even on another player's turn.

**Structures:** Each city may contain one of each type of structure. Research stations are needed to Discover a Cure and for Shuttle Flight. Destroy a research station in a city if it is rioting (replacing a starting research station sticker with a destroyed one), and never build one in a rioting, collapsing, or fallen city.

## Player Reference

| Step | Summary |
|------|---------|
| 1. Do 4 actions | Any combination of the 8 actions; repeats allowed (Generalist: up to 5) |
| 2. Draw 2 cards | Resolve any Epidemics; discard to 7 cards |
| 3. Infect cities | Flip Infection cards equal to the current infection rate; 1 cube each; a city at 3 cubes outbreaks |

**Epidemic (resolve immediately):** 1) Increase — infection rate marker forward 1 space. 2) Infect — bottom card of the Infection Deck: 3 cubes (top up to 3, then outbreak); skip if eradicated. 3) Intensify — reshuffle only the Infection Discard Pile onto the top of the Infection Deck.

**Infection rate:** the marker starts each game on the left-most "2" space of the Infection Rate Track; the current rate is the number below the marker's space; each Epidemic moves the marker forward 1 space.

**Panic levels (cumulative):** 1 Unstable — no effect · 2–3 Rioting — no Direct/Charter Flights in or out; research stations destroyed, cannot be rebuilt or built · 4 Collapsing — discard 1 card of the city's color to Drive/Ferry in · 5 Fallen — 2 cards total to Drive/Ferry in; characters there when it falls are lost.

**Key numbers:** hand limit 7 (Player cards only) · cure = 5 same-color City cards at any research station (Scientist: 4) · max 3 cubes per color per city · setup infects 9 cities with 18 cubes (3/3/3 cities at 3/2/1 cubes) · Player Deck gets 5 Epidemic cards (1 per pile) · Funding 0–10, starts at 4, −2 on a win / +2 on a loss · 2 Game End Upgrades per game.

**Lose immediately:** outbreaks marker reaches the last space of the Outbreaks Track (8th outbreak) · needed disease cubes can't be placed · a player can't draw 2 Player cards (no reshuffling the Player Discard Pile).

**Win:** meet the month's required number of objectives, including all mandatory objectives — the game ends immediately.
