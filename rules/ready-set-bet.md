## Overview

Players bet in real time on four horse races. A House player rolls dice and moves horses while everyone else claims betting spaces. Players settle winning bets and losses after each race, then gain VIP abilities for subsequent races. The player with the most money after race four wins; tied leaders share victory.

This summary covers the 2022 base game, including optional House betting, House rotation, and app play. References are to the [official eight-page rulebook](https://www.alderac.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/ReadySetBet_EN_1P_Rulebook_FINAL.pdf). The board supplies each individual betting space's payout and loss; VIP and Prop Bet cards supply their individual effects.

The nine horses are numbered **2/3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, and 11/12**. Each normal player set has token values **2, 3, 3, 4, 5**. The House tokens have values **3, 3, 4, 4, 5, 5**.

## Setup

Follow rulebook p. 3:

1. Put the Bet Board where everyone can reach it. Shuffle the Prop Bet deck, put it on its labeled space, and reveal **five** cards beside it.
2. Shuffle Exotic Finish Cards. Reveal **one** into the Race 1 space; put the deck on its labeled space.
3. Shuffle VIP Cards into a face-down deck. Put money, black and white Bonus Bet Tokens, and the six result markers nearby.
4. Choose a **House** player and give them the track, dice, and horses. Put each horse in its matching starting gate. Normally the House facilitates rather than competes. The app can replace this role; the variants below let the House compete.
5. Every non-House player chooses a color and takes its five tokens. With **seven or eight non-House bettors**, each returns **one 3 token**, leaving four tokens.
6. Regular bettors receive **no starting money**. A competing House instead starts with **$18** and uses the six House tokens.

For a simpler game, players may omit Prop Bets, Exotic Finishes, and/or VIP Cards. Choose these omissions before play.

## Turn Structure

There are **four races**, each with four steps (pp. 4–7):

1. **Run the race.** The House announces the start, repeatedly rolls two dice, announces each sum, and advances the matching horse. There are no individual player turns.
2. **Place bets simultaneously** while the race continues and betting remains open. These first two steps occur together.
3. **Resolve bets** once a horse reaches space **15**, immediately ending the race. Leave every horse where it stopped; their positions determine all other placings and bets.
4. **Clean up**, except after the fourth race: replace all five Prop Bets, reveal one additional Exotic Finish alongside the existing ones, deal each bettor two VIP Cards to keep one, and return horses to their starting gates. Return resolved Bet Tokens to their owners.

Exotic Finishes accumulate: races 1–4 have **1, 2, 3, 4** available cards. VIPs also accumulate: a bettor normally has **0, 1, 2, 3** active cards during races 1–4. After settling race four, count money instead of performing another cleanup.

### Moving horses and closing bets

Every roll moves its matching horse **one space**. Consecutive rolls for the same horse give the second roll a bonus:

| Horse | Color | Bonus on the second consecutive matching roll | Total movement on that roll |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| 2/3, 4, 10, 11/12 | Blue | +3 | 4 |
| 5, 9 | Orange | +2 | 3 |
| 6, 8 | Red | +1 | 2 |
| 7 | Black | None | 1 |

For a combined-number horse, either number counts: **3 then 2** triggers the 2/3 horse's bonus, as does 2 then 3. Bonuses apply on consecutive rolls **2, 4, 6…** within an uninterrupted run for that horse. The third matching roll moves only one space; the fourth starts a new completed pair and receives the bonus (p. 4).

The House announces each horse crossing the board's red line. When the **third horse crosses**, announce that betting is closed. No further tokens may be placed unless a VIP explicitly allows it. Reaching the finish also closes betting immediately, even if fewer than three horses have crossed the red line.

## Actions

### Placing a normal bet

Before betting closes, put one of your unused tokens on an open betting space. You may place tokens at any pace, use several different spaces for the same horse, or leave tokens unplayed. Each space holds **one token total**; you cannot displace someone else's token. Once placed, a token **cannot move or be removed**. A token's number determines a winning payout, not how much cash you must stake beforehand (p. 5).

### Win, Place, and Show

Each horse has **three Win**, **two Place**, and **two Show** spaces, with different printed payouts and penalties (p. 6).

- **Win:** only the horse that reaches the finish first.
- **Place:** horses finishing first or second.
- **Show:** horses finishing first, second, or third.

The winner therefore pays on all three categories. Determine the remaining positions from the horses' final track spaces; do not keep rolling to finish the race.

If multiple horses tie for **second**, all occupy second and third for betting: all Place and Show, but no horse behind them Shows. Otherwise, all horses tied for **third** Show. Use the double-sided result markers to mark these ties.

### Color and special board bets

| Bet | Winning payout | Loss |
|---|---:|---:|
| A blue horse wins | 5 × token value | $1 |
| An orange horse wins | 3 × token value | $1 |
| A red horse wins | 2 × token value | $1 |
| Horse 7 finishes fifth or worse | 4 × token value | $0 |

For horse 7 to finish fifth or worse, **at least four horses must be ahead of it**. Horses tied with 7 do not count as ahead (p. 6).

### Prop Bets

The five face-up Prop Cards each provide **one** betting space. They require the pictured horse to finish ahead of another horse or an entire indicated group. A tie is a **loss**, including a tie with any horse the selection must beat. Use the payout and penalty printed on that card. Replace all five after each race; they do not accumulate (p. 6).

### Exotic Finishes

Each face-up Exotic Finish has **three** betting spaces, but each player may put **at most one of their tokens on that card**. Multiple players may each claim a different space. Previously revealed cards remain available in later races (p. 7).

| Card | Condition at race end | Payout multiplier | Loss |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Tight Race | Every horse has moved at least 6 spaces | 6× | $2 |
| Late Start | At least 2 horses have moved at most 3 spaces | 4× | $1 |
| Photo Finish | Third place is at most 3 spaces behind first | 5× | $3 |
| By a Nose | Second place is exactly 1 space behind first | 5× | $3 |
| Blow Out | Second place is more than 5 spaces behind first | 4× | $2 |

For Photo Finish, horses tied for second also count as tied for third. These conditions use the stopped board position, not future hypothetical finishing order.

## Scoring / Victory Conditions

After each race, first collect **all winning payouts**, then pay losses (p. 5). Players may resolve their own bets simultaneously.

- A winning bet pays **token value × printed multiplier**. A value-5 token on a 9× space earns **$45**.
- A losing bet costs the **fixed dollar amount in the space's red circle**, regardless of the token's value. That same value-5 token on a space marked −2 loses **$2**.
- A space without a red penalty circle costs nothing on a loss.
- You cannot go below **$0**. Once your money runs out, you owe no remaining loss and carry no debt forward.
- Unplayed tokens have no payout or loss.

After the **fourth** race's complete settlement, compare total money. Most money wins; tied players **share victory**, with no further tiebreaker (p. 7).

## Special Rules & Edge Cases

### VIP Cards and Bonus Bet Tokens

After each of races 1–3, deal each bettor **two VIP Cards**, secretly choose one, discard the other to a shared discard pile, and place the chosen card face up. Its benefit lasts for the remaining game, unless it specifies a one-time effect. Reshuffle VIP discards if the draw deck runs out. **Card instructions override normal rules**, including token stacking or placing bets after the red-line closure (p. 7).

- **Complimentary Chips** pays once when chosen; keep it in front of you afterward so it still counts as a VIP Card.
- **Piggyback Bet** also works with a Bonus Bet Token of the specified number. Follow the card's limit and stacking condition.
- **Snake Eyes & Boxcars** pays $1 whenever a 2 or 12 is rolled. The House should announce these sums distinctly, along with all other rolls that may trigger cards.
- A VIP granting a **black or white Bonus Bet Token** adds it to your token pool for the rest of the game. Keep its card visible to identify the token's owner.
- Bonus token values are **2–7**. Values 4, 5, 6, and 7 add loss penalties of **$1, $2, $3, and $5** respectively, on top of the space's loss. Value 3 has no additional penalty. Value 2's cancellation symbol removes the space's losing penalty entirely. Winning payouts still use the large token value.

### House Bets variant — 2–9 players

The competing House begins with **$18** and receives **no VIP Cards**. Before each race, place its **six tokens** on six different betting spaces, including Prop or Exotic spaces if desired. Place each in the upper-left corner so it does not obscure the printed values. These spaces **remain open to normal bettors** during the race; House pre-bets do not reserve or block them. Resolve House bets using the same payout and penalty rules (p. 8).

### House Rotation variant — 2–8 players

Randomly choose the first House, who uses House Bets. After a race, that player may remain House. If they decline, offer the role clockwise; if everyone declines, the player immediately to the current House's left must take it (p. 8).

While acting as House, a player cannot use their VIP abilities, but still draws and keeps a VIP at the end of the round. **Complimentary Chips** is the exception: resolve its payment immediately when chosen. To compensate for inactive VIPs, the House gains **$3 before race 2, $6 before race 3, or $9 before race 4**, according to the current race.

### App variant — 2–8 players

The optional app conducts and announces races, replacing the House entirely. Everyone bets normally. The physical game remains playable without an app; use a human House if preferred (pp. 2, 8).
