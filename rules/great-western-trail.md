## Overview

Great Western Trail (2016 first edition, eggertspiele; design Alexander Pfister, development Viktor Kobilke) is a game for 2-4 players, aged 12 and up. You are a 19th-century rancher who repeatedly herds cattle from Texas to Kansas City. On your turns you move your cattleman along a winding, forking trail and use the location you stop on: placing your private buildings, buying better cattle for your herd deck, hiring workers (cowboys, craftsmen, engineers), moving your engine up the railroad, upgrading train stations, and removing hazards. Each time your cattleman reaches Kansas City you reveal your hand, earn money for the different cattle types in it, and must deliver to a city along the railroad. Over the course of the game your cattleman reaches Kansas City about 5 to 7 times.

All victory points are awarded at the end of the game (VP symbols on components in front of you plus those marked with your color on the board). The player with the most victory points wins; in the case of a tie, the victory is shared.

## Components

- 1 game board (trail from the lower right corner to Kansas City in the upper left; railroad track; job market; cattle market; foresight spaces; hazard sections for flood, drought, rockfall; Indian trade section; station master spaces; neutral building spaces)
- 4 player boards
- 4 player count tiles (1 per player, double-sided: 3-player front / 4-player back)
- 4 engines, 4 cattlemen, 4 certificate markers
- 56 player discs (14 per player)
- 92 cattle cards:
  - 36 market cattle cards: 7 Holstein (yellow), 7 Brown Swiss (red), 7 Ayrshire (blue), 9 West Highland (brown), 6 Texas Longhorn (purple)
  - 56 player cattle cards, 14 per player: 5 Jersey (gray), 3 Dutch Belt (green), 3 Black Angus (black), 3 Guernsey (white)
- 54 worker tiles: 18 each of cowboy, craftsman, engineer
- 18 hazard tiles: 6 each of flood, drought, rockfall
- 22 teepee tiles: 11 blue, 11 green
  - Worker/hazard/teepee tile backs are marked 1, 2 or 3 so the tiles enter the game in a reasonably balanced way
- 47 building tiles: 7 neutral building tiles (lettered A to G) and 40 private building tiles (10 per player, double-sided, each marked with a number 1-10 and side a or b)
- 4 starting objective cards and 24 objective cards
- 5 station master tiles
- 1 job market token
- 55 coins (35 of value 1 Dollar, 20 of value 5 Dollars)
- 1 scoring note pad

## Setup

Board:

1. Place the game board in the middle of the table.
2. Place the 7 neutral building tiles face up on the 7 neutral spaces (first game: on the space matching each tile's letter A-G; subsequent games: shuffle and distribute at random).
3. Turn the 54 worker tiles, 22 teepee tiles and 18 hazard tiles face down, sort them by the number on their backs (1, 2, 3), shuffle each pile separately, and place them as three face-down piles next to Kansas City. These are the "Kansas City supply".
4. Shuffle the 5 station master tiles and place one at random, face up, on each of the 5 station master spaces of the railroad.
5. Shuffle the 36 market cattle cards into a face-down market cattle stack. Draw 7 cards (2 players), 10 (3 players) or 13 (4 players), sort them by color and place them face up in an overlapping row as the cattle market (left-to-right order: yellow, red, blue, brown, purple).
6. From the designated Kansas City supply pile, reveal tiles one after the other onto the game board until 7 tiles have been placed: a teepee tile goes to the Indian trade section on the empty space with the lowest money value above it (starting at -3); a hazard tile goes to its matching hazard section (flood, drought or rockfall) on the empty space with the lowest number (1 to 4). If no empty space is left for a tile during setup, flip it face down and return it to the Kansas City supply.
7. From the supply pile that contains only worker tiles, reveal worker tiles and fill the job market: start in the top row on the space beneath your player-count sign and fill rightwards, then continue row by row until you place a tile on the space to the left of the job market token. Depending on player count the job market starts with 3 to 7 worker tiles. (In a 3-player game each row comprises only 3 spaces and the entire left column stays empty; in a 2-player game each row comprises only 2 spaces and both left columns stay empty.)
8. Place the job market token on the top dashed round space of the job market.
9. Fill the 6 foresight spaces in Kansas City with face-up tiles from the Kansas City supply — each foresight space takes a tile from the pile whose number matches the number printed on that space (1, 2 or 3).
10. Shuffle the 24 objective cards into a face-down stack and reveal 4 of them in a column below it: the "general objective display".
11. Place the coins within reach as the bank.

Each player:

12. Chooses a color and takes that player board (it has 14 round colored disc spaces). With 3 or 4 players, each player also takes her player count tile, turned to the matching side, and covers the Phase A box of her player board with it.
13. Takes her 10 private building tiles. In your first games, everyone uses the a-sides, displayed in ascending order above the player board. (Experienced variant: one player randomizes the side of each of her tiles, then everyone copies her display.) The side used for each building tile is identical for all players and no one may deliberately flip a tile to its other side.
14. Places her 14 player discs on her player board, covering every colored disc space — only the 2 gray spaces in the upper left stay empty. Places her engine on the starting space (the red building) of the railroad track, her certificate marker on the top space of her certificate track (next to the 0), and keeps her cattleman beside her player board for now.
15. Shuffles her 14 player cattle cards (her herd deck) into a face-down personal draw stack to the left of her player board, then draws a hand of 4 cards.
16. Receives 1 random starting objective card (from the shuffled 4), placed face up below her player board as the first card of her personal objective area. Spare starting objective cards return to the box.
17. Determine a starting player. The starting player receives 6 Dollars, the next player clockwise 7, the third 8 and the fourth 9 Dollars.

Your herd deck consists of your face-down personal draw stack, your hand (hand limit starts at 4), and your face-up personal discard pile. Only when you must draw a card and your draw stack is empty do you shuffle your discard pile into a new draw stack.

## Turn Structure

Beginning with the starting player (the one with 6 Dollars) and going clockwise, each player takes turns consisting of three phases, always in this order:

- **Phase A) Move your cattleman** to another location along the trail.
- **Phase B) Use the action(s)** of the reached location.
- **Phase C) Draw up** to your hand limit.

On your very first turn, instead of moving, place your cattleman on any neutral building tile (sharing with other players is fine) and continue directly with Phase B.

**Phase A — movement:**
- Locations are Kansas City and every tile on the trail (building, hazard and teepee tiles). Empty trail spaces are NOT locations and cost nothing to cross.
- Movement is measured in steps: each location along your path costs 1 step. You must move at least 1 step and at most your current step limit (you start with a step limit of 3 or 4 depending on player count; it can be increased later).
- Always move forward along the arrows; at a fork, choose a path. The location where you end your movement is the one you use in Phase B.
- You may not pass over Kansas City — a cattleman reaching it must stop there.
- Other players' cattlemen never interfere; several cattlemen may share a location.
- **Hands (fees):** if your cattleman moves onto or passes over a location showing a green or black hand, you immediately pay a fee — to the bank for hazard/teepee tiles, or to the owner for another player's private building. The fee depends on the player count and the hand's color (in the rulebook's 4-player example a green hand costs 1 Dollar and a black hand 2 Dollars). If you cannot pay some or all fees along your path you may still move: pay as much as you can and continue. Fees are never paid retroactively. This leniency applies only to hand fees — every other cost or requirement in the game must be paid/met in full.

**Phase B — use the location:**
- **a) A neutral building tile or your own private building tile:** EITHER perform that tile's local actions — each of them at most once, in any order; you may skip some or all; any cost/requirement must be paid/met completely to gain that action's reward; where two actions are separated by a white slash within one local action you may perform only one of them — OR, only if you forfeit all of the local actions, perform one single auxiliary action instead. If the building sits on a trail space with a printed risk action, that risk action counts as one of the tile's local actions for its owner.
- **b) Another player's building tile, a hazard tile or a teepee tile:** your only option is one single auxiliary action.
- **c) Kansas City:** carry out all 5 subphases in order (move your cattleman along the numbered spots):
  1-3. **Foresight 1, 2, 3:** for each of the three pairs of foresight spaces (marked 1, 2, 3), choose ONE of its two face-up tiles and move it to its section:
     - Worker tile: onto the next free space of the job market — always in the row containing the job market token, filling left to right. The last space filled in each row is the token's own space; placing a worker there moves the token along the arrow to the dashed round space of the next row. If the token moves along a **yellow arrow**, immediately refill the cattle market from the market cattle stack up to its initial total (7/10/13 cards for 2/3/4 players; add nothing if it is already at or above that total, or if the stack is empty). If the token moves out of the bottom row along the **red arrow, the game end is triggered** (see Scoring).
     - Teepee tile: Indian trade section, empty space with the lowest money value. If no space is empty, the tile is removed from the game (to the box).
     - Hazard tile: its matching hazard section, empty space with the lowest number (1-4). If no space is empty, it goes to the box.
  4. **Income:** reveal your entire hand and total the breeding values of each DIFFERENT cattle type in it — duplicates of a type count only once, and victory points on cards as well as objective cards are ignored. You may then raise this total with certificates: spend temporary certificates by moving your certificate marker backwards (the number next to its position shows how many you have), and every permanent certificate (on station master tiles you own) automatically adds 1. Take the final total in Dollars from the bank, setting the amount apart so you can still see the total in subphase 5. Then discard your whole hand to your personal discard pile.
  5. **Delivery:** you must move one player disc from your player board onto the crest of a city whose city value is equal to or lower than your just-determined total breeding value. You may never choose a city that already holds one of your discs, EXCEPT the local town Kansas City and the terminal town San Francisco, which may be delivered to repeatedly. A disc taken from a white-cornered disc space may go on any city space; a disc from a dark-cornered space only on a dark-cornered city space. Placing a disc may unlock end-game VP on the crest and may trigger delivery actions you must perform immediately; the action or VP in a green arrow between two crests only triggers/unlocks once BOTH adjacent crests hold your discs (the objective green arrows make you immediately take an objective card from the general objective display onto your personal discard pile — twice, if one disc completes both adjacent arrows, replenishing the display in between). If you place your disc on the Kansas City crest, immediately gain 6 Dollars — but that disc costs you 6 VP at game end. Finally pay transport costs: none if your engine's nose is level with or beyond the chosen crest; otherwise 1 Dollar per cross printed between your engine's nose and that crest's city value (your income always covers this).
  After subphase 5: move your cattleman to the horseman space at the start of the trail (lower right corner), and refill the 3 empty foresight spaces with face-up tiles from the Kansas City supply piles matching each space's number.

**Phase C — draw up:** if you hold fewer cards than your hand limit (start 4, maximum 6), draw from your personal draw stack up to the limit, shuffling your discard pile into a new draw stack only when you must draw from an empty stack. After a Kansas City turn you draw a whole new hand.

**Objective cards in hand** may be played during your own turn, either before Phase A or immediately before/after any ONE action of Phase B — never in the middle of an action and never once Phase C has started. Play one by placing it face up in your personal objective area, then perform its upper-left immediate action or forfeit it.

## Actions

**Reading action icons:** requirements are shown in red, rewards in green/white. You may forfeit a reward completely or partly even when you meet the requirement — except that a Dollar reward must always be taken in full. "Discard" always means from your hand, face up onto your personal discard pile. Discard requirements name exact cards (e.g. one cattle card of any type; one gray "Jersey"; one black "Black Angus"; two cattle cards of the same type). Certificate icons move your certificate marker forward up to the shown number of spaces, never beyond your temporary certificate limit (3 at the start of the game).

**Place a private building tile** (offered by neutral building B; also by the immediate actions of your craftsman row, which cost only 1 Dollar per craftsman instead of 2): take a building tile from above your player board and place it on any empty building space of the trail, or replace one of your own placed buildings with a higher-valued one. Its upper-left corner shows the craftsmen required — your craftsman row must have at least that many — and you pay 2 Dollars per required craftsman (e.g. a 2-craftsman tile costs 4 Dollars). For a replacement you only need, and only pay for, the difference in craftsmen between the two tiles; the old tile is removed from the game. Tiles are never flipped to their other side.

**Hire one worker:** choose one worker tile from the job market — never from the row where the job market token currently sits — and pay the hiring cost printed at the right of its row (the example shows rows costing 6 and 7 Dollars), modified by the action icon: unmodified, 2 Dollars more, or 1 Dollar less. Place him on the leftmost free space of his row in your worker section (each player starts with one printed worker of each type, so the first space of each row is occupied). If the space shows an immediate action, perform it now or forfeit it. If a row is full you cannot hire that worker type. Each worker on the fifth or sixth space of a row is worth 4 VP at game end.

**Trade with the Indians:** take any one teepee tile from the Indian trade section face up in front of you, then gain or pay the money value shown above its space (e.g. a -2 space means pay 2 Dollars; a +6 space means gain 6 Dollars). If the section is empty you cannot take a teepee.

**Remove a hazard:** take any one hazard tile from any hazard section face up in front of you. If the action icon shows a cost, you must pay that many Dollars (otherwise you may not take a tile); with no cost shown it is free. Each hazard tile is worth the 2, 3 or 4 VP printed on it at game end. If all 3 hazard sections are empty you cannot take one.

**Buy cattle from the cattle market:** acquire market cattle cards, placing them face up on your personal discard pile. Your options depend on how many cowboys are in your cowboy row and how much you pay; each cowboy may be used only once per buy action. Rulebook examples: with 1 cowboy, buy one breeding-value-3 card for 6 Dollars OR one West Highland for 12 Dollars; with 2 cowboys, buy one breeding-value-3 card for 3 Dollars OR one Texas Longhorn for 12 Dollars, or split the cowboys (e.g. two separate value-3 cards at 6 Dollars each); with 3 cowboys, one option is exactly 2 value-3 cards for 5 Dollars; with 5 cowboys, one option is exactly 2 West Highland cards for 8 Dollars. Any cowboy not used for buying may instead draw 2 cards from the market cattle stack and add them face up to the cattle market. You can only buy cards currently in the market, and you may always buy fewer than allowed.

**Move your engine forward:** with the engineer icon, move your engine up to as many spaces forward as you have engineers in your engineer row (other icons give fixed amounts, e.g. up to 2 or 3 spaces). Except for the starting space, no railroad space may hold more than one engine — skip occupied spaces entirely as if they did not exist. Turnout spaces (one per train station) each count as one additional space branching between the two spaces they emerge from; from the space with the switch you may enter the turnout if it is free. You may move fewer spaces than allowed, but stopping forfeits any leftover movement.

**Upgrade a train station:** if your engine ends its movement on a turnout space, immediately decide whether to upgrade that station — allowed only if you have not upgraded it before (opponents' discs there do not matter). Pay the upgrade cost printed next to the station and place one of your player discs on its station space (white/dark corner rules as for cities). Then, if a station master tile lies next to that station, you may claim it by appointing one of your HIRED workers currently on the rightmost space of its row: that worker tile moves to the station master space for the rest of the game, and the station master tile goes face up next to your player board. This is your only chance to claim that tile. (Removing the worker re-exposes his board space's immediate action, which a future hire onto that space can use.) A station master tile's upper half is either an immediate action (e.g. perform "Remove a hazard" free of charge OR "Trade with the Indians") or a permanent certificate; its lower half is a unique end-game scoring task.

**Space 39 (last railroad space):** an engine reaching it stops there (upgrade as usual if applicable), then MUST move backwards at least 1 space, to any free space of the track (even a turnout — where you may immediately upgrade). After moving back, gain 3 Dollars.

**Moving the engine backwards** (for actions that require it): skip occupied spaces (moving farther back); you may stop on a free turnout and upgrade its station; you may never deliberately move fewer spaces backwards than required; if your engine is on the starting space you cannot use an action requiring backwards movement; the engine never turns around.

**Gain one objective card:** take a card from the general objective display OR the top card of the objective stack, face up onto your personal discard pile; refill the display from the stack immediately.

**Auxiliary actions:** your player board shows 5 auxiliary actions, each flanked by 2 disc spaces. An action is locked while both its disc spaces are covered; clearing at least one unlocks it (at the beginning of the game only 2 auxiliary actions are available). The five actions (single version): gain 1 Dollar; draw 1 card then discard 1 card; pay 1 Dollar and move your engine 1 space backwards, then move your certificate marker 1 space forward; pay 1 Dollar and move your engine 1 space forward; move your engine 1 space backwards, then remove 1 card from your hand entirely from the game (to the box). If BOTH disc spaces of an action are cleared, the explicit "single or double auxiliary action" icon lets you use its double version — requirement and reward both doubled (draw 2/discard 2; pay 2 Dollars, engine 2 back, marker up to 2 forward; pay 2 Dollars, engine 2 forward; engine 2 back, remove 2 cards; gain 2 Dollars). The single auxiliary action taken ON a location in place of its local actions can NEVER be doubled, even with both disc spaces cleared.

**Notable private building local actions** (in addition to the standard actions above) include:
- **Extraordinary delivery:** move your engine one or more spaces backwards, then deliver to a city crest whose value is at most the number of spaces moved (occupied spaces and turnout spaces do NOT count toward this number). Usual delivery rules apply (no city with your disc except Kansas City/San Francisco; delivery actions trigger), but NO transport costs. If the engine lands on a turnout you may then upgrade its station.
- **Move your cattleman:** move your cattleman forward up to the icon's number of steps (icons of up to 1-5 steps exist; at least 1 step if you move at all), then carry out all of Phase B again at the new location. You do not draw back up to your hand limit in between — only at the end of your turn.
- **Copy the local actions of one adjacent building tile** — neutral, yours or another player's; "adjacent" means no other location and no empty space in between.
- Gain 2 Dollars for each of your private buildings in the woods (a building is in the woods if at least one edge touches woods); move your engine up to as many spaces forward as you have buildings in the woods.
- For each pair of 1 green + 1 blue teepee tile you have collected: move your certificate marker up to 2 spaces forward AND gain 2 Dollars (you keep the teepees).
- Discard one Guernsey for 4 Dollars; discard one Holstein for 10 Dollars; discard 2 cattle cards of the same type for 3 Dollars (a 4-Dollar version also exists); discard one Black Angus to move your certificate marker up to 2 forward; discard one cattle card of any type to gain 3 Dollars AND an objective card taken directly into your hand.
- Hire one worker at 1 Dollar less; move your certificate marker as far forward as possible (to your temporary certificate limit); gain an objective card and move your certificate marker up to 2 forward; move your engine 1 space backwards and gain 3 Dollars; draw up to as many cards as you have cowboys, then discard as many as you drew; gain as many Dollars as you have engineers; upgrade one train station anywhere on the track behind your engine; gain 4 Dollars.

## Scoring / Victory Conditions

**Game end trigger:** the game end is triggered when a player places a worker tile onto the LAST space of the job market while carrying out Kansas City subphase 2 or 3 — this moves the job market token out of the job market along the red arrow. The active player immediately takes the job market token and places it in front of her (it is worth 2 VP). If this happened in subphase 2, she still carries out subphase 3 but may not choose another worker tile (if only worker tiles remain there, she skips subphase 3 entirely); she then completes subphases 4 and 5 and refills the foresight spaces as usual. That was her last turn. Every OTHER player then takes exactly one final turn, moving and using a location as usual; players reaching Kansas City on their final turn carry out all 5 subphases but may not choose worker tiles in subphases 2 and 3 (skipping those subphases entirely if only worker tiles are left), and still refill the foresight spaces. After the last player's final turn the game ends and final scoring begins.

**Final scoring — go through the scoring pad's 11 categories:**
1. **Money:** 1 VP for every 5 Dollars you own.
2. **Buildings:** the VP printed on each of your private building tiles on the game board.
3. **Cities:** the sum of VP you unlocked with discs on the city crests. VP in a green arrow between two crests count only if both adjacent crests hold your discs (example values: Sacramento 6 VP, 4 VP between Sacramento and San Diego, 8 VP by El Paso). Negative city VP are subtracted — the total can be negative, and each disc on the Kansas City crest is -6 VP.
4. **Train stations:** the VP printed next to each station holding one of your discs.
5. **Hazard tiles:** the VP printed on each hazard tile you collected (2, 3 or 4 each).
6. **Cattle cards:** the sum of VP printed on cattle cards anywhere in your deck (draw stack, hand and discard pile).
7. **Objective cards:** first, for each objective card still in your deck, choose to remove it from the game or add it to your personal objective area (without its immediate action). Then score every card in your objective area: if ALL of its tasks are fulfilled, gain its printed VP; if not completely fulfilled, LOSE its printed negative VP (only starting objective cards have no negative value). Each individual fulfilled task may only be counted toward ONE objective card. Tasks include: a private building on the board; a green teepee; a blue teepee; a hazard tile (any kind); a disc on a train station; a breeding-value-3 cattle card in your deck; a West Highland in your deck; a Texas Longhorn in your deck; a disc on San Francisco's city space.
8. **Station master tiles:** the VP from each of your station master tiles' individual tasks (independent of objective-card tasks): per worker in your worker section — 1 VP each, including the printed starting workers; per 2 objective cards in your personal objective area (fulfilled or not); per 2 hazard tiles; per pair of 1 green + 1 blue teepee tile; per 2 certificates (temporary or permanent).
9. **Workers:** 4 VP for each worker on the fifth or sixth space of any row of your worker section.
10. **Player board:** 3 VP if you cleared the marked disc space (the step-limit space that also granted 3 Dollars) during the game.
11. **Job market token:** 2 VP if it is in front of you.

Most VP wins; a tie is a shared victory.

## Special Rules & Edge Cases

- **Disc spaces on your player board:** clearing a disc (by delivering to a city or upgrading a station) unlocks that space's ability. Two spaces increase your step limit — one of these also grants an immediate 3 Dollars and the end-game 3 VP. Two spaces increase your hand limit (4 up to 6), but clearing either costs 5 Dollars immediately — if you cannot pay, you must clear a different space. Certificate spaces raise your temporary certificate limit from 3 (to 4, then 6) — but clearing the 6-certificate space before the 4-certificate space leaves your limit at 3 until the 4-space is also cleared. Spaces in the auxiliary action section unlock/double auxiliary actions.
- **Disc placement emergencies:** if you must place a disc on a white-cornered space but only have discs left on dark-cornered disc spaces, you may use one of those. If you must place a disc but cannot (none left, or you cannot pay to clear one), remove one of your discs from a train station of your choice and place that one instead.
- **Certificates:** temporary certificates live on your certificate track and are spent (marker moves backwards) at income time; permanent certificates on station master tiles add 1 each to every income total automatically for the rest of the game.
- **Deck thinning:** the "remove cards from the game" auxiliary action can shrink your deck without limit. If your deck ever cannot refill your hand to its limit, you simply play with fewer cards.
- **Open/hidden information:** you may look through your own personal discard pile at any time; you may never look through your personal draw stack.
- **Objective stack empty:** vacant display spaces are no longer refilled; once the general objective display is empty, no new objective cards can be gained.
- **Money is unlimited:** substitute something if the bank runs out.
- **Objective cards are optional to play:** you may keep them cycling in your deck instead; at game end, deck-held objective cards are scored only if you then choose to add them to your objective area, whereas cards already in your objective area MUST be scored.
- **Objective card immediate actions** (upper-left corner, on playing the card): move your cattleman up to 3 steps forward ignoring green and black hands — but you may NOT use the location you stop on (moved before Phase A: your normal movement starts from there; moved after using a location in Phase B: proceed to Phase C on arrival; you may never move to Kansas City this way); draw up to 3 cards then discard as many as drawn; move your engine up to 2 or 3 spaces forward; perform one single or double auxiliary action.
- **Risk actions:** some trail spaces print a risk action (e.g. discard 1 cattle card to move your certificate marker 1 forward); a private building placed there grants its owner that action as an extra local action.
- **Kansas City income counts types, not cards:** e.g. a hand with 2 Dutch Belt (value 2), 1 Brown Swiss (3) and 1 Holstein (3) is worth 8 — the second Dutch Belt adds nothing.
- **Only neutral building B and your craftsman-row immediate actions can place buildings;** the craftsman-row route costs 1 Dollar per craftsman instead of 2.

## Player Reference

**Turn:** A) Move 1..step-limit steps (each tile = 1 step; empty spaces free; pay hand fees, or as much as you can) > B) Use location > C) Draw to hand limit.

**Location options:** neutral/own building = local actions (each once, any order) OR 1 single auxiliary action (only if all local actions forfeited). Opponent building / hazard / teepee = 1 single auxiliary action only.

**Kansas City (5 subphases):** choose 1 foresight tile from each pair (workers > job market; teepees > Indian trade; hazards > hazard sections) > Income: breeding values of DIFFERENT types + certificates, take Dollars, discard hand > Delivery: disc onto city with value <= total (no repeats except Kansas City/San Francisco; transport = 1 Dollar per cross short) > cattleman to trail start; refill foresight spaces.

**Job market token:** row fills up > token moves down; yellow arrow = refill cattle market (7/10/13); red arrow out = GAME END (token taker gets 2 VP; everyone else one final turn).

**Workers:** cowboys = cattle market buying power; craftsmen = building placement (2 Dollars per required craftsman; 1 via craftsman-row actions); engineers = engine movement. Can't hire from the token's row. 5th/6th-space workers = 4 VP each.

**Limits:** hand 4 (max 6, 5 Dollars per increase); step 3-4 at start; temporary certificates 3 (then 4, then 6 — must clear 4 before 6 counts).

**End-game VP (11 categories):** 1 VP per 5 Dollars; building tile VP; city crest VP (green arrows need both crests; Kansas City disc = -6); station VP; hazard tiles (2-4); cattle card VP in deck; objective cards (+ full / - unfulfilled); station master tasks; 4 VP per 5th/6th-space worker; 3 VP marked disc space; 2 VP job market token. Most VP wins; ties share victory.
