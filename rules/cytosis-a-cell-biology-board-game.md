## Overview

Cytosis is a worker-placement game that takes place inside a human cell, for 2-5 players. Each player controls a set of colored flasks (their workers). On a turn, a player places one flask on any available location on the cell board; some locations grant resources (mRNA, ATP, Carbohydrates, Lipids, Proteins), while others let the player take an action (converting resources, purchasing cards, or working toward completing a card). Resources are spent to build Enzymes, Hormones, and Hormone Receptors — represented by Cell Component Cards — which score Health Points immediately on completion and, in several cases, again at the end of the game. The game is played across a series of rounds, each split into a flask-placement phase and an event/board-refresh phase, until the deck of Event Cards runs out. The player with the most Health Points at the end of the game wins.

The box also includes two optional bundled modules — a Virus Expansion (for 2-5 players) and a set of Epigenetic Modification Cards — that can be added on top of the Base Game; see Special Rules & Edge Cases.

## Setup

1. Decide whether to play the Base Game alone or add the optional modules. For Base Game only: remove all Virus Expansion components (Virus Attack Cell Component Cards, the 3 Virus Event Cards, the 2 Virus Expansion Goal Cards — "Virus Attack" and "Immune Response" — the 5 Antibody Dice, the 15 Antibody Markers, and the 5 Player Mats) and the 7 Epigenetic Modification Cards, and set them aside in the box.
2. Place the board Base Game side face up in the center of the play area.
3. Separate the macromolecule resources into piles by color near the board, and place the ATP Tokens in a pile near the board. Together these form the general resource stock. Place the 2 Grey Flasks on the Grey Flask area of the board.
4. Separate the Event Cards, Goal Cards, and Cell Component Cards. Shuffle the 12 Event Cards. Randomly remove Event Cards according to player count: remove 2 in a 2- or 3-player game, remove 1 in a 4-player game, remove 0 in a 5-player game. Place the remaining Event Cards face down as a deck near the board.
5. Shuffle the Goal Cards and place a number of them face up above the board equal to the number of players plus 1 (exception: in a 5-player game without the Virus Expansion, use all 5 Goal Cards). Return unused Goal Cards to the box.
6. Shuffle the Cell Component Cards and flip 4 face up into the 4 spots of the Cell Component Card Area. Place the remaining Cell Component Cards face down as a deck to the right of the Cell Component Card Area.
7. Deal each player 3 Cell Component Cards. Each player secretly chooses 2 of the 3 to keep and shuffles the unchosen card back into the Cell Component Card deck.
8. Each player places one of their Player Markers on the "0" space of the Health Track (the numeric track surrounding the board) — this is that player's Health Marker.
9. Determine the first player: whoever was sick most recently takes the First Player Marker and 2 ATP. Moving to that player's left, the next player takes 3 ATP, the next takes 4 ATP, and so on until every player has received ATP; this becomes each player's starting resource stock.
10. Each player then chooses 2 additional resources (any combination of ATP, mRNA, or Lipids) to add to their personal resource stock.
11. Give each player their Player Pieces (Flasks, Transport Vesicle Disks, and Player Markers), all in one color. The number of Flasks given depends on player count: 4 Flasks each in a 2- or 3-player game, 3 Flasks each in a 4-player game, 2 Flasks each in a 5-player game.

Players are now ready to begin.

## Turn Structure

Cytosis is played in rounds; each round has two phases.

**Phase 1 — Flask Placement.** The player holding the First Player Marker goes first, then play continues clockwise. On a turn, a player must place exactly one flask on any available spot on the board and immediately collect the resources or take the action associated with that spot. Play then passes to the left. Placement continues clockwise, one flask at a time, until every player has placed all of their flasks. Instead of placing a flask, a player may choose to collect a single mRNA, Lipid, or ATP, exhausting one of their flasks; an exhausted flask cannot be placed again that round. Only one flask may occupy each spot — once a spot is occupied it is unavailable for the rest of the round.

**Phase 2 — Event Card Revealed and Board Refreshed.** All players retrieve the flasks they placed during Phase 1, and any Grey Flasks return to the Grey Flask Area (Transport Vesicle Disks and any resources on them stay on the board). The top Event Card is revealed and its instructions are followed; this newly revealed card is the only active Event Card (any from previous rounds become inactive). The left-most Cell Component Card in the Cell Component Card Area is discarded, the remaining face-up cards slide left (without changing order) to fill the gap, and new cards are dealt from the top of the Cell Component Card deck to fill any newly opened spots. If the Cell Component Card deck runs out, the discard pile is shuffled to form a new deck.

**End of Game.** Revealing the last Event Card marks the last round. Players play through Phase 1 one final time and then skip Phase 2 entirely, proceeding directly to Scoring.

## Actions

**Collecting Resources** (added to a player's personal resource stock):
- **Nucleus (DNA Transcription):** flask on the spot marked 3 gives 3 black mRNA; flask on the spot marked 2 gives 2 black mRNA.
- **Plasma Membrane (Glucose Transporter):** flask on the spot marked 2 pays 2 ATP for 2 green Carbohydrate resources; flask on the spot marked 1 pays 1 ATP for 1 green Carbohydrate resource.
- **Mitochondria (ATP Production):** flask on the spot marked 2 or 3 gives 2 or 3 ATP respectively; flask on the bottom-most spot pays 1 green Carbohydrate resource for 6 ATP (if fewer than 6 ATP remain in the general stock, the player takes what is available).
- **Smooth ER (Lipid Synthesis):** flask on the spot marked 3 gives 3 yellow Lipid resources; flask on the spot marked 2 gives 2 yellow Lipid resources.
- **Free Ribosome (mRNA Translation):** trade any number of black mRNA resources from the personal stock for the same number of red Protein resources from the general stock.
- **Boost Event Card bonus:** while a Boost Event Card is active, the first player who places on any spot within the boosted location that round also receives the 1 free resource it added, in addition to that spot's normal reward.

**Purchasing Cell Component Cards:** placing a flask on a spot within the Cell Component Card Area requires paying the ATP cost shown next to that spot; the player then takes the card below their flask into their hand.

**Playing an Additional Resource Card:** this hand card may be played at any time on a player's turn (not tied to a flask placement). The player immediately takes the number of resources shown on the card and discards the card to the Cell Component Card discard pile.

**Completing Cell Component Cards** (built up over multiple turns/flask placements):

*Hormone and Hormone Receptor Cards* (Protein Hormone, Steroid Hormone, Protein Hormone Receptor, Steroid Hormone Receptor) are completed through the same 3-step pathway; each step is a separate flask placement, and a player must place at least the number of resources the card requires onto their Transport Vesicle Disk (they may place more):

| Card Type | Step 1 — ER | Step 1 action | Step 2 — Golgi Apparatus | Step 3 — Plasma Membrane |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Protein Hormone | Rough ER | Place a Transport Vesicle Disk on an available budding location; trade mRNA from personal stock 1-for-1 for Protein from the general stock (must start with mRNA — Protein cannot be placed directly from the stock here); place the Protein on the disk | Move the disk to an available budding location in the Golgi Apparatus; add 1 Carbohydrate resource from personal stock | Exocytosis (see below) |
| Steroid Hormone | Smooth ER (Steroid Hormone Synthesis spot) | Place a Transport Vesicle Disk on an available budding location; place Lipid resources from personal stock onto the disk | Move the disk to an available budding location in the Golgi Apparatus; add 1 Carbohydrate resource from personal stock | Exocytosis (see below) |
| Protein Hormone Receptor | Rough ER | Same as Protein Hormone (trade mRNA 1-for-1 for Protein, place on disk) | Move the disk to the Golgi Apparatus; add 1 Carbohydrate resource | Exocytosis (see below) |
| Steroid Hormone Receptor | Rough ER (exception — not Smooth ER) | Same as Protein Hormone (trade mRNA 1-for-1 for Protein, place on disk) | Move the disk to the Golgi Apparatus; add 1 **Lipid** resource instead of Carbohydrate (exception) | Exocytosis (see below) |

**Exocytosis (Plasma Membrane)** — the final step for any Hormone or Hormone Receptor Card: reveal the Cell Component Card being completed; move the Transport Vesicle Disk (with its resources) from the Golgi Apparatus, through the Plasma Membrane, and out of the cell; immediately pay the ATP cost shown on the card; return all macromolecule resources from the disk to the general resource stock (the Transport Vesicle Disk returns to its owner); gain the Health Points shown on the completed card.

If a player places a flask in the Smooth ER, Rough ER, or Golgi Apparatus and no budding Transport Vesicle location is available there, the active player chooses one Transport Vesicle Disk occupying that area (belonging to any player) to remove from the board and return to its owner, then places (or moves) their own Transport Vesicle Disk and its resources onto the newly opened location.

*Enzyme Cards* do not use a Transport Vesicle Disk. They are completed in two steps: place a flask in the Free Ribosome spot to trade black mRNA for red Protein resources, then place a flask in the Cytoplasm (Enzyme Synthesis) spot to reveal the Enzyme Card, immediately pay its ATP cost, pay the required Protein resources (and a Carbohydrate resource if the card requires one) from personal stock, and gain the Health Points shown.

*Alcohol Detoxification Cards* are completed in a single step: place a flask on the Alcohol Detoxification spot within the Smooth ER and pay the ATP cost shown on the card; the player immediately gains 1 Health Point.

**Example (Protein Hormone Card):** a sample card costing 2 ATP, 2 Protein, and 1 Carbohydrate for a reward of 7 Health Points is completed as: (1) flask in Rough ER — place a Transport Vesicle Disk, trade 2 mRNA for 2 Protein, place the Protein on the disk; (2) flask in Golgi Apparatus — move the disk there and add 1 Carbohydrate; (3) flask in Plasma Membrane Exocytosis — reveal the card, move the disk out through the membrane, pay the 2 ATP, return the resources to the general stock, and gain 7 Health Points. (A sample Steroid Hormone card shown in the rulebook costs 4 ATP, 3 Lipid, and 1 Carbohydrate for 9 Health Points, following the equivalent Smooth ER → Golgi Apparatus → Exocytosis path.)

**Taking the First Player Marker (Laureates in Biology):** placing a flask here requires taking the First Player Marker (keeping it for all subsequent rounds until claimed by another player) and then choosing one of: (1) take 1 ATP into personal stock, or (2) place one of the player's two Goal Markers on any available Goal Card above the board (once placed, a Goal Marker may never be moved, and a player may never place both of their Goal Markers on the same Goal Card; a maximum of 2 Goal Markers may be placed on each Goal Card). The first Goal Marker placed on each Goal Card immediately awards that player 3 Health Points; the second Goal Marker placed on that card receives no immediate Health Points.

**Placing a Grey Flask:** before placing their normal flask on a turn, a player may pay 4 ATP to place a Grey Flask first, in addition to their normal placement. The Grey Flask may go on any flask placement spot — available or already occupied — and the player then takes the action associated with that spot. Only one Grey Flask may be paid for and placed per turn, the player must be able to take the action of the chosen spot, and only Grey Flasks from the Grey Flask Area may be used this way.

## Scoring / Victory Conditions

**During the game:**
- Completing a Hormone, Hormone Receptor, Enzyme, or Alcohol Detoxification Card immediately scores the Health Points printed on that card (Alcohol Detoxification Cards always award exactly 1 Health Point).
- **Hormone Receptor passive scoring:** any time an opposing player completes a Protein Hormone Card, every player who has completed a Protein Hormone Receptor Card gains 2 Health Points for each such Receptor Card they have completed; the same applies for Steroid Hormone Cards and completed Steroid Hormone Receptor Cards. In addition, any time a player personally completes a Protein Hormone Card, they score 1 additional Health Point for each Protein Hormone Receptor Card they have already completed (and likewise 1 additional Health Point per completed Steroid Hormone Receptor Card when personally completing a Steroid Hormone Card).
- Placing the first Goal Marker on a Goal Card scores 3 Health Points immediately.

**End Game Scoring** (performed once, after the final Phase 1):
- Before scoring, a player may first collect any macromolecule resources remaining on their own Transport Vesicle Disks still on the board into their personal resource stock.
- **Leftover macromolecule resources:** 1 Health Point for every 4 mRNA resources, 1 Health Point for every 4 Lipid resources, 1 Health Point for every 3 Protein resources, and 1 Health Point for every 2 Carbohydrate resources remaining in the personal stock.
- **Alcohol Detoxification ranking:** a player's "Alcohol Detox" total is the sum of the ATP costs of all Alcohol Detoxification Cards they have completed (cards still in hand do not count). The player with the most Alcohol Detox gains 8 Health Points, the second-most gains 5, and the third-most gains 2; a player who has completed no Alcohol Detoxification Cards automatically scores 0 for this category. In a 2-player game, the most gains 6 Health Points and the second-most gains 3. Ties are broken first by whichever tied player has completed the most Alcohol Detoxification Cards; if still tied, combine the Health Points for the tied rank and the rank(s) immediately below it and divide evenly among the tied players, rounding down (e.g., two players tied for 2nd place combine the 2nd-place value (5) and 3rd-place value (2) = 7, divided by 2 = 3 each; a three-way tie combines that rank and all positions below it, divided among the tied players, rounding down).
- **Enzyme sets:** a "set" is made of completed Enzyme Cards of different colors (two Enzyme Cards of the same color cannot count toward the same set, though a player may score for two separate sets). Health Points earned by number of unique Enzyme colors completed: 2 unique colors = 2 Health Points, 3 = 5, 4 = 9, 5 = 14.
- **Goal Cards:** if a player has placed a Goal Marker on a Goal Card and has completed the number/type of Cell Component Cards that Goal Card specifies, they score the End Game points described on that Goal Card.

**Final Scoring / Victory:** whoever has the most total Health Points wins. If tied, the tied player with the most completed Cell Component Cards wins (only Alcohol Detoxification, Enzyme, Hormone, and Hormone Receptor Cards count toward this tiebreaker). If still tied, the tied player in the last seating position counting from the player holding the First Player Marker wins.

## Special Rules & Edge Cases

- **Grey Flasks:** a Grey Flask placement (4 ATP) is an extra placement in addition to a player's normal flask that turn, and can go on an occupied or unoccupied spot. Only one may be paid for per turn, and only Grey Flasks currently in the Grey Flask Area may be used.
- **Transport Vesicle location contention:** if the Smooth ER, Rough ER, or Golgi Apparatus has no free budding Transport Vesicle location when a player needs one, the active player bumps any one Transport Vesicle Disk in that area back to its owner to free up a spot for their own disk.
- **Cell Component Card Area refresh:** each round in Phase 2, the left-most card is discarded, remaining cards slide left, and new cards refill from the deck; if the deck is exhausted, the discard pile is reshuffled into a new deck.
- **Reduce ATP Cost Event Card:** every Cell Component Card purchased that round costs 1 less ATP; a player who places on the Free Spot while this card is active may also take 1 ATP from the general resource stock.
- **Toxicity Event Card:** players must return macromolecule resources from personal stock to the general stock: any player holding 5 or more of a given macromolecule loses 1 of it, 7 or more loses 2, and 10 or more loses 4.
- **Boost Event Card:** adds 1 free resource to a specific board location; it is awarded (in addition to the spot's normal reward) to the first player who places there, and otherwise remains on the board until claimed.

**2-Player Rule Modifications:**
- Setup: remove 3 Alcohol Detoxification Cards at random from the Cell Component Card deck before dealing hands or filling the Cell Component Card Area, and remove 2 Event Cards at random from the Event deck.
- Gameplay: only 1 flask may be placed in each placement *area* per round (not per numbered spot) — e.g., a flask on the Nucleus's [3] spot makes its [2] spot unavailable too, since both are the same area. Exceptions: the Cell Component Card Area still allows all 4 spots to be filled; the Mitochondria allows 2 of its 3 spots to be filled; the Smooth ER allows 2 spots (up to 1 in Lipid Synthesis and 1 in Alcohol Detoxification/Steroid Hormone Synthesis). A Grey Flask may still be placed in any occupied or unoccupied spot regardless of this restriction.
- Phase 2: discard the 2 left-most Cell Component Cards instead of 1.
- Scoring: Alcohol Detoxification ranking pays 6 Health Points for the most and 3 for the second-most (instead of 8/5/2).

**Virus Expansion (optional module, for 2-5 players):**
- Setup adds: place the board Virus Expansion side up; when shuffling the Event deck, remove 5 Event Cards at random in a 2- or 3-player game, 3 in a 4-player game, or 1 in a 5-player game, then shuffle the 3 Virus Event Cards into what remains; shuffle the Immune Response and Virus Attack Goal Cards in with the 5 Base Game Goal Cards before revealing (players + 1) of them (in a 2- or 3-player game, if both Virus Goal Cards are revealed together, replace one at random with a different Goal Card); shuffle the 6 Virus Attack Cell Component Cards into the Cell Component Card deck after dealing starting hands; give each player a Player Mat and 3 Antibody Markers, placed on the "0" spot of each of the three virus-strain columns (Ebola, Influenza, Rhinovirus) on their mat; place the 5 Antibody Dice near the board. In a 2-player game, set up one extra Player Mat as a "dummy" third player.
- **When Viruses Attack** — triggered whenever a Virus Event Card is revealed, or whenever a Virus Attack Cell Component Card is purchased:
  - Each player rolls a number of Antibody Dice based on their Health Marker's position on the Health Track: 1 die if in the red section, 2 dice if in the yellow section, 3 dice if in the green section (for example, 14 Health Points falls in the yellow section, rolling 2 dice).
  - Each player adds the number of antibodies from the column on their Player Mat matching the attacking virus strain to their dice roll; the total is that player's Antibody Response.
  - Players compare Antibody Responses and gain or lose resources based on rank; a player gaining resources chooses any combination of ATP, mRNA, and Lipids from the general stock, and a player losing a resource chooses to lose any resource from their personal stock or lose 1 Health Point instead. In a worked 3-player example, the highest Antibody Response gained 3 resources, the second-highest gained 1 resource, and the lowest lost 1 resource.
  - After resources are resolved, each player (starting with the First Player and moving in turn order) may pay ATP to raise their Antibody Marker on the matching virus-strain column; each ATP paid moves the marker up one level (2 antibodies).
  - If a Virus Attack Cell Component Card was purchased (rather than an Event Card being revealed), only the purchasing player may pay ATP to raise antibodies; every other player instead loses 1 Health Point outright (players can go negative on the Health Track), and then all players still follow the same Antibody Response steps above.
  - In a 2-player game, the "dummy" player rolls Antibody Dice based on the lower of the two real players' Health Track positions, participates in the resource comparison (its gains/losses stay in the general stock), and then has its own Antibody Marker advanced one level. Ties in Antibody Response are won by the first player (or the player closest to the First Player Marker); in a 2-player game, a tie between a real player and the dummy is won by the dummy.

**Epigenetic Modification Cards (optional module, usable with the Base Game or combined with the Virus Expansion):** recommended only after players are already familiar with the base game. Setup: determine the first player before the Cell Component Card deal step; shuffle the 7 cards and deal 3 to the first player, who secretly keeps 1 and replaces it with a random card from the remaining pool before passing the resulting 3 cards to the player on their left; each player in turn secretly keeps 1 and replaces/passes onward until every player holds one card; unused cards return to the box; each player keeps their card face up in front of them for the rest of the game and may use its ability only on their own turn (unless the card states otherwise). The 7 distinct abilities in the deck (card titles are not given in the rulebook text) are:
1. Trade ATP for Lipids or mRNA at 1 ATP each, or for Carbohydrates at 2 ATP each (any number of times per turn, but not the reverse — macromolecules cannot be traded for ATP).
2. Pay 2 ATP to secretly look at the top 3 Cell Component Cards of the deck; optionally pay 2 more ATP to take one into hand, then return the rest to the top of the deck in any order (once per turn). If a Virus Attack Card is taken this way while playing with the Virus Expansion, it must be played immediately per the Virus Expansion rules.
3. Collect 1 additional macromolecule or ATP each time placing on a numbered resource-collection spot (e.g., a [3] spot yields 4, a [2] spot yields 3); Carbohydrates still cost 1 ATP each regardless.
4. Pay 3 ATP (instead of 4) to place a Grey Flask, otherwise following the normal Grey Flask rules.
5. Once per round, before the First Player places their first flask, this player may pay 1 ATP to place one flask early, then still take their normal turn in first-player order afterward.
6. At the start of every round, collect 1 ATP, 1 mRNA, or 1 Lipid from the general resource stock for free.
7. Purchase Cell Component Cards for free every time a flask is placed in the Cell Component Card Area.

## Player Reference

**Round checklist:**
1. Phase 1 (Flask Placement): starting with the First Player and proceeding clockwise, each player places 1 flask on an available spot and immediately resolves it (or exhausts a flask for 1 mRNA/Lipid/ATP instead). Repeat until all flasks are placed.
2. Phase 2 (Event & Refresh): retrieve flasks (Grey Flasks return to the Grey Flask Area); reveal and resolve the next Event Card; discard the left-most Cell Component Card, slide the rest left, and refill from the deck (reshuffle the discard pile if the deck is empty).
3. When the last Event Card is revealed: play Phase 1 once more, then skip Phase 2 and go straight to Scoring.

**Setup numbers by player count:**

| Players | Flasks each | Goal Cards revealed | Event Cards removed before shuffle-in (Base) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 4 | 3 | 2 |
| 3 | 4 | 4 | 2 |
| 4 | 3 | 5 | 1 |
| 5 | 2 | 6 (or all 5 if no Virus Expansion) | 0 |

**Resource collection quick reference:**

| Location | Cost | Yield |
|---|---|---|
| Nucleus | Free | 3 mRNA (top spot) or 2 mRNA (lower spot) |
| Plasma Membrane (Glucose Transporter) | 2 ATP / 1 ATP | 2 Carbohydrate / 1 Carbohydrate |
| Mitochondria | Free / Free / 1 Carbohydrate | 3 ATP / 2 ATP / up to 6 ATP |
| Smooth ER (Lipid Synthesis) | Free | 3 Lipid (top spot) or 2 Lipid (lower spot) |
| Free Ribosome | mRNA (any amount) | Same amount of Protein |

**End-game scoring quick reference:**

| Source | Value |
|---|---|
| Leftover mRNA / Lipid | 1 Health Point per 4 |
| Leftover Protein | 1 Health Point per 3 |
| Leftover Carbohydrate | 1 Health Point per 2 |
| Alcohol Detox — 1st / 2nd / 3rd | 8 / 5 / 2 (2-player game: 6 / 3) |
| Enzyme sets — 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 unique colors | 2 / 5 / 9 / 14 Health Points |
| Goal Card first marker placed | 3 Health Points (immediate) |

**Key numbers:** Grey Flask cost = 4 ATP (3 ATP with the matching Epigenetic card). Free Ribosome trade ratio = 1 mRNA : 1 Protein. Toxicity Event thresholds = 5+/7+/10+ of a macromolecule loses 1/2/4. Victory = most Health Points; tiebreak 1 = most completed Cell Component Cards (Detox/Enzyme/Hormone/Receptor only); tiebreak 2 = last seated position from the First Player Marker holder.
