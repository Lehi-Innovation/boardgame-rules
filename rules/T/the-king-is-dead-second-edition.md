# The King Is Dead: Second Edition

> ⚠️ **Unverified** — this independently written rules reference is based on the publisher's official Second Edition rulebook, but has not passed the repository's automated semantic verification.
>
> 📄 [Official rulebook PDF (Osprey Games)](https://www.ospreypublishing.com/media/3yxddtqg/tkid2_rulebook.pdf) · [Source reference](https://lehi-innovation.github.io/boardgame-rules/extracted/the-king-is-dead-second-edition-rules.txt)

## Overview

Players influence Scottish, Welsh, and English factions competing over eight regions of Britain. Each player has only eight action cards for the entire game. Manipulating a region and recruiting followers to your own court have competing incentives: recruiting a follower from the board increases your potential political influence but removes that follower from a region. The game ends with either a coronation after eight power struggles or an early French invasion after three unstable regions.

## Components

- 54 follower cubes (18 per faction: Scottish, Welsh, English)
- 24 control discs (8 per faction), 3 instability discs, 4 negotiation discs
- Eight region cards, one victory card, board, cloth bag
- Four identical sets of eight basic action cards (32 total); 12 optional cunning action cards

## Setup

1. **Two players only:** Remove two followers of each faction from the game first.
2. Put two Scottish followers in **Moray**, two Welsh in **Gwynedd**, and two English in **Essex**.
3. Put the other followers into the bag. Randomly deal each player two followers into their personal **court**.
4. Randomly populate every region to **four followers total** (including the two preplaced followers in each home region). Put unused followers in the supply; return bag to the box.
5. Give each player one negotiation disc. Put control discs in the supply and all three instability discs in France.
6. Give each player eight basic action cards: Scottish Support, Welsh Support, English Support, Negotiate, Manoeuvre, Outmanoeuvre, and two Assemble cards.
7. Shuffle the eight region cards and place them face up beside numbered spaces 1–8. Put the victory card nearby.
8. The most recent visitor to a castle starts. Turns go clockwise.
9. **Optional advanced game:** By unanimous agreement, replace each player's three faction Support cards with three secretly dealt cunning action cards. Do not reveal unused cunning cards.

## Turn Structure

On your turn choose **one**:
- **Take an action:** Play one card from your hand, resolve it, then **always summon exactly one follower from any region to your court**. The summoned follower need not come from the region affected by the action, but cannot come directly from supply. Discard the used action card face up. Cards cannot be reused; you have at most eight actions total.
- **Pass:** Do nothing now, but you may play a card on a subsequent turn.

**When every player passes consecutively**, immediately resolve the next power struggle, then resume normal turns with the next player. Passing is not a permanent withdrawal.

### Resolve a power struggle

1. Identify the *lowest-numbered face-up region card*; its corresponding region is contested.
2. If one faction has strictly more followers there than either rival, it controls that region. Place its control disc.
3. If there is a tie for the lead or the region is empty, put an **instability disc** there instead (no faction controls it).
4. Return **all** followers in that region to supply. Turn its region card face down.
5. A third instability disc ends the game *immediately* in a French invasion; otherwise play continues or ends after the eighth struggle.

**Locked regions:** After a region gains a control or instability disc, no actions may place or move followers into it. You may target a different region from the current contested one.

**Partial actions:** Carry out as much of a played card's effect as possible. If none is possible, the card can still be played, and you still summon a follower to court.

## Basic Action Cards

- **Scottish / Welsh / English Support:** Add two followers of the indicated faction from supply into **one** eligible region bordering a region controlled by that faction. Until that faction's home region has either a control or instability disc, you may instead use a region bordering its home (**Moray** / **Gwynedd** / **Essex**, respectively). Place as many of the two as supply permits.
- **Negotiate:** Swap any two face-up region cards that do not already bear negotiation discs, and put your negotiation disc on one of those cards. If fewer than two eligible cards remain, no swap occurs.
- **Manoeuvre:** Exchange one follower in one region with one in another region. Same-faction swaps are permitted. You cannot directly undo another player's previous Manoeuvre unless some other action has occurred since.
- **Outmanoeuvre:** Exchange one follower in one region with two followers in an **adjacent** region. If a full exchange is impossible, exchange one for one where possible. You cannot directly undo another player's previous Outmanoeuvre unless a different action intervened.
- **Assemble** (two copies per player): Add one follower of **each** faction from supply into any eligible region(s), together or separately. Skip any color unavailable in supply.

For Manoeuvre and Outmanoeuvre, the required legal swap must be performed if possible, even if strategically unhelpful. All movement restrictions still apply.

## Scoring / Victory Conditions

### French invasion

Triggered immediately on placement of the **third instability disc**. Each player's score is the number of **complete trios** of followers in their court (one of each faction), i.e. the smallest of their three follower counts. Most complete trios wins. **Tiebreak:** Among tied players, whoever most recently played an action card wins.

### Coronation

Triggered when all **eight** regions have resolved without a French invasion.

1. Rank factions by **number of regions they control**. Ties between factions are decided by which faction **most recently won a power struggle**.
2. The player with the most court followers of the **top-ranked faction** wins.
3. Tiebreak: count court followers of the **second-ranked faction** among tied players.
4. Further tiebreak: the player who **first played all their action cards** wins (that is, finished using cards earliest).

### Four-player teams

Players opposite each other are teammates. **No tactical discussion or showing cards.** Each player has their own court and action cards.
- **Invasion:** Combine teammates' court followers and count complete trios; tied teams break by last team to play an action card.
- **Coronation:** Keep the courts separate; compare each player's followers as normal, and that player's teammate also wins. The final card-exhaustion tiebreak is decided at team level.

## Advanced Game: Cunning Cards

Each player substitutes three unique, secret cunning cards for their three basic Support cards, retaining the other five action cards.

- **Spy:** Copy the face-up top action card of another player's discard pile.
- **Ambush:** Add two Scottish followers from supply to one region, then return one follower from that region to supply; may remove one just added.
- **March:** Move two followers from one region into a single neighboring region. Move one if two are impossible.
- **Plot:** Cannot be played as an action. Reveal when deciding the winner: counts as one follower of any faction you choose. In a coronation, if still tied in followers among the two strongest factions, Plot beats the normal final card-exhaustion tiebreak.
- **Aid:** Choose the faction with the most followers in supply (choose freely among tied factions); place two of its followers into one region.
- **Influence:** Swap one English follower from one region with two non-English followers from another; fall back to one-for-one if necessary.
- **Dispute:** Swap one Welsh follower from one region with one non-Welsh follower from another.
- **Edict:** Swap two Scottish followers from one region with two non-Scottish followers in an adjacent region. If impossible, try 1-for-2 or 2-for-1, then 1-for-1.
- **Resist:** Add two non-Scottish followers to a single region bordering a Scottish-controlled region; prior to Moray resolving, may use a region bordering Moray.
- **Muster:** In a region bordering Scottish-controlled territory (or Moray before it resolves), remove one Scottish follower to supply, then add two followers from supply into that same region. If no Scottish follower is available in an eligible region, add two without removal.
- **Quell:** Welsh equivalent of Muster using Gwynedd.
- **Suppress:** English home/controlled-border action: remove an English follower and another follower from one eligible region, then add a follower from supply there. Follow the partial-action fallback in the official rulebook if a required follower is absent.

## Important Clarifications

- **Recruitment is mandatory after *every* played action**, including a card that produces no board change. A follower must come from a region, not the supply.
- **You may pass repeatedly** and still play cards later.
- **Court followers are never returned** during power struggles.
- **Negotiation discs mark region cards**, blocking further swaps of those cards; they do not mark regions on the board.
- **Faction ranking** for coronation uses controlled *regions*, not total followers on the board.
- Consult the [official rulebook](https://www.ospreypublishing.com/media/3yxddtqg/tkid2_rulebook.pdf) for exact card wording and unusual partial-action interactions; the PDF is the source of authority.
