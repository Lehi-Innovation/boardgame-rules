## Overview

Heat: Pedal to the Metal is a racing game where players drive cars around a track for a set number of laps. Each round, players shift gears (determining how many cards to play), play speed cards to move, and manage their engine's heat to avoid spinning out in corners. The first player to cross the finish line after the required number of laps wins.

## Components

- 2 double-sided game boards with 4 race tracks (USA, France, Great Britain, Italy)
- 6 player mats (one per player color)
- 6 race cars (one per player color)
- 6 Gear pawns (one per player color)
- 72 Speed cards (12 per player)
- 18 Starting Upgrade cards (3 per player)
- 37 Stress cards
- 48 Heat cards
- Rules booklet

## Setup

1. Place the game board in the center (USA track recommended for first game). Use the number of laps printed on the selected board. For a first USA learning race, you may race just 1 lap instead of its normal 2.
2. Each player chooses a color and takes: car, player mat, Gear pawn, 12 Speed cards, and 3 Starting Upgrade cards.
3. Check the board for how many Heat and Stress cards each player adds (typically 6 Heat and 3 Stress).
4. Place player mat in front of you. Shuffle your 12 Speed cards, 3 Starting Upgrade cards, and 3 Stress cards together as your draw deck (left spot on mat).
5. Place 6 Heat cards face up on the Engine spot (middle of mat).
6. Set Gear pawn to 1st gear.
7. Place the remaining Stress cards face up near the board as a reserve.
8. Randomly assign participating cars to the starting grid, placing them one at a time and filling the numbered spots in ascending order. This determines first-round order; players do not choose preferred starting positions.
9. Draw 7 cards into your hand.

**Optional handicap:** An experienced driver racing beginners may remove 1 or 2 Heat from their own car to level the playing field.

## Turn Structure

Each round, steps 1, 2, 3 and 9 are mandatory; steps 4–8 are situational. All players complete steps 1 and 2 simultaneously, in that order. Wait until everyone has finished both steps before starting step 3.

### Simultaneous Phase (Steps 1-2)
1. **Shift Gears** -- Stay in your current gear, or shift up or down one position for free. You may instead shift two positions by immediately paying 1 Heat during this step: move a Heat card from your Engine to your discard pile. You must have that Heat available to pay.
2. **Play Cards** -- Play cards from hand face down equal to your gear number (1st gear = 1 card, 2nd = 2, 3rd = 3, 4th = 4).

### Sequential Phase (Steps 3-9, front car first)

Each player completes all of steps 3–9 before the next player begins step 3. Take turns from the frontmost car to the last. When cars are side by side, the car closest to the Race Line is ahead for all purposes, including turn order.

3. **Reveal & Move** -- Flip cards, resolve every played Stress card, sum values, and move your car forward exactly that many spaces. You cannot voluntarily stop short to avoid a corner; a full destination can force shorter movement as described under Blocked Movement. Finish resolving Stress before Adrenaline. Place your car on the spot closest to the Race Line if available.
4. **Adrenaline** -- The last car to move gains access to 1 extra Cooldown and may add 1 extra speed. You may use either benefit, both, or neither: declining the extra speed does not prevent using the extra Cooldown. If the race started with 5 or more cars, the last two cars to move receive these benefits instead. Use the number of cars that started the race, even after finishers leave the track. Use these benefits during React; the extra speed may be taken before or after Boost. Unused Adrenaline cannot be saved for another round.
5. **React** -- Activate available Cooldown and/or Boost symbols in any order. You may Cooldown Heat from your hand into your Engine, then immediately spend it to Boost.
6. **Slipstream** -- If you end next to another car or in the space immediately behind a car, optionally make one move of 2 spaces forward. Qualifying next to or behind multiple cars does not multiply this move. This does NOT increase your speed for corner checks, but crossing a Corner Line this way still requires checking that corner. A full landing space uses the normal Blocked Movement placement rule; Slipstream does not permit sharing an occupied spot, and any shorter movement still does not change your corner-check speed. On the final lap, you cannot Slipstream across the finish line or after crossing it.
7. **Check Corner** -- Check every Corner Line crossed this turn, including those crossed while Slipstreaming, in the order crossed. For each corner separately, compare your total speed for the round with its Speed Limit and pay Heat equal to the excess, if any. Use the same total speed for each corner; include Boost and Adrenaline speed, but exclude Slipstream movement. If your speed does not exceed a corner's limit, pay nothing for that corner.
8. **Discard** -- Optionally discard unwanted cards from hand, but never Stress or Heat cards. Nobody may browse any discard pile, including their own; only the top card of each pile is public knowledge.
9. **Replenish Hand** -- Move all Play Area cards to your discard pile, then draw only enough cards to bring your hand back to 7. Do not draw seven additional cards. Then the next player starts their steps 3–9.

## Actions

### Cooldown
You may move Heat cards from your hand back to your Engine, up to your available Cooldown allowance. Cooling fewer cards or none is allowed. Available from 1st gear (Cooldown 3) and 2nd gear (Cooldown 1), or from Adrenaline. Adrenaline adds 1 to your allowance, so 1st gear with Adrenaline permits cooling up to 4 Heat from hand.

### Paying Heat
Whenever you pay Heat, move that many Heat cards from your Engine to your discard pile. Heat cards in your hand cannot pay a Heat cost. You cannot choose to Boost without a Heat card available in your Engine; you may first use available Cooldown during React to return Heat from hand to Engine.

### Boost
Once per turn during React, pay 1 Heat from Engine to discard. Flip cards from your draw deck, immediately discarding each non-Speed card, until you reveal a Speed card. Add that Speed card to your Play Area and move its value. Boost increases your total speed for corner checks. Use the same flip procedure as for Stress.

### Stress Cards
For each Stress card you played, flip cards from your draw deck until a Speed card appears (speed 1–4). Immediately discard each flipped non-Speed card, including Heat, Stress and Starting Upgrade cards. Put the found Speed card in your Play Area on top of the played Stress card; its value supplies that Stress card's speed. The played Stress card stays in the Play Area underneath it. Discard both during Replenish Hand. Resolve all played Stress cards before moving on to Adrenaline.

## Scoring / Victory Conditions

- First player to cross the finish line after the required number of laps wins.
- If multiple cars finish in the same round, the car furthest ahead wins (closest to Race Line breaks ties).
- On the final lap, you cannot Slipstream across the finish line, and cannot Slipstream after crossing it. After crossing that final finish line, ignore the speed limits of corners beyond it and move as far as you can.
- On intermediate laps, an otherwise legal Slipstream may cross the start/finish line, and normal corner speed limits still apply beyond it. The finish-line Slipstream restriction and exemption from later corner limits apply only to the final lap.
- At the end of the round, remove all cars that crossed the final finish line and place them in finishing order on the Hall of Fame. Continue playing until all cars finish. Adrenaline eligibility still uses the number of cars that started the race, not the number still racing.

## Special Rules & Edge Cases

- **Spin Out:** If you cannot pay enough Heat for a corner, move all remaining Engine Heat to your discard pile and spin out. Move your car back to the first available space before the corner that caused the spin out. Take 1 extra Stress card directly into your hand if you were in 1st/2nd gear, or 2 if you were in 3rd/4th gear. Then move your Gear pawn to 1st gear.
- **Cluttered Hand:** If Heat cards leave you with too few playable cards for your gear, commit all playable cards you can and fill the remaining required slots with Heat cards in your Play Area. Your car moves zero spaces. Set your gear to 1st, discard all committed cards (including the covering Heat cards), and skip directly to step 9 to replenish. You skip React, so cannot Cooldown or Boost this turn. This forced handling is an exception to the normal prohibition on playing or discarding Heat from hand.
- **Blocked Movement:** Cars never block passage, but if your destination space is full, place your car in the first free spot behind the blocking cars.
- **Track Lines:** In the core game, a Corner Line triggers a corner check when crossed. The Legends Line is used only in advanced play; crossing it alone is not a core-game corner-payment trigger.
- **Running Out of Cards:** Whenever you must draw or flip but your draw deck is empty, immediately shuffle your discard pile into a new draw deck, then continue the interrupted draws or flips. This applies during Stress, Boost and Replenish Hand. Do not include cards still in your Play Area; they have not yet been discarded.
- **Heat Card Cycle:** Heat moves from Engine to discard to draw deck to hand; only returns to Engine via Cooldown.
- Heat cards cannot normally be played or voluntarily discarded from hand; see the forced Cluttered Hand exception above.
