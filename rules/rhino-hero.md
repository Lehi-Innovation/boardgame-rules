## Overview

Build a shared tower of folded walls and roof cards while trying to empty your own hand. Roof symbols change turn order, require extra draws or an additional roof, and send the Rhino Hero figure up the tower. The first player to place their last roof wins; if the tower collapses, the responsible player loses and the other players compare their remaining hands.

This covers the original **Rhino Hero**, not Rhino Hero: Super Battle. [Publisher rulebook](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0559/3445/1810/files/RhinoHero-Instructions.pdf?v=1732061492), printed pages 3–7 (PDF pages 2–6).

## Components

- **31 roof cards**, also called action cards.
- **28 wall cards**, which are bent to support the floors.
- **1 double-sided foundation card** and **1 Rhino Hero figure**.
- Instructions.

The marks on the foundation or uppermost roof specify the wall positions for the next floor. Roof symbols determine special effects. Each side of the foundation supplies a different starting difficulty. See printed page 3.

## Setup

1. Put the foundation in the center of the table. Choose the easier front or the expert reverse side.
2. Shuffle the roof cards. Deal **5 roofs each** with 3–5 players, or **7 roofs each** with 2 players.
3. Put the remaining roofs in a draw pile away from the tower. Keep all walls and Rhino Hero within reach.
4. The player who most recently did a good deed starts. If no agreement is reached, the youngest player starts. Play initially proceeds clockwise.

The starting hand is a pool of roofs to be played, not a hand that is refilled automatically after every turn. See printed pages 4–5.

## Turn Structure

1. Resolve any obligation imposed by the preceding roof, such as drawing a card, skipping your turn, or moving Rhino Hero.
2. Read the wall marks on the current top card. Bend and place the indicated **one or two walls** at the marked positions.
3. Put one roof from your hand on the new walls, aligned in the same orientation as the foundation.
4. Apply its symbol as described below. Unless an effect changes the order, the next player clockwise takes a turn.

A double roof adds another roof directly on top without a new set of walls between the two. Building and moving the figure require care: a collapse ends the game immediately. See printed pages 5–6.

## Actions

### Build a floor

Use the exact number and placement of walls indicated by the foundation or previous roof. Fold the walls to match the marks, then cover them with a roof from your hand. Both hands may be used, but you may touch only the wall or roof you are placing, apart from moving Rhino Hero when required. Do not hold or straighten earlier floors as support.

### Resolve roof symbols

| Symbol | Effect |
|---|---|
| Change of direction | Reverse the direction of play. Ignore this symbol with two players. |
| Take a breather | The next player loses a turn. With two players, the current player takes the next turn too. |
| Additional card | The next player draws **1 roof** before building. |
| Double roof | Place a second roof directly on the first. The second roof cannot itself be a double-roof card. |
| Rhino Hero | The next player moves the figure from its current location onto the newly printed Rhino Hero mark before placing their roof. |

For a double roof, if you have no regular roof available as the second card, ask the player on your **left** to give you one of theirs; this helps them empty their hand. Playing a double roof as your final card still wins. The rulebook permits you then to help your neighbor by adding another roof. See printed pages 5–6.

## Scoring / Victory Conditions

The game ends on any of these conditions:

1. **A player places their last roof:** that player wins immediately.
2. **The tower collapses:** the player responsible loses. Among the other players, the fewest remaining roofs wins. Break a tie in favor of the tied player with the **most special symbols** on their remaining roofs. A further tie is a shared victory.
3. **All 28 walls have been built into the tower:** all players win together, an unusually rare outcome.

There is no running point score. Hand size matters only when comparing eligible players after a collapse. See printed page 7.

## Special Rules & Edge Cases

- Reversal does nothing with two players, while a skip gives the same player consecutive turns. These symbols are not equivalent in a two-player game.
- A roof is played only after its supporting walls have been built. Roof marks govern the **following** floor, not the floor underneath that roof.
- Never stack a double-roof card directly onto another double-roof card as its bonus roof. Obtain a regular roof from your left neighbor if required.
- Having a double roof as your only card does not prevent victory: playing it as your last roof wins.
- A collapsing player cannot win through a low remaining hand. Exclude that player before applying the hand-size and special-symbol tiebreaks.
- Both hands are legal, but supporting the existing tower by touching older walls or roofs is not. Position the new roof in the foundation's orientation rather than rotating it to make the next wall pattern easier.

## Player Reference

| Item | Number or rule |

| Starting roofs, 2 players | 7 each |
| Starting roofs, 3–5 players | 5 each |
| Walls per normal floor | 1 or 2, as marked |
| Roofs used for ordinary building | 1 |
| Extra card symbol | Next player draws 1 before building |
| Collapse tiebreak | Fewest roofs, then most special symbols |
| All walls used | Everyone wins |

Before building, check the previous roof's effect; place its marked walls; move the figure when required; add your roof and pass play in the current direction. Keep the physical tower and the active symbol visible to everyone.
