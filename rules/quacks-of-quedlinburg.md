## Overview

Over **9 rounds**, each player draws ingredients from a private bag to make a potion. Longer potions provide more spending money and points, but a total of **more than 7 white-chip value** normally explodes the pot. Buy ingredients to improve future draws. Most victory points after round 9 wins.

Source: [publisher's English rules](https://www.schmidtspiele.de/files/Retail/72dpi_PNG/88220_Quack_rules_english_2024.pdf), PDF pages 2–8. The PDF contains the blue-ingredient almanac on page 1; the remaining recipe effects below also use the [ingredient-almanac transcription](https://www.ultraboardgames.com/the-quacks-of-quedlinburg/ingredients.php). These are the original Quacks rules, without either expansion.

## Setup

1. The player who most recently cooked takes the shuffled Fortune Teller deck. Put the scoring board centrally, flame on round 1, and colored seals on their starting positions showing zero.
2. Put out orange and black books; use the black-book side matching **2 players** or **3–4 players**. Choose a matched recipe set for the other five colors, identified by its number of bookmarks; start with set 1.
3. Initially expose only the green, blue, and red books. Add yellow before round 2 and purple before round 3. Sort chips by color and value beside their books.
4. Each player takes a pot with the **non-test-tube side** up, bag, scoring marker on their seal, droplet on pot space zero, full flask, rat stone beside the pot, and **1 ruby**. The starting ruby is explicitly included in the publisher's 2024 rules.
5. Put **4 white 1s, 2 white 2s, 1 white 3, 1 orange 1, and 1 green 1** in each bag: 9 chips total, with white values totaling 11.

## Turn Structure

### Round opening

Read one Fortune Teller card for everyone. Purple cards resolve immediately; blue cards modify that round or its end. Yellow/purple rewards are available only after those books have entered play.

Starting in round 2, count rat-tail symbols on the scoring track between your marker and the leader. Place your rat stone that many pot spaces beyond your droplet. Count symbols, not the point difference. A tied leader receives no rat-tail head start. Before round 6, everyone adds **one white 1-chip** to their bag.

### Potions Phase

Players draw independently and simultaneously. After every draw, resolve placement and its effect, then choose to stop or continue. No player may look inside their bag. Stop if your bag runs out. In round 9, the start player calls each draw so all active players reveal a chip together; an empty hand means stopping. Players may agree to this synchronized method in earlier rounds too.

### Evaluation Phase, in order

Your **scoring space is the next space after your last chip**, including an explosion-causing chip. Do not use the occupied space itself. Resolve the following steps; ordered benefits/purchases start with the Fortune Teller holder and proceed clockwise.

| Step | Resolution |
|---|---|
| A. Bonus die | Among nonexploded pots, player(s) whose scoring space is farthest along roll once each. Equal printed money on two different spaces does not tie their distance. |
| B. Chip effects | Resolve black, green, and purple book effects; exploded pots participate. |
| C. Ruby | Gain 1 ruby if your scoring space shows one; exploded pots participate. |
| D. Points | Gain the points printed at your scoring space. |
| E. Shopping | Spend the scoring space's money on up to 2 chips of different colors. |
| F. End | Spend rubies, collect chips back into bags, remove rat stones, pass the Fortune Teller deck clockwise, and advance the flame. |

**If your pot exploded, choose D or E; you do not get both.** A nonexploded pot gets both. Separate bonuses from cards or chip effects are not canceled by explosion.

## Actions

### Place ingredients and resolve explosions

Place the first chip as many spaces beyond your droplet or rat stone as the chip's value. Each later chip advances that many spaces from the previous chip. Leave skipped spaces empty. Optional ingredient effects may alter placement or provide a reward.

Only white chips contribute to the explosion total. Normally **7 is safe; 8 or more explodes**. Leave the chip that caused the explosion in the pot and draw no more chips. Check altered thresholds if an active book or event changes them.

### Flask

Immediately after drawing and placing a white chip, you may empty your full flask to return that chip to the bag, then continue or stop. **You cannot use the flask if that chip already caused an explosion.** An empty flask must be refilled in step F for 2 rubies before it can be used again.

### Shopping and rubies

Spend only this round's money. Buy zero, one, or two available chips; two purchased chips must have **different colors**, even if their values differ. Supplies are limited, and unspent money is lost. After everyone finishes shopping, put purchased and drawn chips into bags.

During step F, each payment of **2 rubies** may advance your droplet one space or refill your flask. You may make multiple payments. Droplet movement changes where future potions start; it does not shift the chips already in the pot.

## Scoring / Victory Conditions

Add points each round from the scoring space, bonus die, cards, and ingredient actions. Die results are **1 point, 2 points, 1 ruby, 1 orange 1-chip, or one droplet advance**, as shown by the rolled face. Move aside an occupied chip if necessary when advancing the droplet; do not shift the potion.

At the pot's endpoint, place a chip reaching or exceeding space **33** on 33 and use the spoon's **15 points and 35 coins**. All nonexploded players reaching this endpoint qualify for the die. Without overflow bowls from the expansion, there is no additional overflow scoring.

At the end of round 9, convert money at **5 coins per point** and rubies at **2 rubies per point**, repeating as affordable. An exploded pot still chooses points **or** shopping money; money-to-points conversion does not remove that choice.

Most points wins. Tied players compare how far they filled their pots in the final round. If still tied, they share the win.

## Special Rules & Edge Cases

- Rats are temporary head starts, recalculated each round. Droplets remain advanced permanently. A point lead without an intervening rat-tail symbol grants no rat movement.
- An explosion removes the die eligibility and forces the points/money choice; it does not erase the chip's distance, ruby reward, or evaluation-B effects.
- Spaces occupied by ingredients and the next scoring space serve different purposes. A blue-chip landing bonus checks its occupied space; the evaluation ruby checks the scoring space.
- Ingredient effects are optional where the book offers an action. They can chain: a chip placed through blue set 1 may itself activate an effect.
- No upgrades apply to orange pumpkins, including the expansion's orange 6s. Treat the printed book as authoritative for available values and supply.
- For the optional test-tube side, put a second droplet on the leftmost tube. Whenever allowed a droplet advance, choose either droplet. Moving the tube droplet one glass right grants the printed bonus immediately: ruby, points, or the indicated chips. Explosion rules still apply.
- Matched ingredient sets are recommended; experienced players may combine books from different sets by agreement. Their prices change as well as their effects.
