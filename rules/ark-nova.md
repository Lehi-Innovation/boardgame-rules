## Overview

Develop a zoo by building enclosures, playing animals and sponsors, and supporting conservation projects. Every turn uses one action card; its position among five slots determines its strength. Appeal generates income, while conservation projects provide valuable bonuses and move a second scoring counter in the opposite direction. The game ends when a player's appeal and conservation counters share or cross a scoring area.

This summary covers the original base game and its solo mode, using [Capstone's rulebook](https://capstone-games.com/cdn/shop/files/Ark-Nova-Rulebook.pdf?v=15269496103518830006), [glossary](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0947/3907/1278/files/Ark-Nova-Glossary.pdf?v=1754428544), and [FAQ](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0947/3907/1278/files/Ark-Nova-FAQ-v2.pdf?v=1754428544). Individual card text and map abilities apply in addition to these rules; the glossary provides card-by-card rulings. Marine Worlds and additional map packs are separate expansions.

## Components

The base-game inventory is listed on rulebook pages 2–3:

- 1 game board, 1 Association board, and 8 double-sided zoo maps: four Map A and four Map 0 fronts, with advanced Maps 1–8 on the backs.
- 212 Zoo cards: 128 Animals, 64 Sponsors, and 20 Conservation Projects; 12 additional Base Conservation Projects; 11 Final Scoring cards; 20 double-sided Action cards in four sets of five.
- 90 standard enclosures: 28 size-1, 24 size-2, 15 size-3, 13 size-4, and 10 size-5. Twelve special enclosures: four each Petting Zoos, Reptile Houses, and Large Bird Aviaries.
- 34 double-sided kiosk/pavilion tiles; 20 partner zoos; 12 universities; 15 unique buildings; 9 bonus tiles; 70 money tokens; 20 X-tokens; eight each Venom, Constriction, and Multiplier tokens; 1 Break token.
- In each of four colors: 3 track counters, 25 player tokens, 4 association workers, and 1 solo tile. Two organizers, icon overview, glossary, and rulebook.

Components are not intended to create a scarcity limit: use a substitute if necessary.

## Setup

Follow rulebook pages 4–5, plus the printed map instructions.

1. Place the game and Association boards. Set the Break token to the start marked for the player count. Put four random bonus tiles on the four blank yellow bonus spaces; return the other five to the box. Leave printed bonus spaces uncovered.
2. Shuffle the 212 Zoo cards. Deal six cards facedown into the display, revealing them only after everyone chooses their starting hand.
3. Stock the Association board with one of each of the five partner zoos and three university types. Reveal three random Base Conservation Projects, or four with four players, below that board. These remain throughout the game.
4. With two players, block the left requirement on the left base project, the middle on the middle project, and the right on the right project. Also block the three spaces of the donation area's left column, using an unused player's tokens.
5. Each player takes a zoo map, a color's pieces, and five Action cards. For a first game use Map A; place its free size-3 enclosure as printed. Place Animals, side I up, in slot 1 and randomly arrange the other four cards, also side I up, in slots 2–5. Map A's printed kiosk counts as an existing building; put a kiosk tile there as a reminder.
6. Cover the seven conservation-bonus spaces on the left of your map with player tokens. Keep one active worker on your notepad; store the other three in the indicated spaces. Start with 25 money, no X-tokens, 0 conservation, and reputation at its first space. Randomly choose a start player. Starting appeal is 0/1/2/3 in clockwise player order.
7. Draw two secret Final Scoring cards. Draw eight Zoo cards, retain four, and discard four. Reveal the six display cards.

After learning the game, use Map 0 or advanced maps. For advanced maps, draw two maps and choose an advanced side, or offer player count + 1 advanced maps and draft in reverse player order. Map bonuses differ and must be followed as printed.

## Turn Structure

There are no fixed rounds. Players take turns clockwise (rulebook pp. 6–8):

1. Choose an Action card. Its slot supplies strength 1–5. Before acting, spend any number of X-tokens, each adding 1 strength. You cannot use tokens gained during the action to increase that same action's strength. The personal limit is five X-tokens.
2. Perform the action, resolving immediate bonuses and card effects as they occur. You cannot choose an ordinary action that does nothing, such as building no building or playing no animals.
3. Move the used card to slot 1 and shift the cards previously to its left one slot right. Resolve effects marked for after finishing only now, in an order of your choice.
4. Refill the display at turn end: slide remaining cards toward folder 1 without reordering them and refill the high-numbered empty folders.
5. If the Break token reached the end, conduct a break after the complete turn. Check game-end conditions at the end of your own turn or during a break.

An upgrade flips one Action card to side II in its present slot. Its improved action becomes available from your next action; upgrade requirements are satisfied immediately. There are at most four ordinary upgrade opportunities: reputation, conservation 2, second partner zoo, and second university. At conservation 2, hiring a worker is an alternative to upgrading, so you cannot upgrade every card.

### Break procedure

When the Break token reaches its final space, the active player gets one X-token, ignores excess movement, finishes the turn, then everyone follows this sequence (pp. 18–19):

1. Discard down to three hand cards, or five with the appropriate university. The hand limit applies during breaks, not continuously.
2. Return all Multiplier, Venom, and Constriction tokens from Action cards to the supply.
3. Retrieve all association workers and replenish the Association board to one of each partner zoo and university still needed by any player.
4. Discard display folders 1 and 2, slide the remaining cards down, and refill.
5. Collect appeal income as printed beside your counter, kiosk income, and all active purple income effects. Each kiosk earns one money per adjacent distinct building, counting pavilions, unique buildings, special enclosures even if empty, and occupied standard enclosures. Empty standard enclosures earn nothing. A building touching a kiosk along multiple hex edges still counts only once for that kiosk. When income order matters, resolve clockwise starting with the break initiator and refill the display after each player.
6. Reset the Break token for the player count and continue with the next player's turn.

## Actions

### Cards — rulebook page 9

First move the Break token two spaces, without taking money. Then draw according to the printed strength table, discarding afterward if required. A discarded card may be one drawn now or an older hand card.

| Strength | Side I: draw / discard | Side II: draw / discard | Snap alternative |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 / 1 | 1 / 0 | — |
| 2 | 1 / 0 | 2 / 1 | — |
| 3 | 2 / 1 | 2 / 0 | Side II only |
| 4 | 2 / 0 | 3 / 1 | Side II only |
| 5 or more | 3 / 1 | 4 / 1 | Either side |

To **snap**, take exactly one display card, regardless of reputation, instead of the normal draw. Side II improves the printed draw table and permits drawing from the deck and/or display folders within your reputation range, in any order. Side II also permits snapping at strength 3 or more and allows reputation to pass 9. The standard draw/discard table is also pictured in the publisher's [supporting rulebook, page 7](https://capstone-games.com/cdn/shop/files/AN_Exp1_Rules_EN_0-7_low.pdf?v=3049443834535182443); the additional expansion-card abilities in that illustration are not part of the base action.

Your reputation counter determines which folders are within range: its folder and every lower-numbered folder. Reputation bonuses are immediate and occur only once; at reputation 15, further reputation instead grants one appeal per step. Display cards are normally replenished only after the entire turn. The discard pile is not searchable unless an ability permits it.

### Build — rulebook page 10

Side I builds exactly one kiosk, pavilion, standard enclosure, or Petting Zoo, with size no greater than strength. Pay **two money per hex** before placing it. Standard enclosures have sizes 1–5, kiosks/pavilions size 1, Petting Zoos size 3. A pavilion grants one immediate appeal.

- Your first building must cover a border space; later buildings must share at least one hex edge with an existing building.
- Never overlap, extend off-map, cover rocks or water, or cover Build-II spaces before upgrading. Rotate tiles as needed. Standard enclosures begin empty-side up.
- Two kiosks must have at least two hex spaces between them: their hexes must be at least three steps apart.
- Immediately collect placement bonuses you cover, before building another building.
- You may have only one of each special enclosure. Ordinary buildings can repeat across actions.

Side II permits multiple **different** buildings with combined size no greater than strength, still costing two money per hex. Build and pay sequentially, so money from a placement bonus can fund a later building. It unlocks Reptile Houses and Large Bird Aviaries, each size 5, and Build-II map spaces. Unique buildings come through their Sponsor cards, not this action. Covering every building space of your map earns seven appeal immediately; rocks and water do not need covering.

### Animals — rulebook pages 11–13

Side I plays zero animals at strength 1, one at strengths 2–4, or up to two at 5 or more; because a normal action must do something, strength 1 requires boosting or an alternative action. Side II plays one at strengths 1–2 or up to two at strength 3 or more (the card illustration is visible in the publisher's [play-by-play guide, page 6](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0947/3907/1278/files/Ark-Nova_StrategyGuide_V1_Web_Updated_5.1.26.pdf?v=1777649815)). Resolve each animal completely before the next:

1. Meet all left-edge requirements. A card does not satisfy its own prerequisites merely by being played.
2. Pay its money cost. A matching partner zoo reduces it by three per matching continent icon printed on that animal, usually three and occasionally six. Triggered income arrives later and cannot pay this cost.
3. Supply an empty standard enclosure of at least the required size and with all specified adjacent rock/water spaces. Flip it occupied-side up. Each ordinary animal takes a whole enclosure, even one larger than required, unless a special ability says otherwise.
4. Place the card and gain its appeal, conservation, reputation, and immediate ability/triggered effects. The new card's own icons count for effects. Resolve simultaneous bonuses in your chosen order; defer after-finishing effects until the whole action and card movement finish.

Side II permits animals directly from the display within reputation range by paying the folder number in addition to normal animal costs. At strength 5 or more, take one reputation at the start of the action, potentially increasing that range. It unlocks cards requiring Animals II. When playing two animals, their sources may be hand, display, or one of each; do not refill between them.

**Special enclosures:** Petting Zoo animals only enter the Petting Zoo; their first, second, and third animals grant 3/6/9 appeal. Eligible reptiles and large birds may use their special enclosure instead of a standard enclosure. Mark the number of spaces required by their special-enclosure icon with player tokens; there must be room. Rock/water requirements still apply. Only when you first build a Reptile House or Aviary may you immediately transfer any number of eligible existing animals into it. For each transfer, mark occupied special-enclosure spaces and empty the smallest occupied standard enclosure meeting all requirements, or the smallest meeting size if no full match exists. This is an abstract reassignment: you need not track which specific enclosure housed each animal.

### Association — rulebook pages 14–16

| Task | Strength cost | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Reputation | 2 | Gain two reputation |
| Partner zoo | 3 | Take an available new continent's partner zoo |
| University | 4 | Take an available university type you do not own |
| Conservation project | 5 | Support a project; optionally introduce one and immediately support it |

Side I performs exactly one task. Place one active worker on its task area. If you already have a worker there, the repeat costs two more workers; after three workers are there you cannot repeat before a break. Other players' workers do not affect your cost. Strength must meet the task's cost.

Take partnerships/universities from the Association board, occupy your lowest empty matching map slot, and collect its bonus. Partner zoos contribute their continent icon and animal discounts; universities supply printed reputation/research icons or the five-card hand limit. Universities and partner zoos must be different types. Maximum four partner zoos; the third/fourth require Association II regardless of how obtained.

To support a project, meet one available requirement and place a token taken from your map's left-side bonus spaces there. Gain the printed reward and activate the uncovered map bonus, choosing whether to take that bonus before or after the project reward. Each player normally supports a particular project only once, and each requirement can be occupied once. You may introduce a new project from hand only if supporting it immediately. Add it above the Association board at the left; shift older projects right. Maximum projects above the board equals player count, or two in solo. Discard overflow on the right and return its tokens to supplies, never to map bonus spaces. Base projects below the board stay indefinitely.

Side II allows several **different** tasks with total strength costs at most your strength, with workers for each. It also permits introducing a project directly from a display folder within reputation range, paying the folder number. After at least one task, you may make one donation without another worker: pay the cheapest open donation amount, cover that space with a supply token, and gain one conservation. At the final 12-money space, further donations cost 12 without covering it. You cannot make a donation as your only task.

**Release into the wild:** Discard an eligible played animal meeting the project requirement's exact standard-enclosure size and icon. First lose only its printed base appeal, not earlier ability bonuses, conservation, or reputation. Remove the appropriate special-enclosure tokens if possible; otherwise empty the smallest occupied standard enclosure meeting all requirements, then size alone if necessary; if none meets size, empty none. Lose the animal's icons, but do not undo benefits already earned. Then receive the project reward. This uses the corrected v1.2/glossary/FAQ rule, including for flock animals.

### Sponsors — rulebook page 17

Side I either plays one Sponsor of level at most strength from hand, meeting its conditions, or moves the Break token by strength and takes that much money even if the track had fewer spaces left. Side II permits multiple Sponsors with total levels at most **strength + 1**, or takes **twice strength** money when advancing the Break track. Side II also unlocks Sponsors-II prerequisites and permits playing Sponsors from within-range display folders, paying each folder number.

Play Sponsors sequentially. Their yellow effects are immediate, blue effects ongoing, purple effects income, and brown effects final scoring. A Sponsor's icons trigger its own applicable effects. Unique-building Sponsors require legal placement of their matching tile; they do not separately charge normal Build-action money. Apply all ordinary adjacency, terrain, and Build-II-space restrictions unless expressly overridden.

### Take an X-token — rulebook page 18

Instead of any ordinary action, select a card, move it to slot 1 normally, and take exactly one X-token, regardless of strength or upgrade. You cannot choose this when already holding five. This is a separate sixth action: a bonus granting a specific action does not allow substituting it; a bonus granting any action does.

## Scoring / Victory Conditions

Conservation bonuses (p. 18): at 2 choose an action upgrade or active worker; at 5 and 8 choose five money or an available bonus tile beside that space, resolving and removing the tile immediately. The first player to reach 10 conservation makes **everyone** discard one Final Scoring card. If nobody reaches 10, discard one each before final scoring instead. Extra cards granted by Resistance can remain and score.

At the end of your turn, or during a break, sharing/crossing a scoring area triggers the ending (p. 19). If triggered at turn end, every other player gets one more turn. If triggered during a break, **all** players get one more turn. Appeal earned during someone else's turn cannot itself trigger the ending until your own turn ends or a break occurs.

Resolve retained Final Scoring cards and all brown endgame effects. Each Final Scoring card gives at most four conservation. Determine the lowest appeal number beside the final conservation space; subtract that target from your appeal. Highest result wins, even if results are negative. Ties favor the player who supported the most projects, counted by tokens removed from the map's left side; remaining ties share victory. Appeal caps at 113 and conservation at 41.

### Solo — rulebook page 20

Use three base projects without two-player blockers. Start at 20 appeal (10 or 0 for harder games). Replace the Break track with a solo tile containing seven unused-color tokens in its left column. Move one left-column token to the right after each turn. When the last moves, place the top token on the cheapest open donation space, move remaining tokens back left, and perform break steps 1–5. With only the final two tokens, moving the last one ends the game without another break. The six rounds therefore last 7/6/5/4/3/2 turns, **27 turns total**. Final score of at least zero wins.

Ignore Break-token movement but still collect any associated money; do not gain a break-trigger X-token. Use printed blue solo replacements for interactive abilities. At most two projects may be above the Association board. Biodiverse Zoo is replaced immediately if drawn in solo. For the three-game challenge, remove used cards, maps offered, and bonus tiles between games, reuse three random removed bonus tiles for game three, and aim for a combined score of at least zero; Assertion and Dominance can still access removed base projects.

## Special Rules & Edge Cases

The glossary (pp. 1–8) and FAQ give the following frequently needed rulings:

- Icons count on played cards' top corners, including printed rock/water enclosure requirements, and on your partner zoos/universities. Left-edge prerequisites, conservation-project icons, and decorative artwork do not count. Bear and Petting Zoo are animal categories unless explicitly excluded. Double icons count twice.
- An upgrade cannot change an action already underway. Money, workers, reputation, and other bonuses gained now may fund later steps of that action; only its strength and side are locked in at the start.
- **Multiplier:** put the token on a card immediately. On its next use it may perform the same action twice at the same slot strength before moving to slot 1. X-tokens boost only one execution each. Resolve after-finishing effects after both; unused tokens vanish at a break. Multiple multiplier tokens add one extra execution each. Taking two X-tokens is permitted; mixing an X-token alternative with the ordinary action is not.
- **Constriction:** token on the targeted highest-slot card(s) reduces the action's strength by two. Do not stack two Constriction tokens on one card. Apply the reduction to both Multiplier executions, then discard the token. At strength zero or lower, boost to at least one or use the X-token alternative.
- **Venom:** tokens go on targeted lowest-slot cards, at most one per card. Using a poisoned card removes its token and avoids the penalty that turn. If none was removed and any remains at turn end, pay two money. You cannot deliberately spend below two and refuse payment: undo the turn and choose a payable action. Breaks clear poison without payment.
- **Hypnosis:** use the targeted player's action's position and upgrade side; your own X-tokens may boost it. Their Venom/Constriction applies, but their Multiplier does not. Move their used card normally. Other prerequisites refer to your own zoo.
- Interactive abilities can only affect eligible zoos with at least five appeal. Where targeting uses highest appeal/conservation and several players tie, choose among eligible tied targets; targeting yourself has no effect. Having the most conservation requires at least one conservation point.
- **Pilfering:** victim chooses a random hand card or five money, if able. With no cards they must pay; with less than five money they must permit a card; with neither, surrender remaining money. Resolve repeated pilfering separately.
- **Flock:** may share without flipping an enclosure if an existing herbivore requires an equally large or larger enclosure. The same qualifying herbivore may support several flock animals. Otherwise fill a normal enclosure; later ordinary herbivores cannot join it.
- **Petting Zoo:** only three animals fit; these count as small animals. Small means printed standard size 1–2 or Petting Zoo; large means printed size 4–5, regardless of actual enclosure used.
- **Digging** refills immediately after each display discard. **Scavenging** draws randomly from a shuffled discard pile; **Snapping** takes a display card only. **Pouch** cards cease functioning; discard them if their animal is released, without losing the previously earned appeal.
- Advanced map rulings: Outdoor Areas enlarge adjacent standard enclosures by two for all purposes, including releases/transfers. Printed special map structures do not count as kiosk-income buildings unless specified. Park Restaurant counts adjacent occupied hexes, including empty enclosures, rather than distinct buildings. Research Institute cannot waive rock/water adjacency; its missing-condition allowance stacks with WAZA Large Animal Program. Hollywood Hills Sponsor reduction requires covering all three marked spaces; those spaces are not ordinary placement bonuses.
