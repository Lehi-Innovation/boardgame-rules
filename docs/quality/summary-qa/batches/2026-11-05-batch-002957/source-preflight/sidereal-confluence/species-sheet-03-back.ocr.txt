Automated Transport Network

When this card is played from the deck take
two colonies from the top of the colony
deck. Automated Transport Network grants
the holder 2 additional colony support,
which can be traded to other players for a
turn by loaning them the card.

If the Im’dril have the Automated Transport
Network, they may use it to hold colonies
into the Economy Phase.

Contextual Integrator Cache

This card makes Acknowledgments more
valuable to their holders, and rewards
the Faderan for Acknowledgments they
previously traded to players. | While
Contextual Integrator Cache can be traded,
doing so does not affect its behavior.

When played from the deck, count the
number of Acknowledgments players other
than the Faderan hold at that moment.
The Faderan gain 1 point for every 3
Acknowledgments, rounded up..

When a_ player invents a_ technology
using small or large cubes and has
an Acknowledgment, the cost of that
technology is reduced by 1 cube. This has

 

no effect on technologies invented with
octagons. Players cannot choose not to use
this effect, nor can they choose to save an
Acknowledgment for a later technology. The
Faderan gain points from Acknowledgments
normally.

Paradise Converter

The converter on this card lets the holder
exchange 1 colony of any climate for 2
points. The world is discarded to the bottom
of the colony deck. The converter is run
during the Trade Phase, at most once per
turn.

Star’s Ruin

When this card is played from the deck, gain
2 points, plus the number of points printed
on the current Invention Bonus. (Star’s Ruin:
is worth more points the earlier in the game
it is played.) It has no effect after it is played.

Gift of the Duruntai

The first time the Gift is traded to a player
each turn, it gives the player who traded it
away the listed resources, and the Faderan
gain one blue cube. Trading it a second time
in the same turn gains no one any resources.
The Gift does not have the Faderan
background, and as such it is not returned

to the Faderan at the end of the turn. If the
Faderan is the first player in a turn to trade
away the Gift, they gain all of the depicted
resources.

Nalgorian Grindstone

To run the Grindstone’s converter, the
player who has it must return 1 point to the
common pool. If the player has no points,
they may not run it. As always, the output of
other economic converters cannot be used
to run this converter during the same turn.

Relic Detector

The Relic Detector has an_ additional
converter for drawing cards from the relic
world deck during the Trade Phase: discard
one colony of any climate to the bottom of the
colony deck and spend three green cubes to
draw one relic world. This converter and the
one on the back of the relic worlds may be
run in the same turn, letting the Faderan get
two relic worlds in a single turn.

Transmutive Decomposer

As with all economic converters, the
Transmutive Decomposer cannot accept
ships as input that were produced by
another converter during the same
Economy Phase.
