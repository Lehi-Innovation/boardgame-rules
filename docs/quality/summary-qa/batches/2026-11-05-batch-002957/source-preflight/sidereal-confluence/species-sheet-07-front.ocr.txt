Flexible Output

Most Unity converters
output wild resources,
which are small and large gray cubes.
Wild resources may be used as if they
were any same-sized color of cube: large
wilds may be spent as black, blue, or
yellow cubes; small wilds may be spent
as green, white, or brown cubes.

Computer Worlds

You have four computer world cards that
start out of play. You can put each into
play by running the converter at the top of
the card. These represent setting up vast
servers on other worlds, and convincing
new sections of the Unity population to
get involved in the growing society.

Advice

For everyone else, the Economy Phase
is about becoming rich, and the Trade
Phase is for fixing the colors of resources.
For you, your economy fixes the colors
of resources, so you must use the Trade
Phase to become rich. Always sell your
gray cubes at a profit. Sell small cubes
for large or one large for two small. Most
players try to get their trades finished early
to get the best prices; be more willing to
wait, as the last few traders will be more

 

 

 

desperate for particular colors of cubes
that are no longer available. You can sell
them your gray cubes at a premium.

For the first two turns, focus on improving
your economy and ignore technologies.
Upgrade cards whenever you can

to increase your production of wild
resources. Besides being excellent trade
fodder, wild resources make it much easier
for you to accumulate large numbers of a
“single color” to invent technologies.
