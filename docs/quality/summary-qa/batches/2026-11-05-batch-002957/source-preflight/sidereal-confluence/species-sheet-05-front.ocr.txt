The Kjasjavikalimm may split their colony
bid in half in order to gain two colonies,
or otherwise manipulate the Colony Bid.

The colony bid proceeds as normal up until
the Kjasjavikalimm would buy a colony or
pass. The Kjasjavikalimm have a third
option: they can split their bid in half, and
go twice later in the Colony Bid. If they
choose to do this, they must split their bid
into two parts as equally as possible. A
bid of 4 becomes two bids of 2, a bid of 5
becomes one of 3 and a second of 2.

When it comes time for the Kjasjavikalimm
to use one of their half bids to buy a
colony or pass and keep the bid, they can
either take a colony where the minimum
bid is equal or less than the current half
bid, or they can keep that half bid. The
Kjasjavikalimm may not split their bid a
second time in the same turn.

In between the two half bids, the number
of colonies the Kjasjavikalimm have
often increases. This will change their
tiebreaker for the second half bid.

Clever players might choose to use
their double bids to block other player’s
colonization, or threaten to do so for
economic concessions.

Expansionist
When setting up the game, the
Kjasjavikalimm count twice towards the
number of players for the colony bid

 

track. (Ex: A 5 player game would be
considered a 6 player game.)

 

Territory Tiles

The Kjasjavikalimm have three territory
tiles which represent their growing empire.
For all purposes, these three tiles are
treated as cards. They start out of play
and can be put into play by running the
converter along the top. Once in play,
they can be upgraded (flipped) by running
the converter along the bottom of the tile.
Both of these converters are run during the
Trade Phase. Like all cards, they can be
loaned to other players for a turn.

Advice

You need very many colonies to play and
flip your territory tiles. If you can manage
to purchase colonies from other players,
do so. The Kt’Zr’Kt’Rtl are a good source
of relatively cheap colonies that you can
use to play and upgrade territories. Other
players may also be willing to sell colonies.
Never play or flip a tile unless you
can immediately make full use of
its economic converters. Spending

 

 

 

colonies to play an unproductive tile is
effectively throwing those colonies away
for no gain. You are not expected to get
every territory tile into play by the end of
the game.

 
   

The Faderan offered a new, hollow
future in return: become one of many
species sharing a galaxy that should
have been the Kjasjavikalimm’s
by right of military prowess. The
Faderan had already managed to
forge an alliance of eight species: too
many for the Kjasjavikalimm to fight
and live. So, grudgingly, the myriad
nations allied under a common
purpose: to beat the Faderan at their
own game and achieve control over
the growing society from the inside.

The strong _ Kjasjavikalimm
nationalism comes from their use
of pheromones to regulate group
emotions and define loyalty. This
allows them to cooperate extremely
efficiently with anyone who smells
like “family”; i.e. their entire nation.
Unfortunately, aliens lack strong
emotional pheromones, and thus
can never be completely trusted.
