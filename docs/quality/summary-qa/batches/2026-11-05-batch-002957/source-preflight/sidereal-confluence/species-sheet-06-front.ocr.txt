 

Nonlinear Thinking

All of the Kt’Zr’Kt’Rtl converters that start
in play are split over two cards. Card
pairs must be arranged as in the picture.
The two cards in a pair cannot be split up,
nor re-arranged. Each card in a pair is
upgraded independently. The Kt’Zr’Kt’Rtl
can trade these pairs of cards to other
players as if they were a single card.

They are returned at the end of the turn,
as normal.

Nullspace Drive

The Kt’Zr’Kt’Rtl have a set of colonies
that they can colonize during the Trade
Phase by running the converter at the top
(which has no output other than to play
the colony). The colonies do not have
the -Kt’Zr’Kt’Rtl background; they are
traded permanently, not for a single turn.
If they are consumed by a converter that
has worlds as an input, they are removed
from the game instead of being put under
the colony deck.

Boundless Expansion
The Kt’Zr’Kt’Rtl can support any number
of colonies.

The Kt’Zr’Kt’Rtl always win colony bid
ties as if they had fewer colonies than
the other players. This does not apply to
research team bids.

’ Dangerously Fast

The Kt’Zr’Kt’Rtl have a tie breaker value
of infinity: if all else is equal they win ties.

Advice

It is imperative that the Kt’ run Expansive
Social Diffusion frequently, especially in
the early game. It is the starting converter
which requires five green as input.

 

Many nullspace colonies cost one yellow
to put into play. If you sell these for more

   

 

than one yellow (say, a yellow and a small
cube), you make a profit for nearly no cost
to yourself. Since nullspace colonies are
played during the Trade Phase, you can
offer a colony to a player, and if they
accept the deal you can then play and sell
them that colony.

If you are playing nullspace colonies to
improve your economy (as opposed to
selling them to other players), ensure
that you have the resources to run their
converters the turn they enter play.
