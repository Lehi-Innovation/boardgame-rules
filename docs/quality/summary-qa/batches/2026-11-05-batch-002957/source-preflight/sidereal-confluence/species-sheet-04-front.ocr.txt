Im’dril fleets provide a number of Fleet

Support to help run certain economic
converters. The portion of the economic
card on the left shows how much Fleet
Support it needs to run that converter
(1 support). The card on the right is a
fleet card and indicates how much Fleet
Support it provides (2 support). To run a
converter, each symbol to its left must
be matched by a symbol at the right of a
fleet card. The support from a single fleet
can be broken up among multiple cards,
and a single card may be supported by
multiple fleets.

player, they will need to loan enough
fleets with them or that player will not be

Advice
The Im’dril have the strongest economic
potential in the game —- if you can

completely turn on their economy you will
do extremely well. It will take you about
nine turns to do this and the game is
only six turns long. The solution is to go
massively into debt to the other players
to get the resources you need to spin up
your economy. You will need to discover
(play) additional fleets to grow your
economy. As fleets are expensive, try to
get them to enter play at the last moment,
just as you need them.

Without colonies, the Im’dril economy is
quite fragile: it can’t recover if you spend
most of your resources on technologies.
Avoid inventing technologies until turn
four; instead use those resources to
continue speeding up your economy.
