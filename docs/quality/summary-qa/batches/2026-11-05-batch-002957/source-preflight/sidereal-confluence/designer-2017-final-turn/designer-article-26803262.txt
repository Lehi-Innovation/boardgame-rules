The official answer from me is, yes, the Zeth get to steal on the last turn of the game.  It's the final thing that happens before counting score.

In practice, end-game theft makes the target lose half a point, and the Zeth gain somewhat less than that, so it has very little impact.

There's an issue: Zeth stealing breaks the rule that the color of resources doesn't matter on the last turn of the game.  Thankfully, it works fine for everyone other than the Zeth, but the Zeth shouldn't run a stealing converter if they wouldn't have the correct colors.  That said, those “correct colors” are usually black, and the Zeth generally produce huge piles of that, so in practice it's usually fine.

That said, I wouldn't bother giving the Zeth more than a ship or (at most) two for safety on the final turn.

Due to the minor impact of stealing on the last turn, you could house-rule it either way.  I prefer to let the Zeth steal, as otherwise their special power has become completely useless on the final turn, so it's more fun for the Zeth.
