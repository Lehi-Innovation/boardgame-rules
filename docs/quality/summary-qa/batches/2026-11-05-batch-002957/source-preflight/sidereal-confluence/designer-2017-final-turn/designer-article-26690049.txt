So, the final “Scoring-Only Economy Phase” is just a normal economy phase.  Turn 6 goes as follows:

[b]Trade Phase[/b]: players invent the last technologies for the game.  Trading is dominated by getting the resources to invent those technologies.  Converters that produce victory points are also pretty valuable, whereas converters that just produce resources are worth comparably little (but not nothing).

“Scoring-Only” [b]Economy Phase[/b]: this is the last economy phase of the game, and has almost no special rules.  Players run what economy they can, producing victory points and some resources.  The only thing that makes this phase different than other economy phases is that it's OK to produce the wrong colors of resources, so long as the sizes are correct.  You won't be able to use the resources to do anything (other than end-game scoring, which doesn't care about resource color), so the resource colors don't really matter anymore. 

Note that you [i]do[/i] still need the correct colors of input resources.

Oh, and as your reminded me, player keep their own donation goods (since there isn't another trade phase to trade them away).

[b]Confluence Phase[/b]: get victory points for sharing technologies, and then the game ends (before bidding starts).  During the end of the game, leftover resources are turned into victory points at a terrible rate.

[center]<=oOo=>[/center]

So, why do I call it a “Scoring-Only Economy” on the back of the Confluence cards?

It's intended as a reminder that the resources you produce during your turn 6 Economy won't be useful for anything other than scoring.  They can't be used to run any further economic converters, and more importantly they can't be used to invent technologies.  This means that the turn 5 Economy is your last chance to produce resources you intend to invent with: if there aren't enough resources in the game to invent a technology after turn 5, then that technology simply can't be invented.

During playtesting, I had problems where players would miss-count the number of useful economy phases left, or forget that the turn 6 economy phase couldn't help with inventing technologies.  I put the full list of phases left in the game on the back of the Confluence cards in the hopes of avoiding that.
