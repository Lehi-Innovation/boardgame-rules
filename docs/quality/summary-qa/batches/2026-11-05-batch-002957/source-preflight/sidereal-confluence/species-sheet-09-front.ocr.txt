“Envoys”
The “Envoys” factory
produces many

resources, which must
be traded or gifted
to the same _ player
along with an envoy
token. As a reminder,
players may not refuse
a gift. The Zeth may trade

(not gift) envoy tokens from their stash to
other players but other players may not
trade envoy tokens between themselves

nor trade them back.
When the Zeth are in the game, an

Theft

additional phase is added: the “Zeth Steal
Phase” is after the Confluence Phase, at
the end of each turn.

 

The Zeth may run their red stealing
converters during the Zeth Steal Phase.
The output of a stealing converter is the
ability to take a set of resources from
another player. If a player doesn’t have
enough resources, the Zeth steal as
much as they can. Stealing converters
care about the size and shape, but not
color, of the resources they steal.

   

  
  

Each stealing converter can
steal from exactly one player.
Furthermore, the Zeth may not use
two stealing converters against the
same player in one turn.

All stealing converters have at least
two ways to pay for their input. If the
target has envoy tokens, less expensive
options become available. This does not
use up those tokens.

Unity wild resources cannot be stolen.

Protection Racket

The Zeth may not steal from a player
that they traded with during the previous
Trade Phase. Use the “Vulnerable / Safe”
tokens to keep track of this for each
player.

This restriction means that the Zeth can
ask players to pay them off if they want to
avoid being stolen from.

Note that donation resources that were
forced to be given to a player are not
trades and do not offer protection from
theft. Players can’t protect themselves
by gifting donation resources to the
Zeth, nor are they protected if they
receive the output of “Envoys” as a gift
instead of a trade. Donations can be
traded to the Zeth, but that must be
clearly stated when the trade occurs,

and the Zeth may refuse.

 

Advice

Ideally, the Zeth want to be paid every
turn from every other player not to steal
from them. A payment of one small
cube, or an equivalent profit on trades,
is an excellent starting value. Be willing
to steal from players who hold out,
but remember that getting bribed, not
stealing, is the objective.

 

Playing the Zeth well is about acting as
much as it is about strategy. Convince
other players that you have an irrational
love of stealing their hard-earned
resources.
