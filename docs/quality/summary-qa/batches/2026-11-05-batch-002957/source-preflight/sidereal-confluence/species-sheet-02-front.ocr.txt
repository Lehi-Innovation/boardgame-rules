Interest Converters

The Eni Et have cards with interest
converters, most of which accept wild
resources. Interest converters accept a
number of resources of a single color and
output more resources of the same color.
This is an exception to the rule that wild
resources need not match.

The Eni Et may not run their own interest
converters, although they can upgrade
the card. Upgraded interest converters
follow the same rules as normal interest
converters, although they might not
produce more resources than those put
in.

 

 

Unity Wild Resources

Unity wild resources can be put into
interest converters and they are not
destroyed, but the converter cannot
output additional wilds.

When running an interest converter with
wild resources, slide those resources
from the input to the output. Then,
fill the remainder of the output with a
single color matching the size of the wild
resources. As input, wild resources can
be mixed with other cubes of the same
color and appropriate size. In this case,
the remainder of the output must be filled
with cubes that match that color.

Advice

Look at the other player’s economies
and resources. If a player has enough
resources to run one of your interest
converters, try to sell it to them. If they
have almost enough, see if you can make
a three-way trade with someone else in
order to get that player both the converter
and the resources to run it. In general,
you can loan converters for half of their
net output.
