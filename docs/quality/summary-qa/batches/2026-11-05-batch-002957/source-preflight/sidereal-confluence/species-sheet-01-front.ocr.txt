Halved Colonization Bids

Caylion bids for colonies are halved,
without rounding. A bid of 1 ship is worth
Ye, which is below the lowest possible
minimum bid. Thus, it can’t be used to
pick up a card.

Odd numbers of ships grant fractional
bids, which can be used to avoid tying
another player.

Bids for research teams are not halved.

 

Seedling Tokens

When a Caylion takes a colony from the
colony bid track, they put a x2 “seedling
token” on it. The economic converter on
colonies with a seedling token may be run
twice each Economy Phase. Both uses
of the converter are still simultaneous
with all other converters.

The converter that flips the colony is
unaffected (its cost is not doubled). The
seedling token stays on a colony that is

flipped, doubling the new converter.

    

 

If a colony with a seedling token is traded,
the seedling token stays on the colony.

If a colony with a seedling token is
discarded under the colony deck, the
seedling token is removed from play.

If another player trades a colony to the
Caylion do not put a seedling token on it.
The colony that the Caylion starts with
does have a seedling token.

Upgrading the Caylion Economy

Some starting Caylion cards have
upgrade converters that produce a
victory point and a yellow resource.
Since these converters are run during
the Trade Phase, the yellow they produce
may immediately be traded away.

Advice

Your colonies are extremely valuable and
extremely expensive. You'll want to bid
five ships on colonies every turn for the
first three turns to ensure that you don’t
delay colonization. To do this, you'll need
to buy ships from other players.

Be willing to sell your
colonies to other players
- a good price in the early
game is three times what
it produces. An excellent
trade is to sell one of your colonies in
