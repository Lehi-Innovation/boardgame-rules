 

Acknowledgements

The Faderan have a set of tokens called
“Acknowledgments”. Acknowledgements
can be traded to players by the Faderan.
Non-Faderan players cannot trade away
acknowledgments. When a non-Faderan
player with an Acknowledgment invents

a technology, they must return one
Acknowledgment to the Faderan, and
the Faderan gain one victory point from
the common pool. If a player completes

multiple technologies in a single turn,
they return one Acknowledgment per
technology completed, and the Faderan
gain one point for each. Thus, each
Acknowledgment accepted will eventually
be worth one point to the Faderan, so long
as the player who holds them invents as
many technologies.

Relic Worlds

The Faderan have a deck of

relic worlds which is shuffled

before the game and kept face

down. On the back of each card

is a converter that can be run during the
Trade Phase to play the top card of the
deck. Despite being printed on the back of
each card, there is only one such converter
— it can be run at most once per turn.

 

 

Most relic worlds have special rules,

covered on the back of this sheet.

Unless otherwise noted, relic worlds ca
be traded and will be returned at the end
of the turn like all other cards.
