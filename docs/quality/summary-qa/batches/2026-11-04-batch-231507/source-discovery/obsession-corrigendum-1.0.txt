5.3 Solo Estate Challenge Variant, Page 60.
There is an error in the GAME END & SCORING section (bullet points 2 and 3). The correct rules
are:

    •    If you complete your tableau before the target round, you get a bonus. Score 5 VP if you
       ﬁnish 1 round early. Score 15 VP (5 + 10) if you ﬁnish 2 rounds early. The incremental round
      bonus increases by 5 VP for every round you ﬁnish early. For example, if you ﬁnish 4 rounds
        early, your bonus is 50 VP (+5 +10 +15 +20 = 50).
    •    If you complete your tableau after the target round, you get a penalty. Lose 5 VP if you ﬁnish
      1 round late. Lose 15 VP if you ﬁnish 2 rounds late. The incremental round penalty increases
       by 5 VP for every round you ﬁnish late. For example, if you ﬁnish 4 rounds late, you lose 50
     VP (–5 –10 –15 –20= –50).
