# El Grande question and answer bank — v1-seed

Scope: 1996 classic English base game pp.1–12; no expansions or optional short game.

Answers fixed before any reader dispatch for this version. Original Q01–Q20 remain unchanged.

## Q01

How are the King and home regions assigned, and where do each player’s initial ten Caballeros go?

**Expected:** Shuffle region cards; reveal top for King, each player takes another for home and places Grande. Two Caballeros home, one on first success-track space, seven court; remaining supply provinces.

**Source:** Publisher PDF pp.2, Preparation

## Q02

Who begins playing power cards in the first and later rounds, in what seating direction do others play, and may a played value be matched?

**Expected:** Players choose first-round starter; previous round’s last player begins later rounds; clockwise; no duplicate value in a round.

**Source:** Publisher PDF pp.3, Play the power cards

## Q03

What determines turn order and court recruitment, and when is recruitment done relative to choosing an action card?

**Expected:** Descending power value determines turns. Recruit at most pictured Caballeros from provinces into court first, then choose face-up action card.

**Source:** Publisher PDF pp.3, Player turns

## Q04

If a power card allows recruitment but your provinces lack enough, what alternative is allowed, and which protected locations cannot supply it?

**Expected:** May remove own Caballeros from board to make up difference; not King’s region or Castillo (taboo and no Castillo removal outside general scoring).

**Source:** Publisher PDF pp.3,4,7, Recruitment; taboo; Castillo

## Q05

Where may ordinary action-card deployment go, may it be split, and must the full printed allowance be used?

**Expected:** From court to regions adjacent to current King and/or Castillo; split freely; printed number is maximum, fewer allowed.

**Source:** Publisher PDF pp.4,7, Deployment; E,F

## Q06

May special action and ordinary deployment be done in either order, may either be interleaved with the other, and may the special action be declined?

**Expected:** Either order, each completed wholly before next; no splitting/interleaving. Special action optional, may select card to prevent it.

**Source:** Publisher PDF pp.3,7, Sequence; skipping

## Q07

What happens to selected and unselected face-up action cards after turns and rounds, and what is exceptional about the King card?

**Expected:** Chosen card laid aside unavailable to later players; remaining face-up cards discarded at round end; King card returns face-up every round, never permanently discarded.

**Source:** Publisher PDF pp.4, Next Round

## Q08

What changes are forbidden in the King’s region, may it still be scored, and what kind of action changes its protected status?

**Expected:** No Caballeros, Grande or mobile scoreboard in/out, even special actions; scoring allowed. King-moving actions move King, changing which region is taboo.

**Source:** Publisher PDF pp.4,7, King; A

## Q09

When putting Caballeros into the Castillo, what must be announced, how does King location affect entry, and when may they leave?

**Expected:** Announce number added; entry whenever adding/moving Caballeros regardless King; leave only at general scoring.

**Source:** Publisher PDF pp.4,5,7, Castillo; C

## Q10

List the timing and ordered stages of general scoring, including when destinations are chosen relative to revealing the Castillo.

**Expected:** After rounds 3,6,9: secretly choose destinations first and place disks face down; uncover and score Castillo; reveal disks and move its Caballeros; score board regions in track order.

**Source:** Publisher PDF pp.3,5, General scoring

## Q11

At general scoring, may one player split Castillo Caballeros between regions, choose the King’s region, or keep them in the Castillo; what happens if the King’s region was chosen?

**Expected:** All must go to single disk-selected region, not King’s region; Castillo emptied. Illegal King choice sends own Castillo Caballeros to own court.

**Source:** Publisher PDF pp.5, Secret disk; move

## Q12

In a five-player scoring of 5/3/1, three players have four Caballeros each and another has three. What do these four players score, and do Grandes count?

**Expected:** Three tied leaders each 3 (second rank); next player third rank gets1. Grandes do not count toward majority.

**Source:** Publisher PDF pp.5,6, Score Regions; example1

## Q13

In a five-player region worth 6/4/2 with counts 4,3,2,2, what does each score?

**Expected:** 6,4,0,0: tied third players drop to fourth.

**Source:** Publisher PDF pp.6, Example2

## Q14

Which scoreboard ranks pay in two-player and three-player games, and what happens to a two-player tie for first?

**Expected:** Two players only first rank; three only first two. Tied first drops to second so two-player tied leaders receive no points.

**Source:** Publisher PDF pp.5, Score Regions

## Q15

When are the King’s and home bonuses awarded, how much is each, and what happens on a tied majority?

**Expected:** Whenever relevant region scored including special scoring; sole majority earns2 King bonus in King region and2 home bonus if own Grande there; neither for tied first.

**Source:** Publisher PDF pp.6,10, Bonuses

## Q16

Does specially scoring the Castillo empty it or redistribute its Caballeros? Describe what happens.

**Expected:** Uncover and score; replace Castillo over same Caballeros; no redistribution until general scoring.

**Source:** Publisher PDF pp.10,11, Special Castillo scoring

## Q17

Can a mobile scoreboard be put at the Castillo, moved into/out of King’s region, stacked on the other, or rotated to change its values?

**Expected:** Castillo allowed; King in/out prohibited; cannot stack; right side up only8/4/0 or4/0/0.

**Source:** Publisher PDF pp.11, Mobile scoreboard

## Q18

What are the legal source/destination restrictions on moving your Grande, may other Grandes already be there, and what changes afterward?

**Expected:** Cannot leave King region or enter King region/Castillo; any other region even multiple Grandes; new region is home.

**Source:** Publisher PDF pp.12, Grande

## Q19

Which power card may the recovery action return, can it be the card used this turn, and must the choice be revealed?

**Expected:** Any one played card, including current turn’s, to hand; need not reveal, though taking visible top is observable.

**Source:** Publisher PDF pp.12, Power Cards

## Q20

When does the standard game end, and how is the winner determined?

**Expected:** After third general scoring following round9; furthest along success track/most points wins.

**Source:** Publisher PDF pp.6, Game End
