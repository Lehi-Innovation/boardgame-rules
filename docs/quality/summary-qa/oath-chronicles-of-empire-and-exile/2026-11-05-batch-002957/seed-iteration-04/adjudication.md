## Q008, reader B: independent resolution — incorrect

The fresh source-based [borderline adjudication](borderline-adjudication.json) resolves the earlier ambiguity: **incorrect**. B instructs world-deck Search to reveal one at a time. Law Glossary **Reveal** expressly means show the card front to everyone (extracted Law line 1424), whereas **Peek** is looking privately (line 1420). Law 5.1.2 instead says draw one at a time and announce the fact that a Vision was drawn. Requiring public disclosure of all drawn fronts changes available information and is a material contradiction.

The Playbook’s Sharing Card Draws discussion permits group agreements; it does not make universal public revelation a required base Search step. The colloquial private-inspection reading is plausible in isolation, but would silently replace a defined, consequential instruction with a different one. The independent resolution therefore supersedes the preliminary partial grade.

The original results are preserved in `results-pre-adjudication.json`. No other grades changed. The independent report’s additional discussion of discarding before playing is **not adopted** as a requirement here: this question asks sources/costs and Vision drawing differences, under the unchanged scoped standard.
