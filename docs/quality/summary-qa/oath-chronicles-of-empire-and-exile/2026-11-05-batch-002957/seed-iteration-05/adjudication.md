## Q013, reader B — incorrect, independently confirmed

B says the suited card(s) at the holder’s site must match none of their faceup advisers. The definite plural set reads as requiring all suited site cards to be unmatched. Law 5.4.1 instead says if any card does not match any advisers: at least one unmatched site card suffices. For example, with a Hearth adviser and both Hearth and Beast cards at the holder’s site, recovery is legal because Beast is unmatched; a requirement that the site cards match none would fail because Hearth matches. This changes recoverability and is material.

B correctly resolves no-advisers, no-suited-cards, and ruin-only cases, but those do not repair the omitted existential condition for mixed sites. A charitable reading could take card(s) as unspecified candidate witnesses rather than all site cards. That linguistic alternative is acknowledged; the grade does not silently insert at least one to repair the direct definite-set statement. A explicitly gives there must be a suited card whose suit matches none and is correct. Law 5.4.1 with official 5.4 errata; snapshot line 116.
