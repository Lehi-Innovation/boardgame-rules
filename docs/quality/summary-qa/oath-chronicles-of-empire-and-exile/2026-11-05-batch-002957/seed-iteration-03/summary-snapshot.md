## Overview

One player is the Chancellor; the others are Exiles or Citizens. Players compete for a changing Oath, personal Visions, or succession to the Chancellor. One player wins alone. After victory, the winner writes the Chronicle, preserving a changed map, population of cards, and political order for the next game. New players can join later games.

This summary covers the complete multiplayer base game, first-game and later-game setup, and Chronicle. It incorporates the official first-printing clarifications, particularly the corrected Darkest Secret recovery restriction and Imperial defense sharing. It excludes the Clockwork Prince and New Foundations. The Law governs over the Playbook and aids except the Site Reference; printed card powers and the rules hierarchy below apply.

## Components

Map with Cradle (2 sites), Provinces (3), Hinterland (3), a facedown discard pile per region, six suit favor banks, round track, Visions Drawn track, world deck and relic deck. The game contains 23 sites, 198 denizens, 5 Visions (4 true Visions and the Conspiracy), 6 double-sided edifices, 20 relics plus the Grand Scepter, 2 banners, 4 goal references, Chancellor board, 5 Exile/Citizen boards, Imperial Reliquary, 6 pawns, 94 warbands (24 purple; 14 in each other color), 36 favor, 20 secret tokens, 10 attack dice, 6 defense dice, an end die, markers and aids. The Archive holds denizens by suit and the Dispossessed; the World Box saves the map and goal.

Your **board** holds resources and your mobile warband force. Your **personal bank** beside it holds unused warbands and held relics/banners. Your **advisers** are up to three cards to its right, counting both faceup and facedown. A revealed true Vision occupies an Exile's separate Revealed Vision space and does not use an adviser slot.

A player's pawn determines **your site** and **your region**. A pawn does not rule a site. You rule each faceup site containing your warbands, the cards at those sites, and your advisers. Sites hold only one color of warbands. All Imperial players rule every purple-garrisoned site. Bandits rule faceup sites without physical warbands; they are enemies to all players and do not compete for victory. Facedown sites cannot hold cards or be ruled.

Denizen suits are Discord, Arcane, Order, Hearth, Beast, Nomad, in that clockwise favor-bank order. A site shows its denizen/edifice card capacity, reveal prompt, relic Recover cost and special power. Both intact and ruined edifices use capacity; a ruin has no suit and cannot be used for Muster or Trade. Relics beside sites do not use their denizen/edifice capacity.

## Setup

### First game: preserve the packet order

Use packets A, B and C and the Playbook's setup, preferably with four players (Chancellor, then Red, Blue, Yellow clockwise). Do not shuffle the prepared first-game decks. The packet-based one-round walkthrough is optional teaching guidance; play afterwards uses the full rules here.

1. Lay out the map. Put Plains faceup in top Cradle slot, Mountain faceup in top Provinces slot, Rocky Coast faceup in top Hinterland slot. Place the other five packet sites facedown in their marked order: Lush Coast in the other Cradle slot, Wastes in middle Hinterland, Salt Flats in bottom Provinces; preserve the other packet placements.
2. Put purple pawn on Plains, Red and Blue on Mountain, Yellow on Rocky Coast. Place 2 purple warbands on Plains and 1 each on Mountain and Rocky Coast.
3. Start round and Visions Drawn markers on their starred spaces (round 1, zero Visions). Use Oathkeeper of Supremacy. Place banners in shared bank, People's Favor normal side with 1 favor, Darkest Secret with 1 secret. Put 3 favor in each suit bank; collect remaining favor, secrets and dice in shared bank.
4. Chancellor takes Chancellor board, Reliquary and Grand Scepter, 3 purple warbands on board and 17 in personal bank (4 are already on map), 2 favor and 1 secret on board, full leftmost Supply, and Oathkeeper title. Each Exile has 3 of their 14 warbands on board, 11 in personal bank, 1 favor, 1 secret, and full Supply.
5. Place Longbows on Plains, Taming Charm on Mountain, Elders on Rocky Coast. Seed Cradle/Provinces/Hinterland discard piles with 3/3/5 cards from the ordered packet. Give facedown advisers: Chancellor Forest Paths; Red Animal Playmates; Blue Naysayers; Yellow A Small Favor. Put remaining 41 denizen/Vision cards from packet B, still ordered, in world deck.
6. Place Ivory Eye facedown at Mountain, next 4 packet relics facedown on Reliquary spaces, remaining 15 in relic deck. Begin Chancellor's turn. The four-player walkthrough can be followed with three people by one person handling two colors; after the walkthrough remove one Exile color's pieces and discard their cards to continue with three.

### Later games: use the Chronicled world (Law §1)

1. Deal saved sites with attached denizens, edifices and relics to map slots: Cradle fully, then Provinces, then Hinterland, top to bottom. Start round 1 and Visions Drawn 0; use saved goal reference.
2. Collect shared pieces and both banners. People's Favor begins normal side with 1 favor; Darkest Secret has 1 secret. Put **3 favor per suit bank**, or **4 each with 5–6 players**.
3. Choose Chancellor (previous winner recommended), seated at Cradle, others randomly. Chancellor takes purple pieces, board, Reliquary and Grand Scepter. Other players in turn order choose a board on its saved Exile or Citizen side and corresponding color's pieces. Starting Citizens receive no Reliquary relic.
4. All start at full leftmost Supply. Chancellor has 2 favor, 1 secret and 3 purple warbands on board; place 2 purple warbands on topmost faceup Cradle site and 1 at each other faceup site with a denizen or intact edifice. Chancellor begins as Oathkeeper; additionally takes Darkest Secret for Devotion or People's Favor for People.
5. Each Exile/Citizen has 1 favor and 1 secret on board; Exiles have 3 own-color board warbands, Citizens 3 purple board warbands. Resolve printed site favor/secret setup prompts from shared bank; if insufficient favor Chancellor chooses allocation. Existing Chronicled site relics stay with sites.
6. Draw 4 relics and place them randomly, one facedown on each Reliquary space; put remaining relics in relic deck.
7. If world deck was not rebuilt during previous Chronicle, first form its top 12 cards from **10 denizens plus 2 random Visions**, its next 18 from **15 denizens plus remaining 3 Visions**, and remaining denizens below. Otherwise keep the saved rebuilt deck.
8. Take 3 total cards from bottom of world deck, one per region discard. Each player in turn order draws 3 from bottom of world deck. Advance Visions Drawn for any Visions drawn. Starting Chancellor, each player places pawn on any faceup site (Chancellor must choose top Cradle site), chooses 1 drawn card as facedown adviser, and discards other 2 using normal regional destinations. Put remaining world deck on map.

## Turn Structure

Play clockwise from Chancellor, one turn each per round, for at most eight rounds. Each turn is **Wake → Act → Rest**. After everyone has acted, check round-end victory and advance round marker if play continues.

### Wake: mandatory order

1. **People's Favor**, if held: either put 1 favor from your board on it or return 1 from it to the least-full suit bank (choose among tied banks). Never remove its last favor: at 1, you must add if able; if you have none, nothing happens. If banner is already Mob-side, repeat this choice once. Afterwards, if it has 6+ favor, flip to Mob. A banner becoming Mob at this last step does not retroactively repeat the payment that Wake.
2. If Exile, check Usurper and Visionary wins.
3. If Exile holding Oathkeeper title, flip it to Usurper. Victory was checked **before** this flip.
4. At Salt Flats, Mine or Drowned City, optionally take 1 favor or secret from that site.

### Act

Take any number of major actions, minor actions and Action powers, in any order, including repeating or taking none, provided you pay each cost. Finish an action before starting another. Spend Supply by moving marker right one space per point. Minor actions use no Supply but may have resource costs.

### Rest: mandatory order

1. Return favor on denizens/edifices to suit-matching banks.
2. Return secrets on denizens/edifices/relics to your board. Flip facedown secrets on your board faceup.
3. Exile/Chancellor refresh Supply to the space showing their warbands remaining in personal bank. For Chancellor: 18+ gives 6 Supply, 11–17 gives 5, 4–10 gives 4, 0–3 gives 3. For Exile: 9+ gives 6, 4–8 gives 5, 0–3 gives 4. Citizen refreshes to the **exact Chancellor marker space**, including leftmost, not by counting purple warbands.
4. Save remaining unspent Supply: move marker one extra space left per unspent Supply, capped at leftmost (7 Supply).
5. Optional Rest powers, each once in chosen order.

## Actions

### Search — 2–4 Supply

Choose either world deck or your pawn's region discard pile, never both. Discard-pile Search costs 2. World-deck Search costs 2 at zero Visions drawn, 3 at 1–2, and 4 at 3–5; Darkest Secret replaces the world-deck cost with 2. Other applicable modifiers still apply.

Draw 3 cards, taking fewer only if the source runs out, a world-deck Vision stops you, or a modifier reduces the draw. You cannot voluntarily stop early. From world deck, draw one at a time: if a Vision is drawn, announce that fact, **stop immediately** and increase Visions Drawn by 1. Drawing Visions from a discard does not stop drawing or advance the track. If source empties, draw only what remains; no reshuffle is implied.

Discard all but 1 drawn card **before** playing the remaining card, or discard that last card too. Options:

- **Site:** play faceup at your pawn's site, only below its denizen/edifice capacity. Gain 1 favor from that card's suit bank. With People's Favor you may first discard a card from any site in your region, then play to any site in your region, subject to locks and capacity; discarded and receiving sites can differ.
- **Adviser:** play faceup or facedown. Up to 3 total, including facedown; before exceeding limit discard an adviser, subject to locked cards. If the incoming card changes the adviser limit, check against the new limit before playing it; use the lowest of multiple limits. **Later options:** as a minor action you may play a facedown adviser faceup to a legal destination or discard that facedown adviser; you cannot discard a faceup adviser at will. Faceup advisers can be discarded only when another rule permits or requires it. Facedown cards have no suit, restriction banner or power; any card, including site-only cards and Visions, can be held facedown.
- **True Vision:** Exile only, faceup in Revealed Vision slot, replacing and discarding old revealed Vision. Not an adviser. Chancellor/Citizens can hold true Visions facedown but cannot reveal them.

**Discard destinations:** facedown atop next region's pile: Cradle → Provinces → Hinterland → Cradle, ordering multiple discards as you choose. For cards in hand/advisers use your pawn's region; when discarding a card at a site in another region, use the card's region. Favor on discarded cards immediately returns to matching bank; their secrets go to acting player's board **facedown**.

**Conspiracy:** anyone may play it faceup; it does not replace a true Vision. You may burn 1 secret to take 1 relic or banner from a player whose pawn is at your site, if at least 2 of your faceup advisers match the suit of any of their advisers. Both of yours can match the same single adviser of theirs. Apply banner seizure penalty. Then return Conspiracy to box, even if no theft was made.

### Muster — 1 Supply

Place 1 favor on an **empty denizen or intact edifice at your pawn's site** (no favor or secrets already). Gain 2 warbands from personal bank onto board; Citizens gain purple. Relics/ruins are ineligible. Rule is not required, and ruling a remote site does not allow remote Muster.

### Trade — 1 Supply

Choose an empty denizen/intact edifice at your pawn's site; no relics/ruins. Choose:

- Put 1 secret on card: gain 1 favor plus 1 per matching faceup adviser, from that card's suit bank.
- Put 2 favor on card: gain 1 shared-bank secret per matching faceup adviser. With no matching advisers gain **zero** secrets.

Facedown advisers never match. Finite source exhaustion gives only available favor/warbands; secrets are unlimited. Costs remain payable even if a gain is reduced to zero.

### Recover — 1 Supply plus target cost

Recover 1 **facedown relic at your site** or either banner from anywhere, including yourself or shared bank. Relics held by players or in Reliquary cannot be recovered. Pay relic's site cost (3 favor to indicated bank, burn 2 favor, burn 1 secret or burn 2 secrets), then hold it faceup in personal bank. You may hold unlimited relics/banners and peek before choosing.

- **People's Favor:** pay any amount of favor strictly greater than its current favor. Put paid favor on banner; distribute old favor singly to suit banks, starting at any chosen bank, then moving away from the world deck through successive banks and wrapping as needed (the printed bank order). Do not return the old favor to the previous holder. Flip it to normal side if Mob. No seizure penalty when recovering.
- **Darkest Secret:** pay any amount of secrets strictly greater than current secrets. Put paid secrets on it; new holder takes 1 old secret, previous holder takes rest. Recovering from self returns all old secrets to yourself; from shared bank, remainder stays in bank.

**Darkest Secret protection, corrected by official errata:** to recover from another player, there must be a **suited card at their pawn's site matching none of their faceup advisers**. Empty sites and ruin-only sites protect it. Ruins and facedown advisers have no suit. If holder has no faceup advisers but a suited site card exists, it **is recoverable**; the printed Law's contrary parenthetical is erroneous. Recovering from self or shared bank ignores this protection.

### Campaign — 2 Supply

Resolve all steps in order. Your **force** normally means board warbands for attacker, and all warbands contributing to defense for defender.

1. **Choose defender:** any other player who rules your site or whose pawn is there. If no player rules your site, you may choose bandits. A Citizen attacking Chancellor/another Citizen is non-Imperial for that Campaign. If Chancellor attacks Citizen, that Citizen is non-Imperial for it.
2. **Declare targets, form pools:** at least one target must be at your pawn's site; if defender rules that site you **must** target it. May target any of defender's sites anywhere on map (1 defense die each). If defender's pawn is at your site, may also target any of their relics (printed defense dice), banners (dice equal resources on banner), or pawn-and-favor together (2 dice). A co-located defender's relic target satisfies the local-target requirement. Attacker chooses up to as many attack dice as warbands in attacking force. Apply relevant modifiers and mandatory title defense bonus: Oathkeeper +1 defense die, Usurper +2.
3. **Battle plans:** red boxes are attacker-only, blue defender-only, gradient either. Attacker chooses eligible ruled plans once each, then defender chooses eligible ruled plans. Plans at a site require rule, not merely pawn presence; bandits use all ruled plans with no cost. A plan can be paid for even with resources on it, but still only used once per Campaign. If an attack die must be removed from an empty attack pool, add a defense die instead.
4. **Roll defense:** shields rolled, doubled for each double-symbol (multipliers stack ×2, ×4, ×8...), **plus** all defending warbands at targeted sites, **plus** defender's board force if their pawn is at attacker site or any targeted site. Doubles multiply shields only, not warbands. Bandits add 1 per targeted bandit site.
5. **Roll attack:** each skull immediately kills 1 own force warband. Count full swords; each pair of hollow swords adds 1, leftover single adds zero. May sacrifice own surviving force warbands for +1 attack each, but if sacrificing must sacrifice **exactly enough to win**, never excess. Attacker wins only if attack **strictly exceeds** defense; tie or lower means defender wins.
6. **Resolve defeat:** defeated side kills half its remaining force, rounded down, and returns survivors to board; bandits cannot be killed and go into hiding. Resolve defeated battle-plan effects. With Imperial defenders Chancellor chooses which warbands die; surviving site warbands go to Chancellor's board, while surviving warbands already on Citizens' boards stay there.
7. **If attacker won:** may place any number, including **zero**, of surviving force warbands on any targeted sites. Zero leaves site to bandits unless another power applies. Take **all** targeted relics/banners. For a seized banner (taken any way except Recover), burn 2 of its resources to minimum 1; People's Favor flips to Mob. If pawn-and-favor targeted, may force defender to travel to a legal chosen site for no Supply, and independently may burn half their board favor, rounded down. Respect site travel restrictions. **Shrouded Wood overrides the attacker’s destination choice** if its enemy ruler chooses where the pawn must go.
8. **Finish battle-plan effects in order:** defeated effects immediately after defeat casualties (step 6); then attacker’s site/relic/banner/banishment results (step 7); then victorious effects; finally “At end, discard” instructions. These effects form part of resolving the Campaign, including after an attacker victory. Transfer Supremacy title **at end of Campaign**, not during temporary loss of site rule.

**Imperial defense:** apply every relevant change below.

- When an Imperial player defends, Chancellor automatically joins as Ally. Other non-attacking Citizens may join with defender's permission if their pawn is at attacker site or any targeted site. 
- Activate all Campaign modifiers ruled by defender/Allies; applicable title defense bonus is shared on the defending side even outside Supremacy. Allied board warbands add defense only if pawn at attacker site or targeted site. 
- Defender/Allies may use plans at Imperial sites and their own advisers, **not other players' advisers**. User pays; same plan cannot be used by multiple players. 
- On powers, “you,” “your enemy,” “the defender,” etc. refer to the entire side, but **your site/your board** still mean that player's own pawn site/board. **Declared defender chooses order, timing and priority** when Allies disagree, including which player uses a shared plan and whether a restrictive plan must be declared first.
- Chancellor chooses which Imperial defending warbands die. Surviving warbands from sites return to Chancellor’s board; surviving Citizens’ board warbands stay on those Citizens’ boards. 
- An intra-Empire Campaign does not make all Citizens non-Imperial; only the specified attacker/defender loses that status temporarily.

### Travel — 1–4 Supply before modifiers

Move pawn to any different site, not necessarily an adjacent slot; site garrisons stay put. Board warbands travel with your pawn in the sense that they stay on your board.

| From / to | Cradle | Provinces | Hinterland |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Cradle | 1 | 2 | 4 |
| Provinces | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| Hinterland | 4 | 2 | 3 |

Reveal facedown destination; put printed favor/secrets there from shared bank and printed number of relics beside it facedown from relic deck. **Travel cost order:** fixed-number cost first, then other increases/decreases. **Free Travel is the final override:** any applicable power saying “spend no Supply” makes Supply payment zero even if another power increases it. This general rule applies to all Travel, including banishment, not only named site examples. Site entry restrictions still apply unless ignored by name/power.

### Minor actions — no Supply

- Play or discard a facedown adviser. To play it, remove it from facedown advisers and play faceup as if finishing Search: a denizen can go to site or advisers as its restriction allows, while an Exile’s true Vision goes to Revealed Vision and discards their previous revealed Vision. Follow site favor and When Played effects. All these later plays, including revealing a held Vision, are minor actions costing no Supply. They occur in Act, not Wake.
- Use an accessible Action power; free powers may repeat unless limited, paid ones obey exhaustion.
- Peek at facedown relics at your site. Once you have peeked at a particular relic, you can peek again from any site. Grand Scepter holder may peek in Reliquary.
- Move any number of your warbands from your site to board **except the last one**. Drop board warbands only at your site **if you rule it**. Bandit sites cannot be occupied by simple drop-off. Citizen pickup requires Chancellor's permission. Imperial players with pawns together may give/take board warbands from each other with recipient/other player's permission.

### Citizenship and exile during play

**Grand Scepter:** cannot use its powers the turn you took it. Its holder, even an Exile, may offer Citizenship to any Exile **including themself**, or exile another Citizen. Reliquary relics cannot be campaigned/recovered, their powers cannot be used, and they do not count toward goals while there.

**Offer:** the Grand Scepter cannot make an offer on the turn its current holder took it. In later turns it may offer any Exile, including itself. Must promise **exactly one Reliquary relic**; may additionally negotiate a binding exchange of favor, secrets, banners and non-Reliquary relics. May let recipient peek at Reliquary relics. Other binding-deal powers cannot trade Reliquary relics.

**Acceptance:** flip to Citizen; replace all own warbands on board/map with purple. If insufficient purple, new Citizen chooses which to remove and which to replace. Discard revealed Vision, flip own Usurper title to Oathkeeper, end Act if own turn, and refresh to full leftmost Supply. Exchange promised pieces; Chancellor gains the newly uncovered **mandatory** Reliquary modifier. Forced Citizenship powers apply acceptance without a negotiated offer. All Imperials rule purple sites; Citizenship does not itself grant a separate shared victory.

**Uncovered Reliquary modifiers, mandatory for Chancellor:** Brutal makes the defeated player kill their entire force when Chancellor attacks, even if Chancellor loses. Decadent makes Provinces/Hinterland-to-Cradle Travel cost no Supply, but Travel to Hinterland costs +1. Careless gives 1 additional favor on every Trade, even when trading for secrets, but 1 fewer secret when trading for secrets. Greedy draws 2 additional Search cards (still stopping at a world Vision) but prohibits Search costing more than 2 Supply.

**Exile another Citizen:** Scepter holder pays them 5 favor, +1 if recipient is Oathkeeper, +1 if recipient holds People's Favor, −1 if payer is Oathkeeper, −1 if payer holds People's Favor (range 3–7). Recipient flips Exile, replaces **board** purple warbands with own color and refreshes full. Purple map garrisons stay Imperial. This does not end their Act by itself.

**Self-exile:** Citizen who does not hold Scepter may give its holder favor equal to own board warbands **plus all their secrets on board and denizen/relic/edifice cards**. Flip Exile, replace board warbands with own color, refresh full, and **end Act**. Cannot self-exile while holding Scepter. Map garrisons stay purple.

## Scoring / Victory Conditions

### Oathkeeper title and four Oaths

| Oath | Oathkeeper criterion | Matching Vision | Citizen Successor goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Supremacy | Rule most sites | Conquest | Hold more relics+banners than Chancellor **and every other Citizen** |
| People | Hold People's Favor | Rebellion | Hold Darkest Secret |
| Protection | Hold most relics+banners combined | Sanctuary | Hold People's Favor |
| Devotion | Hold Darkest Secret | Faith | Hold Grand Scepter |

Current holder keeps title on a tie for its goal. If holder no longer meets goal and several others tie for it, outgoing holder chooses recipient. A new holder always takes it Oathkeeper-side, never Usurper-side. Under Supremacy Chancellor holds it if Empire collectively meets goal; Citizens do not count purple sites separately against each other. Other Oaths concern personal holdings. The Grand Scepter counts as a relic when held; Reliquary relics do not.

**Usurper victory has two timing windows:** at the start of an Exile's turn during Wake, or at the end of round 8 under the endgame priority. From taking Oathkeeper during Act, the normal sequence is next Wake check (not yet Usurper), then flip to Usurper, then win at the following Wake **or at the end of round 8 if that arrives first while still Usurper**. Losing title prevents this win; retaking resets it to Oathkeeper and restarts the sequence. A title still on Oathkeeper at game end is insufficient.

**Visionary victory:** Exile must have true Vision revealed and goal completed at Wake, with **at least 3 Visions drawn from world deck**. Can reveal it earlier but cannot win early. For Conquest/Sanctuary “most” means **strictly more than every other player**, no ties. Compare to Empire's shared rule for sites. Citizens/Chancellor cannot win by true Visions or Usurper.

**Stable regime:** at ends of rounds 5, 6 and 7, roll end die only if Chancellor or Citizen is Oathkeeper. Ends on respectively **6 / 5–6 / 3–6**. Chancellor wins, except a Citizen meeting Successor goal wins **alone instead**. An Exile Oathkeeper/Usurper prevents the roll.

**War exhaustion:** round 8 ends automatically. Check in this exact priority:

1. If Chancellor or Citizen is Oathkeeper: Empire wins (Chancellor unless qualified Successor).
2. Otherwise, Exile Usurper wins.
3. Otherwise, qualifying revealed Exile Vision wins. If several qualify, priority **Conquest → Rebellion → Sanctuary → Faith**.
4. Otherwise Empire wins (Chancellor unless qualified Successor), even if an Exile is only Oathkeeper.

Holding Successor goal never ends game on its own. Resolve it whenever Chancellor would win. After victory, write Chronicle below.

## Special Rules & Edge Cases

### Powers, costs and information

Access a card's power if you rule it or your pawn is at its site; relic holders use held relics. Persistent powers (thick black braid) affect the players specified regardless of ordinary access. Sites specify their own use conditions. Chancellor must apply uncovered Reliquary modifiers; title holder must apply title power.

Pay the braid/text cost. Normally cannot place favor/secrets on a card already holding either; battle-plan payment is the exception, not a license to Muster/Trade there. If power has no cost you can still use it despite existing resources. When paying outside your turn, favor goes immediately to matching bank; secrets stay on your board **facedown**. Facedown secrets cannot pay any place or burn cost until flipped up. Burn sends prompted resource to shared bank. Kill sends warband to its color's personal bank (purple to Chancellor). Sacrifice means choose to kill own warbands.

Resolve as much of used power as possible except optional “may” parts; may pay to use a power even if its effect does nothing. Cannot give away/burn/sacrifice extra resources beyond prompted cost or amount.

Faceup **tree** cards are site-only; **person** cards adviser-only; restrictions also govern moves/swaps. **Locked** cards cannot be discarded, moved or swapped once faceup except in Chronicle. Cards reducing adviser limit require discard based on **new limit before playing**; with multiple limit-changers use lowest, not sum. Respect locks.

Ordinary major-action modifiers are chosen at action start, used once per action, in any order; cannot discard an active modifier during that action. No-cost modifier may be used on multiple distinct actions. Must/cannot modifiers are mandatory. Battle plans instead occur in Campaign step 3, attacker first; each once per Campaign. Wake/Rest powers occur once at their stated phase; When Played is mandatory after site favor, Homeland effects and modifiers.

**Draw outside Search:** the corrected Draw keyword means take from the top of the prompted source; it does not import Search’s entire draw-three/stop procedure into other powers. Whenever a Vision is drawn from world deck, always advance Visions Drawn, including with Oracle. The Search action itself still stops at its first world-deck Vision.

Rules priority: read literally; Law supersedes Playbook/aids except Site Reference; non-italic rules override italic reminders. “Must” overrides ordinary rules; “cannot” overrides even “must”; “ignore” overrides named must/cannot restrictions. “Must if able” has no penalty when unable. Promises are normally nonbinding, and pieces cannot simply be transferred without permission in a rule; Citizenship offers and expressly binding powers are exceptions. Tribunal disputed breach is settled by coin flip. Give and Take are distinct keywords; restricting one does not automatically restrict the other.

Component limits: favor/warbands/cards limited to available pieces; take/gain as much as possible if source short. Secrets and dice unlimited; use multipliers or roll again if needed. Banks/boards unlimited capacity unless explicitly limited. Facedown advisers can be inspected by holder and shown to others; their identity may be discussed or lied about. Private: facedown card fronts, discard fronts, world-deck count, world-deck backs below top. Public: discard counts/backs and board-resource/warband counts, and all other information. Keep facedown advisers distinct. Playbook group agreements about undoing actions or sharing drawn hands are social options, not additional mandatory rules.

### Site powers (Site Reference)

- **Opportunity** (Salt Flats/Mine/Drowned City): at end of your Wake take 1 favor/secret from site, optionally.
- **Homeland** sites: when you play a matching-suit card there and have not discarded a card **of that same suit at that site this turn**, gain its printed reward: 1 secret from shared bank, favor from matching suit bank, 2 warbands, or take its relic treating this as Recover. Discarding a different suit there does not suppress it. Taking the relic by this power costs neither Supply nor the ordinary site resource cost.
- **Coasts:** traveling from one Coast to another costs 1 Supply and ignores Narrow Pass.
- **Plains/Mountain:** must add/subtract 1 attack die when site or anything at site is targeted, even if attacker rules it.
- **River:** Muster there gains 1 additional warband if you rule it.
- **Charming Valley:** traveling from it increases Travel cost by 1.
- **Shrouded Wood:** travel from it costs 2, ignoring Narrow Pass and Hidden Place; if enemy rules it, travel to site of ruler's choice (Chancellor chooses for Empire), even when someone else makes you travel.
- **Narrow Pass:** entering its region from another must travel to it; targeting anything in its region from another region must also target it unless you rule it.
- **Great Slum:** when playing/moving a card there, may first discard a card there.
- **Marshes:** Search while pawn there draws 1 fewer card.
- **Buried Giant:** traveling from it may flip 1 own board secret facedown to spend no Supply and ignore Narrow Pass.
- **Hidden Place:** cannot travel there or declare Campaign targets there unless flipping 1 board secret facedown.
- **Tribunal:** Action if you rule it or pawn there: negotiate binding immediate exchanges of favor and secrets, as well as binding future actions in combination with any player and agreed penalties. This does not grant unrestricted immediate relic/banner transfers.

**Enemies:** for Chancellor/Citizens, enemies are Exiles, not fellow Imperial players. For Exiles and Citizens temporarily non-Imperial during the specified Campaigns, enemies are any other players. Bandits are enemies to everyone and vice versa. During a Campaign “your enemy” means the opposing side: defender and Allies for attacker; attacker for defenders.

**Bandit Crown:** treat bandits as own warbands: rule empty faceup sites, may pick up last physical site warband, add relevant bandits to defense (or attacking force with suitable powers). Bandits themselves cannot be killed, moved or sacrificed, including skulls or battle-plan costs.

### Writing the Chronicle (Law §8, with base errata)

Winner performs these steps in order. After first game open Archive packet: place 8 dividers; put edifices in matching sections; separately shuffle each suit's denizens into its Archive section.

1. **Vow an Oath:** won by Vision alone → next Oath matches that Vision. Any other way or multiple victory methods → choose any Oath **except current one**. Save goal in World Box.
2. **Citizenship:** if winner Exile, flip **all** Citizen boards, including boxed, to Exile. May offer Citizenship to other Exiles except those just flipped. **Chronicle offers ignore all normal in-play offering rules: no required Reliquary relic, resource payment or Scepter permission.** Each accepting Exile must do both: **flip their board to Citizen** and **replace every garrison at each site they rule with exactly one winner-color warband at that site**, regardless of the old garrison size. Winner cannot offer themself Chronicle Citizenship.
3. **Map and edifices:** Imperial winner may build **or** repair **one** edifice. At ruled site with no intact or ruined edifice, replace denizen with matching intact Archive edifice, returning denizen to world deck; or flip a ruled ruin intact. Discard all sites except those winner rules or with an intact edifice; shuffle discarded sites into site deck, discard their denizens, return relics to relic deck and ruined edifices to Archive. At retained sites winner does not rule, flip intact edifice to ruin and discard all denizens there. Set aside **all sites with ruins**, with attached ruins, remaining denizens and relics, in map order top Cradle to bottom Hinterland. Remove pawns/favor/secrets/warbands from map.
4. **Repack map slots:** excluding set-aside ruins, fill Cradle top-down by pushing Cradle sites up then importing Provinces then Hinterland in top-down order. Fill Provinces top-down by pushing up then importing Hinterland top-down. Push Hinterland sites up. Fill empty Hinterland slots **bottom-up** with set-aside ruins **bottom-up**, then empty Provinces slots similarly. Fill any remaining slots with facedown site-deck cards; in each region with no faceup site, flip top one up.
5. **Add 6 denizens:** winner's most common adviser suit determines 3 random Archive cards, next clockwise suit 2, next clockwise suit 1, to world deck. Choose among tied suits; no suited advisers → choose any. Suit cycle Discord→Arcane→Order→Hearth→Beast→Nomad→Discord. If Archive cannot supply the full allocation, do **not** add that allocation: heal Archive instead. Sort all Dispossessed by suit; take 6 random from largest Dispossessed suit stack into world deck; shuffle remaining Dispossessed into matching Archive suit stacks.
6. **Dispossess 6:** set aside all 5 Visions including Conspiracy. Shuffle all discard piles with **losers' advisers**, move 6 random cards unseen to Dispossessed. Winner's advisers are excluded from this pool.
7. **Relics:** save Grand Scepter in World Box; return losing players' relics to relic deck and shuffle. Refill faceup sites with fewer relics than printed R icons up to printed number, facedown. Shuffle winner's remaining relics with Reliquary relics and stack atop relic deck.
8. **Save world:** stack each site's attached cards on it, stack sites so top Cradle on top and bottom Hinterland at bottom, put in World Box. Save player boards on current Exile/Citizen sides.
9. **Rebuild world deck:** shuffle all denizens remaining in play (not those retained at sites/Archive/Dispossessed). Top stack **10 denizens + 2 random Visions = 12**; middle **15 denizens + remaining 3 Visions = 18**; remaining denizens bottom. Save in Archive World Deck section. Chronicle aid's printed “12 denizens + 2” is incorrect; do not make a 14-card top stack.
