## Material contradictions

### Q002, reader A — incorrect: personal rather than global Vision count

A says the Exile must have drawn at least three Visions from the world deck. That active wording makes the qualifying Exile the drawer of all three. Law 3.2 instead requires that at least three Visions have been drawn, tracked on the common Visions Drawn track (Law 2.7.1, 5.1.2). Another player drawing a Vision advances the same threshold. This is a material extra requirement, so incorrect even though the pairings and no-ties rule are correct. A also omits revealed-Vision/goal-completion qualification, but the wrong personal threshold already determines the grade. B uses the passive condition, with no personal-drawer requirement, and is correct. The question still does not require detailed descriptions of each goal, consistent with Q002’s prior scope. Snapshot lines 84 and 188.

### Q009, reader A — incorrect under the literal destination reading

A opens with the correct next-region cycle, then says a hand/adviser card goes to the pile for your pawn’s region. The latter is a direct destination statement and literally identifies the current-region pile, contrary to Law Glossary Discard: Cradle pawn → Provinces pile, Provinces pawn → Hinterland pile, Hinterland pawn → Cradle pile. The two clauses are not mutually consistent under that literal reading.

A charitable alternative is that pile for your pawn’s region means the next-region pile determined *from* the pawn’s region. The opening cycle provides evidence for that intended basis reading, so the writer’s intent is not certain. The grade does not assume that charitable repair: it treats the explicit specific destination as a material contradiction in the requested discard destination. B says cards use your pawn’s region, which supplies the input to its preceding next-region mapping without introducing a different destination. B is correct. Snapshot line 92; Law Glossary Discard, extracted Law lines 1391–1400.

## Missing requested facts

### Q016, reader A — partial: actual defeat casualties and survivor destination omitted

A gives all targets, zero garrisons, banner seizure, banishment, Shrouded Wood, and the complete defeated/target/victorious/end-discard plan sequence. It refers to defeat casualties but never states half of the remaining force rounded down or that survivors return to board. These are expressly part of resolving defeat. They appear in A’s Q015 but cannot be borrowed into Q016. A is therefore partial. B gives the actual casualties/survivors and full effects sequence and is correct. B’s final chronology recap explicitly puts defeated effects after casualties, so its introductory then-finish wording is not treated as reversing that order. Law 5.5.6–8; snapshot lines 127–129.

### Q017, both — partial: general side-power sharing omitted

Both now give the declared-defender timing/order rule, allies and pawn eligibility, modifier activation, shared title bonus, battle-plan access/payment/use limits, Imperial casualty/survivor rules, and temporary non-Imperial cases. Neither states the key’s broader sharing of applicable powers across the defending side. Sharing title bonus and plans plus activating allied modifiers is narrower than the general side-power rule.

The errata’s side-wide interpretation of you/your enemy/the defender substantiates the missing sharing rule. The partial grade does not require exact pronoun wording, and the own-site/own-board exception is explanatory context rather than a newly imposed independent key fact. This preserves the fixed key’s share-side-powers requirement without expanding it to a vocabulary checklist. Law 5.5 and official 5.5 errata; snapshot lines 131–138, especially 136.
