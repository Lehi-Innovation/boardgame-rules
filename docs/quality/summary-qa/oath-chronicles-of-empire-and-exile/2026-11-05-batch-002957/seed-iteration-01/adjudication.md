## Borderline requested scope

### Q001: earliest Usurper timing — partial for both

“When can they first win” makes the round-eight alternative relevant: an Exile may flip to Usurper at their round-eight Wake and then win at the round-eight check before any following Wake, subject to victory priority. Both answers correctly describe ordinary Wake timing and loss/reset, but omit that alternative while calling the following Wake the first win. This is an incomplete exception, scored partial rather than an explicit contradictory ruling. No full round-eight priority recitation is needed here. Law 2.11, 3.1, 3.4.2, 4.1.2–3; summary lines 175–188.

### Q002: names and mappings — correct for both

“List all four Oaths, their matching Visions” requests the four names and pairings. It does not explicitly request each goal’s description. Both readers supply all names, the Exile/revealed-Vision qualification, the three-world-deck-Visions requirement, and strict-most/no-ties. The key’s goal descriptions are contextual facts, not additional requested subparts. Neither timing outside Wake nor a recitation of round-eight priority is separately requested by Q002. Law 2.11, 3.2; errata 3.2; summary lines 168–179.

### Q017: all changes to Imperial Campaigns — partial for both

The broad “what changes” wording includes how Imperial losses and survivors work. Both readers omit that surviving site warbands go to the Chancellor’s board while Citizen-board survivors remain with their Citizen. Reader A gives Chancellor loss selection and declared-defender order control, but omits plan payment/use limits and the full side-pronoun interpretation. Reader B gives plan payment/single-user limits, but omits Chancellor loss selection, declared-defender timing/order control, and side-pronoun interpretation. Both correctly identify the temporary non-Imperial cases, ally eligibility, modifiers, relevant board warbands, and shared title bonus. “The same plan cannot be shared” in B is interpreted in its payment/access context as the correct prohibition on multiple users of one plan; it is not treated as a prohibition on all side power sharing. Law 5.5.1–6 and Imperial survivor box; errata 5.5 (including relocation of the survivor box); summary lines 122–131.

### Q020: Chronicle Citizenship acceptance — partial for both

“Citizenship turnover” includes acceptance, not only offering eligibility and flipping existing Citizens out. Both readers omit the acceptor flip and replacement of all garrisons at the acceptor’s ruled sites with one winner-color warband per site. That conversion materially determines the winner’s retained map. A also omits ignoring normal offer cost/relic rules. Both get Oath choice, eligibility/boxed-board turnover, and corrected deck stack sizes right. Law 8.1–2, 8.8; errata 8.2 and Chronicle Aid; summary lines 232–240.
