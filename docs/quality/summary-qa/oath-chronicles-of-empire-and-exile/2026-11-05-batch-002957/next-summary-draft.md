## Overview

One player is the Chancellor; the others are Exiles or Citizens. Players compete for a changing Oath, personal Visions, or succession to the Chancellor. One player wins alone. After victory, the winner writes the Chronicle, preserving a changed map, population of cards, and political order for the next game. New players can join later games.

This summary covers the complete multiplayer base game, first-game and later-game setup, and Chronicle. It incorporates the official first-printing clarifications, particularly the corrected Darkest Secret recovery restriction and Imperial defense sharing. It excludes the Clockwork Prince and New Foundations. The Law governs over the Playbook and aids except the Site Reference; printed card powers and the rules hierarchy below apply.

A player's pawn determines **your site** and **your region**. A pawn does not rule a site, and warbands on your board do not garrison its site. Even one of your warbands physically on a faceup site is sufficient to rule it, regardless of where your pawn is. You rule each faceup site containing your warbands, the cards at those sites, and your advisers. Moving a pawn by itself leaves site garrisons and their rule unchanged. Sites hold only one color of warbands; different colors cannot coexist there to share rule. All Imperial players rule every purple-garrisoned site. Bandits rule faceup sites without physical warbands; they are enemies to all players and do not compete for victory. Facedown sites cannot hold cards or be ruled.

## Components

Map with Cradle (2 sites), Provinces (3), Hinterland (3), a facedown discard pile per region, six suit favor banks, round track, Visions Drawn track, world deck and relic deck. The game contains 23 sites, 198 denizens, 5 Visions (4 true Visions and the Conspiracy), 6 double-sided edifices, 20 relics plus the Grand Scepter, 2 banners, 4 goal references, Chancellor board, 5 Exile/Citizen boards, Imperial Reliquary, 6 pawns, 94 warbands (24 purple; 14 in each other color), 36 favor, 20 secret tokens, 10 attack dice, 6 defense dice, an end die, markers and aids. The Archive holds denizens by suit and the Dispossessed; the World Box saves the map and goal.

Your **board** holds resources and your mobile warband force. Your **personal bank** beside it holds unused warbands and held relics/banners. Your **advisers** are up to three cards to its right, counting both faceup and facedown. A revealed true Vision occupies an Exile's separate Revealed Vision space and does not use an adviser slot.

Denizen suits are Discord, Arcane, Order, Hearth, Beast, Nomad, in that clockwise favor-bank order. A site shows its denizen/edifice card capacity, reveal prompt, relic Recover cost and special power. Both intact and ruined edifices use capacity; a ruin has no suit and cannot be used for Muster or Trade. Any power printed on the ruin side remains usable under the ordinary access rules and its own conditions. Relics beside sites do not use their denizen/edifice capacity.

## Setup

**First game: preserve the packet order**

Use packets A, B and C and the Playbook's setup, preferably with four players. Choose a Chancellor and seat them on the short edge by the Cradle; seat Red, then Blue, then Yellow clockwise from the Chancellor. Do not shuffle the prepared first-game decks. The packet-based one-round walkthrough is optional teaching guidance; play afterwards uses the full rules here.

1. Lay out the map. Put Plains faceup in top Cradle slot, Mountain faceup in top Provinces slot, Rocky Coast faceup in top Hinterland slot. Place the other five packet sites facedown in their marked order: Lush Coast in the other Cradle slot, Wastes in middle Hinterland, Salt Flats in bottom Provinces; preserve the other packet placements.
2. Put purple pawn on Plains, Red and Blue on Mountain, Yellow on Rocky Coast. Place 2 purple warbands on Plains and 1 each on Mountain and Rocky Coast.
3. Start round and Visions Drawn markers on their starred spaces (round 1, zero Visions). Use Oathkeeper of Supremacy. Place banners in shared bank, People's Favor normal side with 1 favor, Darkest Secret with 1 secret. Put 3 favor in each suit bank; collect remaining favor, secrets and dice in shared bank.
4. Chancellor takes Chancellor board, Reliquary and Grand Scepter, 3 purple warbands on board and 17 in personal bank (4 are already on map), 2 favor and 1 secret on board, full leftmost Supply, and Oathkeeper title. Each Exile has 3 of their 14 warbands on board, 11 in personal bank, 1 favor, 1 secret, and full Supply.
5. Place these denizens faceup: Longbows on Plains, Taming Charm on Mountain, Elders on Rocky Coast. Seed Cradle/Provinces/Hinterland discard piles with 3/3/5 facedown cards from the ordered packet. Give facedown advisers: Chancellor Forest Paths; Red Animal Playmates; Blue Naysayers; Yellow A Small Favor. Put remaining 41 denizen/Vision cards from packet B, still ordered, in world deck.
6. Place Ivory Eye facedown at Mountain, next 4 packet relics facedown on Reliquary spaces, remaining 15 in relic deck. Begin Chancellor's turn. The four-player walkthrough can be followed with three people by one person handling two colors; after the walkthrough remove one Exile color's pieces and discard their cards to continue with three.

**Later games: use the Chronicled world (Law §1)**

1. Deal saved sites with attached denizens, edifices and relics to map slots: Cradle fully, then Provinces, then Hinterland, top to bottom. Start round 1 and Visions Drawn 0; use saved goal reference.
2. Collect shared pieces and both banners. People's Favor begins normal side with 1 favor; Darkest Secret has 1 secret. Put **3 favor per suit bank**, or **4 each with 5–6 players**.
3. Choose Chancellor by any method (previous winner recommended, not required), seated at Cradle, others randomly. Chancellor takes purple pieces, board, Reliquary and Grand Scepter. Other players in turn order choose a board on its saved Exile or Citizen side and corresponding color's pieces. Starting Citizens receive no Reliquary relic.
4. All start at full leftmost Supply. Chancellor has 2 favor, 1 secret and 3 purple warbands on board; place 2 purple warbands on topmost faceup Cradle site and 1 at each other faceup site with a denizen or intact edifice. Chancellor begins as Oathkeeper; additionally takes Darkest Secret for Devotion or People's Favor for People.
5. Each Exile/Citizen has 1 favor and 1 secret on board; Exiles have 3 own-color board warbands, Citizens 3 purple board warbands. Resolve printed site favor/secret setup prompts from shared bank; if insufficient favor Chancellor chooses allocation. Existing Chronicled site relics stay with sites.
6. Draw 4 relics and place them randomly, one facedown on each Reliquary space; put remaining relics in relic deck.
7. If world deck was not rebuilt during previous Chronicle, first form its top 12 cards from **10 denizens plus 2 random Visions**, its next 18 from **15 denizens plus remaining 3 Visions**, and remaining denizens below. Otherwise keep the saved rebuilt deck.
8. Take 3 total cards from bottom of world deck, placing one facedown in each region discard pile. Each player in turn order draws 3 from bottom of world deck. Advance Visions Drawn for any Visions drawn. Starting Chancellor, each player places pawn on any faceup site (Chancellor must choose top Cradle site), chooses 1 drawn card as facedown adviser, and discards other 2 using normal regional destinations. Put remaining world deck on map.

## Turn Structure

Play clockwise from Chancellor, one turn each per round, for at most eight rounds. Each turn is **Wake → Act → Rest**. After everyone has acted, check round-end victory and advance round marker if play continues. Round eight ends the game automatically; do not advance to a ninth round.

### Wake: mandatory order

Ordinary Wake card powers happen once each during Wake and must resolve as much as possible; parts saying “may” remain optional. Rest card powers, by contrast, are optional once each at the end of Rest.

1. **People's Favor**, if held: either put 1 favor from your board on it or return 1 from it to the least-full suit bank (choose among tied banks). Never remove its last favor: at 1, you must add if able; if you have none, nothing happens. If banner is already Mob-side, repeat this choice once. Afterwards, if it has 6+ favor, flip to Mob. A banner becoming Mob at this last step does not retroactively repeat the payment that Wake.
2. If Exile, check Usurper and Visionary wins.
3. If Exile holding Oathkeeper title, flip it to Usurper. Victory was checked **before** this flip.
4. At Salt Flats, Mine or Drowned City, optionally take 1 favor **or** 1 secret from that site: one token total, only if your pawn is there. Remote rule gives no income; an empty site gives nothing, with no bank substitute.

### Act

Take any number of major actions, minor actions and Action powers, in any order, including repeating or taking none, provided you pay each cost. Finish an action before starting another. Spend Supply by moving marker right one space per point. Minor actions use no Supply but may have resource costs. You may end Act with Supply unspent: after refreshing in Rest, add that unspent Supply, capped at the leftmost track space.

### Rest: mandatory order

1. Return favor on denizens/edifices to suit-matching banks.
2. Return secrets on denizens/edifices/relics to your board. Flip facedown secrets on your board faceup.
3. Exile/Chancellor refresh Supply to the space showing their warbands remaining in personal bank. For Chancellor: 18+ gives 6 Supply, 11–17 gives 5, 4–10 gives 4, 0–3 gives 3. For Exile: 9+ gives 6, 4–8 gives 5, 0–3 gives 4. Citizen refreshes to the **exact Chancellor marker space**, including leftmost, not by counting purple warbands.
4. Save remaining unspent Supply: move marker one extra space left per unspent Supply, capped at leftmost (7 Supply).
5. At the end of Rest, optionally use Rest powers, each once in chosen order.

## Actions

### Search — 2–4 Supply

Choose either world deck or your pawn's region discard pile, never both. Discard-pile Search costs 2. World-deck Search costs 2 at zero Visions drawn, 3 at 1–2, and 4 at 3–5; Darkest Secret replaces the world-deck cost with 2. Other applicable modifiers still apply.

Draw 3 cards, taking fewer only if the source runs out, a world-deck Vision stops you, or a modifier reduces the draw. You cannot voluntarily stop early. From world deck, **draw** one at a time, with no required public showing of drawn card fronts. If a Vision is drawn, **announce that a Vision was drawn** (not necessarily which Vision), stop immediately and increase Visions Drawn by 1. Drawing Visions from a discard does not stop drawing or advance the track. If source empties, draw only what remains; no reshuffle is implied.

Discard all but 1 drawn card **before** playing the remaining card, or discard that last card too. Options:

- **Site:** play faceup at your pawn's site, only below its denizen/edifice capacity. Gain 1 favor from that card's suit bank. With People's Favor you may first discard a card from any site in your region, then play to any site in your region, subject to locks and capacity; discarded and receiving sites can differ.
- **Adviser:** play faceup or facedown. Up to 3 total, including facedown; before exceeding limit discard an adviser, subject to locked cards. If the incoming card changes the adviser limit, check against the new limit before playing it; use the lowest of multiple limits. **Later options:** as a minor action you may play a facedown adviser faceup to a legal destination or discard that facedown adviser; you cannot discard a faceup adviser at will. Faceup advisers can be discarded only when another rule permits or requires it. Facedown cards have no suit, restriction banner or power; any card, including site-only cards and Visions, can be held facedown.
- **True Vision:** Exile only, faceup in Revealed Vision slot, replacing and discarding old revealed Vision. Not an adviser. Chancellor/Citizens can hold true Visions facedown but cannot reveal them.

**Discard destinations:** facedown atop next region's pile: Cradle → Provinces → Hinterland → Cradle, ordering multiple discards as you choose. For cards in hand/advisers, discard based on your pawn’s region: pawn in Cradle means Provinces pile; pawn in Provinces means Hinterland pile; pawn in Hinterland means Cradle pile. Never use the same-region pile. When discarding a card at a site in another region, apply that cycle to the card’s region instead of your pawn’s. Favor on discarded cards immediately returns to matching bank; their secrets go to acting player's board **facedown**.

Adviser retention depends on its state:

| Adviser state | Later voluntary options |
|---|---|
| Facedown | Minor action to play faceup legally or discard; may peek |
| Faceup | Cannot discard at will; discard only when a rule requires/allows it, respecting locks |

Both states count toward the adviser limit. Playing over the limit permits the required discard before the new card is played; merely wanting an empty slot does not.

**Conspiracy:** anyone may play it faceup; it does not replace a true Vision. You may burn 1 secret to take 1 relic or banner from any player whose pawn is at your site, including a fellow Imperial player, if at least 2 of your faceup advisers match the suit of any of their advisers. Both of yours can match the same single adviser of theirs. Apply banner seizure penalty. Then return Conspiracy to box, even if no theft was made.

### Muster — 1 Supply

Place 1 favor on an **empty denizen or intact edifice at your pawn's site** (no favor or secrets already). Gain 2 warbands from personal bank onto board; Citizens gain purple. Relics/ruins are ineligible. Rule is not required, and ruling a remote site does not allow remote Muster.

### Trade — 1 Supply

Choose an empty denizen/intact edifice at your pawn's site; no relics/ruins. Choose:

- Put 1 secret on card: gain 1 favor plus 1 per matching faceup adviser, from that card's suit bank.
- Put 2 favor on card: gain 1 shared-bank secret per matching faceup adviser. With no matching advisers gain **zero** secrets.

Facedown advisers never match. Finite source exhaustion gives only available favor/warbands; secrets are unlimited. Costs remain payable even if a gain is reduced to zero.

### Recover — 1 Supply plus target cost

Recover 1 **facedown relic at your site** or either banner from anywhere, including yourself or shared bank. Relics held by players or in Reliquary cannot be recovered. Pay relic's site cost (3 favor to indicated bank, burn 2 favor, burn 1 secret or burn 2 secrets), then hold it faceup in personal bank. You may hold unlimited relics/banners and peek before choosing.

- **People's Favor:** pay any amount of favor strictly greater than its current favor. Put paid favor on banner; distribute old favor singly to suit banks, starting at any chosen bank, then moving away from the world deck through successive banks and wrapping as needed (the printed bank order). Do not return the old favor to the previous holder. Flip it to normal side if Mob. No seizure penalty when recovering.
- **Darkest Secret:** pay any amount of secrets strictly greater than current secrets. Put paid secrets on it; new holder takes 1 old secret, previous holder takes rest. Recovering from self returns all old secrets to yourself; from shared bank, remainder stays in bank.

**Darkest Secret protection, corrected by official errata:** to recover from another player, there must be **at least one suited card at their pawn’s site whose suit matches none of their faceup advisers**. One mismatching card is enough even if every other site card matches; the condition is not that all site cards must be unmatched. Empty sites and ruin-only sites protect it. Ruins and facedown advisers have no suit. If holder has no faceup advisers but a suited site card exists, it **is recoverable**; the printed Law's contrary parenthetical is erroneous. Recovering from self or shared bank ignores this protection.

### Campaign — 2 Supply

Resolve all steps in order. Your **force** normally means all board warbands for attacker, and all warbands contributing to defense for defender. A power may change that force. Choosing fewer attack dice changes only the dice pool: it does not select a smaller attacking force or leave unrolled board warbands outside the force. Skulls and sacrifices use the actual surviving force, not a subset selected by the number of dice.

1. **Choose defender:** any other player who rules your site or whose pawn is there. If no player rules your site, you may choose bandits. A Citizen attacking Chancellor/another Citizen is non-Imperial for that Campaign. If Chancellor attacks Citizen, that Citizen is non-Imperial for it.
2. **Declare targets, form pools:** at least one target must be at your pawn's site; if defender rules that site you **must** target it. May target any of defender's sites anywhere on map (1 defense die each). If defender's pawn is at your site, may also target any of their relics (printed defense dice), banners (dice equal resources on banner), or pawn-and-favor together (2 dice). A co-located defender's relic target satisfies the local-target requirement. Attacker chooses up to as many attack dice as warbands in attacking force. Apply relevant modifiers and mandatory title defense bonus: Oathkeeper +1 defense die, Usurper +2. The mandatory title bonus is itself a Campaign modifier.
3. **Battle plans:** red boxes are attacker-only, blue defender-only, gradient either. Attacker chooses eligible ruled plans once each, then defender chooses eligible ruled plans. Plans at a site require rule, not merely pawn presence. Bandits use all battle plans they rule whose power has no cost; a plan with a cost is ineligible for bandits. They do not waive a plan’s printed cost. A player can pay for a plan even with resources on it, but still only use it once per Campaign. If an attack die must be removed from an empty attack pool, add a defense die instead.
4. **Roll defense:** shields rolled, doubled for each double-symbol (multipliers stack ×2, ×4, ×8...), **plus** all defending warbands at targeted sites, **plus** defender's board force if their pawn is at attacker site or any targeted site. Doubles multiply shields only, not warbands. Bandits add 1 per targeted bandit site.
5. **Roll attack:** each skull immediately kills 1 own force warband. Count full swords; each pair of hollow swords adds 1, leftover single adds zero. May sacrifice own surviving force warbands for +1 attack each, but if sacrificing must sacrifice **exactly enough to win**, never excess. Attacker wins only if attack **strictly exceeds** defense; tie or lower means defender wins.
6. **Resolve defeat before taking targets:** on attacker victory, the defender first kills half of the warbands in its defending force, rounded down, returning the rest to board. On defender victory, the attacker kills half its remaining attacking force, rounded down, leaving the survivors on its board; bandits cannot be killed and go into hiding. Resolve defeated battle-plan effects. With Imperial defenders Chancellor chooses which warbands die; surviving site warbands go to Chancellor's board, while surviving warbands already on Citizens' boards stay there.
7. **If attacker won:** may place any number, including **zero**, of surviving force warbands on any targeted sites. Zero leaves site to bandits unless another power applies. Take **all** targeted relics/banners. For a seized banner (taken any way except Recover), burn 2 of its resources to minimum 1; People's Favor flips to Mob. If pawn-and-favor targeted, may force defender to travel to a legal chosen site for no Supply, and independently may burn half their board favor, rounded down. Banishment itself supplies the no-Supply permission, including when a site normally sets a fixed Travel cost. Destination and non-Supply entry restrictions still apply unless a power ignores them. **Shrouded Wood overrides the attacker’s destination choice** if its enemy ruler chooses where the pawn must go; that changes the destination decision, not the no-Supply payment.
8. **Finish battle-plan effects for either winner:** defeated effects occur immediately after defeat casualties (step 6). If attacker won, resolve targets (step 7), then attacker’s victorious effects. If defender won, skip attacker’s targets but still resolve defender’s victorious effects. Finally, discard any plans from either side that say “At end, discard.” A victorious defender gains no declared targets merely by defending. Transfer Supremacy title **at end of Campaign**, not during temporary loss of site rule.

**Casualty bookkeeping:** remove skull casualties once, when rolled. If the attacker later loses, apply defeat losses to the force **currently remaining after skulls**, rounding the number killed down. For an ordinary attacking force of R physical warbands remaining, defeat kills ⌊R/2⌋ and leaves R − ⌊R/2⌋ on the attacker’s board. A count already described as surviving or remaining after skulls is the updated count; do not subtract the skull losses from it again. Bandit Crown’s bandits still cannot be killed, moved or sacrificed.

**Imperial defense:** apply every relevant change below. For Supremacy, count all Imperial-ruled sites collectively; when that count earns the Oathkeeper title under the ordinary incumbent-tie and transfer rules, the Chancellor physically holds it and always applies its mandatory bonus when any Imperial player defends.

- When an Imperial player defends, Chancellor automatically joins as Ally. Other non-attacking Citizens may join with defender's permission if their pawn is at attacker site or any targeted site.
- Activate all Campaign modifiers ruled by defender/Allies. **Share powers as one defending side:** their “you,” “your enemy,” and “defender” mean the whole side, while “your site”/“your board” still mean each user’s own location/board. This shares the Oathkeeper defense bonus even outside Supremacy. Allied board warbands add defense only if pawn at attacker site or targeted site.
- Defender/Allies may use plans at Imperial sites and their own advisers, **not other players' advisers**. Each shared battle plan may be used **once total per Campaign by one user, who pays its cost**. The same user cannot activate it twice, and a second Ally cannot activate it again.
- On powers, “you,” “your enemy,” “the defender,” etc. refer to the entire side, but **your site/your board** still mean that player's own pawn site/board. **Declared defender chooses order, timing and priority** when Allies disagree, including which player uses a shared plan and whether a restrictive plan must be declared first.
- Chancellor chooses which Imperial defending warbands die. Surviving warbands from sites return to Chancellor’s board; surviving Citizens’ board warbands stay on those Citizens’ boards.
- An intra-Empire Campaign does not make all Citizens non-Imperial; only the specified attacker/defender loses that status temporarily.

### Travel — 1–4 Supply before modifiers

Move pawn to any different site, not necessarily an adjacent slot; site garrisons stay put. Board warbands travel with your pawn in the sense that they stay on your board.

| From / to | Cradle | Provinces | Hinterland |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Cradle | 1 | 2 | 4 |
| Provinces | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| Hinterland | 4 | 2 | 3 |

Reveal facedown destination; put printed favor/secrets there from shared bank and printed number of relics beside it facedown from relic deck. **Travel cost order:** fixed-number cost first, then other increases/decreases. **Free Travel is the final override:** any applicable power saying “spend no Supply” makes Supply payment zero even if another power increases it. This general rule applies to all Travel, including banishment, not only named site examples. Site entry restrictions still apply unless ignored by name/power.

### Minor actions — no Supply

- Play or discard a facedown adviser. The adviser limit is normally 3 total, faceup and facedown combined. Before exceeding it, discard as required, respecting locks. If the incoming card changes the limit, use the new limit before playing it; with several such cards use the lowest limit, not their sum. Facedown advisers have no suit, restriction banner or power; any card, including a site-only card or Vision, may be held facedown. A faceup adviser cannot be discarded at will; another rule must permit or require its discard, respecting locks. To play it, remove it from facedown advisers and play faceup as if finishing Search: a denizen can go to site or advisers as its restriction allows, while an Exile’s true Vision goes to Revealed Vision and discards their previous revealed Vision. Faceup tree cards are site-only and person cards adviser-only; follow Search’s site capacity and placement rules. Chancellor/Citizens cannot reveal a true Vision, though they may hold it facedown. Follow site favor and When Played effects. All these later plays, including revealing a held Vision, are minor actions costing no Supply. They occur in Act, not Wake.
- Use an accessible Action power; free powers may repeat unless limited, paid ones obey exhaustion.
- Peek at facedown relics at your site. Once you have peeked at a particular relic, you can peek again from any site. Grand Scepter holder may use its power to peek in Reliquary, but cannot use either Scepter power on the turn they took it.
- Move any number of your warbands from your site to board, normally leaving at least one there. **Bandit Crown permits picking up the last physical warband**, because its bandits still count as your own warbands. Drop board warbands only at your site **if you rule it**. Without a power granting you rule, bandit sites cannot be occupied by simple drop-off. Citizen pickup requires Chancellor's permission. Imperial players with pawns together may give/take board warbands from each other with recipient/other player's permission.

### Citizenship and exile during play

**Grand Scepter:** cannot use its powers the turn you took it. Its holder, even an Exile, may offer Citizenship to any Exile **including themself**, or exile another Citizen. Reliquary relics cannot be campaigned/recovered, their powers cannot be used, and they do not count toward goals while there.

**Offer:** the Grand Scepter cannot make an offer on the turn its current holder took it. In later turns it may offer any Exile, including itself. Must promise **exactly one Reliquary relic**; may additionally negotiate a binding exchange of favor, secrets, banners and non-Reliquary relics. May let recipient peek at Reliquary relics. Other binding-deal powers cannot trade Reliquary relics.

**Acceptance:** flip to Citizen; replace all own warbands on board/map with purple. If insufficient purple, new Citizen chooses which to remove and which to replace. Discard revealed Vision, flip own Usurper title to Oathkeeper, end Act if own turn, and refresh to full leftmost Supply. Exchange promised pieces; Chancellor gains the newly uncovered **mandatory** Reliquary modifier. **Forced Citizenship** powers apply the same status-conversion steps without a negotiated offer: no required Scepter offer or Reliquary relic gift, and no invented promised exchange. Only an actual removal of a Reliquary relic uncovers its modifier. All Imperials rule purple sites; Citizenship does not itself grant a separate shared victory.

**Uncovered Reliquary modifiers, mandatory for Chancellor:** Brutal makes the defeated player kill their entire force when Chancellor attacks, even if Chancellor loses. Decadent makes Provinces/Hinterland-to-Cradle Travel cost no Supply, but Travel to Hinterland costs +1. Careless gives 1 additional favor on every Trade, even when trading for secrets, but 1 fewer secret when trading for secrets. Greedy draws 2 additional Search cards (still stopping at a world Vision) but prohibits Search costing more than 2 Supply.

**Exile another Citizen:** Scepter holder pays them 5 favor, +1 if recipient is Oathkeeper, +1 if recipient holds People's Favor, −1 if payer is Oathkeeper, −1 if payer holds People's Favor (range 3–7). Recipient flips Exile, replaces **board** purple warbands with own color and refreshes full. Purple map garrisons stay Imperial. This does not end their Act by itself.

**Self-exile:** Citizen who does not hold Scepter may give its holder favor equal to own board warbands **plus all their secrets on board and denizen/relic/edifice cards**. Flip Exile, replace board warbands with own color, refresh full, and **end Act**. Cannot self-exile while holding Scepter. Map garrisons stay purple.

## Scoring / Victory Conditions

### Oathkeeper title and four Oaths

The four Oath names are **Supremacy, People, Protection, Devotion**. One Oath is active in a game. Its criterion determines who holds the title:

| Oath | Oathkeeper criterion |
|---|---|
| Supremacy | Rule the most sites |
| People | Hold People's Favor |
| Protection | Hold the most relics and banners combined |
| Devotion | Hold Darkest Secret |

For Supremacy, the Chancellor counts all sites ruled by Imperial players. When this collective Imperial count earns the title, the **Chancellor** is its physical holder. Apply the ordinary title-transfer and incumbent-tie rules below.

The four true Vision names are **Conquest, Rebellion, Sanctuary, Faith**. Their correspondence is:

- **Vision of Conquest** matches **Oath of Supremacy**: rule the most sites.
- **Vision of Rebellion** matches **Oath of the People**: hold People's Favor.
- **Vision of Sanctuary** matches **Oath of Protection**: hold the most relics and banners combined.
- **Vision of Faith** matches **Oath of Devotion**: hold Darkest Secret.

### Citizen succession is a separate test

A Citizen wins only by replacing a Chancellor victory with a Successor win. Use the current Oath to select this separate criterion:

| Current Oath | Citizen's Successor goal |
|---|---|
| Supremacy | Hold more relics and banners combined than the Chancellor **and every other Citizen** |
| People | Hold Darkest Secret |
| Protection | Hold People's Favor |
| Devotion | Hold Grand Scepter |

A Citizen does not use the Oathkeeper criterion or a Vision goal as their Successor goal. Meeting the Successor criterion alone never ends the game; it matters when the Chancellor would win.

### Title transfer and victory timing

Current holder keeps title on a tie for its goal. If holder no longer meets goal and several others tie for it, outgoing holder chooses recipient. A new holder always takes it Oathkeeper-side, never Usurper-side. Under Supremacy Chancellor holds it if Empire collectively meets goal; Citizens do not count purple sites separately against each other. For Supremacy, defer any title transfer caused during a Campaign until that Campaign ends, rather than transferring upon a temporary loss of site rule. Outside Campaign, apply ordinary title transfers when the criterion changes. Other Oaths concern personal holdings. The Grand Scepter counts as a relic when held; Reliquary relics do not.

**Usurper victory has two timing windows:** at the start of an Exile's turn during Wake, or at the end of round 8 under the endgame priority. From taking Oathkeeper during Act, the normal sequence is next Wake check (not yet Usurper), then flip to Usurper, then win at the following Wake **or at the end of round 8 if that arrives first while still Usurper**. Losing title prevents this win; retaking resets it to Oathkeeper and restarts the sequence. A title still on Oathkeeper at game end is insufficient.

**Visionary victory:** Exile must have true Vision revealed and goal completed at Wake, with **at least 3 Visions drawn from world deck by all players combined**. This is a shared global count, not a requirement for that Exile to draw three personally. Normal revelation of a held true Vision is a minor action during Act, not Wake; it must already be revealed when the Wake victory check occurs. Can reveal it earlier but cannot win early. For Conquest/Sanctuary “most” means **strictly more than every other player**, no ties. Compare to Empire's shared rule for sites. Citizens/Chancellor cannot win by true Visions or Usurper.

**Stable regime:** at ends of rounds 5, 6 and 7, roll end die only if Chancellor or Citizen is Oathkeeper. Ends on respectively **6 / 5–6 / 3–6**. Chancellor wins, except a Citizen meeting Successor goal wins **alone instead**. An Exile Oathkeeper/Usurper prevents the roll.

**War exhaustion:** round 8 ends automatically. Check in this exact priority:

1. If Chancellor or Citizen is Oathkeeper: Empire wins (Chancellor unless qualified Successor).
2. Otherwise, Exile Usurper wins.
3. Otherwise, qualifying revealed Exile Vision wins. If several qualify, priority **Conquest → Rebellion → Sanctuary → Faith**.
4. Otherwise Empire wins (Chancellor unless qualified Successor), even if an Exile is only Oathkeeper.

Holding Successor goal never ends game on its own. Resolve it whenever Chancellor would win. After victory, write Chronicle below.

## Special Rules & Edge Cases

### Powers, costs and information

Access a card's ordinary power if you rule it or your pawn is at its site; relic holders use held relics. Battle plans instead require rule of the card: pawn presence alone does not grant access. Persistent powers (thick black braid) affect the players specified regardless of ordinary access. Sites specify their own use conditions. Chancellor must apply uncovered Reliquary modifiers; title holder must apply title power.

Pay the braid/text cost. Normally cannot place favor/secrets on a card already holding either; battle-plan payment is the exception, not a license to Muster/Trade there. If power has no cost you can still use it despite existing resources. When paying outside your turn, favor goes immediately to matching bank; secrets stay on your board **facedown**. Facedown secrets cannot pay any place or burn cost until flipped up; your own Rest flips secrets on your board faceup. Burn sends prompted resource to shared bank. Kill sends warband to its color's personal bank (purple to Chancellor). Sacrifice means choose to kill own warbands.

Resolve as much of used power as possible except optional “may” parts; may pay to use a power even if its effect does nothing. Cannot give away/burn/sacrifice extra resources beyond prompted cost or amount.

Faceup **tree** cards are site-only; **person** cards adviser-only; restrictions also govern moves/swaps. **Locked** cards cannot be discarded, moved or swapped once faceup except in Chronicle. Cards reducing adviser limit require discard based on **new limit before playing**; with multiple limit-changers use lowest, not sum. Respect locks.

Ordinary major-action modifiers are chosen at action start, used once per action, in any order; cannot discard an active modifier during that action. No-cost modifier may be used on multiple distinct actions. Must/cannot modifiers are mandatory. Battle plans instead occur in Campaign step 3, attacker first; each once per Campaign. A ± symbol permits its user to choose to add or remove the shown die type; it does not change attack dice into defense dice. Either eligible role can choose either sign; role permission comes from the plan’s box: red is attacker-only, blue defender-only, and gradient permits either attacker or defender. The sign does not grant a role permission. Ordinary Wake powers happen once during Wake and must resolve as much as possible; explicit “may” parts remain optional. Rest powers are optional once each at the end of Rest. Opportunity-site income is explicitly optional. When Played triggers on faceup play and is mandatory after site favor, Homeland effects and modifiers; facedown advisers have no power to trigger. Resolve as much as possible except explicit optional parts.

**Enemies:** for Chancellor/Citizens, enemies are Exiles, not fellow Imperial players. For Exiles and Citizens temporarily non-Imperial during the specified Campaigns, enemies are any other players. Bandits are enemies to everyone and vice versa. During a Campaign “your enemy” means the opposing side: defender and Allies for attacker; attacker for defenders.

**Default transfer words:** Burn sends favor/secrets from your board to shared bank, unless a different source is explicitly prompted. Take favor/secrets from another player means their board to your board, not their banner or resources on cards; Give is the reverse. Take adviser cards moves theirs to your advisers; Take a relic from the prompted player’s personal bank, site or draw source puts it in your personal bank, respecting the prompt and placement restrictions. Exchange permits you to Give to the prompted player and them to Give to you; these are optional permissions unless the actual effect or binding agreement imposes more specific requirements. Do not assume an equal or compulsory reciprocal transfer from the word alone.

**After** means immediately following, with nothing between. You cannot insert another normal action, including a minor action, between an event and its “after” effect.

**Peek and Reveal:** Peek lets the acting player look at a card front; Reveal means show its front to everyone. A generic reveal-only instruction is not itself playing that card, so it does not award site-play favor or trigger When Played. Revealing a true Vision by the normal minor action is specifically a play to the Revealed Vision space, with its own rules.

**Moving and swapping cards:** Move follows capacity, placement and restriction rules; Swap moves the two pieces to each other’s location, obeying those rules for both. A move or swap alone is not playing: it grants no site-play favor and triggers no When Played power. An instruction explicitly saying to play does trigger ordinary play effects.

**Draw outside Search:** the corrected Draw keyword means take from the top of the prompted source; it does not import Search’s entire draw-three/stop procedure into other powers. Whenever a Vision is drawn from world deck, always advance Visions Drawn, including with Oracle. The Search action itself still stops at its first world-deck Vision.

Rules priority: read literally; Law supersedes Playbook/aids except Site Reference; non-italic rules override italic reminders. “Must” overrides ordinary rules; “cannot” overrides even “must”; “ignore” overrides named must/cannot restrictions. “Must if able” has no penalty when unable. Promises are normally nonbinding, and pieces cannot simply be transferred without permission in a rule; Citizenship offers and expressly binding powers are exceptions: involved players must give the agreed pieces if able. Only a Citizenship offer may exchange a Reliquary relic, and it must promise exactly one; other negotiated relic exchanges use non-Reliquary relics. Tribunal permits binding immediate exchanges of favor and secrets and binding future actions with agreed penalties; it does not grant unrestricted immediate relic/banner exchanges. A disputed future breach is settled by coin flip. Give and Take are distinct keywords; restricting one does not automatically restrict the other.

Component limits: favor/warbands/cards limited to available pieces; take/gain as much as possible if source short. Secrets and dice unlimited; use multipliers or roll again if needed. Banks/boards unlimited capacity unless explicitly limited. Facedown advisers can be inspected by holder and shown to others; their identity may be discussed or lied about. Private: facedown card fronts, discard fronts, world-deck count, world-deck backs below top. Public: discard counts/backs and board-resource/warband counts, and all other information. Keep facedown advisers distinct. Playbook group agreements about undoing actions or sharing drawn hands are social options, not additional mandatory rules.

### Site powers (Site Reference)

Site powers still respect restriction banners and locked cards. An exhausted secret stays facedown on its owner’s board and cannot pay another secret cost until readied; its owner’s Rest flips it faceup. This also applies to secrets exhausted for site travel or targeting requirements.

A ruined edifice still uses site card capacity, has no suit, and cannot serve for Muster or Trade. Its printed ruin-side power remains usable under ordinary access and its own conditions.

- **Opportunity** (Salt Flats/Mine/Drowned City): at end of your Wake take 1 favor/secret from site, optionally.
- **Homeland** sites: when you play a matching-suit card there and have not discarded a card **of that same suit at that site this turn**, gain its printed reward: 1 secret from shared bank, favor from matching suit bank, 2 warbands, or take its relic treating this as Recover. Discarding a different suit there does not suppress it. Taking the relic by this power costs neither Supply nor the ordinary site resource cost. Resolve normal site-play favor first, then the Homeland reward, then the played card’s When Played power.
- **Coasts:** traveling from one Coast to another costs 1 Supply and ignores Narrow Pass.
- **Plains/Mountain:** must add/subtract 1 attack die when site or anything at site is targeted, even if attacker rules it.
- **River:** Muster there gains 1 additional warband if you rule it: ordinarily 3 rather than the usual 2, subject to available warbands and other modifiers. Without rule, its bonus does not apply and ordinary Muster still gains 2. Muster requires your pawn there; remote rule does not allow it.
- **Charming Valley:** traveling from it increases Travel cost by 1.
- **Shrouded Wood:** travel from it has a fixed base cost of 2 before other modifiers, ignoring Narrow Pass and Hidden Place; if enemy rules it, travel to site of ruler's choice (Chancellor chooses for Empire), even when someone else makes you travel. Banishment spends no Supply but still obeys this destination choice.
- **Narrow Pass:** for Travel into its region from another region, you must travel to Narrow Pass, even if you rule it. For a Campaign targeting anything in its region from another region, you must also target Narrow Pass unless you rule it. Ruling Narrow Pass exempts the Campaign requirement only; a power explicitly ignoring Narrow Pass can bypass its applicable restriction.
- **Great Slum:** when playing/moving a card there, may first discard a card there.
- **Marshes:** Search while your pawn is there must draw 1 fewer card: ordinarily 2 rather than 3, before other draw modifiers. Still stop at the first world-deck Vision or an empty source. Ruling Marshes remotely does not apply this reduction.
- **Buried Giant:** traveling from it may flip 1 own board secret facedown to spend no Supply and ignore Narrow Pass.
- **Hidden Place:** cannot travel there or declare Campaign targets there unless flipping 1 board secret facedown.
- **Tribunal:** Action if you rule it or pawn there: negotiate binding immediate exchanges of favor and secrets, as well as binding future actions in combination with any player and agreed penalties. This does not grant unrestricted immediate relic/banner transfers.

**Bandit Crown:** treat bandits as own warbands: rule empty faceup sites, may pick up last physical site warband, add bandits at targeted sites to defense (or attacking force with suitable powers). Bandits themselves cannot be killed, moved or sacrificed, including skulls or battle-plan costs.

### Writing the Chronicle (Law §8, with base errata)

Winner performs these steps in order. After first game open Archive packet: place 8 cardboard dividers in the small box compartment; put edifices in matching sections; separately shuffle each suit's denizens into its Archive section.

Chronicle Citizenship is a separate permission from an in-play offer:

| Requirement | In-play offer | Chronicle offer |
|---|---|---|
| Who authorizes it | Grand Scepter holder, after the turn taken | Exile winner |
| Required gift | Exactly 1 Reliquary relic | None |
| Other ordinary offer prerequisites | Apply | Ignored; no Scepter permission or resource payment |

1. **Vow an Oath:** won by Vision alone → next Oath matches that Vision. Any other way or multiple victory methods → choose any Oath **except current one**. Save goal in World Box.
2. **Free Chronicle Citizenship (ignore normal offers):** no Reliquary relic, resource payment or Scepter permission is required. If winner Exile, flip **all** Citizen boards, including boxed, to Exile, then may offer Citizenship to other Exiles except those just flipped. Each accepting Exile must do both: **flip their board to Citizen** and **replace every garrison at each site they rule with exactly one winner-color warband at that site**, regardless of the old garrison size. Winner cannot offer themself Chronicle Citizenship.
3. **Map and edifices:** Imperial winner may build **or** repair **one** edifice. At ruled site with no intact or ruined edifice, replace denizen with matching intact Archive edifice, returning denizen to world deck; or flip a ruled ruin intact. Discard all sites except those winner rules or with an intact edifice; shuffle discarded sites into site deck, discard their denizens, return relics to relic deck and ruined edifices to Archive. At retained sites winner does not rule, flip any intact edifice to ruin. **This ruining step discards denizens only at sites where an intact edifice actually flipped to ruin.** Keep denizens at other retained sites unless another instruction discards them; a power retaining a site does not by itself trigger this discard. Set aside **all sites with ruins**, with attached ruins, remaining denizens and relics, in map order top Cradle to bottom Hinterland. Remove pawns/favor/secrets/warbands from map.
4. **Repack map slots:** excluding set-aside ruins, fill Cradle top-down by pushing Cradle sites up then importing Provinces then Hinterland in top-down order. Fill Provinces top-down by pushing up then importing Hinterland top-down. Push Hinterland sites up. Fill empty Hinterland slots **bottom-up** with set-aside ruins **bottom-up**, then empty Provinces slots similarly. Fill any remaining slots with facedown site-deck cards; in each region with no faceup site, flip top one up.
5. **Add 6 denizens:** winner's most common adviser suit determines 3 random Archive cards, next clockwise suit 2, next clockwise suit 1, to world deck. Choose among tied suits; no suited advisers → choose any. Suit cycle Discord→Arcane→Order→Hearth→Beast→Nomad→Discord. If Archive cannot supply the full allocation, do **not** add that allocation: heal Archive instead. Sort all Dispossessed by suit; take 6 random from largest Dispossessed suit stack into world deck; shuffle remaining Dispossessed into matching Archive suit stacks.
6. **Dispossess 6:** set aside all 5 Visions including Conspiracy. Shuffle all discard piles with **losers' advisers**, move 6 random cards unseen to Dispossessed. Winner's advisers are excluded from this pool.
7. **Relics:** save Grand Scepter in World Box; return losing players' relics to relic deck and shuffle. Refill faceup sites with fewer relics than printed R icons up to printed number, facedown. Shuffle winner's remaining relics with Reliquary relics and stack atop relic deck.
8. **Save world:** stack each site's attached cards on it, stack sites so top Cradle on top and bottom Hinterland at bottom, put in World Box. Save player boards on current Exile/Citizen sides. Next game may have different people: after seats are assigned, non-Chancellors choose among these boards on their saved sides in turn order, rather than automatically inheriting a particular person’s former board.
9. **Rebuild world deck:** shuffle all denizens remaining in play (not those retained at sites/Archive/Dispossessed). Top stack **10 denizens + 2 random Visions = 12**; middle **15 denizens + remaining 3 Visions = 18**; remaining denizens bottom. Save in Archive World Deck section. Chronicle aid's printed “12 denizens + 2” is incorrect; do not make a 14-card top stack.
