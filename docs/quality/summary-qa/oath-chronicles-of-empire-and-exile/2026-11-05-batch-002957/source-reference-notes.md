# Source scope and inspection

Assigned sources were read in full: the 12-page Law of Oath dated October 20, 2020 and corresponding complete 32-page Playbook. Multiplayer text only is included; component-sheet reproductions of the automated opponent are excluded. Law section numbers and printed page labels are used in the keys. Extraction line numbers are never presented as PDF pages.

Additional authoritative source: the two-page official Reference Sheet (image-only PDF). Both pages were rendered with PyMuPDF and inspected visually. The Homeland block was inspected at higher resolution: its suppression condition is a prior same-suit discard at that site this turn; it includes secrets and directly takes a relic as recovery. The standalone Site Reference governs over contrary Playbook shorthand. A high-resolution rendering of the Playbook p. 3 Reliquary board confirms Careless gives +1 favor even when trading for secrets, and −1 secret on secret trades. Supply brackets were visually checked in Law setup and Playbook p. 3 board images.

Official errata is the complete Google document titled Oath Clarifications and Errata, updated November 15, 2022, fetched through its text export and preserved in errata.txt. The base-game Law, card and Chronicle entries are included; Clockwork Prince/Princely Reliquary entries are excluded. In particular the no-faceup-advisers Darkest Secret parenthetical is corrected, and Imperial sidewide power sharing overrides the obsolete Glossary You restriction.

No New Foundations, newer bot or expansion mechanics are included. Card-specific powers not reproduced in the complete authoritative booklets remain on their cards; the summary explains the full framework for using them and reproduces all core site procedures.
