## Q001

An Exile takes the Oathkeeper title during their Act phase. When can they first win as Usurper, and what happens if someone takes the title away meanwhile?

**Answer:** At their next Wake they check victory before flipping Oathkeeper to Usurper, so cannot yet win as Usurper; if still Usurper at the following Wake they win. Losing the title prevents this; a new holder receives it Oathkeeper-side. At end of round eight an existing Usurper can win under the ordered endgame rules.

**Source:** Law 2.11, 3.1, 3.4, 4.1.2–3

## Q002

List all four Oaths, their matching Visions, and the extra universal requirement for a Visionary win. Do ties for most count for a Vision?

**Answer:** Supremacy/Conquest: most sites; People/Rebellion: People’s Favor; Protection/Sanctuary: most relics and banners combined; Devotion/Faith: Darkest Secret. Exile must have the Vision revealed, fulfill it at Wake (or the round-eight check), and at least three Visions must have been drawn from the world deck. Most means strictly more than every other player, not a tie.

**Source:** Law 2.11,3.2,3.4; Errata 3.2

## Q003

When are end dice rolled, what results end the game, and can an Exile Oathkeeper permit such a roll?

**Answer:** Only at end of rounds five/six/seven with Chancellor or Citizen Oathkeeper: 6 / 5–6 / 3–6 respectively. Exile Oathkeeper prevents the roll. Eighth round ends automatically.

**Source:** Law 3.3,3.4

## Q004

At round eight, what is the complete victory priority including simultaneous Visions and Citizens?

**Answer:** Empire if Chancellor/Citizen Oathkeeper, then Exile Usurper, then qualifying Exile Visionary (Conquest > Rebellion > Sanctuary > Faith), then Empire fallback. Each Empire win is Chancellor unless a Citizen fulfills the Successor goal, who wins alone instead.

**Source:** Law 3.3.1,3.4; Playbook p26

## Q005

Give the Successor goal for each Oath. May a Citizen win by a revealed Vision or becoming Usurper?

**Answer:** Supremacy: more relics+banners than Chancellor and every other Citizen; People: Darkest Secret; Protection: People’s Favor; Devotion: Grand Scepter. Citizens do not win by Usurper or Vision, only replace a Chancellor win via succession.

**Source:** Law 3.3.1,3.1–2

## Q006

Resolve the People’s Favor at Wake, including the Mob side, its last favor, and when the Mob threshold is checked.

**Answer:** First choose to put one board favor on it or move one from it to least-full bank (choose ties); it cannot lose its last favor, so at one must add if able, otherwise nothing. Repeat that step once if already Mob. After these steps flip to Mob if six or more favor. Then check Exile wins, flip Oathkeeper, and resolve optional opportunity-site income.

**Source:** Law 4.1

## Q007

What happens in Rest, in order, and how do Citizen Supply and saved Supply work?

**Answer:** Return favor from denizens/edifices to matching banks; secrets from denizens/edifices/relics to acting board and flip own facedown secrets up; Exile/Chancellor refresh to personal-bank-warband space, Citizen to exact Chancellor marker space including leftmost; add remaining unspent Supply by moving left, capped at leftmost; optional Rest powers once each any order.

**Source:** Law 4.3; Errata 4.3.3

## Q008

Explain Search sources/costs and how drawing a Vision differs between world deck and discard pile.

**Answer:** Choose only world deck or own-region discard, 2 Supply for discard, world cost 2 at zero Visions, 3 at one/two, 4 at three–five, modified by powers (Darkest Secret makes world draw 2). Draw the prescribed three, or all available if source runs out, except when a world Vision stops drawing or a modifier changes count; from world one at a time, announce a Vision, stop immediately and increment track; discard Vision does neither. Discard all but one before playing or discarding that one.

**Source:** Law 5.1; Playbook p12,32; map Visions Drawn track

## Q009

Where do discarded cards and resources on them go, including a card at a remote site?

**Answer:** Facedown atop next-region pile: Cradle→Provinces→Hinterland→Cradle, acting player chooses order. Base on pawn region for drawn/adviser cards; if discarding a card in a different region, use that card’s region. Favor immediately goes to matching bank; secrets to acting board facedown.

**Source:** Law Glossary Discard

## Q010

How do adviser limits, facedown restrictions, revealing later, and replacing a true Vision work?

**Answer:** Three advisers including facedown; facedown has no suit/banner/power, any card can be held thus. Before playing over limit discard an adviser, respecting locks and any new changed limit. Minor action may play facedown adviser faceup as searched (site/advisers/revealed Vision as legal) or discard it. True faceup Vision only Exile revealed slot, not adviser, replaces/discards existing Vision. Cannot simply discard faceup adviser at will.

**Source:** Law 5.1.4,6.1,7.2,7.6.4; Errata 7.6.4

## Q011

What are Muster and both Trade costs, eligible cards, gains, destinations, and limitations when no advisers match?

**Answer:** Only empty denizen/intact edifice at pawn site, no favor/secrets; not relic/ruin. Muster 1 Supply+one favor on card → two personal-bank warbands to board, purple for Citizen. Trade 1 Supply; one secret on card → one favor+one per matching faceup adviser from matching bank; or two favor on card → one shared-bank secret per matching faceup adviser (zero if none). Finite source shortfall gives as much as possible.

**Source:** Law 5.2–3,9.3

## Q012

How do you Recover a relic versus either banner, and what happens to the old banner resources?

**Answer:** 1 Supply; facedown relic at pawn site only at printed site cost, then take faceup personal bank. Either banner from anywhere, even yourself: pay more favor/secrets than old amount, stack new payment. People’s Favor flips off Mob and old favor distributes singly along banks away from deck from any starting suit wrapping. Darkest Secret: take one old secret, previous holder gets rest (self gets all; bank remainder stays bank).

**Source:** Law 5.4; banner placards

## Q013

When is another player’s Darkest Secret safe or recoverable? Include no advisers, no cards, and a ruin-only site.

**Answer:** Recoverable if at least one suited card at holder’s pawn site matches none of their faceup advisers; no faceup advisers does not protect it if suited card exists. Empty/ruin-only site protects it; ignore suits absent on ruins/facedown advisers. Can recover from self or shared bank freely, still paying cost.

**Source:** Law 5.4.1 corrected by Errata 5.4; banner

## Q014

Explain legal Campaign defender and target selection, including remote sites and a same-site relic target.

**Answer:** 2 Supply; defender another player ruling pawn site or whose pawn there, or bandits if no player rules it. At least one target at pawn site; if defender rules it must target it. May target any defender sites anywhere; relics/banners or pawn+favor only if their pawn is at attacker site. Relic target of co-located defender satisfies local target requirement. Pawn+favor adds two defense dice; sites one each; relic printed shields; banners resource count.

**Source:** Law 5.5.1–2; Errata 5.5.2

## Q015

How are Campaign attack/defense calculated, and how do skulls, hollow swords, ties and sacrifices work?

**Answer:** The attacker may collect attack dice up to the number of warbands in their attacking force (normally those on their board), after accounting for any applicable power that changes that force. Then apply the appropriate dice modifiers. Defense shields multiplied by each double (x2,x4,...), plus warbands at targeted sites and defender board if pawn at attacker or targeted site; bandits one per targeted site. Attack skulls kill own force immediately; paired hollow swords count one, lone hollow zero. Attack must strictly exceed defense; may sacrifice force warbands +1 each, exactly enough to win, no excess; if unable/decline defender wins.

**Source:** Law 5.5.2,5.5.4–6,9.5; official errata Law5.5.2 attacking FORCE ceiling

## Q016

After an attacker wins, resolve defeat and all targets, including choosing zero garrisons, banner seizure, and banishment.

**Answer:** Defeated force kills half rounded down; remaining returns to board; defeated effects. Attacker may move any number including zero from force onto targeted sites, takes all targeted relics/banners; seized banners burn two resources to min one, People’s Favor becomes Mob. If pawn+favor targeted may banish to legal chosen site for no Supply and may burn half favor rounded down. Shrouded Wood can override destination. Victory effects then end-discard plans.

**Source:** Law 5.5.6–8,2.5.3

## Q017

What changes in a Campaign involving Imperial defenders or a Citizen attacking another Imperial player?

**Answer:** Citizen attacker against Chancellor/Citizen is non-Imperial for that Campaign; If Chancellor chooses Citizen as defender, that Citizen is non-Imperial during this Campaign. Imperial defender gets Chancellor automatically as Ally; non-attacker Citizens may join with permission if pawn at targeted site or attacker site. Activate defender/allies modifiers, share side powers and battle plans, one use per plan paid by user, never another’s advisers; qualifying allied board warbands defend. Chancellor chooses losses; survivors at sites go Chancellor board, Citizens keep own board survivors. Declared defender decides contested timing/order. Oathkeeper defense bonus shared even non-Supremacy.

**Source:** Law 5.5,2.11; Errata 5.5

## Q018

State all base Travel costs and what happens when a facedown destination is revealed. How do fixed costs, increases and free Travel interact?

**Answer:** Cradle→Cradle 1, Provinces 2, Hinterland 4; Provinces→any other site 2; Hinterland→Hinterland 3, Provinces 2, Cradle 4. Move pawn, reveal site and add printed favor/secrets from shared bank and facedown relics from relic deck. Apply fixed-number Travel cost first then other modifiers; spend-no-Supply overrides all increases. Pawn movement does not move site warbands.

**Source:** Law 5.6,7.6.2; Errata 5.6

## Q019

How can Citizenship be offered and accepted during play, and what may or must be exchanged?

**Answer:** Grand Scepter holder (not taken this turn) offers any Exile including self exactly one Reliquary relic plus optional binding exchange favor,secrets,banners,non-Reliquary relics. Accepted: flip Citizen, replace all board/map warbands purple as available with recipient choosing shortfall removals/replacements, discard revealed Vision, Usurper→Oathkeeper, end Act if own turn, refresh full. Exchange agreed items; Chancellor gains uncovered mandatory modifier. All Imperials rule purple sites. Reliquary relics inaccessible to Campaign/Recover, powers unusable, do not count goals.

**Source:** Law 2.3,6.6; Grand Scepter

## Q020

Describe the Chronicle’s new Oath choice, Citizenship turnover, and world-deck rebuilding, including why the top stack is not 14 cards.

**Source:** Law 8.1–2,8.8; Errata 8.2 and Chronicle Aid

**Source:** Law 7.4

## Q030

What do tree, person and chain restrictions do to faceup play/move/swap/discard, and how are two adviser-limit reductions combined and checked when played?

**Answer:** Tree site-only,person adviser-only,also move/swap; locked cannot discard/move/swap once played except Chronicle. Restriction banners on facedown cards do not apply. **Source:** Law 7.2,7.6.4; Errata 7.6.4

## Q031

Who can play Conspiracy, what exact theft conditions/cost apply, can two advisers match one enemy adviser, and where does Conspiracy end?

**Answer:** Anyone, even Imperials. Burn one secret to take one relic or banner from any player whose pawn is at your site. Two of your own faceup advisers must each match the suit of at least one of that target player’s advisers; both may match the same adviser. The target need not be an enemy. Playing Conspiracy does not replace a revealed true Vision; banner seizure applies; Conspiracy goes to the box even if no theft is made.

**Source:** Law 5.1.4.IV; Reference p1

## Q039

A defender’s pawn is away from attacker and every targeted site. Do board warbands defend, and do warbands at untargeted sites defend? How are shield doubles applied?

**Answer:** No board force unless pawn at attacker/targeted site; only targeted-site garrisons add,not untargeted sites. Doubles multiply rolled shields exponentially,not warband defense. No intrinsic warband defense dice.

**Source:** Law 5.5.4; Playbook p32

## Q040

Are warbands, favor, secrets and dice component-limited? If a requested gain exceeds a source, what is done, and can you intentionally burn extra resources?

**Source:** Law 9.3,9.5

## Q041

Which game information is private and public? May a holder show a facedown adviser or lie about its identity?

**Answer:** Private facedown fronts,discard fronts,world-deck count and backs beyond top. Public all else including discard counts/backs and board favor,secrets,warbands. Holder may peek/show facedown adviser and players may lie about private info; cannot mix advisers indistinguishably.

**Source:** Law 9.4

## Q042

How do must, cannot, ignore and must-if-able interact; which book takes priority; do a Give restriction and a Take restriction mean the same thing?

**Answer:** Cannot beats must,which beats ordinary; ignore overrides specifically named must/cannot. Must-if-able fulfilled when possible,otherwise no penalty. Law beats Playbook/aids except Site Reference,nonitalic beats reminder. Give/Take distinct; restricting one does not change other.

**Source:** Law 9.1–2; Errata Glossary

**Answer:** No. After means immediately following: nothing happens between the specified event and the prompted instruction. It does not provide a discretionary later window within the action. ## Q099

Unless an effect specifies different locations, where do favor/secrets, an adviser card, and a relic move when you Take them? How does Give reverse the direction? **Answer:** Take moves the prompted favor or secrets from the prompted player’s board to your board, a card from their advisers to your advisers, or a relic from their personal bank—or the prompted site or draw source—to your personal bank. **Source:** Law 10, Take, Give, Burn; Law 9.1 literal interpretation
