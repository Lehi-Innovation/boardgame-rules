What is a border space?
A border space is a space on your zoo plan that does not have another space on at least 1 of its 6 sides.
When several players gain a card during the break, is the display refilled after each player?
Yes, the display is filled after each player.
What happens if my Conservation counter or Appeal counter hits the end of its track? Can I still get more points?
No, you cannot gain more than 113 Appeal or 41 conversation points.
When playing an Animal card, by how much does a partner zoo reduce the cost? What counts as a continent icon for that 
purpose?
You reduce the cost by 3 money per continent icon on that Animal card if you have the matching partner zoo. So normally by 3, but by 
6 in rare cases where there are 2 of the same continent icon on the card. The conditions on the left side of the card never count as icons, 
neither for this purpose nor another.
Is it possible to place and use 2 x2 tokens on the same card?
Yes, you can have multiple x2 tokens on the same card. And yes, you can use multiple x2 tokens and each one will give you 1 extra action. 
So with 2 x2 tokens, you will get up to 3 actions. Though that should be a very rare case.
Can I take 2 X-tokens from a card with a x2 token?
Yes, you can use a x2 token on any Action card to take 2 X-tokens. What you cannot do is take 1 X-token and 1 “regular” action.
The glossary states: “Multiplier [Association, Build, Cards, Sponsors]: The next time you execute the action, you may execute it 
twice.” Is a x2 Token allowed on the Animals card?
Animals was omitted because there are animals that have the Multiplier ability for all of the 4 mentioned actions, but not for the Animals 
action. But if you get a Multiplier token in another way, like the conservation point bonus, you can place it on any Action card, including 
the Animals Action card.
For a card like Pygmy Hippopotamus (“Action: Sponsor”) or Sun Bear (“Action: Association”), are you are allowed to move the 
action card but gain an X-token instead?
No. “Take an X-token” is a separate 6th action. If you gain a specific action (“Action: Sponsor”), you cannot substitute the take 1 X-token 
action instead. You may take an X-token from a Determination ability which grants Any Action.
