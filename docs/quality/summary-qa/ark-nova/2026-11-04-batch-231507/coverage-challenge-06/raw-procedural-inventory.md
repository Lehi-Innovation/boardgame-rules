## Turn engine, timing, icons (main 6,8; glossary1,4; FAQ2–3; icons)

- No formal rounds; one action per turn with triggered effects; strength slot 1–5, chosen card lowered then after action moved to1, intervening left cards shift right.
- X tokens +1 strength each, as many as held, cap5; strength set before action, newly gained X cannot boost ongoing action; >5 may improve nothing.
- Action cannot do nothing; alternative X action exactly one X independently of slot/side, shift any card then gain; forbidden at cap5; cannot spend X to strengthen this sixth action.
- Upgrade flips selected I toII in place, available from next action; immediately counts upgraded for conditions. Four upgrade opportunities, conservation2 option may instead hire worker, so cannot upgrade all five.
- Lock action type and strength at start; resolve sequential tasks/cards/buildings, newly gained resources/cards/workers may be used later in same action; upgrades do not change locked action behavior.
- Immediate effects/bonuses resolve immediately before next card/building. Simultaneous effects choose order. After-finishing effects wait until entire action and card shift complete. Multiple after-finishing effects order chosen.
- Card text overrides rules. Card counts its own icons for immediate and recurring effects. Zoo icons are top-right on own Animal/Sponsor and own partner/university, plus top-left water/rock requirements; left conditions/project symbols do not count. Double icons count twice for triggers/counts/discounts.
- Your zoo includes own cards/map/personal supply; any zoo aggregates players. Connected = uncovered space adjacent to covered space; isolated = not adjacent to covered space; border lacks hex neighbour on at least one edge. Building spaces exclude water/rock (upgraded-only empty spaces still building spaces).
- Color/position effect syntax: blue permanent/recurring, purple break income, yellow instant, brown final. X:Y: no number/per repeats per fulfillment; numbered threshold pays once at highest fulfilled threshold.

## Novelty and saturation stopping discipline for the bank phase

Credit requires a source-supported procedure, rule-defined branch, or consequential priority/timing/exception interaction. A numerical example, swapped resource/icon/name, raw table row, or arbitrary combination of already-tested rules is not new credit. Lookup rows may be genuinely unrequested facts without being novel questions. A bank prompt asking HOW TO READ a table is not necessarily asking every cell; literal prompt coverage must determine edges. A source rule never asked remains a gap even if its answer key happens to mention it. No verdict until full bank/map/lookup/ledger read after READY; no quota or cap.
