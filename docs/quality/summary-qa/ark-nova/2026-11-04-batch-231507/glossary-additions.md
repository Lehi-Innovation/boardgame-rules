### Base glossary: shared principles

Card text takes priority over general rules. Cards count their own icons for instant effects and recurring triggers. Only upper-right card icons, partner-zoo/university icons and upper-left water/rock requirements count as zoo icons; left-side conditions and conservation-project icons do not. Doubled icons count twice and trigger matching effects twice. Terrain adjacency counts distinct hexes: touching one water hex in a larger water area gives one adjacency; several edges touching that hex still give only one. Water/rock illustrations on tiles provide neither terrain spaces nor requirements.

A connected space is **uncovered** and shares an edge with a covered space. An isolated space has no adjacent covered space. A border space lacks a neighboring map hex on at least one of its six sides. "Your zoo" includes your played cards, map and personal supply; "any zoo" includes these for all players.

For an effect written "X : Y," a condition without a number (or with "per") pays once per fulfillment. A numbered threshold pays only once; with several thresholds use the highest one met. Blue effects are permanent/recurring, purple gives break income, yellow is an instant play benefit and brown is final scoring.

Unique buildings must be placed when playing their Sponsor: if placement is impossible, the card cannot be played. Use the matching tile, colored face up; rotate it freely, but do not flip it. Apply ordinary placement rules and any terrain requirements; no size-based construction payment is due. Unique buildings are not enclosures, cannot house additional animals and never count as water or rock spaces.

### Base animal ability clarifications

These clarify the abilities printed on cards; follow each card for its specified amount.

- **Action / Determination:** finish Animals, including moving its card to slot 1, before taking the granted action. Use its Action card normally and move it to slot 1 afterward. A named Action (such as Sponsors) cannot be replaced by taking an X-token; Determination grants any action and may choose the X-token alternative.
- **Boost / Clever:** execute after finishing Animals and moving that card. Clever puts the chosen card into slot 1; cards formerly to its left shift right. Boost puts the named card into slot 1 or 5; moving to 5 shifts cards formerly to its right left.
- **Assertion / Dominance:** search the unused face-down base-project deck, add the chosen project (Assertion) or named project (Dominance) to hand, and return the deck. Dominance has no effect if its named project is already in play. Later play and immediately support the obtained card through Association, placing it **above** the board; it does not join the setup base row below.
- **Digging:** decide again after each card which option to use. A display card discarded through Digging is replaced **immediately**, shifting higher folders down and drawing into folder 6.
- **Flock Animal:** if another herbivore in your zoo requires at least the flock animal's standard-enclosure size, you need not flip an empty enclosure. The same qualifying herbivore can host any number of flock animals and need not itself be a flock animal. Without a qualifying herbivore, house the flock animal normally; a later non-flock herbivore cannot join it in that enclosure. Releasing a flock animal still frees an occupied enclosure under the release/fallback procedure even if the flock animal originally shared. Exact historical residents are not tracked.
- **Full-throated:** activate the lowest remaining reserve worker, moving it to the notepad for immediate use. Take a last-worker map reward if shown. If all workers are active, this ability has no effect.
- **Hunter:** add one revealed Animal card to hand and discard all other revealed cards. With no Animal among them, discard all.
- **Snapping:** take exactly one display card regardless of reputation; drawing from the deck is not an alternative. **Sponsor Magnet:** the display sponsors need not be in reputation range; if no sponsor is present, nothing happens. Ordinary end-of-turn display refill applies.
- **Scavenging:** shuffle the discard pile face down, draw randomly from it, make the card's stated selection, then return the discard pile face up. **Sunbathing:** sold cards go to the discard pile.
- **Pouch:** tucked cards have no remaining function. If the animal is released, discard its tucked cards too, but retain the appeal already earned by tucking them.
- **Resistance:** an extra Final Scoring card drawn before the 10-conservation milestone joins your choices when you discard one card. At game end score every remaining Final Scoring card, each subject to its own four-point cap.
- **Petting Zoo animal:** your first grants 3 appeal, second 6, third 9. The single Petting Zoo holds at most three such animals. They count as small animals.
- **Posturing:** normal placement rules apply, including kiosk spacing; place multiple buildings one at a time. **Peacocking:** may place the Large Bird Aviary before upgrading Build, but other placement restrictions still apply; it fails if you already have that enclosure or no legal site.

**Multiplier.** Place a gained token immediately onto the named Action card; a bonus-tile Multiplier may go on any card, including Animals. On its next use you may execute that action twice in succession at the same base strength, moving its Action card only after both executions. Each X-token boosts only one execution. Each additional Multiplier used adds one execution: two tokens permit up to three actions, not four. You may choose different permitted options within each regular execution (Sponsors could play cards then take money). A multiplied X-token alternative may take two X-tokens, subject to the five-token cap, but may not mix one X-token action with one regular action. Defer after-finishing effects until all executions are done. A break triggered by the first execution waits until the entire turn is finished. Return used tokens to the supply; an unused token is removed during the next break.

**Interactive abilities.** Only zoos with at least five appeal can be affected. The playing animal's appeal may be awarded before or after its ability, so its new appeal normally need not count when deciding the affected player.

- **Constriction:** place tokens on cards in the highest slots (one on 5, or two on 4 and 5). Never stack two Constriction tokens on one card; return duplicates. An affected action loses two strength; this applies to both executions of a Multiplier. X-tokens can restore strength. At effective strength zero or below, use enough X-tokens to reach at least one, use the alternative X-token action, or leave the card until it is at least in slot 3. Return the token after acting, or without effect in the break.
- **Venom:** place tokens on cards in the lowest slots (1, then 2, etc.), at most one per card; duplicates return to supply. Using a card with Venom removes its token and avoids all Venom fees that turn. This includes the alternative X-token action if you hold fewer than five X-tokens. Otherwise, if any Venom remains on your cards at turn end, pay two money. If you cannot, undo the turn and choose a different action; you cannot escape the fee by spending all your money. The next break clears unused tokens without effect.
- **Hypnosis:** choose the affected player among ties for highest appeal; if you yourself are the target it has no effect. Execute the targeted Action card using its owner's upgraded/unupgraded side for that whole action. Other action-card upgrade checks use your own cards. You may spend your own X-tokens. Venom and Constriction on the targeted card apply to you and are discarded afterward; its Multiplier cannot be used. Move the targeted card to slot 1 normally.
- **Pilfering:** ties for highest appeal or conservation let you choose the affected player; a conservation leader needs at least one conservation point. Self-targeting has no effect. The target chooses to give five money or allow a random hand-card draw (never Final Scoring cards). With no hand cards, give five money; with less than five money, give a card. With neither a card nor five money, give all current money, including zero. Resolve repeated pilferings sequentially; the target may make a different choice each time.
- **Jumping:** reaching the last Break space grants the usual X-token and the break waits until after your whole turn.

For animal sizes, **small** means printed standard size 1 or 2, including Petting Zoo animals; **large** means size 4 or 5. Size 3 is neither.

### Base Final Scoring and map clarifications

- **Architectural Zoo (004):** connected means uncovered and beside a covered space. For full coverage count building spaces, excluding water/rock. **Conservation Zoo (005):** count project supports by missing left-map tokens. **Naturalists' Zoo (006):** empty building spaces include upgrade-restricted spaces even if Build was not upgraded, but exclude water/rock. **Biodiverse Zoo (009):** compare category icons in your zoo with the player to your right; if they have none of a category, one of yours suffices for its point. Maximum four conservation points from the card.
- **Map 1, Observation Tower:** its bonus applies only to standard enclosures adjacent to the tower hex itself, not just another rock hex. Housing an animal there gives the two appeal again after an earlier animal was released. Special enclosures get no tower effect. The tower is not a building for kiosk income.
- **Map 2, Outdoor Areas:** a standard enclosure touching the Outdoor Area hex (not merely another water hex) counts as two spaces larger **for all purposes after placement**. This bonus cannot be ignored on release or transfer; a physical size-2 enclosure there is treated as size 4. No effect on special enclosures; the Outdoor Area is not a building.
- **Map 4, Commercial Harbor:** a building on its adjacent building space connects/activates it. Your first building may start beside the Harbor, but need not. Its discard ability may be used before, during or after your action, only once per own turn; never during breaks or another player's turn. The Harbor is not a building.
- **Map 5, Park Restaurant:** break income is one money per adjacent **covered hex**, regardless of building type, occupancy or several hexes belonging to one building. The Restaurant is not a building. Its special-enclosure placement bonus gives **one** of the two pictured special enclosures free, even before Build II, not both.
- **Map 6, Research Institute:** a building on its adjacent building space activates it; your first building may begin there but need not. Thereafter ignore one Animal-card condition, including one required icon or the Animals upgrade. All other conditions and all terrain requirements remain. The Institute is not a building.
- **Map 7, Ice Cream Parlors:** once all printed activation spaces are covered, every kiosk gives one extra money each break, whether built free or normally, even with no normally qualifying neighbors.
- **Map 8, Hollywood Hills:** covering each H reveals deck cards until a Sponsor appears; keep it and discard the others. H is not a placement bonus (so does not trigger Archaeologist). After covering all three, every Sponsor's level is one lower.

### Official base FAQ

- A bonus granting a partner zoo or university takes an **available Association-board tile**, not one directly from general supply. A third/fourth partner requires Association II from any source; upgrade-restricted building spaces require Build II even with free buildings or Sponsors.
- A bonus letting you pay money to play a Sponsor can play it **only from hand**, even if Sponsors is upgraded.
- Appeal cannot exceed **113**, nor conservation **41**; gains beyond those ends are lost. Reputation has its own overflow rule described above.
