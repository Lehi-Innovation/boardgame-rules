GAMEPLAYGAMEPLAY
Ark Nova does not play in formal rounds; instead, players simply take turns (in clockwise order) taking one turn at a time until
the end of the game is triggered. In addition, there are so-called breaks in between, when the game is put on hold for an
administration phase. During these breaks you will discard cards down to your hand limit, recall workers, and gain income,
among other things. The break procedure is explained in detail on page 18.
You always perform one action during your turn, which may trigger additional effects and bonuses. You perform these actions
with the 5 Action cards displayed below your zoo map. You can expand your zoo, play Zoo cards, and draw new Zoo cards. The
position of the Action card determines how much you can do with that action. For more details on this mechanism, see the
chapter A Turn / The 5 Action Cards on page 8.
