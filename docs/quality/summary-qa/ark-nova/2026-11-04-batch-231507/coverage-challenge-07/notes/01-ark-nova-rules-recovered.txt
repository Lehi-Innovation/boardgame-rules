0033  INTRODUCTION & GOAL OF THE GAME 
0034  INTRODUCTION & GOAL OF THE GAME 
0035 In Ark Nova you plan and build a modern, scientifically 
0036 managed zoo. You build enclosures according to the 
0037 animals’ way of life and support conservation projects to 
0038 ensure the preservation of animal species.
0039 Gathering diverse animals increases the attractiveness 
0040 of your zoo, advancing you on the Appeal track and 
0041 generating higher revenues. Likewise, careful stewardship 
0042 of the animals allows you to support conservation projects, 
0043 such as breeding programs, that lead to your advancement 
0044 on the Conservation track and corresponding bonuses. 
0045 These two tracks progress in opposite directions – if your 
0046 counters meet, the game ends. Whoever achieves the best 
0047 combination of appeal and conservation has built the most 
0048 successful zoo and wins the game. 
0049 Association work in different zoo associations allows you 
0050 to partner with zoos and universities around the world. 
0051 Together, with specialists and sponsors, they will help you 
0052 manage the various tasks needed to achieve your goals.
0053 Both the designer and the publisher have made every effort 
0054 to be realistic in the implementation of this game. In rare 
0055 cases, however, concessions have been made for the sake 
0056 of the game mechanics. For example, the koala has the 
0057 subclass bear, which is often used colloquially, but does not 
0058 correspond to reality. The same applies to the enclosure 
0059 requirements.

0003    Your Zoo Map   
0004 Your zoo (1) is established in the large area in the middle 
0005 of your zoo map. This is where you place buildings such as 
0006 enclosures (standard or special enclosures), kiosks, pavilions, 
0007 or unique buildings. An overview of all enclosures shows 
0008 their sizes and shapes (2).
0009 You are not allowed to build on the rock (R) and water (W) 
0010 spaces, but some animals need an enclosure next to a rock or 
0011 water space. All other spaces are building spaces, meaning 
0012 you can place buildings on them. A space that has been built 
0013 on is referred to as covered in game terminology. You are not 
0014 allowed to build on spaces with the icon 
0015  until you have 
0016 upgraded your Build action card (see A Turn / The 5 Action 
0017 Cards on page 8 for more information).
0018 Some spaces show a placement bonus 
0019  (3). You receive 
0020 the bonus indicated by the icon immediately when you cover 
0021 it. The Icon overview explains all bonuses.
0022 The left edge of your zoo map has bonus spaces that can be 
0023 activated by supporting various conservation projects (4). At 
0024 the beginning of the game these spaces hold 7 player tokens. 
0025 Whenever you support a conservation project, you take 
0026 one of these player tokens and place it on the Conservation 
0027 Project card. This always gains you an immediate bonus and 
0028 sometimes you gain that bonus again in each break.
0029 The bottom of your zoo map has card slots to hold your 
0030 Action cards (5). The number above each slot shows the 
0031 strength of the Action card in that slot.
0032 On the more advanced zoo maps you will find a special 
0033 ability that distinguishes your zoo from all others (6). Map 
0034 A, which we recommend for your first game, has the same 
0035 special ability for all players. At the beginning of the game, 
0036 place a 3-space enclosure on the indicated spaces, with the 
0037 side showing the number 3 (the empty side) up.
0038    GAMEPLAY   
0039    GAMEPLAY   
0040 Ark Nova does not play in formal rounds; instead, players simply take turns (in clockwise order) taking one turn at a time until 
0041 the end of the game is triggered. In addition, there are so-called breaks in between, when the game is put on hold for an 
0042 administration phase. During these breaks you will discard cards down to your hand limit, recall workers, and gain income, 
0043 among other things. The break procedure is explained in detail on page 18.
0044 You always perform one action during your turn, which may trigger additional effects and bonuses. You perform these actions 
0045 with the 5 Action cards displayed below your zoo map. You can expand your zoo, play Zoo cards, and draw new Zoo cards. The 
0046 position of the Action card determines how much you can do with that action. For more details on this mechanism, see the 
0047 chapter A Turn / The 5 Action Cards on page 8.

0003    A Turn / The 5 Action Cards   
0004 Your Action cards are located below your zoo map. Each 
0005 card is always assigned to a card slot at the bottom of the 
0006 map. The number on the card slot indicates the strength 
0007  
0008 with which you can use the Action card located there. The 
0009 numbers range from 1 on the far left to 5 on the far right, 
0010 meaning the further to the right your Action card is when you 
0011 perform the action, the more powerful the action will be.
0012 On your turn, choose one of your Action cards to perform its 
0013 action, shift it to be lower than the others in the row, then 
0014 perform the action of that card with a strength of 
0015 . After 
0016 completing the action, remove the chosen Action card from 
0017 its slot, shift all Action cards to the left of the newly empty 
0018 slot to the right, and place the chosen Action card in the 
0019 now-empty slot 1 .
0020 You may use X-tokens 
0021  to increase the strength of an 
0022 action. For each X-token you use, increase the strength 
0023  of 
0024 the action by 1. Then return the token to the general supply. 
0025 You can use as many X-tokens as you like in one action. 
0026 With some actions, however, a strength greater than 5  no 
0027 longer improves the action. You can never have more than 
0028 5 X-tokens. You set the strength of an action before you 
0029 perform it. This means you can’t use X-tokens gained while 
0030 performing the action to increase the strength of this action.
0031 Some Zoo cards allow you to execute an effect “after 
0032 finishing” your action. In this case, complete your action, 
0033 including moving the Action cards, before executing the 
0034 effect. 
0035 At the beginning of the game, all your Action cards are 
0036 displayed on  side I .  If you receive a card upgrade 
0037 , you 
0038 may flip an Action card of your choice from  side I  to  side II . 
0039 The card remains in its current card slot. Starting with your 
0040 next action, you can then use that Action card to perform an 
0041 improved version of the action for the rest of the game.
0042 You may not “do nothing” with your action (e. g. play 0 Animal 
0043 cards or build 0 buildings).
0044 The individual actions are explained in detail on the following 
0045 pages. Here is a short overview of the actions:
0046 CARDS Action (page 9)
0047 The Cards action allows you to gain new Zoo cards (Animals, 
0048 Sponsor, and Conservation Project cards) from the deck or 
0049 the display.
0050 BUILD Action (page 10)
0051 Enclosures are needed to accommodate animals in your zoo. 
0052 The Build action allows you to build standard enclosures for 
0053 animals of all categories or special enclosures for particular 
0054 animal categories. You can build kiosks and pavilions as well. 
0055 ANIMALS Action (page 11-13)
0056 With the Animals action you can add animals to empty 
0057 enclosures. You need to fulfill special conditions for this and 
0058 pay the cost for the animal acquisition. In return, animals give 
0059 you special abilities and add icons to your zoo, which you 
0060 might need to support conservation projects.
0061 ASSOCIATION Action (page 14-16)
0062 With the Association action, you send your association 
0063 workers to work in zoological associations. With their help, 
0064 your reputation increases, you can gain universities and zoos 
0065 from other continents as partners, support conservation 
0066 projects, or donate to animal conservation.
0067 SPONSORS Action (page 17)
0068 The Sponsors action gains you the support of sponsors (by 
0069 playing them into your zoo). They either support you directly 
0070 or fund a project, such as a unique building. Many of them 
0071 gain you an ongoing effect or income. At the end of the 
0072 game, you can gain additional appeal or conservation points 
0073 from your sponsors.
0074 X-TOKEN Action (page 18)
0075 If you find yourself in a situation in which you can’t or don’t 
0076 want to perform any of the 5 actions, you may perform this 
0077 alternative action to gain exactly 1 X-token. You must still 
0078 select and move a card to slot 1, shifting all other cards to the 
0079 right like in other actions. The strength of the slot the card is 
0080 in does not contribute to this action.
0081 There are 4 ways to upgrade one of your Action cards 
0082 in the game: on the Conservation track, the Reputation 
0083 track, and when you gain your second partner zoo or your 
0084 second partner university. One of them allows you to choose 
0085 an alternative. So be aware that you cannot upgrade all of 
0086 your Action cards! In each game you will have to decide which 
0087 upgrades are most important to you.

0041 Finally, it’s time to tally Victory Points. Begin by looking 
0042 at the scoring area where your conservation counter is 
0043 located. Find the lowest appeal value in that scoring area. 
0044 This is your Target Number. Compare your Target Number

0046 Number from your appeal value and the result is your 
0047 Victory Points. The player with the highest positive 
0048 Victory Point total is the winner. In case of a tie, the tied 
0049 player who supported the most Conservation Projects 
0050 wins the game (you can determine this by counting how 
0051 many player tokens have been removed from left side 
0052 of your zoo map). If the score is still a tie, all tied players 
0053 share the victory.

0032 Gameplay / Breaks
0033 Gameplay / Breaks
0034 Every time you end a turn, move the topmost player token in 
0035 the left column of the solo tile onto the space to its right (in 
0036 the right column) (1). If you move the last token from the left 
0037 column into the right column (2) (and thus reveal the break 
0038 icon), you immediately resolve a break. 
0039 At the beginning of the break, place the topmost player token 
0040 onto the open donation space with the smallest number 
0041 on the Association board (3) (if all the other spaces, except 
0042 for space 12, are already occupied place it next to space 12, 
0043 even if this has no effect on the donations) and move the 
0044 remaining tokens back into the left column of the solo tile 
0045 (but now 1 more row is empty). 
0046 Then perform all steps of a break except for step 6. 
0047 The game ends after 6 rounds, when only 2 tokens are left on 
0048 the solo tile and you moved the bottom one to the right. Do 
0049 not trigger another break, but do the final scoring instead. 
0050 If you have at least 0 Victory Points after the final scoring 
0051 (meaning both your Appeal counter and Conservation 
0052 counter have reached the same scoring area or passed each 
0053 other), you have won the solo game.
0054 Difficulty Levels / Solo Challenge
0055 Difficulty Levels / Solo Challenge
0056 We recommend that beginners start the solo game with 
0057 an appeal of 20. Depending on your experience, you could 
0058 instead start the game with 10 or even 0 appeal. Starting the 
0059 solo game with 0 appeal is quite a challenge! Feel free to play 
0060 as many games as you like at each difficulty level.
0061 As a special challenge, play 3 games in a row. In this case, 
0062 do not shuffle the cards after each game. Instead, remove all 
0063 cards from that particular game (discard pile, display, all of 
0064 your cards, and all of the conservation projects) and set up

0074 negative) you have passed the challenge!
