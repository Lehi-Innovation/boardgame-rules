0251                             GAMEPLAYGAMEPLAY
0252 
0253     Ark Nova does not play in formal rounds; instead, players simply take turns (in clockwise order) taking one turn at a time until
0254     the end of the game is triggered. In addition, there are so-called breaks in between, when the game is put on hold for an
0255     administration phase. During these breaks you will discard cards down to your hand limit, recall workers, and gain income,
0256    among other things. The break procedure is explained in detail on page 18.
0257    You always perform one action during your turn, which may trigger additional effects and bonuses. You perform these actions
0258     with the 5 Action cards displayed below your zoo map. You can expand your zoo, play Zoo cards, and draw new Zoo cards. The
0259     position of the Action card determines how much you can do with that action. For more details on this mechanism, see the
0260     chapter A Turn / The 5 Action Cards on page 8.

1733 In the first 2 printings, we had different rules for Releasing an animal into the Wild (page 1 of the Glossary) and Moving an animal into
1734 a newly built special enclosure (page 12 of the Rulebook) when it came to flipping an occupied standard enclosure. That was not our
1735 intent. In both cases, the new rule is:
1736 You must flip the smallest possible occupied enclosure that meets all of the enclosure requirements of the animal (including water and
1737 rock spaces). In the rare case there is no such enclosure, flip the smallest occupied enclosure that meets at least the animal’s enclosure
1738 size requirement. If there is no occupied enclosure at all that meets the animal‘s enclosure size requirement, do not flip any enclosure.

1743 If I use the Boost animal ability to place an Action card from the center to position 1 or 5, how are the other Action cards moved?
1744 If you place the Action card in position 1, cards that were previously to the left of the Action card move 1 position to the right.
1745 If you place the Action card in position 5, cards that were previously to the right of the Action card move 1 position to the left.

1747 If we do not like the interactive abilities like Poison and Pilfering, do we have to sort out the cards?
1748 No, If your group doesn’t like the interactive effects, you can instead use the alternate effects intended for the solo game.

1750 When using Hypnosis, what happens if the Action card I choose has a token on it?
1751 Constriction and Venom tokens on the card affect you too and you discard them afterwards. But you cannot use a Multiplier token on the
1752 card.

1755 If there are several zoos with the most conservation points, you may choose the affected player. To have the most conservation points, a
1756 player has to have at least 1 conservation point.

1861 What is a border space?
1862 A border space is a space on your zoo plan that does not have another space on at least 1 of its 6 sides.

1864 When several players gain a card during the break, is the display refilled after each player?
1865 Yes, the display is filled after each player.

1868 No, you cannot gain more than 113 Appeal or 41 conversation points.

1870 When playing an Animal card, by how much does a partner zoo reduce the cost? What counts as a continent icon for that
1871 purpose?
1872 You reduce the cost by 3 money per continent icon on that Animal card if you have the matching partner zoo. So normally by 3, but by
1873 6 in rare cases where there are 2 of the same continent icon on the card. The conditions on the left side of the card never count as icons,
1874 neither for this purpose nor another.

1876 Is it possible to place and use 2 x2 tokens on the same card?
1877 Yes, you can have multiple x2 tokens on the same card. And yes, you can use multiple x2 tokens and each one will give you 1 extra action.
1878 So with 2 x2 tokens, you will get up to 3 actions. Though that should be a very rare case.

1881 Yes, you can use a x2 token on any Action card to take 2 X-tokens. What you cannot do is take 1 X-token and 1 “regular” action.

1884 twice.” Is a x2 Token allowed on the Animals card?
1885 Animals was omitted because there are animals that have the Multiplier ability for all of the 4 mentioned actions, but not for the Animals
1886 action. But if you get a Multiplier token in another way, like the conservation point bonus, you can place it on any Action card, including
1887 the Animals Action card.

1891 No. “Take an X-token” is a separate 6th action. If you gain a specific action (“Action: Sponsor”), you cannot substitute the take 1 X-token
1892 action instead. You may take an X-token from a Determination ability which grants Any Action.
