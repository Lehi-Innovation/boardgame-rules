0002 ARK NOVA ERRATA VERSIONS 1.0 AND 1.1
0003 In the first 2 printings, we had different rules for Releasing an animal into the Wild (page 1 of the Glossary) and Moving an animal into 
0004 a newly built special enclosure (page 12 of the Rulebook) when it came to flipping an occupied standard enclosure. That was not our 
0005 intent. In both cases, the new rule is:
0006 You must flip the smallest possible occupied enclosure that meets all of the enclosure requirements of the animal (including water and 
0007 rock spaces). In the rare case there is no such enclosure, flip the smallest occupied enclosure that meets at least the animal’s enclosure 
0008 size requirement. If there is no occupied enclosure at all that meets the animal‘s enclosure size requirement, do not flip any enclosure.

0013 If I use the Boost animal ability to place an Action card from the center to position 1 or 5, how are the other Action cards moved?
0014 If you place the Action card in position 1, cards that were previously to the left of the Action card move 1 position to the right.  
0015 If you place the Action card in position 5, cards that were previously to the right of the Action card move 1 position to the left.
0016 If we do not like the interactive abilities like Poison and Pilfering, do we have to sort out the cards?
0017 No, If your group doesn’t like the interactive effects, you can instead use the alternate effects intended for the solo game.
0018 When using Hypnosis, what happens if the Action card I choose has a token on it?
0019 Constriction and Venom tokens on the card affect you too and you discard them afterwards. But you cannot use a Multiplier token on the 
0020 card.
0021 When my Japanese Macaque pilfers from the player with the most conservation points, how do I dertermine the affected player?
0022 If there are several zoos with the most conservation points, you may choose the affected player. To have the most conservation points, a 
0023 player has to have at least 1 conservation point.

0033 If I upgrade an Action card during the course of the action, may I use the upgraded side immediately,  
0034 e.g. make an additional donation in the Association action?
0035 No, the upgraded action is only available to you from your next action on. But for fulfilling conditions, the Action card immediately 
0036 counts as upgraded.
0037 If I get a bonus during the course of the action, can I use that bonus already in that same action? E.g. when starting an upgraded 
0038 Association action of strength 5 with only 1 active association worker, can I first gain 2 reputation to gain an additional 
0039 association worker and then use that worker to gain a partner zoo?
0040 Yes, you can. You do the tasks one after the other, and only when doing the second task do you need to have the second worker. Only the 
0041 strength and kind of an action is locked in at the beginning.

0002 What is a border space?
0003 A border space is a space on your zoo plan that does not have another space on at least 1 of its 6 sides.
0004 When several players gain a card during the break, is the display refilled after each player?
0005 Yes, the display is filled after each player.
0006 What happens if my Conservation counter or Appeal counter hits the end of its track? Can I still get more points?
0007 No, you cannot gain more than 113 Appeal or 41 conversation points.
0008 When playing an Animal card, by how much does a partner zoo reduce the cost? What counts as a continent icon for that 
0009 purpose?
0010 You reduce the cost by 3 money per continent icon on that Animal card if you have the matching partner zoo. So normally by 3, but by 
0011 6 in rare cases where there are 2 of the same continent icon on the card. The conditions on the left side of the card never count as icons, 
0012 neither for this purpose nor another.
0013 Is it possible to place and use 2 x2 tokens on the same card?
0014 Yes, you can have multiple x2 tokens on the same card. And yes, you can use multiple x2 tokens and each one will give you 1 extra action. 
0015 So with 2 x2 tokens, you will get up to 3 actions. Though that should be a very rare case.
0016 Can I take 2 X-tokens from a card with a x2 token?
0017 Yes, you can use a x2 token on any Action card to take 2 X-tokens. What you cannot do is take 1 X-token and 1 “regular” action.
0018 The glossary states: “Multiplier [Association, Build, Cards, Sponsors]: The next time you execute the action, you may execute it 
0019 twice.” Is a x2 Token allowed on the Animals card?
0020 Animals was omitted because there are animals that have the Multiplier ability for all of the 4 mentioned actions, but not for the Animals 
0021 action. But if you get a Multiplier token in another way, like the conservation point bonus, you can place it on any Action card, including 
0022 the Animals Action card.
0023 For a card like Pygmy Hippopotamus (“Action: Sponsor”) or Sun Bear (“Action: Association”), are you are allowed to move the 
0024 action card but gain an X-token instead?
0025 No. “Take an X-token” is a separate 6th action. If you gain a specific action (“Action: Sponsor”), you cannot substitute the take 1 X-token 
0026 action instead. You may take an X-token from a Determination ability which grants Any Action.
