0010 The following applies to all effects, whether on Animal or 
0011 Sponsor cards:
0012 If the effect refers to an icon and this icon appears on the card 
0013 itself, this icon on the card already counts for the effect. This 
0014 concerns one-off effects as well as the triggering of regular effects.

0025 What counts as an Icon
0026 What counts as an Icon
0027 The icons to which card effects refer are located in the 
0028 upper-right corners of the cards and on the partner zoos and 
0029 universities — each of which must be part of your zoo to count. 
0030 These are usually continents, animal categories, and research. 
0031 Water and rock requirements found in the upper-left corners of 
0032 Animal and unique building cards also count as icons.
0033 Example: The Penguin Pool card has 1 bird icon and 1 water icon.
0034 The conditions on the left edge of the card do not count. The 
0035 icons on the conservation projects do not count either, because 
0036 these cards are not considered in a zoo.

0038 Card text overrules rules

0040 applies.
0041 Order 
0042 Order 
0043 Bonuses and effects are always executed immediately. The only 
0044 exceptions are effects that are executed “after finishing”. Execute 
0045 these effects only after you have finished your action and moved 
0046 the Action card to slot 1.
0047 If multiple bonuses and/or effects occur at the same time, you 
0048 may execute them in any order you choose.
0049 Example: You have 4 reputation and play the Sponsor card Zoo School. 
0050 It gains you 1 reputation, 1 conservation point, and allows you to place 
0051 a unique building and take 1 card in reputation range from the display 
0052 (or draw 1 card from the deck). You may take 1 reputation first. Then the 
0053 conservation point, which is your fifth, gaining you 2 more reputation 
0054 through the bonus tile. Then place the unique building, which gains 
0055 you 1 more reputation as a placement bonus. And finally, with the now 
0056 much larger reputation range, take 1 card from the display.
0057 Double Icons
0058 Double Icons
0059 Some cards show the same icon twice. All effects that are 
0060 triggered by this double icon or refer to it are then also triggered 
0061 twice. 
0062 If an animal has 2 identical continent icons and you have the 
0063 partner zoo, its cost is reduced, correspondingly, by 6 money.
0064 If a unique building or Animal card requires two identical 
0065 requirements (e. g. 2 water spaces), the spaces of the building 
0066 must be adjacent to at least 2 such spaces. If 1 single space of the 
0067 building is adjacent to 2 such spaces, that still counts; however, 
0068 if several spaces of the building are adjacent to the same space, 
0069 but no others (i. e. only 1 total), this doesn’t count.

0002 Term Definitions
0003 Term Definitions
0004 Connected: A space is connected if it is adjacent to at least 
0005 1 covered space, but is not itself covered.
0006 Isolated: A space is isolated if it is not adjacent to a covered 
0007 space.
0008 Your Zoo: Your zoo includes your entire card display and 
0009 everything that is placed on your zoo map or is otherwise in your 
0010 personal supply.
0011 Any Zoo: The combined total of all displays of all players’ cards, 
0012 as well as their zoo maps and personal supplies.
0013 Border space: A border space is a space on your zoo plan that 
0014 does not have another space on at least 1 of its 6 sides.
0015 Unique buildings (cards 243 to 257): You must build the 
0016 unique building into your zoo. If you cannot do so, you cannot 
0017 play the card.
0018 Always place a unique building with the colored side face up. 
0019 This side shows a unique icon that you will also find on the 
0020 Sponsor card. Find the tile that matches your Sponsor card and 
0021 place it on your zoo map according to the usual rules. You may 
0022 rotate it in any way you wish, but you cannot flip it over. You 
0023 do not have to pay money to build a unique building. Unique 
0024 buildings are not enclosures for gameplay, meaning you cannot 
0025 place (additional) animals in them. Unique buildings never count 
0026 as water or rock spaces.
