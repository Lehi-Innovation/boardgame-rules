# Nemesis — benchmark-v12 candidate

Original Nemesis basic-board standard semi-cooperative core, six original characters. Original English 28-page manual (including illustrations), official FAQ v2.2 base-game Rules/Actions/Rooms/Items, compatible supplemental wording in original publisher-hosted localizations. Optional modes, boards, campaigns and expansions excluded.

**Q01.** In a four-player standard game, how many Escape Pods are selected, how are they distributed and oriented, and which tokens start in the Intruder bag?

**Q02.** How are Objectives filtered and dealt, when does each player choose one, what happens to the rejected card, and do Eggs trigger that choice?

**Q03.** You use an Action card with printed cost 2 while holding that card, two other Action cards and two Contamination cards. What is discarded, and can Contamination substitute for payment? What if the card offers OR effects?

**Q04.** A Character in a burning Room takes one Action then passes. When do they take Fire damage, can they act again this Player Phase, can they discard cards, and do later players rounds burn them again?

**Q05.** Describe entering an empty unexplored Room: tile, Item Counter, Exploration effect, Noise roll and token destination. How do Silence, Danger and an already occupied destination alter the Noise roll?

**Q06.** What does Careful Movement cost and replace, when is it forbidden, and can a Closed Door be crossed or a miniature stop in a Corridor?

**Q07.** You roll a number for a Corridor already holding Noise, with a Technical Corridors Entrance in your Room. Give the Encounter sequence, Noise removal, token destination and Surprise Attack comparison; do Contamination cards count?

**Q08.** How does Danger resolve with several eligible neighboring Intruders, and when none are eligible? Does moving an Intruder into your Room count as an Encounter? How does Slime affect Silence?

**Q09.** During an Encounter you draw Blank. What happens to Noise and the Blank; when is an Adult added? Contrast Blank in bag development.

**Q10.** Two Intruders attempt to move together from the same Room through one Closed Door. Where do they end, what happens to the Door, can it later close, and would a Noise-triggered appearance be blocked by that Door?

**Q11.** An injured Intruder moves into Technical Corridors with Noise already there. What happens to its miniature, injuries, token and Noise? Can Characters or Doors normally use those Corridors?

**Q12.** Describe an ordinary Shoot versus Melee Action: card/ammo costs, target location, Contamination, a miss, and the two-hit die result.

**Q13.** After damage, how are Larva, Adult and Queen death checked, when is a Carcass placed, and what if a Queen check draws a Retreat arrow?
