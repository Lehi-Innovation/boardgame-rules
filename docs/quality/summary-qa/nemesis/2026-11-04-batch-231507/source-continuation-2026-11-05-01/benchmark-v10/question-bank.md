# Nemesis benchmark-v10 — candidate119-question bank

Not finally frozen until independent closure. Q01–Q108 wording retained; Q109 retired; Q110–Q120 are eleven independently proposed additions. Q45/Q105 source-key clarifications are explicit.

## Q01

In a four-player standard game, how many Escape Pods are selected, how are they distributed and oriented, and which tokens start in the Intruder bag?

Expected: Three random pods; lowest numbered in A, next B, next A, all Locked. Bag: 1 Blank, 4 Larvae, 1 Creeper, 1 Queen, 7 Adults.

## Q02

How are Objectives filtered and dealt, when does each player choose one, what happens to the rejected card, and do Eggs trigger that choice?

Expected: Remove Objectives requiring more players than present; deal one Personal and one Corporate secretly. First Intruder miniature of any type appearing triggers immediate choice by everyone before resolving its Encounter. Remove rejected card face down unseen. Eggs do not trigger it.

## Q03

You use an Action card with printed cost 2 while holding that card, two other Action cards and two Contamination cards. What is discarded, and can Contamination substitute for payment? What if the card offers OR effects?

Expected: Discard the played card and two additional Action cards face up. Contamination cannot pay costs. Choose one OR effect.

## Q04

A Character in a burning Room takes one Action then passes. When do they take Fire damage, can they act again this Player Phase, can they discard cards, and do later players rounds burn them again?

Expected: At end of own round take 1 Light Wound. Passing bars normal Actions this Player Phase, but official FAQ permits Interruption after passing. May discard any number of hand cards on passing. No further Fire wounds from later players rounds this turn.

## Q05

Describe entering an empty unexplored Room: tile, Item Counter, Exploration effect, Noise roll and token destination. How do Silence, Danger and an already occupied destination alter the Noise roll?

Expected: Reveal Room and Exploration token; set counter to token number except Nest/Slime Room; resolve effect and remove Exploration token from game. Roll Noise in empty Room unless Silence or Danger exploration cancels it. Occupied Room (Character or Intruder) gives no movement Noise roll.

## Q06

What does Careful Movement cost and replace, when is it forbidden, and can a Closed Door be crossed or a miniature stop in a Corridor?

Expected: Cost 2 Action cards; replaces post-movement Noise roll with placing Noise in a chosen connected Corridor. Forbidden in Combat or when all destination Corridors have Noise. Closed Doors block player movement; no miniature stops in Corridors.

## Q07

You roll a number for a Corridor already holding Noise, with a Technical Corridors Entrance in your Room. Give the Encounter sequence, Noise removal, token destination and Surprise Attack comparison; do Contamination cards count?

Expected: Resolve Encounter: remove all connected Noise including Technical Corridors; draw bag token; place matching Intruder; compare token number to all cards in triggering hand including Contamination. Fewer cards causes Surprise Attack against triggerer, equal or more does not. Set token aside.

## Q08

How does Danger resolve with several eligible neighboring Intruders, and when none are eligible? Does moving an Intruder into your Room count as an Encounter? How does Slime affect Silence?

Expected: Move all neighboring Intruders not in Combat into current Room. If none eligible, put Noise in each connected Corridor lacking it including Technical Corridors. Moving Intruder into occupied Room is Combat, not Encounter. Slime converts Silence Noise/exploration results to Danger.

## Q09

During an Encounter you draw Blank. What happens to Noise and the Blank; when is an Adult added? Contrast Blank in bag development.

Expected: Encounter replaces connected Noise in every Corridor; return Blank; add Adult only if Blank was last token in bag and a spare Adult is available; no miniature or Surprise Attack. Development always attempts adding one available Adult then returns Blank.

## Q10

Two Intruders attempt to move together from the same Room through one Closed Door. Where do they end, what happens to the Door, can it later close, and would a Noise-triggered appearance be blocked by that Door?

Expected: Both stay in original Room and destroy Door simultaneously. Destroyed Door permits movement but cannot close except Plasma Torch. Noise-triggered appearance ignores Closed Door.

## Q11

An injured Intruder moves into Technical Corridors with Noise already there. What happens to its miniature, injuries, token and Noise? Can Characters or Doors normally use those Corridors?

Expected: Remove miniature, discard injuries, return matching token to bag; keep Noise. Characters barred except Mechanic Technical Corridors card and Technical Corridor Plans Item; Doors never placed there.

## Q12

Describe an ordinary Shoot versus Melee Action: card/ammo costs, target location, Contamination, a miss, and the two-hit die result.

Expected: Both cost 1 Action card against target in same Room. Shoot needs Weapon in Hand and spends 1 Ammo; normal two-hit deals 2 injuries subject to Weapon. Melee draws Contamination to Action discard before choosing target, miss causes Serious Wound, two-hit deals only 1 injury.

## Q13

After damage, how are Larva, Adult and Queen death checked, when is a Carcass placed, and what if a Queen check draws a Retreat arrow?

Expected: Larva dies at 1 injury without Carcass. Adult draws one Attack card blood, dies if blood <= current injuries and leaves Carcass. Queen draws two, sums blood and compares same way; any Retreat arrow causes retreat via Event-card corridor number, discard Event, rather than normal numerical resolution.

## Q14

A Character Escapes a Room with two Intruders toward an empty unexplored Room. When do attacks occur, who is targeted, what happens if they die, and what follows if they survive?

Expected: Pay Movement cost 1, choose neighboring Room. Before moving resolve separate Attack from each Intruder targeting escaper. Death leaves Corpse in starting Room; survival completes move and standard exploration/Noise.

## Q15

In an ordinary Intruder Attack with several Characters, how is the target chosen and ties broken; how is a hit determined; what changes for Larva and where does its Contamination go?

Expected: Fewest Action cards in hand; tie First Player or next in turn order. Matching attacker symbol on drawn Attack card means resolve effect; otherwise miss; ignore blood and discard card. Larva draws no Attack card: place miniature on target Character board and give Contamination on top of Action discard.

## Q16

At three Serious Wounds, does dressing one prevent death from a Light Wound? What happens at a third Light Wound otherwise, what does Heal remove, and where do a dead Characters Corpse and Heavy Objects go?

Expected: No: dressed still counts; any further Wound kills at three Serious. Third Light clears Light track and causes Serious Wound. Heal removes specified Light Wound or discards dressed Serious card. Place Corpse and drop Heavy Objects in death Room.

## Q17

Contrast Canteen/Shower scanning with Surgery: which cards are scanned and removed, how is a Larva handled, and what costs or turn consequences follow Surgery?

Expected: Canteen/Shower optionally scan hand Contamination, remove non-Infected; infected gives Larva and retains card, existing Larva means death/Creeper. Canteen heals 1 Light; Shower removes Slime. Surgery scans deck/hand/discard, removes infected and Larva, then 1 Light Wound, automatic pass, shuffle all cards including hand into deck; empty hand until next turn.

## Q18

How can uncarried Eggs be destroyed with Shoot/Melee, grenades, Molotov and Fire; which Melee penalties are waived, when is Noise rolled and when is the Nest destroyed?

Expected: Each Injury from Shoot/Melee destroys 1 uncarried Egg. Successful Egg Melee retains the ordinary draw of 1 Contamination card onto the Action discard pile. On a missed Egg Melee, no Contamination card and no Wound are suffered. The sources do not specify how the normal pre-roll draw and miss waiver are reconciled; no refund or delayed draw is invented. Grenade destroys 2 Eggs, Molotov 1; Noise after every destruction attempt including miss. Fire destroys 1 uncarried Egg each Fire Damage step. Nest destroyed once all its Eggs taken/destroyed; mark Injury in Nest.

## Q19

What permits or prevents entering an Escape Pod, what must you resolve first, how many Characters and Heavy Objects fit, and what are the options and consequences when you wait?

Expected: Use corresponding Evacuation Room Action cost 2, unlocked Pod with free space, no Intruder/Malfunction. Roll Noise first and fail if Intruder reaches Room. Two Character spaces; Heavy Objects use none. Enter then launch immediately or wait and pass current turn. At start of first round next Player Phase choose wait/pass, launch, or free exit followed by a full two-Action round. Intruder appearing in Section returns all Pod occupants; another boarder may immediately launch.

## Q20

Give the endgame checks in order. What happens with two Damaged Engines, a non-Earth destination (including Quarantine/Mars), and an infected survivor or one carrying a Larva? Do pod passengers need Contamination checks?

Expected: Engines then Coordinates then Contamination then Objectives. If ship survives to checks: two/three Damaged explodes kills all aboard incl Intruders; non-Earth kills sleepers but ship/Intruders survive, Quarantine sleeper survives Mars. All survivors including launched pod passengers scan all Contamination; any infected triggers shuffle all Action/Contamination draw 4, any Contamination kills else survives. Larva skips scanning and directly does shuffle/draw. Survivors then reveal/check chosen Objective.

## Q21

How are the basic and additional Room tiles and Exploration tokens placed at setup; what Coordinates, Destination, Time, Engines, Eggs and Weaknesses start on the board?

Expected: All 11 basic tiles face down in 1 slots; five random additional tiles face down in 2 slots, leftovers boxed unseen; random face-down Exploration per tile. Random face-down Coordinates, Destination B, Time green. Each numbered Engine has shuffled Damaged/Working pair face down, top true status. Five Eggs, three random face-down Weaknesses.

## Q22

How are Player Numbers assigned and Characters drafted; what does each Character receive and where do Characters and the initial Corpse begin? Are Quest Items usable immediately?

Expected: Random Help cards 1 through player count with matching Inventories assign Player Numbers; in number order draw two draft cards, reveal, choose one, shuffle other back. Receive board, ringed miniature in Hibernatorium, shuffled 10 Action cards, starting Weapon in Hand fully loaded, two horizontal inactive Quest Items; activate by printed quest then ordinary Items. Player 1 First Player; blue Corpse in Hibernatorium is Character Corpse Object.

## Q23

When are Action hands refilled and the First Player token passed; what happens when the Action deck empties, and what are the normal discard destinations and visibility?

Expected: At Player Phase start draw to 5, shuffle Action discard whenever required draw finds empty deck; then pass token left except first turn. Actions/Contamination to own Action discard, Items/Event/Attack/Serious Wounds to respective discard piles face up; markers to pools. Search example has explicit face-down return exception.

## Q24

Give the Event Phase order, which Intruders move on an Event card, what controls movement and when the event effect resolves; what happens when Event and Intruder Attack decks run out?

Expected: Time and active Self-Destruct advance, Intruders in Combat attack, Fire Damage, Event card movement then effect, bag development fully resolved before next turn. Move symbol-matching Intruders without Characters in Room via card-number Corridor; ducts remove miniature/return token. Discard Event unless instructed otherwise. Empty Event/Attack decks reshuffle their respective discard piles.

## Q25

In bag development, describe Larva, Creeper, Adult, Breeder and both Queen branches. Which tokens are removed or returned, who rolls Noise and when does the next turn begin?

Expected: Larva removed to token pool, add Adult; Creeper removed to pool, add Breeder. Adult/Breeder cause ordered Noise rolls excluding Combat; token returns. For Adult specifically, return token immediately BEFORE Noise so same token may be redrawn in an Encounter. Queen: Characters in Nest means Queen there and Encounter; otherwise including undiscovered Nest add Egg; return Queen. Fully resolve triggered effects before new turn.

## Q26

When do Fire and Malfunction exhaustion end the game; what if the target Room already has that marker, and where do removed markers go? What happens to sleepers and Intruders when hull integrity is lost?

Expected: Eight of each; required new placement with no pool marker explodes/loses hull integrity, all aboard including sleepers/Intruders die. Existing same marker means nothing happens; max one per Room. Removed markers return to pools.

## Q27

What does a Malfunction disable and leave usable, where can it never be placed, and does it change an Engine token or harm an already-hibernating Character?

Expected: Disables Room Action and Computer; Search still works. Never in Nest or Room Covered In Slime. Special Rooms can malfunction; Engine Working/Damaged unaffected and already-hibernating Characters unaffected. Repair cards/Tools can remove marker.

## Q28

Which Items may be hidden, what do other players see, what is the Inventory limit, and what must happen on use? How are Heavy Items/Objects, added Items and Duct Tape handled?

Expected: Normal Items hidden in unlimited Inventory, others see backs/colors; used Item shown. Heavy Items/Objects public in two Hands, third requires dropping one. Added Items act as one with base card. Duct Tape combines two Heavy Items into one Hand, never Objects.

## Q29

What happens to an Item versus an Object voluntarily dropped, when and at what Action cost may you drop, and how do found Weapons differ from starting Weapons in Ammo?

Expected: Drop freely during own turn no Action; Objects stay in Room, Items discarded/lost. Found Weapon has 1 Ammo versus starting fully loaded, neither exceeds capacity.

## Q30

How does Search choose its deck, draw and keep cards, return the rejected card, change the Item Counter, and handle zero Items, Special Rooms and Blue Items?

Expected: Search Action: choose one matching Red/Yellow/Green deck, white may choose any ONE, draw two, keep one, put rejected card at BOTTOM of same deck, not shuffle. Lower counter one, zero prevents Search except e.g. Scavenging; Special Rooms cannot Search. Blue Items only Craft.

## Q31

How does Craft pay its Action and components, must the result be available, can you inspect the deck, and what Crafted Items exist?

Expected: Cost one Action card plus discard two Items with blue component icons matching two grey result icons, choose/take available card not random draw; unavailable cannot build. May inspect deck any time. Antidote, Taser, Flamethrower, Molotov Cocktail.

## Q32

Who pays for a Trade and who may join; can you give something for nothing, exchange Ammo, or need a separate Pick Up Action for a searched Heavy Item?

Expected: Initiator pays one Action card; Characters in same Room may agree to exchange Items/Objects including gifts. Cannot exchange Ammo. Pick Up Heavy Object cost one applies Corpses/Carcasses/Eggs, not searched Heavy Items.

## Q33

How do you reveal Weaknesses: what Object corresponds to each card, where must it be, is it consumed, who benefits and must you personally research for a Discover Weakness Objective?

Expected: Character Corpse, Intruder Egg, Intruder Carcass correspond to three face-down slots. Research matching Object present in Laboratory, carried or uncarried, reveal and leave card on board, Object not consumed. Discovered rules benefit all; anyone can do research for Objective.

## Q34

What are the common prerequisites and cost for Room Actions, what does a Computer icon itself do, and how does Fire affect Room Actions or Searching?

Expected: Cost two Action cards, Character in matching Room unless explicit special ability, no Combat or Malfunction. Computer icon only significant when another rule references it, unavailable under Malfunction. Fire alone bars neither Room Action nor Search.

## Q35

What exactly does the Armory recharge, by how much, and what limits apply?

Expected: Add two Ammo to one Energy Weapon, never Classic Weapons, never above printed capacity.

## Q36

What are the three mutually exclusive Emergency Room treatments; does dressing remove the Wound or merely its effect, and do identical Serious Wound effects stack?

Expected: Dress all Serious OR heal one dressed Serious OR heal all Light. Dress flips/ignores effect but retains Serious count; identical Serious effects do not stack.

## Q37

What does sending a Signal actually do and what is its game use; can Comms be absent from a normal game?

Expected: Comms Room Action puts Status marker on own Signal space, used for Objectives only. Basic Room so always aboard.

## Q38

How does Fire Control affect a chosen Room and its Intruders if the Room has Fire, or has none?

Expected: Choose any one Room, remove Fire if present; all its Intruders flee random directions, one Event card drawn per Intruder. Works with no Fire.

## Q39

Describe starting, stopping and restarting Self-Destruct, advancing it, the yellow threshold, its final space, a jump while active and a Character already hibernating.

Expected: Start marker first green; advance with Time. Stop removes it, restart green. First yellow irreversible/unlocks all Pods, which can relock; subsequent yellow spaces do NOT re-unlock them. Skull or jump with active sequence destroys ship. Characters cannot initiate sequence with anyone hibernating, but Coolant Leak Event can start it despite sleepers.

## Q40

Which Room can Airlock Control target, what Door condition is required, what happens immediately, and what cancels or completes the procedure and when?

Expected: Any other Yellow Room with no Destroyed Doors in connected Corridors; close every connected Door, mark procedure. Opening any before Player Phase end removes token/cancels. All closed after all players pass kills all Characters/Intruders immediately, remove token and Fire.

## Q41

When does Cabins increase a hand and to what size; what disables it and is it a paid Room Action?

Expected: Passive at start of new turn draws to six instead of five if Character there and no Intruders; Malfunction disables. No paid Action.

## Q42

Can Command Center select a distant Room and individually open some Doors and close others, including a Destroyed Door?

Expected: Choose any one Room and individually open/close any connected Doors, need not all same. Destroyed Door cannot close except Plasma Torch.

## Q43

What can Engine Control Room check or change and how do Malfunctions in an Engine Room affect that check?

Expected: Check status of all three Engines even if corresponding Engine Room malfunctions; cannot change status from Control Room.

## Q44

What does Hatch Control System change and how does Monitoring Room inspect a location: explored or unexplored, what components, secrecy and replacement?

Expected: Hatch flips one Pod Locked/Unlocked. Monitoring secretly looks at one unexplored Room tile and its Exploration token, replaces both, may lie.

## Q45

Does entering Room Covered With Slime require an Action to acquire Slime, can it have an Item Counter or Malfunction, can Slime stack, and does showering extinguish Fire?

Expected: Entry automatically gives Slime; no paid Action. No Item Counter, never Malfunction. Max one Slime, extra does nothing; Clothes/Shower remove. Shower does not remove Fire and Fire allowed there. On first entering the Room Covered With Slime, acquire Slime before resolving its Exploration token. Thus a Silence Exploration effect becomes Danger on that first entry, subject to the already-covered Careful Movement/Reconnaissance cancellation.

## Q46

In Cockpit what can you learn or change, who sees the information, what does Destination refer to and which restrictions apply after someone hibernates or an Intruder is present?

Expected: Check Coordinates secretly and replace, may lie and should remember; Set Destination moves marker to chosen track space referring to one of four card destinations. No Set with Intruder in Cockpit; no change after any hibernation; common Room prerequisites apply.

## Q47

How does Engine checking differ from repairing/breaking; what can stay secret, what must be announced and do you need a prior check or functioning Engine Room?

Expected: Check secretly top token only may lie. Repairs Action or Tools permits take both tokens secretly restack any order. Need announce whether order changed, not whether repaired. Works without prior check and despite Malfunction. Top true status, two Working needed.

## Q48

When is hibernation legal, what roll and failure condition apply, what leaves play on success, and which later ship actions are forbidden? Does later Malfunction wake sleepers?

Expected: Only Time on blue, functioning Hibernatorium without Intruder; Room Action cost two, Noise roll, appearing Intruder fails. Success remove Character miniature and participation until end checks. Destination change and starting Self-Destruct prohibited. Later Malfunction has no effect on sleepers.

## Q49

What ends the game when Time runs out or the last active Character leaves/dies/hibernates; how is an active Self-Destruct resolved and do Intruders die from a normal jump?

Expected: Time final red immediate jump, awake aboard die; Intruders unharmed. Last alive non-hibernating aboard leaves/dies/hibernates: advance active Self-Destruct to final red and destroy ship, else advance Time final red and jump. Then survival/victory checks if survivors.

## Q50

Can a Character directly attack another, and may an Action still harm other crew indirectly? Give source-supported examples.

Expected: Never directly attack Characters; indirect harm permitted, e.g. grenade at Intruder with crew present, trap in burning Room, Self-Destruct.

## Q51

You must place an Adult but all eight models are on board. What explicit procedure applies, and what if an Intruder enters an unexplored Room by movement?

Expected: Remove ALL Adults not in Combat and return corresponding tokens to bag if possible, then place Adult in Encounter Room. Moving Intruder into unexplored Room does not reveal Room/token and is not removed. If all eight are in Combat, the source supplies no executable resolution for freeing a model; do not invent one.

## Q52

For shooting, describe the target-size restrictions of the small curled and larger curved Intruder die symbols; what happens on those same symbols in Melee against an ineligible target?

Expected: Small curled symbol hits Larva/Creeper for one only; larger curved hits Larva/Creeper/Adult for one only; other targets missed. Same target restrictions in Melee; miss causes Serious Wound.

## Q53

At the final Contamination check, a survivor has no Larva and every Contamination card scans non-Infected. Must they perform the four-card draw or remove those cards, and does an earlier launched Pod escape the Objective check?

Expected: No four-card draw unless infected found or Larva on board; end scan does not instruct removing non-infected. Pod passenger still needs surviving Contamination check and chosen Objective fulfillment.

## Q54

Which two component symbols are required for each Crafted Item: Antidote, Taser, Flamethrower and Molotov Cocktail? Describe the symbols rather than guessing Item-card names.

Expected: Antidote: flame and medical cross. Taser: battery/lightning and screwdriver/tool. Flamethrower: flame and screwdriver/tool. Molotov Cocktail: flame and folded cloth/fabric. Each takes two matching blue-component Item cards.

## Q55

What defines being in Combat, how do In Combat Only and Out of Combat Only restrictions work, and may an unmarked Action or Item be used in either situation? Do Eggs cause Combat?

Expected: Sharing Room with Intruder defines Combat, not Eggs. In Combat Only requires it, Out of Combat Only requires none. Unmarked card/Item actions can be used in either subject to requirements; Basic Actions in Combat are limited to Shoot, Melee and Basic Movement as Escape. No Room Actions or Quest activation in Combat.

## Q56

For an Objective that Player X cannot survive, must you personally kill them, does hibernating or escaping count as death, and how does only survivor differ? What must your own Character do regardless?

Expected: X must be dead at game end; need not be killed by own Actions; hibernating or escaping alive is not death. Only survivor means nobody else survives. Own Character must survive by launched Pod or successful hibernation, plus meet Objective.

## Q57

If a Door token must be placed and none are in the pool, what happens? How are Open, Closed and Destroyed Doors represented, and may two Door tokens share one Corridor or be placed in Technical Corridors?

Expected: Take any Door token elsewhere on board and move to required Corridor. Open no token, Closed standing, Destroyed lying. Max one per Corridor; none in Technical Corridors.

## Q58

The first Character dies while the ship's Pods are Locked. What happens to every Pod immediately, and can they subsequently be Locked again?

Expected: All Escape Pods immediately become Unlocked. They may subsequently be Locked and Unlocked normally, for example through Hatch Control System.

## Q59

What does the Storage Room's Search for an Item Room Action let you draw and keep, where exactly does the rejected card go, and what are the common Room Action cost and restrictions?

Expected: Pay two Action cards while in the functioning Storage Room and out of Combat. Choose the Red, Yellow or Green Item deck, draw two cards, keep one, and put the other at the bottom of that deck.

## Q60

While in the Nest, how do you take an Egg still represented on the Intruder board, what does that Action cost, and what happens immediately afterward? How does this differ from picking up a previously dropped Egg token in your Room?

Expected: Use the Nest's Take one Egg Room Action, costing two Action cards and subject to common Room Action restrictions. Take one Egg token from the Intruder board, then perform a Noise roll. The Egg is a Heavy Object and occupies a Hand slot. Picking up an already dropped Egg is the ordinary one-card Pick Up Heavy Object Basic Action; that pickup procedure does not itself instruct a Noise roll.

## Q61

You perform the Action printed on a One Use Only Item. Besides paying its Action-card cost and showing it, what happens to the Item card after use?

Expected: Discard the Item card to the appropriate Item discard pile after using it. This is in addition to discarding the Action cards needed to pay its Action cost.

## Q62

For a rule concerned with the Character's arrival on Earth, where is a Character in a launched Escape Pod treated as having arrived? Does this establish that the ship itself reached Earth?

Expected: Any Character in a launched Escape Pod is always treated as having arrived on Earth. This is the Character's status and does not establish that the ship reached Earth; the ship's destination and survival are checked separately. The passenger still undergoes the normal endgame Contamination and Objective checks.

## Q63

While waiting in an Escape Pod, must you roll Noise when Adult bag development calls for it?

Expected: Yes. Waiting Character is treated as present in the corresponding Room and rolls Noise.

## Q64

A Malfunction is placed in the Evacuation Section while you are already waiting in its Pod. Can you launch, wait or exit, and what does that Malfunction prevent?

Expected: May still launch, wait or exit; Malfunction only prevents using Room Action to ENTER Pods.

## Q65

When Escaping from several differently sized Intruders, how many attacks and in what order?

Expected: One Attack per Intruder, starting with larger ones: Queen, Breeder, Adult, Larva (decreasing size).

## Q66

An Event instructs removing itself from the game but its other effect cannot be resolved. Is it removed?

Expected: Yes, remove it even if its other content cannot resolve.

## Q67

When Adult bag development triggers Noise, when does the drawn Adult token return, and may a triggered Encounter draw that very token?

Expected: Return immediately BEFORE Noise rolls; yes it may be drawn again during an Encounter.

## Q68

A retreating Intruder meets a Closed Door. What happens normally, and what changes under The Way of Moving Weakness for an affected non-Breeder/non-Queen?

Expected: Normally destroys Door and stays in Room; with that Weakness for an affected Intruder other than Breeder/Queen nothing happens at all.

## Q69

Does the Hand Serious Wound increase the cost of Basic Shoot, and why?

Expected: No. Shoot is a Basic Action, not an Item Action.

## Q70

With Vulnerability to Fire discovered, how much extra Injury applies per damage instance, and how many Injuries for a Flamethrower double-crosshair hit?

Expected: One extra per damage instance, x+1; this hit causes 3 Injuries, not one bonus per separate Injury.

## Q71

A Larva attacks a Character who already has a Larva on their board. What happens to each Larva and to the Character?

Expected: Attacking Larva disappears; existing board Larva stays; Character gets one Contamination card, no automatic death from this attack alone.

## Q72

After a Character hibernates, Fire appears in the Hibernatorium. Does the sleeper take Fire wounds and what can still kill them?

Expected: No Fire wounds; treated out of play. Ship destruction can still kill sleeper; endgame survival checks remain applicable.

## Q73

Can the Coolant Leak Event start Self-Destruct while someone hibernates, and how does that differ from starting it as a Character?

Expected: Yes, Event can; Character initiation is prohibited while anyone hibernates.

## Q74

Where do discarded/dead Intruder tokens go, and can they be reused later?

Expected: Unused token pool, not removed from game; may be reused for bag additions or a matching Intruder entering ducts.

## Q75

Can Fire and Malfunction markers be placed in unexplored Rooms?

Expected: Fire yes; Malfunction no.

## Q76

A Character with two Light Wounds receives two more Light Wounds. What Wounds remain after resolving them?

Expected: One Serious Wound from third Light plus one Light Wound remaining from fourth.

## Q77

On a Character death in standard play, what happens to Heavy Objects versus their Item cards?

Expected: Heavy Objects (Corpse/Carcass/Egg) drop in Room; Items disappear from game rather than becoming dropped Objects. Cooperative item retention excluded.

## Q78

Self-Destruct reaches its first yellow space, Pods unlock, then are relocked. Does reaching the next yellow space unlock them again?

Expected: No; later yellow spaces do not re-unlock. Unlock manually.

## Q79

Does destroying the ship fulfill Objectives to kill the Queen or destroy the Nest; does that alone make your Character a winner?

Expected: Yes fulfills those destruction/kill requirements; Character must still survive and satisfy all own Objective requirements.

## Q80

May you intentionally spend an empty Trade Action with no intention of exchange; what if honest negotiations end without a trade?

Expected: Empty Actions that do not meaningfully change game state should be disallowed, including deliberately empty Trade. Honest negotiations may result in no Items traded.

## Q81

What kind of Action activates a Quest Item, may it be done in Combat, and does a Room Malfunction prevent it?

Expected: Basic Action printed on quest card, forbidden in Combat; Room Malfunction does not prevent activation.

## Q82

Which Basic Actions can be used in Combat; can you Trade, Craft or Pick Up an Object there? What about permitted card or Item Actions?

Expected: Only Basic Shoot, Melee, or Basic Movement as Escape. No Trade/Craft/PickUp, no Room Action or Quest activation. Card/Item actions without Out of Combat restriction may be used subject to own requirements.

## Q83

Can Soldier use Covering Fire only on themselves, and how are direction, Noise and Surprise targeting handled when moving two Characters?

Expected: May use for self alone. Both move in direction of their choice, perform one Noise roll; if Encounter, Surprise Attack check is for Soldier.

## Q84

How does Full Auto calculate damage and consume Ammo?

Expected: Remove all Ammo, then one Shoot Action. Add Shoot Injuries plus discarded Ammo divided by two rounded down, plus 1 for Assault Rifle bonus.

## Q85

When can Nerves of Steel be chosen relative to seeing the Attack card, and may it protect you when another Character triggered a Surprise Attack?

Expected: May see Attack card before using it. May use when someone else triggers Surprise but only Soldier benefits; e.g. Frenzy still affects others.

## Q86

Careful Movement or Reconnaissance reveals a Danger/Silence Exploration token. Does that automatic result apply or the movement ability?

Expected: Careful Movement result applies instead; cancels Noise roll and automatic Danger/Silence Exploration results. Reconnaissance also cancels both.

## Q87

May Captain use Order while personally in Combat; may Intercom force someone else to Move out of Combat and what rules apply?

Expected: Captain cannot use Order while in Combat. Through Intercom can force another Character to Move out of Combat with normal Escape rule.

## Q88

Who can draw an Action card when Captain uses Motivation with active Intercom?

Expected: All Characters in Rooms with a Computer and all Characters in Captain Room.

## Q89

Moving via Technical Corridors card or Plans Item reveals a Door Exploration token. Where does the Door go?

Expected: Ignore Door token effect because entry direction cannot be determined.

## Q90

Can you Interrupt a Captain Order played remotely via Intercom when you are not in the Captain Room; does passing bar playing Interruption?

Expected: No remote Interruption: must share Room with Character performing Action. Passing does not bar playing Interruption.

## Q91

After two Actions your hand is empty. Do you automatically pass then, and what timing consequence can Fire or Bleeding have?

Expected: No automatic pass. Wait for next round to pass; can suffer Fire/Bleeding again because passing ends another round.

## Q92

Rest scans several Infected cards while no Larva was on your board. Can the scan kill you from multiple infected cards; when can Rest be fatal?

Expected: No; place one Larva regardless number infected. Can be fatal only if a Larva already was on board before Rest.

## Q93

You throw a Grenade/Molotov at uncarried Eggs in the Nest from a neighboring Room. Where is the required Noise roll resolved?

Expected: In the Character current Room, not the Nest.

## Q94

Scientist uses Intranet to research an Object while carrying it outside the Laboratory. Can that reveal a Weakness?

Expected: No; Object itself must be in Laboratory, even when remote action used.

## Q95

Storage Item Counter is zero. Can you use its Room Action repeatedly, and how is ordinary Search different?

Expected: Yes Room Action usable repeatedly paying cost, subject to normal restrictions. Counter only limits Search Action card; at zero normal Search cannot be used.

## Q96

When the Nest is on Fire and Vulnerability to Fire is discovered, how many of its Eggs are destroyed each Fire Damage step?

Expected: Two Eggs instead of normal one.

## Q97

Take an Egg from the Intruder board and drop it back in the Nest Room. Does it restore the Nest Egg total for destruction purposes?

Expected: No; dropped Egg is a normal Heavy Object in Room and no longer counts toward Nest hit points.

## Q98

Smoke Grenade says each target loses one Action card. Who chooses and where does it go?

Expected: Each targeted player chooses one Action card from hand and discards it.

## Q99

Can Medkit and Bandages be used on other Characters?

Expected: Yes, both.

## Q100

May you use your Energy Charge directly to reload another Character Weapon; what procedure permits that recharge?

Expected: No; Trade Charge to that Character, who uses it during own round.

## Q101

Alcohol scans an Infected Contamination card. Does this place a Larva and what happens instead?

Expected: No Larva from this result; take a new Contamination card.

## Q102

Adding Extended Magazine or Auto Loader to an already-used or empty Energy Weapon: does it immediately provide extra Ammo?

Expected: Yes.

## Q103

When you Craft a Flamethrower, does it start fully loaded or with the one Ammo of a searched Weapon?

Expected: Fully loaded.

## Q104

Does the Hand Serious Wound affect Scout Motion Scanner and why?

Expected: No; unique reaction ability, not Item Action.

## Q105

Describe Antidote: scan scope, removed cards, new Contamination, reshuffling, hand retention and passing; how does Surgery differ?

Expected: Scan all Contamination from hand/deck/discard, remove all Infected from game, take one new Contamination, reshuffle it with all non-Infected scanned Contamination and Action cards except cards left in hand, then pass. May retain hand cards. Surgery instead shuffles entire hand too and does not gain extra Contamination (and its own wound/removal procedure still applies). Antidote also removes a Larva already on the Character board.

## Q106

On exploration, where are Slime, Fire, Malfunction and Door effects placed, and which Corridor receives the Door?

Expected: Slime on Character board, Fire/Malfunction in explored Room, Door in Corridor used to enter. Resolve each token once then remove from game; general placement exceptions apply.

## Q107

During the Event Phase Fire Damage step, how many Injuries does each Intruder in a burning Room take, when relative to attacks, and does it trigger an Injury Effect check?

Expected: Each takes one Injury after Intruder Attack step; place injury and resolve ordinary Injury Effect, including possible death/retreat.

## Q108

Can you perform any Search Action in the Nest or Room Covered With Slime, even though ordinary Room tiles usually permit searching?

Expected: No Search Action in either Nest or Room Covered With Slime; the original PDF explicitly prohibits it.

## Q110

An Intruder is in a neighboring Room, but the connecting Corridor has a Closed Door. May you throw a Grenade into that Room through that Corridor?

Expected: No. A Closed Door also blocks Grenade throwing through its Corridor; it is not merely an obstacle to miniature movement.

## Q111

A Noise marker was placed on the basic board's Technical Corridors space because of a roll in one Room. Later, a Character in a distant Room rolls the number of a different Technical Corridors Entrance: is that entrance already noisy? Also, can a Closed Door make an ordinary Corridor's Noise marker apply only on the other side of that Door?

Expected: The single Technical Corridors marker counts at every Technical Corridors Entrance on the whole basic board, so the later numbered result triggers an Encounter rather than placing a second marker. An ordinary Corridor Noise marker affects the whole Corridor, including both sides of a Closed Door.

## Q112

Outside Combat, what can a single use of Bandages do to Wounds, and what two alternatives does Clothes offer? Can Bandages heal an undressed Serious Wound directly? Give each Item's Action-card cost and whether its alternatives can be combined in one use.

Expected: Bandages costs 1 Action card and either dresses 1 Serious Wound OR heals all Light Wounds. It does not directly heal/discard an undressed Serious Wound. Clothes costs 1 Action card and either discards a Slime marker OR dresses 1 Serious Wound. Choose one effect per Item use; both are Out of Combat Only and One Use Only, so discard the used Item.

## Q113

What can the Fire Extinguisher Item do in one use: which Room or Intruder may it affect, can it both extinguish Fire and force Retreat, and what does it cost?

Expected: Spend 1 Action card and discard the One Use Only Fire Extinguisher. Choose either to discard the Fire marker from your current Room OR choose 1 Intruder in your current Room and force it to Retreat. The two effects are alternatives, not a combined Fire Control System procedure.

## Q114

What are the Taser Item's two alternative effects and their legal target location? If you choose its Character-targeting effect, is just one Action card lost, or what exactly is discarded?

Expected: Choose 1 Intruder in your Room: it suffers 1 Injury and Retreats. OR choose 1 Character in your Room: that Character must discard all cards from their hand. It is the whole hand, not just one Action card. Taser costs 1 Action card and is One Use Only.

## Q115

With no Weakness modifying damage, you shoot a Queen using a Flamethrower. What happens on a small curled Intruder-symbol result, on the blank miss face, and on the two-crosshair face? Include any Fire placement.

Expected: The small curled symbol still deals 1 Injury: Flamethrower always deals at least 1 Injury except on the blank miss face. A blank misses. The two-crosshair result deals the normal 2 Injuries and also places a Fire marker in the Room you are in. General marker-placement exceptions still apply.

## Q116

What Room may a Molotov Cocktail target, and what does it do immediately to Fire, Intruders and Characters there? If you use it in your own Room, does its immediate damage exclude you?

Expected: Choose 1 Room with an Intruder: your Room or a neighboring Room. Place a Fire marker there. All beings in that Room suffer 1 Injury/Serious Wound: each Intruder suffers 1 Injury and each Character suffers 1 Serious Wound, including you if you are there. This is immediate, separate from later Fire damage. It costs 1 Action card and is One Use Only. The Nest rule separately permits a Room of uncarried Eggs to be treated as an Intruder target.

## Q117

When you use your own Energy Charge, how much Ammo may it restore and to what type of Weapon? What alternative Door effect can you choose, and which Doors are in range?

Expected: Either fully load one of your own Energy Weapons (up to its capacity), OR Open or Close 1 Door in a Corridor connected to your current Room. The Door alternative does not also reload a Weapon. The Item is Out of Combat Only, costs 1 Action card and is One Use Only. The ordinary prohibition on closing Destroyed Doors still applies; Energy Charge is not the Plasma Torch exception.

## Q118

Where and at what Action-card cost do you activate the Pilot's Evacuation Key? Once active, where must you be to use it, what can it do to Pods, and is it a reusable remote substitute for Hatch Control System?

Expected: Activate it in the Fire Control System Room as the printed cost-1 Basic activation action (Out of Combat Only; a Room Malfunction does not bar Quest activation). The active Key is used in an Evacuation Section Room to Lock or Unlock 1 of the Escape Pods in that Section. Its use costs 1 Action card, is Out of Combat Only and One Use Only. It is local to that Evacuation Section and is discarded on use.

## Q119

Where and at what cost is the Captain's Log Key activated, and how is the activated Item used to inspect another player's secret Objectives? Must that other Character share your Room?

Expected: Activate it in the Comms Room with the printed cost-1 Basic activation action, out of Combat. The active Log Key costs 1 Action card, is One Use Only and Out of Combat Only; use it in a Room with an available Computer to look at the Objective cards of a chosen player. The chosen player's Character need not share your Room: the card restricts the user's location, not the target's location. A Malfunction makes a Room's Computer unavailable, although it does not prevent the Quest activation itself.

## Q120

What does the Six Shooter card require for reloading, and how many Injuries does its single-crosshair Combat die result deal when no Weakness changes damage?

Expected: It can only be reloaded with the Reload Action card. Its single-crosshair result deals 1 additional Injury, for 2 Injuries total instead of the ordinary 1.
