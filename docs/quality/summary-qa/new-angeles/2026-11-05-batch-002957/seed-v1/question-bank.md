# New Angeles seed bank v1

Source-first; frozen before summary authoring and reader dispatch.

## Q01

Describe complete core setup: which decks are separated, how the starting city and turn track are determined, the initial round/resource/threat/capital positions, corporation sides and cards, rival allocation, and the initial investment choice. Exclude the simplified first-game setup.

**Answer:** Shuffle asset, demand, event and investment decks separately and separate/shuffle the five action types. Draw one shuffled setup card, placing one listed component in each listed district and its upper-right count of facedown assets on turn slots starting at1; box all setup cards. Round1, resources0, revealed demand targets as printed, threat0. Choose corporations and correct4 or5–6 side, matching faceup emergency, draw sheet cards. Random capital token chooses active player, who starts10 capital; clockwise11,12,etc. Shuffle used-corporation rivals plus Federalist, deal one secretly each, set extra partly under board. Each draws2 investments, keeps1 facedown, returns other facedown to deck top; create reserve.

**Sources:** RR p2 Complete Setup steps1–12; RR21.1

## Q02

What action cards does each corporation draw in four-player and five/six-player games, in what order, and how are General draws selected? Include Melange.

**Answer:** Globalsec2security, Haas-Bioroid2labor, Jinteki2biotech, NBN2media, Weyland2construction, followed by1General in4-player games. In5–6 these second draws are respectively1media,1construction,1labor,1biotech,1security. Melange draws1General three times in both sizes, each from a different type. Follow printed top-to-bottom order; choose a General deck not already drawn this Refresh before looking.

**Sources:** Printed corporation-sheets-face/back cells1–6; RR3.1,12.3,29

## Q03

Describe the active player’s turn, including start-of-turn asset timing, Refresh, end-of-turn abilities, hand limit and passing. When does the Action Phase end and must every player have a turn that round?

**Answer:** Start-of-turn assets before Refresh. Refresh readies exhausted assets then draws sheet cards. Resolve one deal. End-of-turn abilities optional once per ability in chosen order; discard excess action cards to hand size5, then pass left. Emergencies do not count. When no turn-track assets remain at turn end, proceed to Production. Turns fixed by setup/event, not player count; some players may have no turn this round.

**Sources:** RR3,6.6,22,30,67

## Q04

List the five deal steps and the order of effect, asset claim and cleanup. Which asset is revealed and can the active player pass on the main offer?

**Answer:** Reveal highest-numbered/rightmost turn-track asset. Active player must propose one faceup hand action or faceup emergency; cannot pass. Others each get one clockwise counteroffer chance from active player’s left. Supporting players each get one clockwise support/abstain chance. Most facedown support wins, main offer wins ties. Winner resolves offer then claims asset faceup; all played action cards discarded facedown to shared pile, emergencies returned to original owners face down if winning, face up otherwise.

**Sources:** RR16; FAQ Deals; LTP p10

## Q05

The first, second and third counteroffers are proposed in one deal. How many hand cards must each proposer discard in addition to their offer? Can the active player counter, can an earlier proposer later support, and is there another circuit of counteroffers?

**Answer:** Costs0,1,2 hand cards respectively based on earlier faceup cards in Counteroffer box. New offer covers earlier ones; only topmost remains operative, earlier ones only set cost. Active player cannot counter without explicit card permission. Earlier proposer becomes supporting player if neither active nor current counterer. No second circuit; each nonactive player gets one chance.

**Sources:** RR14–16,60; FAQ Deals

## Q06

In a deal with a counteroffer, who may support, how are cards committed and counted, may a player support both or add more later, and who wins a2–2 tie? What happens if nobody counteroffers?

**Answer:** All except active and current counterer support unless ability explicitly overrides. Each once clockwise from active’s left chooses one or more hand actions facedown to one offer or abstains; cannot support both or add in another chance. Count cards, not printed effects;2–2 goes to active. If no counteroffer skip Support and resolve main offer.

**Sources:** RR16.4–16.6,59–60; FAQ Deals; LTP p10 step4

## Q07

How can an emergency be proposed or used as support, does it count toward hand size or hand-card theft, and where/state does a stolen emergency return after winning or losing an offer? What may Bryan Stinson steal?

**Answer:** Only faceup emergency can be offered as main/counteroffer; never support. Outside hand, no hand-size count or ordinary hand steal/discard. Bryan can steal2 emergencies of participating corporations, faceup or facedown preserving state. Discarded/proposed stolen emergency returns to original logo corporation, face down if winning, face up otherwise; winning face down cannot be offered until flipped by effect.

**Sources:** RR21,30.1; printed Bryan Stinson asset; LTP p14

## Q08

An exploited district prints primary2 credits and secondary1 entertainment, has a development, an orgcrime unit and is in protest. What does it produce and what is its resulting unrest? How would starting in strike or outage change production, and do non-android districts produce in the normal Production Phase?

**Answer:** Produces0credits and1entertainment: development adds1 to primary but orgcrime blocks primary entirely; protest allows production. After producing, protest becomes strike. Starting strike or outage produces nothing but still exploited if android; strike remains strike and outage remains stable/outage absent Human First. Only districts with androids normally exploited, lowest-numbered first.

**Sources:** RR19,27,38,39,43–44,68

## Q09

When an android district with Human First is exploited, what happens from stable, protest, strike, and already-outage starting states? Is an ordinary failed unrest increase without Human First enough to cause an outage?

**Answer:** Produce first under existing modifiers. Human First adds2 unrest stages: stable becomes strike; from protest or strike cannot rise2, place outage and remove unrest to stable. Already outage still exploited and new outage must move. Ordinary unrest at strike cannot increase but causes no outage; only exploited Human First has this overflow rule.

**Sources:** RR27.1–27.3,31.1,39,68.6–68.10

## Q10

An orgcrime unit is placed into a district already containing orgcrime and its route then meets a prisec-protected district before eventually reaching the Root. Explain its movement, reserve/threat result and contract consequences. How do android moves differ?

**Answer:** New orgcrime must follow outgoing arrow to higher-numbered district, continue past same type or prisec until legal district or Root. Root not a district; return to reserve and threat+2. Movement out of districts and return at Root trigger no removal contracts. Androids instead may relocate to any district ignoring paths, one at a time, never into one already containing android.

**Sources:** RR5,20.1,35–36,38,42,47

## Q11

When does illness add threat during an action, including chosen districts and failed placements/moves? If several ill districts are involved is the increase multiplied? Do production or events trigger illness, and what does an illness token entering the Root do?

**Answer:** Action involves district if chosen or component moved in/out placed/removed, including attempted destinations blocked by components or exhausted reserve. At least one involved ill district gives+2 total, not per district. Unrest actions choose districts and qualify. Exploitation and events do not trigger illness effect. Illness entering Root separately returns reserve and adds2.

**Sources:** RR32,35.2,63; FAQ Illness

## Q12

State each corporation’s contract payment and trigger, who may resolve the triggering action, and the important Haas-Bioroid, NBN, Root and Melange exceptions.

**Answer:** Globalsec1 per enemy removed from district; Jinteki2 per illness removed; Weyland2 per outage removed; NBN1 per unrest stage decreased; Haas2 when1+androids moved, at most once per turn. These require action resolution, irrespective of resolver. Moves and Root removals no district-removal contracts; outage removing unrest no NBN. Melange instead gains3 after event if threat increase over entire event below2, counting Root spillover as well as top effect.

**Sources:** Printed corporation sheets both sides1–6; RR11,35.2,35.6,39.1; FAQ Contracts

## Q13

What may players trade or give away, what items cannot be traded after the official erratum, and when are immediate, future-promise, disputed-binding and three-player trades binding? Does an exhausted traded asset ready?

**Answer:** Capital/assets/promises at any time, gifts allowed. Cannot trade actions, emergencies, investments, rivals, corp sheets. Immediately/completely fulfillable agreed trade binding; three+ players binding if all simultaneous/immediate. Future promises nonbinding, may renege; unresolved binding status before agreement means nonbinding. Traded exhausted assets remain exhausted.

**Sources:** RR65; FAQ Trading and erratum65.5; LTP p14

## Q14

Explain asset timing when several abilities share a timing, what is optional, when exhausted assets ready, and whether a newly gained End of Turn asset can be used this turn. How often can each End of Turn ability be used?

**Answer:** One owner orders own same-timing assets; multiple owners resolve clockwise from active player. May and End of Turn abilities optional. Start-of-turn triggers before Refresh, which readies assets. Exhausted cannot exhaust again. End-of-turn ability once each, chosen order; one received during that step can be used that turn.

**Sources:** RR6.2–6.6,22,26

## Q15

Give the full demand-round order, including what happens when only one resource misses its target, when supplies reset and unchosen investments go, and which steps are omitted in the third demand round.

**Answer:** Score revealed investments before demand. If every resource >=target demand met no increase; any one below fails whole demand and adds printed threat once. Third demand ends immediately after this step, omitting reset/new demand/investment. Earlier demands reset every resource0, old demand bottom new top revealed targets set. Return all investments shuffle, each draw2 keep1 facedown other top facedown without reveal. Advance round to standard.

**Sources:** RR18,33; LTP p9

## Q16

The third demand fails and pushes threat to25. Does investment scoring happen first, and who wins if the Federalist has25 capital? Contrast finishing that demand below25, a tie with your named rival, and having your own rival card at4 versus5–6 players.

**Answer:** Investment scoring first. Threat25 immediately ends game; only Federalist wins if>=25 (all others lose); below25 Federalist loses and each ordinary player independently checks rival. Named rival must be strictly beaten, ties lose. Own rival must strictly exceed at least2 others in4player,3 others in5–6; ties do not count.

**Sources:** RR18.1–18.2,28,50,63.1,69

## Q17

Which information may players communicate about action hands, hand counts, rival cards, investments, hidden turn-track assets, and event cards seen through a reorder effect? What does Thomas Haas require you to announce?

**Answer:** Cannot tell hand identities or lie about hand count. Rivals cannot reveal/discuss. Investments stay facedown until demand; deck cannot inspect after deal and unchosen not revealed. Turn-track assets cannot inspect. Event fronts seen privately kept secret; backs including top public. Thomas user must openly state whether swapping under-board rival with own; occurrence public.

**Sources:** RR2.6–2.8,6.9,24.3,33,50.3; FAQ Thomas Haas

## Q18

Where do discarded action cards, assets and removed components go? What happens if an action deck is empty when a card must be drawn, a reserve component runs out, or production would pass the track end?

**Answer:** Actions facedown shared discard, never inspect facedown discard. Assets facedown deck bottom. Removed components reserve reusable. Empty requested action deck: shuffle entire shared discard, reveal/sort by type then shuffle each into own deck. No reserve component means do not place. Resources stop at final space, cannot reduce below0.

**Sources:** RR2.2–2.4,6.7,10,43.5,47–48,58; LTP p14

## Q19

How are a standard round’s phases ordered, which districts get exploited and in what order, and how is an event resolved, discarded and used to set the next turn track? Must that track be reset after the sixth standard round?

**Answer:** Action then Production (android districts ascending number) then Event. Reveal top event and resolve top to bottom: upper effect, listed components one per listed district, printed lower-right number of assets from deck onto lowest slots facedown. Event bottom of event deck, advance round. No need reset after sixth since no next Action Phase.

**Sources:** RR24–25,27.7,44,51,55,67

## Q20

Is resolving Liquidation exploitation, and which production modifiers apply? How do Unorthodox Knowledge and Caprice Nisei interact, including whether the resolving player is bound by the announced resolution?

**Answer:** Liquidation produces without exploiting; no normal exploitation consequences such as unrest. All production modifiers still apply: strike/outage no production, orgcrime no primary, development+1 primary, track caps. Unorthodox Knowledge cannot be affected by Caprice Nisei, and resolver is not bound by their stated resolution.

**Sources:** FAQ Action Cards Liquidation; RR2.9,43.2–43.6
