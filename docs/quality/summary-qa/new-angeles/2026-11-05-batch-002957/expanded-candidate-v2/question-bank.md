## Q01

Describe complete core setup: which decks are separated, how the starting city and turn track are determined, the initial round/resource/threat/capital positions, corporation sides and cards, rival allocation, and the initial investment choice. Exclude the simplified first-game setup.

**Required answer facts:**

- Separately shuffle the four non-action decks and five action decks.
- Random setup card determines listed city placements and facedown asset count in ascending turn slots; box all setup cards.
- Round 1, resources 0, threat 0; reveal demand and set its target tokens.
- Choose corporations, matching faceup emergencies and correct player-count sheet sides; draw initial sheet cards.
- Random active player starts at 10 capital; clockwise players start at 11, 12, and so on.
- Use participating-corporation rivals plus Federalist; secretly deal one each and put extra partly under board.
- Each draws two investments, keeps one facedown and returns other facedown to top; form reserve.

**Sources:** RR p2 Complete Setup steps1–12; RR21.1

## Q03

Describe the active player’s turn, including start-of-turn asset timing, Refresh, end-of-turn abilities, hand limit and passing. When does the Action Phase end and must every player have a turn that round?

**Required answer facts:**

**Sources:** RR3,6.6,22,30,67

## Q04

**Required answer facts:**

- Five steps in order: Asset, Main Offer, Counteroffer, Support, Resolution.
- Reveal rightmost/highest-numbered turn-track asset.
- Active player must propose a main offer and cannot pass.
- Winner resolves offer before claiming revealed asset faceup, followed by cleanup: ordinary played actions facedown to shared discard; emergencies to original owners facedown if winning, faceup otherwise.

**Sources:** RR16; FAQ Deals; LTP p10

## Q05

**Required answer facts:**

## Q06

**Required answer facts:**

**Sources:** RR16.4–16.6,59–60; FAQ Deals; LTP p10 step4
