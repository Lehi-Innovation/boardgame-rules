A.9 Step 9: Place Score Markers
Each player places their faction’s score marker on the 
“0” space on the score track.
A.10 Step 10: Choose Starting Hands
Each player chooses three cards in their hand to keep 
and puts the other two cards face down on the shared 
deck. When finished, shuffle the shared deck.
