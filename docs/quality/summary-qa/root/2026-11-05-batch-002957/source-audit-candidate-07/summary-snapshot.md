## Overview

This summary covers the **October 13, 2025 Law of Root**: common rules, Standard Setup, the four base factions, Fall (Autumn) and Winter maps, and the three base Vagabond characters. Expansion factions and characters, a second Vagabond, hirelings, landmarks, bots, and Advanced Setup are outside this scope. First to 30 victory points (VP) wins immediately; dominance changes this condition.

## Components

The base game has a Fall/Winter board, two battle dice, four faction boards, 54 shared-deck cards, 16 faction overviews, 4 Eyrie leaders and 2 Loyal Viziers, 3 Vagabond characters and 15 quests. There are 25 Marquise, 20 Eyrie and 10 Alliance warriors; one Vagabond pawn; 7 roosts, 6 each of sawmills, workshops and recruiters, 3 bases; 10 sympathy, 8 wood and 1 keep token; 4 ruins, 4 score markers, 3 relationship markers, 12 clearing markers and 23 items. Items are boots, swords, bags, hammers, tea, coins, crossbows and a torch. Items are not faction pieces or enemy pieces. **Faction-piece ownership cannot change**: each player owns the faction chosen in setup and its listed faction pieces, except items; possessing an item does not make it faction-owned.

## Setup

Use **Standard Setup**, in this order (Law 5.1):

1. Choose factions; determine starting player and seating randomly. Take faction boards and their listed pieces. Use Fall or Winter. Fall has no special rules; on Winter, the river divides forests just as printed paths do. Rivers are otherwise not paths.
2. Put score markers at 0.
3. With two players, remove all four dominance cards. Shuffle shared deck; each player draws **three cards**.
4. **Ruins and items:** Cover the four R building slots with ruins. The separate map item supply is **2 boots, 2 bags, 1 crossbow, 1 hammer, 2 swords, 2 tea, 2 coins**. Keep the four R-marked ruin items separate from this supply: only Vagabond faction setup hides one each of its R-marked bag, boot, hammer and sword beneath the four ruins, **one item beneath each ruin**. Without a Vagabond, ruins contain no items.
5. The map item supply uses its corresponding spaces near the top of the map.
6. Distribute overview cards as desired; put battle dice nearby.
7. Set up factions in their letter order: Marquise, Eyrie, Alliance, Vagabond. Setup order is distinct from turn order.

**Marquise setup (6.3):** Supply 25 warriors and 8 wood. Put keep in any corner (starting clearing). Put one warrior in every clearing except the diagonally opposite corner. Place one sawmill, workshop and recruiter among the keep clearing and adjacent clearings, in any combination respecting slots. Put the remaining five buildings of each type on their tracks from right to left, leaving leftmost space empty.

**Eyrie setup (7.3):** Supply 20 warriors. Put one roost and six warriors in a corner that is not another player's starting corner, diagonally opposite a starting corner if possible. Choose a leader; keep others face up nearby. Tuck the two bird Loyal Viziers into the columns listed on that leader. Fill roost track with the other six roosts from right to left.

**Alliance setup (8.3):** Supply 10 warriors. Put the three bases in their matching board spaces and ten sympathy on its track. Draw **three additional cards** face down into Supporters; these are separate from your three-card hand. Start with no map pieces or officers.

**Vagabond setup (9.3):** Choose Thief, Tinker or Ranger; place pawn in any forest. Shuffle quests and display three face up. Hide one each of the **R-marked bag, boot, hammer and sword** beneath the four ruins: assemble and shuffle the four stacks and return each to an empty R slot so identities are hidden. Take S-marked starting items listed on character: tea, coins and bags go face up on matching tracks, other items face up in Satchel; return unused S items to box. Place each other faction's relationship marker at Indifferent.

**Recommended faction mix (5.2):** Reach should total at least 17 for two players, 18 for three, 21 for four. Base reach: Marquise 10, Eyrie 7, Vagabond 5, Alliance 3. The Law allows adventurous groups to use any mix totaling at least 17.

## Turn Structure

Take turns clockwise: **Birdsong → Daylight → Evening**. “At start” effects precede all other phase effects; “at end” effects follow everything else in that phase, before next phase. Once the last step of a turn begins, no other actions are allowed except explicitly end-of-Evening/turn actions. An action, ability or persistent effect cannot be interrupted unless an effect explicitly permits it; a Marquise March's two moves are one compound action. If simultaneous effects have no clear order or decision maker, the player taking the turn chooses. For battle effects at the same timing, attacker chooses their order. (1.1.3, 1.4, 4.3.)

| Faction | Birdsong, in order | Daylight, in order | Evening, in order |
|---|---|---|---|
| Marquise | Produce wood | Craft; then actions | Draw; discard to five |
| Eyrie | Emergency Orders; add Decree; New Roost | Craft; resolve Decree | Score roost track; draw; discard to five |
| Alliance | Revolt; then Spread Sympathy | Craft/Mobilize/Train in any order and number | Military operations; draw; discard to five |
| Vagabond | Refresh; optional Slip | Item actions in any order and number | Forest Rest; draw; discard to five; capacity check |

## Actions

### Shared movement, crafting and battle

**Rule (2.5):** A player rules a clearing with more total **warriors plus buildings** than each other player. Tokens and pawns do not contribute. Ordinary ties mean nobody rules. Eyrie rule a tie for most if they have at least one Eyrie piece there. Vagabond cannot rule or stop someone else ruling.

**Move (4.2):** Take any positive number of your warriors/pawns from one clearing along a linking path to an adjacent clearing. You must rule origin or destination or both. Recheck rule for each move. You may move the same pieces repeatedly, including on multiple moves in one action. Rivers are not paths unless an effect explicitly treats them as paths. Moving and placing are different procedures. Vagabond has its own movement exceptions below.

Clearings have fox, rabbit or mouse suit and limited building slots. Buildings require open slots; tokens do not occupy these. Ruins block their slots and can be removed only when explicitly allowed. A forest is enclosed by printed paths and clearings; it is adjacent to clearings touching it without crossing a printed path and to forests separated by just one printed path. Winter's river also divides forests. (2.2–2.4, M.1–2.)

**Craft (4.1):** Craft a card from your hand when your faction permits, by activating crafting pieces matching the **crafting icons at bottom left**, rather than paying its card suit. Each icon needs one matching piece activation; the printed icons completely define this crafting cost. **Each crafting piece may activate only once per turn, except Vagabond may reactivate its pawn by exhausting one face-up, undamaged hammer per crafting icon; each icon needs its own hammer payment.** Its suit is its clearing's suit. A three-color question mark accepts any suit. Marquise use workshops; Eyrie roosts; Alliance sympathy. Vagabond uses hammers/pawn as explained below.

- Immediate effect (paper box): resolve and discard card. An item card requires that item in the map supply, or it cannot be crafted. Take it from supply into Crafted Items and score listed VP, subject to Eyrie exception. Ordinary factions do not exhaust crafted items to use them as Vagabond does.
- Persistent effect (stone box): keep card in play area and gain its text. You cannot craft an identical persistent effect already in your play area. Its card is outside your hand and cannot be spent/discarded/used for another purpose unless explicitly instructed.
- **Ambush cards cannot be crafted:** the defender may play one for two immediate hits, or the attacker may play one to foil a defending ambush, matching the clearing. **Dominance cards cannot be crafted:** activate one to change victory condition, or spend it for suit.

**Battle (4.3):** Choose a clearing with your warriors or pawn and an enemy there as defender. **Battle does not require you to rule the clearing.** Resolve these six steps:

1. **Ambush:** Defender may play one ambush matching clearing (birds are wild). Attacker may foil it with a matching ambush. Defender's card is discarded; a foiled ambush deals no hits. Otherwise defender immediately deals two hits, then discards ambush. If no attacking warriors or pawns remain, battle ends immediately. Ambush hits are not limited by defending warriors.
2. **Before-roll effects:** Resolve applicable effects; attacker chooses order if both have them.
3. **Roll:** Higher die is attacker's rolled hits, lower defender's; equal dice give equal results. Each side's rolled hits are capped by its own warriors in battle. Alliance defending reverses the dice; Vagabond uses undamaged swords instead of warriors.
4. **After-roll effects:** Resolve effects affecting battle that do not say “before rolling”; attacker chooses order if both have them.
5. **Count hits:** Add extra hits (uncapped by warriors), subtract ignored hits. If defender has no warriors, attacker deals one extra hit for **defenseless**; Vagabond is instead defenseless if it has no undamaged swords.
6. **Deal hits:** Each hit removes one opposing piece. The side taking hits chooses its own removal order, but **all its warriors must be removed before its buildings/tokens**. It also chooses order of effects triggered by its pieces' removal. Both sides deal their counted hits; losing warriors does not retroactively change the rolled cap. Whoever removes an enemy building/token scores **one VP each**, even while defending and even outside battle. Vagabond takes hits as item damage instead.

Removed faction pieces normally return to supply; tracked pieces return to the **rightmost empty track space**. Placement normally takes pieces from supply, or leftmost on a track. Items removed from the game are removed permanently. Pieces are finite: no proxies. When an instruction cannot place/take/remove all prompted pieces, **you must do the maximum possible and cannot voluntarily do fewer**; this never waives costs or prerequisites, nor the Eyrie's obligation to complete Decree actions. For simultaneous removal, remove all pieces before triggering removal effects. (1.5, G.20, G.24.)

### Marquise de Cat (6)

**Keep:** Only Marquise may **place** pieces in its clearing; other players may **move** pieces into it. The keep cannot be placed again if removed. **Field Hospitals:** Whenever any number of Marquise warriors are removed from a clearing, Marquise may spend one card matching that clearing to place those warriors in the keep clearing instead of supply. This can apply outside battle. Without a keep there is no destination for hospitals.

**Wood production:** Birdsong places one wood per sawmill in its clearing. During Daylight, each Overwork action spends a card matching a sawmill clearing to place one wood there. Both methods draw from the same finite supply of eight wood tokens.

**Birdsong:** Place one wood at each sawmill (one per sawmill if multiple share a clearing), limited by eight wood supply.

**Daylight:** First craft with workshops. Then take **up to three actions**, plus **one per bird card spent outside an action**, in any order and number except Recruit's limit:

- **Battle:** One battle.
- **March:** Up to two moves; either may use the same or different warriors. Do not interrupt the compound action to spend a bird card or take another action.
- **Recruit:** One warrior at **each recruiter**; only **once per turn**. If supply cannot fill all recruiters, place maximum available, choosing among eligible recruiters.
- **Build:** Choose a clearing you rule with open slot and choose building type. Take the leftmost building of that type. Pay cost shown atop its column by removing that many wood from the target clearing or from other ruled clearings linked to it through an unbroken chain of ruled clearings. Place building; score VP on uncovered space. You cannot build without full wood cost, the building and legal slot.
- **Overwork:** Spend a card matching a sawmill's clearing to place one wood there.

**Evening:** Draw one card plus one per uncovered draw bonus; discard hand down to five.

### Eyrie Dynasties (7)

**Lords of the Forest:** Win ties for rule if at least one Eyrie piece there. **Disdain for Trade:** Crafted items score only **one VP instead of printed VP** (other extra crafting-point effects still apply). Builder ignores Disdain.

**Birdsong, in order:**

1. **Emergency Orders:** If hand empty, draw one.
2. **Add to Decree:** Must add one or two hand cards, **at most one added bird card**. Each goes to any column; columns have no size limit.
3. **A New Roost:** If no roosts on map, place one roost and three warriors in a clearing with the fewest warriors **where all these pieces can be placed**.

**Daylight — resolve the Decree:** Craft using roosts first, then resolve **every Decree card**, column by column **Recruit → Move → Battle → Build**. Within one column choose card order freely. Bird is wild. **If any required action cannot be fully completed, immediately enter Turmoil: you cannot skip the card or continue other columns.** Each card requires:

- Recruit: place **one warrior at one chosen roost** in a clearing matching card (two warriors there with Charismatic).
- Move: move at least one warrior **from** a matching clearing; ordinary rule/path requirements apply.
- Battle: initiate battle in a matching clearing containing your warriors and an enemy.
- Build: place a roost in a matching clearing you rule, with open slot and **no roost already there**.

If any required action cannot be completed fully for any reason (including warrior/building supply), immediately enter **Turmoil**; do not skip that card or continue other Decree columns.

**Turmoil, in order:** Lose one VP per bird Decree card **including both Loyal Viziers**; discard every Decree card except Viziers; flip current leader face down and choose a face-up replacement. If none face up, flip all leaders face up before choosing. Retuck Viziers in new leader's columns. End Daylight and proceed to the full normal Evening: **score the roost track, draw one plus uncovered bonuses, then discard to five**. **Turmoil does not remove your map warriors or buildings.**

**Evening, even after turmoil:** Score the VP on rightmost empty roost-track space. Draw one plus uncovered draw bonuses; discard down to five.

| Leader | Vizier columns | Ability |
|---|---|---|
| Builder | Recruit, Move | Ignore Disdain for Trade |
| Charismatic | Recruit, Battle | Each Recruit must place two warriors instead of one |
| Commander | Move, Battle | One extra hit as attacker |
| Despot | Move, Build | When removing at least one enemy building/token in battle, score one extra VP in addition to normal removal points |

### Woodland Alliance (8)

**Guerrilla War:** When Alliance defends, Alliance uses higher die and attacker lower; warrior caps still apply. **Supporters** are a separate face-down stack, inspectable by Alliance. They can be spent only for suit; they do not count as hand cards or toward hand limit. With no bases on map, maximum **five supporters**; incoming cards beyond capacity are discarded. With any base, unlimited supporters.

**Outrage:** Each time another player **removes sympathy** or **moves any warriors into a sympathetic clearing**, that player adds one matching hand card (birds count) to Supporters. Moving a group costs one card per affected move, rather than one per warrior. If no matching cards including birds, show hand to Alliance, then Alliance draws one deck card into Supporters. **If Supporters is full, discard the incoming Outrage card; payment or the no-match draw still occurs.** Merely battling there is no trigger unless sympathy is removed; placing warriors there and moving only Vagabond pawn do not trigger it.

**Losing a base:** Discard all supporters matching its clearing **including birds**, remove half officers **rounded up**. If no bases remain, discard excess supporters down to five. Do this whenever a base is removed.

**Birdsong:** Complete **all Revolts before starting any Spread Sympathy**; each may be performed any number of times. **For both actions, every supporter spent must match the target clearing; birds are wild.** Once spreading starts, you cannot return to Revolt this Birdsong, so newly spread sympathy cannot support a same-Birdsong Revolt:

- **Revolt:** Choose sympathetic clearing without a base, matching a base still on your board. Spend **two matching supporters**. **Remove all enemy pieces there and score one VP per enemy building/token removed**; then place matching base. Place warriors there equal to number of sympathetic clearings matching the base's printed suit (including revolt clearing), and one warrior in Officers box. Score normal VP for removed enemy buildings/tokens. Resolve the effect even if a prompted placement cannot be completed: place the maximum possible, without adding a base-placement prerequisite. Enemy Vagabond damages three items instead of being removed.
- **Spread Sympathy:** Choose unsympathetic clearing adjacent by path to sympathy; if no sympathy anywhere, choose any clearing. Spend matching supporters shown above next sympathy token. **Martial Law:** add one matching supporter if chosen clearing has at least three warriors of **one other player**. Place token and score uncovered space. Only one sympathy per clearing. Keep's placement ban still applies.

**Daylight:** Any order and number:

- **Craft** from hand with sympathy.
- **Mobilize:** Add a hand card to Supporters (respect cap).
- **Train:** Spend a **hand card** matching a clearing with a base to place one supply warrior in Officers box. Supporters cannot pay Train. These officers allow Evening operations, including **Organize: remove one Alliance warrior from an unsympathetic clearing, place sympathy there and score the uncovered VP**. Organize occurs in Evening, not Daylight.

**Evening:** Take military operations **up to your number of officers**, in any order and repetition. Officers stay on the board; taking an operation does not consume an officer.

| Military operation | Procedure for one operation |
|---|---|
| Move | Take one move using normal movement rules |
| Battle | Initiate one battle |
| Recruit | Place one warrior at one clearing with a base |
| Organize | Remove one Alliance warrior from an unsympathetic clearing; place sympathy there; score the uncovered VP |

Organize spends no supporters and has no sympathy-adjacency requirement or Martial Law cost; it still needs legal token placement. **After operations**, draw one card plus one per uncovered draw bonus, then discard hand down to five.

### Vagabond (9; V.1–3)

**Pawn and items:** Pawn is not a warrior, cannot rule or block rule, and cannot be removed. An enemy effect removing **all enemy pieces** from its clearing instead damages **three items**. Items may be face up (ready) or face down (exhausted), and independently undamaged or damaged. To exhaust for an action, choose a face-up **undamaged** item and flip it down. Damaged items cannot pay actions.

Face-up tea/coins/bags in Satchel immediately move to empty matching track space if possible; each track holds at most three of its type. Exhausting an item on track sends it to Satchel. Boots/swords/crossbows/torch/hammers are gained face up in Satchel. Damaging an item sends it to Damaged box **without changing its face**. One hit damages one undamaged item of your choice; ignore excess hits when none remain.

**Birdsong:**

1. **Refresh:** First count tea **already face up on Refresh track**; flip two exhausted items face up per counted tea, then three more exhausted items. Tea refreshed during this step gives no extra refresh this step. Refreshing changes face, not damaged status.
2. **Slip (optional):** Move to adjacent clearing or forest, exhausting **no boots**, even into hostile warriors. Ignore all effects preventing movement **out of** a clearing. Forest adjacency follows map rules, including Winter river.

**Daylight:** Any order/number, as ready undamaged items permit:

- **Move:** Exhaust one boot, plus **one extra boot** if destination has any warriors of any Hostile faction (also for forced moves). Ignore rule. **Cannot enter a forest with this action; from a forest you may move only to an adjacent clearing.** Slip has the special free-boot exception.
- **Battle:** Exhaust one sword to initiate. Maximum rolled hits, attacking or defending, equals **all undamaged swords in Satchel, face up or face down**. As defender you are defenseless only with no undamaged swords. Take hits as item damage.
- **Explore:** Exhaust torch in ruin's clearing. Take one hidden item face up into Satchel or track; score one VP if an item taken. Remove ruin when its last item is taken, freeing the building slot.
- **Aid:** Exhaust **any one item**, give a card matching your clearing to player with faction pieces there, even Hostile. May then take one item from their Crafted Items box face up into Satchel/track; no available item is required to Aid. **Only non-Hostile relationships can advance through Aid; a Hostile recipient stays Hostile.** Check relationship improvement below.
- **Quest (matching clearing only):** Choose a face-up quest matching clearing; exhaust its **two listed items**, put quest in play area completed. Choose either VP equal to matching completed quests **including this one**, or draw two cards. Draw replacement quest into public display. No cards are spent to pay a quest.
- **Strike:** Exhaust crossbow to remove one enemy warrior in clearing. Only if that enemy has no warriors there may you remove another of its faction pieces instead. Check hostility. Strike is not battle and gives no Infamy.
- **Repair:** Exhaust hammer; move one damaged item to Satchel **keeping current face**. Ready tea/coins/bags move to tracks if space.
- **Craft:** Exhaust one hammer per crafting icon; pawn acts as crafting piece matching current clearing, and may activate repeatedly despite normal once-per-turn crafting limit. Clearing must match **all icons**; cannot craft mixed fox/rabbit/mouse requirements from one clearing. Crafted item can immediately be taken face up into Satchel/track. Need supply item as usual.
- **Special:** Exhaust items required by character action below.

**Relationships:** Each other faction starts Indifferent. To advance a non-Hostile marker one space, Aid that faction as many times **in this same turn** as printed between current and next spaces. One Aid counts toward only one improvement. Advance and score VP printed in new space. At final space become **Allied**; each subsequent Aid to that faction scores **two VP**.

**Allied benefits:**

| Action with an Allied faction | Benefit and limits |
|---|---|
| Aid | Each subsequent Aid to this Allied faction scores **two VP** |
| Move | May force warriors of **one** Allied faction to accompany the pawn to its destination clearing. They follow their own movement restrictions, including rule and Eyrie tie advantage. They cannot accompany a move specified to be alone. Moving them into sympathy triggers Outrage |
| Attack | At the start of battle **as attacker**, may treat warriors of **one** Allied faction there as your own. **Rolled-hit cap = those allied warriors plus all your undamaged swords.** They can join against a different enemy; cannot use that ally's warriors to attack the ally itself |

**Taking hits with an ally:** In a battle using Allied warriors as your own, you may absorb hits by removing those warriors or damaging your items. If you remove **more allied warriors than items you damage in that battle**, the ally becomes Hostile **at battle end**. This overrides ordinary hostility timing for those friendly warriors taken as hits against the other enemy. Equal numbers do not trigger the rule.

- **Hostile:** Removing **one** warrior of a non-Hostile faction immediately moves marker to Hostile (except allied battle-loss rule above). **Infamy:** During battle on your own turn, score **one extra VP per hostile piece removed**, except warrior that first made faction Hostile. **Each hostile building/token removed in battle on your turn normally scores two VP: one removal VP plus one Infamy. A hostile warrior scores one Infamy, except the warrior that first caused Hostile status.** Infamy depends on whose turn it is, rather than which side attacks: no Infamy on another player's turn, and none from Strike. Hostile status requires extra boot for moves into hostile warriors, not merely buildings/tokens.
- Aid cannot move marker out of Hostile; it can still obtain a crafted item. Coalition with Hostile faction resets its marker to Indifferent.

**Evening, all four steps:**

1. If in forest, **Rest**: return all damaged items to Satchel and flip them face up; ready tea/coins/bags move to tracks if able. This does not refresh undamaged exhausted items.
2. **Always draw**, even after forest Rest: one card plus one per face-up coins on track.
3. Discard hand to five.
4. Capacity: Satchel **plus Damaged box** may contain **six items plus two per face-up bag on its track**. Track items do not count toward load. Permanently remove chosen Satchel/Damaged items until within limit. Do not check capacity immediately upon acquiring an item.

| Base character | Starting items | Special action |
|---|---|---|
| Thief | boot, torch, tea, sword | **Steal:** exhaust torch to take random hand card from player with faction pieces in your clearing |
| Tinker | boot, torch, bag, hammer | **Day Labor:** exhaust torch to take discard-pile card matching clearing; bird always qualifies |
| Ranger | boot, torch, crossbow, sword | **Hideout:** exhaust torch to repair three items, keeping their faces; immediately end Daylight and begin Evening |

## Scoring / Victory Conditions

**30 VP:** First to 30 wins immediately, on any player's turn. If multiple reach 30 simultaneously, tied player closest clockwise to current player wins, including current player. Removing enemy buildings/tokens gives one VP each regardless of phase or whether attacker/defender. Crafting items scores printed VP except Eyrie's Disdain. Faction scoring is described above. (3.1–2.)

**Dominance (3.3):** In own Daylight at **at least 10 VP**, activate a dominance card from hand into play area; remove score marker and stop scoring VP. Cannot remove/replace activated card. At **start of own Birdsong**, win if ruling **three clearings of matching mouse/rabbit/fox suit**, or for **bird dominance, two diagonally opposite corner clearings**. Meeting condition earlier does not immediately win. Dominance cards cannot be crafted, but can be spent for suit. Whenever one would enter discard, put it near map available instead. During own Daylight, spend matching card to take available dominance into hand. Available **bird dominance remains bird**, so payment must be bird; a bird hand card can pay for non-bird dominance. Activation and taking are separate, neither requires a faction action allowance.

**Vagabond coalition (9.2.8):** Cannot activate normal dominance win. With **four or more players**, during own Daylight at **at least 10 VP**, activate dominance to coalition with another player **not already in coalition**, whose VP is lowest among players other than you (choose among ties). Put your score marker on partner's faction board and stop scoring. If partner wins, you also win, including if partner wins through dominance. Coalition partners are not enemies. A Hostile partner becomes Indifferent. Coalition does not by itself grant Allied relationship powers.

## Special Rules & Edge Cases

- **Precedence:** Card overrides Law; Law overrides Learning to Play. Follow general and faction rules together when possible; if incompatible, faction rule wins. **Cannot is absolute** unless explicitly overridden. (1.1.)
- **Cards:** Birds substitute for any non-bird suit, even for multiple same-suit payments. **If prompted to give or discard cards of a non-bird suit, you must treat your birds as that suit**; the instruction determines how many cards to give or discard. A non-bird cannot substitute when instruction explicitly requires bird. Draw from shared deck top; when deck empties, immediately shuffle discard into new deck. (2.1.)
- Hand size is public, contents private: reveal/show only if explicitly instructed. Face-up piles may be searched/inspected; deck cannot. Agreements are non-binding; cards may be transferred only as instructed; actions require no consent. (1.2–3.)
- **Force:** Resolve forced actions as if affected player chose them, retaining their abilities and restrictions. A player forced to battle makes its battle decisions. (1.5.5.)
- **Place/remove/replace:** Place normally from supply/leftmost track; remove normally to supply/rightmost track, items permanently. Replace requires both removal and placement possible; the keep's placement ban cannot be bypassed by replacing an enemy piece. Swap ignores move/place restrictions. Spending a card means discarding it, with dominance's special destination. (G.5, G.20, G.23–24, G.31, G.33.)
- The faction boards provide track costs, VP and draw bonuses; use the newly exposed spaces after placing/removing pieces. An officer is a warrior in Officers box and is unavailable in supply until removed.

## Player Reference

| Faction | Crafting | Scoring engine | Important limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Marquise | Workshops, before actions | Build and expose VP | Three actions plus birds; Recruit once |
| Eyrie | Roosts, before Decree | Rightmost empty roost-track space each Evening | All Decree cards compulsory; added birds at most one |
| Alliance | Sympathy, throughout Daylight | Place sympathy; remove enemies in revolt | Supporters five without bases; operations up to officers |
| Vagabond | Exhaust hammers, current clearing | Explore, quests, Aid relationships, Infamy | Ready undamaged items pay; capacity checked last |

**Battle order:** Ambush → before-roll effects → dice → after-roll effects → count → deal. **Normal move:** positive group, adjacent path, rule at least one end. **Turn:** Birdsong → Daylight → Evening, clockwise. **Win:** 30 immediately, dominance at start of own Birdsong, or coalition partner's win.
