Law of Root October 13, 2025; common rules, four base factions, Fall/Winter, base Vagabond characters, dominance and coalitions; exclude expansion content, hirelings, landmarks, bots and Advanced Setup.

All 80 questions are retained. Explicit source/key corrections: Q37 in v6, Q04 in v7, Q07 and Q12 in v8. ## Q01

How is rule determined, including ordinary ties, Eyrie ties, tokens and the Vagabond pawn?

**Expected:** More total warriors plus buildings than each other player; ordinary ties mean no ruler. Eyrie win a tie for most if they have at least one Eyrie piece there. Tokens and pawns do not contribute; Vagabond cannot rule or prevent rule.

**Law:** 2.5;7.2.2;9.2.2

## Q02

What makes a normal move legal, can the same warriors move repeatedly, and do rivers count as paths?

**Expected:** Move more than zero warriors/pawns along one linking path to an adjacent clearing; rule origin or destination or both. Same pieces can move repeatedly. Rivers are not paths unless explicitly treated as such.

**Law:** 2.2.1;2.3;4.2

## Q03

Describe battle from ambush through removal: foiling, early ending, dice limits, extra hits, removal priorities and who scores removed buildings/tokens.

**Expected:** Defender may ambush matching clearing; attacker may foil with matching ambush. Unfoiled ambush deals two immediate hits; discard defender ambush, end battle if no attacking warriors/pawns remain. Before-roll effects, roll high attacker/low defender, rolled hits capped by own warriors, after-roll effects, count hits including uncapped extra hits and defenseless extra attacker hit if no defending warriors, then deal hits. Each side chooses own removals, warriors first. Whoever removes enemy building/token scores one, including defender.

**Law:** 4.3;3.2.1

## Q04

How do crafting costs, repeat use of crafting pieces, absent supply items and duplicate persistent effects work?

**Expected:** Activate crafting pieces matching every crafting icon; each piece may normally activate only once per turn. A crafting piece's suit is its clearing's suit, and the card's own suit is not its crafting cost. An item must be available in the map supply or that card cannot be crafted. Resolve an immediate effect and discard the card; a persistent effect stays in the play area, and an identical persistent effect already there prevents crafting another. For Vagabond crafting, exhaust one face-up, undamaged hammer per crafting icon; the pawn may be activated repeatedly, but its clearing must match every crafting icon. Reusing the pawn does not waive the hammer payment for each icon.

**Law:** 4.1.1-4;9.2.1;9.2.5;9.5.8

## Q05

When and at what VP can you activate dominance, what wins each suit, and can an activated card be replaced or discarded?

**Expected:** During own Daylight at least 10 VP, put card in play area, remove score marker, no longer score VP. Mouse/rabbit/fox win ruling three matching clearings at start of own Birdsong; bird wins ruling two opposite corner clearings then. Activated card cannot be removed or replaced.

**Law:** 3.3.1-2

## Q06

**Law:** 3.3.3-4;2.1.1

## Q78

During Alliance Evening, how many military operations may be taken, and for one Recruit operation how many warriors are placed and where?

**Expected:** Take up to the number of officers, in any order and repetition. One Recruit operation places one warrior from supply in any one clearing with an Alliance base, not one at every base. Officers stay on board when operations are used.

**Law:** 8.6.1 preamble;8.6.1.III

## Q79

How many Daylight actions may the Vagabond take, must the action types follow a fixed order, and may the same action type be repeated?

**Expected:** The Vagabond may take Daylight actions in any order and number, repeating or interleaving action types as desired, provided each action can pay its item costs and meet its other requirements. There is no separate fixed action-count allowance. Ranger Hideout still immediately ends Daylight as specified.

**Law:** 9.5 phase preamble;9.2.5;V.3.2

## Q80

What item and how many of it must the Vagabond exhaust to take the Daylight Strike action, and can an exhausted or damaged copy pay that cost?

**Expected:** Exhaust one face-up, undamaged crossbow. An already exhausted or damaged crossbow cannot pay the action cost.

**Law:** 9.5.6;9.2.5
