With So Clover!, secretly write a clue for each pair of keywords. Working together, will the other players figure out your keywords?
So Clover! is a cooperative party game of word association designed by François Romain.
The game in video
With 880 keywords in the game, So Clover! is the ideal game to play with friends and family.
Decipher the clues on your teammates’ clovers to find as many keywords as possible and get the highest score!
Rules in a minute!
Game contents
220 cards, 6 erasable markers, 6 boards, 1 Hall of Fame, 1 rulebook.​
