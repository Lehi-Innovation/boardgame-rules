## Overview

A cooperative word-association game. Each player secretly writes four Clues connecting pairs of Keywords on their Clover board. The others then reconstruct that board’s original card arrangement. Add everyone’s points and try to beat your team’s high score.

This summary covers standard base-game play. The optional **Add a Challenge** variant is excluded.

## Components

- 220 Keyword cards.
- 6 Clover boards and 6 erasable markers.
- 1 Record of Legends and the rulebook.

## Setup

1. Give each player a Clover board and marker.
2. Shuffle the Keyword cards into a face-down deck within everyone’s reach.
3. Each player draws **4 Keyword cards** and places them **randomly** in their board’s recessed spots. Keep your cards hidden from the other players.

## Turn Structure

The game has two phases:

1. **Choosing Clues — individual phase:** everyone works individually, simultaneously and secretly.
2. **Resolution — team phase:** resolve one player’s board at a time. Its owner is the **Spectator**. All other players look at that board and **work together to reconstruct its original Pairs using the written Clues**. The Spectator **cannot communicate in any way** during that Resolution, including speech, hints or gestures. Each player is Spectator exactly once per game.

Choose the first Spectator when Resolution begins. After resolving that board, the player to the Spectator’s **left** becomes the next Spectator.

## Actions

### Choosing Clues

Pick up your board and look secretly at the **8 Keywords on the outside edges** of its cards. The **2 Keywords below each Clue Zone** form a **Pair**: there are four Pairs.

For each Pair, write one Clue connecting **both** Keywords in the corresponding Clue Zone. Write **4 Clues** in total.

A Clue must be a **single word**; **compound words are allowed**. It may be a common noun, proper noun, acronym, number or onomatopoeia, such as Rabbit, Clooney, FBI, 13 or Bzzzz.

Invalid Clues are:

- Either Keyword itself, or its translation into another language: for example, Amour cannot connect Love and France.
- A word in the same family as a Keyword: Prince cannot connect Princess and Brother.
- A made-up word: Cuppajo cannot connect Mug and Morning.

After writing all four Clues:

1. Remove all four Keyword cards from your board.
2. Draw **1 new card without looking at it**, and shuffle it with those four cards.
3. Put your Clover board down **in front of you**. Put the **5 cards in a face-down pile on top of that board**.

Start Resolution only **after everyone has finished** these steps.

### Resolution and verification

The Spectator puts their **Clover board and all 5 cards face up in the middle of the table**. All other players use the four Clues to reconstruct the original Pairs: put the four original cards into the correct spaces and orientations on the board, leaving the extra card off it.

The guessers may discuss their reasoning and physically try card arrangements to visualize combinations. If they disagree about placement, the player **to the Spectator’s left makes the final decision**. Once they agree on a placement, the Spectator verifies it.

- **First attempt, all Pairs correct:** score **6 points**: 1 per card (4) plus a **2-point first-try bonus**. The board is resolved; no second attempt is needed.
- **First attempt, at least one Pair incorrect:** the Spectator **silently removes the incorrect card(s)** and puts them **in the middle of the table**. The others get a **second and final attempt**. Incorrect orientation also makes a card incorrect, as illustrated in the rulebook’s verification diagram.
- **Second attempt:** once the team agrees on the new placement, the Spectator removes any incorrect cards to the middle of the table. Score **1 point per correct card remaining**, for **0–4 points**, with **no first-try bonus**. There is no third attempt.

After resolving the board, the Spectator turns it over, writes its points in the middle, and **discards all 5 used Keyword cards**. The player to their left becomes the next Spectator and a new Resolution begins.

## Scoring / Victory Conditions

| Result for a board | Points |
|---|---:|
| All four cards correct on the first attempt | 6 |
| First attempt has an error; score after the second attempt | 0–4, one per correct card |

After **every player has been Spectator once**, the game ends. Add all the points written on the boards, record the **team total in the Record of Legends**, and try to beat your previous high score.

## Special Rules & Edge Cases

- Score cards, not individual Keywords or Pairs. A second-attempt perfect board is worth **4**, not 6.
- The Spectator’s prescribed removal of incorrect cards supplies the feedback; they may not explain the mistake or otherwise communicate during Resolution.
- Keep the fifth card mixed with the others until the reveal. The standard game adds exactly one unseen card to each original set of four.
- A difficult Pair still needs a Clue. The rulebook advises writing the first word that comes to mind; all Clue restrictions still apply.

## Player Reference

1. **Set up:** board + marker; randomly place 4 hidden cards.
2. **Together but secretly:** write four legal Clues for the four outside-edge Pairs.
3. **Prepare:** remove cards, add 1 unseen card, shuffle all 5; place your board **in front of you** with all 5 cards in a **face-down pile on top**; wait for everyone.
4. **Resolve:** reveal one Spectator’s board and 5 cards. The Spectator is silent; the others reconstruct the four cards. The player on the Spectator’s left settles disputes.
5. **Check:** first-try perfection = 6; otherwise remove wrong cards silently, allow one final attempt, then score 0–4.
6. **Finish board:** turn over, write points, discard all 5 cards; next Spectator is on the left.
7. **Finish game:** after everyone has been Spectator once, total and record the team’s score.
