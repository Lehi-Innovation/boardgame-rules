Hello everyone!

I'm happy to report that John Company will be in your hands shortly. Behind the scenes, Drew and I have been very busy preparing the game for language localization. Part of this process involves combing through the forums and making sure we can catch and fix any little errors that snuck through our editorial process. 

In the coming weeks, I'll be posting a full list of all of the little errors we caught (there are a dozen or so small typos throughout and a few statements that required clarification or expansion). These errors won't stop you from enjoying the game and they are minor enough that we won't be offering anything like an correction pack. Eventually we'll be providing a link in this thread to our comprehensive tracking document so that you can see them all if you'd like.

In the meantime, I wanted to highlight a few small points of errata.

[size=18]Omissions[/size]
[i]One of the perils of doing a lot of development on TTS is that we automated setup very early in the process and a few setup steps got missed in the rules. Thankfully they are at least fairly easy to remember and implement. [/i]

[b]General Setup:[/b]
+ [New Step] Place the loot tokens in the matching regions.
+ [Adjusted Step 4] Clarify that a "Set of family pieces" also includes the family board. 

[b]1758 Setup:[/b]
+ Place the Bengal control token in the Bengal Presidency. 

[b]1813 Setup:[/b]
+ Place the Bombay/Madras/Bengal control tokens in the Presidencies of the same name.
+ Place the +2/-2 Company Share Value token in the Court of Directors. 

[b]Family Actions[/b]
+ Enlist Officers: The Officers-in-Training box is the box with the Officers-in-Training icon (pistol over building that matches the action icon).
+ Seek Shares: The stock exchange track refers to the track above the Board of Directors. 

[size=18]Clarifications[/size]
[b]Rulebook:[/b]
+Commander Rules: You cannot deploy in regions already associated with another Presidency.
+Region Loss: Regions that are lost are no long associated with their presidency.
+Elephant's March: In the third bullet point (when marching to a region that is already sovereign) the wording has been adjusted to harmonize better with the Imperial Ambitions rule as follows: "If the Elephant faces a region that is already dominated by the acting region (with the Elephant’s tail), use the first clockwise region from that border where this is not true."

[b]Blackmail Cards:[/b]
+ Mercantile and Diplomatic Connection Cards. If you reduce a roll to 0 dice, treat the roll as a failure. 

[b]Law Cards:[/b]
+ Military Oversight. The old commander is a candidate to be the new commander as they are now an officer. 

[b]Rules Clarifications[/b]
+ Out of Family Pieces. Players are now forced to cannibalize their position. The rule now reads: If you need to place a family piece but have none, you must choose to take one from any prize. If taken from a prize, lower your victory point marker by the printed value of that prize. (Note: this rule also applies to creating Firm shares in Deregulation.) 
+ No [Family] Action. A new rule has been added to the family action page. It reads: Rarely, players may be unable to take a family action. In these cases, no action is taken and their opportunity token should be placed in their supply of pieces. In practice this only happens if a player has no family pieces, prizes, or money. 
[size=9][i][size=9][i]There are some truly strange situations that can emerge where players run out of pieces. This would mostly happen in the full campaign game with early deregulation and a unprofitable but still strong-ish Company. To this end I had to adjust the Family Action rules as follows:[/i][/size] Connected to that, I revised the Empty Court rule to cover situations where the game runs out of folks to be Chairman and explicitly state that it is possible for there to be no Chairman. Again, this is a crazy situation that I've never seen in well over 100 tests. But it can happen and so the rule has been added.[/i][/size]

+ Deploy Action. You can only deploy to adjacent regions if they are not already Company-controlled. The new wording now reads: "You may select your President’s home region, a region associated with your Presidency, or a region that shares a border with a region associated with your Presidency. You cannot target another President’s home region or a Company-controlled region associated with another Presidency."

+Region Loss and Closing Orders. This rule now only applies to the Invasion Crisis. This narrowing is because the closing of orders already happens in both the default behavior of the Foreign Invasion and Rebellion events. 

In sum, these are pretty small things. I'll be sure to keep this post up to date with any interesting inquiries I happen to see either on BGG or on the discord. And, if you don't see your favorite typo or minor errata called out above, don't worry! I'll have the full list--I just want to use this thread to call any of the larger game-impacting errors that snuck in.
