This is a large print and play project. I have tried to include all of the necessary files for you to create your own set of John Company: Second Edition. 

Please note:
1. When it comes to things like family pieces and tower levels, I strongly encourage you to just find colored pieces of a roughly appropriate shape. However, for the enterprising, I’ve included the raw vectors and stls.

2. All card backs are presented in a reverse order so that you may print them double sided if you wish. Please note that if you print on two sheets of paper, you will need to find the correct front and back pairing. Because this is more difficult for the Ganjifa tiles, I’ve included a little guide on the backs to make it easier.
