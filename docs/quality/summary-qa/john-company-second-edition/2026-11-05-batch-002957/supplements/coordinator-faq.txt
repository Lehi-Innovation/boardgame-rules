JOHN COMPANY: FAQ and Easily Misremembered Rules
Scenarios
* The short and long 1710 scenarios start with the law “Royal Protection” in play.
Negotiations
* If you make a deal where part of the deal happens immediately, and part happens later, the part that happens later is not binding. Curse the sudden yet inevitable betrayal.
* You cannot trade family members with other players; this includes family members on the Stock Exchange or Commanders.
* When you call in a promise card, you must return that promise card to its owner. E.G. if you call the  £2 or less card, the owner must give you  £2 (or however many they have available), and then you return the promise card to them.
* The promise card for officeholders’ bonus, when applied to a Presidency bonus, does not apply to the Writers placed by the President. E.g. The President trades and fills two orders with Writers. The promise card is called, and the President’s £2 bonus goes to the promise card caller, but each writer still receives their £1 bonus separately.
* The promise card for officeholders’ bonus, when applied to a Governor bonus, receives the sum total of the bonus for all rolls, unless the governor catastrophically fails. E.g. Governor rolls 4 dice and succeeds (+£1), Governor rolls 3 dice and fails, Governor rolls 2 dice and succeeds  (+£2). If the Governor stopped rolling here, the promise card could be called for £3. However, if the Governor continues, rolls 1 dice, catastrophically fails, and is fired, then the promise card cannot be called and the previous Governor’s player will keep their £3 bonus.
* If you trade away an “immature” share (one created this turn), the other person receives an “immature” share. It does not become mature by trading it.
* If you receive a “mature” share in trade, it can be used to invest in a firm this turn.
Combat
* Commanders are not officers, and cannot be exhausted to gain a dice in combat.
* Commander receives trophies equal to the number of tower levels in a conquered region, but only receives 1 trophy when mustering a successful defence.
* All officers + Commander always receive a minimum of £1 loot for every successful deployment, including in already Company Controlled regions. This mostly applies to deploying to open orders or clear unrest in a Company-controlled region, but can occasionally come into play when conquering.
* Each Deploy action is a separate event, all steps 1-3 must be completed before trying another Deploy action. You cannot avoid losses by making another check that is successful. During a successful Deploy action, only the participants of the current invasion receive loot. If there was a previous Deploy that failed, those participants receive no loot. This is an exception to the reroll rules on page 16, as the troops involved have to be managed as the resources required.
* Whenever the Company is attacked for any reason (rebellion crisis, invasion crisis, leader event, Foreign Invasion), all regions with any unrest cubes rebel
   * Roll officer death checks after every deployment, regardless of success or failure. For each 6 (not 5), an officer is eliminated. Eliminated officers do not receive loot or count toward the minimum loot level.
* Do not roll officer death checks when mounting defence, only on region loss.
* Roll a separate officer death check for every region loss. This could mean the same officer might be routed multiple times.
* The Company can deploy to regions adjacent to a region they control, even if they do not have control of a Presidency home region.
Family Actions
* You must take a Family Action if able.
* If you cannot take any available Family Actions (because of Spouses and Money), you forgo that Family Action.
Company Offices
* Presidents only get one opportunity to perform their actions. They can attempt to trade as many times if they fail (but not catastrophically fail). However, they may not attempt to trade, have a governor take their actions, then attempt to trade again.
* Presidents are hired by the Director of Trade (or Governor General), not by the Chairman.
* The Governor General earns the company £3 per company-controlled region at the start of the Governor General's turn. This occurs regardless of whether the officeholder rolls any dice or not.
* The Governor General takes 1 action per company-controlled region, but the money, regiments and ships they generate can be distributed among presidencies however they like. For example, at the start of 1813 they could build three ships in the East or give the president of Bombay £6 of tax money, etc.
* During the Revenue phase, the company does not lose standing if its balance is equal to expectations after paying expenses. However, in order to raise its standing, total dividends paid must be greater than expectations.
* The Governor of Bengal (or any other Governor associated with the Bengal Presidency) cannot place a ship in China. However, the Governor General can.
* During the hiring phase, only vacant offices are filled. This includes the Chairman. You cannot have a Chairman election unless the office is vacant.
* The Director of Trade can move ships both into AND out of the Superintendent of Trade in China’s treasury.
* The Chairman can only remain vacant during hiring in the very rare situation during the 1710 Campaign and low player counts. This only occurs when the Court of Directors is empty, the former Chairman is fired, and the former Chairman has no family pieces left in their player area or on prizes. In this situation, the game proceeds with no Chairman. The company may NOT allocate its balance, take debt, or pay dividends. Any positions the Chairman would hire are not hired. All other decisions the Chairman would have to make are made by the former Chairman. Such as:
   * Wielding the gavel/bell and asking players to make orderly decisions.
   * Deciding order of Governors/Commanders if a President is vacant.
   * Deciding which officer to exhaust on defence if both a Commander and Military affairs are vacant.
   * Deciding ties in Initiative if a President is vacant.
   * Deciding to use Royal Protection.
   * An AI card is still flipped for the Crown when skipping a vacant Chairman. 
Events
* Whenever an invasion is successful, the elephant's tail will stay in the attacker's region and rotate to find a different target. After all other crisis events and whenever the elephant marches, it will move to the region on the top card of the event deck.
   * If an empire is fully formed, the elephant will be facing towards the capital to represent a rebellion.
   * When an invasion fails, the attacker loses a tower level. When a rebellion fails, the defender loses a tower level. (In other words, both situations punish the relevant imperial capital.)
* Unrest strength is added for a “Foreign Invasion” event when it causes an attack against the Company. However, if the attack is successful, the unrest strength is not added for determining the new tower level.
* If a Company dominated region loses to a “Foreign Invasion” event, all orders in the region will only be closed once (not twice). This will only cause a Cascade if all orders were closed before the event. (official errata)
* If a Company dominated home region is lost, the Company can continue to control other regions they can hang onto. E.G. If the company has Delhi and Bengal, but loses Bengal, they can continue to control Delhi.
* When the Elephant marches, it uses the symbol on the “inside” of the border to determine which region it crosses into. As an example, if the top of the stack shows Delhi and the symbol on the event card is a circle, then the Elephant will move from the Delhi circle symbol towards its immediate neighbour, i.e. crossing the border from Delhi into Maratha.
Prestige and Blackmail
* Blackmail cards that are discarded (not picked up, or purposely discarded) are discarded facedown.
* 4-vote prestige cards such as the “Labour Newspaper” can be utilised when voting on a law based on the policy chosen by the Prime Minister, regardless of the icon on the law itself.
* The “Hiring Preference” blackmail card cannot be used to force the election of the Chairman.
* The “Hiring Preference” blackmail card can be used if the Prime Minister utilises the “Military Oversight” law to appoint a Commander.
* The “Foundry” is not triggered by “Military” Affairs transferring a regiment, only new regiment creation.
* The family that obtains “Joseph Sedley” spouse does not discard or trade away family members already on the Stock Exchange until they become shares. They may trade them away when that happens (and must do so immediately at that time.
* If you have the “Becky Sharp” spouse, and use her ability to draw from the top of the prestige card deck, you must take the card and not discard it, even if the new card is a spouse that affects you negatively.
* The “Company Fraud” blackmail card can only be played at the start of the Revenue phase. (The TTS version of the card is out of date.
* The “Company Fraud” blackmail card may be used before or after the Company Balance penalty from the “Calico Acts” law.
London Season
* Players take prestige cards in order of money spent on retirement.
* Ties for money spent on retirement are broken first by the number of windows, then clockwise in seating order from the Prime Minister.
Parliament Meets
* Enterprise cards from the London Season are subject to bonuses and taxes just like workshops, shipyards and luxuries.
* Each luxury has a window icon on it, meaning their holders must spend £1 for each during a window tax and must spend £2 for each during the Luxury Tax + Window Tax policies. The “Scottish Castle” has 2 window icons on it, meaning the owner must spend £3 during the Luxury Tax + Window Tax.
* If multiple passed laws would allow you to take extra family actions, only the most recently-passed one is active. The older laws are not discarded, just the extra action portion is disabled.
* The "Tenure Limits" law does not grant an extra "Enlist Writer" family action. It gives you a single writer if you do NOT take the "Enlist Writer" action.
* Ships selected by the "War Against France" law can be selected even if it is controlled by a firm or is in China. Commanders may not be selected.
* When a Commander is demoted by the “Military Oversight” law, they become an officer candidate for hiring immediately. If Nepotism and a lack of consent means that the same officer is the only viable candidate, such is bureaucracy.
* “Writer Graft Reform” only provides the Company £1 for each player Writer, not for filled order tokens.
* “Treasury Reform” means Commanders, Officers, Regiments, and Local Alliances no longer get loot bonuses.
* “Trade Regulations” means Presidents no longer receive their bonus.
* If the selected policy for “Window Tax Repeal” when it is passed includes a window tax, that window tax is paid. The policy is enacted before the law takes effect.
* If the selected policy for “Calico Acts” is a tax on workshops, then the workshop tax is paid before the £2 is received, as this is an “immediate effect”. This could cause someone to lose their workshop before they receive the £2 bonus.
* The “Tenure Limits” law only affects the London Season, so the Chairman does not automatically retire during Final Retirement. The Chairman will have to roll for attrition.
* The “Deregulation” law provides the Prime Minister a Passed Law trophy when it is successfully passed.
* When the “Deregulation” law is “set aside”, it means to put it alongside the board. It is not shuffled into the deck.
* If the “Deregulation” law fails, it is “set aside” again. The Prime Minister could potentially put it up for a vote again in later turns.
* In the rare instance where a vote fails with no opposition, the Prime Minister position stays and the initial policy is the one which is enacted.
* When a law has multiple tax policies, such as Shipping and a Window tax, all are applied simultaneously. If you cannot afford all your taxes, you can pick which taxed items to lose. Such as between a Prize or a Shipyard.
* The type of policy that qualifies for the “Labor Newspaper” / “Social Magazine” is the policy the hand on the Prime Minister’s wheel is pointing at. The Law card has instructions for the Prime Minister on which policy they select, but only the selected policy matters for these enterprises.
Firms
* When a family flips to the firm side, place the family’s opportunity marker on the same family action where it was before.
* Firms must have at least 1 ship to trade.
* Firms may fill 1 more order than the number of ships they have.
* Firms always make their Trade roll at the start of the relevant President’s turn (before any Company actions are taken).
   * If the President successfully Trades, Firms trade at the same time (in initiative order).
   * If the President fails to Trade for any reason, Firms trade at the end of the President’s turn (after the Commander and any Governors have acted).
* Firms may fill orders already filled by any President or firm, Presidents may fill orders already filled by any firm, but Presidents may not fill an order filled by another President. Use the red dividers to keep them separate.
* A firm with a value of 1 can receive emergency investments. Emergency investments cannot lower the value of firm shares lower than 1, any further lowered just stays at one.
* Firms spend their bid money even if they cannot trade because their chosen port is closed.
* If a firm is dissolved on purpose during the Firms phase, all money in the treasury is lost to the bank, ships are returned to their shipyards, and firm shares are sent to debtors' prison.
* Immature shares may not be used to invest in a firm during expenses as an emergency investment. Shares do not mature until the Upkeep and Refresh phase.
* Firms can only be willingly dissolved during the Firms phase.
The Crown
* As Chairman, The Crown will always try to pay dividends once before they check for how much money they want left in the Company.
* When the Governor General acts, whether this is by a player or by the Crown, treat each action as independent of the next when considering what type of action is taken and the favors exchanged by that action. Note also, that the -2 cubes favor allows a player to choose the region/presidency for the action, as well as the type of action.
* Governor General counts as a Governor when the Crown is considering passing the “Zamindar Taxes” law.
* “Board of Control” law when Crown is PM: The Crown will use the ability at the first opportunity ( so it will be whichever office is furthest up the promotion tree. i.e. first to act)
* "War Against France" law when Crown is PM: The Crown will only remove regiments last. For clarity, it would be written: "If the Crown is Prime Minister. They will select player ships/officers, then Company ships, then Crown ships/officers, then regiments."
* The Crown will always exhaust a local alliance first, even if they are targeting a lower number. Eg. if they want 2 dice, and have a 3 Strength local alliance, they will exhaust the local alliance first, and roll 3 dice.
* When a player Chairman gives money to a Crown office as a favor, they must give the entire amount of the money for the favor to a single office: e.g. Crown is in Bull climate, player Chairman gives £2 to three different Crown offices and receives no promise cubes. Alternatively, Crown is in Bull climate, player Chairman gives £3 to two different Crown offices and receives two promise cubes..
* If a Crown Governor General builds Company Ships, it uses the AI card only to determine where they go.
* In 2P you may deny the Crown's investment without paying a promise cube..
* In 2P what happens when one player has a firm and the player without a firm has the player button during Peacock climate?
   * The Crown will still attempt to invest in the player with the firm. The Player button is for if both players have a firm, then the Crown will invest once each in order of the Player button. (In case it runs out of shares/workshops)
* In 2P, nepotism to the Crown is never mandatory.
* In 2P, giving the Crown your consent for nepotism gives you +1 promise cube even if the other player is not a potential candidate.
* In 2P, if both players are candidates and only one player gives consent for nepotism, neither player receives +1 promise cube. The Crown must be successfully hired to provide this favor.
* When can you trade shares with the Crown?
   * "Once the New Company Shares step is complete (and not before), you may: take/give a share to The Crown."
   * After Firms, just before you begin Hiring.
* A player Governor General does not get a promise cube for putting a ship in a Crown Superintendent of China.
* If the Crown uses Royal Privilege to take emergency loans, company standing can drop. This is cumulative and concurrent with the Public Embarrassment of the Company taking emergency loans during expenses. IE: If the Company takes out 3 Emergency loans during expenses, Standing is lowered twice. If the Crown uses Royal Privilege and takes out more Emergency Loans that turn, Standing is not lowered further.
