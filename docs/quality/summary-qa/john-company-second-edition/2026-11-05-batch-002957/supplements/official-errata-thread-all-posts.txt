Hello everyone!

I'm happy to report that John Company will be in your hands shortly. Behind the scenes, Drew and I have been very busy preparing the game for language localization. Part of this process involves combing through the forums and making sure we can catch and fix any little errors that snuck through our editorial process. 

In the coming weeks, I'll be posting a full list of all of the little errors we caught (there are a dozen or so small typos throughout and a few statements that required clarification or expansion). These errors won't stop you from enjoying the game and they are minor enough that we won't be offering anything like an correction pack. Eventually we'll be providing a link in this thread to our comprehensive tracking document so that you can see them all if you'd like.

In the meantime, I wanted to highlight a few small points of errata.

[size=18]Omissions[/size]
[i]One of the perils of doing a lot of development on TTS is that we automated setup very early in the process and a few setup steps got missed in the rules. Thankfully they are at least fairly easy to remember and implement. [/i]

[b]General Setup:[/b]
+ [New Step] Place the loot tokens in the matching regions.
+ [Adjusted Step 4] Clarify that a "Set of family pieces" also includes the family board. 

[b]1758 Setup:[/b]
+ Place the Bengal control token in the Bengal Presidency. 

[b]1813 Setup:[/b]
+ Place the Bombay/Madras/Bengal control tokens in the Presidencies of the same name.
+ Place the +2/-2 Company Share Value token in the Court of Directors. 

[b]Family Actions[/b]
+ Enlist Officers: The Officers-in-Training box is the box with the Officers-in-Training icon (pistol over building that matches the action icon).
+ Seek Shares: The stock exchange track refers to the track above the Board of Directors. 

[size=18]Clarifications[/size]
[b]Rulebook:[/b]
+Commander Rules: You cannot deploy in regions already associated with another Presidency.
+Region Loss: Regions that are lost are no long associated with their presidency.
+Elephant's March: In the third bullet point (when marching to a region that is already sovereign) the wording has been adjusted to harmonize better with the Imperial Ambitions rule as follows: "If the Elephant faces a region that is already dominated by the acting region (with the Elephant’s tail), use the first clockwise region from that border where this is not true."

[b]Blackmail Cards:[/b]
+ Mercantile and Diplomatic Connection Cards. If you reduce a roll to 0 dice, treat the roll as a failure. 

[b]Law Cards:[/b]
+ Military Oversight. The old commander is a candidate to be the new commander as they are now an officer. 

[b]Rules Clarifications[/b]
+ Out of Family Pieces. Players are now forced to cannibalize their position. The rule now reads: If you need to place a family piece but have none, you must choose to take one from any prize. If taken from a prize, lower your victory point marker by the printed value of that prize. (Note: this rule also applies to creating Firm shares in Deregulation.) 
+ No [Family] Action. A new rule has been added to the family action page. It reads: Rarely, players may be unable to take a family action. In these cases, no action is taken and their opportunity token should be placed in their supply of pieces. In practice this only happens if a player has no family pieces, prizes, or money. 
[size=9][i][size=9][i]There are some truly strange situations that can emerge where players run out of pieces. This would mostly happen in the full campaign game with early deregulation and a unprofitable but still strong-ish Company. To this end I had to adjust the Family Action rules as follows:[/i][/size] Connected to that, I revised the Empty Court rule to cover situations where the game runs out of folks to be Chairman and explicitly state that it is possible for there to be no Chairman. Again, this is a crazy situation that I've never seen in well over 100 tests. But it can happen and so the rule has been added.[/i][/size]

+ Deploy Action. You can only deploy to adjacent regions if they are not already Company-controlled. The new wording now reads: "You may select your President’s home region, a region associated with your Presidency, or a region that shares a border with a region associated with your Presidency. You cannot target another President’s home region or a Company-controlled region associated with another Presidency."

+Region Loss and Closing Orders. This rule now only applies to the Invasion Crisis. This narrowing is because the closing of orders already happens in both the default behavior of the Foreign Invasion and Rebellion events. 

In sum, these are pretty small things. I'll be sure to keep this post up to date with any interesting inquiries I happen to see either on BGG or on the discord. And, if you don't see your favorite typo or minor errata called out above, don't worry! I'll have the full list--I just want to use this thread to call any of the larger game-impacting errors that snuck in.

For the vast majority of players, the points above should address all of the outstanding errata. However, in the interest of full disclosure, here's the current internal errata document.

Note that this document is fairly technical, and mostly intended for use by myself and our language partners as we maintain the game's files. It's long and scary and most of the adjustments here are pretty minor. I'll continue to update both this document as well as this thread with any changes. And, after we finish our language localizations, I will be sure to upload a fresh 2nd Printing rulebook which has these changes folded in (I suspect we'll have this  by Christmas or sooner). Again, this document isn't the kind of thing you'd want to print and stick in the box as it's not really intended for use by players, espeically during play. But, it's here all the same in case you'd like to see how the sausage gets made.

It plays very well physically and has a dramatically faster setup time than other games in it's weight class (usually only 5 to 10 minutes or so). If those are your worries, I'm happy to assuage them.

I'll add that the omissions are indeed illustrative, but of perhaps other things than your concerns. The loot tokens got left off because they have no other place to be, and they likewise contribute to the other omissions. They are, in a sense, mechanical markers and correspond to the game state. So, for instance, in 1758 there is a governor of Bengal which means the loot/control piece needs to be in the associated presidency. This is obvious to folks who know the rules and is therefore easy to forget in setup instructions. The share value omission is the only one that cannot be inferred from the system rules and is indeed an oversight owing to TTS, but it's far from fiddly.

After reading the rules and the full errata document, I have a some more suggested errata.
Two caveats:
1. Some errata are clarifications in the form of repetitions of rules mentioned elsewhere. It seems to me that the rule book adopts that repetitive style anyways, and my suggestions are made whenever I felt that an absence of repetition might hinder understanding for novice players or when playing a new scenario for the first time (e.g. with private firms). 
2. The errata are based on my best understanding of the rules. If I’m (dead) wrong on some of them, please let me know so I can adjust for my future games!
3. Though I’ve tried to provide new sentences for the rules, you might come up with better phrasing!

Since page 9 will be the go-to reference text at the end of a game I feel that it should contain all possible ways to score or lose VPs. To that end, here are 2 suggested clarifications:
[b]Power track, p.9[/b]
Players should be reminded that Prestige cards with enterprise icons should be included when totaling power for the power award: though mentioned p.3 and then p.12, people will easily overlook it if only referencing p9.
Here's a suggested sentence: "Remember to count any Prestige cards you own and which have an enterprise icon."
Score courts and workshops, p.9
Players should be reminded to count private company shares for VPs, and also that some players might have one special retirement if they have a share in a private firm: the paragraph "Firms in final scoring" p.37  should essentially be included p.9, perhaps with a modified title:  "[u]Private[/u] firms in Final scoring" and should begin with a sentence to clarify, such as: "when Deregulation is in play and one or more private firms have been created, etc."
Or at least, the section should remind you to refer to p.37 for scoring purposes if playing with Deregulation.

[b]Prestige cards, p.12[/b]
Players should be reminded that they can only peek at remaining blackmail cards and not all of them. As is, the phrasing is ambiguous. The second paragraph sentence might be altered to: “when choosing their card, the acting player is welcome to peek at any blackmail card remaining in the London Season Display.”

The Compa[b][/b]ny’s Structure, p.15
In the caption, the Army Position rectangle is currently filled in black. It might instead be filled in green to match the color used in the structure.

[b]General hiring example, p.16[/b]
the sentence “the consent of both Yellow and Blue thanks to the Nepotism rule” should read “ the consent of both Yellow and Blue because of the Nepotism rule.”

[b]Lease extra ships, p.19[/b]
It should be clarified that extra ships can always be leased, even if there are player ships to be fitted.
“The Manager of Shipping may always lease extra ships, if they have the necessary funds.”

[b]Form Governorship, p.23[/b]
Players might be reminded that the steps to form Governorship are skipped if the Governor General is in play.
“Note: skip those steps if Governor General is in play (see p.27).”

[b]Expenses, p.27[/b]
the sentence  “do not include Commanders” should also include “or local alliances”.

[b]Voting and Prime Minister’s Honor, p.35[/b]
It should be made clear that though the Prime Minister cannot vote against the current law, they can abstain, otherwise the last sentence in the first paragraph at the top of the page: “If no votes were cast in the round the vote must be resolved,” does not make sense. It therefore might be clarified with the following sentence:
“Each player (including the Prime Minister) may decide to vote or abstain. If no votes were cast in the round, the vote must be resolved.”

[b]Mergers, p.39[/b]
It might be useful to clearly state that the primary firm’s value will remain the same after the merger has taken place (and will not be a combination of its value + that of the secondary firms):
“After the merger is complete, adjust the new firm’s value to that of the previous primary firm.”

[b]Filling Orders as a Firm, p.40[/b]
The sentence in the second paragraph, “Orders are filled after the President has made a successful roll while taking the Trade Action,” is to me ambiguous, because the adjective “successful” might imply that no trading is possible for private firms if the President fails the roll check for the Trade Action. It might be rephrased: 
“Orders are filled after the President has made a roll check while taking the Trade Action.” To be overkill, it might be added that “Private firms may trade even though the President’s roll check was a failure or catastrophic failure.”

[b]Firm Expenses and Dissolution, p.41[/b]
A firm’s manager behaves much as the Chairman does. However, the Chairman counts as a share in the Company, while the firm’s manager does, but only for dividends purposes. So it might be confusing to players.
To make this clear, I would add the following note to the following sentence:
“Pay £1 for each share in the firm’s Share box or pay £ equal to the firm value, whichever is higher.” Note: the firm’s manager does not count as a share for firm expenses, only for dividends.

[b]Hobnobbing firms, p.42[/b]
It seems unclear when to (or not to) reset the firms’ dividend markers to zero. I would rephrase the paragraph: 
“After all regions’ trading actions have been taken, any firm that attempted to trade and did not pay dividends this turn should reset their firm’s dividend marker to zero. Only firms that selected the “Hobnob” strategy should keep their firm’s dividend marker in its present state.”

That's it for me. Hope it helps

I think you are confusing the firm manager's share that they created by placing a family piece in the box when they created the firm, with the extra share of dividends they get paid out, which is printed on the firm board. That initial share [i]is[/i] a share in every way. 
That the firm manager has an extra share of dividends doesn't mean that they have an extra share in the company in any other way. It doesn't count towards the ten share limit, it doesn't count towards a majority, etc etc, so calling it a share at all is a mistake. You [i]act[/i] during dividend payouts as [i]if[/i] the firm manager has an extra share. In other words, they don't have an extra share. You just act for one step of the game as if they do.

Regarding ships, I'm now under the impression that their voting power is only applicable if they are fitted (thanks to Ricky Royals excellent videos), however, in looking through the most current PDF (2nd printing) available, I feel there is some room for further clarification around this.  Specifically:

p.35 2nd paragraph which reads:
[q]A player’s votes come from several sources. Each £1 they spend from their family
treasury is worth 1 vote. In addition, any card in their play area is worth votes
equal to the number of vote icons on the card. When casting these votes, rotate
the card to indicate it has been used.
It will be rotated back after the vote is resolved.[/q]

Which, because those icons are visible I had previously been using.  The the ambiguity I felt, as from p.13 for purchase Shipyard which reads:
[q][b]Purchase Shipyard[/b]
Pay £2 to the bank and take one shipyard.
Place the matching ship token on the
shipyard in the space indicated. Shipyards provide a £1 bonus each turn if their
ship has been fitted and provide a single vote in the Parliament Meets phase.
Each
shipyard counts as a shipping enterprise.[/q]

If the above interpretation of the rule is correct, I would suggest adding something within the voting section somethin similar to what was done in the Bonus's section p.27 which reads:
[q][b]Bonuses[/b]
During this phase, players collect bonuses. Every player should take £1 for [b]each
shipyard with a fitted ship[/b] and each workshop they own.[/q]

This is of course, based on my understanding which quite likely could still be wrong.  The Purchase Shipyard wording is likely where the ambiguity sits, as that could be interpreted as both:
If fitted, provides £1 and a vote
or
£1 if fitted, then a vote (regardless)

And while on my wishful thinking spree and again based on my understanding, perhaps moving the vote icon along with the £1 icon within the triangle so it is not revealed when the ship is not fitted.
