Types of Changes
(Note: some of these may fit in more than one category. This taxonomy is not meant to be perfect.)
Typo: Small adjustments, mostly of tense or spelling. A few of these rise to the level of rules corrections (if the typo could cause players to play incorrectly).
Omission: Something that was mistakenly left out of the rules.
Rule Change: An added or adjusted rule that has some ramifications on play. 
Clarification: Usually this means expanding something that was previously only implied or was reworded for clarity or as a reminder.
