It does not permit a partial-bank pass, an early final answer file, reduced asked-fact scope, a replacement blind actor or factual feedback. ## Why the scheduling contract is consistent

V10 line 35 fixes the actor/context, original bank, ordered next unfinished question and ten-additional-question ceiling. Line 37 still forbids prefilling within a turn and requires genuine source reading and manual maps/cases. Line 45 requires per-ID source records during the work. Line 47 distinguishes an ordered prefix from a full draft, prohibits an early final file, limits the progress response to count/next ID/own paths, and promises generic continuation without facts, source maps, keys or grades. Line 49 conditions finalization on all N ordered unique entries and records and preserves the first full draft.

There is no contradiction between the general source-only prohibition and generic scheduling messages: line 47 explicitly authorizes the latter as control messages, while later factual work must derive only from the original two inputs and the reader's own files. “Earlier responses/history” must not be interpreted to prohibit the same actor's explicitly permitted own draft or turn continuity; it prohibits importing other answers or prior experiments. A resumed actor is initially fresh once, then continues its own work. Creating a replacement actor with a condensed factual handoff would be a different process.

At most ten is a ceiling, not a quota or a completeness certificate. A one-question bank can finish in one turn, a ten-question bank can finish after ten, and an eleven-question bank requires another turn. An early interruption can preserve fewer. A repeated zero-progress turn is not an answer-bank success and supplies no guarantee of eventual completion.

## Required operational distinctions

**O1 — Turn-final is a progress event, not a task verdict.** A normal final-channel response may mark the actor idle in its transport; the parent must explicitly resume that same actor/chat until the original full bank and records exist. Do not equate a child turn's final status or reported prefix count with whole-task completion. Do not run final evaluation on a prefix or create `reader-{role}.json` for one. Completion requires actual all-N files and records, followed by the required independent full-bank asked-fact grading. The proposal states these requirements; no deployed orchestration was inspected here.

**O2 — Keep the original bank for completeness checks.** The unchanged checker can return `all_structure_and_declared_source_spans_valid=true` for a ten-entry prefix of a 23-question neutral bank while `exact_ordered_unique_ids=false`. Passing a shortened ten-question list instead makes the ordered-ID flag true. Therefore always retain the original full question list/N, require both flags for final mechanical acceptance, and independently verify source-record coverage. Do not slice the bank to the current turn or rename structural success as a partial factual pass. The checker neither counts work per turn nor reads source-check records or progress messages.

**O3 — Resume from persisted completed work, not from the message alone.** A safe completed prefix has exactly the original ordered unique answer IDs and source records covering those completed IDs. Record serialization order is immaterial. An interrupted question is unfinished; do not advance merely because a partially written entry or a count says it was attempted. Reconcile mismatches between draft and self-check from the reader's own inputs/work before proceeding; never synthesize missing semantic work. The two files are not atomically written together. Preserve genuine incomplete work where permitted, while keeping it clearly outside the completed-prefix count. The neutral model tests conservative prefix bookkeeping, not the sufficiency of any real source record.

**O4 — Freeze the first actual full draft immediately.** The first time the draft contains all N entries, its bytes are the first full draft to preserve, even if finalization later takes another turn. Source records must also be complete before final acceptance. Do not reserialize, reorder, prettify, add review metadata to or repair that draft after it becomes full. Load it to form the separate final file and record actual changes/reasons in the permitted self-check/final review. A digest recorded in an allowed own output can help detect accidental changes; do not introduce additional reader output paths. The neutral fixture shows that a JSON-equivalent reserialization changes bytes, and that final repairs can leave the draft unchanged.

If all N entries/records exist but finalization is interrupted, the next unfinished question ID is null; continue finalization rather than inventing another question or overwriting the first full draft. It would help to explicitly allow this `next_unfinished_id:null` progress state in future wording, but line 49 already supplies the required finalization action.

**O5 — Initial receipt is a global gate.** Line 5 remains unconditional: full original source/question receipt and truncation recovery precede any output write. Line 35 reiterates this for the first turn. If initial delivery itself spans turns or a first turn writes nothing, it must not become a loophole to write early later. Clarifying “before the first output write in any turn” would remove that possible misreading without changing the contract. The bookkeeping fixture assumes a receipt-complete flag; it cannot verify genuine visible reading.

## Blindness-preserving continuations

Use a fixed generic continuation for both actors, apart from established role/path substitutions. For example:

> Continue this same blind task under the unchanged protocol. Resume the next unfinished question from your own draft. Complete at most ten additional ordered questions, using only the original two inputs and your own three files. If all entries and per-ID records already exist, preserve the first complete draft and finish finalization. A partial turn is progress only; the entire original bank remains required.

This message supplies no answer facts, chosen units, failed IDs, key scopes, grades, hints, substantive corrections or another reader's work. Do not adapt continuation wording or stopping decisions to observed factual performance. Continue because unfinished work remains, and keep grading feedback out of the blind context. Counts/next IDs reported by the reader are bookkeeping derived from its original bank and files; they do not prove semantic completeness. Same-context identity must be maintained by the actual orchestration, which this review did not inspect. A constant string alone cannot establish absence of hidden context leakage.

## Neutral evidence and limits

Run `python3 -B fixtures/neutral_scheduling.py` from this directory. It uses only synthetic IDs and a newly authored neutral label passage to exercise the unchanged checker's prefix flags. It tests zero/ten/twenty/full prefixes; missing, extra, skipped, duplicate and reordered IDs/records; premature final files; the ten-question delta; early stops; initial-receipt gating; prefix regression; schedules of 1, 10, 11, 20 and 23 IDs; first-full-draft byte preservation; opaque same-actor identity; constant continuation text; and the shortened-bank pitfall.

The scheduling functions are a review-only reference model of necessary bookkeeping checks. They are not an adopted runtime implementation and do not prove that a reader actually read sources, chose relevant units, resolved cases, wrote genuine source records, complied with a turn cap or remained blind. The fixed-ID test loops do not route actual questions or produce actual answers. The ordinary V9 structural/semantic limits are unchanged. Every actual asked fact and same-entry qualifier/conclusion still requires strict independent full-bank grading; no factual score or cap follows from scheduling or schema bookkeeping.
