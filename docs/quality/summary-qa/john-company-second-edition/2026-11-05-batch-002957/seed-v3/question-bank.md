## Q001

During attrition, how does an office roll resolve, what extra modifier does the Chairman have, and where do a retiring member, card and fatigue go?

**Answer:** Roll separately for each office card, adding 1 for each fatigue on that card and another 1 for the Chairman. A result of 1–2 does nothing; 3–4 adds fatigue; 5 or more retires the member. Move the retiring member to Pensioners, flip the card into Vacant Offices, and return its fatigue to the supply.

**Fixed key (verbatim):** Roll separately per office card, +1 per fatigue and an additional +1 for Chairman. 1–2 nothing, 3–4 add fatigue, 5+ retire: member to Pensioners, card flipped into Vacant Offices, fatigue returned to supply.

**Source:** Rules p11; extraction lines 898–916

**Coverage:** Attrition thresholds and destinations.

## Q002

Who may select London Season cards, in what order and with which tiebreakers? What happens to the display and unused pensioners afterward?

**Answer:** Each player who retires at least one pensioner must take or discard one card from the display. Players act in descending order of actual total retirement spending, with ties broken by most total windows and then clockwise order from the Prime Minister. Players may peek at blackmail cards in the display. Stop selections when the display is empty. Discard any remaining display cards and refill it with three cards, with blackmail facedown and prestige faceup, even if nobody took a card. Return unretired pensioners to the supply.

**Fixed key (verbatim):** Each player retiring at least one pensioner must take or discard one display card, descending actual total retirement spending; ties most total windows then PM clockwise order. May peek at display blackmails. Stop when display empty. Discard remaining display and refill three, blackmail down/prestige up even if none taken. Return unretired pensioners to supply.

**Source:** Rules pp11–12; lines 917–928,1002–1014

**Coverage:** Retirement rewards, turn order and refresh.

## Q003

How do the opportunity marker and extra-action laws affect family actions? If four offices are vacant and the marker already sits on Enlist Writer, how many writers can be placed?

**Answer:** Take the selected normal action once, or twice if the opportunity marker was already there at the start of the phase, and move the marker to the main action. Only the latest passed extra-action law may be used. Its extra action receives no marker doubling and does not move the marker. Four or more vacant offices allow one extra writer once per turn, so repeating Enlist Writer can place three writers in total.

**Fixed key (verbatim):** Normal selected action once, or twice if marker already there at phase start; move marker to main action. Only latest passed extra-action law usable, extra action gets no marker doubling and does not move marker. Four or more vacancies allow one extra writer once per turn, so repeated Enlist Writer can place three total.

**Source:** Rules p13; lines 1076–1104

**Coverage:** Family repetition and extra writer stacking.

## Q004

After Seek Share actions, how are new Company shares created and remaining applicants moved? Where was the initial purchase money paid?

**Answer:** For Seek Share, pay the printed price of an empty Stock Exchange space to the bank. Once there is no Debt, move remaining applicants as far right as possible while preserving their order. The purchase money does not enter a Company treasury.

## Q005

When hiring, how are vacancies ordered, what consent does hiring your own family require, what happens to promoted fatigue, and what if no candidate or hirer exists?

**Answer:** Fill vacancies in ascending order of the office numbers on the card backs. Insert any vacancy created by promotion into that same order. Hiring your own family member requires verbal consent from every other player represented in the candidate pool, except in a Chairman election. Move a promoted member's fatigue to the new card. If there is no candidate, or the hiring office is vacant, skip the office: it remains vacant and takes no actions, including hiring.

**Fixed key (verbatim):** Ascending back-of-card office numbers, insert promotion vacancy in ascending order; own-family hiring needs verbal consent of every other player represented in candidate pool, except Chairman election. Promoted fatigue travels to new card. No candidate or vacant hiring office means skip office; remains vacant and takes no actions including hiring.

**Source:** Rules p14; lines 1146–1169

**Coverage:** General hiring exceptions.

## Q006

How is a Chairman elected, how does a deadlocked election resolve, and does the elected Chairman piece remain a tradable Company share?

**Answer:** A Chairman candidate must own a Company share and obtain the consent of players controlling a pure majority, meaning more than half, of the current shares. The first candidate to obtain that support is elected. If the election deadlocks, the former Chairman chooses any shareholder, including themself, without Nepotism consent. The winner moves one share into the Chairman slot. It remains a share but cannot be traded or used for firm investment.

**Fixed key (verbatim):** Candidate must own share, needs consent of players controlling pure majority (>half) current shares; first to achieve support elected. Deadlock former Chairman picks any shareholder including self without Nepotism. Winner moves one share into Chairman slot; still share but cannot trade it or use it for firm investment.

**Source:** Rules p15; lines 1175–1190

**Coverage:** Election and Chairman share restrictions.

## Q007

Explain success-check results, payment, retries, catastrophic destinations and the restriction against dead actions.

**Answer:** Commit money from the office treasury and the required resources, gather the dice, and then subtract the penalty. Read the lowest die: 1–2 succeeds; 3–4 fails and permits a retry if sufficient resources remain; 5–6 is catastrophic. On catastrophe, the office takes no further actions, the officeholder returns to the supply, the card is flipped into Vacant Offices, and fatigue returns to the supply, subject to specific office exceptions. An action must have an effect or a chance of success.

**Fixed key (verbatim):** Commit office treasury money/resources, gather dice then subtract penalty; lowest die 1–2 success, 3–4 failure retry if sufficient resources, 5–6 catastrophe: no more office actions, officeholder to supply, card flipped to Vacant Offices, fatigue to supply (specific office exceptions apply). No action without effect or chance of success.

**Source:** Rules p17; lines 1330–1365

**Coverage:** Shared check procedure.

## Q008

What are the Chairman’s voluntary borrowing and allocation duties, including consent and track limits?

**Answer:** The Chairman may advance Debt three times freely, adding £5 to Company Balance each time. Every further advance requires consent from the majority of shareholders, counting the Chairman share, and Debt cannot pass the end of its track. The Chairman must allocate all Company Balance to other office treasuries and reduce Balance accordingly. Borrowing may be interleaved with allocation while respecting the limit.

**Fixed key (verbatim):** May advance Debt three times freely, +£5 Balance each. Each further advancement needs majority-shareholder consent including Chairman share; cannot pass Debt track end. Must allocate all Company Balance to other office treasuries, reducing Balance accordingly; borrowing may interleave allocation while respecting limit.

**Source:** Rules p18; lines 1403–1416

**Coverage:** Borrowing and budget allocation.

## Q009

What must Shipping do with its budget? State ship costs, destinations, consent requirements and the prerequisite for buying Company ships.

**Answer:** Shipping must spend until at most £2 remains. Leasing an extra ship costs £2, fitting a ship from a shipyard costs £3 and requires no owner consent, and buying a Company ship costs £5 and is allowed only when no ships remain on shipyards. Place acquired ships in any sea zone; China becomes eligible once created. Return extra ships at Refresh.

**Fixed key (verbatim):** Spend until at most £2 remains. Lease extra £2, fit shipyard ship £3 without owner consent, buy Company £5 only with no ships remaining on shipyards. Place acquired ships in any sea zone (China eligible once created). Extras return at Refresh.

**Source:** Rules pp19,25; lines 1457–1471,1808–1815

**Coverage:** Mandatory Shipping spending and options.

## Q013

How does Governor Administer work across repeated attempts, including bonuses, tax unrest and stopping?

**Answer:** Begin with the printed dice pool. Roll that pool without a penalty and remove one die after every attempt. Success gives a £1 bonus, plus another £1 if the preceding roll failed. Choose to build a Company ship in two successful stages, add a regiment to the associated Army, or add an unsplit £2 to Company Balance or the President's treasury. Each Tax after the first in the turn adds unrest, and every unsuccessful check adds unrest. Attempts may continue until the Governor stops, the pool is exhausted, or catastrophe occurs, applying the general vacancy rule.

**Fixed key (verbatim):** Start printed dice pool; roll pool, no penalty, lose one die after any attempt. Success £1 bonus, extra £1 if preceding roll failed, choose build Company ship in two successful stages, regiment in associated army, or unsplit £2 Balance/President treasury. Tax after first in turn adds unrest. Any unsuccessful check adds unrest. May repeat until stop, pool exhausted or catastrophe (general vacancy rule).

**Source:** Rules p24; lines 1742–1771

**Coverage:** Governor diminishing pool and effects.

## Q014

If Company revenue cannot cover expenses, how do emergency loans work at and below max Debt, how does Standing change, and when are expectations and dividends checked?

**Answer:** Expenses are £1 per current Debt, per officer or regiment excluding the Commander, and per ship in sea zones, including China. Each emergency loan adds £5. Advance Debt if possible, but continue borrowing even at maximum Debt. New Debt adds no interest this turn, and Balance then becomes zero. One or two emergency loans lower Standing by one; three or more lower it by two. Next compare Balance with expectations at the current Standing and lower Standing by one if Balance is below expectations. Then optional dividends pay £1 per share, including the Chairman share. A total dividend payout strictly greater than expectations advances Standing once. Reaching the failure space causes immediate Company failure.

**Fixed key (verbatim):** Expenses £1 per current Debt, officer/regiment excluding Commander, ship in sea zones including China. Emergency loans +£5 each, advance Debt if possible even at maximum continue borrowing; added Debt costs no current-turn interest, then Balance zero. 1–2 emergency loans Standing -1; 3+ -2. Then compare Balance with expectations at current Standing, if below Standing -1. Then optional dividends £1 per share incl Chairman; total dividend payout strictly greater than expectations advances Standing once. Immediate failure when Standing reaches failure.

**Source:** Rules pp25,27; lines 1813–1815,1897–1934

**Coverage:** Revenue order and boundary outcomes.

## Q016

What happens when a law fails versus passes, including support-zero, policy movement, Prime Minister and effect order?

**Answer:** Support of −1 or lower means the law fails. Discard it and resolve any dilemma's If Failed text. The Opposition Leader returns the marker and becomes Prime Minister, then moves the selected policy one adjacent space in either direction and resolves it. Support of zero or higher passes the law: place it with passed laws, give the Prime Minister a token, resolve the selected policy's consequence, and then apply the law's immediate effects. Restore vote cards. If legislation causes Company failure, finish the vote before Final Scoring.

**Fixed key (verbatim):** Support -1 or lower fails; discard and resolve dilemma If Failed, Opposition Leader returns marker and becomes PM, moves selected policy one adjacent space either way and resolves it. Zero or higher passes: law with passed laws, PM gains token, selected policy consequence then immediate law effects. Restore vote cards. If legislation fails Company, finish vote then final scoring.

**Source:** Rules p36; lines 2570–2587

**Coverage:** Vote outcome branches.

## Q017

When can or must a Deregulation session be called in the relevant scenarios, and what are its success/failure effects and exceptions from ordinary voting?

**Answer:** In 1758 and Long 1710, the Prime Minister may call Deregulation at the start of the turn before London Season if Standing or Debt is on a lined space, and must call it if either is on a starred space. On passage, reduce Debt by one for every two Debt, set Standing to S, give the Prime Minister a law token, and activate deregulation. On failure, raise the share VP value up to ±3, keep the law, and retain the Prime Minister. There is no policy, and the Prime Minister may vote against Deregulation.

**Fixed key (verbatim):** 1758/Long1710 start-turn before London: PM may if Standing and/or Debt lined, must if either starred. Pass reduce Debt one per two Debt, Standing to S, PM law token, activate deregulation. Failure raise share VP value up to ±3, law kept, PM stays. No policy and PM may vote against.

**Source:** Rules p36; lines 2588–2601

**Coverage:** Deregulation trigger and exceptions.

## Q018

How can a firm be formed or invested in, where does money come from, and what restrictions apply to Company shares, invested workshops and the ten-share limit?

**Answer:** A player who is not a firm manager may form a firm by returning an eligible Company share or flipping a workshop to its invested side. Flip the player's board, create a family firm share, set value to the leftmost space, and take £5 from the bank into London. Later investment requires the manager's consent; the same share or workshop payment adds £5 to London and creates a new share. Company shares cannot be used when Standing or Debt is on a lined space. A share created this turn cannot be used, even after it has been traded, and the Chairman piece is unavailable. Invested workshops give neither a bonus nor Company-failure VP. The ten-share cap does not prevent further investments, which create no new shares.

**Fixed key (verbatim):** Nonmanager forms by returning eligible Company share or flipping workshop invested; flip own board, create family firm share, value leftmost, £5 bank to London. Later investment manager consent, same share/workshop payment gives £5 London and new share. Weak Company share when Standing/Debt lined prohibited; same-turn share prohibited even traded; Chairman piece unavailable. Invested workshop no bonus/failure VP. Ten-share cap but investments continue without new shares.

**Source:** Rules pp15,38; lines 1184–1187,2679–2724

**Coverage:** Firm capital and restrictions.

## Q020

How are firm expenses and dividends funded and calculated, and what happens to a failing firm, its shares and workshops?

**Answer:** At the start of Revenue, firms act in initiative order, followed by other firms. Break ties by the manager's windows, then clockwise from the Prime Minister. Expenses are £1 per ship plus the higher of the share count or firm value, paid from either treasury. A firm that cannot pay dissolves unless rescued: shares go to Debtors' Prison at −1 VP each, ships return to shipyards, the charter returns to its family side, and workshops remain invested. Dividends come only from India and pay £1 per share plus £1 to the manager for each dividend. A shareholder may forgo payment with the manager's consent. Record the total paid on the retirement marker, then move the remaining India cash to London.

**Fixed key (verbatim):** Start Revenue firms act initiative order then others, ties manager windows then PM clockwise. Expenses £1 per ship plus max(shares, firm value); either treasury. If cannot pay dissolution (or emergency rescue): shares Debtors Prison -1 VP each, ships shipyards, charter family, invested workshops stay invested. Dividends only India, £1 per share plus £1 manager each; shareholder may forgo with manager consent. Record total paid retirement marker then sweep remainder India to London.

**Source:** Rules pp41–42; lines 2827–2855,2888–2918

**Coverage:** Firm cash flow and failure.
