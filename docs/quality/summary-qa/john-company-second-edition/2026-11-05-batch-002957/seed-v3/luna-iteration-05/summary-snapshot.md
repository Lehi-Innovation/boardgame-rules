# John Company: Second Edition

## Overview

Players lead families seeking reputation through the East India Company. The Company is collectively operated by the players who hold its offices. Family wealth funds enterprises, politics and retirements; Company funds belong to individual offices and cannot be spent as family cash. Victory points, rather than cash or Company profitability, determine the winner.

**Scope:** Complete standard multiplayer rules, including the 1710, 1758, 1813 and Long 1710 scenarios and the core deregulation/firm system. This uses the Second Edition rules, not the 2017 edition. The Crown solo/two-player system (Rules pp43–46 and separate Crown Handbook), its difficulty settings, and the optional setup-card Draft variant are excluded. Deregulation is part of the standard advanced scenarios and is included here. Card-specific powers, restrictions, starting holdings and scenario map configurations are read from the relevant components.

## Components

- Board: prizes, Company Balance/Debt/Standing, Court and Stock Exchange, offices and treasuries, Power/Vote/VP/Turn tracks, India and three sea zones. Follow the red ribbon to operate offices.
- Six families: each has 18 family members, VP marker, five promise cards, opportunity marker, family board/firm charter, two firm cubes and four strategy cards.
- Enterprises: 22 shipyards, 20 workshops and 16 luxuries; 20 prestige and 10 blackmail cards; 26 laws, six Company failure cards; scenario and player setup cards; 17 office cards, Governor/China/Governor General overlays.
- India: towers and domes, three sets of empire flags, eight loot/control tokens, Elephant, event tiles, closed/filled order tokens and Presidency dividers. Military and shipping: officers represented by family pieces, regiments, local alliances, player ships and Company/extra ships. Also fatigue/unrest cubes, trophies/passed-law tokens, money, ordinary dice and storm die.

**Limits:** Components are limited: when a supply runs out, complete as much as possible. Cash, trophy/passed-law tokens, dice and regiments may use proxies. Company Balance, VP and Vote tracks are unlimited. All information, including discards and family cash, is public except facedown blackmail and temporarily concealed firm strategies/bids during selection.

**India terminology:** A region's tower levels are its strength; the dome adds none. Orders are connected trading opportunities with printed revenue values; heavy outlines mark home ports. Closed orders cannot be filled. Sovereign regions have a dome without a flag or a capital/star flag; dominated regions have a small flag or no dome. An empire has one capital and at least one dominated region. Remove its capital flag if only the capital remains. Presidencies are Bombay, Madras and Bengal, each with its own treasury, writers, fleet, Army and associated Company regions.

The Elephant is always in play. On a border, its tail is the acting region and its head the target. A sovereign actor threatens Invasion; a dominated actor facing its sovereign threatens Rebellion. An Elephant fully inside a Company region threatens Rebellion there.

## Setup

1. Set out board and supplies. Shuffle India events. Put local alliances in their named Army boxes. Set Balance to £5, Power tokens on their indicated starting positions, family VP markers to zero, loot tokens money-side up in matching regions.
2. Choose a scenario and follow its setup card for Standing, Debt, treasuries, laws, regiments, ships and the political map of India. Put the Turn marker on its date and point the PM dial to the matching date. The scenario determines its final turn.
3. Use that scenario's player setup cards; add three extra cards for five players or six for six players. Shuffle and deal evenly. Take all listed cash, enterprises and positions. Place a family member on each listed office and take its office card. Place share pieces in the Court, writers/officers in their indicated boxes and Commanders in their Army slots. Starting shipyards get their matching ships in the indicated sea zone, or on the shipyard if unfitted. Each starting luxury gives 2 VP.
4. Set remaining office cards nearby, distinguishing vacant offices from offices not yet created. Shuffle prestige and blackmail together; deal three for the London Season display, prestige faceup and blackmail facedown. Shuffle remaining laws. Keep family and firm pieces near each player.

| 1758 | Company starts with monopoly; deregulation can be voted in. |
| 1813 | Starts deregulated, allowing firms; recommended with five or six players. |
| Long 1710 | Same initial position as 1710, with deregulation available; can run eight turns, often twice the normal length. |

## Turn Structure

Each game turn follows: **London Season → Family → Firms (if deregulated) → Hiring → Company Operation → Bonuses → Revenue → Events in India → Parliament Meets → Upkeep and Refresh.** Skip London Season on the game's first turn. Eligible special Deregulation votes occur at the very start of the turn, before London Season.

### I. London Season (Rules pp11–12)

**Attrition:** Simultaneously roll one die for each held office card. Add one per fatigue on that card; Chairman also adds one. Modified 1–2: nothing; 3–4: add fatigue; 5+: retire. Move retiree to Pensioners, flip card into Vacant Offices, return its fatigue to supply. Commanders have no office card and do not roll normal office attrition.

**Retirements:** Starting with the Chairman (former Chairman if vacant), clockwise, each player may retire each pensioner to a prize. Pay its printed cost, move the member to the prize and gain printed VP. Set actual spending aside until London Season ends to establish display-selection order, then pay bank. Prizes may incur upkeep and window taxes. Unused pensioners do not remain for another turn.

**Prestige:** Players who retired at least one pensioner must each take or discard one display card, in descending actual total retirement spending. Ties: most total windows, including new prizes, then clockwise from PM. Acting player may peek at display blackmails. **Do not replace a chosen/discarded card during selection. If the display becomes empty, immediately skip every remaining eligible player.** Finish the display step by **discarding all remaining cards and refilling exactly three: prestige faceup, blackmail facedown, even if nobody took a card**. Return all remaining Pensioners to their supplies.

Spouses usually add VP, impose permanent restrictions and cannot be transferred. Make required enterprise/share trades immediately upon taking the spouse. Retirement discounts can stack, are personal, and each is usable once per London Season and once during Final Scoring. Discounts reduce actual spending and therefore display priority. If Violet Effingham returns shipyards, their fitted ships remain in play; those shipyards cannot be purchased while their ships remain in play.

Prestige enterprises retain their type, power and policy effects and can be traded. Blackmail has a one-time printed effect: once used, place it faceup in its owner's area; it can no longer be traded. Both faceup and facedown blackmail can contribute its printed power at final scoring.

### II. Family (Rules pp13–14)

Starting with the Chairman title holder, clockwise, each player resolves their family action:

1. Check where your opportunity marker was **at phase start** (it begins off the board).
2. Choose a main family action: take it once, or **may take it twice if the marker already matched it**.
3. **Place/move the opportunity marker onto your chosen main action.**
4. After the main action, only the latest passed extra-action law may grant its extra action. This extra action is never doubled by the marker and never moves the marker.

If placing a family piece with none in supply, take one from any of your prizes and lose that prize's printed VP. This applies to firm-share creation too. If no family action is possible, take none and return opportunity marker to supply.

| Family action | Effect |
|---|---|
| Enlist Writer | Place a member in any Presidency's Writers box. With at least four vacant offices, place one additional writer **once this turn**, so a doubled Writer action can place three total. |
| Enlist Officer | Place a member in Officers-in-Training. |
| Purchase Luxury | Pay bank £4; gain luxury, 2 VP while owned, and its window icon; social enterprise. |
| Purchase Shipyard | Pay bank £2; take shipyard and put matching ship on it; shipping enterprise, one vote and £1 bonus while fitted. |
| Purchase Workshop | Pay bank £5; take workshop; manufacturing enterprise, two votes, £1 bonus; worth 1 VP if Company fails unless invested. |
| Seek Share | Place a member on an empty Stock Exchange space and pay bank: the five queue spaces are £2, £3, £3, £4, £5 (two distinct £3 spaces). It is an applicant, not yet a share. |

**New Company shares:** After all family actions, if Debt and applicants remain, move the rightmost applicant into Court and lower Debt by one. Repeat while both remain. Pieces in Court are Company shares. With no Debt, slide remaining applicants as far right as possible, preserving order. Seeking a share never pays family cash into Company treasuries.

### III. Hiring (Rules pp14–16)

Fill vacant offices in ascending number on their card backs. The designated hirer chooses a candidate and moves that member into the office; its owner gets the card. When promoting an officeholder, transfer its fatigue to the new card and insert its old card, vacant, into ascending hiring order. If no candidate exists, or the hirer is vacant, skip the office; it stays vacant and takes no actions, including hiring.

**Nepotism and its exceptions:**

| Selection | Own-family consent requirement |
|---|---|
| Ordinary office hire | Verbal consent from **every other player represented in that office's candidate pool**. |
| Chairman election, including deadlock fallback | **Exempt from Nepotism**; use the shareholder election/fallback rules. |
| Commander appointment | **Exempt from Nepotism**; use Army piece counts. |

| Office | Hirer and candidates |
|---|---|
| Chairman | Shareholders elect as below. |
| Director of Trade | Chairman chooses current officeholder other than Chairman/Governor; if all offices vacant, any writer. Commanders are not officeholders. |
| Manager of Shipping | Chairman chooses any writer. |
| Military Affairs | Chairman chooses a Presidential Commander; if none, any officer; if none, any Officer-in-Training. |
| President | Director of Trade chooses writer or Governor associated with that Presidency. |
| Governor | Associated President chooses writer or officer in the region; Commander is not an officer and is ineligible. Place member on Governor overlay. |
| Superintendent of Trade in China | Chairman chooses any writer; office hired immediately upon creation. |
| Governor General | Replaces Director of Trade and is hired the same way. |

**Chairman election:** Former Chairman first announces **the number of shares needed for a pure majority** (more than half of current shares). Any shareholder may campaign; first candidate supported by players controlling that many shares wins. Move one winner's Company shares into Chairman slot and give title marker. It still counts as a share for dividends, majority and scoring, but cannot be traded or invested in firms. If no candidate gets support, former Chairman picks any shareholder including self; no Nepotism consent. If Court is empty, former Chairman automatically returns, placing a member from supply or, if necessary and possible, a prize (lose prize VP). If impossible, there is no Chairman.

### IV. Company Operation

Follow offices left to right along the ribbon, beginning with Chairman; skip vacant offices. Each office spends only its own treasury. Offices created by actions go into Vacant Offices unless immediate hiring is specified. A newly created Governorship normally awaits a later Hiring phase.

**Success checks (p17):** First commit resources/cash: **all cash for a Company-office check comes from that acting office's treasury and is paid to the bank**. Gather dice, subtract penalties, then roll. Use lowest die: 1–2 success; 3–4 failure, retry if resources and office-specific limits allow; 5–6 catastrophic failure, no more office actions, return officeholder to supply, discard fatigue and flip card into Vacant Offices. Specific Commander/firm procedures modify this. You cannot take a dead action with no effect or chance of success: for example trade with no ship or deploy without dice to trigger possible officer losses.

Office procedures are in **Actions** below. Presidency order is Bombay, Madras, Bengal; each President orders its own Commander, Governor(s) and Trade. China acts after Bengal.

### V. Bonuses (p27)

Each player takes £1 per workshop and per shipyard whose ship is fitted. Invested workshops give no bonus. Transferring an enterprise cannot trigger its bonus a second time.

### VI. Revenue (p27; firms pp41–42)

If deregulated, firms pay expenses/dividends first. Then Company:

1. **Expenses:** Reduce Balance £1 per existing Debt, each officer/regiment in an Army, and each ship in sea zones, including China. Exclude Commanders. If insufficient, take £5 emergency loans, advancing Debt one each until its track end; continue receiving emergency loans even at maximum Debt. Newly added emergency Debt causes no extra current-turn expense. After using emergency loans, set Balance to zero. One or two loans lower Standing one; three or more lower Standing two.
2. **Expectations:** Compare remaining Balance to the expectations under **current** Standing; if lower, lower Standing one.
3. **Dividends:** Chairman may pay any affordable number. Each dividend costs one cash per Company share; pay each owner £1 per share, including Chairman. If total cash paid as dividends is **strictly greater** than expectations, advance Standing one space.

Standing reaching leftmost space means Company failure. During Revenue, go immediately to Final Scoring.

### VII. Events in India (pp28–33)

Roll storm die, resolve storms, then the indicated number of event tiles. Flip each event; where the event uses a region, normally read the **back of the new top tile** in the draw stack, not the tile just drawn.

**Storms:** In rolled sea zone(s), roll once per player-owned ship; Company and extra ships are exempt. Three storms means all zones. 1–2 nothing; 3–4 fatigue, or sink if already fatigued; 5–6 sink. Sunk ship returns to its shipyard, unfatigued. China ships count as Eastern zone. Firm ships use their strategy's zone; Hobnob ships are exempt.

**Closing orders and Cascades:** Return any writer on a newly closed order to its owner's supply; flip a filled token to closed. If told to close an order in an already fully closed region, Cascade: close all open orders directly connected to that region's orders. If a connected order is already closed, close that region's northernmost open order instead. If none are open, it also Cascades. A region can initiate a Cascade only once per event tile but can receive multiple Cascades' effects.

| Event | Procedure |
|---|---|
| Turmoil | Close northernmost open order in top-stack region; if all closed, Cascade. |
| Windfall | Owners take £1 per writer on orders in top-stack region and every bordering region. |
| Shuffle | Elephant's March, then shuffle this tile with remaining draw stack; separately shuffle discards and place on top. If draw stack empty, March last instead. |
| Leader | Top-stack sovereign region gains one tower. Dominated region instead rebels with printed explosion modifier. March only if Elephant was fully inside a Company region that successfully rebelled. |
| Peace | Open orders connected across Elephant's border and add one tower to each touched non-Company region. If Elephant wholly inside a region, instead open all its orders and remove its unrest. Then March. |
| Crisis | Resolve at Elephant's location, tail attacking head, then March. Use procedures below. |
| Foreign Invasion | Roll storm die for target regions: West→Bombay, South→Madras, East→Bengal; all storms means each, none means top-stack region. Per target roll ordinary die for attack strength and resolve Invasion/Company attack. On success close all orders (Cascade if already all closed), remove target's flag and all matching flags if capital, restore dome if Company-controlled, and set tower strength to half attack roll rounded down. March with circle shape only if Elephant was fully inside a Company region that was invaded. |

**Invasion Crisis:** Sovereign attacker's whole empire strength + event modifier must be **greater than** defender's whole empire strength. On success, replace defender's flag with attacker's dominated flag; taking a capital shatters its entire empire. If attacker had no empire, use a free capital/star flag and matching small flag; if none available no new empire/flags form. On failure, attacker loses one tower.

**Rebellion Crisis:** Compare only rebelling region strength + modifier against its sovereign capital's strength; other empire regions do not count. Must be strictly greater. Success makes actor sovereign, removes its flag and closes all its orders (Cascade if already closed). Failure removes one tower from the capital.

**Attacks on Company:** An Invasion or Rebellion against a Company region also triggers secondary Rebellions in every other Company region with unrest. Resolve primary first; then Bombay, Madras, Bengal, each Commander ordering attacks within their Presidency. Attack strengths are distinct:

- **Primary normal Crisis:** normal Invasion/Rebellion attack strength **plus unrest in its target region**.
- **Primary Foreign Invasion:** the ordinary attack die, with no added unrest (designer ruling in FAQ).
- **Every secondary Rebellion:** **only the number of unrest cubes in that secondary region**; no event modifier or empire strength.

 Associated Commander must exhaust active Army pieces totaling attack strength, or as much as possible. Use local alliances' printed strength; they cannot be split. **Insufficient newly exhausted defense strength:** perform Region Loss. **Sufficient defense strength:** remove all unrest in the attacked region and give the Commander one trophy. **If this successful defense defeated a normal primary Invasion, also remove one tower from the attacking region if possible.** Vacant Commander: **Military Affairs itself acts in the Commander's stead**, or Chairman if Military Affairs is vacant; this acting player chooses defense pieces and gains/loses no trophies. This does not appoint a new Commander. If the primary Invasion succeeds against the Company, apply empire growth.

**Region Loss:** Commander returns half their family's trophies rounded up, then Commander member returns to supply. Roll once per officer in associated Army: each 6 removes that officer. Eliminate Governor: return office card to unused stack, fatigue and member to supplies. Remove Governor overlay, partially built Company ship and unrest. Restore dome with one tower (or Foreign Invasion's specified strength) and appropriate invasion flag. Return control token to region, loot-side down: future conquest gets no loot modifier and region loses Presidency association. An Invasion Crisis closes all its orders, or Cascades if already closed; Rebellion/Foreign Invasion already close them. Standing loss equals number of regions lost **so far this turn**, including this one: first -1, second additional -2, etc. If this causes failure, finish Events in India before Final Scoring.

**Elephant's March:** After successful Invasion Crisis, move to successful capital (Imperial Ambitions); otherwise top-stack region. Company region: place fully inside. Dominated region: point toward its sovereign. Sovereign: point across shape-marked border on tile. If that neighbor is already dominated by actor, choose first clockwise neighbor that is not. If all neighbors dominated, use the redirect-arrow shape's border, pointing toward empire capital.

### VIII. Parliament Meets (pp34–36)

PM reveals up to three laws one at a time, chooses one, discards others. Drawing a dilemma forces selection and ends drawing. PM then chooses direction around dial, stopping on first policy space matching law's bottom icon. Selected policy has target and consequence. Read law and set Vote marker to its printed starting support.

**Voting:** Starting PM, clockwise, players cast votes; each £1 family cash buys one vote, plus printed card votes (rotate used cards). Adjust support up/down; tracks unlimited. PM may call another full round or stop after each; a round with no votes must end voting. A player who voted for may later oppose; once opposed, cannot vote for during this vote. Track all opposition votes: biggest opponent holds Opposition Leader; tied current holder retains it. With recipient's consent, votes may be cast on behalf of someone who already opposed or commits to oppose; add them to that person's opposition total. PM cannot oppose or consent to enterprises opposing, except forced use of the "Vote with my enterprises" promise.

**Resolution:** -1 or less fails: discard law, resolve dilemma's If Failed text, Opposition Leader returns token and takes PM dial; new PM moves proposed policy one space either direction and resolves that adjacent policy. Zero or higher passes: place law with passed laws, PM takes passed-law token, resolve selected policy **then** immediate law effects. **Finally restore every card used for voting to its regular orientation**, after either outcome. If law causes Company failure, finish the vote before Final Scoring.

| Policy | Effect |
|---|---|
| Tax | Pay bank £1 per owned target; each unpaid target returns to supply, including Chairman if taxed. Fitted ships of lost shipyards stay in play; shipyard unavailable for purchase until ship returns. Losing prize/luxury removes its VP. |
| Window tax | When selected policy includes windows, additionally pay each window on enterprises and each family member's prize; return enterprise/prize member if unable. |
| Bonus | Take bank £1 per owned target. |
| Power | Swap target Power marker with next higher marker; if already highest, swap any other pair. |

### IX. Upkeep and Refresh (p36)

Pay printed upkeep separately for each family member on a prize. If unpaid, return that member and lose that prize's printed VP. Negotiating help remains possible while game continues. On final turn, perform upkeep but skip Refresh and go to Final Scoring. Otherwise return writers to associated Presidencies, return filled tokens and dividers, return extra ships to supply, reactivate exhausted officers/regiments, exhaust any active local alliances, advance turn and begin London Season. Fitted player ships retain fatigue.

## Actions

### Chairman (p18)

May add up to three Debt freely, each +£5 Balance. Each additional advance requires consent of players controlling majority Company shares (Chairman counts); never pass Debt track end. Then **allocate all Balance** to other offices' treasuries, lowering Balance by each transfer. May interleave borrowing and allocation while respecting total borrowing rules. Cash already in offices remains there for their use.

### Director of Trade (p18)

May make repeated Special Envoy checks: £1 treasury cash per die, no penalty. Success either opens one closed order in a **non-Company** region, or opens China: place Superintendent overlay/card and Chairman hires writer immediately. After all envoys, may make up to two transfers, each moving one writer or ship between any Presidencies/sea zones (including China once created). Cannot transfer while still taking envoys.

### Manager of Shipping (p19)

Must spend treasury until **£2 or less remains**. Lease extra ship £2; fit ship from shipyard £3 without owner consent; buy Company ship £5 **only if no ships remain on shipyards**. Place each acquired ship in any sea zone, including China once office exists. Extra ships serve only until Refresh.

### Military Affairs (p19)

May make up to two transfers, each officer or regiment from one Army to another, remaining in top half. Then assign **every** Officer-in-Training to any Army's top half. Finally appoint Commanders: if vacancy or someone has more total family pieces in Army than current Commander's family, choose among families with most pieces. Move chosen officer to Commander slot; return previous Commander to top as officer. A tie with current Commander's family does not replace them. Nepotism does not apply.

### Presidency operations and Trade (pp20–21)

Each President chooses order of its Commander, Governors and its own Trade. Must allow each position an opportunity but Commander/Governors may decline. Each position finishes all actions before next begins. Trade can go before/after Commander or any Governor but cannot interrupt another position. If President vacant, Chairman sets order only: cannot trade or approve local alliance purchases.

Declare regions for Trade: include home region, and only regions reachable through open orders with available ship capacity. Each £1 President's treasury buys one die; subtract one per declared nonhome region. Failure can be retried during operations if affordable; catastrophe removes President. At most **one successful Trade per President per turn**. Failed attempts and retries belong to that President's uninterrupted opportunity; another position cannot act between its attempts.

Success: one order per ship in its sea zone, as many as possible within declared regions. First writer goes to home port; next orders must be adjacent to one already filled by this trading Presidency. Use actual writers while available, then filled-order tokens. No President may fill another President's home region, even if its office vacant. Increase Balance by each order's printed value, **or half rounded down if a firm already filled it this turn under Deregulation**. President earns bank £1 per order; writer owners earn £1 per writer placed. Separate adjacent writers from different Presidencies with dividers for correct return at Refresh.

### Commanders and Deploy (pp22–23)

First request President's treasury funds for any printed-price local alliances in Army; President must approve purchase. Move purchased alliances to top. Each alliance's strength can be used **once that turn**, for Deploy or defense, never split across purposes.

May Deploy repeatedly while able:

1. Target own Presidency's home, any associated region, or any region bordering an associated region. Cannot target another home region or another Presidency's Company-controlled region. Company region must have unrest or a closed order to give action an effect.
2. Exhaust chosen officers/regiments to bottom for one die each and local alliances for printed dice. Subtract the combined strength of target and **all other regions in its empire, counting target only once**. Company-controlled defense strength is zero. Roll normal check; a noncatastrophic failure can retry using remaining resources.
3. After **each attempt**, owners roll one die per officer exhausted in attempt; each 6 returns an officer to supply. Regiments never roll officer losses.

On success, take loot from the bank: **£4 per target tower + faceup loot modifier, with a minimum of (surviving officers used in this attempt + 1)**. The extra £1 guarantees the Commander's minimum share even if no officers survive. Count only this attempt's surviving participating officers and used regiments/alliances.

| Each payout cycle | Pay in this order, stopping when cash runs out |
|---|---|
| Commander family first | £1 for Commander **plus £1 for each of their used surviving officers**. |
| Other families clockwise | £1 per used surviving officer of that family. |
| Bank last | £1 per used regiment and per used local-alliance **piece**, regardless of strength. |

**Repeat that entire cycle from the Commander until all loot is distributed.** The Commander also receives one trophy per target tower.

**Successful control procedure — finish all these steps after loot and trophies:**

1. Remove the target's tower and dome. If target was an empire capital, remove **all matching empire flags**.
2. Open **all orders** and remove **all unrest** in the target region.
3. **Form a Governorship if the target was not already Company-controlled:** put its loot/control token region-side up above the associated President's box; **place the Governor overlay ON THE MAP IN THE CONQUERED REGION**; send **only the office card** to the Vacant Offices box for later Hiring. If Governor General is in play, do not create Governors/overlays.
4. If the Elephant in the conquered region faced outward, move it **fully inside that region**, now threatening Rebellion.

On catastrophe, Commander returns half own family's trophies rounded up and Commander member to supply. Exhausted officers still check losses. Commander has no office card to vacate.

### Governors: Administer (p24)

Take the printed dice pool. Each attempt rolls the current pool without penalty, then reduces the pool by one **regardless of result**.

| Result | Governor procedure |
|---|---|
| Success | Take £1, or **£2 if the immediately preceding roll was unsuccessful**; then choose exactly one option below. |
| Any unsuccessful roll | Add one unrest in the region. Catastrophe also removes the officeholder under the normal rule. |

| Option on success | Effect and destination |
|---|---|
| Build Company ship | **Two successful Build actions:** if none in region, put a partially built Company ship in the Governor's region; otherwise complete that ship into **the sea zone associated with the Governor's President**. Partially built ship has no other effect. |
| Commission regiment | Place it in **the Army associated with the Governor's President**. Officer-like strength, loot paid to bank, no officer-loss rolls. |
| Tax | Add an **unsplit £2** to Company Balance or the Governor's President's treasury. Each Tax after the first this turn adds regional unrest. |

Repeat using the shrinking pool until choosing to stop, no dice remaining, or catastrophe.

### Superintendent of Trade in China (p25)

Created by successful envoy or Envoy to China law; immediately hire any writer. Its treasury holds ships, not cash. Shipping and Director of Trade may send ships here like any sea zone. They count Eastern for expenses, events, storms and laws. After Bengal, may attempt China Trade **once**: one die per ship, and only if Company regions contain at least one export icon. Success: Balance +£4 and Superintendent bonus £1 per export icon across all Company-controlled regions.

### Governor General (p26)

Law creates this office: immediately make all Governors pensioners, remove their overlays and partially built Company ships, return Governor/Director cards to box. Replace Director of Trade with Governor General, preserving holder and fatigue, and place its ribbon overlay. No further Governors/overlays created upon conquest; printed Company symbols indicate controlled regions. Governor General hires Presidents and uses former Director's treasury and turn position; it is hired like Director and rolls attrition normally. Skip Governor Elimination when regions are lost.

First Balance +£3 per Company region. Then may attempt Govern repeatedly, paying £1 treasury per die, no penalty. **Every 6 rolled adds one unrest to each Company region**, even on successful roll. On failure add another unrest to every Company region. On success, bonus £1 per Company region and **must choose one benefit per region**: complete Company ship into any sea zone including China; regiment into any Army; or £2 Balance/any office treasury **except own**, adding unrest to a Company region. Multiple Tax choices this turn must put their unrest in different regions. At most one successful Govern per turn.

### Deregulation (pp36–42)

In 1758/Long 1710, at turn start before London Season, PM **may** call special Deregulation vote if Standing and/or Debt on lined space, and **must** if either on starred space. Use the ordinary voting procedure and support threshold, with the following exceptions. Pass: lower Debt one per two current Debt, set Standing to S, give PM passed-law token and activate firms. Fail: increase Company share scoring value by flipping/placing Court VP token, maximum ±3. No policy accompanies this law; failure neither removes PM nor discards the Deregulation law, and PM may vote against. 1813 begins deregulated.

Firm managers control firms; firm shares can be traded like Company shares. A firm has two treasuries: **London** takes investment cash and funds ship acquisition/trade bids/expenses; **India** receives trade and funds dividends/expenses. **Family cash cannot be transferred into or out of firm treasuries.** Only specified investment, payout and expense procedures move it.

**Special retirement:** Each shareholder may make one additional retirement per London Season from supply rather than Pensioners, irrespective of number of firms owned. Actual spending cannot exceed **highest total dividend marker** of any firm they own a share in. No retirement from a firm whose marker is absent or zero. Spouse discounts can allow a higher printed-cost prize; retirement otherwise normal, including prestige eligibility. At start of Final Scoring, score firms then allow one special retirement regardless of Company success/failure, before normal final-scoring steps; no display cards in Final Scoring.

#### Firms phase: investments (pp38–39)

After Family share creation, before Hiring, take investments actions without set order until nobody wishes more. Manager resolves timing conflicts within firm; PM resolves conflicts between firms. Then choose strategies.

- **Form a firm — only if you currently manage none:** return one eligible Company share to supply or flip workshop to invested side. Flip family board, place family member as first firm share, set value cube to leftmost track space and take £5 bank to London treasury.
- **Invest:** With manager's consent, same Company-share/workshop contribution creates one firm share and £5 bank to London. Up to ten firm shares; further investments still contribute £5 but create no shares. Cannot use Chairman share. Cannot use Company shares at all when Standing and/or Debt on lined space. Cannot use shares created this turn, even after trading them away. Invested workshops never return to uninvested: no bonus or Company-failure VP; they remain invested even if firm fails.
- **Ships:** London pays bank £3 per ship fitted from shipyard **with owner consent**. May buy player-owned ship already in Company with owner's consent: unfatigued £3, fatigued £2, paid into Shipping treasury. Firms cannot acquire Company or extra ships.
- **Hostile takeover:** If not managing firm and obtain consent of holders of majority of target shares, move firm's ships, money and shares to own charter, copy both cube positions; former manager returns cubes and flips board to family side.
- **Merger:** Managers negotiate with **every shareholder's consent**. Highest-value firm is primary; if tied, players choose among tied. Transfer all secondary shares, ships and treasury funds to primary; secondary boards return to family sides. Negotiate transfers and any shares exceeding ten returned with those owners' consent. Entire agreement is one binding immediate transfer. Primary's value/dividend track remains the continuing firm's track.
- **Voluntary dissolution:** All shareholders must consent; use dissolution destinations below.

#### Firm strategies and competing trade (p40)

**Each firm gets exactly one trade attempt per turn.** Managers select their strategy simultaneously and secretly:

| Choice | Eligibility and commitment |
|---|---|
| Trade zone/home port | Must have at least one ship. **A closed home port is allowed when selecting the strategy.** Commit a secret cash bid from London treasury. |
| Hobnob | No bid, trade or dividends. Still pay expenses; retain previous dividend marker for retirement. |

**Reveal cash bids simultaneously, but keep strategy cards facedown until their matching Presidency begins.**

At beginning of associated Presidency's operations, before any actions, matching firms reveal strategy and roll one die per committed £1, paying bid to bank. At least one 1/2 permits trade. Firm initiative = ship count minus number of successful dice (1s/2s); can be negative. President's successful Trade creates initiative the same way using Presidency ships. After that success, fill orders lowest initiative first. **The President chooses the order among tied traders; the Company does not automatically go first.** If President is vacant or never successfully trades, firms fill **after Commander/Governors had chance to act**, Chairman choosing initiative ties.

Each firm fills as many open orders as possible, up to **ships + 1**, starting strategy home port, always tracing through its own filled route back there. Each firm fills any particular order only once. Place filled tokens; revenue goes to India treasury. Firms may refill orders others/Company filled this turn; Company may refill firms' orders. A previously filled order pays **half listed revenue, rounded down**, not repeated full value. Advance firm Value one space per full £6 added to India treasury this turn.

#### Firm expenses, rescue and dividends (pp41–42)

At start Revenue before Company, firms act in initiative order then other firms; ties by manager's most windows then PM clockwise order. Pay £1 per charter ship **plus higher of share count or firm value**; can use either/both treasuries.

If unable to pay, dissolve unless rescued. Dissolution: shares to Debtor's Prison (each -1 VP), ships back to shipyards, charter back to family side; workshops remain invested. Emergency investments follow normal Company-share/workshop rules, even when ten-share cap prevents more shares: pay expenses, lose any remaining cash, lower Value one space per emergency investment. Alternatively merge with **one** other firm; that firm must first pay its own expenses if not already paid and cover endangered firm's unpaid expenses.

After expenses, manager may pay dividends **from India treasury only**. Each dividend pays £1 per firm share plus £1 manager. With manager's consent a shareholder may forgo payment for any number of shares on any dividend. Record **total actual cash paid out**, not per-share dividend, with dividend cube on Company Balance track. An active non-Hobnob firm paying none records zero; Hobnob retains prior marker. Sweep remaining India money into London. Firm ships face storms in strategy zone; Hobnob exempts them.

## Scoring / Victory Conditions

The game ends after scenario's final turn or when Standing reaches failure. Normally failure is immediate; failure during Events finishes that phase, and legislation failure finishes vote before scoring. Once game over, stop negotiations, calling promises and playing blackmail unless a card explicitly permits final-scoring use.

If deregulated, first score each firm share at its firm's printed VP value and deduct **1 VP per family piece in Debtor's Prison**, summing fractional points before rounding down. Then each shareholder may make one eligible special retirement even if Company failed. Then normal Final Scoring (p9):

1. **Power award:** Total enterprise/share power from current Power track (bottom two positions zero), +1 per passed-law token and trophy, +2 for PM, plus printed card power (blackmail faceup or down, applicable spouse). Most/second awards are **3/1 VP if final turn 1–3, 5/2 if turn 4–6, 8/4 if turn 7–8**. Tied first: only lower award to each tied player; no other award. Tied second receive none.
2. **Court/workshops:** If Company survived, add Court's current VP per Company share. If failed, subtract that value per share and add 1 VP per uninvested workshop. Scores may be negative.
3. **Survival/failure:** If survived, roll office attrition and retire pensioners, without prestige cards. If failed, draw one random Company failure card and resolve its printed consequence instead.

Firm-track VP per share: Value 1 = −1 VP; 2 = 0; 3–4 = ½; 5–7 = 1; 8–9 = 1½; 10 = 2. The firm's Value number is also used for expenses; its VP rate is the symbol beneath that number.

Highest final VP wins. Ties: most windows, then clockwise from PM. Prizes, luxuries and spouse points are generally recorded when gained/lost, not added again at final scoring.

## Special Rules & Edge Cases

**Negotiation (p8):** Trade enterprises, facedown blackmail, family treasury cash, Company/firm shares and promise cards. Cannot trade officeholders, writers, officers, prizes, spouses, trophies, passed laws, played blackmail or PM dial. Cannot transfer Chairman piece. When a share changes family, replace member with new owner's family piece. Adjust VP when luxury ownership changes. A blackmail can be shown privately but never traded faceup.

Immediate transfers are binding; promises of future behavior are not, except printed promise cards can be called to force their guaranteed effect **if possible**. Promise cards may be traded to a third party or back to guarantor. If multiple promises called together cannot all be fulfilled, guarantor chooses which to fulfill. Read each card's timing and limits. No calling promises during Final Scoring. **Transferred resources cannot activate twice:** a workshop paid this Bonuses phase does not pay again if sold during it.

**Cash location matters:** Family treasury can pay family purchases, retirements, upkeep, taxes and deals. Office cash only funds that office. Firm cash uses its specified treasury rules. The unlimited bank does not remove component limits or authorize arbitrary treasury transfers.

**Source boundary:** This is a playable procedural summary used with board and cards. Printed prize/track/scenario values and individual law, spouse, promise and failure effects remain authoritative. Crown rules and optional draft setup are outside scope.

## FAQ & Rulings

**Foreign Invasion and unrest:** Cole Wehrle clarified on April 30, 2023 that unrest does **not** add to a Foreign Invasion's primary attack strength or determine its resulting tower levels. Use the ordinary die roll for both attack strength and halving to create towers; the event still uses the Company-defense procedure and its secondary unrest attacks. Do not apply the normal primary Crisis's added unrest to this special event. [Designer ruling](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2962161/article/42192767#42192767). The rules already avoid closing Foreign Invasion orders twice: Region Loss's additional closure is only for an Invasion Crisis.

**Applicable official errata:** The publisher links the [official errata discussion](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2936700/official-errata), whose original designer post links the full internal correction list. The assigned Second Printing rulebook incorporates the core procedural corrections, including forced prize-piece reuse, empty Court, deployment boundaries, Region Loss and Elephant priority. Corrected setup cards supply Bengal's 1758 control token and 1813 control/share-value tokens. Printed card corrections also specify: Mercantile/Diplomatic Connection reducing a check to zero dice means failure; Company Fraud is played at start of Revenue: roll one die per your officeholder, reduce Balance by twice the highest roll (minimum zero), and take that much cash from the bank; Public Demands Impeachment returns member to supply and office card to vacancies; Military Oversight's demoted Commander is eligible as an officer for reappointment. These are card-specific effects, not changes to the normal check/hiring procedures.
