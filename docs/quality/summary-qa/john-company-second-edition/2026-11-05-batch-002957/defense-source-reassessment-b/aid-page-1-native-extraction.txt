The Operation of the Company
The 
Chairman
Advance Company Debt up to 3 
(or more with consent of Court). 
Increase Balance by £5 each. 
Then allocate all Balance 
to office treasuries.
Director 
of Trade
First, may Special Envoy.
Then, make up to two transfers. Each 
moves one writer or ship between 
Presidencies or sea zones.
Special Envoy: Roll 1 die for each £1 
spent. If successful, open trade with 
China or open one closed order in 
a region not Company-controlled.
Manager of 
Shipping
Spend funds to fit ships, placing them in 
any sea zone. You may only hold £2 in 
your treasury (the rest must be spent). 
£2 for extra ships.
£3 for ships on shipyard.
£5 for Company ships if all player ships have been fitted.
Military 
Affairs
First, make up to two Army 
transfers. Each moves one officer or 
regiment between Army boxes.
Then, assign pieces in Officers-
in-Training box to Armies. 
Finally, appoint Commanders 
if vacant or if another player 
has the most total pieces in 
that Army. You decide ties. 
Presidency 
Operations
Presidents decide the order they, their 
Commander, their Governors act. 
Presidents may Trade. Declare the 
regions you wish to Trade in. (You cannot 
declare more than you could Trade with)
Roll 1 die for each £1 spent. Subtract 
1 die for each region declared that 
is not your home region. 
If successful, fill orders up to 
the number of ships in your 
sea zone in declared regions.
When filling orders, start with home 
port, then adjacent and empty open 
orders. Writers must be placed first. 
Increase Balance by amount listed.
Players gain £1 for each 
writer placed. You gain £ 
equal to # of filled orders.
Important: Each President 
may only have one successful 
trade per turn!
Commander First, may purchase local alliances 
using cash in President’s treasury 
with consent of President.
Then may Deploy. Select a 
target from the following: 
 Ƙ Home region.
 Ƙ Controlled-region associated 
with your President with at least 
one unrest or closed order.
 Ƙ Region adjacent to those that is 
not Company-controlled.
Roll 1 die for each officer, regiment 
used. In addition roll dice equal to 
the strength of any alliances used.
Subtract 1 die for each tower 
level of your target. 
Then, players roll 1 die for each 
officer and remove if a 6 is rolled.
If successful, gather loot.  Take 
£4 for each tower level plus loot 
modifier if faceup (minimum 
officers participating + 1)
Divide loot as follows:
Going from Commander and 
clockwise players take:
 Ƙ £1 if they are Commander.
 Ƙ £1 for each officer used.
After going around, pay £1 for 
each regiment and local alliance to 
bank. Then repeat if loot remains.
Then, after Dividing Loot, Commander takes one trophy token for each tower level. Open all orders 
in the region and remove any unrest. Create Governorship (if not already Company-controlled).
If a catastrophic failure, the Commander is removed and loses half (round up) of their trophies. 
Governor May Administer using your dice 
pool. If successful, you gain £1 and 
pick a result. If you do not succeed, 
add an unrest. Then reduce dice pool 
by one regardless of success. 
If successful after an attempt that was 
not successful, gain £2 instead of £1.
The Administer results are as follows:  
 Ƙ Place a regiment in your President’s Army.
 Ƙ Place Company ship in region. If already there, 
move it to your President’s sea zone instead.
 Ƙ Increase Company Balance or President’s treasury by £2. If done 
more than once this turn, add an unrest each time this is chosen.
Superintendent 
of Trade 
in China
May Trade in China once. Roll 1 die 
for each ship in China. If successful, 
increase Balance by £4 for each export 
icon in a Company-controlled region.
If successful, you gain £1 for each export icon 
in Company-controlled regions.
Governor 
General
First, increase Balance by £3 for each 
Company-controlled region. 
Then may Govern. Roll 1 die for each £1 
spent. For each 6 rolled, add one unrest 
to each Company-controlled region.
If unsuccessful, add one unrest to 
each Company-controlled region.
Important: the Governor General may 
only have one successful Govern per turn!
If successful, you gain £ equal to Company-controlled regions. Then 
pick 1 result for each Company-controlled region from the following:
 Ƙ Place a regiment in any Army.
 Ƙ Place Company ship in any sea zone (including China if open).
 Ƙ Increase Company Balance or any office treasury that is not your 
own by £2, and add an unrest to a region. If taken multiple times, 
you may not stack unrest from this action in the same region.
