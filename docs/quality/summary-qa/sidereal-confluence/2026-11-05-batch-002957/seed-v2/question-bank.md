# Sidereal Confluence seed-v2

Original 2017 WizKids Sidereal Confluence: Trading and Negotiation in the Elysian Quadrant, ten-page SC_Rule Book_061017_Web5 and original printed nine-species fact sheets including Faderan reverse. Excludes 2020 Remastered, expansions, and optional variants.

## Q01

What is the ordinary phase order, when does trading end, and may players still invent immediately before running their Economy?

**Expected:** Trade, Economy, Confluence (share technologies then colony bids then research-team bids). Trading ends when all but one player are done; players may immediately invent or use other Trade abilities before Economy.

**Sources:** Rulebook p6 Phases and Trade Phase

**Novelty:** Core sequence and Trade boundary

## Q02

An agreed future delivery becomes impossible. Everyone rejects substitute compensation. After delivering what is possible, the player still owes one card, one octagon, two cubes and one ship. What must happen and how many points are lost; can the score go negative?

**Expected:** Complete as much of the original agreement as possible. Lose 2 per undelivered card or octagon and 1 per cube or ship: 2+2+2+1=7 points. Scores may become negative.

**Sources:** Rulebook p6 Trade Phase, failed promises

**Novelty:** Binding promises, compensation failure and penalties

## Q03

If a player trades a species-background economic card, a colony and an uninvented research team, which normally return, and when?

**Expected:** The species-background economic card is a loan and returns at the end of the turn. The colony and research team lack player backgrounds and are transferred permanently.

**Sources:** Rulebook pp2,6 Cards and Trade Phase; p9 Ending the Turn

**Novelty:** Ownership and destinations

## Q04

How and when does a player invent from a research team they control, which printed cost is paid, when do the inventor and other species put their matching cards in play, and when is the Sharing Bonus collected?

**Expected:** During Trade run its violet converter, paying one complete alternative set printed to the left, not all sets. Take printed invention points, flip team and immediately play inventor matching library card. In Confluence announce/splay technology, gain current Sharing Bonus per invention, and others play matching cards then, subject to Yengii and era-IV exceptions.

**Sources:** Rulebook p6 Invention; p8 Confluence; Yengii front Secretive

**Novelty:** Invention cost, timing and shared access

## Q05

For a named-card upgrade, must both cards be in play with their owner, where does the spent card go, and can the newly renamed upgraded card still pay an upgrade requiring its old name?

**Expected:** Both must be in play and in their owner possession, not on loan. Flip target and put spent card underneath; each can pay for one upgrade only. A renamed upgraded card is no longer its old name and cannot pay an upgrade requiring that old card.

**Sources:** Rulebook pp6–7 Upgrading, Genetic Resynthesis example

**Novelty:** Named-card upgrade restrictions

## Q06

At ordinary Economy, when is Colony Support checked, where do excess colonies go, can a white converter be skipped or run twice, and can one converter output fund another in that same Economy?

**Expected:** At beginning of Economy check support and put excess colonies under colony deck. Each white economic converter is optional and at most once per turn unless an explicit exception; all run simultaneously so one output cannot feed another this phase.

**Sources:** Rulebook p7 Economy Phase

**Novelty:** Economy limits, support and simultaneity

## Q07

For ordinary donation output, where is it kept and what must its producer do during the next Trade Phase? May a recipient refuse a gift, and are donation victory points tradable?

**Expected:** Keep donation goods in donation area until traded or gifted. Trade all away in next Trade or give away remaining goods at its end. Gifts cannot be refused. Donation VP are an exception to nontradable points.

**Sources:** Rulebook p7 donation goods

**Novelty:** Donation procedure and VP exception

## Q08

How are ordinary colony and research bids committed and resolved? If I bid five ships for a minimum-two card, how much do I spend on buying it; what happens if I pass or no legal card is left?

**Expected:** Announce total ships, secretly divide into colony, research and retained piles; any can be empty. Both bids committed before reveal. Reveal/resolve colonies then research, highest to lowest. Buy one legal card paying all five ships; minimum is an eligibility floor, not the price. Pass keeps bid; must pass if none affordable. Colony bids reveal simultaneously when ready; research bids reveal simultaneously after colony resolution. Equal bids: fewer cards of that type, then higher species tiebreaker.

**Sources:** Rulebook p8 Bidding

**Novelty:** Hidden allocation, order and payment

## Q09

After both bid rounds, which unbought cards are removed, where and in which orientation do they go, and how are tracks refilled?

**Expected:** All remaining cards currently at minimum bid 1 go front-side face up beneath their respective decks. Slide survivors left and fill empty right spaces from top of corresponding deck, without flipping; front side up.

**Sources:** Rulebook pp8–9 Bidding cleanup

**Novelty:** Bid track aging and refill

## Q10

Using standard final scoring, what happens to donations generated in the last Economy, how many points do 2 octagons, 4 large cubes, 3 small cubes and 3 ships add, and how is a tied final score broken?

**Expected:** Producer keeps final-Economy donation points/resources/ships. Each octagon, each pair of large cubes, each group of 3 small cubes and/or ships gives half a point: 1+1+1=3 points. Scores use half points, and there is no tiebreaker.

**Sources:** Rulebook p9 Ending the Game, excluding Precise Resource Scoring variant

**Novelty:** Final donation exception and scoring

## Q11

Caylion commits five ships for a colony. What is its effective bid? When is a seedling token added, and does it stay if that colony is upgraded or traded?

**Expected:** Effective bid 2.5 with no rounding. Add seedling when Caylion takes colony from bid track; starting colony also has one, but a colony acquired by trade gains none. Token stays through upgrade and trade.

**Sources:** Caylion sheet front: Halved Colonization Bids; Seedling Tokens

**Novelty:** Caylion asymmetric colonization

## Q12

May the Eni Et run their own interest converters or upgrade them? A borrower runs an interest converter with two large input cubes and four large output cubes using one blue cube and one large Unity wild: what four cubes result?

**Expected:** Eni Et cannot run own interest converters, including upgraded ones, but can upgrade their cards. Wild passes through unchanged; remaining output must be blue of same size. Result one large wild and three blue large cubes.

**Sources:** Eni Et sheet front: Interest Converters; Unity Wild Resources

**Novelty:** Eni Et lender-only converters and wild preservation

## Q13

A non-Faderan player holding three Acknowledgments invents two technologies in one Trade Phase. Can they trade tokens to somebody else or save them, and what tokens and points move on those two inventions?

**Expected:** Non-Faderan cannot trade away Acknowledgments. Must return one per technology while holding tokens: two return to Faderan and Faderan gains two VP from common pool; holder retains one token.

**Sources:** Faderan front and complete 2019 photo: Acknowledgements

**Novelty:** Faderan acknowledgment lifecycle

## Q14

Does each back in the Faderan relic deck grant a separate draw activation? In what phase is the deck converter used, how is the deck prepared, and can Relic Detector provide a second draw that turn at what additional cost?

**Expected:** Shuffle relic deck before game and keep face down. All backs represent one shared converter, max once per turn in Trade. Relic Detector adds a separate Trade converter: discard one colony of any climate beneath colony deck and spend three green cubes to draw one relic, allowing two draws that turn.

**Sources:** Faderan front: Relic Worlds; back: Relic Detector

**Novelty:** Faderan deck activation and extra draw

## Q15

How may Im’dril Fleet Support be allocated across converters, what must accompany a loan of a support-requiring card, and do borrowed cards lacking support symbols require fleets?

**Expected:** Every required support symbol must match supplied support; one fleet support can split across cards and one card can combine support from fleets. Loan enough fleets with a support-requiring card for recipient to run it. Borrowed cards without symbols need none.

**Sources:** Im’dril sheet front: Fleet Support

**Novelty:** Im’dril support allocation and loans

## Q16

When may Kjasjavikalimm split a five-ship colony bid, into which bids, and may a half be split again? After buying with the first half, which colony count is used for a tie on the second?

**Expected:** At normal chance to buy/pass may instead split into 3 and 2 and act later at those bid positions. Cannot split again that turn. Recalculate current colony count after first purchase for second-half tiebreak.

**Sources:** Kjasjavikalimm front: Military Might

**Novelty:** Kjas split timing and changed tie priority

## Q17

Can Kt’Zr’Kt’Rtl split or rearrange their starting paired cards, upgrade only one half, or loan a pair? How do nullspace colonies enter play, transfer to other players and leave play if consumed?

**Expected:** Starting pairs stay together in depicted arrangement; halves upgrade independently; pair loans as one card and returns end of turn. Nullspace colony enters during Trade by top converter, with no other output; no species background so transfers permanently. If consumed by a world-input converter, remove from game instead of colony-deck bottom.

**Sources:** Kt’Zr’Kt’Rtl front: Nonlinear Thinking; Nullspace Drive

**Novelty:** Kt paired converters and nullspace destinations

## Q18

Which cube colors can large and small Unity wild cubes replace, may Unity wild inputs mix colors, and can Zeth steal Unity wild resources?

**Expected:** Large wild substitutes black, blue or yellow; small wild substitutes green, white or brown. Unity wild inputs accept any same-sized cubes and may mix colors, including gray wilds. Unity wild resources cannot be stolen.

**Sources:** Unity front: Flexible Output; Flexible Input; rulebook p7 Wilds

**Novelty:** Unity flexible size/color and immunity

## Q19

Does an unlicensed player receive a newly invented Yengii technology at Confluence? When may Yengii license it, what card does the recipient get and for how long, and can licensing restore a card spent underneath an upgrade?

**Expected:** No automatic sharing of Yengii inventions. May license in a trade in same Trade Phase as invention; recipient plays its own matching library card permanently. Cannot restore a card hidden under another.

**Sources:** Yengii front: Secretive; Licensing

**Novelty:** Yengii licensing timing and permanence

## Q20

When do Zeth steal, can two stealing converters hit the same player that turn, and is a player protected after trading with Zeth, after gifting donations to Zeth, or after receiving Envoys output only as a gift?

**Expected:** Steal phase after Confluence at end of each turn. Each converter targets exactly one player and two converters cannot hit same player in a turn. Trade with Zeth in previous Trade protects; donation gifts and receiving Envoys as a gift do not. A clearly stated donation trade can protect if Zeth accepts.

**Sources:** Zeth front: Theft; Protection Racket

**Novelty:** Zeth steal timing, target limit and trade protection
