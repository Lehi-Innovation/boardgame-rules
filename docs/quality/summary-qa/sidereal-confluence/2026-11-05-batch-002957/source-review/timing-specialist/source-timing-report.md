# Original 2017 source timing review

Role: source specialist, not final auditor. Scope: the assigned 2017 WizKids booklet, its extraction, original printed species-sheet facsimiles and their provenance, and original component scan assets discovered through the preserved January 2018 module. No summary, answer key, reader, community script, community behavior, later edition, or expansion was used as rules authority.

## Timing ruling

**High confidence: play six turns, with five bidding Confluences and a final Confluence that pays the final technology bonus but has no bidding. There is no seventh Trade/Economy turn.**

1. Turns 1–5 follow the booklet's Trade → Economy → Confluence sequence. Confluence shares inventions and pays the current bonus, then resolves colony and research-team bidding. Return loans and advance the current Confluence card at the turn's end. Apply the species rules when present.
2. Turn 6 has the last Trade Phase and one scoring-only Economy Phase. Donations generated by that last Economy are retained by their producer. Ordinary simultaneous converter/input restrictions still apply; the final resource colors are immaterial to scoring, but resource sizes matter.
3. The final Confluence pays the final bonus for that turn's inventions under the booklet's general sharing rule. The final bonus is **2 points at 3–4 players, 1 at 5–6 players, and 0 at 7+ players; the Yengii bonus is 0 in every case**. There is **no colony bid and no research-team bid** in this final turn.
4. Finish the end-of-turn procedures and score. With Zeth present, its sheet places its Steal Phase after Confluence at the end of each turn, with no last-turn exception printed; the inherited ordering places the last steal before final scoring. This is an application of the sheet's general rule, not a separately printed special final-turn clause.

The chronological placement of final sharing after the scoring Economy is the standard phase order from booklet p6 applied to the special final card; the card does not print a separate reordered sequence. It must not be rewritten as an additional production turn after final sharing.

Booklet p6 gives phase order, p8 gives sharing and bidding, and p9 gives card advancement, last-Economy donation retention and scoring. The p9 prose about having no Confluence cards left is awkward in isolation: treating it as permission to add a seventh full turn contradicts both the six-turn overview and the printed TURN 6 remaining-phase cards. The components resolve the count and eliminate final bidding; no later-edition repair was imported.

## Star's Ruin ruling

**Resolved, high confidence: “Invention Bonus” on the original Faderan reverse sheet refers to the current Sharing Bonus.**

The Faderan reverse's Star's Ruin paragraph grants 2 points plus the points on the current “Invention Bonus.” The actual original **Star's Ruin card, 66/396**, explicitly says that entering play grants **2 victory points plus the current Sharing Bonus**. The exact card crop is `stars-ruin-card-crop.png`; its unedited parent is `component-9-front.jpg`, row 1, column 2. The printed card resolves the sheet's inconsistent terminology. This is the ordinary current Sharing Bonus; there is no separately identified “Invention Bonus” track in the assigned booklet. Do not substitute the research team's own invention-point output or add a second Sharing Bonus payment later for this relic effect.

## Species-sheet scope and remaining gaps

All nine original fronts and the Faderan reverse were viewed directly. The full independent 2019 Faderan front photo was also read to recover text clipped at the right edge of the scan. No material unreadable base-species procedure remained in those images. In particular:

- Caylion: colony bids halved without rounding; research bids unchanged; seedling tokens attach to colonies taken from the bid track and the starting colony, survive loans/trades/flips, double simultaneous Economy uses, and are removed when their colony is discarded. A colony traded to Caylion does not gain one.
- Eni Et: cannot run their own interest converters; matching-color restrictions apply; Unity wild inputs pass through unchanged, while added output uses one matching-size color, matching colored inputs if present. The sheet's specific interest procedure supplies the exception to the booklet's abbreviated wild-resource language.
- Faderan: acknowledgments return one per invented technology while held, paying Faderan one point each; non-Faderan cannot trade them away. Relic deck is shuffled face down; one shared play converter may run at most once per turn. The reverse's relic-specific rules are legible. Relic Detector itself says it cannot be traded on its actual card, which is a card-specific exception to the sheet's general relic-loan rule; no all-card review is claimed here.
- Im'dril: support must cover each indicated support demand; fleet support can split across converters and combine on one converter; unsupported borrowed Im'dril cards cannot run. Borrowed cards lacking support symbols do not need it. No normal colony support; Automated Transport Network explicitly creates the stated exception.
- Kjasjavikalimm: split is elected when their original colony bid would act; split as equally as possible, reinsert the two bids into order, never split again that turn; reassess colony-count ties between the two acquisitions. Count as two players only for the colony bid track. Territory tiles behave as cards and are played/upgraded during Trade.
- Kt'Zr'Kt'Rtl: fixed starting pairs may be loaned as a unit and upgraded independently; nullspace colonies trade permanently and are removed from the game if consumed; unlimited colonies; colony ties always won; infinite final bid tiebreak applies when otherwise equal.
- Unity: wilds substitute only at matching size, are immune to Zeth theft, and pass through Eni Et interest converters without multiplying. Normal one-colony support; computer-world play procedures are printed.
- Yengii: inventions remain private, provide the reduced bonus, and do not count as research teams for bid ties. Licensing places the recipient's matching card into play permanently and may happen during the same Trade Phase as invention; it cannot restore a card hidden under another card.
- Zeth: Envoys output is traded/gifted together to one recipient with a token; tokens cannot circulate among non-Zeth players. Stealing occurs after Confluence, each converter targets one player, no two converters target the same player that turn, colors do not matter, wilds cannot be stolen, and an actual previous-Trade trade grants protection while forced gifts do not.

The timing investigation found **no consequential unresolved source ambiguity** after component recovery in its assigned issues. The precise final Zeth placement above is explicitly marked as inheritance from its general timing rule. No claim is made that every possible original card interaction was inspected or adjudicated.
