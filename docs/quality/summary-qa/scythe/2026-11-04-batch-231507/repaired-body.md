## Overview

Scythe (2016) is an engine-building and territory-control game. This summary covers the **base/core multiplayer game for 2–5 players** from the recovered English rulebook, excluding Automa, expansions, promo additions and optional variants such as Delay of Game. Players take individual turns clockwise, with no rounds. The game ends immediately when someone places their sixth star; the greatest total fortune wins, which need not belong to the player who ended the game.

## Components

The base game has a board; five Faction Mats and five Player Mats; combat, objective, encounter and Factory cards; six structure bonus tiles; resources (wood, food, metal and oil), coins and multipliers; encounter tokens; and power dials. Each faction has one character, four mechs, eight workers, four structures (Armory, Monument, Mine, Mill), four recruits, six technology cubes, six stars, an action token, and popularity/power markers. Characters and mechs fight; workers and structures do not.

## Setup

1. Shuffle combat, encounter and objective decks onto their board spaces. Shuffle Factory cards and randomly select **number of players + 1**, face-down on the board. Return the others to the box **without looking**. Randomly choose one structure bonus tile and place it face-up below the Popularity Track. Put one encounter token on each of the **11 marked territories**. Place coins, resources and multipliers in the supply.
2. Randomly deal each player one Faction Mat and one Player Mat. Seat factions in board order: Nordic, Rusviet, Crimea, Saxony, Polania clockwise, omitting inactive factions.
3. **Faction Mat:** set starting power and draw starting combat cards as indicated. Put the character on its home base, one worker on each of the **two land-adjoined territories** adjoining that base, four mechs over their abilities on the mat, and six stars by its emblem.
4. **Player Mat:** set starting popularity and take starting coins and objectives as indicated (two objective cards). Put six technology cubes on green boxes marked with a black square; four structures on matching boxes; four recruits on bottom-row circular spaces; and the remaining six workers on Produce's red rectangles. Action token starts beside the mat. Leave the Faction Mat's one-time recruit bonus spaces empty.
5. The **lowest-numbered Player Mat** starts; play proceeds clockwise.

Combat-card count is public, contents secret. Objective contents are secret. Coins are not hidden information, but a player need not reveal their total coin value. During combat, players may conceal whether they have committed cards until the reveal.

## Turn Structure

Move your token to a **different section from last turn**, then optionally take that section's top action once, then optionally its bottom action once. You may take either, both, or neither, but must move the token. **Bottom first is not allowed**, and you cannot return to the top action after the bottom. Rusviet may repeat a section, including its Factory card.

Costs are **all exposed red boxes before the action**; benefits are exposed green boxes. First pay the **full cost**, then take as much or as little benefit as desired. You may use top-row gains to pay the bottom-row cost. Exact bottom-row resource costs and coin benefits vary by Player Mat and upgrades: use your mat. If unable to afford any action, take a dead turn by moving the token to a different section and taking neither action. Never pay only part of a cost. Players may overlap a noninteractive bottom-action decision with the next turn, but resolve interactive bonuses and game-end priority in order.

### Control, resources and home bases

A territory is a non-home-base hex, including a lake or the Factory. **A home base is not a territory for any game effect.** By default you cannot move back into any home base, build there or deploy there; setup and forced retreats are exceptions, as is Crimea's unlocked Wayfare. Two bases without matching core factions are inactive expansion placeholders.

You control a territory if you have a unit there, or your structure there with **no enemy unit**. An enemy worker alone on your structure's territory controls it and all its resources. Enemy units do not gain your structure's abilities. You keep those abilities even without control, except the Mill cannot produce on an uncontrolled territory. During a pending battle the defender temporarily controls the territory.

Only wood, food, metal and oil are resources; **workers are not resources**. Resources stay on the board. Pay costs using resources from **any territories you currently control**, even to act elsewhere; spent tokens return to supply. Resource tokens have no supply limit: use multipliers if needed. No limit applies to friendly units or resource tokens on a territory.

## Actions

### Top-row actions

**Move OR coins:** By default, move up to **two different units**, one adjacent territory each, resolving one before the other; you cannot use both selections to move the same unit twice. Alternatively gain **$1**. Upgrades can improve exposed benefits.

- **All units** (characters, mechs, workers) can pick up, carry and drop off any number of resources. Only mechs may also carry any number of **your workers**, never your character. Being carried does not count as the worker's own movement: a carried worker may subsequently move itself, or a worker may move onto a mech and be carried by its subsequent move.
- Units cannot normally cross rivers or enter lakes. Rivers are borders between land hexes; a lake is an entire territory adjoining the surrounding shores. Unlocked abilities give exceptions below.
- All **printed tunnel territories** are adjacent for movement by any unit. Your Mine is an additional personal tunnel, including when an enemy controls its territory; another player's Mine is not your tunnel.
- Any unit may enter a territory held **only by an enemy structure**, taking control without combat. A worker cannot independently enter enemy-worker-controlled territory, or independently move **into or out of** a territory controlled by enemy characters/mechs.
- Your character/mech entering enemy **workers only** stops and cannot move again that turn. Those workers immediately retreat to their home base, leaving resources. Lose **one popularity per worker**, down to zero; insufficient popularity does not prevent the retreat. This is **not combat**.
- Your character/mech entering enemy **characters/mechs** stops and cannot move again that turn. Finish moving the other units, then resolve all combats **before the bottom-row action**; attacker chooses multiple-combat order.
- Your character entering an encounter-token territory stops and **cannot move again that turn**. Resolve the encounter after the whole Move action and all combats, only if the character remains there.
- **Speed** adds one territory of movement to each character/mech selected, not workers. A tunnel jump still costs one movement step; the additional step may be before or after it. A mech can pick up/drop resources and workers mid-movement. Stops for enemy units and character encounters override Speed.

**Bolster:** Pay **$1**, gain **two power OR one combat card** before upgrades. If the combat deck is empty when drawing, shuffle discards into a new deck. If both are empty, draw none. Built Monument also grants **one popularity**.

**Trade:** Pay **$1**, gain **any two resources** (same or different types) **OR one popularity**, before upgrades. Put traded resources on a controlled territory containing at least one worker. You cannot Trade for resources if all your workers are at home. Built Armory also grants **one power**.

**Produce:** Pay every exposed Produce cost (**power, popularity and/or coins**) **before** producing; you must afford all of it. Choose up to **two different controlled territories**, before upgrades; each worker there may produce one token, on its own territory:

| Terrain | Product |
|---|---|
| Mountain | Metal |
| Tundra | Oil |
| Farm | Food |
| Forest | Wood |
| Village | Worker |
| Lake or Factory | Nothing |

Take new workers from the Player Mat **left to right**, after payment, and put them in the producing village. Exposing new costs affects **future** Produce actions, not the cost already paid. You may decline some or all production, including new workers. Workers never return to the Player Mat. A controlled **Mill** provides an additional bonus production territory and itself produces as one worker, in addition to any actual workers there; it can work without an actual worker there.

### Bottom-row actions

Pay the mat's full resource cost; gain its shown coins and primary benefit (coins may be declined). Announce the action so neighbors can check recruit bonuses. Even after completing all upgrades/mechs/structures/recruits, you may still **pay for that bottom action to gain coins and applicable ongoing recruit bonuses**.

- **Upgrade — oil:** Move any technology cube from a green box to an **empty bracketed red box** on your Player Mat, improving a top benefit and reducing a bottom cost. Solid-black-bordered red boxes cannot be upgraded.
- **Deploy — metal:** Choose any mech on your Faction Mat and put it on a **controlled territory with your worker**, never a lake. The uncovered ability applies **from now on to your character and every mech**, not workers.
- **Build — wood:** Take any structure from your Player Mat and put it on a **controlled territory with your worker**. Only **one structure total** may be built per territory, including opponents' structures. Factory allowed; lakes and home bases forbidden. Structures cannot be moved or destroyed. Uncovered top-action benefits apply to future matching actions. **Armory:** +1 power on Trade. **Monument:** +1 popularity on Bolster. **Mill:** bonus Produce territory and one worker's production, only while controlled. **Mine:** personal tunnel for all your unit movement, including Factory Move, even when enemy-controlled. Opponents cannot use your buildings' abilities.
- **Enlist — food:** Take a recruit from **any bottom-row section** of your Player Mat, put it permanently on **any open one-time recruit bonus** on your Faction Mat, and immediately gain that bonus: **two power, two coins, two popularity, or two combat cards**. It cannot later move. The ongoing bonus is the circle uncovered **where you removed the recruit**, independent of your one-time choice.

**Ongoing recruits:** For the rest of the game, when **you, your immediate left neighbor, or your immediate right neighbor** take the corresponding bottom-row action, you may gain its exposed circle bonus. Top actions, encounters and similar Factory actions do not trigger it. Resolve **active player first, then left, then right**; stop if a sixth star ends the game. In two-player play your opponent triggers the bonus **once**, not twice. The mat circles are Upgrade: power; Deploy: coins; Build: popularity; Enlist: combat card (one each).

### Faction and unlocked mech abilities

Each faction's named special ability is available from the start. Mech abilities require removing the covering mech by Deploy. All five factions can unlock Speed as described above.

| Faction | Starting special ability | Riverwalk destinations for character/mechs |
|---|---|---|
| Rusviet | **Relentless:** may repeat last turn's section, including Factory | Farms and villages |
| Crimea | **Coercion:** once per turn spend one combat card as any **one resource**, regardless of card value | Farms and tundra |
| Nordic | **Swim:** workers alone may cross rivers to any land terrain, **not lakes**; does not help character/mechs | Forests and mountains |
| Polania | **Meander:** choose up to **two different options on one encounter card**, in either order; first gain can pay second cost | Villages and mountains |
| Saxony | **Dominate:** no usual limit on combat/objective stars, within six available; may complete both starting objectives, keeping the second after the first | Forests and mountains |

Other unlocked mech abilities:

- **Saxony Underpass:** controlled mountains and all tunnels (including your Mine) are adjacent for character/mech Move actions. **Disarm:** before combat on a tunnel or your Mine, opponent loses two power on the track, once per combat, not per unit.
- **Rusviet Township:** controlled villages and the Factory are adjacent for character/mech Move actions. **People's Army:** in combat with at least one worker, play **one additional combat card total**, still requiring a character/mech. Two mechs and three workers allow three cards.
- **Nordic Seaworthy:** character/mechs move to/from lakes and may retreat to an **adjacent lake** instead of home; mechs may carry workers with them. **Artillery:** before combat, may pay one power to reduce opponent's power by two, once per combat, not per unit.
- **Crimea Wayfare:** character/mechs may move from a territory or home base to **your own or any inactive faction home base** regardless of distance, including inactive expansion bases; not an active opponent's base. **Scout:** before combat steal one random opponent combat card into your hand, once per combat, not per unit.
- **Polania Submerge:** character/mechs move to/from lakes and from any lake to any other lake. **Camaraderie:** no popularity loss for opposing workers forced to retreat **after you win combat as aggressor on your turn**. It does **not** protect against loss for driving off undefended workers without combat.

With Seaworthy/Submerge, workers on lakes require a mech to move off; you cannot leave carried workers or resources behind on a lake when their carrier leaves. Neither ability permits building structures or deploying mechs on lakes. Lake-capable factions can fight on lakes. Polania has no Nordic-style adjacent-lake retreat exception.

## Scoring / Victory Conditions

### Stars and immediate end

Place a star as soon as you achieve: **six upgrades; four deployed mechs; four built structures; four recruits; all eight workers on the board; one revealed completed objective; a combat victory (up to two stars); 18 popularity; or 16 power**. Each goal is awarded once except the two combat stars and Saxony's exception. Other players' stars never block a goal. A placed star is permanent even if power/popularity later drops; current popularity still determines final scoring.

The **sixth star immediately ends play**, with no equalizing turns or further actions. If it comes from a bottom-row action, collect the **primary benefit, coins and your ongoing recruit benefit before placing that star**. Neighbors' power/popularity recruit stars occur only after the active player finishes the action, in active/left/right order, and only if no earlier sixth star ended the game. If units remain intermingled in an unresolved battle, **undo that part of movement**, returning initiating units to the territories they came from; do not resolve another combat.

### Final fortune

Add coins already held, coins for stars, controlled territories and **each complete pair of controlled resources**, plus the selected structure bonus. Pool resources across your controlled territories; ignore an odd leftover token. Workers and combat cards do not score as resources. Controlled lakes count; home bases do not. **Factory counts as three territories total.**

| Current popularity | Per star | Per controlled territory | Per 2 controlled resources |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 0–6 | $3 | $2 | $1 |
| 7–12 | $4 | $3 | $2 |
| 13–18 | $5 | $4 | $3 |

For example, at 10 popularity, five stars, four ordinary territories plus Factory, and 13 resources score **$20 + $21 + $12 = $53**, in addition to held coins and structure bonuses.

**Structure bonus:** Earn it even if enemies control your structures' territories. Score only the selected tile:

| Tile | Count and coin payout |
|---|---|
| Tunnels adjacent to structures | Unique printed tunnel territories, not Mines. 1: $2; 2–3: $4; 4–5: $6; 6: $9 |
| Lakes adjacent to structures | Unique lakes. 1: $2; 2–3: $4; 4–5: $6; 6–7: $9 |
| Encounters adjacent to structures | Unique printed encounter locations, even if token removed. 1: $2; 2–3: $4; 4–5: $6; 6–7: $9 |
| Structures on tunnels | Printed tunnel territories, not Mines. 1: $2; 2: $4; 3–4: $6 |
| Structures in a row | Only your longest continuous straight line. 1: $2; 2: $4; 3: $6; 4: $9 |
| Structures on farms/tundras | Combined qualifying structures. 1: $2; 2: $4; 3: $6; 4: $9 |

Zero qualifying features scores zero. For adjacency tiles, each feature counts once across all structures. Rivers do not break adjacency or a straight row.

Most total coins wins. Break ties in order by **workers + mechs + structures** (not character), then **power**, **popularity**, **resources controlled**, **territories controlled**, **stars placed**.

## Special Rules & Edge Cases

### Combat

After all units finish a Move action, characters/mechs sharing territory with opposing characters/mechs fight, before any subsequent bottom action. The attacker chooses the order of multiple combats. You may attack with zero power/popularity. If both have combat mech abilities, **attacker uses theirs first, then defender**.

1. Secretly choose dial power from **0 to the smaller of seven and your current power** (the track's maximum 16 does not raise the dial limit).
2. Optionally add up to **one combat card per participating character/mech**, even with zero on the dial. Rusviet People's Army adds one card if any workers are present. Workers alone never fight.
3. Reveal simultaneously, add dial and card values; **attacker wins ties**. **Both players pay their dial power** from the track and **discard used cards face-up**. Card values are a temporary boost and do not deduct extra track power.
4. Winner controls territory/resources and must place a combat star if eligible (ordinary maximum two, Saxony unrestricted within six available). You may still fight after reaching your combat-star limit. Losing units—**all characters, mechs and workers**—retreat to their own home base, leaving **all resources** on the battle territory; Nordic Seaworthy can instead retreat eligible units/carried workers onto adjacent lakes.
5. A winning **attacker** loses one popularity per enemy worker forced to retreat, even for workers carried by a defeated mech on a lake; a winning **defender** loses none. Polania Camaraderie provides the combat exception above. The loser draws **one combat card if they revealed at least one power on the dial OR through cards**; a zero-total loser gets none.

A sixth star ends the game before further combat/encounters, with pending attacks undone as above.

### Encounters

Only a character triggers an encounter. It stops on entering the token and cannot move again that turn. After the whole Move action and **all combats**, if the character remains there, discard the token and draw a card, before a following bottom action. If contested entry is lost, the character retreats and **the token stays**. These rules also apply during Factory Move.

Show the art and read thematic text aloud. Normally choose **one affordable option** (Polania up to two different options). You must pay its printed cost, but may take as little of its benefit as desired. Unaffordable coin/popularity options cannot be selected. All resources, workers, mechs and structures gained go to **the encounter territory**, where your character is. Pay **no additional normal action costs** and gain **no additional mat benefits**, including no ongoing recruit bonuses. Put the card **face-down on the bottom of the encounter deck**.

### Factory

The Factory produces no resources and counts as three territories at scoring. **Every player's character**, on its first finish of a Move action there (after winning combat if necessary), must look at the available Factory cards, **choose one**, and return the rest. This is not limited to the first visiting player. Each player gets at most **one permanent card**; later visits cannot exchange it.

Use the card as a fifth action section with optional top then bottom, subject to the usual no-repeat rule (Rusviet exception). Even similar Factory actions give **no corresponding Player Mat coins, structure bonuses or ongoing recruit bonuses**. The bottom action moves **one unit up to two territories within that Move action**, or **three for a character/mech with Speed**; Mine access and all normal movement/stop restrictions apply.

### Objectives and agreements

Only **on your own turn**, before or after completing a top or bottom action, may you reveal a completed objective. You may wait, but must meet **all requirements at the moment of reveal**. Place the objective star and put **both objective cards on the bottom of their deck**. Normally only one objective star; Saxony retains the other card and can score both.

Players may make informal, **unenforceable** agreements. **Coins are the only tangible items exchangeable**; in tournament play even coin exchanges for deals/alliances are forbidden. Negotiation cannot cancel combat already initiated by moving plastic units onto opposing plastic units: fight after Move. Bribes may still influence the outcome.

## Player Reference

**Turn:** different section → optional top → optional bottom; full red cost before optional green gains. Rusviet can repeat. **Normal top actions:** Move two different units one step or gain $1; Bolster $1 for two power or one combat card; Trade $1 for two resources or one popularity; Produce full exposed cost on two territories. Use upgraded mat benefits and building bonuses.

**Bottom resources:** Upgrade oil; Deploy metal; Build wood; Enlist food. Use mat costs/coins. **Sequence after Move:** finish movement → combats → encounter/Factory as applicable → following bottom action. **End:** sixth star immediately; held coins plus popularity-based star/territory/resource-pair income plus structure tile.
