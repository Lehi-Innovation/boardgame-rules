# Dead Last question and answer bank

Version: expanded-v2

## Q01

How does each player prepare their ID and Voting cards, what happens to cards for unused colors, and how is the initial Gold Bar pool dealt?

**Expected:** Stand a visible ID in your chosen color, take the matching Voting set, remove target cards for colors not playing, and retain your Ambush card. Put the Gold deck face down and deal four central Gold Bar cards with values hidden.

**Source:** PDF page(s) 3.

**Novelty:** Distinct procedural rule, source-defined outcome branch, or consequential voting interaction.

## Q02

Is there a player-by-player voting turn order? When may a player change their face-down choice, and when are choices revealed?

**Expected:** There is no turn order. Choices can change until reveal. Once everyone has chosen, all reveal simultaneously.

**Expected:** The tied players conduct another Final Showdown, repeating until a single player at the table has the highest Gold point total. If no winner ends the game, every player returns next round: stand all IDs upright again.

## Q21

## Q22

When casting an ordinary vote rather than Ambush, which card do you choose and where do you put it? What card replaces that choice if you suspect you are the Target?

**Expected:** Choose the Voting card matching the ID color of the person you want to eliminate and place it face down in front of you. Play your own Ambush card instead of choosing another player’s card if you suspect you are the Target.

**Source:** PDF page(s) 5.

**Novelty:** Tests legal action/card identity and face-down placement, rather than reveal timing or Ambush outcome.

## Q23

## Q24

**Expected:** Deal four new central Gold Bar cards for the next round, keeping their values hidden. Refill to four only if play continues; the retained-pot exceptions apply when bars were left behind.

## Q25

In the illustrated vote with four votes for Blue and three for Purple, Blue plays a successful Ambush. Does Purple become the Target because Blue survives, and may voters for Purple survive on that basis?

**Expected:** No. Blue remains the Target from the tally, even though Ambush protects Blue. Purple is not promoted to Target; players who did not vote for Blue are eliminated. Blue also chooses one player who voted for Blue to eliminate.

**Source:** PDF page(s) 7.

**Novelty:** Tests consequential interaction of target determination with later Ambush protection: no retally or promotion of runner-up.

## Q26

Three qualifying players are tied for the highest total at game end. What tie instruction does the booklet give, and does it specify how to arrange a Final Showdown among more than two players?

**Expected:** The tied players must conduct another Final Showdown until a single player at the table has the highest Gold point total. The booklet provides only a two-player Showdown procedure and no arrangement for three or more tied players. A specific multiway procedure is unresolved in this source; do not invent one.

**Source:** PDF page(s) 3, 4, 7, 8.

**Novelty:** Explicitly tests a material source limitation beyond the seed’s exactly-two-player tie. This does not resolve the missing procedure or count as a full procedural PASS.

## Q27

During an extra Final Showdown between two players tied for the qualifying highest total, both Steal. Could a player outside that pair become the winner if the compensation bar gives that player the single highest total?

**Expected:** Yes. Double Steal gives every player outside the pair one Gold Bar from the deck while the finalists get nothing. The tie instruction continues until any single player at the table has the highest Gold point total, so an outside player who thereby becomes the sole highest wins.

**Source:** PDF page(s) 4, 8.

**Novelty:** Tests an exception to treating original tied finalists as the only eligible winners: tie-resolution compensation can make an outsider the single highest player at the table.
