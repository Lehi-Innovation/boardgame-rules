## Overview

Dead Last is a simultaneous voting and negotiation game for 6–12 players. Form alliances, vote together to eliminate opponents, and survive to collect Gold Bar cards. Players eliminated from one round return for the next. Gold collected across rounds determines the winner.

This summary covers the complete English base game under the publisher's Rules 2.0 booklet, including its revised rule for a round with no survivors. No expansions or optional modes are included.

## Components

- 12 Player ID cards and stands, one color per player.
- 12 matching sets of 12 Voting cards. Each set contains 11 cards targeting the other colors and one Ambush card for its own color.
- 80 Gold Bar cards: 26 worth 3 points, 43 worth 4 points, and 11 worth 5 points.
- Two sets of three Final Showdown cards: Share, Steal, and Grab One & Go.

Gold Bars are cards, not points: receiving one bar means receiving one card whose value is 3, 4, or 5 points.

## Setup

1. Each player chooses a color and stands its Player ID card visibly in front of them.
2. Give each player the Voting card set with that color on its backs. Remove cards targeting colors that are not playing. Keep your own Ambush card.
3. Put the Gold Bar deck face down beside the play area. Deal four Gold Bar cards face down to the center; keep their values hidden.
4. Set both sets of Final Showdown cards aside until needed.

## Turn Structure

There is no individual turn order. A round consists of repeated cycles of **Conspire → Vote → Resolve**, using only the surviving players in subsequent votes. After a vote and any Ambush effects, resolve the round according to the number of survivors:

| Survivors | Round resolution |
|---|---|
| Three or more | Continue with another conspiracy and vote among survivors. |
| One | That survivor collects all four central Gold Bars. |
| Two | Those players resolve a Final Showdown for the central Gold Bars. |
| None | Nobody collects the four central bars. Each player who was **not participating in the final vote** receives one Gold Bar from the deck. Leave the four central bars for the next round; add no new bars. |

After round rewards, check for a winner. If there is no winner, everyone returns: stand all Player ID cards upright and begin another round. Refill the central pool to four Gold Bars as needed; bars explicitly left behind remain there.

## Actions

### Conspire

Before every vote, players discuss or signal their intended targets, for roughly a minute and a half at most. Any method of communication is allowed, openly or secretly: speaking, whispering, gestures, showing a Voting card, texting, and so on. Alliances and promises can change. When it is time to vote, the game owner asks everyone for their final decisions.

### Vote

Each surviving player places one Voting card face down. To target someone, use the card matching that player's visible ID color. To defend yourself against being the target, play your Ambush card instead. You may change your chosen card until cards are revealed. Once everyone has chosen, reveal all choices simultaneously.

Count only cards targeting other players; **Ambush cards do not count as votes**. The player receiving the most votes is the Target. If several players tie for the most votes, all of them are Targets.

Resolve elimination:

- Each Target is eliminated unless protected by its own successful Ambush.
- Every other player who did not vote for any Target is also eliminated, subject to the successful Ambush exception below. With tied Targets, voting for any one of them satisfies the voting requirement.
- Knock down eliminated players' ID cards. Leave surviving IDs upright for the next vote.

### Ambush

An Ambush works only if its player is a Target in that vote, including a tie for most votes. A successful ambusher survives and chooses **one of the players who voted for them** to eliminate. They cannot choose an arbitrary player or someone who voted for a different Target.

An Ambush played by someone who is not a Target fails: that player eliminates themselves. Playing Ambush does not add a vote for any color, and does not prevent another player from being a tied Target.

Later votes may have only three surviving players, even though the game starts with 6–12. The booklet illustrates a three-player vote: Red plays Ambush, Yellow votes for Red, and Pink votes for Yellow. Red and Yellow tie at one vote each and are both Targets. Yellow is eliminated; Red survives and can retaliate only against Yellow, who voted for Red. Pink voted for tied Target Yellow and survives, leaving Red and Pink for a Final Showdown.

### Final Showdown

If two players survive the voting, each takes a set containing **Share, Steal, and Grab One & Go**. They may discuss their intentions and a bargain, then each chooses one of these three cards and places it face down. When both are ready, reveal both choices simultaneously and use this table. Numbers are **Gold Bar cards received**, not their point values.

| Your choice | Opponent: Share | Opponent: Steal | Opponent: Grab One & Go |
|---|---|---|---|
| Share | You get 2; opponent gets 2. | You get 0; opponent gets 4. | You get 3; opponent gets 1. |
| Steal | You get 4; opponent gets 0. | Both get 0; see special reward below. | You get 3; opponent gets 1. |
| Grab One & Go | You get 1; opponent gets 3. | You get 1; opponent gets 3. | Each gets 1; leave the other 2 in the center. |

**Grab One & Go always gives its player exactly one bar.** Against either Share or Steal, the opponent receives the other three bars. If both choose Grab One & Go, each receives one and the other two bars remain central.

If both choose **Steal**, neither finalist receives gold. Leave all four central bars for the next round, and give **every other player one Gold Bar from the deck**. Add no new gold to the central pool.

If both choose **Grab One & Go**, each finalist receives one central bar. Leave the remaining two in the center and add just two new bars for the next round.

Every other Showdown pairing distributes all four central bars. If the game continues, deal four new central bars for the next round.

## Scoring / Victory Conditions

Check for victory **at the end of a round**, after Gold Bars have been awarded, regardless of how the round ended. Players who received bars check their accumulated card point values:

- With 6–9 players, reach **at least 25 points**.
- With 10–12 players, reach **at least 24 points**.

If more than one player has reached the threshold, the highest point total wins. If the highest qualifying totals are tied, the tied players conduct another Final Showdown. **Repeat these Showdowns as necessary until a single player anywhere at the table has the highest gold point total.** One extra Showdown does not end the tie if the highest totals remain tied.

**Unresolved source limitation:** the booklet directs tied leaders to a Final Showdown, but its card sets, procedure, and payoff table cover only two players. It provides no arrangement for three or more players tied for the highest qualifying total. This summary cannot supply an official multiway tiebreak procedure; that case requires publisher clarification.

## Special Rules & Edge Cases

- Elimination lasts only for the current round. A player eliminated earlier can receive a consolation bar when the final vote leaves no survivors, or when both finalists Steal.
- The no-survivor reward goes to players absent from the **last vote**, not to players eliminated in that last vote. Those final voters receive no consolation bar for that outcome.
- A double-Steal reward goes to everyone other than the **two finalists**. It is a different trigger from the no-survivor voting result.
- A successful Ambush overrides the usual elimination of its Target, but its retaliatory elimination is restricted to one voter who targeted the ambusher.
- Resolve the entire vote, including Ambush effects, before counting survivors and deciding whether to continue, award the pot, or hold a Showdown.
- Gold Bar values stay hidden when the central pot is dealt. A player reveals reaching the required total at the end-of-round victory check.
- Never treat a Gold Bar reward as a fixed number of points: card values vary from 3 to 5.

## Player Reference

| Step | Reminder |
|---|---|
| Conspire | Agree on a target; any communication method is allowed. |
| Vote | Choose a target card or your Ambush; choices may change until simultaneous reveal. |
| Count | Ignore Ambush cards. Most votes determines all Targets, including ties. |
| Eliminate | Targets and players outside the winning voting group are eliminated; resolve successful Ambush protection and retaliation. |
| End round | One survivor takes 4 bars; two have a Showdown; none rewards earlier-eliminated players from the deck. |
| Check victory | 25 points with 6–9 players; 24 with 10–12. Highest total wins, with Showdowns for tied totals. |
| Restart | Everyone returns. Keep leftover central bars and refill only to four. |
