# Heat core rules question bank

Benchmark version 2 contains 47 questions: original questions 1–20 are unchanged, and questions 21–47 extend rule coverage. Answers use the core booklet only; advanced modules are excluded.

Source: [authoritative extracted core rulebook](../../../../../../extracted/heat-pedal-to-the-metal-rules.txt). References below are extraction line numbers, not PDF pages.

## Question 1

For a track specifying 6 Heat and 3 Stress, what cards form each player's initial draw deck, where do the Heat cards go, what is the starting gear, and how many cards are drawn?

**Answer:** Shuffle 12 Speed, 3 Starting Upgrade and 3 Stress cards (18 total) into the draw deck. Put 6 Heat face up in the Engine. Start in 1st gear and draw 7 cards.

**Source lines:** 77–103.

## Question 2

During Shift Gears, may I stay in the same gear? May I shift from 1st to 3rd, what does it cost, and when and from where is that cost paid?

**Answer:** Staying in the same gear is allowed. A two-position shift such as 1st to 3rd costs 1 Heat, paid immediately during Shift Gears by moving a Heat card from Engine to discard.

**Source lines:** 142–149, 333–346.

## Question 3

In 3rd gear with enough playable cards, may I choose to play just two cards? How many cards must I commit and are they face up or face down?

**Answer:** No. Commit exactly 3 playable cards face down in the Play Area.

**Source lines:** 150–155.

## Question 4

Which steps happen simultaneously, when do the other steps begin, and does one player finish all of steps 3–9 before the next player starts? If two cars are side by side, which acts first?

**Answer:** Steps 1 and 2 are simultaneous across players, in that order. After everyone finishes them, take steps 3–9 one player at a time, each completing their turn before the next, from front to back. The car closest to the Race Line acts first when side by side.

**Source lines:** 117–121, 156–161, 187–188.

## Question 5

Can cars block my passage through a space? If both spots in my destination space are occupied, where do I put my car, and which spot should I prefer?

**Answer:** Cars do not block passage. If the destination is full, stop in the first space with a free spot behind the blocking cars, preferring the spot closest to the Race Line.

**Source lines:** 174–188.

## Question 6

I play a Stress card, then flip a Heat card, a Starting Upgrade card, and finally a Speed 3 card. What happens to each flipped card and the played Stress card, what speed does the Stress provide, and must this be resolved before Adrenaline?

**Answer:** Discard the flipped Heat and Starting Upgrade. Put Speed 3 in the Play Area on the played Stress; the Stress remains in that area until normal cleanup. It contributes speed 3. Resolve all Stress before Adrenaline.

**Source lines:** 189–207, 229–235, 301–308.

## Question 7

In a five-car race before anyone has finished, who receives Adrenaline? What benefits does it give, can its extra movement be taken before or after boosting, and can unused Adrenaline be saved for the next round?

**Answer:** The last two cars to move receive Adrenaline. Each gains access to 1 extra Cooldown and may add 1 speed during React, before or after Boost. Unused Adrenaline cannot be carried over.

**Source lines:** 209–218.

## Question 8

I am in 2nd gear, have a Heat card in hand, and have no Heat in my Engine. During React, can I cool that card into my Engine and then immediately spend it to Boost? Explain the relevant ordering and Cooldown allowance.

**Answer:** Yes. React symbols may be activated in any order. 2nd gear gives Cooldown 1, so first move the Heat from hand to Engine, then pay that Heat from Engine to discard for one Boost.

**Source lines:** 219–228, 240–256, 333–338.

## Question 9

How often may I Boost in a turn, what does it cost, what if my Engine is empty, how is its speed determined, and does that speed count when checking corners?

**Answer:** At most once per turn, costing 1 Heat from Engine to discard. An empty Engine prevents Boost unless Heat is first returned via available Cooldown. Flip and discard non-Speed cards until a Speed card appears; add that card to played cards and move its value. Boost speed counts for corners.

**Source lines:** 201–205, 249–258, 333–338.

## Question 10

My speed is 6. I legally qualify to Slipstream, and the two-space Slipstream takes me across a corner with a speed limit of 4. Is Slipstream optional, what speed is checked for that corner, and how much Heat must I pay if I have enough?

**Answer:** Slipstream is optional and moves 2 spaces without increasing speed. Because it crosses a Corner Line, check speed 6 against limit 4 and pay 2 Heat.

**Source lines:** 259–277.

## Question 11

At total speed 8, I cross two corners, first limit 6 and then limit 4, and I have enough Heat. Do I check only one corner or both, in what order, and how much Heat is paid altogether?

**Answer:** Check both corners in crossing order, using the total speed 8 for each. Pay 2 Heat for limit 6 and 4 for limit 4, totaling 6 Heat.

**Source lines:** 267–280.

## Question 12

In 4th gear, I cross a limit-4 corner at speed 7 with only 2 Heat left in my Engine. What happens to those Heat cards, where does my car go, how many Stress cards do I take and into which zone, and what gear do I end in?

**Answer:** The corner requires 3 Heat but only 2 are available, so pay both Engine Heat into discard and spin out. Move to the first available space before that corner, take 2 Stress directly into hand, and set gear to 1st.

**Source lines:** 270–289, 333–338.

## Question 13

During the optional Discard step, may I discard unwanted Speed cards, Stress cards, or Heat cards? May I browse my own or another player's discard pile, and what information about those piles is public?

**Answer:** Speed cards may be voluntarily discarded; Stress and Heat may not. Nobody may browse discard piles, including their own. Only each pile's top card is public.

**Source lines:** 290–300.

## Question 14

At Replenish Hand, what happens to cards in my Play Area, and do I draw seven new cards or only enough to bring my hand to seven?

**Answer:** Move all Play Area cards to discard, then draw only enough to reach a hand of 7.

**Source lines:** 301–308.

## Question 15

My draw deck runs out in the middle of resolving Stress, before Replenish Hand. What do I shuffle, are the cards in my Play Area included, and do I continue the interrupted flips?

**Answer:** Immediately shuffle the discard pile into a new draw deck, excluding cards still in the Play Area, then continue the required flips.

**Source lines:** 309–316.

## Question 16

When I pay Heat, between which zones does the Heat card move? How can it eventually return to my Engine, and may I normally play it or voluntarily discard it from my hand?

**Answer:** Payment moves Heat from Engine to discard; later reshuffles and draws bring it to hand. Cooldown moves Heat from hand back to Engine. Heat cannot normally be played or voluntarily discarded from hand.

**Source lines:** 320–338.

## Question 17

In 4th gear, my seven-card hand contains only two playable cards and five Heat cards. How do I fill the four-card commitment, how far does my car move, what happens to my gear and committed cards, and may I use React to cool Heat this turn?

**Answer:** Commit the two playable cards and cover the remaining two slots with Heat. Move zero spaces, set gear to 1st, discard all four committed cards, and skip directly to Replenish Hand. React is skipped, so no cooling this turn.

**Source lines:** 347–355.

## Question 18

Two cars cross the final finish line in the same round. Does the one that crossed first automatically win? How is the winner determined, including a side-by-side tie, and when are finished cars removed from the track?

**Answer:** Crossing first during the round does not guarantee victory. Among finishers that round, the furthest ahead wins; closest to the Race Line breaks a side-by-side tie. Remove finished cars at the end of the round and rank them on the Hall of Fame.

**Source lines:** 356–371.

## Question 19

On the final lap, may I use Slipstream to cross the finish line? If normal movement has already taken me across it, may I Slipstream afterward, and must I obey corners located beyond that final finish line?

**Answer:** No Slipstream across the final finish line and no Slipstream after crossing it. Ignore speed limits for corners beyond that line on the final lap.

**Source lines:** 361–365.

## Question 20

A race started with six cars. Four have finished, leaving only two cars racing. In the next round, does Adrenaline go to just the last remaining car or to both remaining cars, and what car count determines that?

**Answer:** Both remaining cars receive Adrenaline because eligibility for one versus two cars is determined by the six cars that started, not the remaining field.

**Source lines:** 209–218, 366–371.

## Question 21

**New coverage:** Tests the source of the lap count and the optional USA learning exception; original Q18 assumes the final lap without defining the race length.

Where do I find the required lap count for the selected track? On the USA track, what is the normal lap count and what shorter first-game learning race does the rulebook suggest?

**Answer:** Use the lap count printed on the board. USA normally uses 2 laps; a first learning race may use 1 lap.

**Source lines:** 70–73, 117–121.

## Question 22

**New coverage:** Original Q1 explicitly fixes a 6-Heat/3-Stress track; this tests the general source of setup quantities.

Should I assume every track always uses exactly 6 Heat and 3 Stress, or what determines how many Heat and Stress cards each player starts with?

**Answer:** Check the selected game board for starting Heat and Stress quantities. Six Heat and three Stress are the typical example, not a universal override of the board.

**Source lines:** 77–86.

## Question 23

**New coverage:** Original Q4 tests ordinary round order, not random initial grid assignment.

How are cars assigned to the starting grid and how does that establish the first round order? May players simply choose their preferred starting position?

**Answer:** Randomly assign participating cars to the starting grid, placing them one at a time into spots in ascending order. This establishes first-round order; preferred positions are not freely chosen.

**Source lines:** 90–92, 158–161.

## Question 24

**New coverage:** The reserve setup instruction is not requested in original Q1 or the spin-out question.

After giving each player their starting Stress cards, what should we do with the remaining Stress cards?

**Answer:** Place the remaining Stress cards face up near the board as a reserve.

**Source lines:** 89.

## Question 25

**New coverage:** Adds an optional setup rule absent from all original questions.

An experienced driver is racing against beginners. What optional setup handicap does the core rulebook suggest, and does it add or remove Heat from whose car?

**Answer:** The experienced driver may remove 1 or 2 Heat from their own car to level the playing field. It is an optional handicap, not extra Heat added to beginners.

**Source lines:** 101–104.

## Question 26

**New coverage:** Original Q2 establishes a two-position shift, but does not test the maximum or permission to buy additional shifts.

During one Shift Gears step, may I go directly from 1st to 4th by paying extra Heat, or what is the largest shift allowed?

**Answer:** No three-position shift is offered. Stay in gear, shift one position for free, or at most two positions for 1 Heat. Paying additional Heat does not grant a three-position shift.

**Source lines:** 142–149, 339–346.

## Question 27

**New coverage:** Tests the timing contrast with original Q8, where cooling can fund Boost during the same React step.

At Shift Gears I am in 1st, my Engine is empty, and I have Heat in hand. May I commit to a 1st-to-3rd shift now and pay for it by cooling that Heat during React later in this turn?

**Answer:** No. The double-shift Heat is paid immediately in step 1 from Engine to discard. Later React/Cooldown cannot fund an earlier shift cost, and Heat in hand cannot pay it directly.

**Source lines:** 333–346, 240–248.

## Question 28

**New coverage:** Original Q6 resolves one Stress only; this adds independent resolution and the distinction between committed and generated cards.

In 3rd gear I commit two Stress cards and one Speed 2 card. The Stress resolutions find Speed 3 and Speed 4. Does each Stress need its own flip sequence, what is my total speed, and do the two found Speed cards count as extra cards I had to commit from hand?

**Answer:** Resolve each Stress separately and add its found Speed card to the Play Area on that Stress. Total speed is 2 + 3 + 4 = 9. Only the original three cards were committed from hand; the found cards are added during resolution, not additional hand commitments.

**Source lines:** 150–155, 196–207, 229–232.

## Question 29

**New coverage:** Tests whether base movement is optional or can be shortened; no original question asks this.

My played cards total speed 8 and no cars block my destination. May I voluntarily move only 5 spaces to avoid crossing a corner?

**Answer:** No. Move exactly the summed speed, 8 spaces here. Mandatory movement cannot be shortened voluntarily to avoid a corner.

**Source lines:** 174–186.

## Question 30

**New coverage:** Combines blocked placement with the corner-speed calculation; original Q5 never checks a corner after blocking.

My total speed is 8, but a full destination makes me stop after only 5 spaces. I still crossed a corner whose limit is 6. For its check do I use speed 8 or the 5 spaces actually moved, and what Heat is due if I can pay?

**Answer:** Use total speed 8, not the shortened distance. Blocking changes placement, not the revealed total speed. Pay 2 Heat for the crossed limit-6 corner.

**Source lines:** 174–186, 267–277.

## Question 31

**New coverage:** Tests the no-crossing branch; original corner questions all cross a line.

I have high speed this turn but stop before the next Corner Line without crossing any Corner Line. Does being close to the corner already require paying Heat for its speed limit?

**Answer:** No. Check a corner only after crossing its Corner Line. Mere proximity without a crossing does not trigger that corner payment.

**Source lines:** 267–277.

## Question 32

**New coverage:** Adds the zero-cost boundary and distinguishes an empty Engine from an unaffordable cost.

I cross a limit-6 corner with no Heat in my Engine. At speed 6, or at speed below 6, must I pay Heat or spin out just because my Engine is empty?

**Answer:** No. Speed equal to or below the limit requires no Heat. An empty Engine alone does not cause a spin out; insufficient Heat matters only when a positive corner cost is owed.

**Source lines:** 270–289, 333–338.

## Question 33

**New coverage:** Original Q12 tests insufficient Heat, not equality between the cost and remaining Heat.

A crossed corner requires 3 Heat and I have exactly 3 Heat left in my Engine. Do I successfully pay and continue, or spin out because paying empties my Engine?

**Answer:** Successfully pay all 3 Heat to discard and continue. Exact payment is enough; ending with an empty Engine does not itself trigger a spin out.

**Source lines:** 275–289, 333–338.

## Question 34

**New coverage:** Original Q11 assumes enough Heat for both corners and Q12 has a single corner; this tests which later corner fails after earlier payment.

In 4th gear at speed 8, I cross a limit-6 corner followed by a limit-4 corner, starting with 5 Engine Heat. Which corner causes a spin out, where do I return, what happens to the Heat, and what Stress and gear penalties apply?

**Answer:** Pay 2 Heat for the first corner, leaving 3. The second requires 4, so pay the remaining 3 and spin out at the second corner. Return to the first available space before that second corner, take 2 Stress into hand, and set gear to 1st. All 5 initial Engine Heat have been discarded.

**Source lines:** 267–289, 333–338.

## Question 35

**New coverage:** Original Q12 tests the high-gear penalty only; this covers the other explicit penalty branch.

I spin out while driving in 2nd gear. How many extra Stress cards do I take, into which zone, and what gear do I end in?

**Answer:** Take 1 extra Stress directly into hand and set gear to 1st. The low-gear penalty is one Stress, rather than the two for 3rd/4th gear.

**Source lines:** 281–289.

## Question 36

**New coverage:** Tests 1st-gear allowance, additive Cooldown and optional use; original Q8 only uses 2nd-gear Cooldown 1.

I have five Heat cards in hand, drive in 1st gear, and qualify for Adrenaline. What Cooldown does 1st gear provide, what is my combined maximum with Adrenaline, and am I forced to use all available cooling?

**Answer:** 1st gear provides Cooldown 3. Adrenaline adds 1, so up to 4 Heat can return from hand to Engine. These are available effects to activate; using every available Cooldown is not compulsory.

**Source lines:** 209–228, 240–248.

## Question 37

**New coverage:** Original Q7 lists both benefits, but does not ask whether they must be used together.

I qualify for Adrenaline but do not want the extra speed because of a corner. May I decline the extra speed while still using its extra Cooldown?

**Answer:** Yes. The extra speed is optional and the extra Cooldown is separately available. Declining the speed does not remove access to Adrenaline cooling.

**Source lines:** 209–228.

## Question 38

**New coverage:** Adds the fewer-than-five branch and separates last-to-move eligibility from position after movement.

Four cars started the race. I am the last car to take my turn but my move overtakes everyone and ends in the lead. Am I still eligible for Adrenaline, and does the second-to-last car also get it in this four-car race?

**Answer:** Yes, the last car to move gets Adrenaline even after overtaking into the lead; eligibility is based on turn order. With four starters only that last car gets it, not the second-to-last.

**Source lines:** 209–218, 366–371.

## Question 39

**New coverage:** Original Q10 assumes legal qualification and never tests which positions qualify.

At the Slipstream step, do I qualify when next to a car, when immediately behind a car, or when neither next to nor behind any car?

**Answer:** Being next to a car or in the space directly behind one qualifies. If neither relationship is present, Slipstream is unavailable. If eligible it remains optional.

**Source lines:** 259–266.

## Question 40

**New coverage:** Adds a phase-order interaction that changes eligibility after Reveal & Move.

My Reveal & Move ends away from every other car, but my later Boost lands immediately behind a car. Can I use Slipstream this turn, or was eligibility already locked in before Boost?

**Answer:** Yes. React/Boost occurs before Slipstream, so use the position after Boost when checking eligibility at the Slipstream step.

**Source lines:** 219–228, 249–264.

## Question 41

**New coverage:** Tests whether simultaneous qualifying cars multiply the action; the original set never tests this.

At the Slipstream step I am next to one car and immediately behind another. Do I gain two spaces for each qualifying car, or just one two-space Slipstream move?

**Answer:** Just one optional two-space Slipstream move. Multiple nearby cars do not multiply the movement into four spaces.

**Source lines:** 259–266.

## Question 42

**New coverage:** Adds the interaction between optional Slipstream movement and the blocked-destination placement rule.

I legally Slipstream, but its destination space has both spots occupied. Does Slipstream allow sharing an occupied spot, or how is that destination handled, and does the reduced movement change my corner-check speed?

**Answer:** Do not share an occupied spot. Apply the blocked-destination rule: place in the first space with a free spot behind the blocking cars, closest to the Race Line if available. Slipstream does not add to corner-check speed, whether its movement is shortened or not.

**Source lines:** 174–188, 259–277.

## Question 43

**New coverage:** Original Q19 asks only about corners beyond the line; this contrasts corners on both sides in one turn.

On my final lap at speed 8, I cross a limit-4 corner before the finish line and then a limit-3 corner beyond it. I have enough Heat. Does crossing the final finish line waive both corner checks, and how much Heat is due?

**Answer:** The pre-finish corner still requires 4 Heat. Ignore only the post-finish corner limit. Crossing the final finish line does not waive a corner crossed before it.

**Source lines:** 267–280, 356–365.

## Question 44

**New coverage:** Tests the nonfinal-lap branch of the exceptions in original Q19.

I cross the start/finish line at the end of a lap that is not my final lap. May a legal Slipstream carry me across it, and do corners beyond it still have their normal speed limits?

**Answer:** Yes, an otherwise legal Slipstream can cross on an intermediate lap. Normal corner limits still apply; the Slipstream prohibition and post-line speed-limit exception concern the final lap only.

**Source lines:** 259–280, 356–365.

## Question 45

**New coverage:** Original Q18 covers ranking and removal, not the condition for ending play for everyone.

The winner has already finished and been placed on the Hall of Fame, but other cars are still racing. Do those cars stop immediately, or when does the race finish for the remaining players?

**Answer:** Continue rounds until all cars have reached the finish. Establishing the winner does not end play for unfinished cars.

**Source lines:** 366–371.

## Question 46

**New coverage:** Adds the source-defined distinction between two named lines; no advanced-play mechanics are requested.

In the core game, does crossing a Legends Line trigger a Check Corner payment, or which line matters and when is the Legends Line used?

**Answer:** Check Corner concerns crossing a Corner Line. The Legends Line is used only in advanced play and is not a core-game corner-payment trigger.

**Source lines:** 162–169, 267–277.

## Question 47

**New coverage:** Tests when the Cluttered Hand condition is evaluated relative to shifting, rather than the already-forced case in original Q17.

I ended last round in 3rd gear and now have only two playable cards plus Heat in hand. If I shift down one position to 2nd before committing cards, do I have to suffer Cluttered Hand because I began the round in 3rd?

**Answer:** No. Shifting down one to 2nd is free and happens before card commitment. Current gear then requires only two cards, so commit the two playable cards and take a normal turn; the previous gear does not force Cluttered Hand.

**Source lines:** 142–155, 347–355.
