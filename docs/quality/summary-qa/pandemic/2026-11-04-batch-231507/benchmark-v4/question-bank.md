# Pandemic — benchmark-v4

## Q01

For 2, 3 and 4 players, how many starting Player cards does each receive, which cards are excluded before dealing, and where do pawns start?

**Answer:** 2 players: 4 each; 3: 3 each; 4: 2 each. Remove Epidemic cards before shuffling/dealing City and Event cards. All chosen role pawns start in Atlanta.

**Source:** extraction 108–128; matching PDF p2 setup table

## Q02

Describe initial infection: marker position, cities and cubes in each group, and destinations of the revealed and remaining Infection cards.

**Answer:** Marker at leftmost 2. Reveal three cities with 3 matching cubes each, three with 2 each, three with 1 each (18 cubes). Nine revealed cards face up in Infection Discard Pile; remaining form Infection Deck.

**Source:** extraction 202–209

## Q03

How is the Player Deck prepared for Introductory, Standard and Heroic difficulty, including uneven piles and unused Epidemic cards?

**Answer:** Use 4, 5, 6 Epidemics respectively; remove unused Epidemics from game. Divide remaining Player cards into that many roughly equal face-down piles; shuffle one Epidemic into each. Stack with smaller piles on bottom.

**Source:** extraction 179–185

## Q04

How many actions may you take, may you repeat or stop early, where do discarded action cards go, and what phases follow before whose turn?

**Answer:** Up to 4; repeats allowed and fewer than 4 allowed. Discard action cards to Player Discard Pile. Draw 2 Player cards, then Infect Cities, then player on left.

**Source:** extraction 189–195,285–290

## Q05

State the requirements and card costs for Drive/Ferry, Direct Flight, Charter Flight and Shuttle Flight, including a white line crossing the board edge.

**Answer:** Drive/Ferry follows a white line; edge lines wrap (Sydney–Los Angeles). Direct: discard destination City card. Charter: discard departure/current City card to go anywhere. Shuttle: both departure and destination have research stations; no card discard.

**Source:** extraction 280–296; matching PDF p4 movement text

## Q06

How do you build a research station normally, and what happens if all six are on the board or the box supplied extras?

**Answer:** Discard matching current-city card and place station in current city. Normally take from supply; if six built relocate a station from anywhere on board. Set extras beyond six aside during setup.

**Source:** extraction 298–302,328–329

## Q07

A non-Medic is in a city with two cured disease colors and an uncured color. What does one Treat Disease action remove, how are the other colors cleared, and where do removed cubes go?

**Answer:** One action removes one cube of a chosen uncured color or all cubes of one chosen cured color. Separate Treat Disease action per cured color; one action does not clear multiple colors. Removed cubes return to cube supply.

**Source:** extraction 303–307,325–327

## Q08

How is a cure discovered, may the research-station city have a different color, what happens to existing cubes, and what if no cubes of that color are on the board?

**Answer:** At any research station discard 5 same-color City cards; city color irrelevant. Move cure marker to corresponding indicator; existing cubes remain and cured disease may still infect. If no cubes on board at cure discovery it is immediately eradicated; flip marker.

**Source:** extraction 318–323,343–347,555–556

## Q09

For normal Share Knowledge, which card may move, where must both players be, whose agreement is required, and what if the recipient reaches eight cards?

**Answer:** Give or take City card matching shared current city; both players there and both agree. Recipient immediately discards a card or plays an Event to return to seven.

**Source:** extraction 310–317,557–562

## Q10

How does the Researcher change Share Knowledge, on whose turn can it happen, and may another player give the Researcher an arbitrary nonmatching City card using that exception?

**Answer:** Researcher may give any City card from her hand to a player in same city. Can occur on either player’s turn through Share Knowledge. Exception is only from Researcher’s hand, not arbitrary transfers to her.

**Source:** extraction 664–674

## Q11

After actions, exactly when do you lose for insufficient Player cards; are the two drawn together, and can discards be reshuffled?

**Answer:** Draw top two together. If fewer than two as about to draw, lose immediately. Do not reshuffle discards.

**Source:** extraction 430–435

## Q12

Resolve an Epidemic whose bottom Infection card names a city already holding two cubes of that color: give all steps, cube placements/outbreak, card destinations, and replacement draw rule.

**Answer:** Increase marker one space. Draw bottom Infection card; add one cube to reach three then outbreak (unless eradicated/protected); discard Infection card. Shuffle only Infection Discard Pile onto top of existing Infection Deck. Remove resolved Epidemic from game; no replacement.

**Source:** extraction 436–460

## Q13

Starting before any Epidemics, give the number of Infection cards flipped after 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6 Epidemics.

**Answer:** 2, 2, 2, 3, 3, 4, 4.

**Source:** matching PDF p3 Infection Rate Track diagram (seven spaces 2,2,2,3,3,4,4); extraction 399–403,439–440

## Q14

If both drawn Player cards are Epidemics, how are they sequenced, when can an Event be used between them, and when is an over-limit hand reduced relative to drawn Epidemics?

**Answer:** Resolve all three steps of first then all three of second. Events allowed after first resolves, before second. Resolve drawn Epidemics before reducing over-seven hand.

**Source:** extraction 457–464,522–523

## Q15

During normal Infect Cities, which deck end and how many cards are used, how is each resolved for a normal, eradicated, or cured-but-not-eradicated disease, and where does the card go?

**Answer:** Top cards equal current infection rate; resolve one at a time. Add one matching cube; if already three trigger outbreak instead of fourth. Eradicated: no cube; cured but not eradicated still infects normally. Discard each to Infection Discard Pile.

**Source:** extraction 399–407,423–429

## Q16

An outbreak spreads to a connected city already holding three cubes of that color. Describe timing, outbreak-marker movements, return spread into the first city, and whether different colors share a single three-cube cap.

**Answer:** Advance marker for initial outbreak and place one same-color cube on every connected city. At saturated neighbor do not add fourth; resolve chain outbreak after current outbreak, advancing marker again. Do not add cube to cities already outbreaked during this Infection card resolution. Up to three of EACH color, not three total.

**Source:** extraction 479–492

## Q17

An Infection card has just been drawn and would cause an outbreak. Can Airlift move the Quarantine Specialist before that outbreak? When may it be played before the next card, who chooses its use, what action cost applies, and where is the played Event discarded?

**Answer:** Cannot play between drawing and resolving card; must resolve outbreak first. Can play after outbreak before next Infection card, on any player’s turn. Event player decides use; no action cost. Normal played Event goes to Player Discard Pile.

**Source:** extraction 496–507

## Q18

How can the Contingency Planner recover an Event: action cost, eligible discard depth, storage capacity, hand-limit treatment and destination after play?

**Answer:** As one action take any Event from anywhere in Player Discard Pile onto Role card. Only one stored at a time; not in hand/does not count toward hand limit. After stored Event played remove from game instead of discard.

**Source:** extraction 582–595

## Q19

For the Dispatcher, state both movement options and permission, whose cards pay Direct/Charter Flights and which city a Charter card matches, whether they may direct another pawn to Treat, and whether they can use Operations Expert’s special move.

**Answer:** As action move any pawn to city with another pawn OR move another pawn as own; owner permission required for another pawn. Dispatcher hand pays Direct/Charter; Charter matches moved pawn’s departure city. Cannot direct nonmovement actions such as Treat. Cannot use Operations Expert special move when moving that pawn.

**Source:** extraction 599–612,676–680

## Q20

What wins immediately, must diseases be eradicated or the board clear, and what are the three exact loss triggers including cube-supply timing?

**Answer:** Discover all four cures: immediate win regardless of remaining cubes; eradication unnecessary. Lose at eighth outbreak/last outbreak-track space. Lose immediately when cannot place cubes actually needed of required color during epidemic, outbreak or infection. Lose when unable to draw two Player cards after actions, not merely when deck becomes empty.

**Source:** extraction 447–451,491–492,515–530

## Q21

Describe setup of the board supplies, research station, outbreak and cure markers, Role and Reference cards, unused roles/pawns, and first-player choice.

**Answer:** Separate cube supplies by color; put six research stations nearby and one in Atlanta. Outbreak marker at 0; cure markers vial-side up near indicators. Each player gets Reference card and random face-up Role with matching pawn in Atlanta; unused roles/pawns removed. First player has highest-population City card in hand.

**Source:** extraction 108–123,187–188

## Q22

Who decides the active player’s actions if teammates disagree, how are hands displayed by difficulty (including the experienced-group option), and which discard piles may anyone inspect?

**Answer:** Active player decides, though advice/discussion encouraged. Introductory: face-up hands. Standard/Heroic: private by default; experienced groups may choose open hands. Either discard pile freely examinable at any time.

**Source:** extraction 196–198,508–514,524–525

## Q23

Compare removing the last cube before a disease is cured with removing the last cube after its cure. How is eradication marked, and do later normal infections or Epidemics place that disease’s cubes?

**Answer:** Uncured last cube removal has no eradication effect. Cured last cube removal eradicates; flip cure marker to eradication symbol. Eradicated disease gets no new cubes during normal infections or Epidemics.

**Source:** extraction 308–309,338–347,441–446

## Q24

An Epidemic’s bottom card names an unprotected city with zero cubes of its non-eradicated color. How many cubes are placed, is there an outbreak simply for reaching three, and where does that Infection card go before Intensify?

**Answer:** Place three cubes. No outbreak merely for reaching three from zero. Discard bottom card to Infection Discard Pile before Intensify.

**Source:** extraction 441–446

## Q25

An Epidemic’s bottom card is of an eradicated disease. Which of Increase, cube placement, discarding the Infection card and Intensify still occur?

**Answer:** Increase still moves marker one space. No disease cubes placed. Discard Infection card and still shuffle Infection Discard Pile onto top of Infection Deck.

**Source:** extraction 436–456

## Q26

Two Epidemics are drawn together. With no intervening effects, no eradication/protection, and zero cubes initially in the second Epidemic city, what card(s) does the second Intensify shuffle and what happens when normal Infections immediately draws that city?

**Answer:** After first Intensify discard pile empty; second Epidemic card is sole Infection card to reshuffle onto top. Second Epidemic gives city three cubes; normal Infections draws it first and causes outbreak.

**Source:** extraction 408–412,441–458; matching PDF p6 yellow double-Epidemic callout

## Q27

What exactly changes for the Scientist’s cure action, and do the research-station and matching-color requirements still apply?

**Answer:** Only four rather than five City cards of disease color. Still Discover a Cure at research station; cards must all match disease color.

**Source:** extraction 318–321,659–661

## Q28

How does Operations Expert build a station: location, action cost, and whether any City card must be held, used or discarded?

**Answer:** As one action build in current city. No City card needed, used or discarded.

**Source:** extraction 632–640

## Q29

State all requirements and limits of Operations Expert’s special travel, including departure location, destination, card choice, action cost and frequency.

**Answer:** As one action once per turn move from research station to any city by discarding any City card.

**Source:** extraction 632–643

## Q30

The Medic treats an uncured disease in a city containing multiple colors. How many cubes and colors can this action remove?

**Answer:** All cubes of one chosen color, even uncured. Other colors require separate treatments.

**Source:** extraction 615–616,625–626; 325–327

## Q31

A cure is discovered while the Medic is already in a city with cubes of that color. Later another player uses Dispatcher or Airlift to move the Medic to another such city. What is removed, when, and at what action cost?

**Answer:** Automatically remove all cubes of cured color from city Medic occupies or enters, including when cure discovered while already there. Works on other players’ turns via Dispatcher/Airlift; automatic removal costs no action.

**Source:** extraction 618–624,681–682

## Q32

What disease placements and outbreaks does the Medic prevent at his location; does that protect adjacent cities or uncured diseases?

**Answer:** Prevents placement and outbreaks of cured diseases in own city. Does not extend to adjacent cities or uncured diseases.

**Source:** extraction 623–630

## Q33

What geographic area and disease colors does the Quarantine Specialist protect from placements and outbreaks, and does the ability change setup cubes?

**Answer:** Own city and every connected city; all disease colors. Prevents both cube placements and outbreaks. No effect on setup cubes.

**Source:** extraction 647–658

## Q34

What does Resilient Population remove, where from and to, and may it remove the Infection card just discarded during an Epidemic before Intensify shuffles it?

**Answer:** Remove any one Infection card from Infection Discard Pile from game. Yes, can play between Infect and Intensify steps including just-discarded card.

**Source:** extraction 45–49; matching PDF p1 Resilient Population card

## Q35

What does One Quiet Night skip, are Infection cards flipped for that skipped step, and does it skip an Epidemic’s Infect step?

**Answer:** Skip next Infect Cities step; do not flip Infection cards for that step. Does not skip Epidemic Infect step (a different step).

**Source:** extraction 61–63,436–456; matching PDF p1 One Quiet Night card

## Q36

What can Airlift move and where, and whose permission is required if the pawn is another player’s?

**Answer:** Move any one pawn to any city. Get permission before moving another player’s pawn.

**Source:** extraction 547–550

## Q37

Which cards count against the seven-card hand limit, how do Role and Reference cards and a stored Contingency Planner Event count, and how is a hand of nine reduced?

**Answer:** City and Event Player cards in hand count. Role/Reference cards and Event stored on Planner Role card do not. Discard cards or play Event cards until at most seven (after resolving drawn Epidemics).

**Source:** extraction 461–464,526–527,583–595

## Q38

What does Government Grant place, where may it be placed relative to the player, and does it require a City card?

**Answer:** Add one research station to any city, including a city the player does not occupy. No City card needed.

**Source:** official-event-puzzle.pdf p2 Government Grant modern base card reproduction

## Q39

What deck and how many cards does Forecast let you draw, view and rearrange, and where are those cards returned?

**Answer:** Draw, look at and rearrange top six Infection Deck cards. Put them back on top of Infection Deck.
