# Source recovery and scope

The authoritative 24,430-byte extraction was read completely (lines 1–682) and preserved in originals. Its setup table and movement labels are incomplete. The recovered eight-page 2015 F2Z publisher booklet matches its seven roles, prose, examples and layout. All eight pages were inspected as rendered images; pages 2–4 recover the setup values, Direct Flight text and Infection Rate track. The separate newer publisher-rules.pdf was an unsuccessful edition-recovery candidate and is retained as history, not authority.

The booklet illustrates Airlift, One Quiet Night and Resilient Population, but does not reproduce Government Grant or Forecast’s full effects. Government Grant is printed in the publisher’s Event Speciale PDF, page 2, with matching modern base-game artwork. Only that base component’s rule is used; expansion cards and puzzle rules are excluded. No puzzle scenario example is needed or used as authority.

Forecast’s complete printed instruction is visible in forecast-card-photo.jpg, a photograph of the matching modern Z-Man base component hosted by a replacement-parts seller. Its artwork, title and visible text fragments are corroborated by the publisher’s official-components-2.webp. The physical component’s text, rather than seller prose, supplies the six-card count and return-on-top destination. This secondary hosting is explicitly recorded in forecast-photo-provenance.json. No unrelated edition or expansion rule substitutes for base rules.

Download URLs, hashes and transport details are retained in the *provenance.json files. The legacy publisher host had an expired TLS certificate; retrieval of the public PDF used disabled certificate verification, recorded in matching-download-provenance.json.
