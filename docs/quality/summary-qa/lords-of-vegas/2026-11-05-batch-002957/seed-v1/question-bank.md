# Lords of Vegas — seed-v1

## Q01

How are starting properties and money assigned, where do the dealt cards go, and how is the End of Game card inserted?

Remove End of Game, shuffle, deal two property cards per player. Mark those two lots; take starting money equal to the sum of their printed card money values, used only in setup. Discard by color/Strip with counts visible. Divide remaining shuffled deck into four roughly equal stacks; End of Game on top of fourth, other three on top.

Source: Original p2 Setting Up; unified p5

## Q02

Who takes the first turn, how does turn order proceed, and what is the normal Draw/Play sequence?

Each rolls two dice; highest roller takes player card and first turn. Pass player card left after each turn. Draw property card, take its lot, pay all owned lots, pay/score indicated casinos, discard appropriately, then Play actions.

Source: Original p2 Taking Turns/On Every Turn; p3 Ending Step 1

## Q03

You draw the card for an empty lot. When is it acquired and paid, and do the printed card starting-money values pay again?

Place your marker before payouts. All marked lots including newly acquired lot pay their owner $1. Card money values are used only during setup, not drawn-turn income.

Source: Original p2 Setting Up/Take Over Your Lot/Owned Lots Pay

## Q04

You draw a lot card for a sprawled tile with an opponent’s 5, although the board prints 2. What happens to the tile and die, and who receives its imminent matching-color money?

Tile stays; replace opposing die with your own retaining current value 5, not printed 2. Acquisition precedes payouts, so you receive $5 for that die when its casino pays.

Source: Original p2 Take Over Your Lot/Money Paid

## Q05

When do same-color tiles form one casino, can they be divided, and is the boss determined by total pips or something else?

Edge-connected same-color tiles on same block form a single indivisible casino; diagonal-only contact does not. Boss is owner of highest individual die, not greatest total pips or most tiles. Casino colors do not denote player colors.

Source: Original p1 Casino Tiles/Player Dice; p2 Who Owns What

## Q06

A three-tile gold casino contains Red 5 and Blue 3 and 2; Red also owns two marked lots. On a gold payout, how much money and how many points does each receive from these holdings?

Red receives $7: $5 die plus $2 lots. Blue receives $5 from dice. Red boss receives 3 casino points, Blue no casino points.

Source: Original p2 Owned Lots Pay/Money Paid/Points Earned

## Q07

How does Pay The Strip differ from a color payout, and what qualifies a casino whose only one tile touches The Strip?

Color pays all casinos of that color anywhere. Strip pays all colors with at least one casino edge adjacent to Strip. Entire qualifying casino pays money/points for all tiles, not only touching tile.

Source: Original p2 Pay Based on Color/Pay The Strip; unified p8

## Q08

Which actions may you take in Play, in what order and how often, and may another player perform an action on your turn?

Build, sprawl, remodel, reorganize, gamble in any order. All may repeat except gamble once per own turn; reorganization also limited per die per turn. No player takes actions on another player’s turn; trading may occur at any time.

Source: Original p3 Step 2 Play/Reorganize; p4 Trading

## Q09

How do you build on your marked lot whose board price is $6 and printed die is 2? State cost, marker and die handling, and tile choice.

Pay $6; choose available casino tile color. Remove/reclaim marker, place tile on your owned lot and your die set to board value 2. Same-color edge neighbors merge automatically.

Source: Original p3 Build; unified p10 Build

## Q10

You boss a gold casino and want to sprawl onto an edge-adjacent empty unowned $9 lot with printed die 3. What do you pay and place, and may you instead sprawl onto an opponent’s marked lot or a merely diagonal lot?

Pay $18, place available gold tile with own die 3. Must boss origin; target empty unowned and edge-adjacent; neither opponent-owned nor diagonal-only target is legal. Cannot sprawl without available tile of same color.

Source: Original p3 Sprawl

## Q11

How does remodeling a two-tile casino work, including eligibility, cost, tile supply, and a merger with a neighboring casino whose highest die beats yours?

Must boss casino; remodel all its tiles into one new color with enough available tiles. Pay $10 ($5 each); replace all tiles with new-color tiles; dice/holdings remain. Edge-adjacent same-color casinos merge; owner of larger die becomes boss even if another player.

Source: Original p3 Remodel and example; unified p11

## Q12

May a non-boss with one die reorganize a casino with dice 5, 3 and 2? State who pays how much, which dice roll and who rolls them, and where multiple dice may return.

Yes, at least one own die suffices; acting player pays $10 total pips of all dice. All dice reroll, each owner rolling their own. Each owner returns dice only to their own original tiles, choosing assignment among those tiles if multiple.

Source: Original p3 Reorganize

## Q13

A die was reorganized earlier this turn and its casino then merges into a larger casino. Can the larger casino be reorganized this turn, and are free boss-tie rerolls prohibited too?

No: no die may be reorganized more than once per turn, so any previously reorganized die prevents reorganization of the enlarged casino. Boss-tie rerolls are free and not subject to reorganization once-per-turn limit.

Source: Original p3 Reorganize; p4 Reroll if Tied; unified p11

## Q14

After a merger, Red 5 and Blue 5 tie above Green 4. What rerolls, at what cost, and who bosses if both 5s reroll to 3? What if all largest tied dice belong to one player, or only smaller dice tie?

Immediately reroll only tied largest dice at no cost. If both become 3, Green unchanged 4 is boss; do not keep rerolling smaller 3s. Same-player highest tie requires no reroll; ties below highest require none. If different players remain tied for current highest, repeat with current highest until one player controls highest.

Source: Original p4 After Each Action Reroll if Tied and diagram

## Q15

You have $18 and gamble at an opponent’s four-tile casino whose boss has $15. What is the maximum bet, who gets the House card, and how many times may you gamble this turn?

Maximum $15: minimum of $20 tile cap, own $18 and boss $15. Give casino’s boss House card; must choose another player’s bossed casino. Gamble once during own turn.

Source: Original p4 Gamble

## Q16

For a legal $4 bet against a boss who has $7, resolve every possible two-dice sum group, including the limited doubled payout. Is a winning bettor also losing their stake?

3/4/9/10/11: win $4 from boss and retain/get stake back. 2/12: nominal win $8 but receive only boss’s available $7 and retain/get stake back. 5/6/7/8: lose/pay $4 to boss.

Source: Original p4 Gamble; unified p13

## Q17

What may be traded, when may trading occur, how are traded lots and dice transferred, and what cannot be promised or traded?

Trade at any time even off-turn; money, lots, casino dice and actions on acting player’s current turn. Replace lot marker with recipient marker; replace die with recipient die at same current pip value, not board value. No points or casino tiles; no promises of future-turn actions/exchanges/transfers. No actions on another player’s turn.

Source: Original p4 Trading

## Q18

Starting at score 8, where do single casino awards of 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 points leave you? Can two separate one-tile casinos combine their awards, and in what order are different casinos scored?

1 leaves 8; 2 or 3 reaches 10; 4 or 5 reaches 12. Insufficient excess at a break is lost; cannot pool separate casino awards. Score each casino independently smallest to largest, player chooses equal-size order; two single-tile awards leave 8.

Source: Original p2 Scoring; p3 scoring example and multiple casinos

## Q19

What happens when the End of Game card is drawn, including remaining actions, final payouts, winner and tied score? Is there an immediate alternative win condition?

Game ends immediately; no subsequent Play actions. Final payout all owned lots and casinos touching Strip, with ordinary money and scoring rules. Most points wins; tied most points breaks by most money. 90+ points wins immediately in base game.

Source: Original p4 Winning; unified p16

## Q20

In two-player play, what happens to F cards drawn during setup and during a turn? May anyone build on F, and can a turn pay more than once before actions?

No building on F block. Setup F card is discarded and replaced; valid two cards determine initial properties/money. During turn do not mark F; pay owned lots and indicated casinos/points, discard then repeat Draw until A–E card resolved. Each such draw pays, so multiple payouts in one turn before Play; End of Game still ends immediately.

Source: Original p4 2-Player Rules/Winning; unified p16 2-Player Rules
