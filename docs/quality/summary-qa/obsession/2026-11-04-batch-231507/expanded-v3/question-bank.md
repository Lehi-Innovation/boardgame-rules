## Q01

How many ordinary turns does each player take in the standard 16-round game, and what happens (including service rotation and ongoing tile benefits) on a courtship round?

**Expected:** Twelve ordinary turns: four seasons, each three ordinary rounds then a courtship. No player turn, rotation, monument reputation or Servants’ Hall use on courtship; only courtship resolves.

**Source:** pp.16,76; extraction lines 614–629,2898–2903

## Q02

State the ordered procedure for hosting an activity, including when round-track events and ongoing effects happen and where used servants, gentry and the tile go at cleanup.

**Expected:** Rotate service; resolve round event then ongoing effects; choose activity; invite exact required gentry; provide service; enjoy favours; optional market buy; clear board. Used servants to Expended Service, played gentry to personal discard, tile upgraded if needed and returned to correct tableau column; last player advances marker.

**Source:** pp.17–20; lines 642–796

## Q03

**Expected:** All servants directly Available; all personal discard gentry to hand; round event then ongoing effects; choose £200 or free market refresh; optional one tile purchase; last player advances marker. Hiring on pass is expansion/optional and excluded here.
