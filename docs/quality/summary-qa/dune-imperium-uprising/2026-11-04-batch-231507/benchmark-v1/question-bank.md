# benchmark-v1

2023 English Uprising v23-10-12, standard 2–4-player multiplayer including required two-player Rival, optional standard CHOAM; no solo, Rival variants, six-player teams or cross-game expansions. Relevant official FAQ January 13 2025 clarifications included.

## Q01

How is the Conflict deck built, and what happens to unused Conflict cards?

**Expected:** Shuffle all four III cards at the bottom, five randomly dealt of nine II cards above, one randomly dealt of three I cards on top: ten total. Return unused cards to the box without looking.

**Source:** Main PDF p4 / extraction 190–209

**Coverage:** Conflict deck construction

## Q02

What are the starting score, Agents, troops and water for a human in a four-player game versus a three-player game?

**Expected:** Four players start at 1 VP; three at 0. Each has two Agents on Leader and third beside board, three troops in garrison with remaining nine in supply, and one water.

**Source:** Main pp4–5 / extraction 254–303

**Coverage:** Player setup

## Q03

Can you Reveal with Agents left, and can you later take an Agent turn in the same round? What cards supply Reveal effects and where do all played cards go?

**Expected:** May Reveal early; no more turns this phase after Reveal. Only cards remaining in hand and just revealed supply Reveal effects, not Agent-turn cards. Clean Up puts all Agent and Reveal cards in discard pile.

**Source:** Main pp8,12 / extraction 360–379,637–673

**Coverage:** Reveal commitment and destinations

## Q04

May an Agent-turn card effect provide resources to pay the destination entry cost? How many Agents can one card send, and can a card without Agent icons be used?

**Expected:** Entry costs paid immediately before any card or space effects; cannot use later gains to pay entry. One card sends one Agent using one matching icon. No-icon card cannot be played on an Agent turn.

**Source:** Main p9 / extraction 425–452

**Coverage:** Entry legality and costs

## Q05

At a Combat space you recruit three troops this turn and already have four garrisoned. How many can deploy? What if the destination is not a Combat space, and where do recruited troops originate?

**Expected:** May deploy all three newly recruited plus at most two older garrison troops, five total. Normal deployment unavailable at noncombat space. Recruit from own supply into garrison; empty supply prevents recruiting.

**Source:** Main p10 / extraction 535–566

**Coverage:** Recruit and deployment limits

## Q06

How do you use a Spy Agent icon, Infiltrate and Gather Intelligence? Can the same Spy pay for both, and when must Gather Intelligence be chosen?

**Expected:** Spy Agent icon permits a connected space without recalling Spy. Infiltrate recalls own connected Spy to bypass another player Agent. Gather Intelligence recalls own connected Spy to draw, chosen immediately after placement before card/space effects. Same Spy cannot do both; separate Spies can.

**Source:** Main p11 / extraction 606–622

**Coverage:** Distinct spy functions and timing

## Q07

With two troops, one sandworm and four revealed swords, what is your strength? What if your final unit is removed? Can you participate in Combat Intrigue with no unit?

**Expected:** Strength 11 (2+2+3+4). No units means zero strength regardless swords. Without a unit cannot participate in Combat Intrigue.

**Source:** Main pp12,14 / extraction 652–665,744–750

**Coverage:** Strength and eligibility

## Q08

If you pass Combat Intrigue and an opponent then plays one, may you act again? When does the window close, and where do troops and sandworms go after rewards?

**Expected:** May act again after earlier pass; closes only when all involved players pass consecutively. Adjust strength for effects. After all rewards troops return to supply not garrison, sandworms to bank, Combat markers reset zero.

**Source:** Main p14 / extraction 744–750,767–769

**Coverage:** Combat priority and cleanup

## Q09

Compare two players tied for first in a four-player Conflict with the same tie in a three-player Conflict. Who takes the Conflict card, and can a zero-strength player get a reward?

**Expected:** Each tied first gets second reward and nobody wins or takes card. In four-player game remaining players compete for third; in three-player game no further rewards. Zero strength never rewarded.

**Source:** Main p14 / extraction 765,792–808

**Coverage:** Tie for first outcome branches

## Q10

What does a sandworm do to rewards: do two sandworms quadruple them, can control or battle icons double, and how is a reward offering 3 spice for 1 VP handled?

**Expected:** One or more sandworms doubles, never quadruples. Control and battle icons do not double. Optional reward cost may be paid second time: 3 spice for 1 VP or 6 for 2, not free second VP.

**Source:** Main p14 / extraction 778–791

**Coverage:** Doubling scope and cost exception

## Q11

When is a normal battle-icon match checked, which cards qualify, is matching optional, and how is a wild icon used?

**Expected:** After winning and taking Conflict card face up in supply check another face-up Conflict or Objective with same icon; must flip pair face down for 1 VP. At Endgame wild can match any of three icons in supply, pair flipped for 1 VP.

**Source:** Main p14,p20 / extraction 770–777,1090–1096

**Coverage:** Battle icon match

## Q12

What are the rewards and reversibility at 2 and 4 Faction Influence, and does tying the Alliance holder at 4 steal the Alliance?

**Expected:** Reach 2 gets 1 VP lost below 2. Reach 4 gets track bonus retained if falling below, can earn again on re-reaching. First to 4 gets Alliance and its VP; tie does not steal, opponent must rise higher. Transfer loses old holder VP and gives new holder VP.

**Source:** Main p7 / extraction 333–344

**Coverage:** Influence thresholds and Alliance

## Q13

When can Persuasion be spent during Reveal, may it be pooled or split, can you buy a Row replacement immediately, and where does the acquired card go?

**Expected:** Before between or after Reveal effects; can pool and split. Refill Row to five immediately; may buy replacement with remaining Persuasion. Acquired card face-up discard, cannot use immediately; unspent Persuasion lost.

**Source:** Main pp12–13 / extraction 645–649,704–730

**Coverage:** Acquisition timing and destination

## Q14

How do Maker bonus spice, the Shield Wall and Maker Hooks affect harvesting and summoning, and where can a sandworm be placed?

**Expected:** Makers phase adds one bonus spice on each unoccupied Maker space, accumulating; visiting collects all bonus spice. Shield Wall blocks worms at Arrakeen, Spice Refinery, Imperial Basin until removed to box. Maker Hooks prerequisite when specified; summoning directly bank to Conflict, never garrison.

**Source:** Main pp10,15,20 / extraction 493–522,567–584,835–845,1130–1135

**Coverage:** Maker and worm restrictions

## Q15

At what point does 10 VP or an empty Conflict deck end play, what happens before comparing final VP, and what are the rulebook resource tiebreakers?

**Expected:** Check in Recall at end of round, not immediately at 10 VP. Resolve Endgame Intrigue first then highest VP wins. Ties spice then Solari then water then garrisoned troops.

**Source:** Main pp15 / extraction 824–855

**Coverage:** End trigger and scoring

## Q16

With optional standard CHOAM, how many contracts start face up and face down, which extra cards are added, is Shaddam compulsory, and are the 10 contrasting-back contracts part of this standard setup?

**Expected:** 20 standard contracts: two face up, eighteen in bank. Add four Intrigue and four Imperium; Shaddam optional. Ten contrasting-back contracts are only Rise of Ix compatibility, excluded from standard setup.

**Source:** Main p16 / extraction 860–879,916–936

**Coverage:** CHOAM setup and compatibility boundary

## Q17

Under CHOAM, what does the contract icon do, what if all contracts are taken, and how are Harvest, Immediate and Acquire The Spice Must Flow contracts completed?

**Expected:** Take one of two face-up into supply then refill face up from bank; if all taken gain 2 Solari (also without module). Harvest: visit Maker and gain specified total spice that turn including other sources. Immediate upon taking. Acquire The Spice Must Flow on next acquiring that card.

**Source:** Main p16 / extraction 880–901

**Coverage:** CHOAM taking and types

## Q18

You gain a board-space contract after already sending your Agent to that space this turn. Can you complete it now? Once completed what do you announce, gain and do with the token?

**Expected:** Cannot complete now; must have held contract when Agent sent, wait future turn. Announce completion, gain printed rewards, flip face down and leave in supply.

**Source:** Main p16 / extraction 902–907

**Coverage:** CHOAM ownership timing and destination

## Q19

In standard two-player play how many Rivals are used, who gets the Ornithopter Objective, who gets First Player, what is the turn order, and does a Rival Reveal?

**Expected:** One shared Rival. Rival gets Ornithopter Objective, other two randomly humans; Rival never First Player. Marker alternates humans, Rival considered clockwise from First Player and acts between humans each round. Takes Agent turns while it has Agent in supply, never Reveals.

**Source:** Supplements PDF pp3–4

**Coverage:** Required two-player setup and turn order

## Q20

A Rival reveals an occupied board space, then a Place Spy card. How does it proceed, which space costs and effects apply, and what happens when House Hagal is empty or Reshuffle is drawn?

**Expected:** Occupied: ignore and reveal again. Place Spy tries empty designated post, ignore placement if none, then reveal another card regardless because no Agent sent. Ignore normal space costs/effects; use Hagal effects only. Empty deck or Reshuffle: immediately reshuffle Hagal and continue.

**Source:** Supplements PDF p4

**Coverage:** Rival card resolution
