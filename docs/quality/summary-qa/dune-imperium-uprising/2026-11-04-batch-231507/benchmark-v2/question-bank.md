# benchmark-v2

2023 English Uprising v23-10-12, standard 2–4-player multiplayer including required two-player Rival, optional standard CHOAM; no solo, Rival variants, six-player teams or cross-game expansions. Relevant official FAQ January 13 2025 clarifications included.

## Q01

How is the Conflict deck built, and what happens to unused Conflict cards?

**Expected:** Shuffle all four III cards at the bottom, five randomly dealt of nine II cards above, one randomly dealt of three I cards on top: ten total. Return unused cards to the box without looking.

**Source:** Main PDF p4 / extraction 190–209

**Coverage:** Conflict deck construction

## Q02

What are the starting score, Agents, troops and water for a human in a four-player game versus a three-player game?

**Expected:** Four players start at 1 VP; three at 0. Each has two Agents on Leader and third beside board, three troops in garrison with remaining nine in supply, and one water.

**Source:** Main pp4–5 / extraction 254–303

**Coverage:** Player setup

## Q03

Can you Reveal with Agents left, and can you later take an Agent turn in the same round? What cards supply Reveal effects and where do all played cards go?

**Expected:** May Reveal early; no more turns this phase after Reveal. Only cards remaining in hand and just revealed supply Reveal effects, not Agent-turn cards. Clean Up puts all Agent and Reveal cards in discard pile.

**Source:** Main pp8,12 / extraction 360–379,637–673

**Coverage:** Reveal commitment and destinations

## Q04

May an Agent-turn card effect provide resources to pay the destination entry cost? How many Agents can one card send, and can a card without Agent icons be used?

**Expected:** Entry costs paid immediately before any card or space effects; cannot use later gains to pay entry. One card sends one Agent using one matching icon. No-icon card cannot be played on an Agent turn.

**Source:** Main p9 / extraction 425–452

**Coverage:** Entry legality and costs

## Q05

At a Combat space you recruit three troops this turn and already have four garrisoned. How many can deploy? What if the destination is not a Combat space, and where do recruited troops originate?

**Expected:** May deploy all three newly recruited plus at most two older garrison troops, five total. Normal deployment unavailable at noncombat space. Recruit from own supply into garrison; empty supply prevents recruiting.

**Source:** Main p10 / extraction 535–566

**Coverage:** Recruit and deployment limits

## Q06

How do you use a Spy Agent icon, Infiltrate and Gather Intelligence? Can the same Spy pay for both, and when must Gather Intelligence be chosen?

**Expected:** Spy Agent icon permits a connected space without recalling Spy. Infiltrate recalls own connected Spy to bypass another player Agent. Gather Intelligence recalls own connected Spy to draw, chosen immediately after placement before card/space effects. Same Spy cannot do both; separate Spies can.

**Source:** Main p11 / extraction 606–622

**Coverage:** Distinct spy functions and timing

## Q07

With two troops, one sandworm and four revealed swords, what is your strength? What if your final unit is removed? Can you participate in Combat Intrigue with no unit?

**Expected:** Strength 11 (2+2+3+4). No units means zero strength regardless swords. Without a unit cannot participate in Combat Intrigue.

**Source:** Main pp12,14 / extraction 652–665,744–750

**Coverage:** Strength and eligibility

## Q08

If you pass Combat Intrigue and an opponent then plays one, may you act again? When does the window close, and where do troops and sandworms go after rewards?

**Expected:** May act again after earlier pass; closes only when all involved players pass consecutively. Adjust strength for effects. After all rewards troops return to supply not garrison, sandworms to bank, Combat markers reset zero.

**Source:** Main p14 / extraction 744–750,767–769

**Coverage:** Combat priority and cleanup

## Q09

Compare two players tied for first in a four-player Conflict with the same tie in a three-player Conflict. Who takes the Conflict card, and can a zero-strength player get a reward?

**Expected:** Each tied first gets second reward and nobody wins or takes card. In four-player game remaining players compete for third; in three-player game no further rewards. Zero strength never rewarded.

**Source:** Main p14 / extraction 765,792–808

**Coverage:** Tie for first outcome branches

## Q10

**Expected:** One or more sandworms doubles, never quadruples. Control and battle icons do not double. Optional reward cost may be paid second time: 3 spice for 1 VP or 6 for 2, not free second VP.

**Source:** Main p14 / extraction 778–791

**Coverage:** Doubling scope and cost exception

## Q11

**Source:** Main p14,p20 / extraction 770–777,1090–1096

**Coverage:** Battle icon match

## Q12

**Source:** Main p7 / extraction 333–344

**Coverage:** Influence thresholds and Alliance

**Source:** Main pp8,15 / extraction 355–360,385–390,824–834

**Coverage:** Round order and restart
