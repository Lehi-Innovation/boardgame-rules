Q01 For two Spirits, how are the Fear pool/deck and Invader deck prepared, what initial Blight pool is used with and without a Blight Card, and what final Invader action completes setup?
ASKED: Two Spirits use 8 Fear markers. | Shuffle and use 9 Fear cards in three groups of 3 separated by Terror II/III. | Prepare 3 random Stage I above 4 Stage II above 5 Stage III, removing one unseen card of each stage. | Initial Healthy pool is 5; initial no-card pool is 11, including exactly one setup bonus. | Finish setup with a normal Explore and leave that card face-up in Build.

Q02 A Spirit chooses Growth with two Add Presence actions. May the second placement use the first as its range origin? Which Presence-track disks may be taken, and how do uncovered Energy/Card Plays numbers combine?
ASKED: Resolve the two placement actions in chosen order; the first new Presence may originate the second. | Take the leftmost remaining disk of either selected track for each placement. | Only the highest uncovered Energy and Card Plays numbers apply; do not add numbers.

Q03 A Spirit has no Presence on its tracks but has Presence on the island and some destroyed disks. How can an Add Presence instruction be resolved, where is range measured, and can destroyed disks be used?
ASKED: With tracks empty, reposition one of that Spirit's island Presence. | Measure the legal destination from existing island Presence, including the disk about to be repositioned, before picking a disk up. | Any of its island disks may supply the placement. | Destroyed disks cannot supply ordinary Add Presence without explicit permission.

Q04 When are Fast and Slow Power Cards paid for and their Elements gained? Can promised Fast-phase Energy pay for the Spirit-phase cards, and do unspent Energy or unused Card Plays carry over?
ASKED: Play and pay for both Fast and Slow cards during Spirit Phase. | Their Elements become available immediately on play/payment. | Fast-phase Energy arrives too late to pay these normal Spirit-phase costs. | Energy carries over; unused Card Plays do not.

Q05 May a Fast Power wait until Slow, may Powers interrupt each other, and when must a Slow Power’s target be selected?
ASKED: Fast Powers must resolve in Fast or be foregone; they cannot wait until Slow. | Finish one Power before another; Powers cannot interrupt one another. | Choose a Slow Power's target when it is used in Slow, using the then-current position and restrictions.

Q06 In a Ravage a land has one Town, one Explorer and two undamaged Dahan, with no Defend or prior Blight. Resolve damage to land, Dahan, surviving counterattack and resulting Fear if the players destroy the Town.
ASKED: Town plus Explorer deal 3 Damage simultaneously to land and Dahan. | Add one Blight to the previously unblighted land. | Destroy one Dahan efficiently and mark 1 Damage on the survivor. | The surviving Dahan still deals 2 counterattack Damage. | Destroying the chosen Town generates 1 Fear.

Q07 Compare a Ravage reduced to zero Damage by Defend with a Ravage skipped entirely. Do Dahan fight back in each, and does Spirit Presence counterattack?
ASKED: Zero Invader Damage after Defend still permits surviving Dahan to counterattack. | Skipping the Ravage entirely gives no Dahan counterattack. | Spirit Presence does not counterattack.

Q08 During Build, what is added to lands containing only Explorers, equal numbers of Towns and Cities, more Towns than Cities, or no Invaders? Are Towns upgraded?
ASKED: Only Explorers: add one Town. | Equal Town and City counts: add one Town. | More Towns than Cities: add one City. | No Invaders: no Build. | Build adds new supply pieces and does not upgrade/remove a Town.

Q09 Which matching lands Explore, how many Explorers are added if there are multiple sources, do Explorers themselves provide a source, and what happens if no Invader Card can be drawn?
ASKED: Explore matching lands containing a Town/City or adjacent to a Town/City/Ocean. | Add one Explorer from supply per eligible land regardless of source count. | Explorers do not provide Explore sources. | Needing to draw from an empty Invader deck loses; drawing its last existing card alone does not.

Q10 After Explore, where do the Invader cards go? How are a Stage III two-terrain card and a Stage II flag handled without an Adversary?
ASKED: Discard old Ravage; move Build to Ravage and the newly explored card to Build. | Stage III affects both terrains under ordinary eligibility restrictions. | Ignore Stage II flag/Escalation without an Adversary.

Q11 In a two-Spirit game with 7 of 8 Fear markers already generated, an effect generates 3 Fear and earning a card exposes Terror II. Explain marker handling, new Terror timing, and when the card is revealed/resolved.
ASKED: The eighth Fear marker earns the top card face-down, then reset all 8 markers. | The remaining 2 generated Fear go toward the next card. | Exposed Terror II takes effect immediately. | Do not reveal the earned card until Fear resolution; resolve it at the then-current Terror level.

Q12 A Fear card resolved in the Fear step earns another Fear card and exposes a higher Terror Level. When is that new card resolved, at which level do subsequent cards resolve, and do “each player” choices require that player’s own Presence?
ASKED: Put a newly earned Fear card at the bottom of the resolving stack and resolve it in the same Fear step. | Subsequent cards resolve only at the newly current Terror Level. | Each-player choices have no default requirement for that player's own Presence or Range; obey the card's restrictions.

Q13 A land with existing Blight has two Presence from Spirit A and one from Spirit B. One new Blight is added after setup. Describe Presence losses, cascade choices and what happens if the cascade enters another already-Blighted land.
ASKED: Destroy one Presence from A and one from B; put them beside the island, not back on tracks. | Add the original Blight and cascade one additional Blight to one chosen adjacent land. | If that land already had Blight, destroy one Presence of each Spirit present and cascade onward again. | Existing Blight quantity does not multiply the outgoing cascade.

Q14 Where does setup Blight come from versus Blight added during play? When a Healthy card empties how is its new pool obtained, does the setup +1 repeat, and can removing Blight make it Healthy again?
ASKED: Setup island Blight comes from the box; play Blight comes from the card/board pool. | On Healthy pool exhaustion flip and follow the reverse instructions, adding its printed new per-player pool from the box. | The setup +1 does not repeat on a flip. | Removing Blight never flips a Blighted card back to Healthy.

Q15 Explain how Elements apply to multiple thresholds and multiple Powers. May an innate be used twice with twice the Elements, may its levels use different lands, and how does an “Instead” level work?
ASKED: Elements are checked rather than spent and can meet multiple thresholds and Powers. | Extra Elements do not give another ordinary Innate use. | An Innate's levels belong to one Power and use one target. | An Instead level replaces previous levels' effects rather than adding to them.

Q16 A legal target lacks pieces needed for one printed instruction. May the Power still be used? May the player choose individual unwanted instructions to skip, or forego the whole Power and recover its Energy?
ASKED: A legal target remains legal even if an effect instruction is impossible. | Do as much as possible: resolve possible instructions and skip impossible parts. | Do not cherry-pick possible mandatory instructions. | The Spirit may forego the whole Power but receives no Energy refund.

Q17 Explain gaining a new Minor or Major Power, including how many are drawn, where kept/unkept cards go, Major Forgetting options and the consequences of Forgetting a card already in play.
ASKED: Choose Minor or Major and draw 4, reshuffling that deck's discard if needed. | Keep one in hand and put unkept cards in their deck's shared discard. | Gaining a Major requires Forgetting one owned card, including hand/discard/in-play/new Major options. | Forgotten Minor/Major goes to its deck discard; Unique goes under its panel. | Forgetting an in-play card removes its Elements and any unused use immediately. | Already resolved effects persist for their normal duration.

Q18 How do Damage, Destroy, Remove and Replace differ for Fear and survival? Can Damage from separate normal Powers combine, does Damage normally hurt Dahan, and does replacement erase prior Damage?
ASKED: Unqualified Power Damage normally hurts Invaders in the target land; it does not normally harm Dahan. | Damage from normal separate Powers can combine during the turn. | Damage reaching Health immediately destroys a piece; survivor Damage clears at Time Passes. | Destroying a Town/City causes 1/2 Fear in addition to explicit Fear. | Remove/Replace themselves generate no destruction Fear. | Replace retains prior Damage and can cause destruction if it meets the new piece's Health. | An outright Destroy instruction eliminates its specified piece regardless of current Damage or Health, unlike Damage that must reach Health. | Remove also takes the specified piece off the board without a Damage/Health test; its no-automatic-Fear distinction remains required. | Replace removes the original piece and puts the specified replacement in its place; preserve the existing retained-Damage/new-Health-destruction requirement.

Q19 Does increased Power Range extend Gather/Push distance, may multiple pieces come from/go to different adjacent lands, may pieces go into the Ocean normally, and may “up to” be zero?
ASKED: Range boosts do not change the adjacent-land distance of Gather/Push. | Gather brings pieces from adjacent land(s) into the target. | Push sends pieces from the target to adjacent land(s), allowing different destinations. | Multiple pieces may come from different source lands. | Ordinary movement cannot send them into Ocean/off-board. | Up to permits zero.

Q20 State all four Terror victory conditions, the three standard defeat routes, and the result when a single Ravage loses a Spirit’s last Presence but surviving Dahan remove the final required Invader. Do you finish other lands first?
ASKED: Terror I needs no Invaders; II no Towns/Cities; III no Cities. | Earning the final Fear card exposes Victory and wins without resolving its text. | Standard defeat routes are exhausted Blight as instructed, any Spirit without island Presence, and needing to draw from an empty Invader deck. | Finish the single-land Ravage and its triggered actions before checking outcomes. | If that tree meets both win and loss conditions it is a sacrifice victory. | Do not finish later land Ravages before checking.

Q21 What makes two lands adjacent, including across boards? Are all outside-edge lands Coastal, is Ocean normally a land, and can a land be adjacent to itself or within Range 1 of itself?
ASKED: Adjacent lands share a border or corner, including across boards. | Coastal means adjacent to printed Ocean; other outside cliffs do not count. | Ocean is normally not a land or in play. | A land is not adjacent to itself but is Range 0 of itself and within Range 1.

Q22 How is a Spirit’s starting hand obtained, where is its setup Presence placed, what if multiple legal setup choices or two placement instructions name the same land, and is its later play confined to that board?
ASKED: Start with the Spirit's four Unique cards in hand. | Each Spirit starts on a different board and follows its back-panel setup. | Choose freely among equally legal setup choices. | Separate placement instructions may name the same land. | After setup Presence and Powers may operate on other boards.

Q23 Does one Presence each from two Spirits form a Sacred Site? Does four Presence from one Spirit count as two sites, and what does “your land” mean?
ASKED: One disk each from two Spirits does not ordinarily form a Sacred Site. | A Spirit normally needs at least two of its own disks. | Four of its disks in one land still form only one site. | Your land means a land with your Presence.

Q24 A Growth option includes an impossible instruction and an explicit cost. Can it be selected, can possible instructions be skipped, and may one Spirit wait for another’s Growth result before choosing?
ASKED: An impossible non-cost Growth instruction does not prevent selecting that option. | Do all possible instructions; do not voluntarily skip mandatory possible parts. | An explicit Growth cost must be paid fully. | A Spirit may wait for another's Growth result.

Q25 What cards does ordinary Reclaim return, and when and how often can a newly uncovered Reclaim One space be used?
ASKED: Ordinary Reclaim returns all personal-discard cards to hand, not in-play or forgotten cards. | Reclaim One returns one personal-discard card once per Spirit Phase at any time during that phase. | It is available the turn uncovered.

Q26 When a track Element is revealed after Growth, when does it become usable? When may an Any track Element be chosen and changed, and what if an in-play card is Forgotten?
ASKED: A revealed track Element is usable immediately and remains available while uncovered. | Choose a passive Any at any time during the turn; once chosen it is fixed until next turn. | Forgetting an in-play card removes that card's Elements immediately.

Q27 A Power’s earlier instruction uncovers an Element needed for a later threshold. Can that later threshold apply? What if Elements are lost instead, and can earlier resolved effects be revised?
ASKED: An Element gained earlier in a Power can satisfy a later threshold. | Losing Elements can prevent a later threshold. | Do not revise already resolved effects retroactively.

Q28 Must a land at Range 0 contain your Presence? Can a numeric Range boost waive a Sacred Site origin, a target-terrain restriction or the text “one of your lands”?
ASKED: An ordinary Range 0 target contains your Presence. | A numeric Range increase does not waive a Sacred Site/origin restriction. | It does not waive terrain or other target contents restrictions, including one of your lands.

Q29 May a legally targeted Power count as used if its instructions accomplish nothing? Does foregoing its text count as targeting it? Can an innate with only its own Speed/Range modification available be used?
ASKED: A legal Power may count as used even if its attempted instructions change nothing. | It needs an instruction with potential to affect game state. | Foregoing its text is not using/targeting it. | Changing only its own Speed/Range does not make an Innate usable.

Q30 A card adds a Town in Slow and says Invaders skip Build this turn. What remains next turn? At Time Passes where do played cards go, what happens to damaged survivors and reminder markers, and do track Elements vanish?
ASKED: The added Town stays next turn as a board change. | The turn's Build-skip effect expires at Time Passes. | Discard played cards to personal discard and clear card Elements. | Heal survivor Damage and remove temporary effects/reminder markers. | Uncovered track Elements remain.

Q31 Do damaged Invaders and Dahan deal less Damage? If Defend 1 and Defend 2 both affect a land with total Invader Damage 4, what Damage reaches the land and Dahan?
ASKED: Damaged Invaders and Dahan deal their full normal Damage. | Defend 1 and 2 stack to Defend 3. | Invader total 4 becomes 1 Damage to land and separately 1 Damage to Dahan.

Q32 If a Town is replaced by an Explorer while retaining 1 Damage, does the replacement itself cause Fear, does the Explorer survive, and why? Can reduced Health destroy a piece without new Damage?
ASKED: Replacement itself generates no Fear. | The Explorer retains 1 Damage, meets its 1 Health and is destroyed. | Reduced Health can immediately destroy a piece without new Damage.

Q33 Do Dahan automatically attack when a Spirit damages or Pushing Invaders into their land, and can a Power’s unqualified Damage hit Dahan? Are Dahan and Invaders limited by the physical supply?
ASKED: Dahan do not automatically attack merely because a Power Damages or Pushes Invaders into their land. | Unqualified Power Damage affects Invaders; Dahan require explicit harmful text. | Invader and Dahan supplies are unlimited; use substitutes when needed.

Q34 Do increased Range and a large number of pieces let a single ordinary Power distribute its effects across multiple target lands? What does Push up to 3 Explorer/Town allow in terms of total pieces?
ASKED: An ordinary Power has one target land regardless of Range or piece count. | Only explicit instructions can affect other lands, such as Push destinations. | Up to 3 Explorer/Town is a combined limit of 3 pieces of either/both types, not 3 each.

Q35 If Gather 3 or Push 3 lacks “up to,” may the player move only one when three legal pieces are available? What if only two are available? Can unrestricted Move leave a piece in its current land?
ASKED: Without up to, move the stated number if possible, so not only one when three are available. | If only two can legally be moved, move both. | Unrestricted Move may leave a piece in the same land and still counts as movement.

Q36 What is the difference between a Growth Move Presence arrow and Add Presence using an island disk? Can the latter pick up a disk outside the shown Range of the destination?
ASKED: Growth Move Presence measures up to the indicated distance from that disk's current land. | Add Presence using an island disk first selects a destination measured from existing Presence. | It may then take an island disk from anywhere, even outside the shown destination Range. | Repositioning under Add is adding rather than a Move instruction.

Q37 May a newly gained Growth Power be played in the same Spirit Phase? Does a Power gained in Fast automatically enter play or resolve immediately, and is there a hand-size or hand-secrecy limit?
ASKED: A Growth-gained card may be played later in the same Spirit Phase. | A Fast-gained card does not automatically enter play. | Gaining that card in Fast does not automatically resolve its effects immediately. | There is no hand-size limit and cards may be shown to others.

Q38 An effect explicitly allows playing a card in Fast after the Spirit used all normal Card Plays. Is this legal and when are its Elements/effects available?
ASKED: An explicit effect-granted Fast-phase play is additional to ordinary Card Plays and is legal after they are exhausted. | Gain its Elements immediately when played. | Resolve it at its Speed in the proper phase outside another action unless explicitly told otherwise.

Q39 If Entwined Power gifts a Major Power, must the recipient Forget? If a Spirit instead gains two Majors, can a single Forget cover both? Is an ordinary forgotten Unique reclaimable?
ASKED: Entwined gifting/transferring a Major is not Gain and requires no Forget. | Two Major gains require two separate Forgets; one card cannot pay both. | A normally Forgotten Unique is under the panel and not ordinarily reclaimable.

Q40 When a Fast Gift of Strength or Powerstorm grants a Repeat, must the card be chosen immediately? Can the chosen card be Slow, can it be an innate, and do you gain its printed Elements again?
ASKED: Choose a card for the granted Repeat when actually using the option, not necessarily when granted. | A Slow card can be chosen and repeated in Slow. | Gift of Strength/Powerstorm say Power Card and cannot repeat an Innate. | Repeating does not grant printed card Elements again.

Q41 If a Power’s Range was increased and it was made Fast, what happens on Repeat? If Elements enabling a non-Range threshold have been lost, does the Repeat automatically retain that threshold?
ASKED: Range and Speed modifications carry over to Repeat, retaining the boosted Range and Fast Speed. | Other thresholds check current Elements and may no longer apply after Elements are lost.

Q42 What happens when Powerstorm itself is Repeated, and can its threshold spend all three Repeat permissions on the same Power Card?
ASKED: Ignore Repeat instructions when repeating Powerstorm; it grants no further Repeat permissions. | Its non-Repeat Energy gain still resolves. | Its threshold permits up to three different cards, not three repetitions of the same card.

Q43 A Power says Repeat this Power in an adjacent land, but that land fails the original Blight target restriction or normal Range. Is it legal, and can the original land be selected?
ASKED: A specified adjacent-land Repeat uses that location instruction in place of ordinary Range/Target restrictions. | It can target the adjacent land despite the original Blight or Range condition. | It cannot target the original land, which is not adjacent to itself.

Q44 Is Presence destroyed on moving into an already-Blighted land? If a cascade loops back into a land it already hit, can it destroy Presence there again, and does existing Blight quantity multiply cascading?
ASKED: Entering an already-Blighted land does not destroy Presence. | A new cascade addition returning there can destroy one Presence per Spirit again. | Existing Blight quantity does not multiply the outgoing cascade.

Q45 If a Healthy Blight pool empties before a cascade is complete, when do you flip/refill and do you finish the cascade? Does removing Blight restore destroyed Presence?
ASKED: On Healthy pool exhaustion immediately flip/refill as directed and continue unfinished cascade/additions. | Removing Blight does not restore destroyed Presence.

Q46 If a Ravage in one land cascades Blight across the board boundary, is that cascade a separate independent action for victory/defeat checks? May players choose another land’s Ravage first to win before a later losing Ravage?
ASKED: A cascade is part of the original single-land Ravage action tree, including across boards. | It is not an independent outcome-check boundary. | Players may choose the land order and win after one complete Ravage tree before a later land would cause loss.

Q47 A Stage III Ravage lists Sands and Jungles. Before it starts one matching land is empty. A triggered effect during another Ravage moves an Invader into that empty land and more Invaders into a land already due to Ravage. Which lands Ravage and which Invaders participate?

Q48 What does the Coastal Lands Invader card affect when Exploring, Building and Ravaging, and do its Build/Ravage effects bypass usual requirements?
ASKED: Coastal Lands affects all Coastal lands on all boards for Explore and its later Build/Ravage. | Ordinary requirements still apply to Build/Ravage; it does not bypass Invader presence requirements. | Explore requires sources, with the Ocean providing coastal access.

Q49 If a Year of Perfect Stillness or Paralyzing Fright skips actions in a land containing a Town, does it stop an adjacent matching land Exploring from that Town?
ASKED: Skipping actions in the source Town's land does not prevent adjacent matching lands Exploring from that Town. | The source check occurs for the destination's Explore, not an action in the source land.

Q50 After Invader cards advance, can Slow Powers remove a newly added Explorer to prevent a future Build, or has it already Built? When does Fear from a Slow Power normally resolve?
ASKED: The just-added Explorer has only Explored; the card is in Build for next turn. | A Slow Power may remove it before then and thereby prevent a future Build if no Invaders remain. | Fear cards earned in Slow normally resolve in the next Invader Phase's Fear step.

Q51 Do each-player Fear choices need different lands or pieces, and may a player use another Spirit’s Sacred Site for a generic Fear-card condition? What if the card explicitly counts each Spirit’s own Sacred Sites?
ASKED: Each-player choices need different lands/pieces only when the card says so. | The same piece may be affected/moved by successive legal player choices. | A generic Presence/Sacred Site condition may use another Spirit's. | Explicit own-Sacred-Site counts use each Spirit's sites individually.

Q52 An effect removes two Invaders in a land with two Cities. Must Explorers be removed first, and if a card instead allows removing a number of Health worth, does existing Damage reduce that Health?

Q53 If a condition says Dahan outnumber Towns/Cities, can it hold with zero buildings? What if both sides are zero?
ASKED: At least one Dahan outnumbers zero Towns/Cities. | Zero versus zero is not outnumbering.

Q54 How does one-Spirit solo change Another Spirit targeting and conditional other-Spirit bonuses? Does one human playing two Spirits get that same self-targeting exception or use one-person setup scaling?

Q55 When players disagree about a Power’s choices, a Blight cascade destination, or a decision outside any land, who ultimately decides?
ASKED: The Power user decides Power choices. | For other decisions seek consensus. | Without consensus, a decision within/about a land belongs to the Spirit starting on that board. | A non-land decision falls to the owner/organizer.

Q56 Do low-complexity Spirits always have to use Power Progression? If used, how are gains and Major Forgetting handled, and how does the introductory no-Blight-Card game end from Blight?
ASKED: Power Progression is an optional teaching aid, not compulsory for low-complexity Spirits. | When used, take the next listed card rather than draw 4/keep 1. | Gaining a Major still requires Forgetting. | In the no-card introductory game, emptying the board Blight pool loses without a flip.

Q59 A single-land Defend-per-Dahan Power resolves, then some Dahan leave. Compare that with Dahan on their Guard and with Encompassing Ward when Presence moves.

Q61 What is Song of Sanctity’s speed if its cost-circle color is missing? What does the missing symbol on Dissolve the Bonds of Kinship’s third line require, and is its Invader-on-Invader Damage simultaneous?

Q62 Does Poisoned Land’s threshold count Blight that the Power itself just added? How is Tsunami’s extra Fear applied, and does an unqualified +4 Damage threshold add 4 to every piece hit earlier?

Q63 Can Wrap in Wings of Sunlight grant Defend if it moves zero Dahan? Can it count as movement when Dahan are moved to their current land if otherwise permitted? Can Blazing Renewal do threshold Damage with no destroyed Presence to add?

ASKED: For Entwined's subsequent gain, the using Spirit selects one of the cards the target did not keep from the initial draw; it does not draw a fresh four. | In solo the two cards gained through this base effect come from the same initial draw of four, not two independent draws.

Q147 When a Power removes Blight from the island, does the token go back to the box or to the currently face-up Blight Card/Invader Board pool? Does a Blighted Island card receive removed Blight too?
ASKED: Removed island Blight returns to the currently face-up Blight Card or board Blight pool, not to the box. | The currently face-up Blighted Island side also receives removed Blight.

Q148 Infinite Vitality is active in its target land. A later effect would add Blight there. Where does that Blight token go, and do the ordinary Presence destruction and cascade from adding it occur?
ASKED: Leave the token in the active Blight Card/board pool in use; do not place it in the land. | This prevented addition causes no Presence destruction. | This prevented addition causes no cascade.

Q149 Blazing Renewal resolves on Spirit B, which has two destroyed Presence. A legal single destination within Range 2 of the using Spirit A’s Presence is available. Can it restore those destroyed disks despite the ordinary Add Presence restriction, and are they returned to the tracks or placed on the island?
ASKED: Yes, Blazing Renewal permits restoring the two given destroyed Presence of target Spirit B despite the ordinary restriction. | The restored disks go directly into the supplied legal island land, not back onto Presence tracks.

Q150 When Vengeance of the Dead meets its threshold, may its triggered Damage be dealt to Invaders in a land adjacent to the target, and does that change the card’s target land?
ASKED: With its threshold, Vengeance of the Dead may deal its triggered Damage to Invaders in lands adjacent to its target. | The original target remains unchanged; qualifying destruction there still supplies the trigger.

Q151 For Song of Sanctity, what happens if the target land contains an Explorer, and what happens if it contains none? May the player select the Blight-removal branch while Explorers are present?
ASKED: If Explorers are present, Push all of them. | If there are no Explorers, instead remove one Blight. | No; the actual Explorer condition selects the branch, rather than a free player choice.

Q152 With Entwined Power’s threshold met, can only the target Spirit gift a card to the using Spirit, or may each gift one to the other? May a gifted card come from cards in play or personal discard instead of hand, and is either gift mandatory?
ASKED: The using Spirit and target Spirit may each gift one Power Card to the other. | Gifts must come from hand; cards in play and personal discard are ineligible. | Each Spirit may independently decline its gift.

Q153 In a standard two-Spirit game with balanced Island Boards, must the boards use only the illustrated bottom-to-bottom layout, or may players join them in another arrangement that fits?
ASKED: Any fitting arrangement of the two balanced boards is legal; the illustrated bottom-to-bottom orientation is not required.

Q154 If Powerstorm grants a Spirit a Repeat of a Power Card that it already paid to play, must that Spirit pay the card’s Energy cost again to use that Repeat?
ASKED: Yes; to use that Powerstorm Repeat, pay the repeated Power Card's Energy cost again, in addition to the original play payment.

Q155 Dahan Enheartened at Terror II or III gives each player Gather and Damage instructions in their chosen land. Players A and B each choose to Gather one Dahan. May A Gather, then B Gather, then both deal their Damage, or must one player finish that player's full instructions before the next begins?
ASKED: One player must finish the full Gather-and-Damage instruction sequence before the next starts; the proposed interleaving is not allowed.

Q156 After a primary action and all triggered actions finish, an Invader is still illegally placed. Do players check victory/defeat with that illegal position first, or correct its position before that check? Assume a legal destination exists.
ASKED: Correct the illegal position after the completed action tree but before checking victory/defeat; check the cleaned-up state.

Q157 Thunderspeaker has met both a threshold making one of its Innates Fast and a threshold that can affect game state. Is the Speed-changing threshold handled only as an ordinary effect when resolving that Innate, or applied before checking the phase in which the Innate can be used?
ASKED: Apply the Speed-changing threshold when checking the Innate's Speed/phase, before its ordinary effects rather than waiting until ordinary effect resolution.

Q158 Bringer has used Vengeance of the Dead in a land. A later action by another Spirit actually destroys a Town there, causing Bringer's Vengeance to trigger. Is the triggered Damage real because the other Spirit caused the trigger, or does it still follow To Dream a Thousand Deaths?
ASKED: The delayed trigger remains Bringer's Power, follows To Dream a Thousand Deaths and deals hypothetical rather than real Damage; the other Spirit causing the trigger does not take ownership.

Q159 A populated land matches both the Ravage card and the Build card this turn. ASKED: No; the first payment skips only the Ravage, not the later Build. | Skipping the later Build requires a fresh optional choice and another of Green's Presence destroyed in that land. | Yes; the Presence remaining before Build still forms the required Sacred Site for the second use.

Q160 A Ravage destroys a Dahan. Thunderspeaker has no Presence in the ravaged land but has its own Presence in an adjacent land. Can Sworn to Victory require destruction of that nearby Presence, or is the rule confined to Presence in the ravaged land?
ASKED: Yes; Sworn to Victory can require Thunderspeaker's adjacent Presence, rather than being confined to the ravaged land.

Q161 The Trees and Stones Speak of War has created its per-Dahan Defend in a land also benefiting from Words of Warning's separate Defend 3. Trees and Stones meets its threshold and Pushes one Dahan out. Does that Dahan carry the separate Defend 3 too, or only its own Defend 2 from Trees and Stones?
ASKED: Only the fixed Trees and Stones Defend 2 travels with that Dahan; Words of Warning's separate Defend 3 does not travel.

Q162 A copy of the core game contains 14 Presence disks of one color. Should all 14 be used for that Spirit's setup and later Presence availability, or is one a spare?
ASKED: Use 13 Presence of that color; the extra disk is a spare and is not available for setup or later gameplay.

Q163 In standard one-Spirit solo, are any of the eight original-core Spirits prohibited because their special restrictions appear to require teammates, including Ocean's Hungry Grasp or Bringer of Dreams and Nightmares?
ASKED: None are prohibited: all eight original-core Spirits are permitted in solo, including the named Ocean and Bringer.

Q164 A Spirit with no Energy remaining receives Gift of Strength and has already used an eligible cost-1 Power Card. May it use that card's Repeat without paying its cost again, or does Gift require the same repayment as Powerstorm?
ASKED: The eligible Gift of Strength Repeat requires no additional Energy payment and may be used with zero Energy remaining.

Q165 When Green adds Presence via Growth, may it use destroyed disks while tracks still contain Presence? What is the price while Healthy versus Blighted? Does this special rule also let an ordinary Power Add Presence use destroyed disks?
ASKED: Destroyed Presence may optionally supply Green’s Growth placements before tracks are empty. | This use is free while Healthy and costs 1 Energy per destroyed Presence added while Blighted. | Steady Regeneration applies to Growth, so an ordinary Power placement needs its own destroyed-Presence permission.

Q166 Ocean has previously Drowned Invaders stored on its panel. Between actions in a later Spirit Phase, may it exchange them for Energy to help pay for cards that turn, or must it wait for another Drowning or the Gain Energy step?
ASKED: Ocean may exchange stored Drowned Invaders at the stated between-actions time in the later Spirit Phase; it need not wait for another Drowning or the normal Gain Energy step.

Q167 Ocean chooses its Growth option with two “Add a Presence in any Ocean” instructions. May one disk be placed in the Ocean of a different island board where Ocean currently has no Presence, without measuring a numeric Range from its existing Presence?
ASKED: The explicit any-Ocean Growth instruction may place in another board’s Ocean without preexisting Presence there or a numeric Range origin.

Q168 When Spirits May Yet Dream grants its target an Element, may that Spirit choose an Element it currently has none of, or must the chosen Element already be present?
ASKED: The granted Element must be a type the target already has at least one of; an absent Element cannot be chosen.

Q169 Green chooses its Reclaim-and-gain-Power Growth group. Does it also perform the separate ALWAYS Add Presence to Jungle/Wetland, or must it choose between that addition and Reclaim?
ASKED: Green performs the separate ALWAYS placement in addition to the selected Reclaim-and-gain-Power Growth group.

Q170 Bringer uses Tsunami with its threshold. Its base Damage would destroy a Town, so that Town is Pushed into an adjacent other Coastal land on the same board. Tsunami’s threshold later deals Damage in that land. Counting only nightmare-destruction Fear from that same Town, can it produce that Fear again during this single Tsunami use, or only once?
ASKED: The same Town provides nightmare-destruction Fear only once during that single Tsunami use; moving it between affected lands does not reset the per-Power, per-Invader limit.

Q171 A legally used Elemental Boon grants Elements through its effect text. It is then legally Repeated using Powerstorm. Does the Repeat grant the Elements specified by that effect text again?
ASKED: The Repeat resolves Elemental Boon’s explicit Element-grant effect again.
