Contents
1 Invader board extension, 20 Scenario markers, 22 of Beast/Wilds/Disease/Strife tokens each, 2 Spirit panels, 2 Adversary tiles, 1 Adversary panel, 1 Adversary-specific Event card, 4 Scenario tiles, 4 player aid cards, 31 Minor Power cards, 21 Major Power cards, 25 Event cards, 8 Unique Power cards, 7 Blight cards, 15 Fear cards
Features
DEFEND THE LAND: Colonizers are trying to take and blight the island wherever they explore. As Spirits of the island, you can defend your home from the invading forces!
MORE SPIRITS: Two all-new and exciting Spirits have been added to bring even more variety and vitality to your game!
EXCITING ADVERSARY: An all-new adversary has been added to bring even more challenge! Branch & Claw adds France (Plantation Colony).
NEW TOKEN TYPE: These Spirits bring more than just new mechanics! These Spirits utilize the new token types of Wilds, Beast, Disease, and Strife.
FOUR MORE SCENARIOS: Even more gameplay with an even higher demand of its players with four more scenarios! Will you be able to overcome them in one game?
