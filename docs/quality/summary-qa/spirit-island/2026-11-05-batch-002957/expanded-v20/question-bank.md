## Q01

For two Spirits, how are the Fear pool/deck and Invader deck prepared, what initial Blight pool is used with and without a Blight Card, and what final Invader action completes setup?

**Expected:** Use 8 Fear markers, 9 shuffled Fear cards divided 3/3/3 by Terror II and III dividers; Invader deck is 3 Stage I above 4 Stage II above 5 Stage III, randomized within each stage with one unused card per stage removed unseen. Initial Blight is 5 with a Healthy Island card (2 per player +1), or 11 without (5 per player +1). Setup Explore the top card normally and leave it face-up in Build; Ravage is initially empty.

**Sources:** CORE p.6; p.4 board; FAQ Setup: one-more-blight-errata

## Q02

A Spirit chooses Growth with two Add Presence actions. May the second placement use the first as its range origin? Which Presence-track disks may be taken, and how do uncovered Energy/Card Plays numbers combine?

**Expected:** Yes; resolve Growth actions in chosen order, so the first placement can extend the second. Each placement takes the leftmost remaining Presence from either chosen track. Use only the highest uncovered Energy number and highest uncovered Card Plays number, not their sum.

**Sources:** CORE pp.8,14; FAQ Core: .7w4g8p3

## Q03

A Spirit has no Presence on its tracks but has Presence on the island and some destroyed disks. How can an Add Presence instruction be resolved, where is range measured, and can destroyed disks be used?

**Expected:** Reposition one of its island Presence instead of taking a track disk. First choose a legal destination measured from existing island Presence, including the disk about to be repositioned, then take any of its island disks. Destroyed Presence is unavailable unless explicitly allowed.

**Sources:** CORE p.14 sidebar; FAQ Core: .bu6od08; .7w4g8oc

## Q04

When are Fast and Slow Power Cards paid for and their Elements gained? Can promised Fast-phase Energy pay for the Spirit-phase cards, and do unspent Energy or unused Card Plays carry over?

**Expected:** Play and pay for all cards in the Spirit Phase, gaining their Elements immediately, including Slow cards. Later Fast-phase Energy cannot pay these earlier costs. Unspent Energy carries over; unused Card Plays do not.

**Sources:** CORE pp.8,13,16; FAQ Core: .7w4g8qz

## Q05

May a Fast Power wait until Slow, may Powers interrupt each other, and when must a Slow Power’s target be selected?

**Expected:** A Fast Power must be used during Fast or foregone; it cannot wait until Slow. Players choose Power order but finish one before another. Choose the Slow target when using the Power in Slow, using the then-current legal range and target, not irrevocably at card play.

**Sources:** CORE pp.8,10; FAQ Core: .9v5ka9d

## Q06

In a Ravage a land has one Town, one Explorer and two undamaged Dahan, with no Defend or prior Blight. Resolve damage to land, Dahan, surviving counterattack and resulting Fear if the players destroy the Town.

**Expected:** Invaders do 3 Damage simultaneously to land and Dahan, adding one Blight, killing one Dahan and damaging the other by 1. The surviving Dahan still deals 2 Damage, destroying the Town and generating 1 Fear. Dahan do not absorb/prevent land damage; partial Dahan damage clears at Time Passes.

**Sources:** CORE pp.9,11,15-16

## Q07

Compare a Ravage reduced to zero Damage by Defend with a Ravage skipped entirely. Do Dahan fight back in each, and does Spirit Presence counterattack?

**Expected:** Surviving Dahan counterattack for 2 each even if Defend reduces Invader damage to zero. If the Ravage is skipped entirely, no counterattack. Presence does not counterattack.

**Sources:** CORE p.9

## Q08

During Build, what is added to lands containing only Explorers, equal numbers of Towns and Cities, more Towns than Cities, or no Invaders? Are Towns upgraded?

**Expected:** Only Explorers or equal Town/City numbers: add one Town. More Towns than Cities: add one City. No Invaders: no Build. These are new pieces from supply; no Town is removed/upgraded.

**Sources:** CORE p.10

## Q09

Which matching lands Explore, how many Explorers are added if there are multiple sources, do Explorers themselves provide a source, and what happens if no Invader Card can be drawn?

**Expected:** Explore matching lands that contain a Town/City or are adjacent to a Town/City/Ocean. Add exactly one Explorer from supply regardless of sources or existing pieces. Explorers are not sources. Needing to draw when the deck is empty loses; drawing the last existing card alone does not.

**Sources:** CORE pp.10,12,15

## Q10

After Explore, where do the Invader cards go? How are a Stage III two-terrain card and a Stage II flag handled without an Adversary?

**Expected:** Discard old Ravage, move Build to Ravage and just-explored card to Build. Stage III acts in both terrain types subject to usual action restrictions. Ignore Stage II flags without an Adversary.

**Sources:** CORE p.10

## Q11

In a two-Spirit game with 7 of 8 Fear markers already generated, an effect generates 3 Fear and earning a card exposes Terror II. Explain marker handling, new Terror timing, and when the card is revealed/resolved.

**Expected:** Move the eighth marker to earn the top Fear card face-down, reset all 8 to the pool and put the remaining 2 generated Fear toward the next card. Terror II applies as soon as exposed (subject to outcome check at action-tree end). Keep card hidden until Fear step, resolving in earned order at the then-current Terror level only.

**Sources:** CORE pp.9,12; FAQ Core: .3y28aut; .9v5kfq7

## Q12

A Fear card resolved in the Fear step earns another Fear card and exposes a higher Terror Level. When is that new card resolved, at which level do subsequent cards resolve, and do “each player” choices require that player’s own Presence?

**Expected:** Put the new card at the bottom of the resolving stack and resolve it in this same Fear step. Subsequent cards use the new Terror Level only. Each player resolves their instructions completely, restricted only by the card; own Presence/range is not required. Generic Presence/Sacred Site conditions may use any Spirit’s unless specifically individualized.

**Sources:** CORE pp.9,12; FAQ Core: .3y28aus; fear-cards-checking-for-presence

## Q13

A land with existing Blight has two Presence from Spirit A and one from Spirit B. One new Blight is added after setup. Describe Presence losses, cascade choices and what happens if the cascade enters another already-Blighted land.

**Expected:** Destroy one Presence each of A and B, putting destroyed disks beside the island, not on tracks. Add the original Blight and cascade one additional Blight to one adjacent land, not all. If that land already has Blight it too loses one Presence per Spirit there and cascades again. Existing amount of Blight does not multiply the one cascade.

**Sources:** CORE pp.9,15

## Q14

Where does setup Blight come from versus Blight added during play? When a Healthy card empties how is its new pool obtained, does the setup +1 repeat, and can removing Blight make it Healthy again?

**Expected:** Setup island Blight comes from the box, not initial pool, and does not cascade or destroy Presence. Play Blight comes from card/board pool. When Healthy empties flip and follow the Blighted instructions, adding the printed new per-player pool from box; do not apply setup +1 again. Removed island Blight returns to currently face-up card/board pool; a Blighted card never flips back.

**Sources:** CORE pp.6,15,18; FAQ Setup: .9v5ka5b; one-more-blight-errata; FAQ Core: .7w4g8r7

## Q15

Explain how Elements apply to multiple thresholds and multiple Powers. May an innate be used twice with twice the Elements, may its levels use different lands, and how does an “Instead” level work?

**Expected:** Elements are checked, not spent, so may meet any number of Powers/thresholds. An innate is normally one use, regardless of surplus Elements. Use all met levels top to bottom on one target, with Instead replacing previous effects. May pretend to have fewer Elements for the entire Power, not cherry-pick levels.

**Sources:** CORE pp.14,16-17; FAQ Core: .bu6od0c

## Q16

A legal target lacks pieces needed for one printed instruction. May the Power still be used? May the player choose individual unwanted instructions to skip, or forego the whole Power and recover its Energy?

**Expected:** May use it; perform instructions in order, skipping impossible parts and doing the rest. Cannot skip possible mandatory instructions at will (apart from permitted choices, up-to, or selecting fewer Elements consistently). May forego the whole Power; no Energy refund, and played-card Elements remain while in play.

**Sources:** CORE pp.16-17

## Q17

Explain gaining a new Minor or Major Power, including how many are drawn, where kept/unkept cards go, Major Forgetting options and the consequences of Forgetting a card already in play.

**Expected:** Choose Minor or Major, draw 4 from that deck, reshuffling its discard if needed; keep one in hand, discard the other three to its deck discard. Gaining a Major requires Forgetting any one owned card (hand, personal discard, in play, even new Major); forgotten Minor/Major goes to its deck discard, Unique under panel. Forgetting in-play immediately loses Elements and unused use; previously resolved effects persist for their normal duration.

**Sources:** CORE p.18

## Q18

How do Damage, Destroy, Remove and Replace differ for Fear and survival? Can Damage from separate normal Powers combine, does Damage normally hurt Dahan, and does replacement erase prior Damage?

**Expected:** Damage normally means to Invaders only, distributed as chosen in target land; can combine within turn, immediately destroying pieces at Damage >= Health. Destroying Town/City produces 1/2 Fear in addition to explicit Fear. Remove/Replace generate none automatically; Replace retains prior Damage and may cause destruction if Damage meets new Health (then destruction Fear applies). Surviving Damage clears at Time Passes. Dahan are harmed by Powers only if explicitly stated. An outright Destroy instruction eliminates its specified piece regardless of its Damage or Health. Remove likewise takes the specified piece off the board without requiring Damage to reach Health. Replace removes the original and puts the specified replacement in its place.

## Q19

Does increased Power Range extend Gather/Push distance, may multiple pieces come from/go to different adjacent lands, may pieces go into the Ocean normally, and may “up to” be zero?

**Expected:** Range boost changes reaching the target, not Gather/Push adjacency. Gather from adjacent land(s) into target; Push from target to adjacent land(s), distributing among them freely. No ordinary Ocean/off-board Push, as Ocean is not a land (specific Spirit exceptions override). Up to may be zero.

**Sources:** CORE pp.13,19,31

## Q20

State all four Terror victory conditions, the three standard defeat routes, and the result when a single Ravage loses a Spirit’s last Presence but surviving Dahan remove the final required Invader. Do you finish other lands first?

**Expected:** Terror I: no Invaders; II: no Towns/Cities; III: no Cities; earning final Fear card reveals Victory and wins without resolving it. Defeat: exhausted Blight pool as instructed (or board pool empty), any Spirit without island Presence, or need to draw Invader card from empty deck. Complete current action plus triggered actions before outcome check; single-land Ravage can yield sacrifice victory if both win/loss met. Do not continue to other land Ravages before that check.

**Sources:** CORE p.12; FAQ Core: .7w4gcs0; .bu6oc8v; .9v5kfq7

## Q21

What makes two lands adjacent, including across boards? Are all outside-edge lands Coastal, is Ocean normally a land, and can a land be adjacent to itself or within Range 1 of itself?

**Expected:** Adjacency is sharing a border or corner, including across boards. Coastal means touching printed Ocean, not cliff edges; other lands are Inland. Ocean is not normally a land/in play. A land is not adjacent to itself but is Range 0 of itself and hence within Range 1.

**Sources:** CORE pp.13,31-32; FAQ Core: is-a-land-adjacent-to-or-within-range-of-itself

## Q22

How is a Spirit’s starting hand obtained, where is its setup Presence placed, what if multiple legal setup choices or two placement instructions name the same land, and is its later play confined to that board?

**Expected:** Take its four Unique cards as starting hand, follow back-panel setup on a different starting board per Spirit, choose among equally legal options, and allow multiple instructions to place in the same land. Later Presence and Powers may use any board.

**Sources:** CORE p.6; FAQ Setup: Spirit-with-multiple-ways-to-set-up; two-setup-instructions-placing-presence-in-the-same-land

## Q23

Does one Presence each from two Spirits form a Sacred Site? Does four Presence from one Spirit count as two sites, and what does “your land” mean?

**Expected:** No, ordinarily one Spirit needs at least two own disks. Four of that Spirit’s disks in one land still make only one site. Your land is a land containing your Presence.

**Sources:** CORE p.13; FAQ Core: .9v5ka9f

## Q24

A Growth option includes an impossible instruction and an explicit cost. Can it be selected, can possible instructions be skipped, and may one Spirit wait for another’s Growth result before choosing?

**Expected:** May select despite an impossible non-cost instruction, doing all possible instructions in any order and skipping impossible parts. Explicit cost must be paid in full. May wait for another Spirit’s Growth result; each obeys own Growth/Energy/play order and all finish Spirit Phase before any Fast resolution.

**Sources:** CORE p.8; FAQ Core: .3y28aul; .7w4g7z1

## Q25

What cards does ordinary Reclaim return, and when and how often can a newly uncovered Reclaim One space be used?

**Expected:** Ordinary Reclaim returns all personal-discard cards to hand, not currently played or forgotten cards. Reclaim One returns one personal-discard card at any time during Spirit Phase once per phase, including the turn it is uncovered.

**Sources:** CORE pp.14,31

## Q26

When a track Element is revealed after Growth, when does it become usable? When may an Any track Element be chosen and changed, and what if an in-play card is Forgotten?

**Expected:** Track Element is usable immediately whenever revealed and continuously while uncovered. Any may be selected at any time in the turn but cannot change once chosen until next turn. Forgetting an in-play card immediately removes its Elements.

**Sources:** CORE pp.14,16,18; FAQ Core: .7w4g8rf; .7w4g8p4

## Q27

A Power’s earlier instruction uncovers an Element needed for a later threshold. Can that later threshold apply? What if Elements are lost instead, and can earlier resolved effects be revised?

**Expected:** Yes, check Elements at the later threshold. Lost Elements can prevent later thresholds. Completed effects cannot be revised retroactively; Speed/Range/targeting modifications must be checked when selecting/using those attributes, and or-branch choice precedes branch resolution.

**Sources:** FAQ Core: .9v5kcmx; .bu6od0c

## Q28

Must a land at Range 0 contain your Presence? Can a numeric Range boost waive a Sacred Site origin, a target-terrain restriction or the text “one of your lands”?

**Expected:** Range 0 ordinarily means a land containing your Presence. A boost only extends numeric Range; it does not waive Sacred Site/source, terrain/contents target restrictions or the requirement that a land be one of yours.

**Sources:** CORE pp.16,32; FAQ Core: .3y28aqg; .7w4g8nq

## Q29

May a legally targeted Power count as used if its instructions accomplish nothing? Does foregoing its text count as targeting it? Can an innate with only its own Speed/Range modification available be used?

**Expected:** May target and use a legal Power even if nothing actually changes, provided it has an instruction with potential to affect game state and attempts its text. Foregoing the text is not using/targeting it. Merely modifying its own Speed/Range does not qualify.

**Sources:** FAQ Core: .7w4gebo; .7w4gee1; can-an-innate-power-that-only-changes-speed-or-range-be-targeted

## Q30

A card adds a Town in Slow and says Invaders skip Build this turn. What remains next turn? At Time Passes where do played cards go, what happens to damaged survivors and reminder markers, and do track Elements vanish?

**Expected:** Added Town remains as a board change. Build skip expires at Time Passes. Played cards go to personal discard, all surviving Invader/Dahan Damage clears, card Elements and temporary effects clear, reminder markers removed. Track Elements remain while uncovered.

**Sources:** CORE pp.10,16-17

## Q31

Do damaged Invaders and Dahan deal less Damage? If Defend 1 and Defend 2 both affect a land with total Invader Damage 4, what Damage reaches the land and Dahan?

**Expected:** Damaged pieces deal normal Damage. Defend adds to 3, reducing 4 to 1 separately to land and Dahan, so no Blight and one Dahan takes 1 Damage if present.

**Sources:** CORE pp.9,18; FAQ Core: .9v5kc4h

## Q32

If a Town is replaced by an Explorer while retaining 1 Damage, does the replacement itself cause Fear, does the Explorer survive, and why? Can reduced Health destroy a piece without new Damage?

**Expected:** Replacement itself causes no Fear. Retained 1 Damage meets Explorer Health 1, so it is destroyed, giving no Fear for an Explorer. More generally, a piece is destroyed immediately once existing Damage meets its reduced/new Health, even without new Damage.

**Sources:** CORE pp.15,18; FAQ Core: .bu6oczl

## Q33

Do Dahan automatically attack when a Spirit damages or Pushing Invaders into their land, and can a Power’s unqualified Damage hit Dahan? Are Dahan and Invaders limited by the physical supply?

**Expected:** No automatic Dahan attack except Ravage counterattack or explicit Power instruction. Unqualified Power Damage is to Invaders; Dahan require explicit harmful instructions. Dahan and Invaders are unlimited; use substitutes when pieces run out.

**Sources:** CORE pp.15-16,18

## Q34

Do increased Range and a large number of pieces let a single ordinary Power distribute its effects across multiple target lands? What does Push up to 3 Explorer/Town allow in terms of total pieces?

**Expected:** No; all ordinary effects remain in one target land, except explicit instructions such as Push destinations. Slash means either/both types within a combined limit: up to three pieces total, not three of each.

**Sources:** CORE pp.16-17,32; FAQ Core: .9v5kavt

## Q35

If Gather 3 or Push 3 lacks “up to,” may the player move only one when three legal pieces are available? What if only two are available? Can unrestricted Move leave a piece in its current land?

**Expected:** Must move three if possible, otherwise as many as possible (two). Unrestricted Move can choose the current land and counts as movement; Gather/Push cannot because they require an adjacent land.

**Sources:** CORE pp.17,19; FAQ Core: .7w4g8of; .9v5kfr0

## Q36

What is the difference between a Growth Move Presence arrow and Add Presence using an island disk? Can the latter pick up a disk outside the shown Range of the destination?

**Expected:** Move measures from that disk’s present land and moves up to the arrow’s distance. Add/reposition first picks a legal destination measured from any existing Presence, then may take any own island disk, even one far from that destination. It remains adding, not a Move instruction.

**Sources:** CORE p.14; FAQ Core: .7w4g8oq; .bu6od08

## Q37

May a newly gained Growth Power be played in the same Spirit Phase? Does a Power gained in Fast automatically enter play or resolve immediately, and is there a hand-size or hand-secrecy limit?

**Expected:** A Growth-gained card enters hand and may be played in the later play/pay step. A Fast-gained card merely enters hand unless explicitly permitted to play it; even explicit play does not immediately resolve unless stated. No hand-size limit; hands can be shown.

**Sources:** CORE pp.8,18; FAQ Core: .9v5kcmw; timing-of-resolving-a-power-played-mid-turn

## Q38

An effect explicitly allows playing a card in Fast after the Spirit used all normal Card Plays. Is this legal and when are its Elements/effects available?

**Expected:** Yes, effect-granted plays are additional to the normal Spirit-phase track limit. Follow payment instructions, gain Elements immediately, and use effects in its speed phase when no other action is resolving, unless explicitly instructed otherwise.

**Sources:** FAQ Core: .bu6ocb5; timing-of-resolving-a-power-played-mid-turn

## Q39

If Entwined Power gifts a Major Power, must the recipient Forget? If a Spirit instead gains two Majors, can a single Forget cover both? Is an ordinary forgotten Unique reclaimable?

**Expected:** Gifting/transferring is not Gain and requires no Forget. Each Major gained needs its own separate Forget, unless explicitly exempted, so one card cannot cover both. A forgotten Unique is under panel and cannot be retrieved by ordinary Reclaim/gain.

**Sources:** CORE p.18; FAQ Core: .7w4g8pe; .7w4gee7; .9v5kc40

## Q40

When a Fast Gift of Strength or Powerstorm grants a Repeat, must the card be chosen immediately? Can the chosen card be Slow, can it be an innate, and do you gain its printed Elements again?

**Expected:** Choose eligible Power Card when actually repeating, not necessarily at grant time. Slow cards can be repeated in Slow; these two effects specify cards and cannot repeat Innates. Printed card Elements are not granted again; explicit Elements in the repeated text are granted again.

**Sources:** CORE p.19; FAQ Core: timing-of-choosing-powers-for-once-this-turn-repeats; .7w4g8op; .3y28aex; .9v5ka9b

## Q41

If a Power’s Range was increased and it was made Fast, what happens on Repeat? If Elements enabling a non-Range threshold have been lost, does the Repeat automatically retain that threshold?

**Expected:** Range/speed modifications carry over, so Repeat retains extended Range and Fast speed. Thresholds otherwise check current Elements; losing them may mean a previously met threshold no longer applies.

**Sources:** CORE p.19; FAQ Core: .9v5kavs; Repeating-internal-range-boosts

## Q42

What happens when Powerstorm itself is Repeated, and can its threshold spend all three Repeat permissions on the same Power Card?

**Expected:** Repeat instructions on a repeated Power are ignored, so repeating Powerstorm grants no Repeat permissions; its non-Repeat Energy gain still happens. Its threshold allows up to three different Power Cards, not the same one three times.

**Sources:** CORE p.19; FAQ Core: repeating-power-cards-that-grant-repeats; .7w4g8q9

## Q43

A Power says Repeat this Power in an adjacent land, but that land fails the original Blight target restriction or normal Range. Is it legal, and can the original land be selected?

**Expected:** Explicit Repeat location overrides original Range/target restrictions, so the adjacent land can be used despite them. Original land is not adjacent to itself and cannot be selected under this instruction.

**Sources:** CORE p.19; FAQ Core: .9v5kfgr; is-a-land-adjacent-to-or-within-range-of-itself

## Q44

Is Presence destroyed on moving into an already-Blighted land? If a cascade loops back into a land it already hit, can it destroy Presence there again, and does existing Blight quantity multiply cascading?

**Expected:** Entering existing Blight does not destroy Presence. Each new Blight addition, including cascade revisits, destroys one Presence per Spirit present and can cascade onward again. Existing Blight quantity does not multiply the single outgoing cascade.

**Sources:** CORE p.15; FAQ Core: .3y28auk; .7w4g8nw

## Q45

If a Healthy Blight pool empties before a cascade is complete, when do you flip/refill and do you finish the cascade? Does removing Blight restore destroyed Presence?

**Expected:** Immediately follow empty-pool instructions, flip/refill from box as directed, and continue unfinished additions/cascades; no setup +1 is added on flip. Removing Blight only returns it to active pool; does not restore destroyed Presence or flip back to Healthy.

**Sources:** CORE pp.15,18; FAQ Core: .7w4g8r7; one-more-blight-errata

## Q46

If a Ravage in one land cascades Blight across the board boundary, is that cascade a separate independent action for victory/defeat checks? May players choose another land’s Ravage first to win before a later losing Ravage?

**Expected:** Cascade remains part of original single-land Ravage action tree, not a separate outcome-check boundary. Players choose land order and may win at the end of one complete Ravage tree before another land Ravages and could cause loss.

**Sources:** CORE p.9; FAQ Core: .7w4geg1; .bu6oc8v; .9v5kfq7

## Q47

A Stage III Ravage lists Sands and Jungles. Before it starts one matching land is empty. A triggered effect during another Ravage moves an Invader into that empty land and more Invaders into a land already due to Ravage. Which lands Ravage and which Invaders participate?

**Expected:** Determine matching eligible lands before resolving the card: initially empty land does not gain a Ravage from this card. In a land already queued to Ravage, new Invaders participate when it resolves. Players may choose any order across both terrains.

**Sources:** FAQ Core: pieces-added-or-moved-by-ravages-do-not-cause-more-ravages; .9v5kaam

## Q48

What does the Coastal Lands Invader card affect when Exploring, Building and Ravaging, and do its Build/Ravage effects bypass usual requirements?

**Expected:** All Coastal lands across boards; later Build/Ravage likewise in Coastal lands. Normal restrictions remain: Build/Ravage only eligible lands with Invaders, Explore requires sources (Ocean provides coast access).

**Sources:** CORE pp.9-10,13; FAQ Core: .7w4g9as

## Q49

If a Year of Perfect Stillness or Paralyzing Fright skips actions in a land containing a Town, does it stop an adjacent matching land Exploring from that Town?

**Expected:** No. Skipping actions in source land does not remove its Town/City or prevent neighboring Explore checking that source; the Explore action happens in the destination land.

**Sources:** FAQ Core: .7w4g8p8

## Q50

After Invader cards advance, can Slow Powers remove a newly added Explorer to prevent a future Build, or has it already Built? When does Fear from a Slow Power normally resolve?

**Expected:** Yes: just-Explored card is now in Build for next turn, so removing that Explorer before then can prevent its Build. It did not Build during that Explore. Fear cards earned in Slow wait until next Invader Phase’s Fear step.

**Sources:** CORE pp.9-10,12,21

## Q51

Do each-player Fear choices need different lands or pieces, and may a player use another Spirit’s Sacred Site for a generic Fear-card condition? What if the card explicitly counts each Spirit’s own Sacred Sites?

**Expected:** Different choices only when the card requires them; a piece may move multiple times across successive player choices if legal. Generic Presence/site conditions accept any Spirit’s; explicitly individual own-site counts use each Spirit’s sites separately.

**Sources:** CORE p.9; FAQ Core: fear-cards-checking-for-presence; .9v5ka9n

## Q52

An effect removes two Invaders in a land with two Cities. Must Explorers be removed first, and if a card instead allows removing a number of Health worth, does existing Damage reduce that Health?

**Expected:** May remove both Cities; Invader includes all three types and no Explorer-first rule applies. Health allowance uses current Health modifiers but marked Damage does not reduce Health.

**Sources:** CORE pp.15,18,30; FAQ Core: .9v5kaai

## Q53

If a condition says Dahan outnumber Towns/Cities, can it hold with zero buildings? What if both sides are zero?

**Expected:** Yes with at least one Dahan and zero buildings. Zero versus zero is not outnumbering.

**Sources:** CORE p.31; FAQ Core: .bu6od0k

## Q54

How does one-Spirit solo change Another Spirit targeting and conditional other-Spirit bonuses? Does one human playing two Spirits get that same self-targeting exception or use one-person setup scaling?

**Expected:** Single Spirit may target itself with Another Spirit, but cannot get bonuses conditional on actually targeting another. One human playing two Spirits fills two player roles, scales by two and does not get the single-Spirit targeting exception.

**Sources:** CORE p.22; FAQ Setup: Can-one-person-play-more-than-one-Spirit

## Q55

When players disagree about a Power’s choices, a Blight cascade destination, or a decision outside any land, who ultimately decides?

**Expected:** Power user chooses Power decisions. Otherwise seek consensus; if impossible, land decisions belong to Spirit starting on that board, and non-land decisions to game owner/organizer.

**Sources:** CORE p.8 sidebar

## Q56

Do low-complexity Spirits always have to use Power Progression? If used, how are gains and Major Forgetting handled, and how does the introductory no-Blight-Card game end from Blight?

**Expected:** Progression is optional teaching aid. Take next listed card into hand instead of draw-four/keep-one; gaining a Major still requires Forgetting. No-card initial pool is 5 per Spirit +1 total and emptying it loses, without flip or ongoing Blighted effect.

**Sources:** CORE pp.3,6,18; FAQ Core: .9v5kavp; FAQ Setup: one-more-blight-errata

## Q57

For Dahan Enheartened at Terror II or III, may each player choose a land already containing Dahan and Gather zero? Must chosen lands differ and is Damage per Invader?

**Expected:** Yes, each player chooses a different land and may Gather up to two (including zero). Then II deals one total Damage if Dahan present; III deals total Damage equal to Dahan present, not that amount to each Invader.

**Sources:** FAQ Core: .7w4g8rq; .7w4g8ra
