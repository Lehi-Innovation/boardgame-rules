**Exact effect text**, transcribing the Fear and Dahan icons as bracketed names:

> 1 [Fear].
> [Dahan] take no Damage from Ravaging Invaders this turn.

**Precise protection scope:** In the target land, the protection applies to Damage dealt to Dahan by Ravaging Invaders during this turn. It prevents that Damage rather than providing a numerical Defend value. It does not protect the land from Invader Damage. It does not say that Dahan ignore all Damage, and it does not protect them from an instruction to Destroy Dahan. Damage from a source other than Ravaging Invaders is outside its printed protection. “This turn” is its duration; “Fast” is the Power's resolution speed, not a restriction limiting protection to the Fast phase. The ordinary Ravage occurs in the Invader phase, after this Fast Power resolves. This card is therefore a suitable original-core example for the distinction between protecting Dahan alone and reducing Damage to the land.
