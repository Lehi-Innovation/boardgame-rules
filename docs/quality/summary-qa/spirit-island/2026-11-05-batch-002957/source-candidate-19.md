# Spirit Island

## Overview

A cooperative game in which each player is a Spirit protecting an island and its native Dahan from colonizing Invaders. All players win or lose together. Grow, play Powers, generate Fear and eliminate the Invaders required by the current Terror Level before Blight, destruction of a Spirit, or the Invader deck defeats you.

**Scope:** Complete standard original base-game procedures on the balanced maps, including normal Blight Cards and solo play. Follow the chosen Spirit panel, Power Cards, Fear Cards and Blight Card for their individual instructions; their explicit exceptions override general rules. The rulebook-linked Living FAQ corrections and core clarifications are applied below and identified in **FAQ & Rulings**. Adversaries, Scenarios, thematic maps, optional difficulty adjustments, optional performance scoring and all expansions/promos/later-product content are excluded. The introductory no-Blight-Card and Power Progression alternatives are described separately in Setup.

## Components

The base box contains 1 Invader Board, 20 Fear markers, 4 double-sided Island Boards; 8 Spirit panels and their 32 Unique Power Cards; 36 Minor and 22 Major Power Cards; 15 Fear Cards and 2 Terror dividers; 15 Invader Cards; 2 Blight Cards; 40 Explorers, 32 Towns, 20 Cities, 36 Dahan and 38 Blight; 13 Presence disks in each of 4 colors; 12 single-turn reminder markers; 32 Energy markers; 8 player aids and 4 Power Progression cards. The included 3 Adversary panels/reminder tiles and 4 Scenario panels are outside this summary's scope. [CORE pp.4–5]

| Piece | Health | Ravage Damage | Fear on destruction |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Explorer | 1 | 1 | 0 |
| Town | 2 | 2 | 1 |
| City | 3 | 3 | 2 |
| Dahan | 2 | 2 in counterattack | 0 |

Invaders and Dahan are not limited by the supplied pieces; use substitutes or Energy markers to mark multiples if necessary. No standard stacking limit applies to pieces in a land. Presence is limited to your disks; destroyed disks are separate from unused track disks. [CORE pp.13,15–16; FAQ .9v5ka9f]

## Setup

**Deck preparation includes randomization, not just selecting counts:** shuffle all Fear Cards before taking the nine used cards; shuffle each Invader stage separately before removing its unseen unused card and stacking the stages.

1. **Island:** Randomly choose one Island Board per Spirit and join the balanced sides into the island. For one Spirit use one board. For two, any fitting arrangement is legal; the rulebook illustrates bottom-to-bottom with Oceans facing opposite outer corners. For three use the triangular layout, and for four the two-row parallelogram layout illustrated in CORE pp.6–7. [FAQ .7w4g8pg] Populate every land with its printed Invader, Dahan and Blight setup icons. Setup Blight comes from the box, never the Blight pool, and does not cascade or destroy Presence.
2. **Invader deck:** Shuffle each stage separately. Remove one card of each stage unseen. Stack **3 Stage I cards on top, 4 Stage II below them, 5 Stage III at the bottom**. Put this 12-card deck in Explore.
3. **Fear:** Put **4 Fear markers per Spirit** in the Fear Pool. Shuffle the 15 Fear Cards and use 9 unseen: three cards, Terror II divider, three cards, Terror III divider, three cards, from top to bottom. Start at Terror I. Leave space for Earned Fear Cards and Fear discard.
4. **Blight:** Randomly select a Blight Card, Healthy Island side up; do not inspect its reverse. Put **2 Blight per Spirit plus exactly 1 additional Blight total** on it. This is the official initial-pool correction. The additional 1 is applied only at setup, never again on a card flip. Remaining box Blight is separate from this pool.
5. **Supply:** Shuffle Minor and Major decks separately, each with its own discard. Make Invaders, Dahan and Energy available to everyone.
6. **Spirits:** Each player takes one color's 13 Presence and reminder markers (any accidental extra disk is a spare, not additional Presence for play), a different Spirit panel, and that Spirit's four Unique cards as their starting hand. Each starts on a different Island Board and follows the back-of-panel setup exactly. Where several choices equally satisfy setup, choose freely; separate Presence-placement instructions may use the same land. Put remaining disks on the dashed spaces of both Presence tracks; the leftmost solid starting spaces remain uncovered. Starting boards do not restrict where a Spirit can act after setup.
7. **Initial Explore:** Reveal the top Invader card and Explore normally. Then put it face-up in **Build**. This Explore is the last part of setup; Ravage starts empty. The first actual turn begins with the Spirit Phase.

**Introductory alternatives (CORE p.3):** Choose a low-complexity Spirit: Lightning's Swift Strike, Vital Strength of the Earth, River Surges in Sunlight, or Shadows Flicker Like Flame. A new player may use its Power Progression: remove the listed Minor and Major cards from the shared decks and reserve them; on gaining a Power, take the next one into hand instead of drawing four. Gaining a Major still requires Forgetting a card. If everyone is new, omit the Blight Card and use the printed board pool: **5 Blight per Spirit +1 total** at setup, losing if it empties; there is no flip or ongoing Blighted effect. Do not use an Adversary or Scenario. Progressions are a teaching aid, not compulsory for low-complexity Spirits.

## Turn Structure

Each turn follows **Spirit Phase → Fast Power Phase → Invader Phase → Slow Power Phase → Time Passes**. Finish a phase before beginning the next. Players choose the order of Powers within the appropriate Power Phase, completing one before the next. All players act simultaneously within a phase and may discuss freely. Each Spirit must finish its own Growth → Gain Energy → Play/Pay in that order, but may wait to see another Spirit's Growth or draws before making choices. **Every Spirit must finish the entire Spirit Phase before any Fast Power resolves.**

### 1. Spirit Phase

**Growth:** Choose one boxed Growth option unless your panel specifies otherwise. Perform all its instructions if possible, in any order you choose. If an instruction is impossible, do as much as possible and skip that part; an explicit cost for an option must be paid in full to choose it. Common effects:

- **Add Presence:** Choose a legal land within the shown maximum Range of your existing Presence. Take the leftmost remaining disk from either Presence track and add it there. With multiple placements, resolve them sequentially: a newly placed disk may be the origin for the next. You may instead **reposition any of your island Presence**, even with track disks remaining: first measure range and select the destination using existing Presence, including the disk about to be repositioned, then take a disk from anywhere on the island. This is still an Add Presence instruction, not a Move instruction. Destroyed Presence cannot be used unless explicitly permitted.
- **Move Presence:** A curved movement arrow moves the specified Presence from its present location up to the shown distance; this differs from Add/reposition. Push/Gather Presence uses normal adjacency rules. Instructions are mandatory where possible; a move up to a Range can be zero.
- **Gain Energy:** Gain the displayed amount in addition to normal income.
- **Gain a Power Card:** Use the Actions procedure below.
- **Reclaim:** Return all cards in your personal discard to hand, not cards currently in play or forgotten cards.

**Gain Energy:** Take Energy equal to the **highest uncovered number** on your Energy/Turn track. Track numbers are not added together. Unspent Energy carries over between turns; it is individual and cannot ordinarily be transferred to another Spirit.

**Play and pay:** Play any number of cards from hand up to the highest uncovered Card Plays number, paying their printed Energy costs now, including Slow cards. Gain every played card's printed Elements immediately. No effects resolve yet and no targets need be committed yet. Card Plays cannot be shared or saved for a later turn. Energy expected from a Fast Power cannot pay these earlier Spirit-phase costs.

A revealed **Reclaim One** track space permits taking one card from your personal discard to hand once each Spirit Phase, at any point in that phase, including the turn revealed. Track Element spaces grant their Elements continuously while uncovered; unlike numbered track benefits these are additional Elements. Choose an **Any** track Element at any time each turn, but once chosen do not change it until next turn. In contrast, a flexible Element directly granted by an action must be chosen immediately when gained. [FAQ choosing-flexible-elements]

### 2. Fast Power Phase

Use Fast Power Cards in play and eligible Fast Innates. Players choose their order; finish one Power before starting another. A Power cannot interrupt another Power. You may forego a Power entirely, retaining a played card's Elements but receiving no Energy refund. A Fast Power cannot be saved for Slow: use it now or forego it.

### 3. Invader Phase

1. **Blighted Island:** If the Blight Card is Blighted, follow its instructions at the specified timing (typically start of each Invader Phase). No such step applies with the printed no-card pool.
2. **Fear:** Turn the earned face-down stack over and resolve its cards **in the order earned**, one at a time, without looking ahead. Use **only the current Terror Level's effect**, even if the card was earned at a lower level. Discard each resolved card to Fear discard. Fear earned during this step can earn more cards: append them to the bottom of the resolving stack and resolve them this same step. Subsequent cards use any newly reached Terror Level. Fear earned later, such as in Ravage or Slow, waits for the next Fear step.
3. **Ravage:** Resolve the card in Ravage, if any.
4. **Build:** Resolve the card in Build, if any.
5. **Explore:** Reveal the next Invader card and resolve Explore. If a card must be drawn and the deck is empty, lose. Revealing the last existing card is not itself a loss.
6. **Advance cards:** Discard the old Ravage card; slide Build to Ravage and the just-revealed Explore card to Build.

An empty action space does nothing. An Invader card affects all matching lands across the entire island, subject to its action's eligibility. A Stage III card with two terrains affects both; players may interleave lands of the two terrains. The Stage II **Coastal Lands** card affects all Coastal lands, not a terrain. **Ignore Stage II flag/Escalation icons without an Adversary.**

Before resolving the actions from a card, determine which lands qualify; players choose the order of those lands. Changes during the card do not add newly eligible lands to that list. Determine eligibility anew for each later card, so a land newly occupied during an earlier Ravage card can Ravage under a later matching Ravage card. In a land already due to Ravage, newly arrived Invaders participate when its Ravage resolves. Resolve each land fully, including cascades and triggered effects, and check outcomes before proceeding to the next. [CORE pp.9–10; FAQ pieces-added-or-moved-by-ravages-do-not-cause-more-ravages; .9v5kaam]

#### Ravage

Matching lands with Invaders Ravage. Total the Damage: **1 per Explorer +2 per Town +3 per City**, then apply Defend and other explicit modifiers. Damage already marked on a piece does not reduce its outgoing Damage. Invaders deal the resulting Damage **equally, separately and simultaneously** to the land and its Dahan:

- **Land:** If it takes **2 or more Damage**, add **one Blight**, regardless of how much more than 2. One Damage to land does nothing and is not saved for another action. Resolve Blight/Presence loss and cascading as described below.
- **Dahan:** Every 2 Damage kills one Dahan; allocate efficiently to kill as many as possible, rather than spreading damage to keep all alive. If 1 remains after that, mark one surviving Dahan with 1 Damage. Return destroyed Dahan to supply.

Dahan do not shield the land; Damage to them is not subtracted from land Damage. **After Invader damage is fully resolved**, surviving Dahan each deal **2 Damage** to Invaders in that land, allocated by the players. A damaged surviving Dahan still deals 2. Surviving Dahan counterattack even when Invader Damage was reduced to zero by Defend, but not when the entire Ravage was skipped. Presence never counterattacks. If a Power changes Dahan Ravage Damage timing, that replaces their usual counterattack timing; each Dahan deals Ravage Damage only once. **Words of Warning** makes Dahan and Invader Ravage Damage simultaneous, so Dahan killed in that exchange still deal their Damage, with no second later counterattack. Losing Earth's defending Sacred Site to the resulting Blight does not retroactively remove its Defend from the simultaneous Damage to Dahan in that Ravage. [FAQ number-of-counterattacks-when-dahan-counterattack-early; .bu6oc8y]

Invaders and Dahan sharing a land do not automatically fight at other times.

Example: A Town, Explorer and two Dahan with no Defend cause 3 land Damage (one Blight), and simultaneously 3 Dahan Damage (one dead, one damaged). The survivor can destroy the Town for 1 Fear. [CORE pp.9,11]

#### Build

In every matching land **with at least one Invader**, add exactly one building from supply:

- More Towns than Cities: add a **City**.
- Otherwise, including equal counts or only Explorers: add a **Town**.

Do not remove or upgrade an existing Town. Empty matching lands do not Build.

#### Explore

Add **one Explorer from supply** to every matching land which either contains a Town or City, or is adjacent to a Town, City, or Ocean. Existing pieces do not stop Explore; multiple sources still yield just one Explorer. Explorers do not act as sources. Land with no qualifying source is not Explored. Skipping actions in a neighboring land does not stop its Towns/Cities serving as sources for Explore elsewhere.

### 4. Slow Power Phase

Use eligible Slow Innates and Slow cards already in play, following the same resolution rules as Fast. Choose targets now, based on the current board. A Slow Power made Fast is used in Fast only, not again in Slow.

### 5. Time Passes

Discard played cards to each owner's **personal discard**. All Damage on surviving Invaders and Dahan clears, Elements from cards go away, and single-turn effects expire; remove reminder markers. Unused Energy remains. Permanent board changes, such as moved pieces, destroyed pieces, removed Blight and earned Fear, remain. Printed track Elements remain while uncovered.

## Actions

### Using Powers and Elements

An Innate is available each turn when its Element requirements are met; ordinarily it costs no Energy and uses no Card Play. Elements are **checked, never spent**. The same Elements can satisfy multiple Powers and thresholds. Gain a card's Elements as soon as it is played, whether or not its text is used, and lose them as soon as it leaves play.

Choose the target **when using a Power**, not when playing its card. Meet both Range/source and target restrictions then:

- The **targeting Range box** gives the maximum land distance from **one of your Presence**. Range 0 requires your Presence in the target land. Origin icons may require your Sacred Site or Presence in a particular terrain.
- Target restrictions may specify terrain, Coastal/Inland, contents, or a Spirit. A slash between piece/terrain icons means either or both types, not an additional allowance for each.
- Unless explicitly different, use **one target land or Spirit, once per turn**. Multiple effects and all innate levels apply to that same target. You cannot pay twice for an ordinary card use or use an innate twice just by having twice its Elements.
- Resolve instructions in printed order, doing as much as possible. Inability to perform one instruction does not prohibit playing or using a Power on an otherwise legal target. Target restrictions are in its target bar, not implied by its text. You may even target a land where the effects accomplish nothing, but must actually attempt the text to count as using/targeting the Power.
- You may forego the whole Power, but cannot arbitrarily omit a possible mandatory instruction after starting. “Up to” allows zero. “Or” chooses between its adjoining clauses, not other separate text on the card. Choose the branch **before resolving either option**; you cannot start one to inspect its results and then switch to the other. [FAQ .9v5kcmx]
- Resolve the base effect and every met threshold in printed order **unless explicit timing text says otherwise**: “First” and “Before” can place a threshold instruction ahead of earlier-printed base instructions. A level saying **Instead** replaces previous effects rather than adding to them. You may treat yourself as having fewer Elements **for the whole Power**, consistently, rather than selecting individual thresholds to omit.
- Check Elements when a threshold is reached: newly gained Elements can enable later levels; losing Elements can prevent later levels, without changing completed effects. Apply a threshold changing Speed, Range or targeting when checking that attribute, before the ordinary effects: for example, Thunderspeaker's met Fast threshold permits using its Innate in Fast. Speed/Range/targeting modifiers are checked in time to use them before targeting, and an “or” choice is made before its branch resolves.
- Non-board-changing effects last for this turn only unless explicitly stated otherwise. Defend and action skips do not remain for future turns.

### Gaining, Forgetting and playing extra cards

To **Gain a Power Card**, choose the Minor or Major deck, draw 4 (reshuffling that deck's discard if needed), keep 1 in hand, and discard the rest to that deck's shared discard. No hand-size limit applies and players may show their hands.

After **gaining a Major**, Forget one of your cards permanently. You may Forget from hand, personal discard, or play, including the Major just gained. A forgotten Minor/Major goes to its shared deck discard; a forgotten Unique goes under its Spirit panel, unavailable to ordinary Reclaim or gain. If you Forget from play, immediately lose its Elements and any unused activation; an already resolved effect persists normally. Each Major gained requires its own Forget; a single card cannot satisfy separate Forget requirements.

An effect that **gifts/transfers** a Major, or explicitly says to **take** one without Forgetting, differs from gaining it. A gain still requires Forgetting unless explicitly exempted. Follow the effect's wording. Cards gained enter hand unless explicitly placed elsewhere. Gaining during Growth allows playing that card in the later play/pay step; gaining it mid-turn does not by itself let you play it.

An effect explicitly letting you **play a card** outside the normal Spirit-phase play step grants an additional play beyond the track limit. Follow its payment instructions, gain Elements immediately, but resolve the new Power only in its appropriate speed phase when no other action is in progress, unless explicitly permitted to resolve immediately.

### Damage, Destroy, Remove, Replace and Defend

“Damage” means **Damage to Invaders** unless explicitly stated otherwise. Normal Spirit Damage does not harm Dahan or the land. Distribute Damage among Invaders in the target land however you wish; Explorers need not be hit first. Damage from separate normal Powers and counterattacks can accumulate within a turn. A piece is destroyed immediately when its marked Damage reaches/exceeds its Health. Reduced Health can also destroy a previously damaged piece; Damage does not itself reduce Health. If a damaged piece moves out of a Health bonus or into a Health penalty and is thereby destroyed, destruction occurs in its destination land, never in an in-between location. [FAQ if-an-invader-with-health-bonus-and-damage-is-moved-where-is-it-destroyed]

**Destroy** returns pieces to supply regardless of Health when directly instructed. Every destroyed Town generates **1 Fear** and every destroyed City **2 Fear**, in addition to explicit Fear from the effect. Explorers generate none automatically. **Remove** returns the specified pieces without requiring Damage to reach their Health and without destruction Fear. **Replace** removes a piece and adds another, retaining its marked Damage: the replacement itself produces no Fear, but if the retained Damage destroys the new piece, destruction Fear applies normally. For simultaneous replacements, select all pieces before replacing any: the same original City cannot become a Town and then an Explorer within that one simultaneous instruction. Separate completed instructions or actions may replace the resulting piece again (e.g., successive players resolving **Trade Suffers** at Terror III).

**Defend N** reduces each occurrence of Invader Damage to both land and Dahan by N for this turn, to a minimum of zero. Multiple Defend effects add. It does not reduce Spirit Damage or make Dahan do less counterattack Damage. Land and Dahan receive the reduction separately, not by splitting a shared allowance. A protection explicitly limited to Dahan is different: **Concealing Shadows** makes Dahan take no Damage from Ravaging Invaders this turn, while land still takes its normal Damage and can Blight. Protected surviving Dahan then counterattack normally. It does not protect them from direct Destroy instructions or Damage from other sources. [FAQ .7w4g8nu; original card text]

### Gather, Push and other movement

**Gather N:** Move N of the specified pieces from adjacent land(s) into the target land. **Push N:** Move N from the target to adjacent land(s). Multiple pieces may come from or go to different adjacent lands. A numeric instruction without “up to” is mandatory to the extent possible; “up to” may be zero. Increased Power Range does **not** increase Gather/Push distance. Ordinary movement cannot send pieces off the island or into the Ocean; explicit Spirit rules can change Ocean legality.

A land is not adjacent to itself, so cannot Gather/Push to itself. It is at Range 0 of itself and hence within any greater Range. An unrestricted “Move” can move a piece to its current land and counts as movement, unlike Gather/Push, which require adjacency.

### Repeat

A Repeat activates a Power's effects again; it is not a second card play and grants no additional printed card Elements. It **does** count as another Power use. For effects counting cards played (such as Boon of Vigor), a Repeat adds no card play; count the card's original play only. A repeated effect explicitly granting Elements grants those again. Recheck thresholds using your current Elements; they were not spent on the original, but may since have been lost.

Retain changes to the Power, including extra Range or changed speed. An ordinary Repeat uses the Power's **most recently assigned speed**: a Slow Power repeats in Slow even if its permission came from a Fast Power; a formerly Fast Power explicitly made Slow before repeating now repeats in Slow. An instruction to Repeat immediately instead follows that explicit timing, directly after the originating use. [FAQ repeating-powers-with-changed-speeds] You may postpone choosing which eligible card to repeat until you actually repeat it, and make different choices/targets then. For **Powerstorm** and **Gift of Strength**, the Spirit receives a once-this-turn option rather than a grant-time selection of cards: a card played later that turn may be selected while it remains in play, subject to the permission’s restrictions and cost. [FAQ timing-of-choosing-powers-for-once-this-turn-repeats] Gift of Strength imposes its printed card-cost eligibility ceiling but requires no additional Energy payment for the Repeat, so an eligible card may be repeated with no Energy left. If the permission requires paying a cost, pay it as instructed. **Powerstorm** requires paying the repeated Power Card's Energy cost again when exercising each Repeat, including its threshold's further permissions; the original play payment does not cover it.

If a Repeat explicitly specifies its location, that location overrides the usual Range and target restrictions (e.g., “Repeat in an adjacent land” must be adjacent to the first target, and cannot be the original land). Otherwise choose any normally legal target, including the original. **Ignore Repeat instructions on a repeated Power:** repeats never chain into more repeat permissions. Other non-Repeat effects of that repeated Power still work.

## Scoring / Victory Conditions

Standard play has a shared win/loss result, not competitive points. The current Terror Level determines what must be absent from the entire island:

| Terror Level | Victory condition |
|---|---|
| I, starting level | No Invaders of any type |
| II | No Towns or Cities; Explorers may remain |
| III | No Cities; Explorers and Towns may remain |
| Victory, after earning the last Fear Card | Win regardless of Invaders; do not wait to resolve that card |

**Defeat routes:** Any Spirit has no Presence left on the island; the active Blight supply empties and its instructions say to lose (including the no-card board supply or final Blighted side); or you must draw an Invader card to Explore but none remains. A Healthy card's pool emptying usually flips it and refills according to its reverse, rather than immediately losing.

**Outcome timing (Living FAQ correction):** Finish the current action and all actions it triggers, including cascades and Dahan counterattack during a single-land Ravage, before checking victory/defeat. This is an **action tree**. Check before the next independent Power, next land's Invader action, or other separate action. If both win and loss are met at this check, the Spirits win a **sacrifice victory**. A final counterattack can therefore win despite losing the last Presence or Blight in that same Ravage. Do not finish Ravages in all other lands first. Earning Fear reveals Terror dividers immediately; use their new conditions at the next outcome check. [CORE p.12; FAQ .bu6oc8v, .9v5kfq7]

## Special Rules & Edge Cases

### Geography and Presence

Each balanced board has eight lands, two each of Jungle, Mountain, Sands and Wetland. Lands are adjacent if they touch by border **or corner**, including across boards. Ocean is not normally a land or in play. Only lands touching a printed Ocean are Coastal; the other outside edges are cliffs, not coast. Other lands are Inland.

A land with your Presence is **your land**. Your **Sacred Site** is a land with **two or more of your Presence**, not one disk each from different Spirits. Three or more still count as only one Sacred Site there. Any number of Spirits and disks can share a land. Explicit special rules such as River's Domain can create Sacred Sites differently, without adding imaginary Presence disks. Presence actions normally affect disks on the island, never track disks, unless stated otherwise.

Destroyed Presence stays beside the island, not back on tracks, and only an explicit destroyed-Presence effect can return it. Adding or moving Presence into a land with existing Blight is legal and does not itself destroy it; **adding Blight**, not the mere presence of Blight, destroys Presence.

### Blight procedure

After setup, take Blight added to the island from the active card/board pool. Whenever one or more Blight is added to a land:

1. Destroy **one Presence from each Spirit** with Presence there, not every disk and not just one disk total.
2. If the land already had any Blight, **cascade**: also add **one** Blight to **one adjacent land** chosen by the players. Resolve its Presence loss and cascade if it was already Blighted. The amount of existing Blight does not multiply the cascade. Cascades can cross boards and remain part of the original action; revisiting a land destroys another disk per Spirit there and continues cascading.

The original land still receives its Blight; cascading does not replace it. If a pool empties during this process, immediately follow the card's instructions, flip/refill as directed and continue unfinished Blight additions/cascades. Add the reverse side's printed number per Spirit from the box; **do not add the initial +1 again**. If an instruction must add more Blight **simultaneously** than remains on Healthy, flip immediately before that addition, carry the remaining old Blight into the newly created pool and fully resolve any immediate flip effects as nested actions, then return to add the required Blight. Do not add the setup +1 again. [FAQ .9v5kfqt]

Blighted card effects apply at their printed timing. Without a card, the printed board pool empties into defeat at the action-tree outcome check.

Removing island Blight returns it to whichever side of the card is currently face-up (or the board pool if no card). A Blighted card never becomes Healthy again, however much Blight is removed. Removing Blight does not restore destroyed Presence.

### Fear procedure

Fear is island-wide. For each Fear, move one marker from the Fear Pool to Generated Fear. When all **4 per Spirit** markers have moved, earn the top Fear Card face-down, return all markers to the pool and continue with any leftover Fear toward the next card. If earning a card exposes a Terror divider, the new Terror Level applies immediately; its victory condition can end the game at the outcome check. Earning the final card reveals Victory; its text never needs resolving.

Fear effects are not Spirit Powers. For “each player,” one player follows all instructions, then the next; choices can affect anywhere allowed by the card without the player's own Presence or Power Range. Generic Presence/Sacred Site requirements can use any Spirit's; wording explicitly counting each Spirit's own sites is individual. The same piece can be chosen or moved by multiple players unless the card requires different choices. Fear effects last for the turn unless they change the board. For outcome timing, a card containing **only “each” instructions** creates one action per “each” (e.g., per player). A card mixing a global instruction with “each” instructions is one overall action with nested actions per “each”; complete that whole action tree before checking outcomes. [FAQ actions-for-fear-and-event-cards-with-both-each-and-non-each-instructions]

If a single action removes a number of Health worth of Invaders scaling with a count, total its allowance and choose a set within that total rather than treating it as fixed-size batches. Separate each-player removal allowances cannot be pooled. [FAQ scaling-removal-of-health-worth-of-invaders]

### Solo and decisions

All eight original-base Spirits, including Ocean and Bringer, are legal in one-Spirit solo and can win solo. With one Spirit, use one board and all per-player amounts for one. “Another Spirit” Powers may target yourself, but you do not receive a bonus conditional on targeting someone other than yourself. One person controlling several Spirits fills several player roles; scale by the number of Spirits/roles, not physical people, and this is not the one-Spirit solo targeting exception.

The Spirit using a Power makes its choices. For other decisions, seek consensus. If agreement fails, decisions within a land belong to the Spirit whose starting board contains it; other disputes go to the game owner/organizer. [CORE p.8]

## FAQ & Rulings

The rulebook directs rules questions to the [official Living FAQ](https://querki.net/u/darker/spirit-island-faq/). The linked [Core game](https://querki.net/raw/darker/spirit-island-faq/Core%2Bgame) and [Setup](https://querki.net/raw/darker/spirit-island-faq/Setup) captures, plus their applicable linked general Range clarifications, supply the following core rulings. They supplement and, for stated corrections, supersede the printed rulebook. This summary applies their original-core portions; references to expansion mechanisms in their examples do not add those modules to the game.

### General and card clarifications

**Title aliases:** FAQ headings calling the cards “Call on Midnight’s Dreams” and “Overseas Trade Seems Safer” refer to the original printed “Call on Midnight’s Dream” and “Overseas Trade Seem Safer,” respectively.

- **Initial Blight:** Exactly one extra Blight total at setup, with or without a card; never repeat this on flips. This and outcome timing above are substantive corrections to older rulebook wording. [Setup: one-more-blight-errata]
- **Range boosts:** Numeric Range boosts do not waive source/target restrictions or the requirement “one of your lands.” A Power still requiring your Sacred Site must have such an origin. General Power Range boosts affect Range values with a **printed Range arrow** in Powers **used by the Spirit receiving the bonus**, including effect-text arrows, subject to all restrictions. They do **not** increase the amount of another Range bonus: another boost cannot turn **Reaching Grasp**'s printed +2 into a larger bonus. They do not extend another Spirit's Powers merely because those Powers target the boosted Spirit. A bonus restricted to Powers does not extend Growth; Power Cards also resolve after normal Growth has finished. A special rule granting extra Range specifically for targeting lands with certain contents changes only the targeting Range box: **Shadows of the Dahan** does not extend effect-text Range arrows. [Core: special-rule-range-bonuses-to-targeting; official FAQ: [Range boosts](https://querki.net/raw/darker/spirit-island-faq/What-do-range-boosts-affect)] [Core: .3y28aqg, .7w4g8nq]
- **Internal Range origins:** A Range arrow inside a land-targeting Power's effects is measured from the **target land**, unless stated otherwise. In a Spirit-targeting Power's effects, measure from the Presence of the Spirit following that instruction (usually the target Spirit), unless the Power says otherwise. Apply the **Power user's** Range bonuses, even if another Spirit supplies the measurement origin. **Blazing Renewal** explicitly uses the Power user's Presence as its origin. Bonuses belonging only to the instructed Spirit do not extend someone else's Power. [Official FAQ: [internal Range](https://querki.net/raw/darker/spirit-island-faq/range-in-text-of-powers-that-target-spirits)]
- **Changing contents:** A single-land Power normally counts contents when used and fixes the effect then. Island-wide effects defined for a class of lands check current contents when relevant (for example, **Dahan on their Guard** counts Dahan when Damage occurs). **Encompassing Ward** follows the target Spirit's current Presence, rather than marking the lands occupied when it was used. An explicitly moving effect is an exception. [Core: do-island-wide-effects-check-land-contents-at-resolution, .7w4ganu]
- **Innates with only self-modification:** A level merely changing a Power's own Speed/Range is insufficient to count as using/targeting it; it must have some instruction with potential to change the game state, even if no actual change occurs. [Core: can-an-innate-power-that-only-changes-speed-or-range-be-targeted]
- **Repeat limits:** **Powerstorm** and **Gift of Strength** repeat Power **Cards**, not Innates. Powerstorm's threshold repeats up to three **different** cards. A Range/Speed change granted by a threshold carries to a Repeat even if that threshold is no longer met; it does not stack the same Range bonus again. [Core: .3y28aex, .7w4g8q9, Repeating-internal-range-boosts]
- **Triggered effects:** “After” effects resolve after the whole originating action, not after each instruction within a Power. A triggered effect cannot trigger itself, even indirectly. Separately repeated instances can trigger one another, but neither can recur within its own chain. **Vengeance of the Dead** uses this timing; its corrected base text grants 3 Fear and, after an effect destroys Towns/Cities/Dahan in target land, deals 1 Damage per such piece destroyed. Its threshold may send that Damage into adjacent lands, while the original target remains the land whose qualifying destruction triggers it. The triggered effect still belongs to the Spirit that used Vengeance: if Bringer used it, its later Damage follows To Dream a Thousand Deaths even when another Spirit's action caused the triggering destruction. [Core: what-items-are-triggered-actions, .bu6oc8x, .7w4gdth, .7w4gdtj]
- **Fear cards:** **Dahan Raid/Dahan Enheartened** deal the listed total Damage, not that Damage to each Invader. Updated Dahan Enheartened II/III: each player chooses a different land, Gathers up to 2 Dahan, then deals 1 Damage (II) or 1 per Dahan (III) if Dahan are present; gathering zero is allowed. **Trade Suffers III** only downgrades in a Coastal land, whether City→Town or Town→Explorer. **Overseas Trade Seem Safer II** cancels an entire Coastal Build that would build a City; it does not substitute a Town. A normal Town Build still happens. **Scapegoats II/III** is one action per land with Towns/Cities: II destroys 1 Explorer per Town and 2 per City; III destroys all Explorers, then 1 Town per City. [Core: scapegoats-action] [Core: .7w4g8rq, .7w4g8ra, .bu6ocpc, .7w4g8qr]
- **Reading choices and Damage:** In **Crops Wither and Fade**, always gain 2 Fear before the City-or-Town replacement choice; “or” only divides adjacent clauses. **Veil the Night's Hunt** must put each point on a different Invader, wasting excess when there are fewer Invaders than Dahan. An unqualified “+N Damage” threshold adds total Damage at that step, not N to every previously damaged piece. Damage boosts still belong to the original Power when it counts what it destroyed. [Core: .9v5kavn, .7w4g8qc, .7w4g8p0, is-boosted-damage-from-the-same-source]
- **Core Power specifics:** **Song of Sanctity** is Slow, despite missing cost-circle ink in some printings. Its Explorer condition selects its effect: with Explorers, Push all of them; otherwise remove 1 Blight. This is not a free choice to remove Blight while Explorers remain. **Dissolve the Bonds of Kinship** line 3 Pushes all Explorers **to as many different lands as possible**, using every available legal destination up to the number of Explorers; its Invaders damaging each other do so simultaneously, **before** the Explorer Push as the threshold specifies. **Poisoned Land**'s threshold counts the Blight that its own earlier instruction added. **Tsunami**'s threshold adds Fear for each other Coastal land it affects. [Core: .7w4g9dg, .9v5kc31, .7w4ganc, .7w4g8q3, .7w4g8pc]
- **Dread Apparitions Fear sources:** While its effect is active, Power-generated Fear in its target land adds **Defend 1 per Fear**. Fear from **To Dream a Thousand Deaths** counts; normal Fear for destroying Towns/Cities does not, even when a Power caused the destruction. [Original core card text]
- **Location-dependent effects:** **Wrap in Wings of Sunlight** resolves its threshold’s **First, Gather up to 3 Dahan** before its base Move; those gathered Dahan may then move and allow the destination’s Defend. It grants no Defend if zero Dahan move: there is no moved-to land. Moving Dahan to their original land can count as Move if the Power permits. **Blazing Renewal** permits its target Spirit to add its destroyed Presence directly to one legal island land, not to tracks. Its printed “adds 2” is mandatory to the extent possible, using the Power user's Presence for its internal Range; it cannot deal threshold Damage with no destroyed Presence restored: “that land” refers to where it adds Presence. **The Trees and Stones Speak of War** deals 1 Damage per Dahan and grants Defend 2 per Dahan in its target land. Its threshold moves Defend 2 with each Dahan it Pushes, leaving the remaining Defend in the target. Only this Power's Defend 2 per pushed Dahan travels; separate Defend from another effect, such as Words of Warning, stays in its original land. A later Power moving those Dahan does not carry that Defend. [Core: .9v5kfqd, .9v5kfr0, .bu6oc8n, .bu6ocxq]
- **Protection:** **Infinite Vitality** replaces an attempted Blight addition by leaving that Blight in the active Blight Card/board pool. That prevented addition neither destroys Presence nor cascades. Its threshold protects Dahan from Damage/Destroy, not Remove/Replace. Its Blight protection does not make the land an illegal cascade destination. [Core: .bu6ocxx, .7w4g8q2]
- **Entwined Power:** Shared Presence applies only to targeting (Range/source/target boxes), not to text effects or counting “your lands.” One disk each can count as a Sacred Site for their Power targeting, but is not a real shared site for Fear Cards or other effects. River's special Wetland-site rule remains River's own. For its base card gains, the **target Spirit** first makes the normal draw-four/keep-one gain; the **using Spirit** then gains one of those same three unkept cards, without drawing another four. In solo, both gains come from that single four-card draw. The threshold also lets the using and target Spirits each independently gift the other one Power Card **from hand**, optionally; cards in play or personal discard cannot be gifted by it. The threshold gives 6 Energy in solo; each Major gained still needs a separate Forget. If a transferred Unique references a special effect defined by a Spirit's rule, such as Drown, that definition still applies; skip instructions requiring a personal track you do not have. [Core: .3y28auq, .3y28aeu, .7w4g9jw, .7w4gee7, .7w4g8p9]
- **Spirit-targeting Powers:** When the target Spirit selects “one of their lands,” it chooses the land and does as much there as possible, even if another of its lands would permit more instructions. Thus **Mantle of Dread** may Push an Explorer from a land without a Town, and **Tidal Boon** may Push Dahan from a land without a Town. Tidal Boon's “if Dahan are pushed” clause applies only to that Power's own Push. When Ocean uses Tidal Boon, Ocean may move Dahan pushed to its Ocean by that use to **any Coastal land**, including a nonadjacent one, instead of Drowning them. Ocean chooses; this alternative is optional. [Original core card text] Spirit-targeting Fear without a specified land has no particular land for **Dread Apparitions**. ### Core Spirit exceptions and interactions

Use each panel's full rules and Innates; these clarifications do not replace their printed effects.

- **Lightning's Swift Strike:** During Fast, **Swiftness of Lightning** can make one Power Fast per current Air Element; Innates count as Powers too. Recheck current Elements each time; previous uses count against the current allowance. It grants one use at Fast, not an extra use in Slow. **Harbingers of the Lightning** generates its Fear in the target land. - **River Surges in Sunlight:** **River's Domain** makes its Wetland Presence a Sacred Site without increasing its disk count. When moving/affecting “a Sacred Site,” River may choose 1 or 2 actual Presence there as applicable. [Core: .9v5kfd7, .7w4gebd]
- **Shadows Flicker Like Flame:** Pay **Shadows of the Dahan**'s 1 Energy when using the Power, to ignore numeric Range to a land with Dahan. Source restrictions (such as needing a Sacred Site) and other target requirements remain. The payment's modification carries to a Repeat without paying that surcharge again; an explicit Repeat location still overrides normal targeting. For **Darkness Swallows the Unwary**, choosing lower Elements cannot select the second level without the first when both require the same Elements; if destroying Explorers under that level, first Gather an Explorer if able. [Core: .7w4g8pt, .7w4g8nq, .7w4g8r6, .bu6od0c]
- **A Spread of Rampant Green:** **Choke the Land with Green** is optional (some printings omit “may”). Each use skips just that Ravage or Build action and costs one of Green's Presence in its Sacred Site there. A later Build needs a fresh decision and another disk: starting with three, Green may skip the Ravage, retain a two-disk Sacred Site, then skip the Build and end with one disk. **Steady Regeneration**'s Healthy/Blighted test refers to the Blight Card, not Blight in the destination land. When adding Presence via Growth, Green may use destroyed disks instead of track disks even while disks remain on tracks: this is free while Healthy, or costs 1 Energy per destroyed disk while Blighted. This permission applies only to Growth; an ordinary Power needs its own explicit destroyed-Presence permission. Green's separate **Always** Range-2 Jungle/Wetland placement is additional to its one chosen Growth group, including when it chooses Reclaim/gain a Power. It still chooses only one of the optional groups. Growth instructions from multiple options can be ordered together, so a gained Power may inform the mandatory placement. - **Thunderspeaker:** Presence may accompany each moved Dahan, including generic Move as well as Gather/Push; two Dahan can carry two Presence. **Sworn to Victory** triggers after an entire single-land Ravage for each Dahan destroyed there: destroy one of Thunderspeaker's Presence within Range 1 per Dahan, even if no Blight was added. Finish the Ravage, cascades and counterattack before this triggered loss. [Core: .bu6oc6l, .bu6oc73, .bu6ofsi]
- **Ocean's Hungry Grasp:** On a board with any of Ocean's Presence, the Ocean is a Coastal Wetland for **Powers and Blight**, not for normal Growth, Invader actions or Fear Cards. Powers and Special Rules can measure Range through it as a land. [Core: range-through-ocean] Powers can target it and move Invaders/Dahan into it; Blight can cascade into it. Other Spirits' Presence may end up there via a legal Power, including Thunderspeaker accompanying moved Dahan. You can measure Growth placement Range **from existing Ocean Presence**, although normal Growth cannot add to the Ocean just by treating it as a Coastal Wetland. The Growth group explicitly adding two Presence in **any Ocean** can place them in any board's Ocean, even a board where Ocean has no Presence, without a numeric Range requirement. Its other group's Range-1 **Coastal land** placement does not permit placing in the Ocean. Other placement must respect Ocean's land-type restriction unless expressly ignored (e.g., **Indomitable Claim** for Inland Presence). - **Drowning:** Ocean's rule Drowns Invaders/Dahan **after** they move into the Ocean on a board with Ocean's Presence; Ocean need not have a disk in the Ocean itself. Drown destroys: Town/City Fear happens immediately, and drowned Invaders go on Ocean's panel. At any time, trade stored Invaders: **X printed Health worth of drowned Invaders for 1 Energy**, X = number of Spirits; ignore Health modifiers and make change. They need not be exchanged when Drowned; Ocean may exchange them between actions during a later Spirit Phase, including before paying for cards. Dahan drowning gives no Energy. Ordinary destruction, including thematically watery Powers, is not Drowning unless specified. If Ocean loses all Presence on that board mid-action, Ocean In Play lasts through the action tree; afterward non-human pieces left there remain physically but out of play until it returns. Humans cannot stay there between action trees: if Drowning fails, Push survivors to non-Ocean lands within that action. This is an actual Push and can trigger applicable movement effects, unlike later cleanup. Failed Drowning yields no Energy. Remaining illegal humans are cleaned up to the nearest legal land afterward. Cleanup is not an action, move, removal or destruction and triggers nothing, and occurs before the outcome check. If no legal land exists return humans to supply as cleanup. [Core: .7w4g8qy, .9v5kbka, .7w4g8pb, .9v5ka9g, .7w4g8r4, .9v5kfqb, .9v5kfqs, .7w4gdtl]
- **Bringer of Dreams and Nightmares:** Its Powers' explicit Damage/Destroy cannot actually harm Invaders/Dahan or destroy anything except its own Presence. An explicit instruction to Damage or Destroy Dahan does nothing to those Dahan; skip that instruction and resolve the rest of the Power normally. For each Power use, evaluate what it would destroy if resolved normally: generate **0 Fear per Explorer, 2 per Town, 5 per City**, and Push the would-be-destroyed Explorers/Towns. No real Damage is marked, so different Powers or a Repeat cannot combine their hypothetical Damage; multiple instructions **within one Power** can. Existing real Damage from others helps its hypothetical destruction and remains unchanged afterward. Within a single Power use, a given Invader can be pretend-destroyed only once. Repeats can “destroy” the same piece again for Fear. It does not get bonuses conditional on **actual** destruction (e.g., Mists of Oblivion or Vengeance of the Dead) from these pretend destructions. Bringer can Remove/Replace, add real Blight (destroying Presence normally), modify Invader actions or cause actual Ravage. ## Player Reference

**Order:** Growth → Energy → play/pay cards → Fast Powers → Blighted instructions → Fear → Ravage → Build → Explore → advance cards → Slow Powers → Time Passes.

**Ravage:** 1/2/3 Damage per Explorer/Town/City; Defend protects land and Dahan separately; 2+ land Damage adds one Blight; surviving Dahan deal 2 each. **Build:** more Towns than Cities → City, otherwise Town; no Invaders → none. **Explore:** matching land with/adjacent to Town/City, or Coastal → one Explorer.

**Powers:** Pay on play, Elements now, effects at speed, target when used; one target/turn/use unless explicit exception. Elements are not spent. **Fear:** destroy Town +1, City +2; full pool earns a card and carries excess. **Blight:** take from active pool, lose one Presence per Spirit, cascade one adjacent land if already Blighted. **Outcome:** finish the action tree, then check current Terror victory and all loss routes before the next independent action.
