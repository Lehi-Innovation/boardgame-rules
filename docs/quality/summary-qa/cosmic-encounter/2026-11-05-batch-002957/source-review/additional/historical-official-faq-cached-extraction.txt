L4: Errata and Clarifications
L5: Losing Alien Powers
L6: A player loses his or her alien power whenever he or
L7: she has fewer than three home colonies (two in a four
L8: planet game) rather than basing this on the number of
L9: home colonies a player has lost.
L10: Macron Alien Power
L11: Macron should read "You have the power of Mass.
L12: When you are the offense, use this power before
L13: launching in an encounter. When you are an ally,
L14: use this power after allies are invited. If you are the
L15: offense or an ally, you may only send one ship into the
L16: encounter.
L17: As a main player or an ally, use this power after cards
L18: are revealed. Each of your ships adds 4 to your side's
L19: total in the encounter instead of 1.
L20: When collecting compensation or rewards, each of your
L21: ships is worth two ships."
L22: This clarifies that Macron may be zapped either when
L23: sending ships into the encounter (to allow him to send
L24: up to 4 ships worth 1 each) or after cards are revealed
L25: (in which case he's just out of luck).
L26: Spiff Alien Power
L27: Spiff should read “...if both players revealed attack
L28: cards and you lose the encounter by 10 or more” rather
L29: than “...if both players revealed attack cards and your
L30: total was 10 or more less than the defense’s”. This
L31: allows Spiff to still use his power in encounters where
L32: the low total wins, as it originally did.
L33: Warrior Alien Power
L34: Warrior’s first paragraph should read “You have the
L35: power of Mastery. After an encounter in which you
L36: were a main player, add one token to this sheet if you
L37: won that encounter (or made a deal during it) or two
L38: tokens if you lost that encounter (or failed to make
L39: a deal during it). In either case, add one extra token
L40: if playing with four planets per player.” This allows
L41: Warrior to collect tokens even if his power was zapped
L42: during the encounter.

L68: Q: What happens if a player needs to draw a new hand
L69: and both the cosmic deck and discard pile are empty?
L70: A: Cosmic Quake! Every player discards his or her
L71: hand, and the discard pile is shuffled to make a new
L72: deck, then 8 cards are dealt to each player.
L73: Q: When should alert levels on aliens be mixed?
L74: A: It is recommended that players start with green aliens
L75: only with no flares when first playing, then add flares.
L76: Play a couple of games, then introduce yellows to the
L77: green alert. Play a few more games with those, then add
L78: the red level aliens. The alert level is not how powerful
L79: they are, but how experienced your group should be to
L80: include them in the game.

L98: A: No.
L99: Q: After you play a flare, at what point does it re-enter
L100: your hand?
L101: A: Immediately after resolving its effect. So, for
L102: instance, when playing the Oracle Wild flare, you
L103: play the flare faceup in front of you, mix your hands
L104: together, get your new hand at random from the mixed
L105: cards, and then return the Oracle flare to your hand
L106: (since its effect is now finished).

L108: planets?
L109: A: Your ships exit the warp, then any ships that have
L110: no colony to return to are returned to the warp. If you
L111: are the offense, you may place up to four of your ships
L112: in the hyperspace gate before the others are lost to the
L113: warp.

L117: a player’s turn and precedes that player’s first regroup
L118: phase. The regroup phase occurs at the start of each of
L119: a player’s encounters, so potentially it can happen twice
L120: per turn.

L171: Q: If a power such as Calculator, Mirror, or Tripler
L172: affects a card, does a Morph copy the card's value
L173: before or after the modification?
L174: A: The Morph copies whatever value is initially
L175: revealed. If that value is later modified, the Morph’s
L176: value does not change with it. However, the Morph’s
L177: value may be affected by modifications separately.
L178: For instance, if the Tripler revealed a Morph and his
L179: opponent revealed a 12, the Morph would become a 12
L180: and then get divided to a 4 by Tripler’s power. On the
L181: other hand, if Tripler revealed the 12 and his opponent
L182: revealed the Morph, the Morph would become a 12,
L183: but would not get divided to a 4 just because the card it
L184: morphed into did.
