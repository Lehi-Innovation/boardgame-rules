## Overview

This summary covers the original **Fantasy Flight English 2008 CE01** game for 3–5 players, including its fifty original aliens, normal flare system and applicable official FAQ rulings. Optional Technology, Four Planets, Hidden Powers, Rotating Powers and Freewheeling Flares are excluded, as are Anniversary bonus content and all expansions. Read an individual card's printed effect and timing when playing it; the flare rules below explain the shared system rather than reproduce every flare card.

Each player leads an alien trying to establish five **foreign colonies**. A colony is one or more ships of one color on one planet; extra ships on that planet do not make extra colonies. Several colors can coexist there, each with its own colony. Home colonies are in your own system; foreign colonies are outside it. Five foreign planets can all be in one other system. Alien powers override conflicting general rules.

## Components

The original box contains 1 warp, 5 colony markers, 1 hyperspace gate, 25 planets (5 per color), 100 ships (20 per color), **50 alien sheets**, 20 destiny cards, 72 cosmic cards (encounters, reinforcements and artifacts), 50 flare cards plus an alternate classic Filch flare, 20 Tech cards, 42 cosmic tokens and 7 Grudge tokens. The Genesis planet, Lunar Cannon token and Prometheus token support the excluded Technology variant. Use only one version of Filch's flare in a game. There are no Cosmic Combo cards or 51st alien in this edition's setup.

## Setup

1. Put the warp in the center. Each player takes five planets of one color, places four of their twenty ships on each, and places their colony marker beside the warp's 0 space.
2. Remove destiny cards naming unused player colors; shuffle the rest, including wilds and specials.
3. Shuffle the flares, deal two to each player, and give them the matching alien sheets. Each secretly chooses one alien, initially placing it facedown, and returns the other sheet to the box. This is the standard selection procedure; Hidden Powers is a separate excluded variant. Reveal chosen powers for ordinary play. Green/yellow/red indicate recommended experience, not strength. Beginners may start with green aliens without flares.
4. Add **both flares dealt to every player**, including rejected aliens' flares, to the cosmic deck. Add random extras if needed to reach ten flares total; shuffle. Deal eight-card hands. Keep card faces private unless an effect allows showing them.
5. Draw destiny until a player color appears; that player starts. Shuffle the drawn card back into the destiny deck. Play passes left thereafter.

Apply printed alien Game Setup instructions as well. If chosen aliens have a printed incompatibility, remove the alien bearing the restriction, keeping the unrestricted alien. Its player may use their originally rejected alien or draw a replacement unused flare and take that alien, redrawing conflicts. Gambler's printed restriction is **Do not use with Sorcerer**; using them together is an optional FAQ-permitted relaxation, outside this standard setup.

## Turn Structure

The active player is the **offense**. Offense and defense are the **main players**. A turn normally has one encounter and, optionally, a second after a successful first encounter. Winning or making a successful deal is successful; making a deal is **not a win** for an effect that only says win. A second encounter requires at least one encounter card already in the offense's hand; you cannot start it hoping to obtain one before Planning. After the second encounter, or an unsuccessful first encounter, the turn passes left. Machine provides an exception.

### Start Turn and hand replacement

Start Turn occurs only before the first encounter of your turn, before Regroup effects. If offense has no encounter cards, reveal and discard the remaining hand, then draw eight new cards. You may first play legally playable non-encounter cards but are not required to play them. Hate draws a needed starting hand before using its power.

At Planning, a defense lacking encounter cards reveals/discards the remainder and draws eight cards, repeating until it has an encounter card. An offense that runs out later in its turn **when it needs to play an encounter card** ends its turn instead of drawing a replacement: offense and all allies return committed ships to any of their own colonies. The normal no-colony relocation rule applies. There is no automatic draw at the end of an encounter or turn, or merely for running out of cards. Alien and card effects can grant different draws.

### 1. Regroup

Offense must retrieve one of its ships from the warp if available, placing it on any existing home or foreign colony. The original rulebook puts this retrieved ship directly in the gate if offense has no colonies. The FAQ adds that a player who has no colonies or ships on their turn may instead place the Regroup ship on any home planet. Ordinary relocation with no colony to receive ships sends them to the warp; the stated Regroup exceptions take precedence.

### 2. Destiny

Draw destiny to determine the defense and target system. **When only one card remains, do not draw it**: shuffle it with the destiny discards, then draw from the new deck.

- Another player's color: encounter that player in their home system.
- Your own color: redraw, or target one chosen foreign colony in your home system, making its owner the defense. Other colonies there are bystanders. You can instead aim at a home planet with **no ships of any color**, moving up to four ships from other colonies there to reclaim it automatically. This counts as a win and successful encounter, proceeds only through Launch, and can qualify you for a second encounter.
- Wild: choose another player and encounter them in their home system.
- Special: follow its conditions, defense and location. For color-dependent effects such as Shadow, treat it as the designated defense's color.

Destiny hazard symbols have no effect in this base game. Ordinarily you cannot attack a player's foreign colony in a third player's system: a color/wild/special targets its defense in that player's home system, while your own color permits driving out a foreign colony in your own system. If you already have colonies on **all planets in the defensive system**, draw destiny again.

### 3. Launch

Aim the gate at one planet in the indicated system; put one to four of your ships in it, drawn from any combination of your existing home and foreign colonies. You may strip a colony, but removing its last ship immediately eliminates it. Ships returning from an encounter or warp cannot return to that now-empty location as though it remained your colony.

Defense cannot add or subtract ships on the target. The owner of a home system defends their planet even with zero ships there. Other colors' colonies are **bystanders**: they add nothing to the defense and the encounter result does not harm them. When driving out a foreign colony, target only that one color's colony; all other colors are bystanders.

If you cannot put any ship in the gate or the last offensive ship is removed, continue the encounter. Winning alone does not give offense a colony without a ship to land, unless a specific effect allows it.

### 4. Alliance

Offense names invited allies first; they wait to answer. Defense then invites allies, possibly including the same players. Neither main player may invite the other. Starting with the player left of offense and proceeding clockwise, each other player accepts one invitation or declines all, then commits ships before the next player decides. You may join only one side or neither; normally an invitation is required.

Each ally contributes one to four ships from any of their colonies. Offensive allies place them on the gate; defensive allies place them beside the target planet, **not on it**. The FAQ nevertheless considers defensive allies in the gate for effects referring to that location. Parasite changes invitation eligibility.

### 5. Planning

Each main player selects an **encounter card**—attack, negotiate or Morph—and places it facedown. Allies do not play encounter cards. Resolve applicable powers at their stated before/after-selection timing; a phase label does not erase that timing. Defense replaces an encounter-less hand as described above. Offense cannot normally replace its hand now.

### 6. Reveal

Reveal the encounter cards simultaneously, subject to special powers. Resolve the encounter-card pairing below, applicable powers, and the reinforcement response window. Distinguish effects requiring an originally **revealed attack** from later conversion of a negotiate into an attack: such conversion does not retroactively trigger Cudgel.

### 7. Resolution

Carry out the relevant outcome, compensation and other effects. Discard revealed encounter cards after resolving the encounter, unless an effect changes their destination. Check ordinary victory at the very end of Resolution. Then have an eligible optional second encounter or pass left.

## Actions

### Attack against attack

Each side's total is its main player's attack value plus all ships on its side: offense and offensive allies in the gate; defense on the target plus defensive allies beside it. Higher total wins; **defense wins ties**. Powers and played cards may change this calculation or the winner.

- **Offense wins:** all offense and offensive ally ships land on the target, establishing or reinforcing each color's colony. Defense and defensive ally ships go to warp. Bystanders survive. Advance a colony marker only for a newly established foreign colony, not reinforcement of an existing colony.
- **Defense wins:** defending and bystander colonies stay. Offense and offensive allies go to warp. Defensive allies return their committed ships to any existing own colonies, even different origins; helping defend does not grant a colony on the defended planet. Each defensive ally also receives **one defender reward per ship contributed**. For each reward, draw one cosmic-deck card or retrieve one own warp ship to any own colony; combine these choices freely.

### Attack against negotiate: compensation

The negotiate player automatically loses; resolve the winner's ship destinations normally. The losing main player randomly takes one card from the opponent's hand for each of **their own ships actually lost to the warp**. Exclude allied losses: allies receive no compensation. If the opponent lacks sufficient cards, take the entire hand. Cards are taken without seeing their faces. Compensation is stolen hand cards; defender rewards are deck draws and/or warp retrieval.

No compensation for ships prevented from reaching the warp (Zombie/Observer) or removed by Void. Healer returning ships **after** they enter the warp does not remove compensation eligibility. Special winners such as Human/Pacifist can change who wins; compensation still requires the relevant attack-versus-negotiate condition.

### Negotiate against negotiate: making a deal

Both sides' allies return ships to their own colonies, gain nothing and cannot participate in the deal. The main players have **one minute**. They may transfer cards from their hands and/or each grant the other **one colony on a planet where the giver already has a colony**, including a foreign colony. The recipient can use any own ships not in the warp. Existing ships coexist; the giver need not vacate, and vacating would eliminate the colony being offered. Return any remaining gate ships to offense's colonies.

A successful deal must actually transfer at least one card or colony; it may be one-sided. You cannot agree to do nothing. Tangible terms are restricted to permitted cards/colonies and specific power/card exceptions such as Zombie's liberation; future promises are unenforceable. Card terms may be explicit or vague but must be truthful and honored (if you promise your highest attack, give it). If time expires without agreement, **each main player loses three ships of their choice to the warp**, and offense cannot take an ordinary second encounter. Successful deals qualify for an ordinary second encounter but do not trigger win-only powers.

### Morph

The single base Morph becomes an exact copy of the opponent's initially revealed encounter card: attack copies its initial value, negotiate becomes negotiate. Resolve that pairing normally. Subsequent modifications of the opponent's card do not automatically carry over to Morph; Morph can be modified separately. For example, Tripler revealing Morph against attack12 copies12 and divides its own copy to4; Tripler revealing12 against Morph changes its own12 to4, while opponent's Morph remains12. Morph returns to its normal identity after the encounter. If two Morphs could be played against each other, both sides would lose all involved ships to warp; there is only one in this base deck.

For encounter resolution (card types/values, totals, winner/loser, deals, compensation/rewards and involved ship disposition), use current modified card type/value. Other effects normally refer to original printed identity. Morph's printed identity remains Morph. A later card conversion does not change what card was originally revealed for a reveal-trigger condition.

### Artifacts and reinforcements

Artifacts are not encounter cards. Follow each artifact's printed effect, permitted role and timing; normally discard after play. **Cosmic Zap** cancels a power use, including your own, and prevents further uses for that encounter. Cards that cancel cards follow their own printed instructions.

Reinforcements are not encounter cards. During Reveal, after encounter cards are revealed, either main player and any ally may play them to add to **either side's total**, including the opposing side. Players may respond with further reinforcements until all pass; then resolve using the new totals. Bystanders cannot play them merely because they are present on the planet.

**Official FAQ wording conflict:** FAQ p3 explicitly permits involved players to play reinforcements on either side regardless of the revealed card types; p4 also answers “Can you play a reinforcement card on a negotiate?” with “No.” The ordinary attack-total procedure above is unambiguous. The supplied FAQ does not explain whether its p4 answer means a reinforcement cannot change the negotiate card itself or contradicts its p3 permission. These statements should be checked against a publisher clarification before ruling on that disputed non-attack case.

### Flare system

Flares are not encounter cards. Each has printed Wild and Super effects, usage roles and timing. Normally use Wild; if it is your own alien's flare and your power is available, **you must use Super**, not choose Wild. If your power is lost or zapped, you can use only Wild until your power returns. Merely acquiring someone else's alien sheet does not itself grant that alien's Super according to the FAQ.

Return a flare to hand **immediately after its effect finishes**, rather than discard it, unless its effect states otherwise. A player may play **at most one flare per encounter**, and an individual flare can be used **only once per encounter even if it changes hands**. A Card Zap targeting the flare counts against the player's limit. Prevention of play differs from cancellation after play: see Anti-Matter's FAQ exception. If a Super enables a power use, that power can still be Cosmic Zapped even outside its usual role/phase. The flare remains in hand rather than being discarded or forced to Wild; Wild becomes available while the power is zapped. When your own Wild would otherwise be handed to your alien, keep it after resolution.

## Scoring / Victory Conditions

Track **current** foreign colonies, decreasing the marker when one is lost. At the **very end of Resolution**, a player with five foreign colonies wins; multiple players meeting the condition together share victory. Other effects still have until that point to prevent ordinary victory. An effect explicitly saying immediately win ends the game when resolved, unless zapped or specifically interrupted. Masochist and Tick-Tock have printed alternate win conditions; Tick-Tock can share a deal-created win as described in its entry.

## Special Rules & Edge Cases

### Colonies, relocation and losses

Removing your last ship from a planet immediately loses that colony. Returning ships must go to **existing** colonies, not abandoned planets; if no colony can receive a required relocation, those ships go to warp unless an explicit exception applies. You still defend an empty home planet with zero ships. Extra ship losses normally let the losing player choose their ships, unless the effect specifies someone else. A requirement to lose ships outside the encounter cannot be paid with encounter ships; if you have no eligible ships, lose none. Likewise you cannot activate a sacrifice requiring an outside ship by substituting an inside ship. Forced losses/sacrifices normally choose ships you control, not ships already in warp, removed or captured.

“Losing ships” are encounter participants that should suffer defeat's loss; ships “lost to warp” actually reach it. “Sent” ships can be lost through a separate effect, including voluntary sends, without being encounter-losing ships. An interruption preventing warp arrival means no actual warp loss. These distinctions matter for compensation, Healer, Observer, Void and Zombie.

### Power loss, zapping and timing

With fewer than **three home colonies**, immediately turn your alien sheet facedown and lose its power; you cannot use it one last time. Restore it faceup when you again occupy at least three home planets. Masochist expressly keeps its power with fewer colonies. Losing a power also removes that alien identity for power-dependent effects and access to its Super.

A power may be Cosmic Zapped **only when being used**, not preemptively. The bold-italic **use** on an alien sheet identifies its cancellable use. Cancel that effect and prevent further uses until the encounter ends, including when zapped during Start Turn. Clauses that do not require use—such as Warrior adding experience, Zombie liberating ships in a deal, or placing a Citadel—are not themselves zappable uses. Zapping a use does not disable such clauses.

You may wait to see a power's immediate effect (Chosen's drawn cards, Hate's selected discard, Philanthropist's offered card), but the zap window is short, in that same phase. You cannot wait until later reinforcements change the outcome and then undo an earlier power, or zap after moving to the next phase.

Resolve actual simultaneous contradictory effects in order: **offense; defense; other players clockwise starting left of offense**. First honor more specific before/after timing within a phase. Finish one effect before starting another, except a Zap or an effect expressly allowing interruption. An optional effect's owner may change their mind when another effect or timing order changes the game state. Alien powers are normally usable once per relevant context, unless their text says otherwise.

Cards may be legally played before compensation starts, but compensation does not permit arbitrary interruptions. Once an effect targets a hand and begins resolving, only a Zap or specific interrupt can intervene. You cannot wait for Hacker to select you and then dump reinforcements: the Reveal window has already passed.

### Card information, discards and exhaustion

Hand size is public; card faces are private unless an effect says otherwise. You may make claims or lie about your hand during ordinary public table talk, but cannot show it; Mind has an additional secrecy restriction and deal terms must be honored. Table talk is public unless an effect permits secrecy. Discards are faceup and public, including all cards discarded together. When discard order matters, discard non-encounter cards first in alphabetical/numerical order, then encounter cards starting with offense clockwise and ending with defense, whose encounter card is on top.

Base card types are **attack, negotiate, Morph, reinforcement, flare and artifact**; encounter is a category, not one card type. Card-type costs such as Hate or Plague distinguish those six. No expansion card types are in scope.

If the cosmic deck runs out, reshuffle available discards. If a needed draw has no deck or discards, a **Cosmic Quake** makes everyone discard their hands; shuffle and deal eight to each player. If still insufficient, **Cosmic Aftershock** gathers all cosmic cards, including Miser's hoard and other set-aside cards, shuffles them, and replenishes Game Setup card holdings as at setup (including Miser's hoard). This is a needed-draw exhaustion rule, not an optional hand refresh.

### Original fifty alien reference

The entries below summarize the original printed sheets, with applicable base-only FAQ corrections. Roles, phase and optional/mandatory status matter. A non-use clause is identified where its timing or zappability differs from the main use. Read these together with the shared power/colony rules above.


#### Amoeba

**Ooze — Main player only; optional; Planning.**

- After encounter cards are selected but before reveal, if you have at least one ship in encounter, may increase or decrease your ships there.
- May remove some or all of your ships to your colonies, or add any number (even above normal maximum four) from any of your colonies.
- If you win but have no ships left in the encounter, cannot receive a colony.


#### Anti-Matter

**Negation — Main player only; mandatory; Reveal.**

- After both main players reveal attack cards, use power to make lower total win.
- When used, your ships and any offensive and defensive allies' ships are subtracted from the appropriate side's card.
- Opponent's total is otherwise figured normally.


#### Barbarian

**Loot and Pillage — Offense only; mandatory; Resolution.**

- After winning an encounter, before compensation (if any) collected, use power to loot opponent's cards.
- Take and look at opponent's hand. For each of your ships in encounter, may choose one card from that hand and add it to own.
- Afterwards discard remainder of opponent's hand.


#### Calculator

**equalize — Main player only; optional; Planning.**

- After cards selected but before reveal, may declare an equalize.
- If both revealed cards are attack cards, higher card's value is reduced by lower card's value; lower card retains its value.
- Printed example: 15 versus 8 becomes 7 versus 8. Conclude encounter normally.


#### Chosen

**Divine Intervention — Main player only; optional; Reveal.**

- After revealing encounter cards, may pray for divine intervention once per encounter: draw three cards from deck.
- If none are encounter cards discard them, no further effect. If any are encounter cards may choose one to replace own revealed encounter card (discard replaced card).
- If own revealed card and chosen replacement are both attacks, may instead add chosen card value to revealed attack value, or replace as normal.
- Discard all other drawn cards; resolve encounter with new card or card value.


#### Citadel

**Fortification — As any player; optional; Planning.**

- During each player's turn after destiny drawn, may play an attack card from hand faceup next to any planet in any system as a citadel.
- If planet with one or more citadels attacked, after encounter cards selected but before reveal may use power to activate ALL citadels on that planet.
- Add combined value of activated citadels to defense total.
- If activated and defense loses, discard citadels. If defense wins or citadels not activated, they stay.


#### Clone

**replicate — Main player only; optional; Resolution.**

- After encounter resolved and any compensation claimed, may use power to return own encounter card to hand instead of discarding.


#### Cudgel

**Smash — Main player only; mandatory; Resolution.**

- When you win an encounter in which you revealed an attack card, use power to force opponent to lose extra ships of opponent's choice equal to number of your ships in encounter, in addition to normal losses.


#### Dictator

**Command — Not offense; mandatory; Destiny.**

- When not offense, before destiny chosen, use power to take destiny deck, look through it and choose any card; played as though offense had drawn it.
- On own turn, or any time zapped, remaining destiny cards shuffled and one dealt randomly.


#### Fido

**Fetch — As any player; optional; Resolution.**

- After encounter cards discarded at end of encounter, may retrieve one of those discarded cards and offer to another player.
- If refused, may keep it. If accepted, other player keeps it, then you may either retrieve one ship from warp or draw one card from deck.


#### Filch

**Theft — Main player only; optional; Resolution.**

- After encounter cards discarded at end of encounter, may retrieve opponent's card from discard pile and add to own hand.


#### Fodder

**Overwhelm — Main player only; optional; Reveal.**

- After both main players reveal attack cards, may discard any or all attack cards in hand that are BOTH higher than own played attack and lower than opponent's played attack; add their values to own total.


#### Gambler

**Bluff — Main player only; optional; Reveal.**

- After opponent reveals encounter card may keep own facedown, stating what it is; may lie.
- If opponent does not challenge, conclude encounter as if statement true; place own encounter card facedown on bottom of deck instead of discarding.
- If challenged reveal own card. If you lied, lose as many ships to warp as you had in encounter. If truth, opponent loses as many ships as opponent had in encounter.
- Those lost ships MAY NOT be ships involved in encounter. Afterwards conclude normally using revealed cards.
- Restriction: Do not use with Sorcerer.


#### Grudge

**Revenge — Main player only; mandatory; Alliance.**

- After alliances formed, give grudge token to each player invited by you who did not ally with you.
- If you win or make deal, each grudge-token player discards it and loses one ship of their choice to warp.
- If you lose or fail deal, each grudge-token player discards it and loses four ships of their choice to warp.
- Lost ships cannot include ships used to ally with the other side.


#### Hacker

**Hack — Main player only; optional; Resolution.**

- When collecting compensation may choose player to collect from, whether or not opponent. Look through that player's hand and choose desired compensation cards.
- When opponent collects compensation from you, may select cards opponent gets.


#### Hate

**Rage — Offense only; mandatory; Start Turn.**

- At start of own turn use power; first choose and discard a card from hand.
- Every other player must choose either discard same card type (attack, negotiate, artifact, etc.) or lose three ships of YOUR choice to warp.
- If discarded card attack, opponents must discard attack of equal or higher value. Opponent with no cards of discarded type must lose ships instead.
- If after using power you have no encounter cards in hand, draw new hand.


#### Healer

**Heal — As any player; optional; Start Turn, Regroup, Destiny, Launch, Alliance, Planning, Reveal, Resolution.**

- When ANOTHER player loses ships to warp or has ships removed from game, may return ALL ships just lost to that player and earn one deck card.
- Healing does not prevent compensation. Healed player replaces ships on any of their colonies.
- During encounter may heal several players, drawing one card for each.


#### Human

**Humanity — Main player or ally only; mandatory; Reveal.**

- After encounter cards revealed use power to add 4 to own side's total.
- If power zapped, own side automatically wins encounter.


#### Kamikaze

**Sacrifice — Main player only; optional; Planning.**

- Before encounter cards selected may send up to four own ships from any own colonies to warp. Draw two deck cards for every ship sent.
- If power zapped, sacrificed ships are not lost to warp.


#### Loser

**Upset — Main player only; optional; Planning.**

- Before encounter cards selected may declare upset. Once declared both main players must play attack cards if possible.
- After reveal winning side loses and losing side wins.
- Occurs after ALL other game effects resolved, including Human's power being zapped.


#### Machine

**Continuity — Offense only; optional; Resolution.**

- Own turn is not limited to two encounters.
- After completing an encounter, successful or not, may have another encounter as long as at least one encounter card remains in own hand.


#### Macron

**Mass — Main player or ally only; mandatory; Launch, Alliance.**

- As offense use before launching ships. As defense or ally use after allies invited.
- As offense or ally may send only ONE ship into encounter.
- Each own ship adds 4 instead of 1 to own side's total.
- When collecting compensation or rewards, each own ship worth TWO ships.


#### Masochist

**Hurt Yourself — As any player; mandatory; Regroup.**

- At start of any player's regroup phase, BEFORE offense retrieves warp ship, use power to win if all your ships lost.
- Lost ships include those in warp, removed from game, and captured by other players.
- Do not lose power for too few home colonies. May still win normally.


#### Mind

**Knowledge — As any player; optional; Alliance.**

- Before allies invited may look at entire hand of ONE main player. If main player, may look at opponent's hand.
- May NOT tell any other players what cards seen in a player's hand using power.


#### Mirror

**Reversal — Main player only; optional; Planning.**

- After encounter cards selected but before revealed may call reversal.
- Reverse digits of any attack cards revealed in this encounter; resolve encounter using reversed values.
- Examples: 15 becomes 51, 20 becomes 02, 08 becomes 80.


#### Miser

**Hoard — As any player; optional; Start Turn, Regroup, Destiny, Launch, Alliance, Planning, Reveal, Resolution.**

- Setup: Deal TWO separate eight-card hands; look at them and choose one hoard, facedown on sheet.
- Whenever wishing to play a card, may use power to play from hoard instead of normal hand.
- May not add gained cards to hoard; other players may not look at or draw cards from hoard.
- If hoard has no encounter cards, may reveal it, discard it, and get new eight-card hoard.


#### Mite

**Bluster — Offense only; mandatory; Launch.**

- Before allies invited, if defense has more than three cards, use power. Defense must either discard down to THREE cards at random or let you establish colony on targeted world.
- If granted colony, land any own ships in gate on targeted planet; coexist with defense ships (which do not go to warp); encounter immediately ends successfully.
- If defense discards down to three at random, continue normally.


#### Mutant

**Regenerate — Main player only; optional; Alliance.**

- Before allies invited, if fewer than eight cards, may refill hand.
- Draw cards ONE AT A TIME, at random, from any player(s) or deck until full hand of eight.


#### Observer

**Protect — Main player or ally only; mandatory; Resolution.**

- As ally, whenever own ships should be lost to warp, instead return to any own colonies and keep using them.
- As main player, whenever any own allies should lose ships to warp, instead allow those allies to return ships to any of their colonies of their choice.


#### Oracle

**Foresee — Main player only; mandatory; Planning.**

- Before encounter cards selected, force opponent to play encounter card FACEUP.
- Only after seeing opponent's card select and play own card.


#### Pacifist

**Peace — Main player only; mandatory; Reveal.**

- If you reveal negotiate and opponent attack, use power to win encounter.
- If both reveal negotiate, attempt deal as usual.


#### Parasite

**Infest — Not main player; optional; Alliance.**

- When own turn to ally, may ally with either side as if invited even when not invited, sending one to four ships as usual.
- Cannot bypass another game effect specifically preventing alliance, e.g. Force Field artifact.


#### Philanthropist

**Giving — Main player or ally only; optional; Alliance.**

- After alliances formed may give ONE hand card to either main player; if main player must give to opponent.
- Recipient immediately adds card to hand and may play it normally.
- If afterwards own hand has no encounter cards AND you are main player, draw new hand.


#### Reincarnator

**Reincarnation — Main player or ally only; mandatory; Resolution.**

- When losing encounter or failing deal, draw alien power at random from unused pile and become that alien.
- Redraw if alien has Game Setup text or is not allowed in current game.
- If lose later encounter, discard that alien and draw new one. Retain Reincarnator power while using others.
- Aliens able to copy power may copy BOTH Reincarnator and currently reincarnated power; if they lose an encounter while copying must reincarnate, losing original power.


#### Remora

**Cling — As any player; optional; Start Turn, Regroup, Destiny, Launch, Alliance, Planning, Reveal, Resolution.**

- Whenever another player retrieves one or more warp ships, may retrieve ONE own warp ship as well.
- May NOT retrieve ship in same encounter it went to warp.
- Whenever another player draws one or more deck cards, may draw ONE deck card as well.


#### Reserve

**Reinforce — Main player or ally only; optional; Reveal.**

- After encounter cards revealed may play one or more attack cards of 06 OR LESS from hand as reinforcement cards of listed value.
- When another player plays reinforcement, may use power and discard negotiate card to cancel and discard that reinforcement.


#### Shadow

**Execute — As any player; mandatory; Destiny.**

- Whenever any OTHER player's color or special destiny targeting another player drawn, choose one of that player's ships from any colony of YOUR choice and send to warp.
- On wild destiny may execute one ship of ANY other player regardless of whom offense chooses to attack.


#### Sorcerer

**Magic — Main player only; optional; Planning.**

- After encounter cards selected but before reveal may switch encounter cards with opponent; opponent reveals your card and you reveal opponent's.
- Restriction: Gambler sheet says do not use Gambler with Sorcerer.


#### Spiff

**Crash Land — Offense only; optional; Resolution.**

- When losing encounter, if both main players revealed attacks AND own total was 10 OR MORE less than defense, may land ONE own ship otherwise lost to warp on winning defensive planet.
- Ship coexists with ships already there.
- Does not allow coexistence in places or with aliens that state otherwise.


#### Tick-Tock

**Patience — As any player; mandatory; Resolution.**

- Setup: Place ten tokens on sheet; EIGHT if playing four planets per player.
- Each time any player wins as defense OR successful deal made between any two players, discard one token from sheet.
- If no tokens left on sheet immediately win game. May also win normally.


#### Trader

**Transference — Main player only; optional; Planning.**

- Before encounter cards selected may trade hands with opponent. Both then keep new hands.


#### Tripler

**Thrice — Main player only; mandatory; Reveal.**

- After revealing attack, adjust its value: 10 OR LESS triple value; MORE THAN 10 divide value by three, rounded up.


#### Vacuum

**Catharsis — As any player; mandatory; Start Turn, Regroup, Destiny, Launch, Alliance, Planning, Reveal, Resolution.**

- Whenever own ships lost to warp, take along UP TO equal number of other ships.
- YOU specify affected players and how many ships each must lose. Targeted players choose colony/colonies ships come from.
- Losses to Vacuum in addition to normal encounter losses.


#### Virus

**Multiply — Main player only; mandatory; Reveal.**

- After revealing attack, multiply number of OWN ships in encounter by own attack-card value instead of adding.
- Allies' ships still added to total as usual.


#### Void

**Eradicate — Main player only; mandatory; Resolution.**

- When winning encounter remove LOSING SIDE'S ships from game instead of sending to warp.
- Cannot reduce any player to fewer ships than number of foreign colonies needed to win game.
- Eradicated ships that would reduce player below this number go to warp normally.


#### Vulch

**Salvage — As any player; mandatory; Start Turn, Regroup, Destiny, Launch, Alliance, Planning, Reveal, Resolution.**

- Whenever any OTHER player discards artifact, whether after playing or not, add artifact to own hand.
- Own played artifacts discarded normally and CANNOT be salvaged.
- When drawing new hand, retain old artifacts AFTER revealing them, then draw eight new cards IN ADDITION.


#### Warpish

**Necromancy — Main player only; mandatory; Reveal.**

- After revealing attack add 1 to own total for EACH ship in warp, yours or otherwise.


#### Warrior

**Mastery — Main player only; mandatory; Reveal.**

- Add one token each time you win encounter or make deal, TWO each time lose or fail deal. In either case add ONE EXTRA if playing four planets per player.
- As main player after revealing attack use power to add 1 to own total per sheet token; tokens NOT discarded.


#### Will

**Choice — Offense only; mandatory; Launch.**

- After drawing destiny use power to encounter ANY alien on ANY planet of own choice instead of alien mandated by destiny.
- Example: may encounter Virus ships on Mind planet although destiny directed Oracle.
- Any OTHER effects caused by drawn destiny still occur normally.


#### Zombie

**Immortality — As any player; mandatory; Start Turn, Regroup, Destiny, Launch, Alliance, Planning, Reveal, Resolution.**

- Whenever own ships should be lost to warp, instead return them to any own colonies and keep using them.
- May free ANY player's ships from warp back to any colonies that player has as part of deal.
