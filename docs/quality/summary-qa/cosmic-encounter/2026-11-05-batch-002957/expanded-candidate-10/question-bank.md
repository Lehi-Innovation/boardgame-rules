# Expanded candidate 10 — source review pending

## Q001

For a three-player standard game, how are aliens selected, which flares enter the cosmic deck, how many cards does each player receive, and how is the first player selected?

- Deal two shuffled flares to each player; secretly choose one corresponding alien, return other sheet.
- Include all six dealt flares plus four random extra flares: ten total.
- Eight-card hands.
- Draw destiny until a player color appears, that player starts; shuffle drawn card back into destiny.

## Q002

What counts as one colony, can several players have colonies on one planet, and do five foreign colonies have to be in different systems?

- One or more same-color ships on a planet are one colony.
- Different colors can coexist, one colony per color per planet.
- Five planets outside own system; may all be in same system.

## Q003

At the start of an encounter, must the offense retrieve a warp ship if available, and where does the original rulebook place it when the offense has colonies or has none?

- Must retrieve one if available.
- Any existing own home or foreign colony.
- If no colonies, original rulebook places it directly in hyperspace gate.

## Q004

What happens when destiny has only one card left, and what choices does drawing your own color offer if your home system contains a foreign colony or a completely empty planet?

- Do not draw last card; shuffle with destiny discards then draw.
- May redraw own color or attack one selected foreign colony in own system.
- May reclaim completely ship-empty home planet with up to four ships from other colonies, a successful winning encounter through Launch only.

## Q005

Without a special power, may offense attack Blue’s foreign colony in Red’s home system after drawing Blue, and who defends Red’s empty home planet if foreign bystanders are present?

- No: Blue draw targets Blue in Blue home system.
- Red defends own planet with zero ships.
- Foreign colonies are bystanders: no defensive contribution, unaffected by outcome.

## Q006

From where and how many ships may the offense launch, may it strip a colony, and can the defense move ships onto or off the targeted planet during Launch?

- One to four offense ships from any own colonies, same or several.
- May strip but last ship removed immediately loses colony.
- Defense cannot add or subtract ships on target.

## Q007

In what order are allies invited and committed, can someone invited by both sides join both, and where do defensive allies put their ships?

- Offense invites then defense; invitees wait until both invitations.
- Starting left of offense clockwise each accepts/declines and commits before next player.
- One side only or neither; only invited side normally.
- Each ally one to four ships from own colonies; defensive ships next to, not on target.

## Q008

An attacking side has an attack 10 and six ships; defense has attack 12 and four ships. Who wins, and what happens to offense, offensive allies, defense, defensive allies and bystanders?

- Totals16 each; defense wins ties.
- Offense and offensive ally ships to warp.
- Defense colony and bystanders remain.
- Defensive allies return ships to any own colonies, no new colony on defended planet; receive one reward per committed ship.

## Q009

When offense wins an attack, where do ships on each side go, do existing bystander colonies survive, and does reinforcing an existing colony earn another colony?

- Offense and offensive allies land on target, establish or reinforce colonies.
- Defense and defensive allies to warp.
- Bystanders survive.
- Reinforcement of existing same-color colony is not new colony.

## Q010

A defensive ally contributes three ships to a winning defense. May they combine cards and warp retrieval as rewards, and where do the allied and retrieved ships go?

- Three rewards, each one deck card or one own warp ship, may combine.
- Allied ships return to any existing own colonies, even different origin.
- Retrieved ships to any own colonies; no right to land on defended planet without existing colony.

## Q011

If a main player’s negotiate loses to attack and that player loses three ships to warp while two allied ships also go there, how much compensation is collected, from where, and what if opponent holds fewer cards?

- Three random cards from opponent hand, not five; allied losses excluded.
- Take entire hand if fewer than three.
- Allies get no compensation.

## Q012

When both reveal negotiate, what happens to allies, what may the main players exchange, where may a new colony be granted, what makes a valid deal, and what is the failure penalty/time limit?

- Allies return to own colonies and get nothing, excluded from deal.
- One minute; exchange hand cards and/or at most one colony per player on planet giver already occupies, own or foreign.
- Use any recipient ships not in warp; coexist.
- At least one card or colony changes hands, may be one-sided; promises unenforceable and tangible card terms must be honored.
- Failure: each main player loses three chosen ships to warp; no second encounter.

## Q013

What does Morph copy and when, does it track later changes to the opponent’s card, and what happens after the encounter?

- Exact opponent encounter card initially revealed.
- Later changes do not automatically alter Morph; it can be separately modified.
- Returns to Morph identity after resolution and normally discarded with encounter cards.

## Q014

Who may play reinforcements, when, on which side, and when is the reinforcement response window finished?

- Offense, defense or allies only.
- Reveal after encounter cards revealed.
- Either side, including opposing side.
- Continue responses until all pass then resolve totals.

## Q015

What are the ordinary per-player and per-card flare limits, when does a played flare return to hand, and when does an alien use its own Wild instead of Super?

- Player at most one flare per encounter; given flare once per encounter even transferred.
- Immediately after effect resolves returns to hand.
- Own matching alien must use Super normally; power lost or zapped allows only Wild until restored.
- Cancellation targeting flare counts toward player limit.

## Q016

When offense lacks encounter cards at start of turn, when defense lacks them at Planning, and when offense loses its last one later, what are the hand replacement and ship-return rules? Is playing other cards before replacement compulsory?

- Offense start: reveal/discard remaining hand, eight new cards.
- Defense when required: reveal/discard, eight new, repeat until encounter card.
- Other cards may legally be played beforehand but not compulsory.
- Offense later when needs encounter card: turn ends, no new hand; gate offense and allies return to own colonies.
- No automatic draw at encounter/turn end.

## Q017

After a successful first encounter may offense start a second with no encounter card, hoping to obtain one later? What ends an ordinary turn after first or second encounter?

- No, need at least one encounter card in hand to start second.
- First win or deal allows optional second; first loss or failed deal ends turn.
- After second ends regardless outcome, pass left.

## Q018

Exactly when is an ordinary alien power lost and regained for home colony count, can it be used one last time upon loss, and does a Cosmic Zap stop every printed part of the sheet?

- Fewer than three home colonies: immediately lose, turn sheet down; no last use.
- Regain at least three home colonies: restore faceup.
- Zap cancels use and further uses current encounter; clauses not requiring use still function, e.g. Warrior experience and Zombie deal liberation.

## Q019

When contradictory effects really occur simultaneously, what player order resolves them? Can a player generally interrupt a resolving effect with another non-Zap action, and may a power be preemptively Cosmic Zapped?

- Offense, defense, other players clockwise from left of offense.
- Complete effect before another except Zap or specific interruption permission.
- No preemptive Cosmic Zap; must power use.

## Q020

When are ordinary five-foreign-colony victories checked, can wins be shared, and what happens when Tick-Tock removes its last token because two main players make a deal that gives both their fifth foreign colony?

- Required relocation with no colony goes to warp unless a specific exception applies.

## Q143

Under the original rulebook, a special destiny card designates the offense itself as the defense. May that player instead try to drive out a foreign colony in their own system; how is the actual defending colony chosen and what happens to other colors there?

- Original rulebook permits driving out a foreign colony after a self-designating special, as after own color.
- Offense chooses one player's foreign colony as defense in own system.
- All other colonies are bystanders and unaffected.

## Q144

Oracle and Sorcerer are the main players. Explain their complete card-selection and swapping sequence, including which card is faceup or facedown at each step and whether their sharing a Planning phase makes the effects simultaneous.

- Oracle acts before selecting its card, forcing Sorcerer to play faceup.
- Oracle sees that card, then selects and plays its own facedown.
- Only after both selections may Sorcerer switch encounter cards, before revealing Oracle's card.
- The before/after-selection timings govern; not simultaneous merely because both are Planning.

## Q145

When using Clone Wild, what happens to an artifact you play, may it be played again during the same encounter merely because it was retained, and what printed role/phase applies to the flare?

- May retain the artifact instead of discarding it.
- Printed permission is to play it again during a later encounter, not repeated replay in the same encounter.
- Any player; any phase, with artifact's own legal timing still relevant.

## Q146

Using the classic Filch Wild flare, what items may you illicitly take and from which locations, where may the retrieved ships go, when must the flare be revealed and when may it be zapped, what happens if caught, and what changes about the deck/discards after it is revealed? Under its printed role and phase permissions, may an uninvolved player perform the classic Filch Wild cheating action during another player's Regroup?

- May illicitly take own ships from warp to colonies or cards from deck/discard even without entitlement.
- Does not need reveal unless caught, then may be zapped.
- If caught lose one ship to warp and return items caught filching.
- Once revealed deck/discards placed next to user for access.
- Yes under those permissions: classic Filch Wild is As Any Player and Any Phase, so an uninvolved player and Regroup are allowed.

## Q147

For the original classic Filch Super shown in the rulebook, which player role and phase are printed, whose used encounter card may be filched, and must the user have participated in that encounter?

- As any player during Reveal.
- May filch any other player's used encounter card whether involved or not.

## Q148

Under the retained official Mind Wild erratum, when can the flare be played, which later draws does that one play intercept, what does the user do with each intercepted card, and may the information be shared?

- Play when another player draws a card from the cosmic deck.
- For rest of encounter intercept cards that same player must draw, not every player's later draws.
- User draws, inspects, and gives each card to that player.
- May not share the information with other players.

## Q149

Under the retained Reincarnator Wild erratum, what event and target restrictions permit giving the flare to another player, when does that target replace its power, and which replacement aliens are rejected?

- After another player besides Reincarnator loses or fails a deal, may give them the flare.
- Immediately after end of encounter target discards current power and draws a new one.
- Reject Game Setup and disallowed aliens and draw again.

## Q150

Before choosing aliens, how are the warp, player planets, ships and colony markers set up in a standard game, which destiny cards are removed or retained, and may both Filch flare versions be included?

- Warp in center; each player chooses a color and takes its five planets.
- Each player has twenty ships, four on each home planet.
- Colony markers begin beside the warp at zero foreign colonies.
- Remove destiny cards for unused player colors; retain used colors, wilds and specials and shuffle.
- Use either standard or classic Filch flare, only one version; return the other to box.

## Q151

In an ordinary encounter, what opponent and system choices does a wild destiny card permit, how does a special destiny card determine the defense and location, what color does a special count as for color-sensitive effects, and do hazard symbols change base play?

- Wild permits choosing another player, encountered in that player's home system.
- Special states defense conditions and location; follow its text.
- For effects special is treated as the designated defense's color.
- Hazard symbols have no effect in this original base game.

## Q152

May the two main players invite one another as allies? Without an exception, may an uninvited other player join, may an invitee refuse, and how many ships from where does each joining ally commit? After alliances, which players select encounter cards, which types are legal, how are they placed/revealed, and when are they normally discarded?

- Neither main player may invite the other.
- Non-main players normally need invitation; may decline all invitations.
- Each ally commits one to four own ships from any own colonies.
- Only offense and defense select one encounter card each: attack, negotiate or Morph.
- Normally placed facedown at Planning and revealed simultaneously at Reveal, subject to powers.
- Normally discard revealed encounter cards after resolving encounter, unless an effect changes destination.

## Q153

Can an artifact be selected as the encounter card, how do you determine when and how it may be played, and what is its normal destination after play?

- Artifacts cannot be played as encounter cards.
- Each artifact's printed timing and instructions determine when/how it can be played.
- Normally discard an artifact after playing it.

## Q154

When a deal grants a colony, must the recipient use only its ships on the hyperspace gate or may it use other ships, and which ship location is excluded? After the deal, where do any remaining offensive gate ships return?

- Any of the recipient's own ships not in the warp may establish its deal colony; not restricted to gate ships.
- Any offensive ships remaining on gate after the deal return to any of offense's existing colonies.

## Q155

A defensive ally has three defender rewards. May it draw a card, inspect it and then decide whether the remaining rewards will be more cards or warp ships? When must it commit to the full mix of reward sources?

- Must declare all reward sources before taking any rewards, including the complete base cosmic-card/warp-ship mix.
- Cannot inspect an initial draw before deciding remaining reward sources.

## Q156

When Mobius Tubes releases a player's ships and those ships are placed on that player's existing colonies, do the ships land simultaneously or one ship at a time?

- The ships land on colonies simultaneously.

## Q157

Without a specific card or alien exception, do you draw a card or new hand merely because an encounter ends, a turn ends, or your hand becomes empty?

- No automatic draw is caused merely by an encounter ending, a turn ending or the hand becoming empty.

## Q158

During an ordinary Launch after destiny specifies the system, who chooses the target planet within that system, and where are the offense's launched ships placed? During Alliance, where does a joining offensive ally place its committed ships?

- The offense points the gate at one planet in the system indicated by destiny.
- Place the launched offense ships on the wide end of the hyperspace gate.
- Place the joining offensive ally's ships on the hyperspace gate.

## Q159

Without a special exception, how many defender rewards does a successful defensive ally receive for its contributed ships, and may it choose to skip those rewards?

- Receive one defender reward for each ship contributed to the successful defense.
- For each contributed ship the ally must draw one cosmic-deck card or retrieve one own warp ship; the ordinary rule does not permit declining the reward.

## Q160

Under the original rulebook, name the seven phases of an ordinary encounter in order. How is the separate Start Turn step related to this sequence on the first encounter?

- Regroup, Destiny, Launch, Alliance, Planning, Reveal, Resolution, in that order.
- Start Turn occurs before the first encounter's Regroup effects, and only on that first encounter.

## Q161

In an ordinary attack-versus-attack encounter, what does a legally played reinforcement card do to the total of the side it is played on?

- Add the legally played reinforcement card's printed amount to the chosen side's encounter total.

## Q162

For the FAQ-quoted Mite Super instruction to take any cards its opponent discards, is pickup restricted to encounter cards, or can it include another original-base card type such as a discarded artifact?

- It is not restricted to encounter cards: that instruction allows taking any cards the opponent discards, including a discarded artifact.

## Q163

In the official FAQ Oracle Wild example, are the hands kept separate or mixed together, is the new hand chosen selectively or at random, and where is the Oracle flare during that process and afterward?

## Q164

For a legally played Loser Wild flare, must it be played before encounter cards are selected, or can it first be played after selection?

- Loser Wild is played before encounter cards are selected; it cannot first be played after selection.

## Q165

Must a player be a main player or an ally to play Cosmic Zap on an alien's power use, or may a bystander play it?
