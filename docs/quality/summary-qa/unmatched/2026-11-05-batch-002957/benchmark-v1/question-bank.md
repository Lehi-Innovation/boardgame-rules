# Unmatched fixed seed bank v1

Prepared from sources before summary creation and reader dispatch.

## Q01

How is a duel set up: cards and health, who places where and in what order, legal sidekick spaces including a multizone hero space, when is Alice’s initial size chosen, and who takes the first turn?

**Expected:** Choose a battlefield; each player takes their hero’s matching components and sets character-card starting health (undialed sidekicks have 1 health each); Shuffle each 30-card deck separately and draw 5; Younger places hero on 1 then sidekicks in separate spaces sharing any zone of that hero; choose Alice’s size on placement; Older places hero on 2 then sidekicks by the same rules; Younger takes first turn

**Source:** rulebook p6; p5

## Q02

What actions must you take on a turn, may you repeat or skip an action, and when and how do you enforce the 7-card hand limit if you temporarily hold 9?

**Expected:** Take 2 actions from maneuver, scheme, attack; may repeat the same action but cannot skip; May hold 9 during turn; at end discard 2 to own discard pile to reach 7, then opponent’s turn

**Source:** rulebook p8–9

## Q03

During an ordinary maneuver, in what order do drawing and movement occur, which fighters may move how far, and can you pass through friendly or enemy fighters, end on an occupied space, or interleave two fighters’ moves?

**Expected:** Draw 1 first, mandatory; movement optional; Each fighter may move 0 up to character move value along adjacent connected spaces, distances independently; Move one fighter completely before another in any order; May pass own friendly fighters but never end occupied; cannot pass opposing fighters

**Source:** rulebook p9

## Q04

How do you BOOST maneuver movement, what happens to the discarded card’s printed effect, who receives the movement increase, and may a defeated sidekick’s unusable card pay for it?

**Expected:** Discard 1 hand card and add its BOOST value to movement value for the maneuver, applying to each fighter; Ignore discarded card’s effect; Cards unusable because their fighter is defeated may still be discarded to BOOST

**Source:** rulebook p9–10

## Q05

Your empty deck is asked to draw 2 cards by a mandatory effect while your hero and a sidekick survive. Do you reshuffle or decline the draws, and what damage does each fighter take?

**Expected:** Do not reshuffle or decline; draws mandatory unless otherwise specified; Each failed draw immediately deals 2 damage to each fighter, thus 4 each over two draws if game continues and they survive; hero defeat ends game immediately

**Source:** rulebook p9; p14

## Q06

For a melee fighter and a ranged fighter, which targets are legal if adjacent across a zone boundary, nonadjacent in the same zone, or in a disconnected part of the same colored zone?

**Expected:** Both may target adjacent opposing fighters regardless of zone; Only ranged may target nonadjacent fighters in same zone, including separated parts of a same-pattern zone; Melee may only target adjacent fighters

**Source:** rulebook p7; p11

## Q07

When is an attack action illegal, who must be named as attacker, what cards may each combatant use, may the defender decline a card, and when are the cards revealed? How do versatile cards count for other effects?

**Expected:** Illegal without attack-capable card in hand or valid target for a fighter; Declare active attacking fighter and target; attack card must permit that fighter; Defender may decline; if defending use defense-capable card permitted for defending fighter; Cards placed face down attacker then defender and revealed simultaneously; Versatile usable for attack or defense and counts as both for other effects

**Source:** rulebook p11; p16

## Q08

Give the full combat resolution order, including simultaneous effect priority, whether effects are optional, other after-combat game effects and played-card destinations.

**Expected:** Resolve immediately effects then during-combat effects then combat damage/result then after-combat card effects then other after-combat game effects; At any same timing defender effect first; Card effects mandatory unless specified otherwise; Finally put played cards in respective face-up discard piles

**Source:** rulebook p12

## Q09

An attack value 4 meets defense value 4, then a card effect deals 2 damage to the defender. Who won and why? With no defense card against an attack value 4, how much attack damage is dealt?

**Expected:** Equal values cause no attack damage; defender wins despite 2 effect damage; Winner determined only by damage from attack itself: at least 1 means attacker wins, none means defender wins; Undefended value 4 deals 4 attack damage

**Source:** rulebook p12–13

## Q10

A sidekick reaches zero during combat but its hero lives; what happens to the token and its played card’s after-combat effect? What changes if instead a hero reaches zero, even if a later healing effect was pending?

**Expected:** Immediately remove defeated sidekick; its played after-combat effect still resolves if game not ended; Hero reaching zero immediately loses and game ends; no later healing or after-combat continuation can save it

**Source:** rulebook p12; p14

## Q11

How do you play and resolve a scheme, can you use a scheme restricted to a defeated fighter, and who may inspect whose discard pile when?

**Expected:** Play scheme face up from hand; declare a permitted active fighter; cannot play if listed fighters defeated; Resolve effect then place in own face-up discard pile; Both players may inspect either discard pile at any time

**Source:** rulebook p10

## Q12

State Alice’s starting health, movement, attack type, Jabberwock stats, initial-size choice, and the combat bonuses for BIG and SMALL. Do the size bonuses apply to Jabberwock?

**Expected:** Alice 13 health, move 2, melee; Jabberwock 8 health, melee; Choose BIG or SMALL on Alice placement and track with size token; BIG adds 2 to Alice attack-card values; SMALL adds 1 to Alice defense-card values; Bonuses are Alice only, not Jabberwock

**Source:** alice-character.png; rulebook p6

## Q13

State Arthur and Merlin’s starting health, attack types and move value. When and how may Arthur boost an attack using his ability, and what if the opposing card cancels his attack-card effects?

**Expected:** Arthur 18 health melee; Merlin 7 health ranged; move 2; When Arthur attacks may play a BOOST card face down alongside attack card, adding its BOOST value to attack; If opponent cancels attack-card effects, BOOST discarded without effect; Ability applies to Arthur attacks

**Source:** king-arthur-character.png; rulebook p10

## Q14

State Medusa’s starting health, move and attack type; her sidekicks’ number, health and attack type; and the timing, optionality, target and amount of her special ability.

**Expected:** Medusa 16 health, move 3, ranged; 3 Harpies, 1 health each, melee; At start of own turn may deal 1 damage to one opposing fighter in Medusa’s zone

**Source:** medusa-character.png; rulebook p5

## Q15

State Sinbad and Porter’s health and attack types and normal move value. With 3 Voyage cards already discarded, how far may each move in a maneuver and what is a newly played base-2 Voyage’s combat value before other modifiers? Does the played Voyage count itself yet?

**Expected:** Sinbad 15 health melee; Porter 6 health melee; move 2; Maneuver allowance 2+3=5 for each fighter; Voyage value 2+3=5; played card not yet discarded and does not count itself

**Source:** sinbad-character.png; sinbad-action-cards.png r1c5/r2c5; rulebook p12

## Q16

What exactly do Alice’s Eat Me and Drink Me schemes do, in which order, and may she refuse the size change after taking their first benefit?

**Expected:** Eat Me moves Alice up to 3 spaces then changes size; Drink Me draws 2 cards then changes size; Change BIG to SMALL or SMALL to BIG; mandatory size change, even if Eat Me movement chosen as zero

**Source:** alice-action-cards.png r1c6/r2c1/r2c5–6; alice-character.png; rulebook p12

## Q17

Jabberwock attacks with Jaws That Bite, Arthur defends with Skirmish, both value 4. Arthur moves Jabberwock away so it has no adjacent fighters. Resolve damage, winner, timing and the Jaws effect. If a friendly fighter alone were adjacent instead, could Alice decline to damage it?

**Expected:** No attack damage; Arthur defender wins; Defender’s after-combat Skirmish moves one combatant up to 2 first; Then Jaws deals 2 to any one fighter adjacent to Jabberwock; none means no damage; If only friendly adjacent it must take 2, because effect mandatory and any fighter includes friendly

**Source:** rulebook p12–13; alice-action-cards.png r3c1–2; king-arthur-action-cards.png r4c1–3

## Q18

Arthur uses The Holy Grail. At its after-combat timing he has respectively 4, 5, or 0 health. What happens in each case, and can any healing exceed the dial maximum?

**Expected:** At 4 (or less but above zero) set Arthur to 8; At 5 no healing from Grail; At 0 hero defeated and game already ended, no rescue; Fighters cannot gain health above dial maximum

**Source:** king-arthur-action-cards.png r2c2; rulebook p5; p14

## Q19

What movement exception does Winged Frenzy grant, to which fighters and for how far? What happens afterward if a Harpy has been defeated, where may it return, and may it end on an occupied space?

**Expected:** Move each own fighter up to 3, may pass opposing fighters; Then return one defeated Harpy if any to any space in Medusa’s zone; Destinations must be unoccupied: one fighter per space

**Source:** medusa-action-cards.png r3c3–4; rulebook p7; p9

## Q20

Sinbad plays Voyage Home with 4 other Voyages in his discard pile. If uncancelled and game continues, what is its value, what returns where after combat, where does Voyage Home itself end, and what is his next unboosted maneuver allowance?

**Expected:** Value 2+4=6 during combat; After combat return all other Voyage cards from discard to hand; Voyage Home then goes to discard at combat end; Only Voyage Home remains there so next maneuver allowance 2+1=3 for each own fighter

**Source:** sinbad-action-cards.png r1c5; sinbad-character.png; rulebook p12
