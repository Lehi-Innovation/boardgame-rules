That's correct. Additional considerations for things like Sinbad's and Arthur's ability. No boost value for very specific things like keeping Sinbad from "dumping" his voyage cards. And a nice, general rule of thumb is to give a higher boost value to a card that might become less useful if drawn at a particular moment or state in the game, i.e., if it's more situational.
