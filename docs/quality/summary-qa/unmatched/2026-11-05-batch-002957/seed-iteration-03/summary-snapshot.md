# Unmatched: Battle of Legends, Volume One

## Overview

A card-driven duel: maneuver your hero and sidekicks, play schemes, and attack opposing fighters. Reduce the opposing **hero to zero health** to win immediately. “Fighter” includes a hero or a sidekick; miniatures represent heroes and tokens represent sidekicks.

Scope: standard **two-player duel**, with **Alice, King Arthur, Medusa and Sinbad**, including their original character cards and 30-card decks. Optional team play (rulebook p15), other sets, and Adventures are excluded. The core rules below come from the English licensed booklet; the fighter and card references also use the original components preserved in the publisher co-created [Unmatched Official module](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2132611236). Card-specific effects apply at their stated timing.

## Components

- One double-sided board with two battlefields; 4 hero miniatures; 6 sidekick tokens; 7 health dials; 4 character cards; 120 action cards (30 per hero); Alice’s size token.
- Before first play, assemble each health dial from its two circles and rivet.
- Each player uses only their chosen hero’s matching deck, fighters, character card, health dials and special components.

### Fighter reference

The move value applies to **each** of that player’s fighters during maneuver. Harpies have no health dials; each has 1 health. Other fighters have individual dials. Health cannot increase above the highest number on that fighter’s dial.

| Hero | Starting health | Attack | Move | Sidekick(s): starting health, attack |
|---|---:|---|---:|---|
| Alice | 13 | Melee | 2 | Jabberwock: 8, melee |
| King Arthur | 18 | Melee | 2 | Merlin: 7, ranged |
| Medusa | 16 | Ranged | 3 | 3 Harpies: 1 each, melee |
| Sinbad | 15 | Melee | 2 | Porter: 6, melee |

## Setup

1. Choose a battlefield and place it on the table. Each player chooses a hero and takes that hero’s components.
2. Set the hero’s and sidekick’s dials to the starting health above; undialed sidekicks have 1 health each.
3. Each player shuffles their **30 action cards into one separate face-down deck**, then draws **5 cards**.
4. The **younger player** places their hero on starting space **1**, then their sidekick(s) in **separate spaces sharing a zone with that hero**. If the hero’s space belongs to several zones, use any of those zones. Make beginning-of-game decisions now: when placing Alice, choose **BIG or SMALL** and display the size token.
5. The **older player** places their hero on starting space **2**, then places sidekicks under the same rules.
6. The younger player takes the first turn.

### Battlefield

One fighter per circular space. Spaces connected by a line are **adjacent**. A **zone** consists of all spaces with the same colored pattern, even if separated on the board. A space showing several patterns belongs to all those zones. Adjacency and shared zones are different tests.

## Turn Structure

At the start of Medusa’s turn, her optional special ability may resolve (see below). Then take **2 actions** from **maneuver, scheme, attack**. You may repeat an action, including maneuver twice; **you cannot skip an action**.

You may hold more than 7 cards during your turn. **At the end of your turn**, discard excess cards to your own discard pile until you have **7**, then the opponent takes their turn.

## Actions

### Maneuver

1. **Draw 1 card first, mandatory.**
2. **Optionally move** each of your fighters, one at a time, up to your move value (plus applicable bonuses). Each fighter chooses its own distance, including **zero**. Choose their order, but finish one fighter’s movement before starting another’s.

Ordinary movement enters adjacent spaces along connecting lines. You may pass through your own friendly fighters, but **cannot finish in any occupied space**. You may **not pass through opposing fighters**. An effect that moves opposing fighters follows the same rules **from their owner’s perspective**. Apply these movement restrictions to card movement unless that card states an exception.

**BOOST movement:** during a maneuver, discard **1 card from your hand** to add its printed **BOOST value** (the small circled number, not its combat value) to the movement value for this maneuver. Each of your fighters receives that allowance. Ignore the discarded card’s effects. A card that cannot be played because its fighter is defeated **can still be discarded to BOOST**. Effects may separately permit boosting attacks; boosting is not otherwise a universal option.

### Scheme

Play a scheme (yellow lightning icon) from hand **face up**, declaring a fighter allowed by its fighter label as the **active fighter**. You cannot play a scheme if its listed fighters are defeated. Resolve its effect, then put it in your discard pile.

### Attack

You cannot take an attack action without an attack-capable card in hand or without a valid target for one of your fighters.

1. **Declare** your attacking fighter (active fighter) and the opposing fighter targeted. Any fighter may attack an **adjacent** opponent, even across zone boundaries. **Melee** fighters may attack only adjacent opponents. **Ranged** fighters may also attack any opponent sharing a zone, regardless of distance or adjacency, including another separated part of that zone.
2. **Choose cards:** attacker plays an attack card face down that the attacking fighter is allowed to use. Defender may decline to play a card; otherwise plays a legal defense card face down for the defending fighter. **Reveal simultaneously** after both choices.
3. **Resolve combat** in the order below, then discard played cards to their owners’ piles.

Attack-only cards are red, defense-only blue, and **versatile** cards purple. Versatile cards may attack or defend and **count as both attack and defense cards for other effects**. A fighter-specific card cannot be used by a different fighter; **ANY** permits any of your fighters. A pure defense card cannot initiate an attack; a pure attack card cannot defend.

### Combat timing, damage and winning combat

1. Resolve **IMMEDIATELY** card effects.
2. Resolve **DURING COMBAT** card effects.
3. Determine combat result: defender takes attack value minus defense value, to a minimum of zero. If no defense card, take the attack value. Reduce the defending fighter’s health by the damage dealt. Defense does not itself deal return damage.
4. Resolve **AFTER COMBAT card effects**. A defeated fighter’s played card still resolves its after-combat effects **unless the game has already ended**.
5. Resolve other after-combat game effects, such as a hero’s special ability.
6. Finally place all played cards in their respective discard piles.

**Whenever effects share a timing, the defender’s effect resolves first.** Card effects are **mandatory unless they say otherwise** (for example “may”); this can harm your own fighters. Complete them in the printed order. A card whose effects are canceled keeps its printed combat value; the canceled effects do not resolve.

**Winning combat is not winning the game:** the attacker wins combat only if the **attack itself** deals at least 1 damage. If it deals none, the **defender wins**, including a tie of values or a defense that prevents the attack damage. Damage from effects does **not** change this winner.

## Scoring / Victory Conditions

There is no points score. Any fighter reduced to **zero health for any reason is defeated**. Immediately remove a defeated sidekick token. A Harpy is defeated by any damage because it has 1 health.

**A defeated hero immediately loses the duel, and the game ends at once.** Do not continue pending effects to heal that hero or finish the turn. Defeating a sidekick alone does not win the game.

## Special Rules & Edge Cases

### Cards, exhaustion and healing

- Drawing cards, whether from maneuver or an effect, is **mandatory unless explicitly optional**.
- With an empty deck, your fighters are **exhausted**. Each time you must draw a card you cannot draw, **each of your fighters immediately takes 2 damage instead**. Do **not reshuffle** your discard pile. A mandatory draw of 2 with an empty deck therefore causes two separate 2-damage events to every surviving fighter, unless hero defeat has already ended the game. If only one card remains, draw it and take the exhaustion damage for the next missing draw.
- Played and resolved cards and discarded cards go into your own **face-up discard pile**, distinct from the face-down deck. Both players may inspect either discard pile **at any time**.
- Recovery cannot raise a fighter above their dial maximum. A later recovery effect cannot undo the immediate game end from hero defeat.
- “Move” follows paths and blockers; “place” puts the fighter directly in the permitted destination, with **one fighter per space** still required. Card permission to place anywhere is not limited to maneuver distance or a movement path.

### Hero abilities

**Alice:** choose BIG or SMALL when placing her at setup. When **BIG**, add **2 to Alice’s attack-card values**. When **SMALL**, add **1 to Alice’s defense-card values**. This includes versatile cards used in that role. These bonuses belong to **Alice, not Jabberwock**. “Change size” switches BIG ↔ SMALL and updates the token; it is mandatory when the effect says so. After-combat size changes occur after the current combat result and do not retroactively change its damage.

**King Arthur:** Arthur and Merlin each use **move 2** during maneuver, before any BOOST. When **Arthur attacks**, he may BOOST the attack by playing a hand card **face down alongside his attack card**. Add that card’s BOOST value, ignoring its effects; the BOOST card is discarded. If the opponent **cancels the effects on Arthur’s attack card**, this ability’s BOOST is **discarded without effect**. This is Arthur’s ability, not Merlin’s. **Noble Sacrifice** separately permits another BOOST during combat, in addition to the ability’s BOOST, if its effect is not canceled.

**Medusa:** **at the start of your turn**, you **may deal 1 damage to one opposing fighter in Medusa’s zone**. This is optional, not an attack action, and does not require adjacency. Harpies do not project this ability from their own zones.

**Sinbad:** when you maneuver, each of your fighters may move **+1 space for each VOYAGE card in your discard pile**, added to the normal move value 2 and any maneuver BOOST. The bonus also applies to Porter. A Voyage’s own combat value is its **printed 2 plus 1 per other Voyage in the discard pile during combat**, before any other modifiers. Resolve this bonus during combat before calculating damage. The card currently being played is not yet in that pile; played cards enter it only at the end of combat. Voyage Home can remove the accumulated cards from the pile, reducing later bonuses.

## Player Reference

- **Turn:** optional Medusa start trigger → 2 mandatory actions → discard down to 7 → opponent.
- **Maneuver:** draw 1 → optional BOOST of movement → move each fighter independently, optionally zero.
- **Attack:** declare fighter and target → legal face-down cards → simultaneous reveal → immediately → during combat → damage/winner → after-combat cards → other after-combat effects → discard.
- **Same timing:** defender first. **Combat winner:** attack damage only. **Duel winner:** opponent’s hero defeated, immediate end.
- **Empty-deck draw:** no reshuffle; 2 damage to every own fighter for each missing card.
- **Sources:** licensed rulebook pp4–14 and icon reference p16; original four character cards and 6×5 action sheets in the publisher co-created module. Team play p15 is outside this summary.
