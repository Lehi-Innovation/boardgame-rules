Version v12-expanded. Original2020 base/core standard2–4 players, required House Hagal included; solo/variants/expansions/Uprising excluded. Original20 questions and expected-answer strings preserved. See explicit correction histories. Historical Q14/Q53 shorthand means the 2-Influence space awarding 1VP.

## Q01

How is the Conflict Deck built, top to bottom, and what happens to unused Conflict cards?

**Expected:** Shuffle each tier; top 1 random I, then 5 random II, then all 4 III, ten total. Return unused cards unseen to box.

**Source:** ["Main PDF pp4–5"]

## Q69

In a three-player Conflict, the three humans finish with 8, 6 and 4 strength, with no ties. Who wins, and which reward does each receive? What changes for an ordinary third-place finisher in a four-player game?

## Q76

When you play Master Tactician during Combat, can you gain its three-sword bonus and also retreat troops from that same play, or must you choose between the printed effects? If you choose retreat, where do those troops go?

**Expected:** Choose one of the two effects: gain the three-sword bonus, or retreat up to three of your troops from the Conflict to your garrison. You cannot resolve both alternatives from the same play. Choosing retreat does not also award the printed three swords.
