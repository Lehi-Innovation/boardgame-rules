# Source scope and inspection

Primary source: publisher-authored original Dune: Imperium 2020 rulebook, 20 PDF pages. Main rules pp1–16, Board Space Guide pp17–18, House Hagal p19 (general and two-player procedures) are included. Solo-only p20 and solo-specific clauses of p19 are excluded. Complete supplied extraction read; local PDF independently extracted. PDF pp16–18 visually inspected: confirms Combat labels, Agent icons and Sell Melange rates absent from extraction. Images retained.

Official FAQ last updated January 13, 2025: all four pages read. Only entries relevant to original base cards, Leaders and procedures apply. Entries and clauses for Rise of Ix, Immortality, Uprising, six-player, promo/additional Leaders, grafting, contracts, Spies, sandworms, tech and solo excluded. Shared entry examples from expansions do not extend scope. Relevant main-book rule corrections supersede the original print text, notably Alliance loss and Sort Through the Chaos Mentat timing.

Uprising supplements: consulted to identify the separate Uprising Board Space Guide, Rival-card rules, CHOAM module and six-player team supplement. All are outside this game. Its extraction is retained solely as excluded-scope identification, never as original Imperium rules authority.

Sources supplied by coordinator. Original download URLs and PDF hashes are copied in source-provenance.json. No additional downloads were needed.

Additional visual source pass: PDFpp4–13 and19 were rendered and inspected in full. The p7 Corner the Market illustration supplies two independent VP clauses; p9 Sardaukar Legion supplies the Reveal-deployment exception. Both are explicitly covered. Ordinary numeric printed-card examples are not treated as separate rules without a distinct outcome/timing/exception. Images are retained.

Original Harvest Spice inset recovery: main PDFp19 raster149×107 could not establish target selection. The original card image600×430 was obtained from Dire Wolf’s October9,2020 Paul Dennen designer diary and visually matched to the rulebook illustration. It resolves highest-bonus priority, total-spice tiebreak and no-bonus redraw. See harvest-source-provenance.json. Only original base two-player rules used; solo material on that page remains excluded. Duke Leto’s p6 Leader text was also enlarged and inspected.

The p14 strategy-page Machinations inset and p12 Master Tactician card were visually inspected for source-printed allocation/OR choice rules; ordinary illustrated numeric statistics were rejected as duplicate instances of already-covered procedures.
