## Overview

Orléans is a bag-building game of trade, development, and public works in medieval France. Draw Followers, plan actions with their professions, then alternate actions with other players. After 18 rounds, score coins, goods, and the combined value of built Trading Stations and collected Citizens.

**Scope:** The 2014 dlp base game for 2–4 players: English rules pp.12–21 and the 20 base Place Tiles on pp.22–23. Optional pregame exclusion of Place Tiles and all expansions are excluded. The Bathhouse below uses this booklet's original draw-three, choose-two rule.

## Components

- Large game board, Beneficial Deeds board, 4 player boards and 4 Followers bags.
- 4 Merchant tokens, 40 Trading Stations (10 per player), and 28 markers (7 per player).
- 104 Character/Follower tiles; each player's four marked starting Followers are a Farmer, Boatman, Craftsman, and Trader. Other professions include Knights, Scholars, and Monks.
- 90 goods: 24 grain, 21 cheese, 18 wine, 15 wool, 12 brocade.
- 16 Technology tiles, 14 Citizen tiles, 47 coins, 20 Place Tiles (categories I and II), 18 Hour Glass tiles, 2 overview cards, and Start Player token.

## Setup

1. Place both shared boards. Sort neutral Followers by profession into their game-board buildings, Technology into its supply, and coins nearby. Put 13 Citizens on their marked board/Deed spaces; set the remaining Citizen aside for the final Trading Station award.
2. Each player takes a board, bag, Merchant, 10 Trading Stations, 5 coins, 7 markers, and their four marked starting Followers. Put the starting Followers **on your Market**, the Merchant in Orléans, and one marker on the first space of each of the six Character Tracks and the Development Track. Keep coins and unbuilt stations in front of you. Development Status starts at 1; initial draw allowance is 4.
3. Separate Place Tiles into categories I and II. Players may inspect these stacks at any time.
4. Remove the lighter Pilgrimage tile from the Hour Glass tiles, shuffle the other 17 face down, then put that Pilgrimage on top. Thus round 1 always has Pilgrimage.
5. Shuffle goods face down, then place one face up on each eligible Road/Waterway goods space. In 4-player games fill every such space. Sort remaining goods face up into the Goods Market.

**Fewer players:** Remove all starting Followers of unused colors. Before placing goods, make these additional removals:

| Players | Remove neutral Farmers, Boatmen, Craftsmen, Traders | Remove neutral Knights, Scholars, Monks | Random goods removed | Goods spaces left empty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 2 of each profession | 3 of each profession | 6 | Spaces marked 4 |
| 2 | 4 of each profession | 6 of each profession | 12 | Spaces marked 3 or 4 |

The youngest player takes the Start Player token.

## Turn Structure

Each of the 18 rounds has seven phases:

1. **Hour Glass:** Start Player reveals the top tile. Most events wait until Phase 6; Pilgrimage instead prohibits Monk recruitment during Phase 5.
2. **Census:** Compare markers on the Farmers Track, regardless of how many Farmer tiles are currently in bags. The unique leader gains 1 coin; a tied lead gives no coin. The unique last player pays 1 coin; a tied last place pays nothing. **In a 2-player game the last player never pays**, but a unique leader still gains 1 coin.
3. **Followers:** Draw any number up to the allowance shown on your Knights Track (initially 4), limited by free Market spaces. Unused Market Followers remain between rounds and occupy capacity. You may inspect your bag at any time, but shuffle its contents afterward. For each draw you decline, you may instead move one Follower from an Action Space back to your Market. This is the permitted way to recover assigned Followers; you cannot move a Follower directly from one Action Space to another.
4. **Planning:** Assign Market Followers to matching Action Spaces on your board and acquired Places. You may leave Followers on the Market or fill an action gradually over multiple rounds. An action is activated only when all required spaces are occupied by Followers or legal Technology. Town Hall is an exception: one of its two spaces is enough. An activation can be saved for a later round. Begin planning simultaneously; announce completion. If order matters, plan in clockwise order from Start Player.
5. **Actions:** Starting with Start Player, go clockwise repeatedly, taking **one activated action or passing** per turn. Choose your actions' order freely. Passing ends your participation in this Action Phase, even if other players continue or you have unused activations. The phase ends when everyone has passed. After executing an action, return its Followers to your bag; Technology stays on its spaces. Unused activations, and incomplete actions, keep their Followers on the board for future rounds.
6. **Event:** Resolve this round's event for all players (Pilgrimage already affected Actions). Payments, and any necessary order-sensitive resolution, proceed clockwise from Start Player. Coins paid go into the common supply; received coins come from it.
7. **Start Player:** Pass the token to the player on your left.

## Actions

The required professions below are the printed Action Spaces, before any permitted Monk/Place substitution or Technology. A plus sign means all listed Followers are required.

| Place | Required Followers | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Farm House | Boatman + Craftsman | Put a new Farmer in your bag; advance Farmers Track one space and take its depicted good. |
| Village | Farmer + Boatman + Craftsman | Choose **one** recruitment: Boatman, Craftsman, or Trader (details below). |
| University | Farmer + Craftsman + Trader | Put a new Scholar in your bag; advance Scholars Track one space and gain its depicted Development Points. |
| Castle | Farmer + Boatman + Trader | Put a new Knight in your bag; advance Knights Track one space. This track sets future Phase 3 draw allowance. The fourth Knight keeps allowance 7 and gives its Citizen to the first player there. |
| Monastery | Scholar + Trader | Put a new Monk in your bag; no extra track reward. |
| Ship | Farmer + Boatman + Knight | Move Merchant along one Waterway (blue connection) to an adjacent town. |
| Wagon | Farmer + Trader + Knight | Move Merchant along one Road (brown connection) to an adjacent town. |
| Guildhall | Farmer + Craftsman + Knight | Build one Trading Station in your Merchant's current town, subject to the limits below. |
| Scriptorium | Knight + Scholar | Gain 1 Development Point: move one space on the Development Track. |
| Town Hall | 1 or 2 eligible Followers | Permanently send one or both to Beneficial Deeds, as detailed below. |

**Village recruitment:** Put the chosen neutral Follower into your bag and advance its profession's track one space:

- **Boatman:** Gain the depicted coins. At the last space, the first player there gains a Citizen instead of coins.
- **Craftsman:** Gain a Technology tile, set it beside your board, and use it only **after you pass**. It cannot help you execute another action this round after that pass.
- **Trader:** Gain one Place Tile and set it beside your board. Your first Traders Track advance permits category I only; every later advance allows I or II. You may activate the new Place or use its special ability under the normal timing rules.

**Character-track limits:** Once on a track's last space, you cannot take the corresponding action again. Recruiting the fourth Knight does **not** raise the draw allowance above 7 at that step; the first player there may receive the Citizen. Read subsequent allowances from the track.

**Movement and goods:** One Ship/Wagon action traverses just one connection. You may take **one** good from that connection, choosing one if several lie there, and keep it beside your board. Empty Roads/Waterways are legal and yield no good. Movement itself does not build Trading Stations.

**Building:** Guildhall requires your Merchant in the town. A town with any player's Trading Station cannot receive another. **Orléans is the exception: each player may build one station there.** Only built stations count for income and final scoring.

**Town Hall and Beneficial Deeds:** During Planning, place one or two neutral Followers in Town Hall. Your four marked starting Followers may not enter it. In one Town Hall action, send one or both to any free Deed spaces matching their exact professions; these may be on different Deeds. Take the reward for each placement: the depicted 1, 2, or 3 coins; for Canalization choose 1 coin **or** 1 Development Point. Whoever fills a Deed's last unoccupied space takes its Citizen. Sent Followers remain there for the rest of the game. Substitutions, including Monks and Place abilities, cannot replace the exact professions depicted on Deeds.

**Monks:** On ordinary actions, a Monk can replace any other profession. The reverse is forbidden: no other Follower or Technology can replace a required Monk.

**Technology:** A Technology permanently fills one required Action Space and remains after execution. Your first placed Technology must replace a **Farmer**; later ones may replace any profession except Monk. Never place more than one Technology at a single Place or place one at a Place requiring only one Follower. Once placed, Technology cannot be moved. A Craftsman-acquired tile is used only after passing.

## Scoring / Victory Conditions

Each Development Point advances your Development marker one space. On moving **onto or past** a coin space, collect its depicted coins; the first player onto/past a Citizen space collects that Citizen. On reaching/passing a Development Status space, your Status immediately becomes its shown value. Status begins at 1 and also determines Income and Hospital payments.

Finish the entire round in which the 18th Hour Glass tile is drawn, including its event. Before scoring, the player with the **unique most built Trading Stations** takes the set-aside Citizen. A tie gives nobody that Citizen.

| Possession | Victory points |
|---|---|
| Coins | 1 each |
| Grain / cheese / wine / wool / brocade | 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 each |
| Built Trading Stations and collected Citizens | (number of built stations + number of Citizens) × final Development Status |

Highest total wins. Ties go to the player furthest ahead on the **Development Track**; if still tied, share victory.

## Special Rules & Edge Cases

**Events:** Each of the following occurs on three Hour Glass tiles.

| Event | Effect |
|---|---|
| Pilgrimage | No Monk recruitment in this round's Action Phase; this restriction applies before the Event Phase. |
| Income | Gain coins equal to Development Status. |
| Harvest | Pay one grain, cheese, or wine to the Goods Market. If you cannot pay food, pay 5 coins instead. If unable to pay those coins, undergo torture for the shortfall. Wool and brocade are not food. |
| Taxes | Count all your goods; pay 1 coin per complete group of 3 (round down). A shortfall causes torture. |
| Trading Day | Gain 1 coin per built Trading Station. |
| Plague | Draw 1 random Follower from your bag. A neutral Follower returns to its game-board supply and becomes recruitable again; do **not** move its track marker back. If it is one of your marked starting Followers, keep it and lose no Follower this round; do not redraw. |

**Torture (bankruptcy):** Whenever you cannot make a required payment, pay available coins and replace **each missing coin** with one item from any mix of:

- A Trading Station, built or unbuilt.
- One random Follower drawn from your bag. Return and redraw marked starting Followers until drawing an eligible neutral one.
- One Development Point: move one space left, but **never onto or past a coin space**.
- One Goods Tile, one Place Tile, or one Technology tile.

Items surrendered to torture are **removed from the game**, rather than returned to their supplies. Only lost Development Points can be regained. Marked starting Followers always stay with you, on your board or in your bag; they cannot be sent to Deeds, lost to Plague, or paid in torture.

**Limited supplies:** Character, Technology, and Goods tiles are finite. You cannot play an action that provides an unavailable tile, including when another part of that action would still give a reward. An action becomes available again as soon as its required tile is available again, subject to the usual action requirements. Harvest and Plague can restore goods/Followers to supply this way. If the common supply has insufficient coins, players cannot receive coins at that moment.

## Player Reference

**Round:** Reveal event → Census → Draw/recover assigned Followers → Plan → Alternate one action/pass → Resolve event → Pass Start Player left.

**Recruitment:** New Followers enter your bag; starting marked Followers begin on the Market. Farmer/Boatman/Craftsman/Trader/Scholar/Knight tracks grant their printed rewards; Monks give no extra bonus.

**Key limits:** Passing is final for this round. Unused actions retain tiles. One connection and at most one good per movement action. Technology stays on its space. Beneficial Deeds require exact neutral professions. Final score is coins + goods values + (built stations + Citizens) × Development Status.
