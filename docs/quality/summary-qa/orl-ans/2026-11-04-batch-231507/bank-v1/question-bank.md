# Orléans bank-v1 question and answer bank

Orléans 2014 dlp base game, 2–4 players; English booklet pp.12–21, base Place Tiles pp.22–23, components p.24. Excludes optional Place Tile exclusion and expansions.

## Q01

In a 3-player Census, two players tie furthest ahead and one is alone furthest behind. Who gains or pays? What changes for an unequal 2-player Census?

**Answer:** No one gains for a tied lead; the unique last player pays 1 coin. With 2 players, unique leader gains 1 coin and last pays nothing.

**Source:** p.14; extraction lines 129–133

**Distinct coverage:** Census ties and two-player exception

## Q02

Your Knight draw allowance is 6, but only two Market spaces are free. Must you draw six? May you draw more than two? What happens to unused Market Followers?

**Answer:** Drawing is optional up to the Knight allowance and free Market capacity; draw at most two. Unused Market Followers remain and occupy spaces.

**Source:** p.14; lines 135–142

**Distinct coverage:** Draw allowance versus capacity and retention

## Q03

May you partially fill an action over several rounds, execute it before it is full, or save a fully activated action for a later round? Where do its Followers go when it is eventually executed?

**Answer:** May fill over rounds, cannot execute until all required spaces occupied. May save activation with tiles left in place. On execution Followers go to own bag; Technology stays.

**Source:** p.15; lines 154–170

**Distinct coverage:** Partial/full activation and destinations

## Q04

How do players alternate actions in Phase 5? If you pass with an unused activated action, can you act again later this round and what happens to that activation?

**Answer:** Start player then clockwise, one action or pass per turn, repeat until all pass. Passing ends own actions this round even if activated actions remain; unused activation and Followers stay for a later round.

**Source:** p.15; lines 159–170

**Distinct coverage:** Action turn cycle and irreversible pass

## Q05

What does the Farm House action recruit, where does it go, and what track movement and immediate reward follow?

**Answer:** Take Farmer into own bag; advance Farmer track one space and take depicted good.

**Source:** p.16; lines 186–191

**Distinct coverage:** Farm House effect

## Q06

What three professions can Village recruit, and what distinct track reward accompanies each?

**Answer:** Choose Boatman, Craftsman or Trader, put recruit in bag and advance corresponding track. Boatman gains depicted coins (first at last space gets Citizen instead); Craftsman gains Technology usable only after passing; Trader gains Place Tile.

**Source:** pp.16–17; lines 192–208,224–234

**Distinct coverage:** Village choices and rewards

## Q07

You just recruited a Craftsman. Can its Technology help another action this Action Phase? What must your first Technology replace, and what are the placement limits for all Technology?

**Answer:** Only use after passing, so no further own action this round. First must replace Farmer; later any required Character except Monk. Max one per Place, no single-Character Places, cannot move after placement; remains until game end.

**Source:** p.16; lines 202–218

**Distinct coverage:** Technology timing and restrictions

## Q08

On your first Trader-track advance may you choose a category II Place? What categories are allowed later, and may players inspect the stacks?

**Answer:** First advance category I only; later I or II. Place beside board; can activate/use ability; stacks inspectable at any time.

**Source:** p.17; lines 224–236

**Distinct coverage:** Place acquisition categories and access

## Q09

What do University, Castle and Monastery each give and where do recruits go? Does the fourth Knight increase the draw allowance, and what happens once a character track is at its last space?

**Answer:** University Scholar into bag, advance Scholars and gain depicted Development Points; Castle Knight into bag, advance Knights for draw limit; fourth Knight stays draw 7, first there may take Citizen. Monastery Monk into bag, no additional bonus. At final track space cannot take corresponding action again.

**Source:** p.17; lines 237–260

**Distinct coverage:** Other recruitment and final track boundaries

## Q10

Can a Monk replace a Knight for an ordinary action? Can a Knight or Technology replace a required Monk? Can a Monk replace a required profession on Beneficial Deeds?

**Answer:** Monk may replace Knight or any other Character in ordinary actions. No other Character or Technology can replace required Monk. Beneficial Deeds require exact depicted types, no Monk substitution.

**Source:** pp.17–18; lines 253–257,290–293

**Distinct coverage:** Wild substitution exceptions

## Q11

What exactly does one Ship or Wagon action move, and how many goods can you take if two lie on that connection? May you use an empty connection?

**Answer:** Ship moves to one adjacent town along Waterway; Wagon likewise along Road. May take one good from connection even if multiple; keep by board. Empty links legal, give no good.

**Source:** pp.18,21; lines 263–270,388–389

**Distinct coverage:** Movement scope and goods selection

## Q12

What does Guildhall build, where must your Merchant be, and may you build where an opponent has already built? State the Orléans exception.

**Answer:** Build Trading Station in Merchant current town; no existing station of any player allowed. In Orléans each player may build one.

**Source:** p.18; lines 271–275

**Distinct coverage:** Station construction and exception

## Q13

What does Scriptorium do? When Development movement lands on or passes coin, Citizen and status spaces, what rewards/status changes occur?

**Answer:** Scriptorium gives 1 Development Point/advance 1 space. Landing/passing coin gives depicted coins; first landing/passing Citizen gets it. Landing/passing status immediately sets Development Status to shown value (initially 1).

**Source:** pp.18–19; lines 276–278,306–316

**Distinct coverage:** Development action and crossing rewards

## Q14

Can Town Hall work with one Follower, can it send two in one action, and can your starting colored Followers be used? What rewards and destinations apply, including Canalization and completing a Deed?

**Answer:** One or two can activate, move one or both to free matching Deed spaces; colored starters prohibited. Receive depicted 1/2/3 coins per tile; Canalization 1 coin or 1 Development Point. Tile remains permanently. Completing last vacant Deed space earns its Citizen.

**Source:** p.18; lines 279–296

**Distinct coverage:** Town Hall routing, rewards and completion

## Q15

What does Pilgrimage forbid, when does it apply, and when are the other events resolved? Which event opens the game?

**Answer:** Pilgrimage forbids recruiting Monks during Phase 5 Actions. Other events resolve Phase 6. Pilgrimage is deliberately first/top tile.

**Source:** pp.13–14,20; lines 99–103,123–127,343–345

**Distinct coverage:** Event timing exception

## Q16

During Harvest you have wool, wine and coins. Which goods qualify, where does paid food go, and what if you have no food and fewer than five coins?

**Answer:** Grain/cheese/wine qualify, wool does not. Must pay one food to Goods Market; if no food pay 5 coins, if unable undergo torture for missing coins.

**Source:** pp.20–21; lines 348–351,364–379

**Distinct coverage:** Harvest food, fallback, destination

## Q17

During Plague you draw a neutral Scholar; during another Plague you draw your colored starting Trader. What is lost, where does it go, do tracks fall, and is a replacement draw required for the starting Trader?

**Answer:** Neutral Scholar returned to game board, available again; do not move Scholar track backward. Colored starter cannot be lost; keep it and lose no tile this round, no redraw.

**Source:** p.20; lines 357–361

**Distinct coverage:** Plague neutral/marked branches

## Q18

You owe three coins but have only one. Explain torture payment choices, restrictions on sacrificing Followers or Development Points, and where lost items go.

**Answer:** Pay available coin and replace each missing coin (two) with one item, any mix: built/supply Trading Station, random bag Follower (redraw colored starters), Development Point (left one, never onto/past coin space), Goods, Place, Technology. Remove items from game, only Development Points can be regained.

**Source:** p.21; lines 364–379

**Distinct coverage:** Bankruptcy payments and irreversible loss

## Q19

What happens if an action would give an unavailable Character, Technology or Goods Tile? Can it resume when stock returns? What if the coin supply is temporarily empty?

**Answer:** Tiles limited: cannot play action providing unavailable tile. Harvest/Plague can return stock, permitting actions again. With insufficient supply coins, players cannot receive coins at that moment.

**Source:** p.21; lines 381–385

**Distinct coverage:** Exhaustion and restored availability

## Q20

At game end two players tie for most built Trading Stations. Who takes the bonus Citizen? Give final values of all five goods and the scoring formula for stations/Citizens; how are equal final scores broken?

**Answer:** Tied most stations gives no bonus Citizen. Grain 1, cheese 2, wine 3, wool 4, brocade 5; coins 1 VP each; (built stations + collected Citizens) × Development Status. Highest VP; tie furthest ahead on Development Track; still tied share win.

**Source:** p.19; lines 317–335

**Distinct coverage:** End bonus, complete scoring and tiebreak
