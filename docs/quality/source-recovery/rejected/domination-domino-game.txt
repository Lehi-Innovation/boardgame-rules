Game Board, 112 Dominoes, 140 Chips, 4 Domino Trays
Object
Dominate the Game Board by conquering and
claiming the most territories.
Set-Up
• Open up the Game Board and place it in the
  center of the playing area where everybody
  can reach it.
• Place all of the Dominoes, face down, off to the
  side of the Game Board and shuffle them around.
• Everybody take a Domino Tray and all of its
  color-matching chips.
• Everybody take 7 Dominoes and place them in
  your Tray, so only you can see them.
Play
• Take turns. On a turn you must place one Domino
  from your tray on the Game Board. It must be
  placed between two squares where the number of
  pips (dots) on both ends of the Domino match
  the number of
  pips on the
  squares it
  touches.
• At the end of your turn, take one Domino from
  the side to replenish your Tray back to seven.
• As the game progresses and you play a Domino
  that conquers (seals off) a territory, place one of
  your chips in that territory
  to claim it, thus ending
  your turn.
• Sometimes the
  placement of a single Domino
  conquers two territories simultaneously. When this
  happens, you may claim both territories by placing
  a chip in each one.
• You must play one (and only one) Domino on each
  turn. You may not pass your turn. You may not
  play an extra domino after conquering territory.

Winning
When all of the Dominoes have been played,
the game is over. Everybody count the number
of territories you claimed. If you dominate the
Game Board, you win!
