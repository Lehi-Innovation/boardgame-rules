## a-feast-for-odin — MAJOR

**Verdict: MAJOR** — A concrete, repeated placement-legality error: the
summary tells players ore can go anywhere except the Banquet Table, but the
source explicitly forbids ore on houses (sheds/stone houses/long houses).
This is a scoring-relevant misstatement (ore fills negative-point gaps), not
flavor.

### Findings

1. **MAJOR — Ore-on-houses contradiction.**
   - Summary (Actions § Goods Tile Placement): "Ore and silver can fill gaps
     in placement areas. **Ore cannot be placed on the Banquet Table; silver
     coins can.**" Repeated in Player Reference: "Ore | Any placement area
     (ore cannot go on Banquet Table)."
   - Source (l.379–381): "The stone and long houses are special in that they
     allow you to place orange and red food tiles in them as well as the
     usual green and blue goods and silver coins, **but not ore**."
   - Source (l.746–747, "Placing Goods on the House Tiles"): "At any time,
     you may place orange farm goods, red animal products, green craft
     products, and blue luxury goods **as well as silver coins** on empty
     spaces of your houses." — ore is conspicuously absent from this
     enumerated list, confirming the exclusion is deliberate and total (not
     stone/long-house-only vs. shed — sheds take no goods tiles at all,
     "Sheds require wood and stone," l.377).
   - Ore IS confirmed usable on the home board (l.305–307, l.326–331) and
     exploration boards (l.732–738, "Placing Goods on the Exploration
     Boards... green craft products and blue luxury goods as well as silver
     coins and ore tokens"), so the source draws a real, load-bearing
     distinction between boards (ore allowed) and houses (ore forbidden)
     that the summary's "any placement area" phrasing erases in both
     places it appears.
   - Impact: a player following the summary would legally-per-summary place
     ore in a stone/long house to cover a negative-point space, changing
     that house's final score versus a correctly-played game.

### Verification quota
- **Victory/game-end, sentence-by-sentence:** confirmed exactly — "The game
  ends immediately after the Feast phase of the final round. You do not
  receive any bonus during the final round" (l.1552–1553); "The player whose
  possessions are worth the most wins the game... In case of a tie, there
  are multiple winners" (l.1562–1565).
- **Every scoring number** (l.1566–1584): ships 3/5/8, emigration 18/21,
  exploration 4–38, sheds/houses 8/10/17, sheep 2(3 pregnant)/cattle
  3(4 pregnant), English Crown +2, Thing Penalty −3 (l.625) — all confirmed
  verbatim against the summary's Scoring table.
- **5+ procedural claims:** all 12 phase names/order (l.410–659) match
  exactly; exploration board ship/Viking requirements (l.1298–1307) match
  exactly (Shetland/Faroe: any ship + 1 Viking; Iceland/Greenland/Bear
  Island: knarr/longship + 2; American continent: longship + 3); Overseas
  Trading cost (1 silver + 1 knarr, l.980–987) matches; start-player
  determination (l.517–520) matches.
- **Term of art:** no control/majority mechanic in this game; "placement
  area" is the term in question and is exactly where the finding above
  lives — the source uses one term for two different rule sets (boards vs.
  houses) and the summary collapses them.
- **Exhaustion:** weapon deck reshuffle confirmed verbatim — "When the draw
  pile runs out of cards, shuffle the discard pile and form a new face down
  draw pile" (l.1548–1549) matches summary's Weapon Cards note exactly.

## frosthaven — PASS (independent re-audit of a prior MAJOR)

**Verdict: PASS.** The banner previously flagged six errors (Impair
definition, summon timing, battle-goal count, Ward duration, end-of-turn
looting mechanics, condition-removal timing). All six were re-checked from
scratch against the 230KB source and are now correct; a further independent
pass covering scenario-level math, attack resolution, and monster AI found
no additional issues.

### Re-verification of prior findings
1. **Impair.** Summary: "the character cannot use or trigger any items
   (bonuses already gained from items stay active)." Source (l.1992–1995):
   "Impair can only be gained by characters. The character cannot use or
   trigger any items, but bonuses previously gained from items are still
   active." Confirmed fixed.
2. **Summon timing.** Summary: "A character summon's turn in the initiative
   order is always directly before their summoner's turn." Source
   (l.2176–2178): "A character summon's turn in the initiative order is
   always directly before their summoner and is separate from the
   summoner's turn." Confirmed fixed.
3. **Battle goals.** Summary: "Deal three battle goals to each character;
   each secretly keeps one." Source (l.476): "Deal three battle goal cards
   to each character." Confirmed fixed (this game uses 3, not Gloomhaven's
   2 — no cross-contamination).
4. **Ward.** Summary: "the next time the figure suffers damage... it
   suffers half that amount... and ward is then removed (single use, not
   ongoing)." Source (l.1935–1938): "The next time the figure suffers
   damage from any source, they instead suffer half that amount of damage
   (rounded down), and ward is then removed." Confirmed fixed.
5. **End-of-turn looting.** Summary: "they must loot any loot tokens or
   treasure tiles in their hex (end-of-turn looting — characters only)."
   Source has a dedicated, unconditional section titled "End-of-Turn
   Looting" (l.2591–2599): "Characters must loot any loot tokens or
   treasure tiles present in their hex at the end of their turn. No figures
   besides characters perform end-of-turn looting." This is confirmed as a
   real, separate, mandatory mechanic distinct from the range-based "Loot X"
   ability (l.2244–2249) — the summary's description of both mechanics is
   accurate.
6. **Condition removal timing.** Summary: "Conditions are NOT removed at
   end of round — timed conditions expire at the end of the affected
   figure's next turn." Source's End of Round step list (l.3157–3172) has
   five steps and none of them removes conditions; the general Conditions
   rule (l.1908–1911) states removal is tied to "the end of the figure's
   next turn," never to round end. Confirmed fixed.

### Verification quota
- **Victory/game-end:** "The scenario is lost if all characters become
  exhausted or some other loss condition... is completed if the specific
  goal... is achieved... play continues until the current round is over...
  If the scenario would be both lost and completed in the same round, the
  scenario is lost" (l.3236–3244) — matches summary exactly.
- **Scoring (no VP, but all numeric thresholds):** trap damage 2+L
  (l.985–987), hazardous terrain 1+⌈L/3⌉ (l.990–994), scenario level formula
  avg÷2 rounded up / solo variant (avg+1)÷2 (l.1142–1149, 4630–4641) — all
  confirmed exact, including the solo-mode worked example matching the
  summary's implied formula.
- **5+ procedural claims:** Attack Modification Order 4-step sequence
  (l.1683–1694) matches; Advantage/Disadvantage rolling-modifier chain and
  ambiguity tiebreak (l.1825–1852) matches; item loadout limits

- **Exhaustion:** two-way character exhaustion (0 HP, or <2 hand AND <2
