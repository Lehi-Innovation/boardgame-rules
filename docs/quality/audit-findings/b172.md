## blood-on-the-clocktower — MAJOR

**Verdict: MAJOR** — The Character Distribution by Player Count table (Townsfolk /
Outsiders / Minions / Demon counts for 5-15 players) appears twice in the summary
(Setup step 6, and again in Player Reference) with fully confident, specific numbers,
but this table has **zero support** anywhere in the 2499-line extracted rulebook text.
The source explicitly defers this exact information to a separate physical "Setup
sheet" / "Traveler sheet" component that is not part of the extracted rulebook text.
Getting these counts wrong would mean setting up the wrong number of good/evil
characters for every single game run from this summary — this is foundational to the
entire game, not a flavor detail.

### Findings

1. **MAJOR — Character Distribution table has no source support.**
   - Summary (Setup step 6 and Player Reference, identical table both places): "| Players
     | Townsfolk | Outsiders | Minions | Demon | | 5 | 3 | 0 | 1 | 1 | | 6 | 3 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
     ... | 15 | 9 | 2 | 3 | 1 |"
   - Source: "Secretly choose characters. Take all of the Townsfolk character tokens out
     of the chosen edition box, and choose the appropriate number for the number of
     players, **as listed on the setup sheet**." (l.505-508). The glossary confirms this
     table lives on a separate physical component: "Setup sheet: The sheet that details
     what the Storyteller needs to do before beginning a game." and "Traveler Sheet: The
     sheet placed under the Town Square. **It lists how many Outsiders and Minions are in
     the current game** and what the Travelers' abilities are." (end-of-document
     glossary). No numeric player-count table appears anywhere in the extracted text.
   - No support found (searched: "outsider", every "N players"/"N player" occurrence,
     "distribution", "townsfolk", numeric lines near "minion"/"demon" — the closest hits
     are component/page-number lists, not a setup table). The rulebook's own text
     confirms the table is off-page (on the physical Setup/Traveler sheet insert), so
     this specific 11-row, 4-column table was necessarily written from outside knowledge,
     not from this source.

## hansa-teutonica — MAJOR

leaving only the "Number of Bonus Markers" row labels. This is one of six scoring
categories used in every game; getting it wrong changes final scores and potentially
the winner.

1. **MAJOR — Bonus Marker prestige-point values have no source support.**

     collected bonus markers (whether used during the game or not) you gain prestige

     6 or 7 / 8 or 9 / 10 or more" (l.485-495) — the row labels for 2-3 through 10+ are
     present, but no "1" row and **no point-value column at all** follow; the very next
