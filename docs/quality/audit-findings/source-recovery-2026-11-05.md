# Specific source-recovery findings — 2026-11-05

## Death Stacks

The inventor’s [current rules](https://www.stevecobb.com/Death%20Stacks%20--%20game%20instructions.htm) permit moving any top portion of a controlled stack, with distance determined by the portion moved. They also require reducing a controlled stack taller than four at the start of the turn (explicitly dated 2005), and specify six two-checker starting stacks. The summary omits partial-stack moves and the mandatory reduction, and leaves the starting stacks unspecified. These omissions change legal play. Original HTML and selected text are preserved by the recovery inventory. Verdict: MAJOR, specific findings only; no full audit and no repair.

## Fields of Action

The [Edward Jackman rules account hosted at Utrecht](https://webspace.science.uu.nl/~bodla101/d.gam/linesofaction.html) states that pieces may jump other pieces, isolated pieces have a special move, and five consecutive-numbered enemy pieces may be captured in any order. Immobilizing the opponent also wins. The [encyclopedic account](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fields_of_Action) independently agrees on the isolated move and both victory conditions. The summary prohibits jumping/isolated movement, requires captures in ascending order and prescribes passing when immobile. Verdict: MAJOR, specific findings only; no full audit and no repair.

## Pentominoes

The [Bhat/Fletcher university reference](https://www.cs.brandeis.edu/~storer/JimPuzzles/PACK/Pentominoes/LINKS/PentominoesBhatFletcher.pdf), pages 2–3, describes choosing from the unused pentominoes and winning by making the last legal placement. [Golomb's December 2007 IEEE puzzle column](https://www.itsoc.org/sites/default/files/2021-03/itNL1207.pdf), page 6, independently confirms the shared unused set and first-player-unable-to-place loss. The summary instead divides the set into six pieces each, permits passing and counts pieces played to select a winner. Those are concrete conflicts with the named competitive game, distinct from its solitaire tiling puzzles. Verdict: MAJOR, specific findings only; no full audit and no repair.

## Clomping Creatures

Correct guesses award the riddle and corresponding path cards; play ends when the riddle pile is exhausted. The summary invents creature movement, dice/movement cards and board encounters, omitting the actual tactile guessing turn. The original manual and its selected French chapter are retained in the recovery inventory. Verdict: MAJOR, specific findings only; no full audit and no repair.

## Giganten

The original French [Carlit Dinosaures/Giganten rules](https://regle.escaleajeux.fr/giga2_rg.pdf), printed pages 5–7 (PDF pages 6–8), show a square grid with normal orthogonal movement and normally optional challenges against adjacent enemies after moving. The summary specifies a hex grid and entering an occupied enemy hex to fight. Printed page 7 explicitly leaves identical, equal-strength dinosaurs in place after a draw; different-family equal-strength opponents both leave the board. The summary removes both on every tie. Those specific movement/combat conflicts are visible in the original scans, independently of imperfect table OCR. Verdict: MAJOR, specific findings only; no full audit and no repair.

## Composio

The original [Composio rules sheet](https://regle.escaleajeux.fr/compo_rg.pdf), PDF page 2, requires revealing one letter while keeping the full word private until the round ends. Repeated letters are legal (the worked example reveals i, i, q, o). The final surviving player scores the full word length if valid, or loses one point per stone for an invalid final word; no other player scores that round. The summary instead announces every word, requires new letters and substitutes survival/letters-added scoring. The original sheet was visually checked; it specifies twelve rounds twice despite OCR misreading one occurrence. Verdict: MAJOR, specific findings only; no full audit and no repair.
