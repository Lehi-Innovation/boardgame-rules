Goal
Find matching pairs of Elemons to catch them. Once you have three of a kind you can summon a Gigamon. The first player to summon 3 Gigamons is the winner.
Components
42 Elemon Tiles (6 of each kind),
7 Gigamon Figures,
3 Rock Tokens,
4 Reference Cards,
Illustrated Rules.
Setting up
Put the Gigamons and the Rock tokens aside.
Mix the Elemon tiles face down and make a 3 by 3 square!
The remaining tiles form the draw pile.
The youngest player goes first, then the game goes clockwise.
How to play?
Each turn, a player turns over 2 Elemon tiles, one at a time.
If the Elemons are different, flip them down again.
If the Elemons match, take the tiles and place them face up in front of you...
(See
Elemon Powers
)
...and replace the missing tiles with face down tiles from the drawing pile.
Endgame
When you have 3 identical Elemons, discard them and claim the corresponding Gigamon. If a player already has it, you can steal it from him!
If you have 3 Gigamons, you win the game!
If the draw pile is empty, play until there are no more matches.
The winner is the one with the most Gigamons. In case of a tie, the one with the most Elemons wins.
Elemon Powers
When 2 Elemons match, you can trigger the Elemon's power.
Polymon
is everyone's buddy. He always makes a match with the other Elemons. The other Elemon's power doesn't trigger.
Floramon
is as nice as can be. She will give you a tile from the drawing pile.
Pyromon
is as feisty little rascal. He burns and destroys another player's Elemon. YOU choose!
Hydromon
can see the future. Everyone else closes their eyes while you look at 4 of the tiles in play.
Geomon
is strong as a rock. He puts a rock on a tile which can't be touched. Remove the rock at the beginning of your next turn!
Aeromon
is free as a bird. He will swap one of your Elemons with another player's Elemon. YOU choose!
Electromon
is as quick as lightning. He makes you play again.
