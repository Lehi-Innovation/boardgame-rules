1 or More Players

Contents:

* 25 Geometric Shapes

* Game Unit with Timer and Pop-up Tray
* Label Sheet

Your Object:
Be the quickest to fit all 25 shapes
into the matching holes in the tray.

GET READY!

1. First wipe the top of the game unit with a clean, dry
cloth and then carefully apply its label. HINT: To apply
label more easily, hold it by the corners and line up the
start/stop opening before positioning the rest of the
label. See Figure 1.

2. Then carefully twist each shape off the plastic runners
and discard runners.

GO!

To start the timer, move the switch to ““START” and
quickly start fitting the shapes into their matching holes.
See Figure 4.

* If you finish before the timer runs out,
quickly move the switch to “STOP” Your turn is then
ever, The number that the arrow is pointing to on the
dial is the next player's time to beat! (If you're playing
solo, try again to beat your own time!)

* If you do NOT finish before the timer runs
out, the tray will pop up—scattering the shapes on the
tray ALL OVER! Your turn is then over and the original
60 seconds remains the time to beat. See Figure 5,

The next player then gathers all 25 shapes and takes his
or her turn.

THE WINNER!

The player who matches all 25 shapes in the shortest
time wins.

NOTE TO PARENTS:

With Younger Children, try playing the game
without the timer so that the children get used te fitting
the shapes into the matching holes. After they‘ve

learned where the shapes go, then add the challenge of
the ticking timer.
