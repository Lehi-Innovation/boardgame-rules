PAPER-AND-PENCIL PROCEDURAL CORE
Before fighting, agree on the sort of duel. The page distinguishes friendly duels,
serious first-blood duels and more lethal contests; do not infer one universal ending.
Players first choose movements privately from the printed five-choice movement system.
Reveal those choices and consult the Movement Effects Table. This determines attacks
available in the round, the movement outcome and resulting target opportunities.
Next choose the target for the first attack and whether it is a cut or thrust.
Reveal attack/defence choices and determine the applicable die-roll modifiers from
combatant Cut/Thrust totals and target selection. Use the Combat Matrix for that attack
against that defence. The modified percentile roll must equal or exceed its number;
a lower result misses. After a hit, consult the wound procedure for type and effects.
If attacks remain in the round, return to the attack step.
