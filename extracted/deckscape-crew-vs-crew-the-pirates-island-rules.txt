DECKSCAPE CREW VS CREW: THE PIRATES' ISLAND — EDITION-SPECIFIC CORE REFERENCE
English editorial facts from What's Eric Playing?, November28,2022, and Dale Yu,
The Opinionated Gamers, May26,2021. Both complete substantive reviews were read.
These are accounts of the competitive pirate box, not a generic cooperative demo.
Original English card2, private cards15/16, Victory9 and publisher back cover support identity.
This is NOT a complete instruction-card set, puzzle deck, minigame rules or final tie procedure.

ERIC'S COVERED PROCEDURES
Make two crews of approximately equal size/ability. Follow the deck's instructions in order.
The smaller crew can receive a balancing card worth four starting coins.
For each challenge, crews receive information and solve in real time. The first crew ready
calls STOP and checks its answer. Correct: that crew receives the Victory card. Incorrect:
the opposing crew receives it. If both agree to skip an unsolved puzzle, neither receives it.
Complete the deck, then compare coin totals: the crew with more coins wins.
The report's playtime estimates and preferred player counts are not scoring rules.

DALE'S INDEPENDENT COVERED PROCEDURES
The crews are red and blue. Each receives a private color-coded information card before
the shared challenge is read. The reward is the next deck card; coin value on its reverse
is learned after award. His group agreed not to work ahead; that is a HOUSE CONVENTION.
It must not be confused with an original enforced timing or reveal-order rule.
