Goth — limited original McNutty core movement/tombstone/special-space reference

GOTH: THE GAME OF HORROR TRIVIA — LIMITED CORE REFERENCE; ENGLISH
Editorial facts from William F. Billy-B's complete substantive Puzz.com review.
Publication date is not established. Original McNutty2002box back corroborates identity.
This is NOT the full manual: blood-pool effects, Full Moon resolution, movement-direction
restrictions, category selection and complete turn continuation remain unavailable.

The review describes two to four players or teams and four graveyards. Each begins at
the blood pool beside its graveyard. Roll a die each turn and move around the cemetery.
Each correctly answered question supplies one tombstone to the answering side's graveyard.
The first side to accumulate thirteen tombstones wins. A DEAD space costs a turn;
GRAVEROB permits stealing a tombstone from another side. Blood-pool spaces also have
effects, but the review does not explain them; this reference does not invent those effects.
It reports200cards with five questions per side, giving2000questions.
The writer says he played with several groups. His suggested seven-tombstone shortening
is a HOUSE VARIANT, separate from the stated thirteen-tombstone standard.

The original box back also prints correct answer = one gravestone and thirteen to win.
No manual interior or complete card set is recovered. No MAJOR/PASS/full audit or body repair.
