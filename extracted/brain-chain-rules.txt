INSTRUCTIONS

OBJECT OF THE GAME

The object of Brain Chain is to be the first player or team to form a chain of at least six “links” of the same color
in a row horizontally, vertically or diagonally.

GAME COMPONENTS
Brain Chain consists of a uniquely-designed interactive game board, two boxes of questions and answers, a deck
of movement cards, Brain Pills, a playing pawn, three sets of colored chips called “links,” and these instructions.

The game board. The board consists of two separate playing areas: the Exterior Track and the Category Grid. The
Category Grid is the white area in the center of the game board. Each square in the Category Grid represents a
subject area for questions, such as Entertainment, History, Geography, Science, etc. The Exterior Track is the
red track that runs around the outside of the Category Grid and includes the four yellow corner squares. The
playing pawn is moved clockwise around the Exterior Track and may never be placed in the Category Grid.
Links may only be played on the Category Grid and may never be placed on the Exterior Track.

Movement Cards and Brain Pills. Movement cards are played to move the pawn clockwise around the Exterior
Track from one to five spaces. There are also four movement cards that allow a player to advance the pawn
clockwise to the next yellow corner. Brain Pills may be used with a movement card to move additional spaces.
Each Brain Pill allows the pawn to be moved one additional space. A player must play one movement
card on each turn. A player does not have to play any Brain Pills, but may play as many as desired on each turn.

GAME PREPARATION AND SET-UP

Choosing Teams. ‘Iwo or three players can play individually. If there are more than three players, they should be
divided evenly into two teams. If the players cannot be divided into two even teams, one team may have an extra
player. The players should sit so that they are physically alternated around the game board.

Game Set-up. Each team selects a link color and a box of questions. The dealer gives each player four movement
cards and one Brain Pill. If one team has an extra player, that player does not receive any movement cards or a
Brain Pill, and may only help answer questions for the team. The remaining movement cards and Brain Pills
should be placed where indicated on the board. Place the playing pawn on the yellow start/move link square on
the Exterior Track.

HOW TO PLAY

The player to the left of the dealer begins the game. To begin each turn, a player must play one movement card
and as many Brain Pills as the player wishes, and move the pawn clockwise around the Exterior Track. All
players move the same playing pawn. Unless the pawn lands on a yellow corner space, the player picks a
category from the Category Grid that is in the same horizontal row or vertical column as the pawn (not
diagonally), and not already occupied by a link. The other team will then read the question from that category.
The player may consult his/her team, but only the player whose turn it is may announce the “final answer.” If
answered correctly, that player places a link on the chosen square in the Category Grid and takes a Brain
Pill. If answered incorrectly, the player does not place a link or take a Brain Pill. Whether or not the question
was answered correctly, the player draws a movement card so that the player is holding four at the end of the
turn. The player’s turn is then over and play proceeds clockwise.

If the playing pawn lands on a yellow corner space instead of a red space, the player should follow the directions
on that space. After following the directions, the player takes a movement card so that the player is holding four.
The player’s turn is then over. The player does not receive a Brain Pill for following the instructions on the
corner spaces, except for the “Wild Spot.” If the player answers the “Wild Spot” question correctly, then the
player receives a Brain Pill and places a link on the Category Grid.

If a player forgets to take a movement card at the end of that player’s turn, he/she must play with one less card
until that player’s next turn. At the end of that player’s next turn, he/she may pick up as many as needed so that
the player has four movement cards. If a player forgets to draw a Brain Pill after answering a question correctly,
that player will lose the Brain Pill forever once the next player begins his/her turn!

Other than discussing the answer to a question, no “table talk” is allowed between team members. Players may

not coach each other while deciding where to move or which category question to choose. If table talk occurs,
the opposing team may immediately remove one link of their choosing.

WINNING THE GAME

To win the game a player/team must form a Brain Chain of six links in a row vertically, horizontally or
diagonally on the Category Grid. Note: The playing pawn is considered a link during a player’s turn.
Therefore, a Brain Chain may be formed by connecting five links in a row vertically, horizontally or diagonally on
the Category Grid and moving the pawn to an exterior square adjacent to the five links to form six in a row. An
opponent does not form a Brain Chain by connecting the pawn to five links in a row of another color.

QUESTION CATEGORIES

Business, Entertainment, Geography, History, and Sports questions all deal with the subject matter indicated.
The Science category may include questions about nature, mathematics, inventions, ecology or space exploration.
Potluck questions may deal with any subject. Player’s Choice means the player may choose from any of the eight
categories, while Opponent’s Choice allows the opponents to discuss which category they should give the player.
They must decide on a category, however, before drawing a question card and reading it to the player.

Oddball (ODD) questions are unique. They consist of four alternatives; three of them possessing some positive
trait in common. The fourth one does not connect with the others. The answer identifies the oddball and
provides a brief explanation of the positive connection between the similar three. Choosing the correct answer
allows the player to gain a link in the category square. Unlike the other categories, the player must also correctly
state the connection between the three common items in order to gain a Brain Pill.

OPTIONAL TIMED ANSWER RULE
Many players enjoy listening to teams think through their answers. However, if everyone agrees a one-minute or
ninety second time limit can be set for a team to answer its questions. This keeps the game moving along.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
May I just play Brain Pills without playing a movement card? No. Each player must play a movement card
on each turn. Likewise, a player may not play more than one movement card per turn.



May I move the playing pawn in any direction? No. The playing pawn may only be moved clockwise.

If the pawn lands on the corner square marked “move link,” may I move my own link? Yes. You may
move one link of any color to any other unoccupied square.

May I choose a category that is diagonal from the playing pawn? No. While a Brain Chain may be formed
diagonally, the category can only be chosen from those that are in the same horizontal row or vertical column as
the playing pawn. If the pawn is on a corner space, you do not “choose diagonally” but simply follow the
instructions in the gold corner square. If the playing pawn is moved to the corner square marked “Wild Spot,”
any unoccupied square may be chosen.

Do I get an additional turn if I answer a question correctly? No. The advantage of answering a question
correctly is receiving a link in the square attempted and a Brain Pill. Your turn is then over.

May I purchase additional question cards? Absolutely! Simply visit our website for all Brain Chain products.
