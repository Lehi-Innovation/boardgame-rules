Equipment
A 5x5 square player board.
The players share a single die to roll on their turns.
Each player also has a set of six, six sided dice (stones) that they use as playing pieces. These dice are never rolled, the number of pips on top serve to identify each one.
Setup
Player 1 sets up their stones in one corner of the board arranged to their choosing and Player 2 does the same in the opposite corner.
Turn
On your turn, roll the single, shared die. If your stone matching that number is still in play, you must move it, even if the only move captures one of your own pieces.
Moves can be orthogonal or diagonal.
If your stone that matches the rolled number has already been removed from play, you may choose either your stone with the next higher or the next lower number to move instead.
Winning
To win the game a player must occupy the corner field of the opponent with a playing piece.
A player loses if he has no more playing pieces left on the board.
