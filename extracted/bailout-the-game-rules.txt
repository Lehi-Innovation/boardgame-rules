The Game

When you lose, you win!!

For 2 to 6 players / Ages 14 and up
Rules © 2009, RJS Architects, Inc.
Printed in the U.S.A

CONTENTS

* | Money Trail to BAILOUT Bank board * 6 pawns
* | die * | deck of Frantic May and Frivolous Mac
cards containing 25 cards *1 deck of Sales and
Acquisition cards containing 25 cards

* 3 denominations of assets (money printed with black
ink) 50 of each kind * 3 denominations of debt (money
printed with red ink) 50 of each kind * 6 stock
certificates | per bank (player) * 24 T-bills.

GOAL

To be the player (bank) that acquires the most amount
of debt when all players have reached BAILOUT
Bank.

GAME SET UP

To start the game read all of the directions. Pick a
player to be the BAILOUT Banker. The BAILOUT
Banker is responsible for handing out all assets, debt,
and T-Bills during the game. To begin the game the
BAILOUT Banker gives each player $2 billion $500
million dollars in assets (money printed with black
ink) in these denominations: 4 - $500 million dollar
bills, 5 - $100 million dollar bills. Each player is a
bank during the game. Players pick from one of the
following banks: Bankruptcy O' America (red),

Washed Up Mutual (blue), Worth Farless (yellow),
Greedy Investors of America (black), Liquidation
Brothers (green) and No Cashvia (white). Each
player takes the pawn that matches the bank they have
chosen and places it on the matching color building
along BAILOUT Blvd. Players also take the
matching Bank Stock Certificate to their bank.

RULES OF PLAY

The youngest player goes first and the rest of the
players follow in a clockwise order. The players roll
the die and move the indicated number of spaces on
the die. When a player lands on a space, they follow
the directions on that space. When a player lands on a
space that tells them to go back, they follow the
directions on the space they are instructed to go back
to. A player may occupy the same space as another
player.

BANK STOCK CERTIFICATES

Players have 1 Bank Stock Certificate worth 50% of
their total bank assets or debt at the end of the game.
Keep all Bank Stock Certificates visible to other
players during game the play. See Hostile Takeover
Space for Bank Stock Certificate transactions. See
Winning the Game for how to cash in your Bank Stock
Certificates.

MONEY

Every player starts off with assets (money printed with
black ink). As you play the game every player will go
from having assets to having debt (money printed with
red ink). Example of money changing: Player has $200
million in assets. Player lands on lose $450 Million.
Player turns in $200 million in assets to the
BAILOUT banker and receives $250 million in debt
from the BAILOUT banker. Equation: $200 million
(your assets) - $450 million (debt received) = $-250
million in debt.

When a player is required to give 50% of their assets
or debt to another player and an uneven amount arises
the player who must give the money away keeps the
larger amount. Example: Player One has $250 million
and must give 50% away. Player one keeps $150
million and gives Player Two $100 million.

All players must keep their money visible.

CARD SPACES

FRANTIC MAY AND
FRIVOLOUS MAC
SPACES AND CARDS

A player picks up the top card every time they pass
over or land on this space; even if they land on lose a
turn. All of the cards contain losses from bad CDO's
(collateralized debt obligations) and bad MBS's
(mortgage backed securities). These are mortgages
that were grouped together and sold to your bank.
When a player passes over this space, the player must
first follow the instructions on the space they have
landed. Once the player has completed the space's
instructions, the player may then read the Frantic May
and Frivolous Mac card.

There is a section on these cards called Advice from a
great American. This section does not affect the
game play! This section is made up of interesting
quotes that are relevant to the context of the game.

Alternate Play Option for Advice from a great
American:

The player who picks up the card picks an opponent.
The card holder reads the quote. If the opponent is able
to identify the author of the quote, they lose $100
million. If they fail to identify the author of the quote,
the card holder loses $100 million.

SALES AND ACQUISITIONS

SPACES AND CARDS

When a player lands on this space they select the top
card and look at it, keeping it hidden from all other
players. The player then selects an opponent. The
opponent selects the next card. The player who
landed on the space gets to decide if they will keep
their card or trade it with their opponent. Once the
cards are kept or traded, both players may look at their
cards. Then the player who landed on the space
decides who will play their card first. The player and
their opponent may now play their cards. The player
that landed on the space and the opponent they chose
do not have to play each other after the cards have
been traded. Meaning, the player and the opponent
may select any other player to use their card against.

There is strategy involved. It is important to pay
attention to all of the other players assets or debt

during the game.

SPECIAL SPACES

BAILOUT BLVD

The bank buildings are on BAILOUT Blvd. To start
the game every player puts their pawn on the bank
building they have selected.

STOCK MARKET

When a player lands on this space they must play the
stock market. The player rolls the die again. If the roll
is an odd number, gain $100 million times roll in
assets. Ifthe roll is an even number, lose $100
million times the roll in debt.

T-BILLS

When a player lands on this space, they receive one
T-Bill from the BAILOUT Banker. Near the end of
the game, the players will reach the Stop T-Bills
mature space and find out the worth of their T-Bills.
See T-Bills Mature Stop Space.

SPECIAL SPACES CONTINUED



BANK HOLIDAY

When a player lands on this space they lose all of their
assets or debt. The player hands over all of their assets
or debt to the BAILOUT banker. This space only
refers to asset or debt and does not apply to T-bills or
Bank Stock Certificates.

HOSTILE TAKEOVER

When a player lands on this space they take from the
player of their choice a Bank Stock Certificate. This
Bank Stock Certificate is worth 50% of the bank's
assets or debt at the end of the game. A player may
take only | Bank Stock Certificate during their turn. A
player may take a Bank Stock Certificate from a
player even if that player is not the original owner of
the Bank Stock Certificate.

There is strategy involved. It is important to pay
attention to all of the other players assets or debt
during the game.

BAILOUT BANK

The first player to get to BAILOUT Bank loses $1
billion dollars. When all players reach Bailout Bank
the winner of the game can be determined.

STOP SPACES

There are two stop spaces on the board. No matter
what a player rolls, they must stop and follow the
directions on that stop space.

BANK STRESS TEST

When a player lands on this space, they must announce
to every other player their company's total current
assets or debt.

T-BILLS MATURE

When a player lands on this space, they must roll the
die again and multiply the number on the die by 100
million and then multiply that by the number of
T-Bills owned to determine the total amount of assets
they gain.

WINNING THE GAME

The game continues until every player reaches
BAILOUT Bank. Every player remains in play until
the last player reaches BAILOUT Bank. Meaning a
player that has reached BAILOUT Bank can be the
opponent of another player landing on the Sales and
Acquisitions Space or their Bank Stock Certificate
may be taken when another player lands on the Hostile
Takeover Space.

Once every player has reached BAILOUT Bank; any
player who has their Bank Stock Certificate owned by
another player must give 50% of their debt or assets to
that player. Ifa player owns their own Bank Stock
Certificate they keep all their assets or debt. If another
player owns your Bank Stock Certificate, divide your
money into 2 equal piles and give them one of the
piles. If you own a Bank Stock Certificate other than
your own and another player owns yours, all players
involved divide their assets or debt into 2 equal piles.
Each player then gives one of the piles to the player
who owns their Bank Stock Certificate.

All exchanges of assets or debt occur at the same
time. DO NOT take 50% of another player's assets or
debt, add it to your own then divide it equally to give
to the owner of your stock certificate.

Once all players have given and received assets or debt
from the Bank Stock Certificates they own, every
player adds up their assets or debt.

The player with the most debt gets the BAILOUT!
and wins the game.
