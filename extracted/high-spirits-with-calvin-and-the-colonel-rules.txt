= | HIGH SPIRITS

with Calvin and the Colonel
A CARD GAME for 2, 3, or 4 players

“HIGH SPIRITS” is an easy-to-play card game which is full of surprises and
hilarity! In each deal, fifteen cards are placed face up into the slots in the playing
board. Players then select one of three bottom cards each turn as they try to

gather the most points of Calvin, Colonel, or Maggie-Belle cards. The value of
the cards changes each time a new deal is made for a total of four deals. The
“HIGH” cards give the points while the “SPIRIT” cards tell the players what to do,
sometimes for good, sometimes for bad, but always for high-spirited fun and

enjoyment.
THE OBJECT OF THE GAME is to be the player with the most money at the

end of the game.

of each type card at the end of each ‘Deal’.

2. The CARDS consist of 2 separate packs of 30 cards each. The ‘‘HIGH’’
cards are divided into 10 blue ‘‘Calvin’’ cards, 10 red ‘‘Colonel"’ cards, and 10
green “Maggie-Belle’’ cards, each with a point value for score. The ‘'SPIRIT"’
cards have no point value,.but rather contain instructions for the players. KEEP the
"HIGH" cards SEPARATE from the ‘SPIRIT’ cards. Shuffle each pack and place

VISUALLY CHECKED ORIGINAL BOARD PAYOUT TABLE
Amounts are paid for the highest and second-highest total in the named color, not paid per point. A negative amount is a loss. With two players there is no second-place payout; ties pay or charge both players the same amount, as the original directions specify.
Deal1: Colonel +30/+15; Maggie-Belle +20/+10; Calvin -20/-10.
Deal2: Calvin +60/+30; Colonel +40/+20; Maggie-Belle -40/-20.
Deal3: Maggie-Belle +90/+45; Calvin +60/+30; Colonel -60/-30.
Deal4: Colonel +120/+60; Maggie-Belle +80/+40; Calvin -80/-40.
