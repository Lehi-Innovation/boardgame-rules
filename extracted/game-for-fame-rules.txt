WHAT HAPPENS NEXT?

e Once the first team has completed their MONEY MAKER card
challenge, they place that card at the bottom of the pile.

e Play then passes clockwise to the next team.

e A player from that team now picks up the next MONEY MAKER card
and follows the instructions on the card.

e Once all teams have taken one turn, play goes back to the
first team.

e Make sure you take it in turns within your team to be the reader of
the MONEY MAKER cards.

Why take it in turns to be the reader? Lots of the MONEY MAKER card challenges

involve all team members, but the reader is often the most active player. Taking it
in turns to be the reader means that everyone gets a varied game.

e Once any one team reaches the $30 Million Red Carpet, play stops.

e The space your team has ended on tells you how much each
member of that team has earned.

e So if your team's pawn finishes on the $23 Million space, all players in
that team have earned $23 Million each. It's a good idea for each
player to write this figure down.

e Now nominate one player to read out all of the CREDITS that have
been placed on the BANK space during the game.

e Each CREDIT has a player's name and an amount written on it.
e Players should add each amount they are awarded to their total.

e So if a player's team ended on the $20 Million space and the player
was awarded 2 CREDITS as an individual, one worth $3 Million and

another worth $5 Million, that player would have a total of $28 Million.

e The player with the highest total earnings is the winner!

PREPARING TO PLAY

e Each team chooses one pawn from the box to represent
them and places it on the START space on the board.

e Take the sandtimer, notepad, pencils and CREDITS out of
the box so they are ready to use when you need them.

e You may also need a glass of water for each team
(players gargle at their own risk).

e Place one pile of MONEY MAKER cards on top of
the other to create one pile, then place them
face down on the board.

e Please don't shuffle the cards.

What happens if | shuffle the cards? The cards are in a specific

order fo ensure great gameplay whether you're playing in 2, 3 or 4 teams.

But if they get mixed up it’s no big deal - you'll still have fun. If you want to put them back in
the original order, they are numbered in the bottom right hand corner of each card.

Card | should be the first card you pick up (placed face down on the top of the pile) and
card 130 should be at the bottom of the pile.

LET’S GET STARTED

Gameplay is simple. Each team takes it in turns to pick up a
MONEY MAKER and follow the instructions printed on the card so...

e As reader of these instructions, your team will go first.

e A player from your team picks up the top MONEY MAKER card and
reads the challenge on the card out loud (unless stated otherwise).

e MONEY MAKER cards are self-explanatory - simply follow the
instructions on the card to complete the challenge.

e@ MONEY MAKER cards have various outcomes but their main purpose

is to either:
e Move your team around the board.

e Or earn CREDITS for individual players.

You don’t need any more info about the MONEY MAKERS before you
start playing - everything you need is on the cards themselves.

Go to WHAT HAPPENS NEXT? at the back of this instructions pamphlet if
you're happy to jump straight in.

If you would like more information on the different types of
MONEY MAKER cards before you get going, then keep reading.
