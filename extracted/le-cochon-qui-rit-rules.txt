Game Rules
Reassemble your pig first and you win!
At the start of the game,
the pigs must be completely taken apart. Roll a die in turn; the player who rolls the highest score starts the game.
To be allowed to take a pig body, the player must roll at least one 6 with the 3 dice. If so, the player may roll the dice again.
To be allowed to place attributes, it is enough to roll aces: 1 ace for 1 leg, 1 ear or 1 eye. To place the tail, the player must roll 2 aces in the same roll.
Clarification: if the player obtained aces at the same time as his first 6, he may add elements. However, aces obtained before this first 6 do not count.
The same player keeps rolling the dice until he no longer gets any aces. He then passes the dice to the neighbouring player and waits for his turn.
The first player to have completely assembled his pig has won!
