Ants in the Pants Game Rules
Components
16 Ants
Pants
Suspenders
Cardboard dog
Instructions
Object of the Game
Have fun! Make your ants jump into the pants. Finish first and you'll be the winner!
Setup
Insert the suspenders into the dog as shown in Figure 1.
Insert the cardboard dog into the slots inside the pants. See Figure 2.
Attach the suspenders to the front and back of the pants. It's a snap! See Figure 3.
Place the pants in the middle of a table or on the floor.
Choose a set of 4 matching color ants. Each player chooses a different color set: red, yellow, green, or blue. Place your ants in front of you and you're ready to play. All players do the same.
Game Play
One player calls out "Go" and all of you try to make your ants jump into the pants. Here's how: Press your finger down on the tail of the ant.
As your finger slips off, your ant will jump up and, hopefully, into the pants. Try to bounce the ants off either side of the dog and into the pants.
End of the Game
If you're first to get all 4 of your ants into the pants, you win the game!
