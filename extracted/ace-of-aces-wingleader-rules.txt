Ace of Aces Wingleader — WWII lookup/bank reference and complete original ammo errata

ACE OF ACES: WINGLEADER — WWII BOOK-LOOKUP/BANK/AMMO REFERENCE; ENGLISH
Rick Martin, ArmchairGeneral, March9,2021, complete substantive review read.
JosephJ.ScoleriIII,1999collector guide, complete Wingleader and expansion sections read.
EDITORIAL FACT EXTRACTION plus complete image-checked errata transcription below.
NOT the full Nova1988rulebook, gamebook pair, complete aircraft set or starting-page table.
No WWIHandyRotary/Powerhouse,JetEagles,LostWorlds or Chessex expansion substituted.
Review has a level-name slip: defaultP51/FW190is once called basic, then later explicitly
distinguished as introductory from the all-aircraft basic game. Collector confirms Introductory,
Basic and Optional structure; this reference preserves that distinction without inventing intermediate rules.
Component counts differ: review says24double-sidedcards; collector says12double-sidedcards
for24aircraft, with12morecards/24moreaircraft in Chessex expansion. Counts not silently harmonized.

Two books show P51andFW190cockpit views. Choose a pictured maneuver on the current page,
announce its page number, turn to the opponent's announced page, then use the original chosen
maneuver's entry there to find the final page. Both players reach the same final page.
A firing picture indicates a possible shot; damage depends on the chosen aircraft/range.
Accumulated damage equal to or exceeding hull capacity destroys the aircraft.
Introductory play uses the two default planes; reaching223ends combat as contact is lost.
Basic play uses aircraft cards for24aircraft. Five bank states range90degreesleft,45left,
level,45right,90right. Cards impose aircraft-specific maneuver formulas, sometimes multiple
page transitions/commitment; ending maneuver determines the next bank state, marked by a token.
Basic setup secretly chooses bank states and cross-references a StartingPageTable not recovered here.
At223in basic play, players secretly choose to leave or search; full reconnection procedure absent.
Speed and altitude are optional. Complete flight formulas, gun ranges/damage values, recovery,
search resolution and all tailing/optional exceptions must be read in the original components.
The guide says WWIcritical-hit rules are absent; wind/gun-jam rules in the local summary are not certified.

Wingleader Errata
Axis Book: Page144is not a Tailing page. The Allied player does not give a clue on this page.
Allied Book: Page143is not a Shot page. Ignore the "F" on this page.
Ammo Rule: Every time you shoot, subtract one point from the ammo number on your Aircraft Card.
When your ammo number is zero, you may not shoot. Your ammo number may never be less than zero.
(You are not required to shoot just because you are on a Shot page.)
Optional Rules:
Starting altitude: When playing using altitude you may start the game at either
(A)both players start at10,000' or(B)each player secretly chooses a starting altitude
at1,000'increments between5,000'and20,000'.
P-38 Aircraft Card: The damage points for the P-38should be26not36.

Tiny original FW190card cells are not reconstructed; actual card/gamebook photos support identity only.
