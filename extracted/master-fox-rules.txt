35 Goal of the Gane 2&

Master Fox, that famous vulpine fellow, wants to take a vacation
from his domain of Maupertuis for a while. Before he leaves, he
must choose a replacement from among the most intrepid foxes
in the forest.

To prove your worth, capture loot from the neighboring farm, while
completing missions that the illustrious sly fox sets for you.

The first to collect 10 Egg tokens wins and becomes the new Master
Fox. For experienced players, the goal is 15 Egg tokens.

¢ Ruse Pawns

Ruse pawns add tricks to the hunt. They come into play gradually.
You may have only one Ruse pawn in your sack each round. If you
have more than one in your sack, none of your Ruse pawns have
any effect.

e Adding Ruse Pawns
Begin adding Ruse pawns on the second round. Added Ruse pawns
stay in play until the end of the game.

BsEnd of the Gamers

The game ends when one player collects at least 10 eggs (or 15
for experienced players). Then, the player who collected the most
eggs wins the game. In case of a tie, the youngest tied player wins.
