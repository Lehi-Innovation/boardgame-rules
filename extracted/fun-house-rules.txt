Fun House — original Pressman core and selected skill instructions in original photos

FUN HOUSE — ORIGINAL PRESSMAN1988 CORE / SELECTED SKILL REFERENCE; ENGLISH
Editorial procedural fact extraction from original instruction photographs.
Main source: complete photographed instruction front, public replacement listing photo2.
Companions: original reverse photo3; clearer BGG front/board photo; original board/copyright photos.
The photographed instructions show both sides, but this SELECTED REFERENCE does not certify
every tiny reverse-side paragraph. Amazing Maze, Pocket Pool and Super Silly Skill Ball fine
print is not completely transcribed; no missing words or numerical rules are reconstructed.
Consult original photos for the printed diagrams, assembly and remaining skill instructions.
No complete physical question/answer strip set or playable component set is recovered here.
This is rules_reference_images, not a full manually verified rulebook transcription.

IDENTITY AND COMPONENTS
Pressman original1988 television-show Fun House, BGG16982. The board copyright close-up
explicitly identifies Pressman1988. The local summary's Milton Bradley publisher is wrong.
The printed front lists board, movers, eight physical Fun House pieces, rings, marbles,
bowling pins, question/answer strips, an answer-reveal slide and instructions.
No die/spinner or generic challenge-card/prize-token procedure appears in the core rules.
The twelve skill names visible on the reverse and board are Amazing Maze, Donut Toss,
Trashketball, Hot Shot, Pocket Pool, Bagel Roll, Boppin' Bowlin', Down the Tubes,
Super Silly Skill Ball, Hoopla, Hole-in-One and Looney Line-up.

SETUP
Play is always by teams. With2or3people, each player is a team. With4people, make two
teams of two. Apply the supplied labels to the indicated game pieces/box; each team chooses
a mover and places it at Start. Put the physical game pieces within easy reach off the board.
Insert a question strip into the answer-reveal slide so that the top question is visible.
Do not raise it far enough to show the ANSWER below that question until it is answered.

PLAYING, SCORING AND MOVEMENT — main front read in full
The team containing the youngest player starts. It chooses one of the twelve skill games.
One member of that team plays it, then one player from EACH OTHER TEAM plays that same game.
After everyone has played, each team moves its own mover according to that particular
skill game's rules. The selecting team's turn therefore can move every team's piece.
Selection then passes clockwise to the next team. Someone on that team announces the
game named by the space where ITS team's mover currently stands. One player from every
team plays that game, and each team moves clockwise according to its score/result.
If a selecting team is still at Start, it may choose any skill game for that round.
With two-person teams, alternate teammates so everyone participates in the skill games.

QUESTION SPACES
When a team lands on a Question Space, someone on an opposing team reads a question aloud.
After the answer, anyone may raise the strip until the answer is visible in the slide.
Correct answer: move the answering team's mover forward2spaces.
Incorrect answer: that mover stays on the question space; no backward movement is printed.

ENDING AND TIES — complete paragraph and following tie example checked in listing photo2
The first team to reach the finish line wins. If multiple teams reach or pass Finish in the
SAME ROUND, the team with the highest score in the LAST SKILL GAME PLAYED wins.
If multiple teams have that same score, the one crossing the line by the most spaces wins.
Exact count is not required to reach Finish. There is no printed two-circuit requirement.
This is not a cumulative-points victory game and the tie is not decided by prize tokens.
In skill games, tied scores receive the same movement. The printed example gives scores
8,8,3: both8s get the highest-score movement3, and the other team gets next-highest2.

SELECTED CLEARLY READABLE REVERSE PROCEDURES — not all twelve games certified
Hot Shot: use the Crazy Catapult about6inches from the face-up Skill Ball Target.
Each team shoots three marbles. Highest total moves3spaces, next highest2, third highest1.
Donut Toss: use the Backboard's Amazing Maze side; toss rings with the Crazy Catapult
roughly36inches away. Each team gets three rings; move one space per ring landing on a post.
Down the Tubes: assemble the Goofy Golf Club/Amazing Maze/High Roller Ramp/Funnel course
as pictured. Each team gets three goes; a marble going through the funnel counts1point.
Highest total moves3spaces, next highest2, third highest1. Once the course is completely
set up and tested, do not move its parts until all teams have completed their turns.
Hole-in-One: with the Backboard Hole-in-One side up, putt a marble from the pictured
starting divot toward the hole using the Goofy Golf Club. Each team gets five tries;
move as many spaces as successful holes.
Looney Line-up: use the same side and starting divot, trying to get each marble to stop
in the trough just below the hole. Each team has five tries and moves one space for each
marble in the trough. Leave captured marbles there until all five have been used.
