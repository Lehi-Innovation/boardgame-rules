ADVENTURER — YAQUINTO SETUP/ORDER/BLASTER REFERENCE; ENGLISH
AlanE.Paull, OpenBox, WhiteDwarf30, April/May1982, printed/PDF16–17.
Complete Adventurer review visually read:16column3bottom,17column1andcolumn2opening.
EDITORIAL FACT EXTRACTION of the covered original game rules, not a verbatim review/manual.
The adjacent Champions/ThievesWorld/InvasionEarth rules are excluded.
This is a NARROW REFERENCE, not complete setup, turn resolution or a playable game.
No original action/movement/attack/weapon/damage charts or scenario victory rules supplied.
No Swashbuckler-only pirate/musketeer chart is certified interchangeable with Adventurer.

The review identifies Yaquinto AlbumGame, ThomasO'Neill/GeorgePetronis, two square-grid
boards for a bar-room brawl and spaceship game. It describes101counters,twochartcards,
an ordersheetcard and twodice; other sources'111countercount not silently resolved.
Players normally control2or3characters. Racial type affects activities; strength,endurance
and expertise are rolled before play. The review gives no complete rolling distribution.
Characters are randomly positioned for the brawl and receive written orders in SIX-SEGMENT turns.
Characters navigate and manipulate increasing wreckage: tables/chairs/shelves can be shoved
or flipped; objects can be thrown and barrels rolled. Exact action codes/costs/tests are absent.
In the bar-room game, blasters ONLY STUN, for up toTWO TURNS; hand-to-hand attacks and
improvised objects remain important. Liquid can be poisonous to a different metabolism,
but the full poison test is not given. In the spaceship scenario, blasters are LETHAL,
LONG-RANGE weapons and the initial setup is more organized; the two scenarios differ.
Androidbartender/roboticbouncer and serving-wench rules are OPTIONAL, not required core actions.

Full original rules, exact order/movement/attack/injury/poison procedures, starting attributes,
scenario setup distributions and ending/score conditions remain missing. No MAJOR/PASS/full audit.
