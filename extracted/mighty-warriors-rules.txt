Below the cities of humanity, underneath the deepest drains and
sewers, mutant creatures rule a world of caverns and tunnels. The
worst of these creatures are Skaven— Chaos Ratmen whose secret
tunnel systems connect with the cellars and drains of humanity’s
proud cities. No-one suspects the extent of the Skaven’s crawling
evil They bring plague, fire and death to the prosperous cities of
mankind. In Mighty Warriors a band of Heroes, led by a Noble
Knight, Fearless Dwarf and Heroic Elf, enter a Castle that has been
overrun by the hideous Ratmen. The Heroes and the Skaven must
fight a battle for ultimate control of the Castle.

NUMBER OF PLAYERS
Mighty Warriors can be played by two to
four players. The sides are as follows:

2 Players — one player takes the Heroic
Forces, the other takes the Forces of
Chaos.

3 Players — one player takes the Heroic

Forces, and the other two players play-

the Forces of Chaos as a team.

4 Players — two players play the Heroic
Forces as a team, and the other two
play the Forces of Chaos as a team.

SETTING UP

The Mighty Warriors board system is
designed so that each time you play, the
Castle has a different layout. Always start
with the Great Hall. Place the other tiles
face down in a pile. The players take
turns to pick the top room and lay it on
the floor, linking it up to the next room
so the movement squares on each room
match up. When all the rooms are laid
out, fold the doors and put them into the
clip bases so they stand up. Take it in
turn to place the doors so they link
ot together, until there are no doors
elt.

THE FORCES OF CHAOS

The Forces of Chaos in the Castle are led
by the Chaos Wizard. The Forces of
Chaos are the evil Skaven — feared Chaos
Ratmen.

THE HEROES

The band of Heroes are led by 3
characters — the Noble Knight, the
Fearless Dwarf and the Heroic Elf. They
lead the band of Mighty Warriors into the
Castle.

Each player chooses a side and takes his
miniatures. You can find out which
miniatures go with which side by looking

Skaven’s shields.

The Heroic player takes the Heroic Army

and you've selected and assembled your

PLACING YOUR FORCES

Take it in turns to place your miniatures
one at a time in any room of the Castle,
until no rooms are empty. Once you
have placed a miniature in a room, your
Opponent may not put a miniature in it as

THE OBJECTIVE OF THE GAME
The objective of Mighty Warriors is to
capture your opponents shield before
your opponent captures yours.

GAME TURN SEQUENCE

Note that each side completes the full list
of actions before the other side can do
anything in response.

1. MAGIC

Only the Elf and the Chaos Sorcerer may
cast spells. Each has a Spell Scroll listing
the spells they know. To cast a spell,
drop 2 dice in the Combat Tray. If either
dice lands in a HIT square, choose one,
look up the number rolled on the Spell
Scroll. The spell’s effects can be applied
immediately, or after moving. If neither
dice lands in a HIT square, the spell fails.

2. MOVE -

Roll a dice.The number rolled is the
number of move points which you have
for that turn. Each of your models may
move a number of squares up to the
number rolled. Models can move in any
direction including diagonally. They may
not move through any square occupied
by another model of either side, or
through squares which are blocked

OPENING AND CLOSING DOORS
Your miniatures can open and close

3. COMBAT

The procedure for firing missile weapons
and fighting hand-to-hand is basically the
same. The differences are as follows:

All missile weapons must be fired before
any hand-to-hand fighting.

Missile weapons can be fired at any
opponent in the same room, but hand-

to-hand combat can only be conducted
against models in adjacent squares.

Check your Force card to see which of
your models can fire missiles and when. ~

Wherever you enter a square that
touches (even just on corners) a square
that is occupied by an enemy piece, then
you are in base to base contact and can
attack that miniature in hand-to-hand
combat

Hand to hand combat is resolved just
like shooting - look at your Forces roster
sheet and roll the correct number of
combat dice in the combat tray.

For each model making an attack
(whether by missile or hand-to-hand), do
the following:

1) Choose a target

2) Look at your Force card to see how
many dice to drop in the Combat Tray

3) Choose the highest number of any
dice landing in a HIT square. Look up
the number on the Combat Result
chart on your Force card to find the
effect on the target.

Killed models are removed from play.

When a Miniature is stunned, he has
been hit and knocked down. He still has
a chance to recover, as his armour has

- stopped the blow from doing any real

damage

--- Page 4 ---
Stunned models are placed on their
sides. They may be attacked as normal
but any further hits count as a Kill,
regardless of the number rolled

SPECIAL COMBAT RULES

Skaven are not as strong as men at arms,
but there are more of them. Also each
Skaven has a shield. The first time a
Skaven is attacked and either wounded
or killed, then he can take the blow on
his shield. Remove the shield from that
playing piece. Next time it is attacked ,
that Skaven has no shield to defend it
and is stunned or killed as usual. This
rule means that effectively each Skaven
has 2 chances to survive.

COMBAT EXAMPLE

The Heroic Elf shoots his bow at a
Skaven warrior, and so rolls 2 dice
in the combat tray.

One of the dice lands in a HIT
square while the other lands in a
MISS square. The dice that landed in
the HIT square is a 4.

The Heroic player now looks on his
Army List card, where he sees that a
Skaven is stunned on a 1,2,3 or 4,
and killed on a 5 or 6. The Elf scored
a 4 with the dice that hit, so the
Skaven is stunned.

The Chaos Horde player decides that
the Skaven Warrior will parry the
blow with his shield. This means
that the Skaven Warrior ignores the
stun result and loses its shield
instead.

4. RECOVER FROM STUNS

Roll a dice for each of your models still
lying on their sides at the end of your
turn:

1, 2, or 3: the model is still stunned; try
again next turn.

4, 5, or 6: the model recovers and can be
stood upright and used as
normal.

USING THE COMBAT TRAY

Whenever the rules tell you to drop dice
into the Combat Tray, you must do so
from a height of at least 30cm (12
inches). Any dice that bounce out of the
tray count as misses. More than half a
dice must be in a square for the result to
count. If there is any dispute, re-roll the
dice.

After you have cast any spells,moved,
shot, fought and checked your stunned
miniatures for recovery, your turn is

ended.

WINNING
The winner is the first player to capture
the enemy shield

EXPANDING YOUR GAME

board and playing pieces with another
copy for an even bigger castle in which
to fight yor battles, with twice the number
of heroes and skaven

You can even combine Mighty warriors
with The Ultra Marines game, to fight
battles between skaven warriors and

HEROIC ARMY FORCE LIST (workshop object0, assembly page5)
Noble Knight: no missile weapons; four dice in hand-to-hand combat.
Fearless Dwarf: no missile weapons; three dice in hand-to-hand combat.
Heroic Elf: two dice when firing his bow and two dice in hand-to-hand combat. He may either shoot or fight in a turn, never both. He moves two extra spaces beyond the number rolled for movement.
Warriors: no missile weapons; two dice in hand-to-hand combat.
Combat results, effect on target:
Roll1: Skaven Warrior/Champion stunned; Chaos Sorcerer no effect.
Roll2,3,4: either target stunned.
Roll5: Skaven Warrior/Champion killed; Chaos Sorcerer stunned.
Roll6: either target killed.

CHAOS HORDE FORCE LIST (workshop object2, assembly page6)
Skaven Warriors: no missile weapons; one combat die. Their shield can absorb the first stun or kill with no effect on the model; remove the shield afterward. This also applies to Champions.
Skaven Champions: four Champions lead the Warriors; they normally move and fight like Warriors. Roll on the Champions table at game start for their special power. Different colored bases identify Champions.
Chaos Wizard: no missile weapons; two dice in hand-to-hand combat.
Champions table, D6:
1 Slingshots: Champions roll two dice in shooting or one in combat.
2 Agility: Champions move two extra spaces per turn.
3 Magic Immunity: Champions are immune to magic.
4 Battle Rage: Champions each roll one extra die in combat.
5 Tough: a hit die score of1 or2 has no effect on Champions.
6 Choose any two of the above powers for your Champions.
Combat results, effect on target (Dwarf, Warriors, Elf, Knight):
1: all no effect.
2: all no effect.
3: Warriors stunned; other three targets no effect.
4: Dwarf and Warriors stunned; Elf and Knight no effect.
5: Dwarf and Warriors stunned; Elf killed; Knight stunned.
6: all killed.

HEROIC SPELL SCROLL (workshop object4, assembly page7)
Drop two dice into the Combat Tray and choose any number landing in a HIT square. Consult the chart. If a spell affects a model other than the Elf, its target may be in the same room as the Elf or any room joined to it by a doorway piece.
1 Invisibility: the Elf is invisible and may not be attacked for one turn.
2 Speed: roll a die; move any one of your models that many spaces in addition to its normal move.
3 Lock Room: select either the Elf's room or a room connected to it by a doorway. No model may enter or leave that room for one complete turn.
4 Lightning Strike: drop four dice into the Combat Tray to attack one enemy model, before or after moving.
5 Mighty Wind: select any room connected by a doorway to the Elf's room. Every model in that room is stunned. Cast before or after moving.
6 Fireball: select any room connected by a doorway to the Elf's room. Drop four dice into the Combat Tray to attack every model in the selected room. Cast before or after moving.

CHAOS SPELL SCROLL (workshop object5, assembly page8)
Drop two dice into the Combat Tray and choose any number landing in a HIT square. Consult the chart. If a spell affects a model other than the Chaos Sorcerer, its target may be in the same room as the Sorcerer or any room joined to it by a doorway piece; individual spell text supplies its specific scope.
1 Armour of Chaos: replace the shield of any one Skaven in the Sorcerer's room or a room connected to it by a doorway.
2 Warp Tunnel: the Sorcerer may swap places with any Skaven model in any room anywhere on the board.
3 Battle Rage: any one Skaven in the Sorcerer's room or a room connected by a doorway may throw double its normal number of attack dice this turn only.
4 Doom Sword: the Sorcerer's attacks hit automatically this turn only; do not throw any dice.
5 Berserk: the Sorcerer may make two attacks this turn, rolling three dice per attack in the Combat Tray.
6 Raise Dead: roll a die and return that many dead Skaven Warriors to play in the Sorcerer's room. If fewer dead models are available than the die roll, the extra ones are lost.
