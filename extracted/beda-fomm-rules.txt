BEDA FOMM — ORIGINAL GDW 1979 GAMEPLAY REFERENCE; ENGLISH
R. Jordan, Phoenix issue24, printed23 at PDF27. Cover and full review visually read.
EDITORIAL FACT EXTRACTION from the operative review paragraphs, not a verbatim rulebook.
The right-hand Grapevine news column and neighbouring Commando article are excluded.
This is NOT the complete four-page GDW manual; full setup, unit/terrain costs, CRT,
morale-track details, exact exit-count victory thresholds and all exceptions remain absent.
No later Consim2010 rules, tournament variant or anonymous sequence aid substituted.

Original-edition identification: GDW120Series, Frank Chadwick;17x22inch map,120counters,
four-page booklet with tables on its back; two dice needed but not included.

Morale: each parent formation starts with morale5–12. A daughter unit retreating or being
eliminated permanently reduces the parent's morale by one. Most combat outcomes call
for a two-dice morale check, sometimes modified. Exceeding parent morale by1–3 forces
retreat; exceeding it by4or more eliminates the checking unit.

Each player-turn has Movement, Barrage, Antitank and Combat phases.
Movement: transports are restricted to roads/tracks and stacking limits apply at all times.
Jordan's overview says armour may enter all enemyZOCs; personnel may enter enemyZOCs
other than armourZOCs; other units cannot voluntarily enter enemyZOCs. This is an overview,
not the complete eligibility rule: see Sharif's separately attributed exception below.
Barrage: both phasing and non-phasing players may use attack barrage; results do not
depend on target strength, while armour receives a favourable column shift. The phasing
player can instead use interdiction, increasing the movement cost to enter the shelled hex.
Line-of-sight restrictions apply, but their full details are not given here.
Antitank: non-phasing units with offensive antitank strength and certain phasing units
can fire at adjacent enemy armour; non-phasing player shoots first. Eligibility details absent.
Combat: adjacent non-armour enemies may be attacked, with limited SupportFire/FPF provisions.
Daylight turns from7am–6pm last one hour; there are two night turns, during which entering
enemyZOCs and fighting are forbidden. Campaign runs noonFebruary5 to noonFebruary7,
at most28turns. Victory depends on Italian units entering from NORTH and exiting SOUTH.
The review does not give the full numerical victory schedule.

SEPARATE CORROBORATING REFERENCE: Alan Sharif, IPW review, preserved by Grognard.
This original GDW120Series review, publication date unknown, adds:
Personnel may enter enemy-armourZOCs if stacked with friendly armour. Some units can
infiltrate enemyZOCs, but cannot take part in that turn's combat. Artillery must be stacked
to fire together. AntitankCRT outcomes are NoEffect or TargetEliminated. Ordinary combat
uses odds comparison and retreats arise from failed morale checks. Italians must exit SOUTH;
many Italian units are road-bound. Reviewer strategy/opinion is not a formal rule.
