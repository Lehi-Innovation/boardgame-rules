D-DAY: THE GREAT CRUSADE — LIMITED THIRD-PARTY TURN-SEQUENCE REFERENCE; ENGLISH

Shared opening: on turn1place German resistance, determine Allied support, place support
markers and resolve landings. Then Allies roll two dice for weather and record air,
naval,battleship support and interdiction. Both sides roll replacements and immediately
spend steps, Germans first. Check applicable Allied airborne/battleship withdrawals.
Allied turn: designate air strikes; move/reinforce; German eligible artillery bombards;
Allied combat; Allied supply check/mark OOS; remove Allied disruption/air-strike markers;
resolve bridges. German turn: move/reinforce/create eligible breakdown battalions;
Allied artillery/air/naval bombardment; German artillery bombardment; German combat;
German supply check/mark OOS; remove German disruption; resolve bridges.
