VISUALLY CHECKED ORIGINAL READING NOTES
Page1 lists39 scoring tokens with total value163, not OCR34. Page2 gives11 starting points for2-4 players, and9 for5-6 players (one5,one3,one1); OCR misreads9 as4. Three special colored DICE select the words, not chips. Page4 awards the caller2points per unguessed pronounced word with2or3 opponents, and3points with4or more opponents. Page5 gives each guesser1point per correct word, but each wrong guess pays1point TO THE CALLER. The leaflet allows2players in setup but its caller-reward text does not explicitly cover only1opponent; that original gap is preserved, no reward invented. Original scan remains authoritative.
