SELECTED COMPLETE VISIBLE BOXES
Formation changes cost15DP and half a move. Before movement, facing changes up to45°
are free; over45° costs a quarter move. A separate DURING-MOVEMENT box prints a quarter
move for every25°; retain that printed distinction, not an assumed45° correction.
Uncompleted charge movement ends with30DP if still not in contact.
Rally uses d10, class modifiers A−3/B−2/C−1/D0/E+1, leader modifiers, casualties,
nearby enemy flank/rear pressure, and extra route/broken modifiers. Test against current
morale; success stops/turns the unit and permits new orders. Full reaction tables absent.
Blizzard leads to NO FIRE. In rain/downpour, javelin/slinger/dart/axe units follow the
visible allowed branch; other types cannot fire. Do not infer off-image fire modifiers.
