INTRODUCTION

THE AIM OF THE GAME

RAYE is a game for up to 6 Clubbers.

Take out the Vibe cards, Dance cards and flyers, shuffle and put them to one side,
take out the money, €nergi points. Punch out the dancing Playing Pieces, Police
Car, Police Helicopter, Diversion Counters, Speaker Site Markers and Psychedelic
WOW Trip-Tych’s from the sheet included in the game box. The Playing Pieces,
Police Pieces and Speaker Markers should be inserted into the clear plastic stands
provided, ready for play. Look at the board and you will see that it consists of ‘TRIP
CITY’ at the centre of the board, with 6 different coloured Smiley ‘Clubs’, all covered
in a grid of Hexagons or ‘Hexes’.

This means it is possible (conditions permitting) to mave in any one of 6%
different directions at any one time.

This is surrounded by the M25, with 9 numbered <3) arages leading through ‘A’ and
‘B’ roads, across the land or ‘E’scape., to different numbered ‘Meeting Points’ and
Rave Sites.

The M25 can be recognised by the fact that it’s got 3 dotted lines on it. This makes
it a very FAST road.

The ‘A’ roads have 2 dotted lines, and the lowly old ‘B’ roads, 1 dotted Jine. This will
be important when it comes to moving as you can move quicker on faster roads.
Having punched out the pieces and put them together, got the other components
ready, and having looked at the board and found Trip City with its different coloured
Clubs, worked out which the @)arage points are, the meeting points are, and the
different types of Road, you are now ready to set up the game and learn to play.

This is actually pretty easy so at first glance you might wonder why are there so
many instructions! The answer is these cover THREE versions of the game, and in
order to make them so easy even the most addled brain would be able to
understand they are a step by step guide. Just read through following each point so
it's clear and then move on to the next. At the end all the basic rules of play are then
summarised in a few paragraphs.

The three versions consist of a ‘QUICK EDIT MIX ,' this consists of points one to
ten, and should be enough to get you started, you can then refer to the other
sections to clarify any problems. There is also a ‘FAST AND FURIOUS Mix, a race
game to get you used to moving about, and a ‘DANCE Mix’, to get you rockin’ to

the beat!

SETTING UP THE GAME
The game is suitable for between 2 and 6 players.
A Dealer (i.e. Banker) is chosen, who organises the STASH, Cards, ® nergi’s etc,
someone... well... trustworthy? The Deaier can either play in the game or simply
handle the business.
To set up the game, each player is dealt £50, 2 @nergi's and 1 Flyer.
Each player chooses a Club and places their corresponding coloured Piece on it.
The two police vehicles are put on the two blue police station Hexes at the centre of
Trip City.
Each player throws the 8 sided ‘Diamond’ dice in turn, to determine the playing
order.
The highest score throws again to start and players take turns in a clockwise
direction, the 8 sided ‘Diamond’ dice being used for movement. The 6 sided ‘Cubic’
dice is used for deciding Rave venues (but more of that later).
