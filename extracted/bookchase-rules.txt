BOOKCHASE — LIMITED ORIGINAL-GAME CORE REFERENCE; ENGLISH
Editorial procedural facts from Hannah Ackroyd, April27,2012, Bookchase Board Game.
Complete substantive matching article read on its direct permalink. The adjacent Articulate
post on the original tag page is EXCLUDED; none of its timer/guess/movement rules are used.
This account distinguishes original Bookchase from the separate Penguin presentation.
NOT the full rulebook, question deck or Award/Sentence special-space procedures.

Visit the six differently colored sections and answer a multiple-choice question correctly
to obtain a book of the corresponding color. Collect one of every color, then return to
the central square by an EXACT dice roll while still retaining all six books. Award/Sentence
events may take books away, so merely having collected six earlier is insufficient.
The six categories are Fantasy/ScienceFiction,Children/Fun,Travel/Adventure,Classics/Modern,
Poetry/Plays and Crime/Thrillers. Optional book-jacket stickers customize the small books.
This is the writer's played account, not an inference from a generic trivia game.

Separate original2007publisher overview: choose a bookshelf; up to six players can play,
or more in teams. One book from every category and returning home wins. Questions,
Bookshop,Library and chance/transfer events can supply books; events can also remove them.
The overview does not supply the missing detailed movement, space or card resolutions.

Complete setup, ordinary dice/movement/direction restrictions, exact shop/library/card
procedures and full ending exceptions remain missing. No claim all Penguin editions match.
