Jeson Mor — specialist rules reference

JESON MOR
Traditional - Mongolia
This game is played on a 9x9 board
with the following setup:
HORSES
- Each horse moves and
		captures like
          the Chess knight (i.e., one cell on an orthogonal direction then one
          cell diagonally forward on that direction; it may jump).
GOAL
- Wins the player
          that first occupy the center cell and then leaves it, or by capturing
		all adversary horses.
An example
Blue  moves and wins in 5 moves. There is no way
        that Green is able to stop the invasion of cell e5. There are 4 blue
        horses in direct move range, against just 3 green horses.
