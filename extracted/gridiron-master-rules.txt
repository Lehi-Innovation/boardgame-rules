gridiron-master — limited English procedural reference

GRIDIRON MASTER — LIMITED ORIGINAL PUBLISHER RULES/FAQ; ENGLISH
Editorial procedural facts from the original PHI Sports Games Rules page, archived2011.
The complete substantive page and displayed passing diagram were read. The advertised
Game Guide PDF returned an actual404 and is not claimed recovered. American/Canadian
edition differences and detailed scoring/down/kicking rules are not supplied here.

A regular quarter contains TWO TOTAL possessions for either team; this is not two each.
Timed halves last one hour. Recommended move clock:20seconds. Each player has three
timeouts per half, at most three minutes each. Exceeding20seconds incurs a five-yard
Delay of Game penalty, applied at the play's end.
A direct snap may go to a non-quarterback only with the quarterback in shotgun and
the recipient immediately left or right of him. Tight ends move as chess knights,
including jumping pieces. To pass, have a receiver open horizontally,vertically or
diagonally and announce the pass as the move. The defense moves before the offense
chooses among receivers that were open. An interception requires moving into the hole
next to the receiver on the direct passer–receiver line. Neighboring lateral holes can
force an incompletion; consult the retained original diagram for geometry.
A rusher may block an opponent on the same side of scrimmage; opposite-side blocks
require both pieces on the line. Remove blocked pieces until the play ends.
Linemen use pawn movement. Tight end/fullback/outside linebacker use knight movement;
medium-color receiver/running back/defensive back rook; light-color equivalents bishop;
quarterback/middle linebacker KING; silver receiver/safety queen.
