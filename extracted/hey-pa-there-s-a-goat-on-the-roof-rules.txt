Hey Pa! There’s a Goat on the Roof — original1966instruction images; local convenience PDF

CHECKED GAMEPLAY TRANSCRIPTION FROM BOTH ORIGINAL INSTRUCTION IMAGES
This is a manual gameplay transcription in plain language. Both unchanged original photographs remain the authority. Automatic OCR was too garbled to use and is excluded from this canonical text.

OBJECT AND COMPONENTS
Collect more cans than any other player. The original game lists a farm board with structures, a spinner, six goats, a farmer, cards, a bell and cans. The leaflet specifies two to six players and copyright1966 Parker Brothers.

PREPARATION
Each player chooses a goat and puts it in the Goat Pen. Put all cans in that pen. Place the farmer on the tree stump/chopping block beside the barn. Keep the cards and bell beside the board.

TURN AND ORDINARY MOVEMENT
Everyone spins; the highest result takes the first turn and spins again for that turn. Move the goat the entire spun count along a path from the pen. Subsequent players take turns in the same manner.
Outside the Goat Pen, choose forward or backward movement, but keep the chosen direction throughout that move. Pieces can share a space or an object. A full-count move can force a goat away from its intended destination.
Land by exact count on a colored space: draw a card and carry out its directions.
Land by exact count on a star: put the goat on the nearest farm object and take one can from the pen.
If the goat begins a turn on an object, its jump down to an adjacent star uses one space of that turn's movement. Continue along the path using the remaining count. After spinning one, the goat stays on that star. It cannot jump back onto the same object until it has first moved off the star on a later turn.

CANS AND CARDS
Take and return all cans through the Goat Pen; players never transfer cans directly to each other. A penalty larger than a player's holdings takes only the cans they have: nobody owes cans.
A Ring the Bell card takes effect immediately: ring the bell and move the farmer from the stump into the barn, or from the barn back to the stump.
A Corn Field, Pond or Flower Garden card sends the goat to that area's square space. There is no additional penalty; it takes its next turn normally.

ROOF AND ENDING
A goat can jump onto the barn roof only while the farmer is INSIDE THE BARN. The PLAYER must spin a count sufficient to traverse the stump and reach the roof in that same turn; an exact count is not required. Count the stump as one space, but a goat may never stop there. The stump must remain available for the farmer to emerge and block access to the roof.
When a goat reaches the roof, its owner takes three extra cans and the game ends. The player with the most cans wins, which need not be the player whose goat reached the roof.

COMPARISON WITH THE TYPED WEB COPY
The House of Games ending paragraph incorrectly runs the words into the Farmer must spin a number. The original second image clearly requires the farmer to be inside the barn and the PLAYER to spin the necessary number. This missing condition is not an unresolved original-rule ambiguity.
The physical board/structures, spinner, complete action-card deck, goat/farmer pieces, bell and cans remain required. They are not reproduced as a complete playable set here.
