KAMAKURA — ORIGINAL1982WEG GAMEPLAY REVIEW REFERENCE; ENGLISH
Peter Hatton, The Wargamer22, printed44–45. Both original offeredGIF pages visually read.
EDITORIAL FACT EXTRACTION of the Kamakura review only, not a verbatim publisher manual.
On page44 the review starts at the bottom of column3 and continues into column4;
page45's opening lines complete it and give the author. Other game reviews are excluded.
850x1100 originals retained unchanged; enlarged crop was a review aid, not a new source.
Full setup, orders/adjudication exceptions, unit conversion costs/eligibility, garrison strengths,
scenario maps/forces and full optional rules are not present. No complete playable set or audit.

The review identifies MichaelMills, WestEndGames1982 and the Diplomacy-derived Japan game,
not the later unrelated territory/weapon card game or a historical miniature ruleset.
Five scenarios cover civil wars and the1281Mongol invasion; the review's historical period
is1156–1338, differing from the local overview's1192–1333. No metadata repair inferred.

Written orders are revealed simultaneously. In clashes, the force with other units ordered
to support it occupies the province. Forces are adjusted every winter to match the number
of productive provinces occupied. Turns are quarterly. Double-strength units are possible.
Non-player productive provinces have neutral garrisons. Armies can convert into fleets
and fleets into armies. The reference does not explain all conversion or support procedures.
Multiple fleets may coexist without conflict in oceans. Victory is control of HALF the
productive provinces, as stated in the review; scenario exceptions are not fully supplied.

REVIEWER INFERENCE, NOT CERTIFIED PRINTED RULE: Hatton says the booklet does not state
the standard Diplomacy one-unit-per-province-at-end-of-turn rule, but believes it intended,
because it explicitly gives the multiple-ocean-fleets exception. This remains his inference.
REVIEWER CLAIM: Yamashiro should be yellow rather than red on the map. No publisher erratum
or independent map printing check was obtained; do not treat this as a certified correction.

OPTIONAL, NOT REQUIRED BASE RULES: typhoons, earthquakes, famines, pirates, warrior-monks,
and reserves. Hatton recommends the separate warrior-monk faction for balance; this is advice.
His comments about amphibious invasions restricted to spring concern the typhoon option,
not an unconditional rule. The review describes four civil-war scenarios, one hypothetical,
supporting2–5players with a possible sixth using warrior-monks; the1281invasion scenario
has several different rules. Full scenario procedures and typhoon tables remain missing.
