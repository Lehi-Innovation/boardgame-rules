Components
1 game board
4 red dinosaurs
4 blue dinosaurs
4 yellow dinosaurs
4 green dinosaurs
1 black dinosaur (Tyrannosaurus Rex)
2 white dice
1 black die
To identify a dinosaur, look at the number printed under each piece. The dinosaur catalogue is given at the end of this rulebook.
Object of the Game
Be the first player to bring your 4 dinosaurs onto the 4 HOME squares.
Setup
Each player chooses a colour and places their 4 dinosaurs on the START squares of the same colour. The Tyrannosaurus Rex is placed in its lair.
Each player rolls the three dice. The player who rolls the highest score goes first.
Playing the Game
On their turn, the player first rolls the three dice. They must move the Tyrannosaurus Rex first, by the number of squares shown on the black die.
The Tyrannosaurus Rex may be moved clockwise or counter-clockwise. It must always stay on the orange track.
After moving the Tyrannosaurus Rex, the player then moves their dinosaurs in one of the following ways:
either move one dinosaur by the total shown on the two white dice;
or move two dinosaurs: one dinosaur by the number shown on one white die, and the other dinosaur by the number shown on the second white die.
A player's dinosaurs must always move clockwise.
Attack
If a player's dinosaur lands on a square occupied by a dinosaur of another player, a combat takes place.
The player who owns the dinosaur that arrives on the occupied square rolls the three dice, then the other player rolls two dice. The player who makes the higher score wins the combat. In case of a tie, the player who rolled two dice is declared the winner.
The winning dinosaur stays on the square. The losing dinosaur must go to one of the squares of the violet track "river bed". If no square is free, the losing dinosaur returns to a START square.
When the combat is over, the turn of the attacking player ends.
Tyrannosaurus Rex
If the Tyrannosaurus Rex lands on a square where another dinosaur already stands, that dinosaur is captured and placed in the lair of the Tyrannosaurus Rex.
The captured dinosaur may be freed as soon as its owner rolls a six with one of the two white dice on a later turn. Instead of moving one of their dinosaurs six squares, the player may use that six to free their dinosaur and place it on a free START square of its colour.
Pink Squares
The Tyrannosaurus Rex is not allowed to enter the pink squares located near the START and HOME squares. These squares are reserved for the other dinosaurs.
Violet Track "River Bed"
All dinosaurs may enter the violet track, except the Tyrannosaurus Rex.
A player's dinosaur may enter the river bed only if the player rolls a six with one of the two white dice. The player then moves that dinosaur six squares (taking the track as soon as it is on the bridge).
The Winner
The first player to place their 4 dinosaurs on each of the HOME squares of the same colour is the winner.
To place a dinosaur on a HOME square, the die must show the exact number needed to reach that square.
Simplified Rule
For younger players, the game may be simplified as follows:
The Tyrannosaurus Rex moves only clockwise.
Dinosaurs are not allowed to fight when they are on the same square.
Dinosaur Catalogue
Each entry gives the dinosaur's nickname, era (Époque), size (Taille), weight (Poids), diet (Régime alimentaire) and origin (Origine), as printed on page 2 of the rulebook.
1. Tyrannosaurus Rex
Nickname: Lizard tyrant (
Lézard tyran
)
Era: 65 million years ago
Size: 12 metres long
Weight: 8 tonnes
Diet: Carnivore - likes hadrosaurs
Origin: North America and Asia
2. Styracosaurus
Nickname: Spiked lizard (
Lézard à piques
)
Era: 70 million years ago
Size: 5.2 metres long
Weight: 2.7 tonnes
Diet: Herbivore
Origin: North America
3. Stegosaurus
Nickname: Pea head (because of the size of its head) (
Tête de pois
)
Era: 150 million years ago
Size: 9 metres long
Weight: 2 tonnes
Diet: Herbivore - especially likes trees and shrubs
Origin: North America
4. Giganotosaurus
Nickname: Banana head (because of the shape of its head) (
Tête de banane
)
Era: 100 million years ago
Size: 13 metres long (bigger than T. Rex, but don't tell it!)
Weight: 8 tonnes
Diet: Carnivore
Origin: South America
5. Deinonychus
Nickname: Clawed feet (
Pattes griffues
)
Era: 110 million years ago
Size: 2 metres tall
Weight: 80 kg
Diet: Carnivore - fast and ferocious predator
Origin: North America
6. Protoceratops
Nickname: Headbutt (
Coup de tête
)
Era: 80 million years ago
Size: 1.8 metres long
Weight: 400 kg
Diet: Herbivore
Origin: Asia (Mongolia)
7. Oviraptor
Nickname: Egg thief (
Voleur d'œufs
)
Era: 80 million years ago
Size: 1.8 metres long
Weight: 40 kg
Diet: Omnivore
Origin: Asia (Mongolia)
8. Ornithomimus
Nickname: Ostrich dinosaur (
Dinosaure-autruche
)
Era: 70 million years ago
Size: 4 metres long
Weight: 150 kg
Diet: Herbivore
Origin: North America
9. Diplodocus
Nickname: Long tail (
Longue queue
)
Era: 150 million years ago
Size: 27 metres long (the bone of its tail is 14 metres long)
Weight: 12 tonnes
Diet: Herbivore - loves ferns, moss and conifers
Origin: North America
10. Brachiosaurus
Nickname: Giraffe dinosaur (
Dinosaure-girafe
)
Era: 150 million years ago
Size: 30 metres long
Weight: 30 to 50 tonnes
Diet: Herbivore - prefers fruit and leaves
Origin: North America and Africa
11. Riojasaurus
Nickname: Vino Dino (
Vino Dino
)
Era: 220 million years ago
Size: 10 metres long
Weight: 1 tonne
Diet: Herbivore - ferns, trees and leaves
Origin: South America (Argentina)
12. Wannonasaurus
Nickname: Half portion (
Demi-portion
)
Era: 85 million years ago
Size: 60 centimetres long
Diet: Herbivore
Origin: Asia
13. Velociraptor
Nickname: Wrecker (
Saccageur
)
Era: 80 million years ago
Size: 1.8 metres long
Weight: 60 kg
Diet: Carnivore
Origin: Asia (Mongolia)
14. Iguanodon
Nickname: Iggy (
Iggy
)
Era: 130 million years ago
Size: 10 metres long
Weight: 5 tonnes
Diet: Herbivore
Origin: Asia, North America and Europe (a very widespread species)
15. Minmi
Nickname: Covered in bumps (
Couvert de bosses
)
Era: 115 million years ago
Size: 3 metres long
Weight: 1 tonne
Diet: Herbivore
Origin: Australia
16. Megalosaurus
Nickname: Eats everything (
