The article distinguishes A3R's integrated diplomacy, limited supply, limited offensives,
overruns and revised air/naval warfare. Rule numbers below are the author's references.
Terrain defense multipliers accumulate: mountain behind river quadruples defense; fortress
against invasion quintuples it. Capitals avoid the exploiting-armour negative multiplier.
Overrun14.8: units entering from one hex, including armour, require at least6:1odds and
spend one additional movement factor; they may continue moving and make further overruns.
Adverse defense multipliers accumulate, but defense never falls below face value.
Breakthrough/exploitation cannot be made against vacant hexes, except beaches.
Limited offensives17allow individual paid attacks/missions while other front units count
for attrition. Invasions allowed; airborne drops, breakthroughs and exploitation prohibited.
Construction19: submarine1BRP/factor;ASW/SAC2BRP/factor. BRPs may be voluntarily destroyed
during construction to avoid an unwanted double turn. US strategic redeployment20is1per20BRPs;
naval redeployment is declared and can be intercepted. Other exactSRlimits are not supplied.
Limited-supply armour losesZOCand moves3;3–4infantry also moves3. Airborne drops24remain
range6and require at least as many uninverted friendly air factors in range as enemy factors.
Airborne units attack only their drop hex and can be rebuilt after one-turn delay.
Partisans25may be built one per country per turn; each controlled hex costs the enemy1BRP/turn.
Air attack on fleets26uses air defense first, then surviving air factors roll2dice on an
AirAttackTable; aborted factors may return in further sorties, subject to stated conditions.
Air cover can fight attackers but cannot itself attack fleets or take another mission that turn.
Fleet combat27uses2dice/table; damaged factors abort/return, undamaged factors continue only
after winning. US/Britain/Germany may fight a second round after losing the first; others retreat.
Fleet replacement costs3BRP/factor; rebuild caps differ by nation. Damage repairs free in port,
but the factors stay inverted for the remainder of the turn. Complete modifier tables absent.
Invasion-placed bridgeheads are unlimited supply sources that turn; later need unlimited supply.
Supply29is checked before ground movement and after combat for previously unsupplied units.
Unlimited supply grants full abilities; limited supply fully supports only infantry/replacements.
Partially supplied armour/airborne act as infantry; air/naval units can move/stage but cannot
fight from unsupplied bases. Unsupplied ground units/bridgeheads/airbases are removed after
post-combat supply checks and cannot be rebuilt until the next turn. Sea supply can be intercepted.
The article has a Basra-source inconsistency: map discussion14calls it Axis, while logistics16
lists Iraq for WesternAllies. No resolution or repair is inferred from this scoped reference.
