MANUALLY READ EXCERPTS — SEPARATE FROM RAW OCR
Page 1: Blue and Red goals are closest to their respective entry boxes. White grid squares extend into
the goalie safety zone. The listed opportunity actions include body check, hand/foot check, block,
ball/elbow strikes, super catch, suicide save, save, friendly body throw and catching a pass.
Catching a pass costs zero points. A deflection immediately into a wall is rerolled. An on-target shot
deflected by the goalie into the scoring zone is a goal. A goalie cannot be hurt making a goalie save.
For a score the ball must strike the BACK of the scoring zone. Shots outside the 45-degree marks are
rebound shots. A thrown ball-carrying player may score by maneuvering into the scoring zone, requiring
at least one Agility check and two play points in the square before the goal, and suffers a Fullback
body check on hitting the wall. With no goalie in or adjacent to a goal square, the ball may be passed in.
Face-offs occur at the beginning of each period or the turn after a goal. A thrown player attempts only
one action per square traveled. Checking or being checked by the goalie in the opposing goalie safety
zone incurs an automatic five-turn penalty against the opposing player, even if the goalie checks.
OPTIONAL RULE, explicitly so labelled: catching a pass requires rolling less than the player's Agility.

Page 2: Track has Forward, Left/Right Halfback, Left/Right Fullback and Goalie, each with 1–12 and STUN.
This is a record sheet, not proof that every player receives 12 points each turn. Allocation is missing.

Page 3 action costs: move one square 1; move while evading 2; reorientation 3; pass 2; shoot 4;
body check 6; hand/foot check 3; block 4; strike with ball 3; strike with elbow 4; assault 6;
super catch 6; suicide save 2; save 4; body throw 7 and automatically ends the thrower's turn.
Scoring table: on-target S begins at die result 6 for range 1–3, 7 for range 4–7, 8 for range 8+,
and 9 for rebound shots, through result 12. Shot modifiers: Forward 0, Halfback −1, Fullback −2,
Goalie −3. An on-target shot must be blocked or saved to prevent a score.
Defense table: D means ball deflected; C means ball caught. Die modifiers are −1 for an adjacent-square
attempt and −2 for a goalie on a rebound shot. Full defense and damage matrices remain in the original
image. Damage-letter definitions and the rest of the checking/stealing rules are not supplied here.
