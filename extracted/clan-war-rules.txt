Clan War — early boxed-game procedural reference by Brian Bankler

CLAN WAR — LIMITED EARLY BOXED-GAME REFERENCE; ENGLISH
Editorial rules facts from Brian Bankler's complete substantive review, originally on
his website in 2000 and reposted June 19, 2005. Public 2011 archive capture retained.
The author says a rules rewrite is forthcoming. A bracket about the newer card supply
and later comments on Daimyo Edition are excluded; edition equivalence is not assumed.

Build armies to a points limit; off-clan units generally cost 20% extra.
Customize a thirty-card tactical deck, start with three cards, usually draw one per turn;
one player gets two, but its selection procedure is unstated. Cards assigned to units
can be used only by those units and stop counting against the hand limit.
Each unit rolls d10 initiative; tens allow another roll to add/subtract. Leaders modify
initiative by skill. Phases: initiative, orders/spells/cards, movement, extra unengaged
cavalry movement, shooting, melee, reserve movement, rally/card bookkeeping.
Units move in initiative order, normally four inches forward; formations can maneuver.
Visible archers roll individual attacks: die plus bonus must exceed the target number;
a ten hits and rerolls for another hit. Roll damage per hit, sum exploding dice, divide
by ten for wounds. Melee uses contacting figures and similar resolution.
Morale failure breaks; another failure routs. Reserve movement requires at least twelve
inches from enemies and no shooting. Finish at the agreed time/turn limit and assess
VPs for casualties, broken units, leaders and scenario objectives.

The review is not the original manual: exact setup, hand limits, rounding, all combat/
formation/terrain/leadership tables, morale thresholds, rally details and VP schedule
remain missing. The author's approximate six-to-ten-turn estimate is not a fixed limit.
No blanket claim about final Daimyo rules, optional no-card play or later corrections.
