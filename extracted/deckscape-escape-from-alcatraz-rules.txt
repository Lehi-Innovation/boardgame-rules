DALE'S COVERED CORE
Play cooperatively. Open the deck in its indicated orientation and follow the first cards.
Record the start time when instructed after introductory setup. Agree on a proposed puzzle
solution, then turn that card to check. Follow the new directions; found item cards may be
retained for later puzzles. Wrong answers are recorded but do not prevent continuing.
The separate prison newspaper contains information needed for the puzzles.
End performance combines elapsed time with incorrect-answer penalties.
His60-card count differs from the companion's64; no silent reconciliation is made here.

SEPARATE REPORTED ALARM PROCEDURE — not an original-card transcription
The Chief distinguishes alarms A from penalty X marks. On a wrong puzzle answer, add
one A, then add as many Xs as the current total of As. Thus an existing3A followed by two
errors becomes4A/4X, then5A/9X. Each accumulated X adds one minute to final elapsed time.
Story-imposed alarms can increase A without immediately adding X. Hints do not add penalties
in this account. Ending bonuses and original grading bands are not fully recovered.
