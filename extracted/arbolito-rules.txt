Arbolito — Red Cat Games English rules

ARBOLITO — GAME RULES
Publisher PDF, two spreads containing printed pages 1–4.
Text transcribed and checked against both original page images; diagram labels and promotional/production credits omitted. The unchanged PDF remains authoritative.

1–6 players; age 7+; 15–20 minutes.

COMPONENTS
54 cards (each consisting of 1, 2 or 3 color areas)
2 cardboard trees (including 1 spare tree)
14 acorn tokens
6 effect tokens
This rulebook

OBJECT OF THE GAME
Be the first player to hang all of your cards on the tree and win the required amount of acorn tokens.

GAME SETUP
1. Assemble the tree as shown on the picture and place it in the middle of the table within reach of all players.
2. Shuffle the cards to form a deck and place one card randomly on each of the five branches of the tree.
3. Deal cards to each player according to the number of players: 2–3 players: 5 cards; 4 players: 4 cards; 5–6 players: 3 cards. See rules for the solo mode on page 4.
4. Place the received cards on the table (either side up — the pattern on both sides of the cards is the same). Throughout the game, the cards must be on the table, clearly visible to other players.
5. Place the rest of the cards as well as acorn and effect tokens nearby so that all the players can easily reach them.

HOW TO PLAY
The person who last climbed a tree takes their turn first. Players then take turns clockwise.
On your turn, choose one of your cards and connect it to one already hanging on the tree, matching them by color. To do this, you need to connect the slot of your card with the slot of one of the cards on the tree.
If 1–3 cards fall during your turn, add any 1 of those cards to your cards on the table and discard the remaining ones. This ends your turn.
If 4+ cards fall during your turn, add any 2 of those cards to your cards on the table and discard the remaining ones. This ends your turn.
NOTE: if a card didn't fall but is touching the table surface, it is considered fallen. Carefully disconnect it. After this you can either choose this card among all the fallen cards and add it to your cards or discard it.
If a card didn't fall but is touching the fence or tree trunk it is not considered fallen, therefore shouldn't be removed.
A player cannot have more than 6 cards. In case you are obliged to take new cards but you already have 6, nothing happens.
After you successfully place the last one of your cards on the tree, take an acorn token as a reward and draw new cards from the deck according to the number of players: 2–3 players: 4 cards; 4–5 players: 3 cards; 6 players: 2 cards.
Play the next round following the same rules. The players who have not hung all their cards on the tree yet, continue playing with the cards they have.
IMPORTANT! THE GAME FOR TWO PLAYERS CONSISTS OF THREE ROUNDS INSTEAD OF TWO. IN THE THIRD ROUND, EACH PLAYER DRAWS 4 CARDS.

Notes:
Hang cards on the tree using one hand only.
You may connect only one card to one slot.
You may change the position of the card unless you let go of it.
You are not allowed to touch the cards on the tree with anything but the card you are hanging.
If the first card connected directly to the branch fell down, you may hang a new card of any color on the empty branch during your turn.
In case you cannot hang any of your cards on the tree during your turn, you will have to draw another one from the deck. Find the first card from the deck that can be played and use it.
In case you have to draw new cards but the deck is empty, shuffle the discard pile to form a new deck from it.
If cards fall down from the tree before the start of the next player's turn, the last player will have to choose 1 or 2 of them (according to the number of fallen cards) and add them to their cards.

SPECIAL EFFECTS
Connecting two areas of the same color with images of a bird and a cat makes it possible to activate special effects.
CAT + BIRD: On your next turn, connect one of your cards to any card on the tree, ignoring the colors. Immediately after activating this effect, place an effect token on the card that you want to play next.
CAT + CAT: Hang an additional card on the tree.
Notes: if you can't hang any card on the tree (you don't have cards of the matching colors), nothing happens; if you used the last one of your cards when activating this effect, take an acorn token, draw new cards (depending on the number of players) and immediately hang one of them on the tree.
BIRD + BIRD: Choose a player to draw 1 extra card from the deck.
IMPORTANT: If any card fell during the player's turn, the effect is not activated. You can activate only one effect per turn.
EXAMPLE: on his turn, Victor attached one of his cards with a cat to another card with a cat that was already hanging on the tree (1), which gave him the opportunity to hang an additional card right away. He decides to connect a card with a bird to the cat card (2), even though the second effect won't be activated.

END OF THE GAME
The end of the game is triggered when one of the players receives the second acorn token (the third acorn token in a two-player game).
Other players can't apply their BIRD + BIRD effect to this player and make them take another card. After that, the rest of the players must complete the round so that everyone takes an equal number of turns. If after this several players manage to get rid of all their cards and receive their second or third acorn token, they share the victory.

SOLO MODE
Hone your skills in the solo mode! Try to grow the most magnificent tree by hanging cards from the deck on it one by one. Will you manage to use all the cards?
Effects from the base mode are not applied in the solo mode. Instead, every time you connect two color areas with animal images on them, set the top card from the deck aside as a reward — this card will be considered played during scoring.
THE GAME IN SOLO MODE ENDS WHEN:
At least one card falls from the tree. Count the number of cards left in the deck. The fewer cards are left, the better is your result. Try to improve it next time!
The player hangs the last card from the deck on the tree or sets it aside as a reward. Congrats! You are truly one of a kind!
