Gameplay Hints

While searching for the treasures, don't forget to watch the moves of other
players. Don’t make it too easy for them to reach the castle with what may be
knowledge of the right treasure.

Do not worry too much if you cannot find the treasure called for at the castle at
any given time. It is more important to remember the location of those which
you do find.

During the game, no player may change the position of any of the trees.
