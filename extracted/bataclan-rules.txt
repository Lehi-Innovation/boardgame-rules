Purpose of Game
To get rid of all the dice by stacking them up on the common pile.
How the Game Is Played
Start of Game
The throwing of a "G" decides who begins.
This player then throws all his dice: to start the pile he must get a "G". He then puts this dice in the centre of the table. If the first player does not get a "G" it's the next player's turn and so on until one player gets a "G". As soon as a player has placed the "G" at the bottom of the pile he can begin to pile up his dice in ascending order if his throw gave him a 2, then a 3, then a 4, then a 5, then a 6, then once again a "G", etc. (fig. 1)
How the Game Is Played
In turn the following players throw their dice and place them on the pile, always in ascending order.
The players do not have to place all possible dice on the pile resulting from their throw, but they must place one dice on the pile if they can.
From the second round onwards the players can choose the dice they wish to throw and those they wish to keep: at least one dice has to be thrown each time.
When a player knocks over the pile or a part of the pile he must add all the fallen dice to his own (fig. 2).
"G"
The "G" side of the dice is worth "1" point but can also be a joker: it can represent any value (fig. 3).
When the "G" is used to represent a value other than "1" the player must turn it over so the correct value is visible on the pile.
End of Game
The first player to have placed all his dice on the pile has won.
The runners-up are determined by:
continuing the game
or by counting their remaining dice, the one with the fewest dice is second, etc.
