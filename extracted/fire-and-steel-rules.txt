FIRE AND STEEL — ORIGINAL1978GDW ORDER/SEQUENCE REFERENCE; ENGLISH
JamesB.Byrne, preserved Grognard review; original publication date not established.
EDITORIAL FACT EXTRACTION from the complete reread original15mm subsection.
NOT a verbatim review transcription or the full56page rulebook/organization/nationality-card set.
The subsequent System7SpecialEdition and cardboard-module catalogue are excluded.
Reviewer explicitly says he has not played these miniatures rules; no playtest endorsement inferred.
Exact unit movement/ranges,fire/melee/morale tests,setup and victory procedures remain missing.

Original game identified as GDW1978FireandSteel for15mmNapoleonicminiatures,GregNovak,
JohnHarshman development,RogerMacGowan cover. Two56pagebooks accompany general combat,
terrain,unit capability,weather/officer and roster aids, plus23nationality-characteristic cards.
The review says most general tables are repeated in the rules, while nationality characteristics
and casualty rosters are exceptions. Losing a nationality card therefore leaves required data missing.
Original ground scale is reported as1inch=50yards, conflicting with25yards in other metadata;
unresolved. The review gives1turn=1hour; exact printed-manual scale is not independently verified.
A figure represents20infantry,20cavalry,10artillerists or2guns/limbers in this account.

Complete reported turn order:
1 Determine weather.
2 Write orders.
3 Declare charges.
4 First movement, with possible morale checks.
5 Resolve fire.
6 Check morale before melee.
7 Resolve melee.
8 Second movement for breakthrough/exploitation. Repeat phases4–8only for units that breached
  an enemy line through melee AND still have movement allowance. No general second move granted.
9 Check casualty-related morale.
10 Check fear-of-disaster morale.
11 Rally.

Every formation performing an activity needs written orders. The rules give general guidance
and specific restrictions; this reference does not supply their complete contents. A player's
general figure can affect combat and morale. Beyond written orders, the reviewer reports
no further command/control system. No automatic exemption from order writing is inferred.
Weather affects sight,movement and fire; daylight varies with season. Terrain affects sight
and movement, but exact weather/terrain effects and officer modifiers are not supplied here.

The organization booklet describes national organization,mounting and formations for1806–1815;
it does not replace the numerical nationality cards. Review distinguishes the game's1808–1815scope.
System7is separately reviewed as a48pagerulebook/counteradaptation using1inch=40yards.
Its rule changes,unit notation,module catalogue and play opinions are NOT imported into this reference.
The local summary's designer/scale/sequence are not comprehensively audited or repaired.
